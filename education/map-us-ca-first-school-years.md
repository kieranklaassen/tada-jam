# California (`us-ca`): kindergarten and grade 1, ages 5 to 7

This map is the pack's own text, not official text, and does not replace the records it names.

## Ages and levels

The levels come from `education/ages.ts` through the lookup.

- **Age 5**: `kindergarten`, and `preschool-tk` in its later band (4 to 5 ½ years) for a child in transitional kindergarten; see `map-us-ca-ages-2-to-5.md`. Mapping: official.
- **Age 6**: `kindergarten` and `grade-1`. Mapping: official.
- **Age 7**: `grade-1` only. Mapping: derived; typical ages, not law. The lookup prints a gap: grade 2 is not in the pack, and a child who starts the year at seven is in it.
- **Age 8**: no level and no record. The lookup prints `Not covered:`: grade 2 and grade 3 are not in the pack.

At ages 5, 6 and 7 the lookup also returns the subject's `cross-grade` lane, labelled `[cross-grade]`: its statements hold for every grade. Age is a hint and never gates content.

## What each subject holds

Every record of the grade lanes has the standing `state-board-adopted-standard`.

**Mathematics.** California Common Core State Standards: Mathematics. Kindergarten has 24 records in five domains (Counting and Cardinality 9, Operations and Algebraic Thinking 5, Number and Operations in Base Ten 1, Measurement and Data 3, Geometry 6). Grade 1 has 23 in the last four (8, 8, 4 and 3). A cluster heading, and sometimes a footnote, accompanies a statement, and Limits reflects both. A kindergartner says the count sequence, counts and compares groups, joins and splits small amounts, and names shapes. A first grader adds and subtracts, reads two-digit numbers as tens and ones, measures length and reads clocks. The cross-grade lane holds the 8 Standards for Mathematical Practice.

**Reading and language.** California Common Core State Standards: English Language Arts and Literacy. Each grade has 41 records in six domains (Reading: Literature 9, Reading: Informational Text 10, Reading: Foundational Skills 4, Writing 7, Speaking and Listening 6, Language 5); five placeholder rows are skipped. A kindergartner meets how print works, letter names and sounds, and the sounds inside spoken words. A first grader decodes words of one and two syllables and writes opinion, informative and narrative pieces. Each lane frame lists the records corrected from the adopted document where the department's export has a defect. The cross-grade lane holds the 32 anchor standards.

**Science.** Next Generation Science Standards for California Public Schools. Kindergarten has 10 records (Physical Science 4, Life Science 1, Earth and Space Science 5) and grade 1 has 9 (4, 3 and 2). Kindergarten covers pushes and pulls, sunlight, weather and what living things need; grade 1 covers sound, light, the sky, and how young resemble their parents. The cross-grade lane holds 6 engineering design expectations, three of them for kindergarten to grade 2.

**Practical life and feelings.** Health Education Content Standards for California Public Schools. Kindergarten has 64 records in six content areas, among them Mental, Emotional, and Social Health (13) and Nutrition and Physical Activity (10). Grade 1 has 53 in three: Growth and Development 6, Injury, Prevention, and Safety 25, Personal and Community Health 22. The cross-grade lane holds 8 overarching health standards, adopted, and 114 Transformative Social and Emotional Learning competencies, `voluntary-guidance`. 38 of those are in the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades.

## Limits the records state

Each limit is from the `### Limits` of the record beside it.

- Saying the sequence stops at 100, in ones and tens only (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.mathematics.objective.k-cc-1`).
- Counting things: up to 20 in a row, a grid or a ring, and up to 10 when scattered (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.mathematics.objective.k-cc-5`). Written numerals run from 0 to 20 (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.mathematics.objective.k-cc-3`).
- Kindergarten adds and subtracts within 10 (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.mathematics.objective.k-oa-2`), with fluency only up to 5 (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.mathematics.objective.k-oa-5`). Ten and some more ones covers 11 to 19 only (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.mathematics.objective.k-nbt-1`).
- Comparing: exactly two objects; units and numbers are not mentioned (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.mathematics.objective.k-md-2`).
- Grade 1 counts to 120, forward (`edu.us-ca.grade-1.mathematics.objective.1-nbt-1`). It adds and subtracts within 20, with fluency only within 10 and no time limit (`edu.us-ca.grade-1.mathematics.objective.1-oa-6`). Sums stay within 100 (`edu.us-ca.grade-1.mathematics.objective.1-nbt-4`).
- Length is a whole number of copied units; rulers and standard units are not mentioned (`edu.us-ca.grade-1.mathematics.objective.1-md-2`). Clock time: hours and half-hours only (`edu.us-ca.grade-1.mathematics.objective.1-md-3`).
- All 26 letters, capital and small, by name (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.reading-language.objective.rf-k-1`). Sound work uses spoken words and no print (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.reading-language.objective.rf-k-2`, `edu.us-ca.grade-1.reading-language.objective.rf-1-2`). The sight words listed are examples (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.reading-language.objective.rf-k-3`).
- Word-meaning work is done with adult guidance and support (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.reading-language.objective.l-k-5`, `edu.us-ca.grade-1.reading-language.objective.l-1-5`).
- A push or pull test varies strength or direction, never both (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.science.objective.k-ps2-1`).
- No list or count of feelings is given (`edu.us-ca.kindergarten.practical-life-feelings.objective.k-1-1-m`).

## What the pack does not hold here

- Grade 2 and grade 3.
- Arts, career technical education, computer science, English language development, history and social science, physical education, school library and world languages.
- An adopted standard for self-care or social and emotional learning: none exists. Grade 1 health has no content area for feelings or food.
- Confirmation of three science records, two in kindergarten and one in grade 1: two official renditions differ, and the lanes' review files under `education/reviews/us-ca/` record the reason. Every other record of these lanes was `confirmed` when this map was checked; the lookup prints each state.
