The department's Transformative Social and Emotional Learning competencies, with its Conditions for Thriving, as a word-processor file. It is the optional source of the pack: the canonical rendition for the 114 voluntary competency records in cross-grade practical life and feelings.

Standing: the introduction of the document says these tools were developed for voluntary use.

Not a PDF: read word/document.xml inside the file. The competencies are 15 tables (5 competencies, each under Identity, Belonging and Agency) with five columns: Early Elementary, Late Elementary, Middle School, High School, Adult. Each column holds 38 cells, 190 in all, and each cell starts with its printed code. The pack records the first three columns.

Artefacts: the document does not say which grades a band covers. One Late Elementary cell carries the code of the Early Elementary cell beside it, and two codes have no space after them. The second half of the file is guidance for adults and is left out.
