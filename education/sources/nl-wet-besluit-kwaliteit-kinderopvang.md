The decree on the quality of childcare. Four statements are recorded: artikel 2, onderdelen a to d, the four pedagogical aims that work out responsible childcare.

Standing: artikel 2 opens with the duty of the holder or the childminder to see to these four things, taking the stage of development of the children into account.

Read as XML: every article, annex and division carries its path in the attribute bwb-ng-variabel-deel, and a statement is found by that path. The publication data (meta-data, jcis) is not part of the text. Artikel 2 (/Hoofdstuk1/Artikel2) is a lead-in sentence and a list of four lettered parts; each part is one statement.
