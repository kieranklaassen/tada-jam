The preschool and transitional kindergarten learning foundations, the Language and Literacy Development domain. It is the canonical rendition for preschool and transitional kindergarten reading and language, for its Foundational Language Development sub-domain only (23 foundations, PDF pages 21 to 77).

Standing: Education Code 48000(f) names these foundations as developed by the department and as what transitional kindergarten curriculum is to be aligned to.

Read with pdftotext in plain mode. A contents list at the front names every strand, sub-strand and foundation with its page. In the body a foundation first appears as its heading, the labels Early, 3 to 4 ½ Years, Later, 4 to 5 ½ Years, then the Early statement and the Later statement, then two columns of examples that interleave. The heading block repeats on continuation pages, so the first occurrence is the one to take. Layout mode puts Early and Later side by side on each line.

Artefacts: plain mode drops line-break hyphens, and the age label is spaced two ways ("4 ½" and "4½"). The file has no copyright page and no date inside it, and the department edits these files in place, so the pin is the only version marker. The file holds a second sub-domain, English Language Development (23 foundations at three levels, from PDF page 82), which is left out. Both sub-domains number from 1.1 and several foundations carry the same number and the same title in both, so a locator must name the sub-domain. One repeated heading misspells its title, and PDF page 77 prints the number of one foundation over the title of another.

Reuse: published on the department's website, whose copyright statement (read 2026-10-01; the page says it was last reviewed August 12, 2025) requires permission for any reproduction. No permission has been asked, so the wording is not committed.
