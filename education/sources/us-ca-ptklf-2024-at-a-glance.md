The preschool and transitional kindergarten learning foundations at a glance: all 220 foundations of the ten domains, statements only, in one file. It is the check rendition for every preschool lane and the index the expected counts were taken from.

Standing: Education Code 48000(f) names these foundations as developed by the department and as what transitional kindergarten curriculum is to be aligned to.

Read with pdftotext in plain mode: every foundation heading is followed by the two age labels and then the Early statement and the Later statement, in that order, with no examples. PDF pages 2 and 3 are an overview; the statements start on page 4.

Artefacts: plain mode drops line-break hyphens. Eight footnotes point to the full document, and some foundation and sub-strand titles end in an asterisk that points to them. The file has no copyright page and no date inside it, so the pin is the only version marker.
