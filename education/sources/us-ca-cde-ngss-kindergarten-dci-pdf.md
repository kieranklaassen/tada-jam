The kindergarten science standards arranged by disciplinary core idea. It is the check rendition for kindergarten science and for the K-2 engineering design expectations in cross-grade science.

Standing: the search tool's science page says the State Board approved these standards in 2013. The PDF prints no adoption statement.

Read with pdftotext -layout: all 13 codes sit on the line of their statement (plain mode: 3 of 13).

Artefacts: the engineering codes are printed with an en dash after the K. The file name and cover say "topic", but the file is the arrangement by disciplinary core idea, with each expectation defined once. The text layer has stray spaces inside words ("obser vations"), which a wording comparison must tolerate. No double asterisk in this grade. The link was found by a search of the department's site, because the index page could not be read.

Reuse: the licence of the science standards grants copying to states, districts, schools, teachers and non-profit education entities. This repo is none of those, so the wording is not committed. The file is also published on the department's website, whose copyright statement (https://www.cde.ca.gov/re/di/cr/) says: "Except where specifically noted, permission must be obtained for reproduction of any portion of the material on this website."
