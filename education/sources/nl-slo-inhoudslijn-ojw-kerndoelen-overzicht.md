A table of every goal of the six lines of this area, 18 pages: line, cluster, fase, the goal, and the numbers of the 2006 core goals it serves. It is a further rendition of goals whose canonical rendition is the open data (nl-slo-curriculum-inhoudslijnen) and whose check rendition is the PDF of each line. It is read for the frame of a lane only: no record is read from it or checked against it.

Standing: SLO, "Opbouw van de inhoudslijnen" (8 August 2024), says the core goals and the reference levels are the legal frame, and that these goals say what a school can offer, not what a child must know. Guidance of the curriculum institute, written for the 2006 core goals.
