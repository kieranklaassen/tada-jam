A young-child card of the curriculum institute: one A3 page of aims for the first years of primary school (fase 1). 87 bullets in five columns: Zelfbeeld, Relaties, Zelfsturing, De ander, Kiezen. All are recorded, in fase 1 practical life and feelings. This area has no inhoudslijn and nothing of it is in the open data.

Standing: SLO, "Doelen jonge kind" (31 July 2023), presents the cards as an overview of what can be offered in the first years of primary school. Guidance of the curriculum institute, not law.

The card has a text layer. It is read by column with pdftotext -bbox, because plain mode mixes the columns. The title and the three heading bars (Emotionele, Sociale and Morele competenties) are drawn as images; the five column headings and the sub-headings are text. The card prints no codes: a statement is named by its heading bar, its sub-heading and its position.

Other renditions: none. Nothing of this card is in the open data.
