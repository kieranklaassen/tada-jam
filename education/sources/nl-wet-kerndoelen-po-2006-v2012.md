The decree of the 2006 core goals as it read until 31 July 2026, with all 58 goals. It is the canonical rendition of the goals that were struck on 1 August 2026 and that a school may still use: Nederlands 1 to 12 and Rekenen/wiskunde 23 to 33.

Standing: artikel 6 of the Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026 lets a school use the core goals for Dutch and arithmetic "zoals deze luidden" before 1 August 2026, until 1 August 2031. This version is that text. Every record read from it says that the goal was struck on 1 August 2026 and may be used until 1 August 2031. The Frisian goals 17 to 22 were struck at the same time and artikel 6 does not cover them.

Read as XML: every article, annex and division carries its path in the attribute bwb-ng-variabel-deel, and a statement is found by that path. The publication data (meta-data, jcis) is not part of the text. A goal is a numbered list item in the division "Kerndoelen" of its area. Kerndoel 11 has two paragraphs and a list of three dashes and is one statement. Kerndoel 22 is marked up as a paragraph, not a list item, so counting list items gives 57.

Artefact: the index of the legislation repository lists a wrong size and hash for this old version. The hash pinned here is of the file as served.

Other renditions: the open data (nl-slo-curriculum-kerndoelen). In goals 1 to 12 and 23 to 33 it differs four times: quote marks in 10 and 12, a hyphen in 25, and 11, where the data stops after the first of three rules.

Pin: the XML of the legislation repository is byte-stable (two fetches 25 minutes apart gave the same hash), so its bytes are pinned. The page of the same version on wetten.overheid.nl (the landing link) prints the day it was read and is not what is pinned.

Reuse: Auteurswet, artikel 11. There is no copyright on laws and decrees, so the wording is committed, each record with its source line.
