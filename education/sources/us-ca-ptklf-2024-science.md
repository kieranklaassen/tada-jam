The preschool and transitional kindergarten learning foundations, the Science domain: 29 foundations. It is the canonical rendition for preschool and transitional kindergarten science.

Standing: Education Code 48000(f) names these foundations as developed by the department and as what transitional kindergarten curriculum is to be aligned to.

Read with pdftotext in plain mode. A contents list at the front names every strand, sub-strand and foundation with its page. In the body a foundation first appears as its heading, the labels Early, 3 to 4 ½ Years, Later, 4 to 5 ½ Years, then the Early statement and the Later statement, then two columns of examples that interleave. The heading block repeats on continuation pages, so the first occurrence is the one to take. Layout mode puts Early and Later side by side on each line.

Artefacts: plain mode drops line-break hyphens, and the age label is spaced two ways ("4 ½" and "4½"). The file has no copyright page and no date inside it, and the department edits these files in place, so the pin is the only version marker.
