A young-child card of the curriculum institute: one A3 page of aims for children in childcare before school (peuters). 54 bullets in five columns: Zelfbeeld, Relaties, Zelfsturing, De ander, Kiezen. All are recorded, in peuters practical life and feelings.

Standing: SLO, "Doelen jonge kind" (31 July 2023), says of the cards for peuters that their aims give a free view of what a childcare worker might attend to. Guidance of the curriculum institute, not law.

The card has a text layer. It is read by column with pdftotext -bbox, because plain mode mixes the columns. The title and the three heading bars (Emotionele, Sociale and Morele competenties) are drawn as images; the five column headings and the sub-headings are text. The card prints no codes: a statement is named by its heading bar, its sub-heading and its position.

Other renditions: none. Nothing of this card is in the open data.
