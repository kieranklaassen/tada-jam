SLO's bundle of the core goals for primary school in all nine areas, kerndoelen 1 to 40, 67 pages. It is the check rendition of the draft core goals the pack records (burgerschap 19 and 20, mens en maatschappij 28, mens en natuur 29 to 32), and a third rendition of the 2026 core goals, read for the frame only.

Standing: the colophon calls these the "definitieve conceptkerndoelen", delivered to the ministry in 2025, which become binding once they are laid down in law. Kerndoelen 1 to 18 in it are law since 1 August 2026 by the legal text; the record is marked as a draft because the goals it is the check rendition of are.

Where it is published: the landing page named in this record does not link the file itself. It links the sub-page "Kerndoelen primair onderwijs", one click deeper, and that page links the file (seen on 2026-10-02).

Read by column with pdftotext -bbox: from the second page on a PDF page is a spread of two printed pages. A doelzin is printed as its number and letter ("19A") and the items are bullets without letters.

Differences: its wording follows the law and the consultation draft, not the open data. It does not print the illustrations ("Te denken valt aan") that the open data holds.
