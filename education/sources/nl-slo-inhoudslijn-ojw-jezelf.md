One inhoudslijn of the curriculum institute as published on slo.nl: 2 A3 pages with the goals of the line in three columns, fase 1, fase 2 and fase 3. It is the check rendition of the goals of the line OJW/ja in the open data (nl-slo-curriculum-inhoudslijnen), which is the canonical rendition.

Standing: SLO, "Opbouw van de inhoudslijnen" (8 August 2024), says the core goals and the reference levels are the legal frame, and that these goals say what a school can offer, not what a child must know. Guidance of the curriculum institute, written for the 2006 core goals.
