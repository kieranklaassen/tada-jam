One inhoudslijn of the curriculum institute as published on slo.nl: 7 A3 pages with the goals of the line in three columns, fase 1, fase 2 and fase 3. It is the check rendition of the goals of the line RW/bew in the open data (nl-slo-curriculum-inhoudslijnen), which is the canonical rendition.

Standing: SLO, "Opbouw van de inhoudslijnen" (8 August 2024), says the core goals and the reference levels are the legal frame, and that these goals say what a school can offer, not what a child must know. Guidance of the curriculum institute, written for the 2006 core goals.

Read by column with pdftotext -bbox: the three columns nearly touch, and plain mode mixes them. A label column on the left names the clusters. A statement opens with a dash. The PDF prints no codes.

Differences from the open data: the PDF prints an example in italics and brackets with about a third of the statements, which the data leaves out, and it prints sub-points with another mark. The records keep the wording of the data and hold the example as accompanying text, read from this PDF by script; where the data also drops statement text around an example, the record holds the whole statement as this PDF prints it (manifest/nl-additions.ts lists those goals). The grouping is not the same. The PDF prints ten goals under "Vermenigvuldigen en delen met decimale getallen" (pages 4 and 5), one in the fase 2 column and nine in the fase 3 column; the data lists those ten under the cluster before it, "Vermenigvuldigen en delen met hele getallen", all linked to fase 3, and fills the cluster of decimal numbers with copies of the six goals of "Combinaties van en relaties tussen bewerkingen". The goal numbers in the data (aanbodid) agree with the PDF. The records follow the PDF's grouping and fase; the corrections are listed in manifest/nl-additions.ts and in the frames of the two lanes.
