The grade 6 document of the Preferred Integrated Course Model for grades 6 to 8, arranged by disciplinary core idea. It is the canonical rendition for grade 6 science: only this document says which middle school expectations belong to grade 6.

Standing: the search tool's science page says the State Board approved these standards in 2013. The PDF prints no adoption statement. That this is the State Board's preferred model rests on the file's own title; the department page that says so could not be read on the retrieval date.

Read with pdftotext -layout, which keeps each of the 19 codes on the line its statement starts on. Plain mode lists the codes of a page first and the statements after.

Artefacts: in layout mode the three boxes under the expectations run side by side and interleave; only the expectation rows are needed. Two codes are printed with an en dash before the last number. A single asterisk marks engineering integration and a double asterisk a California clarification statement (21 double asterisks in the text).

Reuse: the licence of the science standards grants copying to states, districts, schools, teachers and non-profit education entities. This repo is none of those, so the wording is not committed. The file is also published on the department's website, whose copyright statement (https://www.cde.ca.gov/re/di/cr/) says: "Except where specifically noted, permission must be obtained for reproduction of any portion of the material on this website."
