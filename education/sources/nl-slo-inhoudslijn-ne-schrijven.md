One inhoudslijn of the curriculum institute as published on slo.nl: 3 A3 pages with the goals of the line in three columns, fase 1, fase 2 and fase 3. It is the check rendition of the goals of the line NE/SCH in the open data (nl-slo-curriculum-inhoudslijnen), which is the canonical rendition.

Standing: SLO, "Opbouw van de inhoudslijnen" (8 August 2024), says the core goals and the reference levels are the legal frame, and that these goals say what a school can offer, not what a child must know. Guidance of the curriculum institute, written for the 2006 core goals.

Read by column with pdftotext -bbox: the three columns nearly touch, and plain mode mixes them. A label column on the left names the clusters. A statement opens with a dash. The PDF prints no codes.

Differences from the open data: the data keeps the examples in brackets that the PDF prints, and the PDF prints sub-points with another mark. The grouping is not the same: under "Schrijven: kenmerken van de taakuitvoering" the PDF prints seven rows of goals and the data has six sub-clusters. The row "inhoud/vorm" (page 2) is not in the data at all: 5 goals in the fase 1 column, 6 in the fase 2 column and 10 in the fase 3 column. For those 21 goals this PDF is the only rendition: they are records read from it by locator, with an empty code, in the three reading and language lanes.

Pin: the bytes of the file. The PDFs of slo.nl are stable: one of them was fetched a second time and gave the same hash.

Reuse: published on slo.nl, whose notice allows copying in whole or in part with the source named. The file carries no notice of its own. The wording is committed, each record with its source line.
