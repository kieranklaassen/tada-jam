The introduction to the preschool and transitional kindergarten learning foundations. It holds no foundation statement. The lane frames read it for the two age ranges and for how a foundation is laid out.

Standing: Education Code 48000(f) names these foundations as developed by the department and as what transitional kindergarten curriculum is to be aligned to.

Read with pdftotext in plain mode. It gives the Early range as three to four and a half years and the Later range as four to five and a half years, and calls them overlapping.
