The adopted English language arts and literacy standards as a PDF. It is the canonical rendition for cross-grade reading and language (the College and Career Readiness anchor standards, PDF pages 16, 26, 32 and 37) and the check rendition for the English language arts export.

Standing: the title page says the State Board adopted the standards in August 2010 and modified them in March 2013. The document carries the notice that it is not binding on local educational agencies.

Neither text mode of pdftotext reads the grade standards: the pages are landscape tables of three grade columns, which plain mode interleaves and layout mode sets side by side. Read with pdftotext -bbox and assign each word to a column by the x position of the standard numbers under each header row. Some pages hold two tables, so header rows are found per table.

Artefacts: the header row of PDF page 62 is drawn twice; one grade 5 writing standard is printed without the period after its number; the anchor standard pages are two columns and plain mode returns their items out of order. The anchor sets are printed again on PDF pages 52, 61, 71 and 76, and Reading and Writing a third time on pages 86 and 91. The PDF writes a grade band with an en dash where the export has a hyphen.

Reuse: published on the department's website, whose copyright statement (read 2026-10-01; the page says it was last reviewed August 12, 2025) requires permission for any reproduction. No permission has been asked, so the wording is not committed. The document's own publishing page also reserves all rights and does not authorise reproduction for resale.
