The draft decree that adds the core goals of the remaining areas to the decree of 2026, as put to public consultation by the ministry of education: 101 pages. It is the canonical rendition of the draft core goals the pack records: 111 statements from Onderdeel C Burgerschap (kerndoelen 19 and 20), Onderdeel E Mens en maatschappij (kerndoel 28: its sentence and doelzinnen A, D and E) and Onderdeel F Mens en natuur (kerndoelen 29 to 32).

Standing: a draft that is not in force. The text opens with blanks where the dates of signing go, the consultation page gives the status "Gesloten", and the last article of the draft sets 1 August 2027. Every record read from it is marked as a draft not yet in force. The draft also withdraws the Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO.

Read by word position (pdftotext -bbox), every page split into two columns at one fixed x position: the primary-school goals are amendment F of ARTIKEL I, PDF pages 3 to 18, as tables of two columns that nearly touch, the doelzin on the left and its items on the right. Column 1 of a page holds the headings, the goal sentences and the doelzinnen; column 2 holds the items and the page footer. In layout mode the lines of the two columns interleave, so a doelzin and the items beside it cannot be taken apart there; that is why the file is not read that way. Later pages print goals with the same numbers for special and secondary education. Mens en natuur has 60 items. In layout mode an item letter twice stands alone on its line (32 B a, 32 C a), and a reading that misses those lines finds 58; read by word position every letter stands on one line with its text.

Pin: the PDF is made at the moment of download, so its bytes differ on every request and cannot be pinned. It is pinned by its extracted text: the pin above is the hash of the text `pdftotext` reads from it, after normalising, and the raw file is kept in the store beside it.

Other renditions: SLO's bundle of July 2026 (nl-slo-kerndoelen-po-bundel) prints the same goals and is the check rendition. The open data (nl-slo-curriculum-fo) is older: in the goals recorded, 5 wordings differ; it gives the ids of the nodes only.

Reuse: Auteurswet, artikel 15b. The draft is a work made public by the government without any reservation of rights, on it or with its publication. It is not yet a decree, so artikel 11 does not apply to it yet. The wording is committed, each record with its source line.
