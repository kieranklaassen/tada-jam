One inhoudslijn of the curriculum institute as published on slo.nl: 3 A3 pages with the three goals of the line and two tables of four columns, one per pair of groepen. It is the check rendition of the goals of the line NE/TB in the open data (nl-slo-curriculum-inhoudslijnen), which is the canonical rendition.

Standing: SLO, "Opbouw van de inhoudslijnen" (8 August 2024), says the core goals and the reference levels are the legal frame, and that these goals say what a school can offer, not what a child must know. Guidance of the curriculum institute, written for the 2006 core goals.

Read by column with pdftotext -bbox: the three columns nearly touch, and plain mode mixes them. A label column on the left names the clusters. A statement opens with a dash. The PDF prints no codes. It also prints word lists per pair of groepen, which are not in the data and are not recorded.

Differences from the open data: the data keeps the examples in brackets that the PDF prints, and the PDF prints sub-points with another mark. The grouping is the same. Beyond its three goals, which are in the data, this PDF is two tables by pair of groepen (groep 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 and 7-8), not by fase: a list of terms about language, and a list of spelling categories and rules. They are no goals and are not in the data. The frames of the reading and language lanes quote the columns of the groepen of each fase, read by script with the lines of the page as printed; the items the PDF sets in bold cannot be told from the others in its text layer.
