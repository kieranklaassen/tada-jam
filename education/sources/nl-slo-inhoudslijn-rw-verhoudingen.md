One inhoudslijn of the curriculum institute as published on slo.nl: 3 A3 pages with the goals of the line in three columns, fase 1, fase 2 and fase 3. It is the check rendition of the goals of the line RW/verh in the open data (nl-slo-curriculum-inhoudslijnen), which is the canonical rendition.

Standing: SLO, "Opbouw van de inhoudslijnen" (8 August 2024), says the core goals and the reference levels are the legal frame, and that these goals say what a school can offer, not what a child must know. Guidance of the curriculum institute, written for the 2006 core goals.

Read by column with pdftotext -bbox: the three columns nearly touch, and plain mode mixes them. A label column on the left names the clusters. A statement opens with a dash. The PDF prints no codes.

Differences from the open data: the PDF prints an example in italics and brackets with about a third of the statements, which the data leaves out, and it prints sub-points with another mark. The records keep the wording of the data and hold the example as accompanying text, read from this PDF by script; where the data also drops statement text around an example, the record holds the whole statement as this PDF prints it (manifest/nl-additions.ts lists those goals). The grouping is the same.
