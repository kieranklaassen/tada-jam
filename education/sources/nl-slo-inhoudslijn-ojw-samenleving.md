One inhoudslijn of the curriculum institute as published on slo.nl: 3 A3 pages with the goals of the line in three columns, fase 1, fase 2 and fase 3. It is the check rendition of the goals of the line OJW/ds in the open data (nl-slo-curriculum-inhoudslijnen), which is the canonical rendition.

Standing: SLO, "Opbouw van de inhoudslijnen" (8 August 2024), says the core goals and the reference levels are the legal frame, and that these goals say what a school can offer, not what a child must know. Guidance of the curriculum institute, written for the 2006 core goals.

Read by column with pdftotext -bbox: the three columns nearly touch, and plain mode mixes them. A label column on the left names the clusters. A statement opens with a dash. The PDF prints no codes.

Differences from the open data: the data keeps the examples in brackets that the PDF prints, and the PDF prints sub-points with another mark. In one goal of this line the data leaves the example out, and the record holds it as accompanying text. The grouping is the same, but the data lacks one goal: under "Consument zijn" the PDF prints four goals in the fase 2 column (page 2) and the data has three. The fourth is a record read from this PDF by locator, with an empty code.
