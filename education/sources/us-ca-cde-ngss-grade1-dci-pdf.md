The grade 1 science standards arranged by disciplinary core idea. It is the check rendition for grade 1 science.

Standing: the search tool's science page says the State Board approved these standards in 2013. The PDF prints no adoption statement.

Read with pdftotext -layout: all 12 codes start a row (plain mode: 10 of 12).

Artefacts: the engineering codes are printed with an en dash after the K.

Reuse: the licence of the science standards grants copying to states, districts, schools, teachers and non-profit education entities. This repo is none of those, so the wording is not committed.
