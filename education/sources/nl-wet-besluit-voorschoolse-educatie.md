The decree on early-years programmes (voorschoolse educatie). One statement is recorded: artikel 5, the sentence that names the four areas a programme must cover: taal, rekenen, motoriek and sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling.

Standing: a condition set by decree for early-years programmes (decree of 7 July 2010, Stb. 2010, 298). Artikel 2 has a child follow the programme from the day it is two and a half.

Read as XML: every article, annex and division carries its path in the attribute bwb-ng-variabel-deel, and a statement is found by that path. The publication data (meta-data, jcis) is not part of the text. The statement is the first paragraph of /Artikel5. It names areas, not goals, and it is recorded once, under practical life and feelings.
