The decree that sets the core goals for Dutch and for arithmetic and mathematics in primary school. It is the canonical rendition of the 2026 core goals: annex 1, Onderdeel A Nederlands (kerndoelen 1 to 9, 114 statements) and Onderdeel B Rekenen en wiskunde (kerndoelen 10 to 18, 90 statements).

Standing: artikel 1 sets the goals of annex 1 as the core goals meant in artikel 9 of the Wet op het primair onderwijs. The decree is of 10 June 2026 (Stb. 2026, 198) and in force since 1 August 2026.

Read as XML: every article, annex and division carries its path in the attribute bwb-ng-variabel-deel, and a statement is found by that path. The publication data (meta-data, jcis) is not part of the text. Each core goal is one table: the number, the goal sentence, then a row per doelzin with its items. Annexes 2 to 4 hold the functional core goals for special education and are not recorded. Artikel 6 lets a school use the struck 2006 goals for Dutch and arithmetic until 1 August 2031.

Artefacts: item 10 B a prints four fractions as images (274440.png to 274443.png, beside the XML in the repository and not part of the pinned file); the wording holds the transcription of each where it stands, marked as an image: [afbeelding: (1/3)]. The transcriptions are listed in manifest/nl-additions.ts with the hash of each image file. The goal sentence of kerndoel 5 prints "zicht" where "zich" is meant, and the record keeps what is printed.

Other renditions: the open data (nl-slo-curriculum-fo) has the same nodes, one for one, and 18 of its wordings differ, nearly all by a typo or a dropped word in the data: 8 of the 114 of Onderdeel A Nederlands and 10 of the 90 of Onderdeel B Rekenen en wiskunde. The frames of end-of-primary reading and language and of end-of-primary mathematics list them by code. SLO's bundle of July 2026 (nl-slo-kerndoelen-po-bundel) follows the law.

Pin: the XML of the legislation repository is byte-stable (two fetches 25 minutes apart gave the same hash), so its bytes are pinned. The page of the same version on wetten.overheid.nl (the landing link) prints the day it was read and is not what is pinned.
