The adopted mathematics standards as a PDF. It is the canonical rendition for cross-grade mathematics (the Standards for Mathematical Practice, PDF pages 13 to 15) and the check rendition for the mathematics export.

Standing: the title page says the State Board adopted the standards in August 2010 and modified them in January 2013. Page ii carries the notice that the document is not binding on local educational agencies.

Read with pdftotext in plain mode: one column, in order. Layout mode also works but puts a domain code on the line of the domain name.

Artefacts: plain mode drops line-break hyphens; footnote superscripts fuse to the last word ("strategies.1"); footnotes at the foot of a page are numbered lists that look like standards; the list of practices is repeated, titles only, in a box in every grade overview; one grade 4 standard is printed without the period after its number. The PDF prints only the number and letter under a domain code heading and never a full dotted code.

Reuse: published on the department's website, whose copyright statement (read 2026-10-01; the page says it was last reviewed August 12, 2025) requires permission for any reproduction. No permission has been asked, so the wording is not committed. The document's own page ii also reserves all rights and does not authorise reproduction for resale.
