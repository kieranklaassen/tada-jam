A young-child card of the curriculum institute: one A3 page of aims for the first years of primary school (fase 1). 97 bullets under six heading bars. All are recorded, in fase 1 mathematics.

Standing: SLO, "Doelen jonge kind" (31 July 2023), presents the cards as an overview of what can be offered in the first years of primary school. Guidance of the curriculum institute, not law.

The statements are drawn as images: only the headings and the bullets are in the text layer, so a script cannot extract the wording. It is read by on-device text recognition of the rendered page and checked by eye against the page, and the lane's frame says so. The card prints no codes: a statement is named by its heading bar, its sub-heading and its position.

Other renditions: none of the card itself. It restates the fase 1 goals of the open data in its own grouping and partly in its own words: measured on the recognised text, 52 of its 97 statements have the same text as a fase 1 goal of the open data, 24 a close one and 21 none. Where the text is the same, that goal is the statement's second rendition.
