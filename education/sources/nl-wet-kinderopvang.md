The childcare act. One statement is recorded: artikel 1.49, eerste lid, the sentence that defines responsible childcare (verantwoorde kinderopvang) by four aims.

Standing: the article is a duty of the holder of a childcare centre, set by law.

Read as XML: every article, annex and division carries its path in the attribute bwb-ng-variabel-deel, and a statement is found by that path. The publication data (meta-data, jcis) is not part of the text. The path of the paragraph is /Hoofdstuk1/Afdeling3/Paragraaf2/Artikel1.49/Lid1. Artikel 1.50 is about staff and holds no aims.
