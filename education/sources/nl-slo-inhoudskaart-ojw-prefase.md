A young-child card of the curriculum institute: one A3 page of aims for children in childcare before school (peuters). 53 bullets under six heading bars. 20 are recorded in peuters science and 17 in peuters practical life and feelings; 16, on society, geography and history, are outside the four subjects. One of the 17 in practical life and feelings stands under a geography heading ("Inrichting en indeling van de ruimte", bullet 2) and is about the care for the environment.

Standing: SLO, "Doelen jonge kind" (31 July 2023), says of the cards for peuters that their aims give a free view of what a childcare worker might attend to. Guidance of the curriculum institute, not law.

The statements are drawn as images: only the headings and the bullets are in the text layer, so a script cannot extract the wording. It is read by on-device text recognition of the rendered page and checked by eye against the page, and the lane's frame says so. The card prints no codes: a statement is named by its heading bar, its sub-heading and its position.

Other renditions: none. Nothing of this card is in the open data.
