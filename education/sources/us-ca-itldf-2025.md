The infant-toddler learning and development foundations, second edition, developed by WestEd for the department: 34 foundations in five domains, each with indicators for three age periods. It is the canonical rendition, and the only one, for every infant-toddler lane.

Standing: the publishing page (PDF page 3) says the Department of Social Services published it.

Read with pdftotext in plain mode. Each foundation opens on its own page: its heading, its general statement, a pointer to the first four months, the three column headers and then the three indicators in that order, each its own paragraph; the indicator for 23 through 36 months is the third. The examples below interleave across the three columns. Layout mode sets the three columns side by side.

Artefacts: plain mode drops line-break hyphens; 24 control characters, one before each "(continued)" heading; the examples hold several scripts; the contents page lists strands only and names one strand differently from the body ("Executive Functions" for "Executive Functioning"); one page misspells a foundation title. The introduction (PDF pages 31 to 35) restates every foundation's general statement, which gives the statements, not the indicators, a second printing in the same file.

Reuse: the publishing page prints the copyright notice quoted above and no licence or permission statement, so the wording is not committed. No landing page was found; the link is the direct link to the file.
