One inhoudslijn of the curriculum institute as published on slo.nl: 4 A3 pages with the goals of the line in three columns, fase 1, fase 2 and fase 3. It is the check rendition of the goals of the line NE/LE in the open data (nl-slo-curriculum-inhoudslijnen), which is the canonical rendition.

Standing: SLO, "Opbouw van de inhoudslijnen" (8 August 2024), says the core goals and the reference levels are the legal frame, and that these goals say what a school can offer, not what a child must know. Guidance of the curriculum institute, written for the 2006 core goals.

Read by column with pdftotext -bbox: the three columns nearly touch, and plain mode mixes them. A label column on the left names the clusters. A statement opens with a dash. The PDF prints no codes.

Differences from the open data: the data keeps the examples in brackets that the PDF prints, and the PDF prints sub-points with another mark. The grouping is the same; three heading bars add "(vanaf groep 3)", which the titles of the data lack. After the goals of "Oriëntatie op en lezen van zakelijke teksten" (page 1) and of "Oriëntatie op en lezen van fictie" (page 3) the PDF also prints, per fase column, what the texts of the fase are like (teksten en tekstkenmerken); that is no goal and is not in the data, and the frames of the reading and language lanes quote it.
