# California (`us-ca`), ages 9 to 12: grades 4, 5 and 6

This map is the pack's own text, not official text. The records it names are read before a claim rests on them.

## Ages and levels

The lookup reads `education/ages.ts`. At every age here the mapping is `derived` (from the first-grade entry rule: typical ages, not law), no sub-band is printed, and the subject's `cross-grade` lane is returned beside the grade, labelled cross-grade.

- Age 9: `grade-4`. Grade 3 is not in the pack, and the lookup says so.
- Age 10: `grade-4` and `grade-5`.
- Age 11: `grade-5` and `grade-6`.
- Age 12: `grade-6`. Grade 7 is not in the pack, and the lookup says so.

Age is a hint: it may set a default and never gates content.

## What each subject holds

Every record here is a State Board-adopted content standard (`state-board-adopted-standard`), except the competencies below.

**Mathematics** (Common Core State Standards: Mathematics): 34 records in grade 4, 35 in grade 5 and 42 in grade 6, five domains each. Grade 4: place value, multi-digit multiplication and division, fractions and decimals. Grade 5: fractions whose denominators differ, decimals, volume. Grade 6: ratios and rates, negative numbers, expressions and equations, area, data. `cross-grade/mathematics`: the eight Standards for Mathematical Practice, which set no grade content.

**Reading and language** (Common Core State Standards: English Language Arts and Literacy): 43 records each in grades 4 and 5, in six strands. Grade 6 has 70 in two parts: 41 for English language arts, with no foundational skills strand, and 29 for literacy in history/social studies, science and technical subjects, written for the band of grades 6 to 8. `cross-grade/reading-language`: the 32 anchor standards.

**Science** (California's Next Generation Science Standards): one record per performance expectation, with its clarification and assessment boundary. 14 in grade 4 (energy, waves, living structures, the Earth's surface), 13 in grade 5 (matter, living things, Earth's systems, sun and stars) and 19 in grade 6. Grade 6 follows the integrated course model: middle school codes carry no grade, and the lane holds the expectations that model places in grade 6, the band's four engineering design expectations among them. `cross-grade/science`: six engineering design expectations, three each for the K-2 and 3-5 bands, none for grade 6.

**Practical life and feelings** (health education standards). Grade 4, 85 records: Alcohol, Tobacco, and Other Drugs; Injury, Prevention, and Safety; Nutrition and Physical Activity. Grade 5, 69 records: Growth, Development, and Sexual Health; Nutrition and Physical Activity; Personal and Community Health. Grade 6, 76 records: the first two areas of grade 4, and Mental, Emotional, and Social Health. Growth, Development, and Sexual Health; Alcohol, Tobacco, and Other Drugs; Injury, Prevention, and Safety are sensitive: a design that needs them reads each record's Limits and `no-attainment-claims.md` first. `cross-grade/practical-life-feelings`: the eight overarching health standards, and 114 Transformative Social and Emotional Learning competencies (`voluntary-guidance`), 38 per band: Early Elementary, Late Elementary, Middle School. The document ties its bands to no grades, as every Late Elementary record's Limits says, so the band a plan uses is its own choice.

## Limits the frameworks state

Each limit is in the Limits of the record named. In mathematics that section sometimes also reports a cluster heading or footnote.

- Grade 4 whole numbers: up to and including 1,000,000 (`edu.us-ca.grade-4.mathematics.objective.4-nbt-1`).
- Grade 4 multiplication: at most four digits by one, or two by two (`edu.us-ca.grade-4.mathematics.objective.4-nbt-5`). Division: at most four digits by one in grade 4 (`edu.us-ca.grade-4.mathematics.objective.4-nbt-6`), by two in grade 5 (`edu.us-ca.grade-5.mathematics.objective.5-nbt-6`).
- Grade 4 fraction denominators: 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, then 8, 10 and 12, and also 100 (`edu.us-ca.grade-4.mathematics.objective.4-nf-1`). The grade 5 statement gives no list (`edu.us-ca.grade-5.mathematics.objective.5-nf-1`).
- Dividing fractions: in grade 5 only a unit fraction by a whole number, or the reverse (`edu.us-ca.grade-5.mathematics.objective.5-nf-7-c`); a fraction by a fraction in grade 6 (`edu.us-ca.grade-6.mathematics.objective.6-ns-1`).
- Decimals: tenths and hundredths in grade 4 (`edu.us-ca.grade-4.mathematics.objective.4-nf-6`); calculation to hundredths in grade 5 (`edu.us-ca.grade-5.mathematics.objective.5-nbt-7`).
- Unit rates: non-complex fractions only (`edu.us-ca.grade-6.mathematics.objective.6-rp-3-b`).
- Equations: x + p = q and px = q, with non-negative rational numbers (`edu.us-ca.grade-6.mathematics.objective.6-ee-7`).
- Volume and area: right rectangular prisms with whole-number edges in grade 5 (`edu.us-ca.grade-5.mathematics.objective.5-md-5-b`), fraction edges in grade 6 (`edu.us-ca.grade-6.mathematics.objective.6-g-2`). Grade 6 area names no formula and no circles (`edu.us-ca.grade-6.mathematics.objective.6-g-1`).
- Data: the named measures are median, mean, interquartile range and mean absolute deviation (`edu.us-ca.grade-6.mathematics.objective.6-sp-5-c`).
- Reading: no word list (`edu.us-ca.grade-4.reading-language.objective.l-4-4`), no words per minute (`edu.us-ca.grade-5.reading-language.objective.rf-5-4`), and no separate grade 6 level in a band record (`edu.us-ca.grade-6.reading-language.objective.rst-6-8-7`).
- Science: the assessment boundary leaves out density (`edu.us-ca.grade-5.science.objective.5-ps1-3`).
- Health: the food label record asks for no calculation (`edu.us-ca.grade-4.practical-life-feelings.objective.4-3-2-n`); the competency lists no emotion words beyond stress and anxiety (`edu.us-ca.cross-grade.practical-life-feelings.objective.late-elementary-1-b-2`).

## What the pack does not hold here

- Grade 3 and grade 7.
- Arts, career technical education, computer science, English language development, history–social science, physical education, school library and world languages.
- Science for grades 6 to 8 in the discipline-specific course model.
- Skipped placeholder rows: `RL.4.8`, `RL.5.8`, `RL.6.8`, `WHST.6-8.3`.
- The High School and Adult bands of the competencies.
- Confirmation of every science record: 4 of 14 in grade 4, 5 of 13 in grade 5 and 3 of 19 in grade 6 are `unconfirmed (wording-differs)`, each for a real difference between two official renditions that the verdict's note in `education/reviews/us-ca/<grade>/science.json` describes.
