## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child trusts that they can learn, and holds that they are able and that they matter as a member of their community. The child practises taking an optimistic view.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) says "I can't do it yet" and tries the zip, the word or the cartwheel again. They do a class job that others depend on, such as feeding the fish, and say why it matters. When something goes wrong they say one thing that could go better next time.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. This is a belief and a habit of thought, not a skill that is shown once. The optimistic outlook is something the child practises; the statement does not say they hold it all the time.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. After one failure many children say "I am bad at this" and treat it as fixed. Others say they can already do everything, which is not the same as believing they can learn.
