## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child knows what feedback is for. The child is able to listen to feedback that is simple and meant to help, and to accept it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) shows a block building and hears "the roof falls because the wall is thin; try a wider wall". They look at the speaker, wait until the end, and try the change. Asked why the teacher said it, they answer "so my building gets better".

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The feedback is simple and constructive. The statement is about receiving feedback and does not mention giving it.

### Common mistakes

Children hear feedback on the work as "you are bad at this" or "she does not like me". They argue or explain before the other person has finished. They say "okay" and change nothing.
