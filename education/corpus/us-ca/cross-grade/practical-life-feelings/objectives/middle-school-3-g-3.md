## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Middle School band the student sees correctly where things are unfair and what a community needs. Working with peers and with adults, the student acts on real problems so that the community becomes fairer, healthier, more caring and more inclusive.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young person in the Middle School band, about 11 to 14 years old (the document itself names no ages), surveys their neighbourhood or school and records a need with evidence, such as a street with no safe crossing or students who have no lunch. With classmates and a teacher or local organiser, they plan and carry out something real: a letter to the council, a food collection, a presentation to the school board. They check afterwards what changed.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. Recognition is to be accurate. Action is taken together with both adults and peers, and on issues from the real world. The statement names no issue, no scale and no form of action.

### Common mistakes

Young people often decide what a community needs without asking the people concerned. Many choose a problem too large to act on and do no more than raise awareness, for example with a poster. Some treat one event as having solved the problem and do not check the result.
