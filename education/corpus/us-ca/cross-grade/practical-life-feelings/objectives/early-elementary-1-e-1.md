## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child works out how they could be of help to other people, and then does it, in school, at home and in other places.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) carries the snack basket, holds the door, shows a new classmate where the coats go, or sets the table at home. Asked "how could you help here?", they look around, name one job, and do it.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The statement names school and home and leaves the other settings open. It sets no kind or size of help.

### Common mistakes

Children help by taking over: they do the puzzle for the other child, who did not ask. They name help only for adults' jobs and do not see that a classmate needs something. They say they would help and then do not notice the moment to do it.
