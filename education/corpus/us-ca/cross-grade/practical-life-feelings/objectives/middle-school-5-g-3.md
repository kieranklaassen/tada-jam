## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Middle School band the student foresees what will most probably follow from what they and other people do. The student picks out how a choice affects their goals, near or further off, and how it affects the wider community, the country and the planet.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young person in the Middle School band, about 11 to 14 years old (the document itself names no ages), says aloud before acting, for example before skipping practice, posting a photo of a friend or buying a cheap item, what is most likely to happen next and after that. They draw a chain from one choice to its effect on a goal for this month and a goal for next year. They follow a second chain outward: from one purchase or one habit to their town, their country and the environment.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. The consequences asked for are the most likely ones, not every possible one. Both the student's own actions and other people's are covered. Effects are named at several scales: the planet, the country, the wider community and the student's own goals, whether near or further off.

### Common mistakes

Young people often see the immediate result of a choice and not the one a month later. Many imagine the best or the worst outcome instead of the most likely one. Effects on people they do not know, or on the environment, are commonly left out.
