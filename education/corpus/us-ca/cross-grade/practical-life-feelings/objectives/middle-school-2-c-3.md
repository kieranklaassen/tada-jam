## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Middle School band the student thinks about different ways of getting through hard times, both those that happen to one person and those a group goes through together. The student reflects on where their own inner strength and hope come from, and on what they want from a relationship that supports them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young person in the Middle School band, about 11 to 14 years old (the document itself names no ages), reads or hears how different people came through a hardship, such as an illness, a move or a flood that hit a whole town, and compares what each did. They name what helped them continue in a hard time of their own: a belief, a person, music, a memory. They list what they want from someone they go to for support, such as listening without judging or keeping a confidence.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. The verbs are "consider" and "reflect": the statement asks for thinking, and does not require the student to have overcome anything. It covers hardship faced alone and hardship shared by a group. It names no kind of adversity and no particular source of strength.

### Common mistakes

Young people often think there is one right way to cope and judge those who cope differently. Many believe needing support shows weakness. Some can name who helps them but have not thought about what that person does that helps.
