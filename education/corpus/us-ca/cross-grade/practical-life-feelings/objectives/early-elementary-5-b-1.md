## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child acts fairly and honestly, and can explain why relationships need both. The child explores ethical, caring things that can be done for the planet.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) says "I spilled it" when asked, gives back the extra card they were dealt, and shares out the pieces evenly. They say what happens between friends when one lies or cheats: "then she will not believe me next time." They water a plant, pick up litter in the yard, turn off a tap, and talk about why.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. For the planet the verb is "explore": no particular actions or knowledge are set. The statement asks the child to explain why honesty and fairness matter to relationships, not to define either.

### Common mistakes

Children tell the truth when it costs nothing and deny what they did when they expect trouble. They think a rule of the game is fair when it helps them. They explain honesty by punishment ("or you get told off") and not by trust between people.
