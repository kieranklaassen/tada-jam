## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child talks about how they themselves, other people, and various cultural and social groups are alike and how they differ.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) stands next to a classmate and says "we both have a baby brother; I speak Spanish at home and you speak Tagalog". In a circle the children talk about what different families eat, wear or celebrate, and say what is the same in all of them.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The statement asks for discussion, not for a judgement about any group. It names no groups.

### Common mistakes

Children treat their own way as the normal one and the other way as strange or wrong. They assume one child stands for a whole group ("all people from there do that"). They see differences that show, such as skin or clothes, and miss the likenesses and the differences that do not show.
