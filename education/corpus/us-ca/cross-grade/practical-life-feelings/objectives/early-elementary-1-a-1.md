## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child can say what they are like and what has happened in their life, and their simple picture of who they are grows more accurate. The child knows what they like and what they do not like.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) draws themselves and says three things about the drawing: "I have curly hair, I am a big sister, I moved here last year." They sort pictures of foods or activities into a "like" pile and a "do not like" pile and say why one card went where it did.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The sense of self asked for is a basic one that is becoming more accurate, not a finished one. The statement lists no particular characteristics or experiences.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age describe themselves by what can be seen or owned ("I have a bike") and leave out what they are like inside. They rate themselves as good at everything. They copy a friend's likes and dislikes and say them as their own.
