## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This is the eighth of the eight overarching health standards, which hold for all students in every grade. Every student is to show that they can encourage good health and help keep it up, for themselves, in their family and in their community.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young child (about 5 to 7) reminds a little brother to wash hands, asks the family to go for a walk, and makes a drawing that tells classmates to drink water. An older child (about 9 to 12) makes a poster or gives a short talk about sun safety, helps plan a class clean-up, and encourages a friend to tell an adult about a problem.

### Limits

This overarching standard is State Board-adopted, holds for every grade and sets no grade-specific content; each grade's own health standards give it. It names whose health is meant (the child's own, the family's, the community's) and no topics.

### Common mistakes

Children think health is each person's own business and that telling others is bossy. A young child tells others the rule ("you must") without giving a reason, and the others do not listen. Children leave out the community and think only of home.
