## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This is the sixth of the eight overarching health standards, which hold for all students in every grade. It is about aims: every student is to show that they can apply the skills of setting goals so that health improves.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young child (about 5 to 7) picks one small thing to do for their health this week, such as brushing teeth each night or drinking water at lunch, and puts a mark on a chart each time. An older child (about 9 to 12) writes down a goal with a number and a date, such as running three times this week, breaks it into steps, and checks at the end what was done.

### Limits

This overarching standard is State Board-adopted, holds for every grade and sets no grade-specific content; each grade's own health standards give it. It sets no length of time for a goal and names no kinds of goal.

### Common mistakes

Young children name a wish ("be strong") and not something they can do. Children choose a goal too large or too far off to check, and drop it after the first missed day.
