## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Middle School band the student's understanding of critical thinking grows deeper, as does their understanding of how to judge and decide with discernment. The student applies these skills to messages from the media and to sources found online.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young person in the Middle School band, about 11 to 14 years old (the document itself names no ages), takes a viral post, an advertisement and a news article on one topic and asks of each: who made this, why, what evidence is given, and what is left out. They look up a claim in a second and third source before repeating it. They explain a decision, such as whether to share a video, by the reasons for and against.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. The word "deepen" assumes some earlier understanding and sets no end point. Two fields of use are named: media messages and online sources. No method for checking a source is named.

### Common mistakes

Young people often judge a source by how professional it looks or how many people have shared it. Many accept a claim that fits what they already believe and check only the ones they dislike. Some take critical thinking to mean doubting everything, and so trust nothing or treat all sources as equal.
