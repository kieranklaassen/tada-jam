## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child puts into words what they believe about matters that count for the child personally, for the child's family, and for the community where the child learns.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of about ten finishes the sentence "I believe ... because ..." about something they care about, such as how animals are treated or whether everyone gets a turn. The child explains a belief their family holds, such as eating together or caring for grandparents, and one the class shares, such as listening when someone speaks. The child says it aloud, writes it, or draws it for others. The document names no ages for this band; the child described here is in the later primary years, about 8 to 11.

### Limits

This statement belongs to the Late Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The statement asks the child to state beliefs; it does not ask the child to defend them against objection or to change anyone's mind. It names three circles the topics matter to (the child, the family, the learning community) and no particular topics. This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard.

### Common mistakes

Children state a preference ("I like dogs") when asked for a belief. Many repeat a parent's or friend's view without being able to give a reason of their own. Some assume everyone at home and in class believes the same and are surprised by a different view.
