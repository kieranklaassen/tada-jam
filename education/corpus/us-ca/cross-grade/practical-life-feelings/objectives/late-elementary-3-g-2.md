## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child looks into why taking part in a community and serving it matters, and what it can achieve. Working together with other people, the child finds ways to add something useful to the community where the child learns.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of about ten helps at a school garden day or a food collection and afterwards says what changed because people came. In a class meeting the child and classmates list jobs the school needs (reading with younger pupils, sorting the lost property, welcoming new children) and choose one to take on. The document names no ages for this band; the child described here is in the later primary years, about 8 to 11.

### Limits

This statement belongs to the Late Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The verb for community participation and service is "explore", so no amount of service is required. The second part is about finding ways to contribute, together with others, and is tied to the learning community. No activities are named. This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard.

### Common mistakes

Children see service as a single event done for a reward or praise. Many suggest what adults should fix and not what they can do themselves. Some pick a project they like without asking what the community needs.
