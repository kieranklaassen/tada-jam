## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child can point out the relationships and the situations in which they grow calm. The child understands how to keep their body healthy and starts doing so, in the ways they themselves can control.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) says "I feel calm when I read with Grandpa" or "in the book corner", and goes to that place or person when upset. They do the health actions that are theirs to do: drink water, wash hands, brush teeth, run at break.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The child begins to practise; steady habits are not claimed. Physical health is limited to what is within the child's own control, which leaves out what adults decide for them.

### Common mistakes

Children name what they enjoy most (a loud game, a screen) as what calms them. They hold themselves responsible for things adults control, such as what is for dinner, or think nothing about their health is theirs to decide.
