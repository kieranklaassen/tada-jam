## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child, when supported, can say what will probably follow from something they do. The child explores cause and effect, and explores how a choice can affect much more than the person who makes it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) is asked "what will happen if you leave the lid off the paint?" and answers "it will dry out and nobody can paint tomorrow". They push the first domino and watch the row fall. With an adult they follow one choice outward: "I dropped the wrapper, the wind took it, it went into the pond, the duck could eat it."

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. Naming consequences is done with support. For cause and effect and for wider impacts the verb is "explore": no set of examples or level of understanding is given.

### Common mistakes

Children name only what happens to themselves ("I will get told off") and not what happens to others. They see the next step and not the one after it. They take an outcome they did not intend as not their doing ("I did not mean to").
