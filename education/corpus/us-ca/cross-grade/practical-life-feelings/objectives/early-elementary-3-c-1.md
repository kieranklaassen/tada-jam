## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child takes part in making shared agreements that steer how they deal with other people and with their surroundings, and in keeping those agreements up. The child is kind to people whatever their background and whatever they have lived through.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) sits in the circle while the class makes its agreements, offers one ("we walk indoors", "we put the blocks back"), and puts a handprint or name under the list. Later they point to the list to remind a friend, and keep to it themselves. They share and speak kindly with every classmate, not only with their friends.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The child contributes; the statement does not say the child makes or enforces agreements alone. It gives no content for the agreements.

### Common mistakes

Children treat an agreement as the teacher's rule and follow it only when watched. They use it to correct others and do not apply it to themselves. Their kindness goes to friends and to children like themselves, and to nobody else.
