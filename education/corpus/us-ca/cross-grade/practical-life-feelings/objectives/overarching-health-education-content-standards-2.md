## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This is the second of the eight overarching health standards, which hold for all students in every grade. Every student is to show that they can examine what shapes health, both what comes from inside a person and what comes from outside.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young child (about 5 to 7) says who helps them stay healthy at home, and notices that they want a snack after seeing it in an advertisement or in a friend's hand. An older child (about 9 to 12) lists what led to a choice, such as being tired, a friend's dare or an advertisement, and sorts the list into what came from inside them and what came from outside.

### Limits

This overarching standard is State Board-adopted, holds for every grade and sets no grade-specific content; each grade's own health standards give it. It does not list which inside or outside influences are meant.

### Common mistakes

Children see the outside influence and miss the inside one: they say "my friend made me" and leave out being hungry, bored or curious. Young children take an advertisement as plain information and do not see that it is trying to sell.
