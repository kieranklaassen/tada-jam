## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Middle School band the student understands what ethics is and why it matters, and is able to use it in their own life and in their learning. The student also understands the part they and other people play in using the earth's resources ethically.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young person in the Middle School band, about 11 to 14 years old (the document itself names no ages), works through real cases: whether to copy a friend's homework, what to do with money found in the corridor, whether to credit a source. They give a reason for their choice beyond "I would get caught". They track what their household or class uses in a week, such as water, paper, food and electricity, and say what they, their school and companies or governments could each do about it.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. Application is named for two settings: personal and learning. The statement names no ethical theory or rule. For the planet's resources it asks for understanding of roles, the student's own and other people's; it names no resource and no action.

### Common mistakes

Young people often equate ethics with rules and with not being caught. Many see cheating on schoolwork as harmless when "everyone does it". On resources, some think their own actions make no difference at all, and others think individual actions are the whole answer and overlook what larger actors do.
