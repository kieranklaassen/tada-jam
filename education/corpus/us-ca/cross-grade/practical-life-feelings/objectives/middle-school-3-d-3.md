## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Middle School band the student knows the right time and place to go, of their own accord, for help and resources. The student is starting to grasp that the people in a community depend on one another.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young person in the Middle School band, about 11 to 14 years old (the document itself names no ages), goes to the counsellor, the librarian, a tutor or a trusted adult before a problem has grown, and knows which of them deals with what. They name whom they would ask for help with schoolwork, a health worry or trouble with a friend. They trace who depends on whom in their school day: the bus driver, the kitchen staff, the classmate who shares notes, and the people who rely on them.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. Seeking help is described as proactive, meaning before being told to. Understanding interdependence is only begun at this band. No particular resources or supports are named.

### Common mistakes

Young people often wait until a problem is serious before asking, or think asking shows weakness. Many know one source of help, usually a parent or a friend, and take every kind of problem there. Few have thought about who depends on them.
