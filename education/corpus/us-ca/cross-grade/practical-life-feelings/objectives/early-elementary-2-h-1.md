## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child, with guidance, keeps order in the places they use and among their own things.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) hangs their coat on their own hook, puts the lunch box on the shelf and the pencils back in the case, and clears the table before the next activity. An adult points, reminds, or shows a picture of where things go.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The child does this with guidance, not alone. The statement does not say which spaces or belongings, or how tidy.

### Common mistakes

Children put everything in one pile or one drawer and call it tidy. They tidy when told and do not see the mess themselves. They cannot find a thing later because it has no fixed place.
