## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Middle School band the student is curious on purpose and practises it, and meets diverse groups with an open mind. The student looks for experiences that are new to them and that they can grow from.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young person in the Middle School band, about 11 to 14 years old (the document itself names no ages), sits with a group they do not usually join and asks questions about what its members do and care about, listening before giving an opinion. They sign up for something unfamiliar, such as a debate club, a new sport or a community event of another culture. Afterwards they say one thing they learned and one idea of theirs that changed.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. Curiosity is described as a deliberate practice, not a trait. The statement names no groups and no kinds of experience. The new experiences are ones the student can grow from; it sets no number of them.

### Common mistakes

Young people often stay with the same friends and activities because trying something new in front of peers feels risky. Some ask questions of a person from another group that treat them as an oddity, not as a person. Many decide what a group is like before meeting it and look only for what confirms that.
