## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child picks out goals of their own and goals shared with a group, and names simple steps for reaching them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) says "I want to learn to ride without training wheels" and says what comes first: get the helmet, practise on the grass, ask Dad to hold the seat. With the class they agree on a shared goal, such as a tidy reading corner, and each child says one thing they will do for it.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The steps are simple ones. The statement asks the child to pick out goals and to name steps; it does not say the goals must be reached, and it sets no time span.

### Common mistakes

Children name a wish with no action in it ("be the best"). They give the goal as its own step ("I will just do it"). In a group goal they say what others should do and leave out their own part.
