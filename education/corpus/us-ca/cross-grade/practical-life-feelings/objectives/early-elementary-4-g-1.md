## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child makes known what they think, what they need and what they want, in a way that does no harm to other people.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) says "I want a turn, please", "I need help with my zip" or "I have an idea: the bridge could go here", in a speaking voice and at a moment when the other can listen. They wait for a pause, raise a hand in the group, and use words in place of grabbing or pushing.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The statement does not say how the child communicates (speech, gesture, drawing) and does not define a negative impact.

### Common mistakes

Children get what they want by grabbing, shouting or crying. Others say nothing and their need stays unmet. They interrupt, or say what they want as an order ("give it"), and the other child refuses.
