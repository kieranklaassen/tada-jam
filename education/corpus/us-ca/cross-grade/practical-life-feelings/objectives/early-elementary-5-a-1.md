## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child shows that they are curious about people and situations they have not met before, and open to them. New experiences interest the child.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) watches a new classmate, then goes over and asks their name or what they like to play. They try an unfamiliar game, food or tool after watching for a while, and ask "what is that?" and "can I try?"

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. This is a disposition, not a skill with a level. The statement does not say the child must like or join every new thing.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Many children of this age stay back from new people and new things at first and need time to watch; that is not the same as a lack of interest. Others say "I don't like it" before trying.
