## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

What they do, when they do it and what they are responsible for: in the Middle School band the student takes an active part in organising all three.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young person in the Middle School band, about 11 to 14 years old (the document itself names no ages), keeps a planner or calendar and writes in tests, practices and chores themselves. They pack their own bag the evening before and tell a parent in advance about a clash between a match and a family event. They notice on Wednesday that an assignment is due on Friday and decide when to do it.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. The wording speaks of an active role; it does not say the student manages these alone. It names three things to manage: the student's time, their activities and their responsibilities. It names no tool or method and sets no amount.

### Common mistakes

Young people commonly underestimate how long a task takes and start too late. Many rely on a parent's reminders and do not see that as someone else managing their time. Some write a plan and do not look at it again.
