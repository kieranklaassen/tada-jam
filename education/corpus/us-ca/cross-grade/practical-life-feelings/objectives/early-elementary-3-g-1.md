## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child can say how they could take part in communities and groups or add something to them. The child can also say how they could treat people who belong to other communities or groups with respect, compassion, fairness and helpfulness.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) says what they can do in their class, family or team: "I can water the plants", "I can cheer for the others". Asked about children from another class or another school, they say what they could do: share the playground, invite them into a game, listen when they talk.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The statement asks the child to name ways; doing them is not stated here. It names no groups.

### Common mistakes

Children name help only for their own group and see another class or team as rivals. They name very large deeds ("I will give everyone a house") and no small one they can do. They say "be nice" and cannot say what that looks like.
