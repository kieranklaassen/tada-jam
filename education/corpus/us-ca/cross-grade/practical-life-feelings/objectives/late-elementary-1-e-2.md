## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child describes the part they play and what they add in each of the settings they take part in. The child picks out traits and habits of their own, and how these could affect other people in the community where the child learns.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of about ten lists the places they belong to (home, class, a team, a club) and beside each writes what they do there: set the table, keep score, help a younger reader. The child names a habit of their own, such as humming while working or always tidying the shelf, and asks a table partner how it affects them. The document names no ages for this band; the child described here is in the later primary years, about 8 to 11.

### Limits

This statement belongs to the Late Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The statement names no particular settings or roles. The effect of traits and habits is tied to the learning community only, and the wording says they "may" affect others, so no fixed effect is claimed. This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard.

### Common mistakes

Children name a role only where they hold a title (captain, line leader) and overlook everyday contributions. Many list only good traits, or only faults. Children often do not notice that a habit that does not bother them (talking while thinking, tapping) bothers someone nearby.
