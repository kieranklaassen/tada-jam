## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This is the first of the eight overarching health standards, which hold for all students in every grade. It is about knowledge: every student is to understand the core ideas behind improving health.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young child (about 5 to 7) names the parts of the body they wash, says that food and sleep help them grow, and points to foods they eat every day. An older child (about 9 to 12) explains in their own words why the body needs water, sleep and movement, and what a germ does.

### Limits

This overarching standard is State Board-adopted, holds for every grade and sets no grade-specific content; each grade's own health standards give it. It names no topics or facts. It asks for understanding, where standard 7 asks for doing.

### Common mistakes

A young child can repeat a health rule without knowing the reason for it. Children often think a healthy body is one that is not ill at the moment, and leave out sleep, feelings and movement.
