## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In the Early Elementary band, a child practises, within the groups of the setting where they learn, how to lead and how to add to the group's work.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in the early primary years (about 5 to 8; the document itself names no ages) takes a turn as line leader or table helper, hands out the materials, and tells the group what comes next. On another day they follow someone else's lead and add an idea or do a share of the job.

### Limits

This is voluntary guidance, not an adopted standard. It belongs to the Early Elementary band, which the document ties to no grades. The child practises; the statement does not claim skilled leadership. The groups are those of the learning setting; other groups are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Children take leading to mean giving orders and going first. A child who is not the leader stops taking part. They do not see quiet contributions, such as listening or fetching what is needed, as helping the group.
