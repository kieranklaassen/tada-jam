## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Across kindergarten to grade 2, the child looks at test results for two objects that were both made to solve one problem, and uses them to compare what each object does well and what it does poorly.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in kindergarten to grade 2 tests two paper boats by adding pennies to each until it sinks, and tallies the count for each boat. The child also notes which boat tipped over more easily. The child says what each boat was better at and worse at, pointing to the tallies.

### Limits

The expectation holds for the whole band from kindergarten to grade 2, not for one grade. It carries no clarification statement and no assessment boundary. The comparison is between two objects made for one problem, and it is based on data from tests.

### Common mistakes

Declaring one object the winner and saying nothing about what the other did better. Judging by which looks nicer or which the child made. Testing the two objects in different ways, so the results cannot be compared.
