## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Across grades 3 to 5, the student plans fair tests of a model or prototype and runs them, keeping the variables under control and looking at where the design fails, in order to find what about it could be made better.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A student in grades 3 to 5 loads a paper bridge with coins one at a time, using the same span and the same coins for every design, and counts until it collapses. The student looks closely at where it bent or tore. The student writes down which part to strengthen in the next version.

### Limits

The expectation holds for the whole band of grades 3 to 5, not for one grade. It carries no clarification statement and no assessment boundary. The tests are fair tests with controlled variables, and their aim is to find what to improve.

### Common mistakes

Changing more than one thing between tests, so the test is not fair. Treating a failure as the end instead of as information about where to improve. Testing once and trusting the single result.
