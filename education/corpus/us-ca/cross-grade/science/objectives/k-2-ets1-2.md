## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Across kindergarten to grade 2, the child makes a simple drawing, sketch or physical model that shows how an object's shape helps it do the job a given problem calls for.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in kindergarten to grade 2 draws a scoop for moving sand and shows its curved bowl, or shapes one from clay or card. The child points to the part of the shape that does the work and says what it does: "the sides stop the sand falling out". The child compares it with a flat piece that does not hold sand.

### Limits

The expectation holds for the whole band from kindergarten to grade 2, not for one grade. It carries no clarification statement and no assessment boundary. The drawing or model is a simple one, and the problem is given to the child.

### Common mistakes

Drawing what the object looks like, with decoration, without showing how its shape helps. Making a model whose shape does not suit the job, such as a flat scoop. Saying what the object is for but not which part of the shape does it.
