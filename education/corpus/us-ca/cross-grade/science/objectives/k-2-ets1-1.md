## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Across kindergarten to grade 2, the child finds out about a situation that people would like to be different by asking questions, observing, and collecting information. From this the child states a simple problem, one that a new or better tool or object could solve.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in kindergarten to grade 2 watches classmates struggle to carry crayons to the table and asks them what goes wrong. The child looks at how the crayons spill and counts how many are dropped. The child then says the problem in one sentence: "We need a way to carry crayons without dropping them."

### Limits

The expectation holds for the whole band from kindergarten to grade 2, not for one grade. It carries no clarification statement and no assessment boundary. The problem is a simple one that an object or tool could solve. This expectation ends at defining the problem.

### Common mistakes

Naming a thing to make before finding out what the problem is. Stating a wish that no object or tool could solve. Asking no one and relying on a guess about what people want changed.
