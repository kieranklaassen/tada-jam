## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child writes clearly and so that the parts connect. The style, the organisation and the way the ideas are developed suit the audience, the purpose and the task.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young child (about 5 to 7) writes a birthday invitation that says who, when and where, because a guest needs those facts. An older child (about 9 to 12) writes a formal letter to the head teacher and a note to a friend about the same event, and changes the greeting, the length and the wording for each reader.

### Limits

This is an anchor standard that holds from kindergarten to grade 12: it sets no grade-specific content, and each grade's own standards give the detail. Three features (development, organisation, style) are to suit three things (task, purpose, audience). No text type and no length is named.

### Common mistakes

Young children leave out what the reader needs because they know it themselves. Older children write in the same style for every reader and task. Their ideas appear in the order they thought of them, so the piece is hard to follow.
