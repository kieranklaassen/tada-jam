## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child collects information that relates to the subject from several sources, digital as well as print. The child judges how accurate and how credible every one of them is, and works the information into their own writing without plagiarising.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young child (about 5 to 7) recalls what happened on a class walk, or finds the answer to a question in a book the teacher provides. An older child (about 9 to 12) gathers notes on sea turtles from a library book, a museum web page and a video, checks who made each and whether they agree, and writes the report in their own words with a list of the sources.

### Limits

This is an anchor standard that holds from kindergarten to grade 12: it sets no grade-specific content, and each grade's own standards give the detail. Sources are multiple, in print and digital form; no number is set. Each is to be judged for credibility and accuracy. The anchor names no citation format.

### Common mistakes

Young children report what they imagine instead of what the source says. Older children copy sentences and change a few words. They trust the first search result without asking who wrote it.
