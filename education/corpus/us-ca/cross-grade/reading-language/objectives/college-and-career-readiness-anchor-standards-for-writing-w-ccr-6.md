## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

With the help of technology, the Internet included, the child writes and publishes, and also works and communicates with other people.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young child (about 5 to 7), sitting with an adult, types a sentence on a tablet and adds it to a class book. An older child (about 9 to 12) drafts a report in a shared document, replies to a partner's comments in the margin, and posts the final version to the class page.

### Limits

This is an anchor standard that holds from kindergarten to grade 12: it sets no grade-specific content, and each grade's own standards give the detail. The Internet is named as one technology; no tool is named. Technology has two uses here: producing and publishing writing, and working with others. No keyboarding level is set.

### Common mistakes

Young children press keys at random or lose their work by closing it. Older children spend the time on fonts and layout instead of on the writing. In a shared document they overwrite a partner's text instead of commenting on it.
