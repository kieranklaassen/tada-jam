## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

For analysis, reflection and research, the child's support is evidence taken out of texts, which may be informational, literary or both.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young child (about 5 to 7) answers a question about a story by pointing to the page that shows the answer. An older child (about 9 to 12) writes that a character is generous and quotes two actions from the novel as evidence; in a science report the child supports a statement with a figure from an article.

### Limits

This is an anchor standard that holds from kindergarten to grade 12: it sets no grade-specific content, and each grade's own standards give the detail. The texts may be literary, informational or both. Three uses are named: analysis, reflection, research. No number of texts is set.

### Common mistakes

Young children answer from memory or from their own life, not from the text. Older children write their view with no evidence, or add a quotation without saying what it shows.
