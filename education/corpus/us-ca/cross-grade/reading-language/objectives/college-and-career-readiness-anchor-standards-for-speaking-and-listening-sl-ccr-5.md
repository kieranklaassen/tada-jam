## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In presentations, the child uses visual displays of data, and digital media, with a plan: to convey information and to make the presentation easier to understand.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A young child (about 5 to 7) adds a drawing to a description to show what a pet looks like. An older child (about 9 to 12) gives a talk on recycling, shows one bar chart of the class's weekly waste at the moment the numbers are mentioned, plays a ten-second recording where it explains a point, and leaves out slides that add nothing.

### Limits

This is an anchor standard that holds from kindergarten to grade 12: it sets no grade-specific content, and each grade's own standards give the detail. Two aids are named: visual displays of data, and digital media. Their use is to be strategic, that is, chosen for a purpose. No tool is named.

### Common mistakes

Young children draw a picture unrelated to what they are describing. Older children fill slides with text and read it out. They add images, sounds or effects for decoration, which take attention away from the point.
