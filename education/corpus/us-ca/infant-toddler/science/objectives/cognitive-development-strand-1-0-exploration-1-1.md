## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This foundation is about a child's growing grasp that doing one thing makes a second thing happen. By 23 to 36 months, children make simple predictions of what is going to happen, and they think back over why an event took place.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A two-year-old says "fall down" before pushing a tower of blocks, then pushes it. After water spills, the child looks at the tipped cup, points and says "I bumped it". The child presses a switch several times and watches the light each time.

### Limits

The predictions are called simple. The indicator does not say what they are about, and it names no materials or events. It does not state that a prediction has to be correct or that the child explains a cause in full sentences. It describes what children typically show at this age, not a requirement on a child.

### Common mistakes

Naming as the cause whatever happened just before, even when it is unrelated. Expecting the same result from every object, for example that everything dropped will bounce. Giving a wish as the reason ("because I want it") instead of a cause.
