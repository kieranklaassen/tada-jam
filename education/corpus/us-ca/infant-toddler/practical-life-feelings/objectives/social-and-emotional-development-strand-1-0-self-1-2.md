## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This foundation is about a child coming to see that his or her own actions can change the surroundings. By 23 to 36 months, children show they know what they are able to bring about, and at times they mention these abilities when they tell what they are like.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A two-year-old says "I do it" and pulls up a zip, or says "I make it go" and pushes the swing. The child also says "I big, I can jump" or "I strong" and shows the jump to an adult.

### Limits

Mentioning abilities when describing oneself happens only sometimes. The indicator names no abilities and does not state that the child's view of them is accurate. It describes what children typically show at this age, not a requirement on a child.

### Common mistakes

Claiming to be able to do things well beyond reach, such as lifting a heavy box or driving a car. Believing the child's own action caused an event that only happened at the same moment. Refusing all help, including for steps the child cannot yet do.
