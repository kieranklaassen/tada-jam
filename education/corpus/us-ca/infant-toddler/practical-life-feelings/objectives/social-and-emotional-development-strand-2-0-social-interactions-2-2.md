## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This foundation is about a child feeling along with what another person is feeling. By 23 to 36 months, children show that they know someone else's feelings can differ from theirs. They show concern for other people, and at times they answer what a person needs emotionally with something that could help that person feel better.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A two-year-old sees another child crying, stops playing, looks worried and says "he sad". The child brings the crying child that child's own blanket, pats the child's back, or fetches an adult.

### Limits

Responding in a helpful way happens only sometimes, and the indicator says the response might help, not that it does. It does not name the feelings involved. It describes what children typically show at this age, not a requirement on a child.

### Common mistakes

Offering what comforts the child, such as the child's own soft toy, instead of what comforts the other person. Becoming upset and crying too instead of helping. Watching closely without doing anything.
