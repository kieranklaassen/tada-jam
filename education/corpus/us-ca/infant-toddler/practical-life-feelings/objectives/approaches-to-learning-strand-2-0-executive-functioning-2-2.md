## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This foundation is about a child holding back urges and controlling his or her own actions. By 23 to 36 months, children say more exactly what they need or want and change what they are doing so as to hold back an urge or control their behaviour, with a caregiver guiding them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A two-year-old says "I want the red truck" instead of grabbing it or screaming. Reminded "wait for your turn", the child pulls a hand back and stands beside the other child. The child slows to a walk at the door when the adult says "walking feet".

### Limits

The indicator states caregiver guidance; it does not describe a child stopping an impulse alone. The words about guidance come last in the sentence, and it is open whether they cover the telling as well as the adjusting. No situations and no length of waiting are named. It describes what children typically show at this age, not a requirement on a child.

### Common mistakes

Saying the rule aloud ("no touch") while doing the thing anyway. Stopping when reminded and starting again a moment later. Waiting only while the adult is watching.
