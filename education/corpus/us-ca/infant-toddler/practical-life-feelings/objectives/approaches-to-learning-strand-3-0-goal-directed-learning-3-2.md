## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This foundation is about a child joining with other people to get something done. By 23 to 36 months, children play together and take part in joint activities that have a simple task or aim, and they do so more and more on their own.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two two-year-olds carry a large cushion to the reading corner, each holding one end. A two-year-old and another child take turns dropping blocks into a bucket until it is full, then tip it out together; an adult helps only when they pull in different directions.

### Limits

The goals or tasks are simple. Independence is increasing, not complete, so adult help at times still fits the indicator. It gives no group size and no length of time. It describes what children typically show at this age, not a requirement on a child.

### Common mistakes

Playing beside another child with the same materials without a shared aim. Taking over the whole task or all of the materials. Leaving partway when the other child does it differently.
