## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This foundation is about a child copying what other people do, say, sound like or gesture, straight away or after a delay. By 23 to 36 months, children copy language, gestures and actions that have more than one step, which they saw someone do earlier, in a different setting, or both.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A two-year-old picks up a toy phone, holds it to an ear, says "hello, yes, bye" in a parent's tone and puts it down. At childcare the child stirs an empty bowl, tastes from the spoon and blows on it, as seen in the kitchen at home the day before.

### Limits

The indicator covers copying after a delay, in a different setting, or both. "Multiple steps" is not given a number. It names gestures, language and actions, and it does not state that the copy is exact. It describes what children typically show at this age, not a requirement on a child.

### Common mistakes

Doing the steps in the wrong order or leaving out a middle step. Copying the visible movement without its purpose, such as wiping a table with an empty hand. Repeating words heard from adults at a moment where they do not fit.
