## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

This foundation is about a child responding to other children and joining in with them. By 23 to 36 months, children play cooperatively, in a simple way, with other children, around an activity or an aim they have in common.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two two-year-olds fill one bucket with sand together and tip it out, or move a wagon with one pulling and one pushing. One child holds the track piece while the other rolls the car down it, and then they swap.

### Limits

The cooperative play is simple and is built around one shared goal or activity. The indicator gives no number of children, no length of play and no rules to follow. It describes what children typically show at this age, not a requirement on a child.

### Common mistakes

Doing the same thing side by side, each with a separate pile, instead of one thing together. Wanting to do every part alone, so the other child has nothing to do. Ending the play by taking the shared object away.
