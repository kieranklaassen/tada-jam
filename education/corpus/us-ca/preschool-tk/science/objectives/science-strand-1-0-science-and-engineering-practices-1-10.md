## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

While playing and while investigating together with others, a child works out explanations and solutions and communicates them to others. At the earlier age (3 to 4½ years) these are simple. At the later age (4 to 5½ years) they are more precise and more detailed.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A younger child says "the boat sank because I put all the stones in", or "put a block under it" when the bridge sags. An older child says "it sank because the stones were all on one side, so it tipped and the water came in; spread them out", and shows a friend with the boat.

### Limits

The statement names no topics. Explaining, proposing a solution, and communicating these to others are all named; the setting is play and shared investigations. It does not say the explanation must be scientifically correct. What changes with age: from simple explanations and solutions to more detailed, more precise ones.

### Common mistakes

Explaining by describing again what happened ("it fell because it fell down"). Giving a cause that is about wishes ("it wanted to"). Having a solution in their hands but not putting it into words for the other children.
