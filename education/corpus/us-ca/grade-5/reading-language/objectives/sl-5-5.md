## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Where it suits the presentation, the child adds visual displays and multimedia components such as sound or graphics so that the main ideas or themes are developed more fully.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child prepares a talk on the water cycle and decides that a labelled diagram will help the listeners follow the steps. The child draws or finds the diagram, adds a short sound recording of rain if it helps a point, and points to the display at the moment the talk reaches it. The child leaves out a picture that has nothing to do with a main idea.

### Limits

Components are added when appropriate, so not every presentation needs them. Graphics and sound are examples of multimedia components. The purpose named is to develop main ideas or themes. The statement names no tools or software.

### Common mistakes

Children add pictures, sounds or effects because they like them, not because they support a main idea. They put the whole text of the talk on a slide and read it out. They show a chart and never refer to it.
