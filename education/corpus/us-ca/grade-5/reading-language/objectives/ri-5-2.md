## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child finds at least two main ideas in a text and explains how key details back each of them up. The child also summarises the text.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads an article about the Gold Rush and writes down two main ideas, such as why people came and how the land changed. Under each idea the child lists the details from the article that support it. The child then writes a summary of a few sentences that leaves out minor facts and their own opinion.

### Limits

Two or more main ideas are required, not one. The supporting details are key details of the text. The statement sets no length for the summary and does not say whether it is written or spoken.

### Common mistakes

Children name the topic ("whales") instead of a main idea, which is a full statement about whales. They pick an interesting detail as a main idea. Their summary retells everything in order or adds their own opinion.
