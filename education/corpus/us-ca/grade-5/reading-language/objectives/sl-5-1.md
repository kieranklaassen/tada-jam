## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child takes part effectively in many kinds of collaborative discussion (in pairs, in groups, and led by the teacher) with varied partners about grade 5 topics and texts, adding to what others say and stating their own ideas clearly. Before a discussion the child reads or studies the material that was set, and during it refers openly to that reading and to whatever else they know about the topic when exploring ideas. The child keeps to the agreed discussion rules and carries out the role they were given. The child asks specific questions and answers them, with comments that add to the discussion and develop what others have said. The child goes back over the key ideas that were voiced and draws conclusions from what was learned in the discussions.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads the assigned chapter the night before and comes with two notes on it. In a group of four the child takes the role of timekeeper or note-taker, waits for a turn, and says "I agree with Maya, and the text also says ...". The child asks a classmate "What makes you think that?" and, at the end, says in two sentences what the group worked out.

### Limits

Topics and texts are grade 5 ones. Three settings are named: one-on-one, groups and teacher-led. Preparation, agreed rules and assigned roles are all part of the statement. The statement sets no group size and no length of discussion.

### Common mistakes

Children wait for their turn to say a prepared point and do not respond to what was just said. They answer a question with a comment on a different subject. They repeat a classmate's point as if it were new, or sum up only their own view when asked for the group's conclusions.
