## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

So that they can speak or write about a subject with knowledge, the child puts together information from several texts that deal with that one topic.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads three short texts about earthquakes, takes notes from each on cards, and sorts the cards by subtopic, not by source. The child then gives a short talk or writes a paragraph that uses facts from all three texts and does not repeat a fact.

### Limits

The statement says "several" texts, all on one topic; no exact number is set. The product may be written or spoken. This statement does not mention a list of sources.

### Common mistakes

Children summarise each text in turn instead of combining them. They rely on one text and add a single fact from the others. They do not notice when two texts give different figures for the same thing.
