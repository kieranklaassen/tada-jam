## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child compares and contrasts stories of one genre, for example adventure stories or mysteries, looking at how each handles themes and topics that are similar.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads two mysteries and notes for each who solves the puzzle, how clues are given, and what the story says about telling the truth. The child puts the notes side by side and explains one way both stories treat the theme alike and one way they treat it differently.

### Limits

The stories compared belong to the same genre. Mysteries and adventure stories are examples. The comparison is about how the stories approach similar themes and topics. The statement sets no number of stories beyond the plural.

### Common mistakes

Children compare plots event by event and say nothing about theme. They say two stories share a theme because they share a genre. They choose stories whose themes have nothing in common, so no comparison can be made.
