## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child draws on several print or digital sources and shows that they can find fast what a question asks for, or work out a problem without wasted effort.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child has a question such as "How long does a sea turtle live?", an encyclopedia, a book with an index, and a website. The child chooses where to look first, uses the index or a search box and the headings to reach the right page, and reads only the part that answers the question. The child checks a second source when the first is unclear.

### Limits

More than one source is used, and sources may be print or digital. Speed and efficiency are part of the statement, but no time limit is stated. The statement does not ask the child to judge whether a source is reliable.

### Common mistakes

Children start reading a source from its first page instead of using the index, the contents or the headings. They type a whole question into a search box and take the first result. They stop at the first sentence that contains the key word, whether or not it answers the question.
