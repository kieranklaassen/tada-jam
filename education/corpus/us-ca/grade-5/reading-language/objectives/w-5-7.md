## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child carries out short research projects in which several sources are used and different aspects of one topic are investigated, so that knowledge is built.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child picks the question "How do bees make honey?" and splits it into parts: the hive, the flowers, the bees' jobs. The child reads a book, an article and a video transcript and takes notes for each part. The child then tells or writes what they found.

### Limits

Projects are short; no number of days or pages is set. Several sources are required; no exact number is given. The topic is looked at from different aspects, not from one. The statement does not say what form the result takes.

### Common mistakes

Children use a single source and call it research. They collect facts about one part of the topic and ignore the rest. They pick a topic so broad ("animals") that no short project can cover it.
