## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child gets relevant information either by recalling it from their own experiences or by collecting it from print and digital sources. In notes and in finished work the child summarises or paraphrases that information, and supplies a list of the sources used.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a page about the rainforest, closes the book, and writes two notes in their own words on a card, with the book's title on the back. The child adds a note from a class trip to a botanical garden. At the end of the report the child writes the titles and authors of the books and websites used.

### Limits

Information may come from the child's experience or from print and digital sources. The child summarises or paraphrases; this statement does not mention direct quotation. A list of sources is required, and no citation format is named.

### Common mistakes

Children copy sentences word for word into notes and then into the report. They change one or two words and think that is paraphrasing. They forget to record where a note came from and cannot make the source list afterwards.
