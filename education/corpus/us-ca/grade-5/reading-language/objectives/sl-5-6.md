## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child changes the way they speak to fit different contexts and tasks, and speaks formal English when the task and situation call for it. The statement points to Language standards 1 and 3 of the same grade for the specific expectations.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child explains a science result to the class in full sentences without slang, then explains the same result to a friend at lunch in casual words. Before speaking to a visiting adult or giving a presentation, the child decides that formal English fits and replaces "gonna" and "stuff" with exact words.

### Limits

Formal English is asked for when the task and situation call for it, not at all times. The statement names no particular contexts. For the detail of grammar and language use it refers to Language standards 1 and 3 of the grade.

### Common mistakes

Children use the same casual speech in a presentation as with friends. They think formal English means long or unusual words and use words they do not control. They treat informal speech as wrong everywhere instead of as fitting some situations.
