## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The year-end expectation is independent, proficient reading with understanding of literature whose complexity is at the top of the band for grades 4 to 5. Poetry, plays and stories are named as included.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child chooses a grade 5 novel, a play script or a poem and reads it alone, without an adult explaining it first. Afterwards the child talks about what happened, why characters acted as they did, and what the work means.

### Limits

The level is the top of the complexity band for grades 4 to 5, reached by the end of the year. Reading is independent; no support is mentioned. Stories, dramas and poetry are all included. The statement does not define the band with a number or a reading-level scale.

### Common mistakes

Children finish a book and can retell events but cannot explain motives or theme. They avoid poetry and plays and read only stories. They choose books well below the band and so never read at the level the statement names.
