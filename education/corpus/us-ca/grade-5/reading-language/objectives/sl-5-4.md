## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When giving a report about a text or topic, or presenting an opinion, the child orders the ideas logically, supports the main ideas or themes with suitable facts and with descriptive details that are relevant, and speaks clearly at a pace listeners can follow. One part is an opinion speech that the child plans and gives: the speech states the opinion, sets out evidence for the speaker's position in a logical order, uses transition words such as therefore and consequently so that opinions and evidence are connected effectively, and closes with a statement that relates to the position. A further part is recitation from memory: a poem, or part of a speech or of a historical document, delivered with gestures, expression and a rate that suit the piece.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stands and gives a short report on a book, with three points in order written on a card. The child plans an opinion speech on whether the school should have a garden, says the opinion first, gives the evidence, uses "therefore" to connect them, and ends by returning to the position. The child memorises a poem and says it to the class, slowing down and using a hand gesture where the poem calls for it.

### Limits

The opening line covers reports and opinions on a topic or text. The opinion speech has four named elements: a stated opinion, evidence in logical order, transition words and a concluding statement. Recitation is from memory and may be of a poem, or of part of a speech or of a historical document. No speaking time and no length of piece is set. Both lettered parts, the opinion speech and the recitation, end with the California mark: California adds to each.

### Common mistakes

Children read a report from the page with eyes down and speak too fast to follow. They give an opinion and a list of facts with no words connecting them. When reciting, they hurry to finish before they forget, with a flat voice and no pauses.
