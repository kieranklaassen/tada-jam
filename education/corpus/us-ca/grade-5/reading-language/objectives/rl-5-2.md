## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

From the details of a story, play or poem the child works out a theme. The details include the way characters in a story or play react to challenges, and the way a poem's speaker thinks about a topic. The child also summarises the text.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child finishes a story in which a girl keeps practising after failing a tryout, and states a theme as a full sentence about people in general. The child points to two things the character did that show it. For a poem, the child says what the speaker thinks about the subject and which lines show it. The child then retells the text in a few sentences.

### Limits

One theme is asked for ("a theme"). The kinds of text are story, drama and poem. The theme is to come from details in the text. No length is set for the summary.

### Common mistakes

Children give a topic word ("friendship") instead of a theme stated as a sentence. They state a lesson that fits only this plot ("The girl should try out again"). They mix their own opinion into the summary.
