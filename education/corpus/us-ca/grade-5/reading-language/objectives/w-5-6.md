## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child writes and publishes with technology, the Internet included, and also uses it to work and communicate with other people; adults give some guidance and support. The child keyboards well enough to type two pages or more in one sitting.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child types a report in a word processor, with an adult helping to set up the shared document. A classmate leaves comments in the document, and the child answers them and changes the text. The child types for a whole work session, with both hands on the keyboard, until two pages are filled, then posts the finished piece to the class page.

### Limits

Adult support is described as "some" guidance and support. The typing minimum is two pages at one sitting; no typing speed or accuracy figure is stated. The Internet is named as one technology. The statement names no particular tools.

### Common mistakes

Children type with one or two fingers while looking at the keys and cannot keep going for two pages. They treat a first typed draft as finished because typed text looks neat. In shared documents they overwrite or delete a partner's work instead of commenting.
