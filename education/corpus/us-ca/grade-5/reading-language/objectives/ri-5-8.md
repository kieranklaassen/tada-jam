## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child explains how an author backs up particular points in a text with reasons and evidence, and matches each reason or piece of evidence to the point it supports.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads an editorial that argues for a longer recess. The child underlines each point the author makes, then draws a line from every reason, fact or example to the point it belongs with. The child notices a point that has no evidence attached and says so.

### Limits

The task is to explain and to match; the statement does not ask the child to judge whether the argument is right. Both reasons and evidence are named. The statement sets no number of points.

### Common mistakes

Children restate the author's opinion instead of the reasons given for it. They attach a piece of evidence to the wrong point because it stands nearby in the text. They treat any fact in the text as evidence, including facts that support nothing.
