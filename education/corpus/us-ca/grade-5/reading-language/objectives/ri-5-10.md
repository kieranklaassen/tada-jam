## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The year-end expectation is independent, proficient reading with understanding of informational texts whose complexity is at the top of the band for grades 4 to 5. Science, history/social studies and technical texts are named as included.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child picks up a grade 5 science chapter or a set of written instructions and reads it alone, without an adult explaining it. Afterwards the child answers questions about it, retells the main points, or follows the instructions correctly.

### Limits

The level is the top of the complexity band for grades 4 to 5, reached by the end of the year. Reading is independent; no support is mentioned. The named text types are science, technical texts and history/social studies. The statement does not define the band with a number or a reading-level scale.

### Common mistakes

Children read the words of a hard informational text accurately and still miss the meaning because they skip diagrams, captions and headings. They read a technical text such as instructions once from start to finish, the way they read a story, and do not go back to a step.
