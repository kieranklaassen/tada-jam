## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child writes numerical expressions that contain grouping symbols (braces, brackets or parentheses) and works out the value of expressions that contain them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child works out 2 × (8 + 7) and 2 × 8 + 7 side by side and says why the answers differ. Given the numbers 3, 4 and 5 and a target, the child places parentheses to reach it. For an expression with brackets inside braces, the child works from the innermost pair outward, rewriting the line after each step.

### Limits

Three grouping symbols are named: parentheses, brackets and braces. The expressions are numerical: no letters stand for numbers. The statement sets no limit on how deeply the symbols are nested or on the size of the numbers, and does not mention exponents.

### Common mistakes

Working strictly from left to right and ignoring the grouping symbols. Believing that multiplication always comes before division, and addition before subtraction, because a memorised word lists them in that order. Working out the inside of the parentheses correctly and then dropping the number that multiplies it.
