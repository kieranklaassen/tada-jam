## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names what shapes a person's health habits, both from inside the person and from outside.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old takes one habit, such as brushing teeth or wearing a helmet, and writes two columns. Under 'inside me' go being tired, liking the taste, feeling embarrassed, what they believe; under 'outside me' go family rules, friends, adverts, what is in the house. The child marks which entries make the healthy habit more likely and which make it less likely.

### Limits

The statement names the two kinds of influence and lists none. It asks the child to name them, not to resist them.

### Common mistakes

Children name outside influences, especially parents and adverts, and find none inside themselves. Many think an influence is always bad. Some confuse an influence with the habit itself.
