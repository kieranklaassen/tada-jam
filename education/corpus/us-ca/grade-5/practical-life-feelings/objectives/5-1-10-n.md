## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells how being active, resting and sleeping affect one another.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old explains that a body that has moved a lot during the day falls asleep more easily, and that a rested body has more energy to play. The child keeps a diary for a week with hours slept, time spent moving and a note on how they felt, and looks for a pattern. The child says what a soccer practice is like after a short night.

### Limits

The statement gives no hours of sleep or minutes of activity. It asks how the three are connected, not for a schedule.

### Common mistakes

Children treat rest and sleep as the same thing. Many think activity only makes a person tired and miss that it also helps sleep and gives energy the next day. Some count sitting with a screen as rest.
