## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child regularly uses ways of keeping the sun from doing harm.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old puts on sunscreen before going out and again after swimming or after two hours, wears a hat with a brim and sunglasses, and chooses the shade at midday. The child checks the day's UV index and says what it means for break time. The child covers the spots that are often missed: ears, neck, nose and the tops of the feet.

### Limits

The statement belongs to the standard on practising behaviours, so it asks for doing. It names no strategies, no sun protection factor and no times of day. Sun safety also appears as an example in the knowledge statement 5.1.1.P.

### Common mistakes

Children think sunscreen is needed only on hot or cloudless days. Many put it on once and do not reapply after water or sweat. Some think a tan or darker skin means no protection is needed.
