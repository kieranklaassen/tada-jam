## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells what people do to keep food safe to eat while they handle it and get it ready.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old lists the steps for a kitchen: wash hands with soap, rinse fruit and vegetables, keep raw meat apart from food that is eaten uncooked, cook food through, and put leftovers in the refrigerator. The child gives the reason for each step, for example that germs multiply on food left out in the warmth. The child puts picture cards of the steps in order.

### Limits

The statement asks the child to describe and names no particular practices. Carrying them out while making a meal or snack is 5.7.2.N.

### Common mistakes

Children think food is safe when it looks and smells fine. Many rinse their hands with water only, or wash them once and then touch raw meat and other food in turn. Some believe a refrigerator kills germs.
