## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child has an aim for how active they are and checks over time how near they have come to it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old has a goal such as 'sixty minutes of movement every day' or 'run the school lap without stopping'. The child writes down minutes or laps each day, draws them as a bar chart and reads off whether the bars are rising. The child tells a partner what next week's target is.

### Limits

The statement is about tracking a goal that already exists and gives no amount of activity, time span or way of recording. The matching statement for nutrition is 5.6.1.N.

### Common mistakes

Children write down what they planned instead of what they did. Many set a goal far beyond what they do now and stop after a few days. Some measure something other than what the goal names, such as counting days when the goal is in minutes.
