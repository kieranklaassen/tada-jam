## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Given a Nutrition Facts label, the child points to its main parts and names them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old turns a cereal box around, finds the panel and puts a finger on the serving size, the calories, and the lines for fat, sugar, sodium and protein. The child reads each heading aloud and says which line tells how many servings the package holds. The child does the same on a milk carton and finds the same parts in the same places.

### Limits

The statement asks only for finding the main parts and does not list which parts count as main. Working out what the numbers mean is 5.3.2.N.

### Common mistakes

Children skip the serving size and read every number as if it were for the whole package. Many take the percent column for an amount in grams. Some confuse the ingredient list with the panel of numbers.
