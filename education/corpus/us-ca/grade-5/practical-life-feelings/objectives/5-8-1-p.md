## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child urges the people around them to cause as little pollution of the environment as they can.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old makes a poster for the school corridor, speaks at a class meeting, or asks their family to switch off the car engine while waiting. The child gives a reason with the request, such as what exhaust does to the air around the playground. The child shows the behaviour themselves and thanks people who join in.

### Limits

The statement belongs to the health-promotion standard, so the child acts toward other people, not only for themselves. It names no kind of pollution and no audience. That each person has a duty toward the environment is the knowledge statement 5.1.6.P.

### Common mistakes

Children scold or give orders instead of encouraging. Many ask for a change they do not make themselves. Some give no reason, so the request is ignored.
