## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows the making of a healthy snack or meal, keeping the food clean while preparing it and while storing it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old washes hands with soap, wipes the counter, rinses the vegetables and cuts them on a clean board with a tool that suits their age. The child assembles a wrap or a fruit salad, covers what is left over and puts it in the refrigerator. The child says at each step why it keeps the food safe.

### Limits

The statement asks for one meal or snack and requires clean practice both in preparing and in storing. It names no recipe, tools or cooking method. Describing safe handling in words is 5.1.5.N.

### Common mistakes

Children wash their hands at the start, then touch hair, face or a phone and carry on. Many use the same board or knife for raw food and for food that is ready to eat. Some leave food out afterwards and forget the storing step.
