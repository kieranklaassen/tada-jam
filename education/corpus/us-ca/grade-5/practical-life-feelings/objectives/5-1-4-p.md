## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells what new body care becomes necessary once puberty begins.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old explains that sweat glands become more active and skin and hair get oilier, and names the care that follows: washing daily, using deodorant, washing hair more often, changing clothes after sport. The child explains what is needed when periods start. The child compares this with the routine of a seven-year-old and says what has been added.

### Limits

The statement is about the start of puberty and asks for a description. It lists no needs. A matching statement in the growth and development content area (5.1.10.G) gives examples, and a hygiene plan is asked for in 5.6.2.G.

### Common mistakes

Children do not notice their own body odour and think they need no change. Many cover odour with spray instead of washing. Some wear the same sports clothes several days in a row.
