## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells what shapes how a person eats and how active they are, both from inside the person and from the world around them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old lists what led to their last snack and their last hour of play: hunger, mood, taste and habit; family, friends, advertisements, price and what was in the kitchen. The child sorts the list into 'from inside me' and 'from outside me'. The child says which one mattered most that time.

### Limits

The statement covers both eating and activity and both kinds of influence, and names none of them. Family and culture (5.2.2.N) and advertising (5.2.3.N) have statements of their own.

### Common mistakes

Children see only outside influences and say 'I just felt like it' for the rest, without naming hunger, mood or habit. Many think an influence is always bad. Some confuse an influence with a choice: 'my friend made me'.
