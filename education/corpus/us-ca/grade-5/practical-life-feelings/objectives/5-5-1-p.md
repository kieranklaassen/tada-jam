## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works through the steps of making a decision to arrive at choices of their own that are good for their health, for the health of the community, and for the environment.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old takes a real choice, such as how to get to school or what to do with an empty bottle, and writes the steps: name the choice, list the options, think through what each does to me, to my neighbourhood and to nature, pick one, and review it afterwards. The child says the steps aloud while working through a second example with a partner. The child explains which effect settled the choice.

### Limits

The statement names three kinds of health the choices should serve and does not spell out the steps of the process. A second statement applies the same kind of process to the question of medical help (5.5.2.P).

### Common mistakes

Children decide first and fill in the steps afterwards to fit. Many weigh only the effect on themselves and leave out the community and the environment. Some list one option only.
