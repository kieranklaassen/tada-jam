## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child explains how metabolism and the nutrients a person takes in are connected.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old says in their own words that the body breaks food down and uses what is in it for energy, growth and repair. The child follows a sandwich after it is eaten and draws arrows from the food to moving, growing and keeping warm. The child tells what happens when a person takes in more, or less, than the body uses.

### Limits

The statement does not say how much detail about metabolism is expected and names no nutrients. It asks for an explanation of the link, not for calculating energy.

### Common mistakes

Children think metabolism only means how fast a person burns food. Many believe food simply passes through the body and leaves it, without becoming energy or new tissue. Some treat calories as a harmful substance instead of a measure of energy.
