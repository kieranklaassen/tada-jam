## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child explains what eating well and moving the body do for a person's health.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old tells how food and movement work on the body: food gives energy and material to grow, movement strengthens the heart, lungs, muscles and bones. The child gives an example from their own week, such as feeling tired after skipping breakfast or sleeping well after a day of sport. The child draws eating and activity on one side of a page, health on the other, and labels each arrow between them.

### Limits

The statement treats eating and activity together and names no amounts of either. The benefits of regular physical activity alone are 5.1.11.N.

### Common mistakes

Children think health only means not being ill. Many believe exercise cancels out any food, or that eating well makes exercise unnecessary. Some see health as a matter of body size alone.
