## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

At school and in the community, the child encourages and supports two things: eating well, and more chances to be physically active.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old makes a poster for the cafeteria about trying the salad bar, or writes to the principal asking for balls and ropes at recess. The child invites classmates to a walking group before school and proposes a fruit break at a class meeting. The child asks a neighbourhood adult where children can play safely and passes the answer on.

### Limits

The statement names two places, the school and the community, and two aims: healthy eating, and more opportunities for activity. It does not say what form the encouragement takes.

### Common mistakes

Children tell others what not to eat instead of inviting them to something. Many make a poster and regard the job as done, without checking whether anyone acted on it. Some address only friends and leave out the adults who decide, such as the principal.
