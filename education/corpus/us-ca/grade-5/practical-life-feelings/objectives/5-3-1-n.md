## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child finds guidelines, suited to their age, on what to eat and how active to be.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old asks where to look: the school nurse, a leaflet from the doctor, the health textbook, a government food guide. The child finds the page meant for children of their age, not the one for adults, and reads out which foods and how much daily movement it advises. The child tells who published it.

### Limits

The statement is about finding the guidelines and does not name a source or say what they contain. Comparing one's own habits with them is 5.5.3.N.

### Common mistakes

Children take the first thing they find, such as an advertisement or a video, as a guideline. Many use advice written for adults. Some cannot say who wrote a page and so cannot tell whether to trust it.
