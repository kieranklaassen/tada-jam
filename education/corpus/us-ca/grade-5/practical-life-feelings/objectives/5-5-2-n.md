## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child goes through the steps of making a decision to work out which activities build up physical fitness.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old lists activities open to them, such as cycling to school, swimming, jump rope and a video game, and asks of each: does it make my heart beat faster, does it build strength, can I do it often? The child compares the options, chooses two for the week and says why. A week later the child tells whether they kept to the choice.

### Limits

The statement does not spell out the steps or say what kind of fitness is meant. It names no amount of activity.

### Common mistakes

Children choose the activity they already like without asking whether it raises fitness. Many count any time outdoors as exercise. Some pick a plan they cannot keep, such as an hour's run every day.
