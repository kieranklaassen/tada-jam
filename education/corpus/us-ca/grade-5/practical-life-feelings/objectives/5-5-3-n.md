## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child sets their own habits of eating and of being active beside today's guidelines for their age and sees where the two match and where they differ.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old writes down what they ate and how long they moved on three days. The child lays the record next to a food guide and an activity guideline for children and marks each line: meets it, under, over. The child says one thing that surprised them.

### Limits

The statement asks for a comparison and does not name the guidelines. Finding them is 5.3.1.N, and tracking a goal is 5.6.1.N and 5.6.2.N.

### Common mistakes

Children remember a good day and record it as a usual day. Many leave out drinks and snacks. Some compare with what a friend or a parent does instead of with the guideline.
