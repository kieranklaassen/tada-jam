## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells what the state of the environment around a person does to that person's health.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old reads the day's air quality report or heat warning and says what it means for playing outside. The child links cause to result: smoky air to coughing and asthma, dirty water to stomach illness, loud noise to hearing damage, strong sun to burns. The child walks around the school and points out one thing that helps health, such as shade or clean drinking water, and one that harms it.

### Limits

The statement names no particular conditions and asks only for a description. Sun protection is a separate practice statement (5.7.3.P), and the duty to protect the environment is another statement (5.1.6.P).

### Common mistakes

Children think of the environment only as nature far away, not as the air, water and noise around them. Many link health only to germs and food. Some see only what harms and leave out what helps.
