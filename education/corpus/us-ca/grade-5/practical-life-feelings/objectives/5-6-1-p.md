## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child has an aim that helps keep the environment safe and keeps track of how far along they are in reaching it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old picks a goal such as 'bring a refillable bottle every school day' or 'our class halves its paper waste'. The child tallies each day on a chart, weighs or counts the waste each week, and compares the numbers with the week before. When the numbers stop improving, the child says what to change.

### Limits

The statement is about tracking, so a goal has to exist first; it does not say who sets the goal or how large it is. It names no time span and no measure. Tracking a personal health goal is the neighbouring statement (5.6.2.P).

### Common mistakes

Children set the goal and never check it again. Many choose a goal that cannot be counted, such as 'save the planet'. Some record only the good days.
