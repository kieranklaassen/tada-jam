## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child keeps body and teeth clean as a regular habit, and does it well.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old brushes teeth for two minutes twice a day, flosses, washes hands with soap before eating and after the toilet, showers, and puts on clean clothes. The child does this without being reminded. The child checks their own work, for example by looking in a mirror for missed spots along the gum line.

### Limits

The statement belongs to the standard on practising behaviours, so it asks for doing. It names care of the body and of the teeth and sets no frequency or duration.

### Common mistakes

Children brush too briefly and miss the back teeth and the gum line. Many rinse hands with water only. Some keep the habit only while an adult checks.
