## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child has an aim for their own health and keeps track of how close they are to reaching it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old has a goal such as 'floss every night' or 'sleep nine hours on school nights'. The child marks a calendar each day, looks at the week on Sunday, and says how many days worked. The child names what stopped them on the missed days and adjusts the plan.

### Limits

The statement is about tracking, so it assumes a goal already exists. It does not say what the goal is, how long to track it, or how. Tracking a goal for the environment is the neighbouring statement (5.6.1.P).

### Common mistakes

Children stop recording after a few days. Many give up the whole goal after one missed day. Some pick a goal too vague to check, such as 'be healthier'.
