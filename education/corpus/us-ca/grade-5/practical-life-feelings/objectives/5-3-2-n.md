## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child reads a food label and works out what it tells them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old reads the label on a juice bottle and answers questions: how many servings are in the bottle, how much sugar is in one serving, how much in the whole bottle. The child compares two cereals on the same line and says which has more fibre. The child reads the ingredient list and finds which ingredient there is most of.

### Limits

The statement speaks of food labels in general and does not name which parts or numbers. Finding the parts of the Nutrition Facts panel is 5.1.2.N; this one is about making sense of what they say.

### Common mistakes

Children compare two packages without checking that the serving sizes are the same. Many read the amount per serving as the amount in the whole package. Some think the percent daily value is the share of the food that is made of that nutrient.
