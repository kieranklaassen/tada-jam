## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises asking for help clearly and well when there is a health problem or an emergency.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old role-plays an emergency call: says their name, where they are, what happened and to whom, answers the operator's questions and stays on the line. For a smaller problem the child practises telling a teacher 'my chest feels tight and I need my inhaler' instead of 'I feel funny'. The child speaks slowly and loudly enough and repeats the message when it was not heard.

### Limits

The statement covers everyday health problems as well as emergencies. Its verb can mean rehearsing the skills or using them when help is really needed, and the statement does not confine it to either. It does not list the skills or name the emergency number.

### Common mistakes

Children end the call too early or forget to say where they are. Many describe the problem too vaguely for the listener to act. Some wait and say nothing because they fear being wrong or causing trouble for someone.
