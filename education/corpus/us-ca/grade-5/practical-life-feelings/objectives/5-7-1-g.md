## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child does the things that help them grow and develop well in the years of puberty.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old goes to bed early enough to get the sleep a growing body needs, eats regular meals with enough calcium and iron, drinks water, and is active every day. The child washes daily and talks to a trusted adult when a change worries them. The child continues this at home, without a teacher watching.

### Limits

The statement belongs to the standard on practising behaviours, so it asks for doing, not describing. It names no specific behaviours. A footnote cites the Education Code.

### Common mistakes

Children know the habits and do not do them, most often with sleep. Many skip meals or cut down on food because of worries about body shape. Some see exercise as only for sporty children.
