## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows they can keep what they eat and how much they move in balance with each other.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old plans a day and matches food to movement: a bigger breakfast and extra water on a day with a soccer match, a smaller snack on a day spent reading. The child keeps a log of meals and activity for a few days and says whether the two fit. The child changes one thing on a day when they do not.

### Limits

The statement gives no calorie figures or amounts of activity. It asks the child to show the balance in practice, not to calculate it.

### Common mistakes

Children think of balance as earning food with exercise, or as exercising to undo a treat. Many eat less on active days because they are busy. Some forget drinks when they count what they take in.
