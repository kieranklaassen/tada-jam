## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows that looking after their health routines is their own job.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old packs their own water bottle and sports kit, goes to bed at the agreed time without being sent, and puts on a helmet before anyone tells them to. When a habit is missed, the child says so and starts again instead of blaming someone. The child reminds themselves with a note or an alarm they set.

### Limits

The statement belongs to the standard on practising behaviours and asks the child to show responsibility. It names no particular habits.

### Common mistakes

Children think remembering health habits is a parent's job. Many do the habit only when reminded and count that as their own doing. Some blame a missed habit on being busy or on someone else.
