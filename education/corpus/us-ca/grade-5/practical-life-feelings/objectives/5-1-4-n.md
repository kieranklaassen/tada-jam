## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child gives the reasons that more portions are advised from certain food groups than from the rest.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old looks at a food guide and points to the group with the largest share and the one with the smallest. The child says what the body gets from each, for example fibre and vitamins from vegetables, and why it needs a lot of one and only a little of another. The child compares two plates and explains which one matches the guide.

### Limits

The statement does not name the groups or give the numbers of portions, and it does not say whose recommendations are meant. Describing the groups and their portions is 5.1.1.N.

### Common mistakes

Children think the group with the most portions is the most important one and that the small groups can be left out. Many answer 'because it is healthy' without saying what the body uses the food for. Some take a high portion count to mean they may eat as much as they like.
