## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child makes a plan for keeping their body clean while it goes through puberty.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old writes a morning and an evening routine: shower or wash, deodorant, clean underwear and socks, wash the face, brush teeth. The child adds what to keep in the school bag, such as deodorant or pads, and a day for washing sports clothes. The child hangs the plan where they will see it and marks it each day for a week.

### Limits

The statement belongs to the goal-setting standard and asks for a plan, not for proof that the plan is followed. It does not say what the plan contains; the neighbouring statement 5.1.10.G gives examples of hygiene practices. A footnote cites the Education Code.

### Common mistakes

Children write a plan too vague to follow, such as 'stay clean'. Many leave out what is new at this age, such as deodorant, washing sports kit or changing pads. Some plan a routine that needs a parent to remind them at every step.
