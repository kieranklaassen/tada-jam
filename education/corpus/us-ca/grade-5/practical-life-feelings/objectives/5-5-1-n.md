## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

To pick out healthy foods for snacks and meals, the child goes through the steps of making a decision.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old plans tomorrow's lunch box in steps: say what is needed, list four or five possible foods, check each against a food guide or its label, choose, and afterwards say whether the choice worked. The child does this aloud with a partner for an after-school snack. The child gives the reason one option was dropped.

### Limits

The statement does not spell out the steps of the process or define what counts as healthy. The same kind of process is applied to fitness activities in 5.5.2.N.

### Common mistakes

Children pick the food they like first and then make the steps fit. Many list a single option. Some decide by one feature, such as 'no sugar', and ignore the rest.
