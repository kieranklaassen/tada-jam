## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child has an aim for their own nutrition and keeps track of how close they are to reaching it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old has a goal such as 'a vegetable with every dinner' or 'water instead of soda at lunch'. The child ticks a chart each day, counts the ticks at the end of the week and compares them with the week before. When the count drops, the child says what made it hard and what to change.

### Limits

The statement is about tracking, so a goal has to exist first; it does not say who sets it or what it is. It names no time span and no way of recording. The matching statement for physical activity is 5.6.2.N.

### Common mistakes

Children set a goal and never look at it again. Many choose a goal that cannot be counted, such as 'eat healthier'. Some record only the days that went well.
