## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child explains what moderation in eating means.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old explains that moderation means not too much of any one food and that no food is forbidden. The child shows it with real things: a small handful of chips beside the whole bag, one glass of juice beside the carton. The child says how often a sweet treat fits into a week and what they would eat the rest of the time.

### Limits

The statement asks for the idea only and gives no amounts or serving sizes. It does not name foods to limit.

### Common mistakes

Children hear 'moderation' as 'eat less of everything' or as a rule against treats. Many think a food is either allowed or banned. Some judge an amount by the size of the package and count one bag as one serving.
