## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells how the methods used to advertise and market products sway what people pick to eat and drink.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old looks at a cereal box, a soda commercial and a shop shelf and points out the methods: a cartoon character, a toy inside, a famous athlete, bright colours, a place at a child's eye level. The child says what each method tries to make a buyer feel or do. The child compares what the advertisement shows with what the label says.

### Limits

The statement names no particular techniques or media. It asks for a description of their effect; answering peers and media is 5.4.1.N.

### Common mistakes

Children believe advertisements only inform and do not see that someone is selling. Many think only younger children are affected, not they themselves. Some take a picture of fruit on a package as proof that fruit is inside.
