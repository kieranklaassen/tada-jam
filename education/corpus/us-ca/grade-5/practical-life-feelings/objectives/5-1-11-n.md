## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names the good that regular physical activity does in four areas: for the body, for learning at school, for the mind, and for getting on with other people.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old sorts cards into four piles headed body, school, mind and friends: 'stronger heart', 'easier to concentrate in class', 'feel calmer', 'make friends on a team'. The child adds one card of their own to each pile. The child tells a classmate which of the four they notice most after recess.

### Limits

The statement names four kinds of benefit and asks the child to name them, not to explain how they come about. It says the activity is regular without giving how often or how long.

### Common mistakes

Children name only benefits for the body, such as strong muscles. Many do not connect movement with schoolwork or mood. Some count only organised sport as physical activity and leave out walking, cycling and play.
