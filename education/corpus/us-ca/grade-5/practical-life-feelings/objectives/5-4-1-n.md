## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When peers or media sway what the child eats or how active they are, the child uses communication skills to handle it well.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old practises with a partner: one offers an energy drink or says 'nobody plays outside any more', the other answers clearly, 'No thanks, I'll have water' or 'I'm going to the park, come along'. The child says what they want without insulting the friend and repeats it when pressed. The child also asks a question back about an advertisement: 'Who says this is good for me?'

### Limits

The statement names two sources of influence, peers and media, and covers both food and activity. It does not say which communication skills are meant.

### Common mistakes

Children give in to keep a friendship, or refuse so rudely that the friendship suffers. Many say no once and stop when asked a second time. Some do not see an advertisement as something a person can question.
