## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child explains that looking after the environment and keeping it from harm is a duty every person shares.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A ten-year-old gives reasons why caring for air, water and land is everyone's job, and not only the job of adults, companies or the government. The child names what they do themselves: put litter in a bin, turn off a tap, sort recycling, walk or cycle short distances. The child explains what would happen if each person left it to someone else.

### Limits

The statement asks for an explanation of a duty and names no actions. Setting and tracking an environmental goal (5.6.1.P) and encouraging others to pollute less (5.8.1.P) are separate statements.

### Common mistakes

Children think one person's actions make no difference. Many see caring for the environment as picking up litter only. Some place the whole duty on adults or factories.
