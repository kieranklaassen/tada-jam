## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student uses models to describe that the energy in what animals eat started as energy from the sun. The wording notes what animals use that energy for: moving, growing, repairing the body and keeping it warm. The clarification's examples of models are flow charts and diagrams.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student picks a meal, such as a cheese sandwich, and draws a flow chart backward from each part: cheese to milk to cow to grass to sun. The student adds labels for what the animal's body does with the energy. The student explains the chart to a partner, starting from the sun.

### Limits

The expectation states no assessment boundary. The verb is to use models; the two kinds of model in the clarification are examples.

### Common mistakes

Stopping the chain at the plant and not reaching the sun. Thinking plants get their energy from soil or water. Believing animals that eat only meat get no energy from the sun.
