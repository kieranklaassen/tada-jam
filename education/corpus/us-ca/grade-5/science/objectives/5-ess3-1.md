## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student gathers information and puts it together to show how particular communities apply ideas from science to look after the environment and the resources the Earth provides.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student reads about how a town cleans its waste water or protects a wetland, takes notes from two or three sources, and combines them on one page. The student interviews someone who works at a local recycling centre or water district and adds what they said. The student tells the class which science idea the community relied on.

### Limits

The expectation carries no clarification statement and no assessment boundary. It asks for information that is gathered and combined; it does not mention designing a solution.

### Common mistakes

Listing good deeds, such as picking up litter, without naming the science idea behind the action. Copying from one source and not combining several. Writing about what one person does when the expectation is about communities.
