## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child finds the missing whole number in an equation that adds or subtracts and links three whole numbers. The examples put the unknown in different places: as the total (6 + 6 = □), as the amount that is subtracted from (5 = □ - 3), and as an addend (8 + ? = 11); in each the child finds the number that makes the equation true.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a card with eight, a plus sign, an empty box and "= 11", sets out eight counters, adds counters until there are eleven and counts the ones added. The child writes that number in the box and reads the whole equation aloud to check that it is true.

### Limits

Each equation has three whole numbers and one operation, adding or subtracting. Neither the statement nor its cluster heading sets a number range; the examples stay within 12, and the neighbouring statements work within 20. The unknown appears in a different position in each example. The stored wording is the export's as corrected from the adopted document at two places.

### Common mistakes

Adding the two numbers that are visible whatever the equation says, so that the box in "8 plus what makes 11" is filled with 19. For the example with the unknown before the minus sign, subtracting the visible numbers and answering 2. Filling the box without reading the equation back to check it.
