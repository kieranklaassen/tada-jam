## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child reads the time from a clock with hands and from a clock with digits, says it and writes it down, to the hour and to the half-hour.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child turns the hands of a practice clock to show "half past three", then reads a time that an adult sets on it. The child writes 3:30 beside a drawn clock face and pairs a clock with hands with the digital display that shows the same time.

### Limits

Hours and half-hours only, on both kinds of clock. Quarter hours, five-minute steps and minutes are not mentioned. The statement does not mention morning and afternoon labels.

### Common mistakes

Mixing up the hour hand and the minute hand. At half past, naming the hour that the short hand is moving toward, so that 3:30 is read as 4:30. Reading the long hand at the 6 as "six minutes" and writing 3:06.
