## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child connects counting with adding and subtracting. The example given is that adding 2 can be done by counting two steps onward.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child has six counters under a cup and two beside it, taps the cup and says "six", then touches the two and says "seven, eight". To take two from nine the child counts back "eight, seven". On a number path the child moves a marker two steps forward and reads where it stops.

### Limits

The statement itself gives no number range; its cluster heading sets the range for adding and subtracting at 20. Its only example is counting on to add; subtraction is named but given no example.

### Common mistakes

Counting everything from one and not starting from the first amount. Saying the starting number as the first step, so that six and two more comes out as seven. Doing the same when counting back, so that nine take away two comes out as eight.
