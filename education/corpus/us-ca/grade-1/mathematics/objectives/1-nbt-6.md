## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child subtracts one multiple of 10 from another, both between 10 and 90, where the answer is zero or more. The child works with concrete models or drawings and with strategies that rest on place value, on properties of the operations, or on how adding and subtracting are related, connects what was done to a way of writing it down, and says why it works.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays out seven ten rods for 70, takes three away and reads "four tens, forty". The child says "seven tens take away three tens is four tens, like seven take away three" and writes 70 - 30 = 40.

### Limits

Both numbers are multiples of 10 from 10 to 90, and the answer is never below zero. Subtracting other two-digit numbers is not covered by this statement. Models, drawings and the three kinds of strategy are the named ways of working.

### Common mistakes

Treating 70 - 30 as 7 - 3 and writing 4. Counting back by ones from 70 and losing count. Hearing or reading thirty as thirteen.
