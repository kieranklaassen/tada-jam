## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child counts up to 120 and can begin from any number below 120. Within that range the child reads numerals, writes them, and writes the numeral that tells how many objects are in a group.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child counts aloud from 87 onward, past 100 and up to 120. The child counts a pile of 43 sticks and writes "43" on a card beside it. The child points to numbers on a chart that runs to 120 and reads each one aloud.

### Limits

The top of the range is 120. Counting forward is what the statement names; counting backward and counting in steps are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Stalling or misnaming at a change of decade, for example saying "thirty-ten" after 39. Past 100, writing what is heard: 1005 for one hundred five, or 1010 for one hundred ten. Reversing the digits of a teen number, such as writing 71 for seventeen, because the spoken word begins with the ones.
