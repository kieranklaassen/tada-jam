## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child forms full sentences whenever the task and the setting call for them. A cross-reference points to Language standards 1 and 3 of grade 1 for the specific expectations.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Asked "Where is your coat?" during a class sharing time, the child answers "My coat is on the hook" and not only "hook". Telling the group about a drawing, the child says whole sentences, each with a who and a what.

### Limits

Complete sentences are expected when the task and situation make them appropriate, not in every exchange. The statement names no sentence types of its own; it points to the grade 1 Language standards 1 and 3. In this lane L.1.1 sets out the sentence kinds, and the lane's frame lists L.1.3 as a placeholder for a standard that begins in a later grade.

### Common mistakes

Answering with one word or a fragment when a full sentence was asked for. Joining many clauses with "and then" into one long sentence. Starting with "because" and never giving the main part of the sentence.
