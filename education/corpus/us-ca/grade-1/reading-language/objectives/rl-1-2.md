## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells a story again with its important details and shows that they understand its main message or the lesson it teaches.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child retells a story just heard or read, with puppets, with picture cards put in order, or with the voice alone: who was in it, and what happened first, next and last. Asked what the story teaches, the child says something like "it is better to tell the truth".

### Limits

The retelling includes key details; the statement gives no number of details and no story length. The child shows understanding of the story's central message or lesson. No support condition is stated.

### Common mistakes

Telling only the ending or one favorite part. Retelling events out of order, or giving every small event the same weight as the main ones. Giving an event from the story ("the wolf fell in the pot") when asked for its lesson.
