## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child takes part in shared conversations on grade 1 topics and texts, with a variety of partners, both children and adults, in small groups and in larger ones. The child keeps to the discussion rules the group has agreed on, for example talking one at a time on the topic or text being discussed and listening carefully to the others. The child builds on what others have said by replying to their comments over several turns. When something in the topic or text being discussed is unclear, the child asks questions until it is clear.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a circle of classmates talking about a book, the child waits until the speaker has finished, looks at whoever is talking, and then adds to what was said ("I agree with Mia, and also..."). The child stays with the same subject for several turns. When confused, the child asks "What do you mean?" or "Can you say that again?"

### Limits

Topics and texts are grade 1 ones. Partners include both peers and adults, and groups are both small and larger. The two discussion rules given, careful listening and one speaker at a time, are examples of agreed rules. Replies run over multiple exchanges; no number is given.

### Common mistakes

Waiting for a turn only to say something unrelated to what the last speaker said. Interrupting, or talking only to the adult and not to other children. Staying silent when confused instead of asking.
