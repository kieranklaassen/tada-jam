## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Guided and supported by adults, the child makes and publishes writing with several kinds of digital tools, and this includes working together with classmates.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

With an adult beside them, the child types a sentence on a keyboard or a tablet, finds the space bar and the shift key, adds a drawing or a photo, and prints the page for a class book. Two children take turns typing the lines of one shared piece.

### Limits

All of this is done with guidance and support from adults. The statement asks for a variety of digital tools and names none. Both producing and publishing are covered, and working with peers is included. No typing speed and no length are stated.

### Common mistakes

Searching for each letter so slowly that the sentence is forgotten. Typing all in capitals or without spaces. One child of a pair doing all the typing.
