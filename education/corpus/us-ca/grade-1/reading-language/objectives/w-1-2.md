## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child writes a text that informs or explains: it says what the topic is, gives a few facts on it, and comes to some kind of close.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes a few sentences about something they know, such as snails: one sentence that says what the piece is about, two or three facts, and a last sentence that closes it. The child may draw and label a picture beside the writing.

### Limits

Three elements are asked for: a named topic, some facts about it, and some sense of closure. The statement gives no number of facts and no length, and states no support condition; adult support for strengthening writing is in the neighbouring W.1.5.

### Common mistakes

Writing opinions ("snails are cute") in place of facts. Moving to a second topic partway through. Listing facts without first saying what the topic is, or stopping with no closing sentence.
