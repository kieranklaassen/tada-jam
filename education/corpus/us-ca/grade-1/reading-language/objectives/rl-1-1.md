## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child asks about the important details of a literary text and, when asked about them, answers.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a story is read, the child asks something they wonder about ("Why did the fox hide?") and answers an adult's who, what, where, when and why questions, turning back to the page that holds the answer.

### Limits

Both asking and answering are covered. Questions concern key details of the text. No support condition and no number of questions are stated. The kind of text comes from the strand, literature; RI.1.1 asks the same of informational texts.

### Common mistakes

Answering with a guess, or with a similar event from the child's own life, instead of what the story says. Making a comment when asked to pose a question. Answering a "why" question by retelling what happened.
