## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

After hearing a text read aloud, or taking in information given by voice or through other media, the child poses questions about its important details and replies to such questions. The child also gives simple directions of two steps, says such directions back, and carries them out.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a story is read aloud or a short video is watched, the child asks one question about it and answers who, what and where questions without seeing the text. The child hears "Put the book on the shelf, then sit on the carpet", says it back, and does both steps in order. Then the child gives a two-step direction to a partner.

### Limits

The material is heard or viewed (a read-aloud, spoken information, other media); the statement is not about text the child reads alone. Questions are about key details. Directions are simple and have two steps; the child gives them, restates them and follows them. California adds the part on two-step directions.

### Common mistakes

Doing only the last step heard, or doing the two steps in the wrong order. Restating one step and dropping the other. Answering a question about a read-aloud from the pictures remembered and not from what was said.
