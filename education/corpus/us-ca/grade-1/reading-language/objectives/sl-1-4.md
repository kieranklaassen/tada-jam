## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child describes people, things, places and happenings, gives details that fit, and makes ideas and feelings clear. The child also learns songs, rhymes and poems by heart and says or sings them with expression.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells the class about a trip to the market: who was there, what the place looked and smelled like, what happened, and how it felt. The child says a short poem or sings a song from memory, with a loud or a soft voice, pauses and gestures that fit the words.

### Limits

Four kinds of subject are named: people, places, things and events. Details are relevant ones; no number is given. California adds the part on learning poems, songs and rhymes by heart and reciting them. The statement gives no length for a poem or a song and states no support condition.

### Common mistakes

Listing what happened with no detail ("we went to the park and then we went home"). Leaving out who or where, as if the listener had been there. Reciting in a flat, hurried voice, or stopping after the first lines because the rest was not learned.
