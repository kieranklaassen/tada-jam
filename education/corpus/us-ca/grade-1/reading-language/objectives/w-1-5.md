## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Guided and supported by adults, the child keeps a piece of writing on one topic, takes up the questions and suggestions that classmates offer, and, where needed, makes the writing stronger by adding details.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads their piece aloud to a partner, hears a question such as "What color was the kite?", and adds a word or a sentence that answers it, with a caret or on an extra line. With an adult's help the child crosses out a sentence that is about something else.

### Limits

All of this is done with guidance and support from adults. The questions and suggestions come from peers. The change named is adding details, and only as needed; the statement does not mention correcting spelling or rewriting the whole piece.

### Common mistakes

Hearing a classmate's question as criticism and changing nothing. Answering the question aloud without adding the answer to the page. Adding details that belong to a different topic.
