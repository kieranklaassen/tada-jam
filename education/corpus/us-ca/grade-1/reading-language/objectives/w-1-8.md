## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Guided and supported by adults, the child answers a question either by remembering things from experience or by collecting information from sources they have been given.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Asked "What do ducks eat?", the child thinks back to a visit to the pond and tells what they saw, or looks in the two books and the photo an adult has set out and marks the places that answer the question. The child says, draws or writes the answer.

### Limits

All of this is done with guidance and support from adults. Sources are provided to the child; the statement does not ask the child to find sources. The information serves to answer a question. The statement does not say whether the answer is written or spoken.

### Common mistakes

Giving information that is true but does not answer the question. Making up an answer when neither memory nor the source holds one. Copying a whole page instead of picking the part that answers the question.
