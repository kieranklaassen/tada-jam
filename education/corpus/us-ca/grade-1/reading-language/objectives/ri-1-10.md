## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child reads informational texts that are suitably hard for grade 1, and has prompting and support while doing so. In this reading the child calls up what they already know that has to do with the events and information in the text, and checks whether their predictions of what comes next in it turn out right.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before reading a text about the ocean with an adult, the child tells what they already know about the sea. Partway through, the child says what the next page will probably tell, then turns the page and says whether that was right. The child reads the text, and the adult helps with hard words.

### Limits

The whole statement is done with prompting and support. Texts are informational and of a difficulty suited to grade 1; the statement gives no level scale and no text list. California adds both lettered parts: calling up prior knowledge and confirming predictions. Part b speaks of confirming predictions; the statement does not separately mention making them.

### Common mistakes

Telling a long personal story that has little to do with the text when asked what they already know. Keeping to a prediction after the text has shown something else, or never going back to check it. Later reporting their own prior knowledge as something the text said.
