## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child describes the main ideas of an informational text, drawing on both its pictures and the details it gives.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child points to a diagram of a plant and to a sentence about roots, and says in their own words what that part of the text teaches. For each thing said, the child puts a finger on the picture or the detail that it comes from.

### Limits

Both illustrations and details of the text are drawn on. What is described is the text's key ideas; the statement gives no number of ideas and no support condition.

### Common mistakes

Describing only what is in the picture and leaving out what the words add. Listing details one after another without saying the idea they belong to. Describing a striking part of an illustration that is not a key idea of the text.
