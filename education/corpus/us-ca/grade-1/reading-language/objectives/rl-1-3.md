## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Drawing on important details, the child describes who is in a story, where and when it takes place, and the main things that happen.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells what a character looks like, does and feels, and points to the part of the story that shows it. The child says where the story happens and names its two or three biggest events, or draws them and explains the drawing.

### Limits

Three story elements are named: characters, settings and major events. Descriptions rest on key details from the story. No support condition and no number of details are stated.

### Common mistakes

Naming a character without describing them ("there is a bear"). Describing a character by looks only and leaving out what the character does or feels. Mixing small events with major ones, or leaving the setting out.
