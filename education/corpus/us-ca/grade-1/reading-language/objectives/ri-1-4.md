## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

To find out, or make clearer, what a word or a phrase in an informational text means, the child poses questions and replies to them. A cross-reference points to Language standards 4 to 6 of grade 1 for further expectations.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Reading a text about volcanoes, the child stops at "erupt", asks "What does erupt mean?", and with a partner or an adult looks at the sentence and the picture to answer. Asked what a phrase means, the child answers in other words or points to the part of the page that explains it.

### Limits

The statement is about words and phrases met in a text, and covers both asking and answering. It states no support condition and names no strategies of its own; for those it points to the grade 1 Language standards 4 to 6 (L.1.4, L.1.5 and L.1.6 in this lane). California adds to this statement: the state's mark stands after that cross-reference.

### Common mistakes

Passing over an unknown word without noticing that it was not understood. Asking about a word and accepting any answer without checking that it fits the sentence. Answering with a meaning the word has elsewhere but not in this text.
