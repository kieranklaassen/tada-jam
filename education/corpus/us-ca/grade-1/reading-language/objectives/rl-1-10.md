## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child reads grade 1 prose and poetry, texts whose difficulty suits the grade, and has prompting and support while doing so. In this reading the child brings to mind what they already know that relates to the text's events and information, and checks whether what they predicted would come next in the text does come.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before a story about a lost dog is read with an adult, the child tells about a time when something of theirs was lost. Partway through, the child says what will happen next, reads on, and says whether it happened. The child reads the lines of a short poem aloud, and the adult joins in on hard words.

### Limits

The whole statement is done with prompting and support. Texts are prose and poetry of a difficulty suited to grade 1; no level scale and no text list are given. California adds both lettered parts: calling up prior knowledge and confirming predictions. Part b speaks of confirming predictions; the statement does not separately mention making them.

### Common mistakes

Predicting what the child wishes would happen, with no link to what the story has shown so far. Forgetting the prediction once the reading goes on, so that it is never checked. Telling prior knowledge unrelated to the text and later mixing it into a retelling.
