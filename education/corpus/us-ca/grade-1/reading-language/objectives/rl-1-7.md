## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child describes the people, the place or the happenings of a story, drawing on its pictures and on details from it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a picture of a character frowning in the rain and says how the character feels and where they are, then finds the sentence that says the same or adds more. The child points to what in the picture or in the words shows it.

### Limits

The child draws on both illustrations and details of the story. What is described is characters, setting or events; the wording joins the three with "or". No support condition is stated.

### Common mistakes

Describing only the picture and leaving out what the words say, or the reverse. Adding things that neither the picture nor the words show. Naming things in the picture one by one without saying what they tell about the character, the place or what happened.
