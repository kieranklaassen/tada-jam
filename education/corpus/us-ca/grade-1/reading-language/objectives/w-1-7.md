## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child takes part in group projects that combine research and writing. The example given is looking through several how-to books about one topic and then writing a set of ordered instructions based on them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a small group with an adult, the child looks through three books on planting seeds, tells the group one step found in a book, and helps put the steps in order on a shared chart. The child writes or draws one of the steps.

### Limits

The projects are shared: the child takes part in a group, and the statement does not ask for research done alone. The how-to books and the instructions are an example, not the only kind of project. The example speaks of several books without giving a figure. No support condition is stated.

### Common mistakes

Copying a sentence from a book without understanding it. Writing steps out of order, or leaving out a step. Leaving the work to others in the group, or working alone on something other than the shared topic.
