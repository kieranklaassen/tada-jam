## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child sets story characters side by side and says how their adventures and what they go through are alike and how they are different.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After two stories, or one story with two main characters, the child tells one thing that happened to both characters and one thing that happened to only one of them. The child may lay picture cards for each character in two rows and pair the cards that are alike.

### Limits

The statement speaks of characters in stories, in the plural; it does not say whether the characters come from one story or from several, and gives no number of stories. Both likenesses and differences are asked for. No support condition is stated.

### Common mistakes

Retelling each story in turn without comparing. Comparing what the characters look like instead of what happens to them. Giving only differences.
