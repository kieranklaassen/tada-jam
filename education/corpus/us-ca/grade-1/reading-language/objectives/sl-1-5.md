## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Where it fits, the child supports a description with a drawing or some other visual display, so that the feelings, thoughts and ideas in it are clearer.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While telling about a lost tooth, the child draws the moment it came out and points to the drawing while talking. The child may also hold up an object, a photo or a model made of blocks to show what words alone leave unclear.

### Limits

The display is added to a description, and only when appropriate; the statement does not require one every time. Drawings are named; other visual displays are left open. The purpose is to make ideas, thoughts and feelings clearer. No support condition is stated.

### Common mistakes

Making a drawing that is unrelated to what is being described, or one that only decorates. Showing the drawing without speaking, so that the picture replaces the description. Spending the whole time on the drawing and not finishing the description.
