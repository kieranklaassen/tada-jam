## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child says what an informational text is mostly about and tells back its important details.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After reading or hearing a short text about bees, the child says in a few words what the whole text was about ("how bees make honey") and then tells three or four important things it said, in their own words. The child may lay picture cards of the details under a card for the topic.

### Limits

The main topic asked for is that of the text as a whole; the statement does not mention topics of separate paragraphs. Details are retold, not only recognized. No number of details and no support condition are stated.

### Common mistakes

Naming one detail, often the most recent or the most striking, as the topic. Giving a topic that is too wide ("animals") for a text about one animal. Retelling facts the child knew beforehand that the text did not contain.
