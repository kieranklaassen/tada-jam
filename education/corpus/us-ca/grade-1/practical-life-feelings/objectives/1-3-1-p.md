## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name people who help others stay healthy, both where they go to school and out in the community.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child matches pictures of people to what they do: the school nurse, the kitchen staff, the caretaker who cleans, the PE teacher; the doctor, the dentist, the pharmacist, the lifeguard, the crossing guard. The child walks to the nurse's room and the school office and says who works there.

### Limits

Both the school and the community are named. No roles are listed and no number is given. Its code places it under finding sound health information.

### Common mistakes

Naming only doctors. Not counting people who keep illness or injury from happening, such as the cook or the crossing guard. Thinking these people are only for when someone is already ill.
