## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell what a person does to let someone who can help know about a danger.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child practises with a partner: go to the nearest adult they trust, say what is happening, say where, say who is hurt. On a toy phone the child presses the emergency number, gives their name and where they are, answers the questions and stays on the line. The child says their home address from memory.

### Limits

The statement names no way of reporting: no person, no phone number, no words to say. Its code places it under communicating with others, so it is about telling, not about dealing with the danger. Reporting touch that is not all right has its own statement (1.4.2.S).

### Common mistakes

Shouting "Help!" without saying what is wrong or where. Not knowing their address or the full names of the adults at home. Waiting for another person to tell.
