## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can show the right way to pick up a heavy school bag or backpack and to carry it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child bends the knees, takes the bag in both hands, holds it close to the body and stands up with a straight back. The child puts an arm through each strap and pulls the straps until the bag sits high on the back. When packing, the child puts the heaviest book against the side that touches the back.

### Limits

Two kinds of bag are named, a backpack and a bag for books, both heavy; lifting in general is not. No weight is stated. The verb is to show, although the code places the statement among the concepts to know.

### Common mistakes

Bending at the waist with straight legs to pick the bag up. Hanging the bag from one shoulder. Leaving the straps long so the bag hangs low and swings.
