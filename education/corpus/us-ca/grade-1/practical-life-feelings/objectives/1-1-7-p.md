## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child talks about the ways one person's actions change the community and the environment.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child drops a wrapper in the bin and talks about what the yard would look like if everyone dropped theirs on the ground. The child turns off a running tap, waters a plant, and coughs into an elbow, and for each says who or what is better off. The class picks up litter and looks at how much one group collected.

### Limits

Both the environment and the community are named. No particular behaviours are listed. Materials to reduce, reuse or recycle are the next statement (1.1.8.P).

### Common mistakes

Thinking one wrapper cannot matter, without adding up what many people do. Thinking "the environment" means far-off forests and seas and not their own street or classroom. Noticing only harmful actions and not helpful ones.
