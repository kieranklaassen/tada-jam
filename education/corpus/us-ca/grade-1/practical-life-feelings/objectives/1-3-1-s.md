## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name the people they would go to when they feel scared for their safety or feel that someone means them harm.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child traces a hand and gives each finger one helper: a parent, the teacher, the playground supervisor, a grandparent, a neighbour the family knows. For each one the child says where that person is found. The child says who they would go to in a shop or a park if none of those five were there.

### Limits

The statement gives no number of people and names no roles. It asks for a list of people; what to say to them comes in later statements (1.4.1.S and 1.4.2.S).

### Common mistakes

Naming one person only, with nobody else if that person is not there. Naming another child, a pet or a made-up hero. Having helpers for home and for school but none for anywhere else.
