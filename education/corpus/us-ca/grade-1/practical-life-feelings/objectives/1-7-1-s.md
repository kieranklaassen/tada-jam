## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child actually does the things that keep them safe, in the community, in school and where they live.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child walks in the corridor, holds the rail on the stairs, carries scissors with the points down and closed in the fist, and picks toys up off the floor. At the kerb the child stops, holds an adult's hand, looks both ways and listens before stepping out. The child asks the owner before touching a dog they do not know.

### Limits

Three settings are named. The verb is to practise: the child does these things, more than once. Naming them is an earlier statement (1.1.5.S), and rehearsing emergency plans is the next one (1.7.2.S).

### Common mistakes

Doing it only while an adult is watching. Knowing the rule and dropping it when excited, such as running into the road after a ball. Doing it in one place only, for example looking both ways at the school crossing but nowhere else.
