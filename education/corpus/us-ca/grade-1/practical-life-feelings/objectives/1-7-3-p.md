## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows how to keep themselves protected in the sun, and shows how to choose a sunscreen and put it on.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before going out the child puts on a hat with a brim all round, sunglasses and a long-sleeved top, and picks the shaded bench. With an adult the child looks at two bottles, finds the SPF number on each and picks one. The child squeezes some out and rubs it on face, ears, neck, arms, backs of hands and tops of feet, and asks for help with their back.

### Limits

Two parts are required: protecting oneself in general, and choosing and putting on sunscreen. The statement gives no SPF number, no time of day and no rule for how often to put more on. Why it matters is an earlier statement (1.1.2.P).

### Common mistakes

Missing ears, neck, nose and the tops of the feet. Using a tiny amount once and not again after swimming. Wearing a cap that shades the forehead and leaves ears and neck in the sun.
