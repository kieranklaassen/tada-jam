## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child talks about what simple warning labels, symbols and signs to do with safety are telling people.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child matches picture cards (a stop sign, a red and a green pedestrian light, an exit sign, a wet-floor sign, a poison symbol, a "no entry" circle) to what a person does, and acts it out: stops, waits, walks to the door, steps round the puddle, leaves the bottle shut. On a walk through the school the child points to each sign and tells a partner what it says to do.

### Limits

"Basic" is the only limit stated; no list of signs is given. The child talks about meaning; reading the full text of a label is not mentioned. Finding warning labels on products is the statement before this one (1.1.2.S).

### Common mistakes

Going by colour alone, for example taking every red sign to mean stop. Reading a picture literally, such as taking a skull to mean pirates and not poison. Knowing what a sign is called but not what a person should do when they see it.
