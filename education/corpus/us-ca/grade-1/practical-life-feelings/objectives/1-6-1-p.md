## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child draws up a plan for caring for their teeth and keeping their body clean.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child draws a strip of pictures for the morning and one for the evening: brush teeth, wash face, bath or shower. The child adds pictures for moments that are not tied to the clock: wash hands before eating and after the toilet. The child puts the strips up next to the basin and says when each step happens.

### Limits

The statement covers both teeth and personal hygiene. It asks for a plan; showing brushing, flossing and hand washing comes under later statements (1.7.1.P and 1.7.2.P). It states no number of times a day. Its code places it under goal setting.

### Common mistakes

Making a plan with no "when" in it, such as "be clean". Putting in so many steps that it is dropped after two days. Listing morning and night only and forgetting hand washing before meals and after the toilet.
