## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can give reasons that a person needs sleeping and resting in order to grow as they should and stay well.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells how they feel after a short night (grumpy, slow, unable to listen) and after a full night. The child puts a doll to bed and tells it why: "Your body grows while you sleep, and you will have energy to play." The child names quiet things to do when tired in the daytime: lying down, looking at a book, sitting in the shade.

### Limits

The statement names both sleep and rest, and both growth and health. It gives no number of hours and no bedtime. Its code places it under analysing what influences health, so the child explains a cause; a bedtime routine is not asked for.

### Common mistakes

Seeing sleep as lost play time or as a punishment. Not linking a bad mood or a lack of energy today to going to bed late yesterday. Thinking rest only means sleeping at night, so a quiet break in the day does not count.
