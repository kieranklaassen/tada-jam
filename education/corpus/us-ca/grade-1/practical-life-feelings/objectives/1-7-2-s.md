## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child rehearses the plans made for a fire, for other emergencies and for keeping safe, both in school and where they live.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In school the child stops when the alarm sounds, leaves everything, lines up, walks out quietly and stands at the class meeting spot until names are called. With the family the child walks two ways out of their bedroom, goes to the meeting spot outside, and practises crawling low and touching a door with the back of the hand before opening it.

### Limits

Two settings are named, home and school; the community is not. Three kinds of plan are named. The statement does not say how often. Behaviour during drills is also asked for in the personal health group (1.7.4.P).

### Common mistakes

Hiding under a bed or in a cupboard. Going back for a toy or a pet. Treating a drill as play: running, talking, and missing the adult's instructions.
