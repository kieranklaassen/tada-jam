## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

On the playground and in class, the child prompts others to act safely.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says in a friendly voice, "Walk, please, the floor is wet," or "One at a time on the slide." The child shows a new classmate how scissors are carried and pushes their own chair in so nobody trips. The child draws a picture of one playground rule and tells the class about it.

### Limits

Two places are named: the classroom and the playground. "Others" is not defined. The verb is to encourage; the statement does not ask the child to enforce rules.

### Common mistakes

Shouting orders, or reporting every small thing to the teacher, and calling that encouraging. Reminding others and not doing it themselves. Grabbing or holding another child to stop them.
