## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child thinks through what to do, one action after another, when there is an emergency or when things could turn dangerous.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

With cards for one scene (smoke in the corridor; a friend who fell from the climbing frame and is not getting up; matches found on the pavement) the child lays out what to do in order: stop, move away from the danger, tell an adult or call for help, wait in a safe spot. The child says why that order, and what could go wrong if two cards were swapped.

### Limits

The statement covers emergencies and also situations that might become dangerous. It names no steps and no situations. Recognising an emergency is a separate statement (1.1.9.P), and rehearsing plans is another (1.7.2.S).

### Common mistakes

Wanting to deal with the danger themselves first: putting out the fire, lifting the friend. Going back for a toy or a pet. Hiding or standing still when the plan is to go to an adult.
