## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can pick out which materials people could recycle, use again, or use less of.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sorts clean items by hand (newspaper, a drinks can, a plastic bottle, a glass jar, a cardboard box, a banana peel) into recycling, rubbish and "keep to use again". The child gives a jar a new job as a pencil pot and draws on the back of a used sheet. The child names one thing the class could use fewer of, such as paper towels.

### Limits

Three actions are named. The statement lists no materials. It does not mention local collection rules or the recycling symbol.

### Common mistakes

Calling all three actions "recycling". Putting anything made of plastic, or anything dirty, into the recycling. Having no example for using less, which is the hardest of the three to see.
