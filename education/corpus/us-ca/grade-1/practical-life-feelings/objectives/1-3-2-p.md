## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can give reasons that a guardian or parent keeps a record of their child's health.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

With a parent the child looks at their own vaccination card or growth chart and finds a date, a height and a weight. Playing clinic, the child measures a doll, writes a mark for each "injection" and puts the paper in a folder. The child says what the paper is for: "So the doctor knows which shots I have had and what I am allergic to."

### Limits

The statement asks for the reason only. It does not say what a health record holds, and it does not ask the child to keep or read one.

### Common mistakes

Thinking the record matters only when they are ill. Assuming the doctor remembers everything about every child. Mixing it up with a school report.
