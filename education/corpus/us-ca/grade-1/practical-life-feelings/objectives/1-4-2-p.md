## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

In an emergency, the child shows that they can use the skills of communicating well.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

On a toy phone the child presses the emergency number and says, "My grandma fell and cannot get up," then gives their name and address, answers each question and stays on the line until told to stop. In the playground version the child runs to the nearest adult and says what happened and where.

### Limits

The statement names no particular skills, no phone number and no words to say. Which situations count as emergencies is a separate statement (1.1.9.P).

### Common mistakes

Speaking too fast or crying so the words cannot be understood. Not knowing their address. Ending the call too soon, or not calling for fear of being in trouble.
