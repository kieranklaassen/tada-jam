## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can say why staying protected from the sun matters.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells what a sunburn feels like and how long it lasted. On the playground at midday the child finds the shade and stands in it. The child puts a hat on a doll before "taking it outside" and says, "Too much sun burns skin, even when the air is not hot."

### Limits

The statement names no ways of protecting oneself; those are in a later statement (1.7.3.P). It gives no times of day and no weather.

### Common mistakes

Thinking no protection is needed on a cloudy or cool day. Believing only people with light skin can be harmed. Thinking a burn does no harm because it goes away.
