## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child teaches family members and children of their own age how to keep the sun from harming their skin.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reminds a younger brother to put his hat on, and holds out the sunscreen to a friend before recess. The child shows a friend the places people miss: ears, neck, nose. The child draws a picture with a hat, shade and sunscreen in it and explains it to the family at home.

### Limits

The people to be taught are family and peers. The statement is about harm to skin; it does not mention eyes or heat. How to protect oneself is shown under an earlier statement (1.7.3.P).

### Common mistakes

Nagging or frightening people when the task is to explain. Passing on a wrong idea, such as that protection is only needed on a beach. Telling others and not doing it themselves.
