## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name things people do to stop communicable diseases, the illnesses that pass from one person to another, from spreading.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sorts picture cards and says what each shows: washing hands with soap, sneezing into the elbow, putting a used tissue in the bin, staying home when ill, keeping one's own cup and toothbrush, getting a vaccine at the clinic. The child picks the card that fits a moment in the day, such as "just before lunch".

### Limits

The statement asks for ways and lists none; it names no diseases. Showing the ways is a later statement (1.7.2.P). The difference between diseases that spread and diseases that do not is a separate statement (1.1.6.P).

### Common mistakes

Covering a cough with the hand and then touching things. Thinking medicine or vitamins are what stops an illness from spreading. Treating every illness as catching, and so keeping away from a child with asthma.
