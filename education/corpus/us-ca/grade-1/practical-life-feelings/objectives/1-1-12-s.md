## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can say what a few easy ways of settling a disagreement are.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

With a partner and one toy that both want, the child names a way and acts it out: taking turns, sharing, choosing by a quick hand game, saying "I don't like it when you grab", walking away to calm down, asking an adult to help. The child says in a sentence what "taking turns" means.

### Limits

"Simple" is the only limit; the statement lists no techniques and gives no number. The child says what the ways are; using them in a real quarrel is not asked here. What is gained by settling things without violence is a later statement (1.5.2.S).

### Common mistakes

Thinking a conflict is settled only when one side gets everything it wanted. Treating "tell the teacher" as the only way. Repeating a phrase such as "use your words" without being able to say which words.
