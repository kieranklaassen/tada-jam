## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows the right way to act in a drill for a fire, for an earthquake or for any other disaster.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a fire drill the child stops at once, leaves coat and bag, lines up, walks without talking, follows the teacher to the meeting area and answers when their name is called. In an earthquake drill the child drops to the floor, gets under a desk, covers head and neck with one arm and holds a desk leg until the adult says it is over.

### Limits

Fire and earthquake are named, and other disasters are left open. The statement is about drills and does not say where they take place. Rehearsing plans in the home as well as in school is a statement in the safety group (1.7.2.S).

### Common mistakes

Talking, laughing or pushing because "it is only practice". Stopping to fetch a coat or a bag. Mixing up the two responses: running for the door when the ground shakes, or getting under a desk when the fire alarm sounds.
