## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name things people do to make getting hurt less likely, in the community, in school and where they live.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

For each danger in a picture the child says or shows the fix: picks the toys up off the stairs, turns the pot handle inward, walks and does not run beside the pool, holds an adult's hand in the car park, tells an adult about a spill. The child pairs a "danger" card with a "what to do" card.

### Limits

The same three settings as in the statement before it, with no particular injuries named. Naming is asked for here; doing these things for real is a later statement (1.7.1.S). Travel by car or bus has its own statement (1.1.10.S).

### Common mistakes

Answering "be careful" without naming an action. Thinking only adults can make a place less dangerous. Reciting a rule without being able to say which danger it is for.
