## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell about the gear and equipment that protect the body and when each is the right one to use.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child matches each item to an activity and to the part of the body it covers: knee and elbow pads and wrist guards for skating, a life jacket for a boat, shin guards for football, a mouth guard, goggles. The child puts one item on, fastens every strap and shows that it does not slip.

### Limits

The statement names no gear and no activity. The verb is to explain, although the code places the statement among behaviours to practise. Helmets for cycling and skating have their own statement (1.5.4.S), and protection from the sun is in the personal health group (1.7.3.P).

### Common mistakes

Wearing gear loose or unfastened. Thinking gear is only for people who are still learning. Believing that with gear on, a risky move cannot hurt.
