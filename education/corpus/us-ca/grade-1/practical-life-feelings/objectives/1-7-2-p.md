## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows how to keep illness from spreading. The examples given are washing hands often and covering a cough or a sneeze.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At the basin the child wets both hands, soaps them, rubs palms, backs, between the fingers, thumbs and nails for the length of a short song, rinses and dries on a clean towel. The child coughs or sneezes into the bend of the elbow, or into a tissue that goes straight in the bin, and then washes.

### Limits

Two examples are given, as examples. The statement gives no length of time for washing. Naming such ways is an earlier statement (1.1.4.P); here the child does them.

### Common mistakes

Rinsing without soap, or for two or three seconds. Missing thumbs, nails and the gaps between fingers. Coughing into the palm and then touching things.
