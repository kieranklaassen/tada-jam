## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows they can be a support to schoolmates living with a long-lasting (chronic) disease or condition. The examples given are epilepsy, diabetes, allergies and asthma.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child keeps their peanut snack away from a friend with an allergy and washes hands after eating it. When a classmate has trouble breathing, or falls and shakes, the child fetches an adult at once and moves back to leave space. The child walks with a friend to the office for their medicine, saves them a place in the group, and does not tease about an inhaler or a snack eaten at an odd time.

### Limits

Four conditions are given, as examples. The statement does not say what support consists of. Describing the symptoms of such conditions is an earlier statement (1.1.5.P).

### Common mistakes

Keeping away from the classmate for fear of catching the condition. Trying to do what only an adult should, such as giving medicine or holding down someone who is shaking. Staring or teasing, or doing everything for the classmate as if they could do nothing.
