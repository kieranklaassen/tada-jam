## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child goes through the steps of making a choice in order to judge how their own cleanliness habits help keep them healthy.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a real moment of choice (wash hands before the snack or start eating; brush before bed or skip it) the child says the two choices aloud, says what would follow from each, picks one and does it. Later the child tells how it went. The child lays three cards in order: "my choices", "what happens next", "what I pick".

### Limits

Only personal hygiene is covered. A process is required, but the statement does not set out its steps. Making a hygiene plan is the next statement in this group (1.6.1.P).

### Common mistakes

Choosing whatever is quickest and skipping the thinking about what follows. Judging only by today ("nothing bad happened"). Giving the answer an adult wants without a reason.
