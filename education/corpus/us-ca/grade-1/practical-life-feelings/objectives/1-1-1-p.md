## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can say why it matters to look after teeth and keep the body clean in ways that really work.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells what happens when teeth are not brushed (food stays on them, holes form, a tooth aches) and when hands and body are not washed (germs spread, skin smells, people get ill). The child runs their tongue over their teeth before brushing and after it and says how they feel different. The child looks at the water after washing muddy hands.

### Limits

The statement covers both teeth and the rest of the body. It names no particular practices and no number of times a day. It asks for the reason; showing how to brush and floss is a later statement (1.7.1.P), and making a plan is another (1.6.1.P).

### Common mistakes

Thinking baby teeth do not matter because they fall out. Taking "looks clean" to mean clean, since germs cannot be seen. Counting a quick rinse with water as washing.
