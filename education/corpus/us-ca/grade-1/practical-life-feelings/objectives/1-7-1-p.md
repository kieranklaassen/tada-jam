## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows the correct way to brush their teeth and to floss.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child puts a pea-sized amount of paste on the brush and makes small circles on the outer sides, the inner sides and the chewing surfaces, along the gum line, then brushes the tongue and spits. With floss the child slides the thread down gently, curves it round one tooth, moves it up and down and goes on to the next gap. On a large model of a mouth the child shows a partner each surface.

### Limits

Both brushing and flossing are required. The statement gives no length of time and no number of times a day. It does not mention help from an adult.

### Common mistakes

Scrubbing the front teeth only and missing the inner sides and the back teeth. Pressing hard and stopping after a few seconds. Snapping the floss down onto the gum.
