## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When asking for help with a problem to do with health, the child shows that they can use the skills of communicating well.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child goes up to an adult, gets their attention ("Excuse me, Ms Lee") and says what is wrong, where and since when, pointing to the place: "My tummy hurts here, since lunch." "I feel dizzy." "I need my inhaler." With a partner as the nurse, the child answers questions: "Does it hurt a lot or a little?"

### Limits

The statement is about health problems in general; emergencies have their own statement (1.4.2.P). It names no particular skills and does not say whom the child asks.

### Common mistakes

Saying only "I don't feel good" with no place or kind of feeling. Waiting silently, or crying, in the hope that an adult notices. Telling a friend and not an adult.
