## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name what is gained when a quarrel is settled without violence.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child acts out two endings to one quarrel over a ball, one with grabbing and one with taking turns, and says what came next each time: nobody is hurt, the two are still friends, the play carries on, nobody is sent inside. The child draws the two endings side by side.

### Limits

The statement asks for benefits and lists none. The ways of settling a quarrel are an earlier statement (1.1.12.S). Its code places it under decision making, so the child compares outcomes of a choice.

### Common mistakes

Seeing hitting back as fair and as the only thing that stops the other child. Thinking that not fighting means losing. Naming only "I won't get told off" and no gain for the other child.
