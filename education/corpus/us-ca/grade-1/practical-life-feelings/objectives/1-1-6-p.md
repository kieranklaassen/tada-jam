## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can say what sets a disease that can be passed on (communicable) apart from one that cannot be passed on (noncommunicable).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sorts cards into "you can catch it" (a cold, the flu, chickenpox, pink eye) and "you cannot catch it" (asthma, an allergy, diabetes). The child says one sentence for each pile: "A cold can come from someone's sneeze." "Sitting next to a friend with asthma cannot give me asthma."

### Limits

Two groups only are named, and no particular diseases. The statement does not ask what causes each kind. Germs are discussed in a neighbouring statement (1.1.3.P).

### Common mistakes

Thinking every disease can be caught, and so avoiding a classmate whose condition does not spread. Thinking a person who does not look ill cannot pass anything on. Mixing up "serious" with "catching".
