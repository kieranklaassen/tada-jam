## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name how they would let someone know about touch that is not all right.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child names adults they could tell (a parent, the teacher, the school nurse) and practises a plain sentence: "Someone touched me and I did not like it." The child says what they do if the first adult is busy or does not understand: tell again, or tell another adult.

### Limits

The statement lists no particular ways and no particular people. Telling the kinds of touch apart is an earlier statement (1.1.7.S), and refusing is another (1.1.13.S).

### Common mistakes

Believing it has to stay secret because the person said so. Expecting to be in trouble for telling. Hinting and then giving up when the first adult does not understand.
