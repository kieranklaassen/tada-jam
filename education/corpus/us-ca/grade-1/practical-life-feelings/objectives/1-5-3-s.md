## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child judges why it should be made known that someone has a weapon.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears a story: a classmate shows something in his bag and says, "Don't tell." The child says what could happen if nobody tells and what happens if somebody does, and then says which is better and for whom, including the classmate.

### Limits

The statement is about the reasons, and it follows the earlier statement on weapons (1.1.9.S). It does not say who receives the report. A footnote cites the Education Code.

### Common mistakes

Staying silent out of loyalty to a friend. Thinking it only needs telling if the person has said they will use it. Calling it tattling.
