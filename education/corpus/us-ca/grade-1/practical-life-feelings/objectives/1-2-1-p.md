## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell in what ways friends and family shape a person's good health habits.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says who taught them to brush their teeth, who reminds them to wash their hands, and which friend asks them to run and climb at recess. The child tells about a time they copied someone: "My friend ate carrots at lunch, so I tried one." The child draws one person and the habit they got from them.

### Limits

Two groups are named, family and friends. The statement is about positive practices; it does not mention unhealthy influence. Other sources, such as screens or advertising, are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Saying "I just do it myself" and not noticing whom they copy. Assuming whatever friends or family do must be good for the body. Counting only being told, and not seeing that watching someone is also an influence.
