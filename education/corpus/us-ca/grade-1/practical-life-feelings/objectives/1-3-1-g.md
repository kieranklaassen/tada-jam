## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child knows that information about growing and developing can be got from a parent, a guardian or another grown-up they trust.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child names two or three grown-ups they would ask, and says where each one is usually found. The child practises asking a question aloud: "Why did my tooth fall out?" or "Will I be as tall as my brother?" When a classmate gives a doubtful answer, the child says, "Let's ask the teacher."

### Limits

The statement names parents and guardians and leaves the other trusted adults open. It is about knowing whom to ask, not about what the answers are. It does not mention books, screens or other children as places to find answers.

### Common mistakes

Asking only other children and taking their answers as true. Thinking any adult will do, including one they do not know. Believing questions about the body are not allowed, and so not asking at all.
