## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child talks about why it matters to keep germs from spreading.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

With a little glitter on one child's palm, the children shake hands and touch a door handle, then look at where the glitter went and talk about it. The child tells what could happen to a class when one child with a cold shares a cup. The child says who at home could catch what they bring back.

### Limits

The statement asks for talk about the reason. Naming ways to stop diseases spreading is the next statement (1.1.4.P), and showing them is a later one (1.7.2.P). No particular germs or illnesses are named.

### Common mistakes

Thinking germs are dirt that can be seen, so hands that look clean carry none. Believing germs come only from dirty things and not from a person's cough or sneeze. Thinking a cold comes from cold weather or wet hair.
