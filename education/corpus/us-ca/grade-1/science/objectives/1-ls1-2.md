## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

From texts and other media, the child works out patterns in what parents and their young do that help the young stay alive. The clarification's examples are the signals the young give, such as cheeping, crying and other calls, and what the parents do in answer, such as protecting, comforting and feeding them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks through picture books and watches short films of birds, cats and penguins with their young, and says what the young one did and what the parent did next. The child sorts picture cards into "young one calls" and "parent helps" and pairs them. The child says what is the same across the animals.

### Limits

The expectation states no assessment boundary. It names texts and media as where the child finds the patterns; it does not mention observing live animals. The signals and responses in the clarification are examples.

### Common mistakes

Describing one animal family and not looking for what repeats across several. Assuming every animal parent looks after its young the way human parents do. Reporting what the parent does without the signal from the young that came before it.
