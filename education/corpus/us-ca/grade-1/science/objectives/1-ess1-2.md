## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child observes at several points in the year and connects how much daylight there is with the time of year. The clarification puts the emphasis on comparing winter daylight with daylight in fall or spring, in terms of more and less.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In winter the child notices whether it is light or dark when they eat dinner or leave school, and draws it. The child does the same in spring and puts the drawings side by side. The child says which season had more daylight.

### Limits

The assessment boundary asks only for more or less daylight; putting a number of hours or a clock time on it is left out. The clarification compares winter with spring or fall; it does not name summer.

### Common mistakes

Thinking the whole day, not the lit part of it, gets longer or shorter. Confusing a dark, cloudy day with a short day. Linking daylight to bedtime rules and not to the time of year.
