## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child judges what difference keeping the rules makes to how safe people are. The rules meant are those of the community, the school and the family.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child picks one rule from each setting, such as no running by the pool, walking in the school corridor, and telling a parent where they are going. For each the child says what could happen with the rule and without it. The child decides which rule protects the most people and explains why.

### Limits

The statement names three sources of rules and asks for a judgement of their effect. It lists no rules. Keeping the rules in daily life is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often see a rule as an adult's wish and not as protection against a known danger. Many think a rule matters only when an adult is watching. Some judge a rule useless because they once broke it and nothing happened.
