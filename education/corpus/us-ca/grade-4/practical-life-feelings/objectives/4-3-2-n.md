## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child reads the label on a food package to find out the amounts of sugar and of nutrients in the food.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child picks up two real packages, such as two breakfast cereals, finds the nutrition facts panel, and puts a finger on the serving size line and then on the sugar line. The child reads the grams of sugar per serving on each and says which has more. The child finds protein, fibre or a vitamin on the same panel.

### Limits

The statement names sugar and nutrients in general and does not say which nutrients. It does not ask for calculations such as percentages. It names the label as a whole and no one part of it.

### Common mistakes

Children often read the number for one serving as the number for the whole package. Many compare two products without checking that the serving sizes match. Some look only at the front of the pack.
