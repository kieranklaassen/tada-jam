## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows, by doing them, ways of keeping out of bullying and out of harassment of other kinds.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child rehearses walking with a friend or a group, staying where adults can see, and answering a taunt once in a calm, firm voice before walking off. The child rehearses standing straight and looking ahead. The child rehearses not forwarding an unkind message and blocking the sender.

### Limits

The statement asks the child to carry the strategies out and names none of them. It does not say whether it means not being a target, not taking part, or both.

### Common mistakes

Children often answer a taunt with a bigger insult, which keeps it going. Many think avoiding means hiding or staying home from school. Some believe ignoring always works and keep silent when the bullying continues, though they should tell an adult.
