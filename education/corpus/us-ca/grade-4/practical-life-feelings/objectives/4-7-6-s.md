## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows they can carry out a plan for getting to safety when an earthquake, a flood, a fire or another natural disaster happens.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child walks the planned route from their seat or their bed to the meeting place without stopping for belongings. In a fire drill the child feels a door for heat with the back of the hand, stays low, and goes out by the second exit when the first is blocked. In an earthquake drill the child takes cover first and leaves only when the shaking has ended.

### Limits

The statement names three events and adds further natural disasters as an open group. It asks the child to carry the plan out. It does not say where the plan applies. Naming the steps of getting ready is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often apply the fire plan to an earthquake and run out during the shaking. Many go back for a pet, a toy or a phone. Some hide in a closet or under a bed during a fire, where rescuers cannot find them.
