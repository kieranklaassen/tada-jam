## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells how to get help when something worries them, when someone threatens them, or when they are being abused.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child names three adults they could go to and practices the opening words: "Something happened and I need help." The child says what to do if the first adult does not listen: tell another one. The child finds the school counselor's room and learns that a call to an emergency number is one more way to reach help.

### Limits

The statement names three conditions and leaves the ways of getting help open. It asks for describing and does not define abuse. Naming safe people and places is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often think telling an adult is tattling, or that they will be punished for it. Many stop after one adult fails to act. Some believe a promise to keep a secret must be kept even when the secret is about being hurt.
