## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child looks closely at the methods adverts use to sell tobacco, alcohol and other drugs.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child studies a printed advert for beer or an old cigarette poster and circles what it shows: happy friends, sport, bright colours, a cartoon figure, a slogan. The child says what the advert leaves out, such as illness or cost, and who it seems to be aimed at.

### Limits

The statement is about adverts only. It names no media and gives no list of methods. The child studies existing adverts; making a counter-advert is not asked for.

### Common mistakes

Children often take an advert as plain information and do not see that it is made to sell. Many think they can recognise every advert and miss product placement or sponsorship. Some judge the product by how much they like the people shown.
