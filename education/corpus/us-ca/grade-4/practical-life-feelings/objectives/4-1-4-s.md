## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child looks closely at how harassment and bullying affect other people.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a short story about a classmate who is teased every day and says how that classmate might feel, sleep, learn and behave afterwards. The child draws or tells what changes for the children who watch: fear, silence, joining in. The child compares a class where this happens with one where it does not.

### Limits

The statement names no particular effects and no time span. It says "on others" and does not say who those others are. Naming the forms of bullying is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often think the harm ends when the teasing stops and do not connect it with later sadness, stomach aches or not wanting to go to school. Many believe words and exclusion hurt less than hitting. They rarely notice that children who only watch are affected too.
