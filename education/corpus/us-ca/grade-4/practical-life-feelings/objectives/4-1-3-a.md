## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child says how a medicine differs from a drug that the law forbids.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child holds an empty, clean medicine box and reads who it is for, how much to take and who prescribed it. The child then says why a pill offered by a stranger or a friend is not the same: nobody checked it, no doctor or parent chose it, and the law does not allow it. The child sorts picture cards into two groups, "given by a trusted adult to help the body" and "not allowed".

### Limits

The statement names two groups only and asks the child to explain how they differ. Alcohol and tobacco are not mentioned in this item. Misuse of a medicine, such as taking another person's or taking too much, is not mentioned either.

### Common mistakes

Children often think every medicine is safe in any amount because it comes from a pharmacy. Some judge a substance by how it looks, such as a powder or a needle. Many do not know that a medicine prescribed for one person can harm another.
