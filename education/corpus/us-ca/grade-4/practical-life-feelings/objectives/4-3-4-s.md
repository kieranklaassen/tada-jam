## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names grown-ups they trust and can tell when someone might harm another person or harm themselves.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lists by name the adults they would tell: a parent, a teacher, the school counselor, the principal, a coach. The child rehearses the words for it: "My friend said he wants to hurt himself" or "He said he is going to hurt someone tomorrow." The child says why the telling happens at once and without first asking the friend's permission.

### Limits

The statement asks for naming the adults and lists none. It covers risk to the person themselves and risk to other people.

### Common mistakes

Children often keep a friend's secret out of loyalty. Many think a threat was a joke and not worth telling. Some tell another child and assume that counts as reporting.
