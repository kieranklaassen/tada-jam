## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child makes their own promise to wear the right safety gear for whatever they are doing.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes or says a pledge in their own words, for example "I will put my helmet on before every bike ride, even a short one." The child hangs the helmet on the bike's handlebar so it is within reach. The child tells a family member the pledge and asks to be reminded.

### Limits

The statement is a goal the child sets for themselves and names no gear and no pursuit. It sets no time span for the pledge. Naming the gear and wearing it are separate grade 4 statements.

### Common mistakes

Children often make a broad promise ("I'll always be safe") that names no action. Many keep the pledge only when an adult is present. Some drop it when friends ride without gear.
