## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows they can read the label on an everyday product kept in the house and act on it. The label points concern four things: what the hazards are, how to handle the product safely, where to keep it, and how to throw it away correctly.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes an empty, rinsed container of dish soap, glue or bleach and finds the signal word, such as Caution, Warning or Danger. The child reads aloud what to do if the product gets in the eyes and where it is to be kept. The child sorts several empty containers by how each must be thrown away: trash, recycling, or a collection point.

### Limits

The statement covers ordinary products found in the house and four label topics. It asks for reading and for acting on what is read. What to do after a poisoning is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often read the brand name and the front picture and skip the small print on the back. Many do not know that Danger signals a higher hazard than Caution. Some think an empty container is harmless and can go in any bin or be refilled with a drink.
