## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows how to say no in a way that keeps them out of a gang.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child role-plays being asked by an older child to carry a package, wear certain colors or join a group at the corner, and answers with a firm no. The child gives a short reason or none, names somewhere else they have to be, and leaves. The child rehearses the same answer when the offer comes with a gift or a threat.

### Limits

The statement asks the child to show the refusal and gives no script. Saying no in other risky situations is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often think an offer from a relative or a friendly older neighbor cannot be gang recruiting. Many believe a small favor carries no commitment. Some think refusing once ends the pressure and are unprepared for the second request.
