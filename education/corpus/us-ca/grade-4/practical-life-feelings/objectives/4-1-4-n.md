## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names the right ways of preparing and storing food so that it stays safe to eat.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child washes hands with soap before touching food and rinses an apple under the tap. The child puts milk and leftovers back in the fridge, keeps raw meat on a different board from salad, and checks the date on a package. The child says which lunchbox items need an ice pack.

### Limits

The statement names two areas, preparation and storage, and gives no temperatures or times. Why unsafe food makes people ill is the next item (4.1.5.N).

### Common mistakes

Children often judge by smell or look alone whether food is safe. Many rinse their hands with water only, or wash only after eating. Some believe food left out for hours becomes safe again once it is back in the fridge.
