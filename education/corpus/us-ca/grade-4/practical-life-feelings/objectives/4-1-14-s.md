## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names the protective gear a person needs for a given sport or leisure pursuit. Helmets, pads and mouthpieces are the examples given.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays out real items and matches them to pursuits: a helmet with cycling and skateboarding, shin guards with soccer, a mouthpiece with football, wrist guards with skating. The child packs a bag for one chosen sport and checks that nothing is missing. The child says which body part each item protects.

### Limits

The three items are examples, not a full list, and no sports are named. The statement asks for naming the gear. The reason it matters and the wearing of it are separate grade 4 statements.

### Common mistakes

Children often think a helmet is the only gear that counts. Many use one helmet for every pursuit and do not know that a bicycle helmet and a batting helmet are built differently. Some leave out gear for pursuits they see as play, such as scooters and trampolines.
