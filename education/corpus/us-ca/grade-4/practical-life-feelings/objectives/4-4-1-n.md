## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows that they can ask, clearly and in a way that works, to be given healthy options to eat.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says to a parent in the shop, "Can we get apples for my lunchbox this week?", in a polite and clear voice. In a role-play the child asks a cafeteria worker for the vegetable side dish, or asks a friend's parent for water in place of soda. The child gives a reason and says thank you.

### Limits

Standard 4 is interpersonal communication: the child shows the asking, which is more than knowing which foods are healthy. The statement does not say whom the child asks or where.

### Common mistakes

Children often complain about what is offered and make no request. Many ask so vaguely ("something healthy") that the adult cannot act on it. Some stop after one refusal, or feel it is rude to ask in another person's home.
