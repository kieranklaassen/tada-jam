## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises being the one responsible for their own physical activity.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child goes outside to play after school without being sent, puts on their own shoes and helmet, and asks a friend to join. At break the child chooses a running game. The child turns off the screen at the agreed time and starts the activity they had planned.

### Limits

Standard 7 is practising health-enhancing behaviours: the child does the behaviour. The statement gives no minutes, no kind of activity and no setting. A plan for activity is a separate item (4.6.2.N).

### Common mistakes

Children often wait for an adult to organise activity. Many count the whole time at a sports practice as active when most of it was spent standing. Some stop as soon as they are a little tired or the weather is poor.
