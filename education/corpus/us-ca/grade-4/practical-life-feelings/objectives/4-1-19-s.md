## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows how to get away when someone touches them in a way that is not right or tries to take them away.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child rehearses in a role play led by a trusted teacher: says "No!" in a loud voice, steps back out of reach, runs toward other people, and tells a trusted adult right away. The child shouts words that make bystanders look, such as "This is not my parent!" The child names the nearest safe place on the way to and from school.

### Limits

The statement names two situations and lists no particular actions. It asks the child to show the actions, which goes beyond talking about them. It sits under essential concepts; a second grade 4 statement, under practicing healthy behaviors, has the same wording.

### Common mistakes

Children often think danger comes only from strangers and do not expect it from someone they know. Many believe it is rude to shout at an adult or to disobey one. Some freeze, or keep it secret afterwards because the adult told them to.
