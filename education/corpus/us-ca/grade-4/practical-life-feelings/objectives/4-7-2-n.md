## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises keeping down, by their own effort, the sugar they take in from what they drink, snack on and eat.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child pours water or milk in place of soda and chooses fruit over sweets for the afternoon snack. The child checks the sugar line on a label before picking a cereal bar. When sweets are offered at a party, the child takes a small amount and then stops.

### Limits

The statement is about less sugar, not none, and gives no amount in grams. It names three sources: food, snacks and drinks. Finding the sugar on a label is 4.3.2.N.

### Common mistakes

Children often do not count drinks, including juice and flavoured milk, as sugar. Many think only sweets contain sugar and miss it in sauces, yoghurt and cereal. Some believe a food labelled "fruit" or "natural" has no sugar.
