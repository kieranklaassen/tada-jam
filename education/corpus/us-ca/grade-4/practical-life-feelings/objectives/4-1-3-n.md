## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells how eating, moving the body and being healthy are linked.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child explains with a simple drawing that food gives energy and that moving uses it. The child describes two days, one with a long football practice and one spent sitting, and says how hunger and thirst differ between them. The child says what happens over months when a person eats much more than the body uses, or too little.

### Limits

The statement asks for a description of the link among three things and gives no numbers. Calories are not mentioned. The gains from activity alone are a separate item (4.1.7.N).

### Common mistakes

Children often think exercise makes any food choice harmless. Many think healthy means thin. Some see food only as a source of energy and miss that it also builds and repairs the body.
