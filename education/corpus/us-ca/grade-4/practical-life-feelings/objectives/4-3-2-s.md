## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows how to reach emergency help and how to speak with the responders so that they understand.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child role-plays a call with a partner who plays the dispatcher: says what happened, gives the address, gives their name, answers each question and stays on the line until told to hang up. The child rehearses speaking slowly and loudly enough. The child tells a pretend paramedic at the door where the hurt person is.

### Limits

The statement asks the child to show two things: reaching help, and speaking with the people who answer or arrive. It names no phone number. Phoning 9-1-1 is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often hang up as soon as they have said what is wrong. Many do not know their full address and say only "my house". Some are afraid they will be punished for calling and wait too long.
