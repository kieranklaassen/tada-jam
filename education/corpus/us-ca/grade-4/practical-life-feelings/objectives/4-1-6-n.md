## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child says why drinking a lot of water matters, and most of all while a person is exercising hard.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child fills a water bottle before a PE lesson and drinks at each break in a running activity. The child says what water does: it replaces sweat, cools the body and helps the child continue. The child names signs of too little water, such as thirst, a dry mouth, a headache or dark urine.

### Limits

The statement gives no amount of water per day. It names hard exercise in particular and does not mention heat or illness. It does not compare water with other drinks; sugar in drinks is 4.7.2.N.

### Common mistakes

Children often wait until they are very thirsty before they drink. Many think juice or soda does the same job as water. Some believe drinking during sport causes a side pain and so avoid it.
