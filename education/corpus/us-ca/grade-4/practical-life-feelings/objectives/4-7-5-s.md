## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child acts out ways of getting free and reaching safety in two situations: an attempt to abduct them, and touching that is not appropriate.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child rehearses a set sequence until it is quick: shout no, pull away, run to a place with people, tell. The child tries twisting a wrist out of a grip toward the holder's thumb, and dropping a held backpack to run. The child repeats the telling step with a second adult when the first does not act.

### Limits

The statement names two situations and no particular actions. It sits under practicing healthy behaviors, so the child carries the actions out. A grade 4 statement under essential concepts has the same wording.

### Common mistakes

Children often rehearse quietly and politely, though a real escape needs a loud voice. Many run to hide alone where no one can see them when they should go toward people. Some think that once they are away they do not need to tell anyone.
