## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names ways to make getting hurt less likely in six situations: falls, the playground, going on foot, riding in a motor vehicle, being around water, and fires.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes one situation at a time and states a rule for it: buckle the seat belt on every ride, look left, right and left again before crossing, swim only with an adult watching, stop, drop and roll if clothing catches fire. The child walks the playground and points out where to wait for a turn and where the ground is hard. The child checks a stairway for things someone could trip on.

### Limits

The statement names six situations and sets no number of ways for each. It asks for naming them and does not ask the child to carry them out. Describing the hazards themselves is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often know the rule but believe it applies only on long or fast journeys, so they skip the seat belt on a short ride. Many think a driver can always see them and stop in time. Some believe floats or water wings make deep water safe.
