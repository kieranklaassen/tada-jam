## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names what can shape a person's choice about using tobacco, alcohol or other drugs. Some of these things come from inside the person and some from outside.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child draws the outline of a person and writes inside it what comes from within: curiosity, wanting to belong, feeling sad or bored, the person's own values. Around the outline the child writes what comes from outside: friends, family habits, adverts, films, rules. The child marks each one with a plus or a minus for whether it makes use more likely or less likely.

### Limits

Standard 2 is analysing influences: here the child names influences of both kinds, and the statement gives no list of them. A close look at adverts is the neighbouring item (4.2.2.A).

### Common mistakes

Children often name only pressure from friends and miss their own feelings as an influence. Many think an influence is always harmful and overlook a family habit or a rule that makes use less likely. Some believe adverts affect other people and not them.
