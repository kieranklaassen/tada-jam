## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names what makes a person more active or less active: some of it lies within the person, some of it around them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child thinks of last weekend and says what led to moving or to sitting. From inside: energy, mood, liking a sport, confidence. From outside: weather, a safe place to play, friends, family plans, screen rules, having a ball or a bike. The child writes each one on a card and puts it on the "inside" or the "outside" half of a sheet.

### Limits

Standard 2 is analysing influences: the child names both kinds, and the statement lists none. The same question is asked about food in 4.2.1.N. The child is not asked to change the influences.

### Common mistakes

Children often name only outside barriers such as rain and miss their own feelings, such as the fear of being chosen last. Many do not see screens as an influence. Some think influences only work against activity.
