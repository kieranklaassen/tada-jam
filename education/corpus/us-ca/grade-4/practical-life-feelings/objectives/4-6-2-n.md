## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child prepares a plan for which activities they will pick to move their body, both in the home and during the school day.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child fills in a grid for one week with two rows, one for school and one for home. In the school row the child writes break-time games and PE; in the home row, a bike ride, dancing, walking the dog. The child adds what each one needs: shoes, a ball, a friend, a parent's permission.

### Limits

The statement names two settings and gives no minutes and no number of days. Standard 6 is goal setting: the product is the plan, and doing it is 4.7.4.N.

### Common mistakes

Children often plan only for the days with organised sport and leave the other days empty. Many plan activities that depend on an adult driving them. Some write the activities they think the teacher wants and not the ones they would do.
