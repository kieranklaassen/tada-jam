## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names how a person can become more active and how to check how much they move.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child lists changes they could make: walk to school, play tag at break, take the stairs, help in the garden. The child keeps a paper log for a week with the minutes of movement on each day, or counts steps with a simple pedometer, and reads the log at the end of the week.

### Limits

The statement has two parts, adding activity and checking it, and names no tool and no target. Standard 1 is essential concepts: the child names the ways, while planning is 4.6.2.N and doing is 4.7.4.N.

### Common mistakes

Children often overestimate how long they were active and count a whole PE lesson, including the waiting. Many think checking needs a device. Some see sport as the only way to add activity.
