## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child prepares a plan for picking healthy things to eat and drink.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child writes a one-week goal, such as "water with lunch every school day" or "a fruit in my lunchbox", and the steps toward it: tell a parent, put it on the shopping list, pack it the night before. The child marks a chart each day and reads it at the end of the week.

### Limits

Standard 6 is goal setting: the product is the child's own plan. The statement covers food and drinks and sets no time span and no number of goals. Carrying the plan out belongs to standard 7 (4.7.1.N and 4.7.2.N).

### Common mistakes

Children often set a goal too broad to act on ("eat healthy"). Many plan foods the family does not buy and leave out the step of asking for them. Some plan to remove a food entirely and abandon the plan the first time they eat it.
