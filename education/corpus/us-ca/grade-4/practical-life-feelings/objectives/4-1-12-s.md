## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names good things a young person can do or join instead of taking part in a gang.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lists groups and pastimes open to them nearby: a sports team, a youth club, music, art, an after-school program, helping in a community garden. The child matches each with what it gives: friends, belonging, something to be good at, adults who watch over them. The child picks one and tells a partner about it.

### Limits

The statement names no particular choices and no number of them. Saying what a gang is and what harm it brings are separate grade 4 statements.

### Common mistakes

Children often name only what they already do and do not know what is on offer nearby. Many list pastimes without seeing which need each one meets, such as belonging or feeling safe.
