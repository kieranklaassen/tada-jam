## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

For each food group, the child says how many servings are advised and how big one serving is.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child measures out one serving with real tools: a cup of cereal, a slice of bread, a piece of fruit about as big as a fist. The child lays out picture cards of one day's eating under a heading for each food group and counts whether each group reaches the advised number.

### Limits

The statement gives neither the numbers nor the sizes, and it does not name the guideline they come from. It does not say how many food groups there are. The child only states the recommendation; making a plan with it is 4.6.1.N.

### Common mistakes

Children often take the whole package or the whole plate as one serving. Many treat "a serving" and "the amount I usually eat" as the same thing. Some count a mixed food such as pizza in one group only.
