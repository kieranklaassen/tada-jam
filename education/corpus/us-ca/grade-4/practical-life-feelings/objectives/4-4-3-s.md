## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child reports it when a person is bullied or harassed, or when something else dangerous is happening.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child rehearses a report with a teacher: who did it, what happened, where, when, and who saw it. The child writes a short note for a class report box as a second way. The child explains the difference between telling to get someone punished and telling to keep someone safe.

### Limits

The statement does not say to whom the report goes or in what form. Besides bullying it covers harassment and unsafe situations in general. Naming trusted adults is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often stay silent because they fear being called a tattletale or fear revenge. Many report only what happens to them and not what they see happen to someone else. Some give a vague report ("he's being mean") that an adult cannot act on.
