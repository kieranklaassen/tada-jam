## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child urges other people to take particular steps that would make the school or the house safer.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child spots one concrete problem, such as a smoke alarm with no battery or bags left in a classroom doorway, and asks the person in charge to fix it. The child makes a poster or gives a short talk naming the step and the reason. The child checks a week later whether it was done.

### Limits

The statement is about urging named steps and not about general appeals to be careful. It covers two settings, joined by "or", and lists no steps.

### Common mistakes

Children often make a general appeal ("be safe") that names no action. Many point out a hazard but do not say who should do what about it. Some scold instead of asking, and the listener stops listening.
