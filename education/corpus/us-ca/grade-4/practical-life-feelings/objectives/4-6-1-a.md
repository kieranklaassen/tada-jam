## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child prepares a plan for picking healthy things to do in place of using drugs or tobacco.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child lists activities they like that meet the needs people give as reasons for using: something to do when bored, a way to calm down, a way to be with friends. The child chooses two or three, such as football after school or drawing, and writes when, where and with whom.

### Limits

The statement names tobacco and drugs; alcohol is not in this item. Standard 6 is goal setting: the product is a plan. The statement does not ask for the plan to be carried out or checked afterwards.

### Common mistakes

Children often write a wish ("I will never smoke") and not a plan with steps. Many list activities they cannot get to. Some do not connect the activity with the reason a person might use.
