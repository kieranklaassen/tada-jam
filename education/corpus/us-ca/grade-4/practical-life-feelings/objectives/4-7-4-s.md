## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child keeps the laws and rules that protect people, in three settings: the community, the school and the family home.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child wears a seat belt on every ride, crosses at the crosswalk when the signal allows, and rides a bicycle on the correct side with a helmet. In school the child walks in corridors and keeps to the drill rules. In the house the child asks before turning on the stove and keeps stairs clear.

### Limits

The statement covers rules and laws alike, in three settings, and lists none. It is about keeping them in daily life. Judging their effect is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often keep a rule only where it was taught, for example walking in the school corridor but running at the public pool. Many copy an adult who breaks a law, such as crossing against the signal. Some do not know that certain rules, such as wearing a seat belt, are laws.
