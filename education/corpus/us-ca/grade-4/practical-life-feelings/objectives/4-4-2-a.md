## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child rehearses asking for help in clear spoken words at a time when people nearby are using tobacco, alcohol or other drugs.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child practises a phone call to a parent: says where they are, what is happening and what they need ("Please come and get me"). The child practises the same request face to face with a trusted adult, speaks loudly enough, and repeats it if the adult does not understand the first time.

### Limits

The statement is about spoken requests; written messages are not mentioned. It covers moments in which the use is happening around the child. It does not say whom the child asks.

### Common mistakes

Children often hint and do not say plainly what they need. Many leave out where they are. Some wait until the situation is over because they fear being blamed for having been there.
