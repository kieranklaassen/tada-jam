## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child gives reasons why a weapon being present is dangerous, whether in the community, in the family home or at school.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child explains to a partner what can go wrong when a weapon is within reach: someone is hurt by accident, an argument ends in a serious injury, a child mistakes a real one for a toy. The child states the rule for finding one: do not touch it, leave the area, tell an adult. The child says why bringing one into a classroom endangers everyone there.

### Limits

The statement covers three settings and asks for explaining only. A footnote cites the Education Code. The statement names no kind of weapon and says nothing about handling one.

### Common mistakes

Children often believe they can tell a real gun from a toy by looking. Many think a weapon in the house makes everyone in it safer and do not think of accidents. Some believe that looking at or holding a found weapon is harmless as long as they are careful.
