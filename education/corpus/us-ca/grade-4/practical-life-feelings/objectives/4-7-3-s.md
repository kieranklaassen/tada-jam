## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child puts on, or works with, the safety gear and other protective items that suit what they are doing.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child fastens a helmet so it sits level and the strap is snug under the chin before riding. The child puts on shin guards for soccer, a life vest in a boat, and goggles for a science task. The child checks each item for cracks or a broken buckle and tells an adult when something no longer fits.

### Limits

The statement is brief and names no item and no pursuit. Naming what is needed and explaining why are separate grade 4 statements.

### Common mistakes

Children often wear a helmet unbuckled or pushed back off the forehead. Many keep wearing gear they have outgrown or that is cracked. Some put it on for organized sport but not for play in the street.
