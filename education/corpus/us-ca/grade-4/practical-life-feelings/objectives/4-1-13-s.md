## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows the right way to pick up and carry a heavy school bag, whether a book bag or a backpack.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child bends at the knees, keeps the back straight, grips the bag with both hands and stands up using the legs. The child puts on both shoulder straps, tightens them so the bag sits high on the back, and places the heaviest books closest to the body. The child takes out what is not needed that day.

### Limits

The statement is about two kinds of school bag only. It asks the child to show the technique with the body, which goes beyond telling it. It gives no weight limit.

### Common mistakes

Children often sling the bag over one shoulder or let it hang low on loose straps. Many bend at the waist and twist to swing the bag up. They tend to carry everything every day because they do not sort the bag.
