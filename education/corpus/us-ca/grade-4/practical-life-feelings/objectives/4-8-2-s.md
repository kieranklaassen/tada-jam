## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child acts as a friend and a helper to a person who has been bullied.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sits beside the bullied classmate at lunch, invites them into a game, and says "That wasn't fair, are you okay?" The child walks with them to tell a teacher. The child keeps including them on the following days, not only on the day it happened.

### Limits

The statement names no particular acts. It is about what happens after the bullying. What a witness says and does during it is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often feel sorry but stay away for fear of becoming a target too. Many think one kind sentence is enough and then go back to their own group. Some try to help by attacking the bully, which the statement does not ask for.
