## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells how the steps of making a decision help a person pick a healthy way of being active.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child has a free afternoon and says the steps aloud: the options (a bike ride, a video game, football with neighbours), what is good and bad about each for the body and for safety, the choice, and afterwards how it went. The child writes the steps on a card and uses them again on a rainy day.

### Limits

Standard 5 is decision making: the child describes the process, and the statement does not fix the steps. The matching item for food and drinks is 4.5.1.N. Making an actual plan is 4.6.2.N.

### Common mistakes

Children often list one option only, the activity they already like. Many do not treat safety, such as a helmet or a safe place, as part of the decision. Some take the first idea as the decision and compare nothing.
