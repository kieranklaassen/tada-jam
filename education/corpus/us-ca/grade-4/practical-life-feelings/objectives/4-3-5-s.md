## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows the steps of phoning 9-1-1, or a different emergency number, and of giving the facts the call-taker needs.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child works with an unplugged or toy phone: unlocks it or finds the emergency button, presses the digits, and waits for the answer. The child gives the facts in order: what is wrong, where, who is calling, the phone number. The child tries both a mobile phone and a desk phone, since the steps differ.

### Limits

The statement asks the child to show two things, the dialing and the giving of facts. It does not list which facts count as appropriate.

### Common mistakes

Children often do not know how to place an emergency call from a locked mobile phone. Many give a first name and no address. Some think the number is also for small problems, or call it to test it.
