## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child gives reasons why treating people with respect, compassion and courtesy leads to fewer quarrels and encourages people to act without violence.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child replays a short scene two ways, once with a shove and an insult and once with "excuse me" and "are you all right?", and says how each one ends. The child tells what a hurt classmate needs and what a kind response would be. The child names one polite habit, such as waiting for a turn to speak, and says which arguments it prevents.

### Limits

The statement names three qualities and asks for explaining the link. It does not ask for practice here. Settling a quarrel that has already started is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often treat politeness as a rule made for adults and do not connect it with how peers react to them. Many think showing respect means agreeing or giving in. Some believe respect is owed only to people who showed it first.
