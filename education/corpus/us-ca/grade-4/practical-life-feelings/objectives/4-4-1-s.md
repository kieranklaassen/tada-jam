## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows they can use the skills of refusing when a situation carries a risk.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child role-plays a dare, such as crossing a busy road or climbing onto a roof, and answers with a clear "No, I'm not doing that." The child stands straight, looks at the other person, repeats the no when pushed, suggests something else, and walks away if the pushing goes on. The child tries the same refusal with a friend and with an older child.

### Limits

The statement names no particular situations and no set of steps. It asks the child to show the skill, which goes beyond describing it. Saying no to a gang is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often say no with a laugh or in a quiet voice, which leads to more pushing. Many give a long excuse that the other child can argue with. Some refuse a stranger firmly but cannot refuse a close friend.
