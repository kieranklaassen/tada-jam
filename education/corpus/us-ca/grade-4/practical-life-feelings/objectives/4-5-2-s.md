## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child looks into what follows when someone harasses or bullies another person.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child follows one act, such as a cruel nickname that spreads, through a chain of results: the targeted child stops coming to lunch, friends stop talking to each other, a teacher intervenes, the one who started it loses trust and faces school discipline. The child draws the chain as boxes and arrows. The child says which results last longest.

### Limits

The statement names no particular results and does not say whose they are: the target's, the bystanders' or the bully's. A separate grade 4 statement looks at the effect on other people.

### Common mistakes

Children often think the only result is punishment for the bully if caught. Many do not expect lasting results, such as a child changing schools or staying fearful for years. Some believe that if the target does not cry or complain, no harm was done.
