## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names where correct facts on staying safe and avoiding injury can be found.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sorts possible sources into ones to rely on and ones to doubt: a firefighter, the school nurse, a doctor, a parent and a government leaflet, against an advertisement, a rumor and an unknown video. The child gives a reason for each choice, such as the person's training or who wrote it. The child looks at a leaflet and finds who published it.

### Limits

The statement asks only for naming the sources. It lists none and gives no test for accuracy.

### Common mistakes

Children often trust whatever appears first in a search or what a popular older child says. Many treat an advertisement as neutral advice. Some think anything printed or posted online must be true.
