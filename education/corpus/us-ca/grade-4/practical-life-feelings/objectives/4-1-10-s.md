## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child says what a gang is. The child also tells what sets it apart from three other kinds of group: a clique, a team that plays a sport, and a club.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sorts described groups into the four kinds and gives a reason for each choice. The child fills in a comparison chart: what the group does together, who may join, whether members are pushed to break rules or laws. The child says in one sentence what a gang is.

### Limits

The statement names three groups to compare with and gives no definition of its own. The harm gangs bring and the other things a child can do are covered by separate grade 4 statements.

### Common mistakes

Children often call any group of friends with a name or matching clothes a gang. Many know gangs only from films and cannot say what makes one different: members commit crimes or use violence together. Some do not know the word clique and confuse it with a club.
