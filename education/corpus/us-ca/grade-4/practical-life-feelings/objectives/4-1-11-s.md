## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells what harm can come from the things gangs do.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lists what can happen to a young person who joins: getting hurt, being arrested, losing friends, being made to hurt someone. The child reads a short account about a neighborhood with gang violence and says who else is affected: families, bystanders, younger brothers and sisters. The child says why leaving is hard once someone has joined.

### Limits

The statement names no particular harm and does not ask for detail about violence. Saying what a gang is and naming other things to do are separate grade 4 statements.

### Common mistakes

Children often see only the appeal shown in music and films: money, belonging, protection. Many think the risk falls only on the member and not on family or bystanders. Some believe a person can leave whenever they want.
