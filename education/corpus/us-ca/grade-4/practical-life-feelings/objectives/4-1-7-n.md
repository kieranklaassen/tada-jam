## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells what good comes from being active, both at a medium effort and at a hard effort.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child walks briskly for a few minutes and then runs, each time putting a hand on the chest to feel the heartbeat and noticing the breathing. The child says which effort was medium (talking is still easy) and which was hard (talking is difficult). The child lists gains: a stronger heart, muscles and bones, better sleep, a better mood, easier concentration.

### Limits

The statement names two levels of effort and neither defines them nor gives minutes per day. It asks about the good effects only. Ways of becoming more active are the next item (4.1.8.N).

### Common mistakes

Children often count only organised sport and leave out walking, cycling or playground play. Many think activity is good only for muscles or for weight. Some believe that being out of breath means something is wrong.
