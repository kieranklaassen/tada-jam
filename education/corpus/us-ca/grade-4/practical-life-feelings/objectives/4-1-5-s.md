## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names simple rules for staying safe when severe weather or a natural disaster happens. The examples given are tsunamis, earthquakes and floods.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child matches each event with its first action: get under a sturdy table and hold on when the ground shakes, move to high ground when a flood or a tsunami threatens, stay indoors away from windows in a storm. The child acts out drop, cover and hold on beside a desk. The child tells a partner why running outside during shaking is unsafe.

### Limits

The statement asks for basic guidelines only. Its three events are examples and not a full list. It does not say how many guidelines a child should know. Getting ready in advance and carrying out an escape plan are separate grade 4 statements.

### Common mistakes

Children often apply one rule to every event, for example hiding under a table in a flood. Many want to run outdoors when shaking starts. Some think a tsunami is a single wave and go back to the shore after it.
