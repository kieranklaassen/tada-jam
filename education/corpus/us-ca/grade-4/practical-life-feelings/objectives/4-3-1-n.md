## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names where to find facts that can be trusted on which foods are healthy and safe.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child lists who and what to ask: a parent, the school nurse, a doctor or dietitian, the cafeteria manager, the label on a package, a government nutrition guide. The child holds an advert next to a nutrition guide and says which of the two is trying to sell something.

### Limits

The statement covers two topics, food safety and healthy eating, and names no sources. Standard 3 is accessing valid information: here the child names where to look, and reading a label for what a food holds is the next item (4.3.2.N).

### Common mistakes

Children often treat a claim on the front of a package, or a video by a popular presenter, as reliable. Many think one source is enough. Some say "I heard it" and cannot say where the fact came from.
