## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tries out, together with other people, ways of settling a disagreement that work.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child and a partner act out a quarrel over a shared ball and go through steps: stop and cool down, each says what they want in "I" sentences, each repeats what the other said, they think of two or three solutions and pick one both accept. The child tries taking turns, sharing, and asking a third person to help. Afterwards the pair says whether the solution held.

### Limits

The statement names no particular techniques. It asks for practice with other people, so talking about the steps alone does not meet it.

### Common mistakes

Children often start by blaming ("you always") when they could say what they want. Many think a quarrel is settled only when one side wins. Some skip the cooling-down step and try to talk while still angry.
