## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child says why protective equipment matters in sports and when active outdoors. The items named are life vests for the water, mouth guards, pads and helmets, along with further gear of the kind.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child holds a helmet, feels the hard shell and the foam, and says which body part it covers and what a fall without it could do. The child pairs each item with the injury it prevents: a mouth guard with broken teeth, knee pads with scraped or cracked knees, a life vest with drowning. The child tells a younger pupil why the gear goes on every time.

### Limits

The statement names four items and adds an open group of similar gear. It asks for the reason the gear matters. Picking the right gear for a pursuit and putting it on are separate grade 4 statements. No particular sports are listed.

### Common mistakes

Children often think gear is only for beginners or for fast riding, and not for a short trip. Many believe a good swimmer needs no life vest. Some think a helmet that sits loose or tipped back still protects.
