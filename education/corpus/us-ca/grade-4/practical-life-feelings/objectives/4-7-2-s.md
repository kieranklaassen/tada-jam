## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child rehearses, at school and in the family home, the steps for being ready when a disaster comes.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes part in the school's earthquake and fire drills: drops under the desk, holds on, then walks the exit route to the gathering point. With the family the child walks the two ways out of each room, goes to the meeting spot outside, and checks the emergency kit. The child times how long the family takes to get out.

### Limits

The statement names two settings; the community is not among them here. It asks for doing, which goes beyond naming the steps. It names no particular disaster.

### Common mistakes

Children often treat a drill as a break and talk, push or run. Many know the school route but have never walked the route out of their own bedroom. Some want to collect toys or pets before leaving.
