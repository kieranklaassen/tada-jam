## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names the rules that home and school set on using drugs, tobacco or alcohol.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child asks a parent what the rule is at home and writes it down in one sentence. At school the child finds the rule in the student handbook or on a sign at the gate and reads it aloud. The child says who made each rule and what happens when it is broken.

### Limits

The statement covers two places, the home and the school, and asks only that the child name their rules. Laws of the state or the country are not mentioned. The child is not asked to judge the rules.

### Common mistakes

Children often assume the rule in their own home holds in every home. Some believe a school rule applies only to pupils and not to adults or visitors on the grounds. Some confuse a rule with a reason: "it is bad for you" is a reason, not the rule itself.
