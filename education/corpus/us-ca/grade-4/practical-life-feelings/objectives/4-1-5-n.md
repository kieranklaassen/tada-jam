## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child gives an account of how germs that make people sick can be present in what we eat.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child tells the path of a germ: from unwashed hands, a dirty board, raw meat or an animal onto food, and then into the mouth. The child says that germs are too small to see and multiply fastest in warm food that is left out. A child rubs washable paint on their hands, touches a slice of bread, and looks at where the marks are.

### Limits

The statement asks for an explanation and names no particular germs or illnesses. The actions that keep food safe are the item before it (4.1.4.N).

### Common mistakes

Children often think food with germs always looks or smells bad. Many believe every germ is harmful. Some think cold kills germs, when cold only slows their growth.
