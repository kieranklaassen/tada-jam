## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells how to go through the steps of making a decision in order to pick nourishing things to eat and drink.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child stands at a pretend snack table and says the steps aloud: what am I deciding, what are my options, what is good and bad about each, which one do I choose, how did it turn out. The child uses the same steps on a real choice, such as which drink to take at lunch.

### Limits

Standard 5 is decision making: the child describes the process, and the statement fixes neither the number nor the names of the steps. It covers food and drinks. The matching item for activity is 4.5.2.N.

### Common mistakes

Children often go directly to the choice and skip listing the options. Many consider only taste. Some leave out the last step, checking afterwards how the choice worked.
