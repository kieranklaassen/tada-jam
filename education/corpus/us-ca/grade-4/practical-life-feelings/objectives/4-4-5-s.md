## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows the words and the actions a bystander can use on seeing someone bullied.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child role-plays being the one who sees it: says "Stop, that's not okay" in a firm voice, stands beside the targeted child, invites that child to come away, and goes to get an adult. The child rehearses choosing the safe option when the bully is bigger: fetch help and do not intervene alone. The child refuses to laugh or to pass on an unkind message.

### Limits

The statement covers the witness's own words and actions and lists none of them. Reporting, and befriending the bullied child afterwards, are separate grade 4 statements.

### Common mistakes

Children often think watching in silence is neutral, though an audience encourages the bully. Many believe the only choices are to fight the bully or do nothing. Some laugh along out of nervousness or fear of becoming the next target.
