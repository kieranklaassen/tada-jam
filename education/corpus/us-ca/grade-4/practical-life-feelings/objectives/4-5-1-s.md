## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child judges how well different ways of staying clear of a possibly unsafe situation would work.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child is given a scene, such as friends wanting to take a shortcut across train tracks, and lists three things they could do. For each one the child says what would probably happen next and how safe it is. The child picks the best one and gives the reason.

### Limits

The statement asks for a judgement between strategies, which goes beyond listing them. It names no particular situations.

### Common mistakes

Children often judge a choice by whether they would get caught, not by whether someone could be hurt. Many stop at the first idea and do not compare it with others. Some rate a plan as safe because a friend they trust suggested it.
