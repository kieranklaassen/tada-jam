## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child urges other people to live without tobacco, alcohol or other drugs.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child makes a poster for the school corridor with one clear message and a reason. The child tells a younger sibling why they have chosen not to smoke, or thanks a friend for saying no. The child signs a class pledge and asks a classmate to sign too.

### Limits

Standard 8 is health promotion: the child speaks to other people and not only about their own choice. The statement does not say who those people are or what form the encouragement takes.

### Common mistakes

Children often lecture or shame a person who uses, and that person stops listening. Many repeat frightening stories that are not true. Some think encouraging a person means reporting them.
