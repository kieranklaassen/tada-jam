## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works out what is gained when a dispute is settled peacefully, with no violence.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child compares two endings of one playground quarrel, one settled by talking and one by a fight, and lists for each who was hurt, who is still friends and who was sent to the office. The child counts the good results of the peaceful ending. The child tells about a disagreement of their own that ended well with nobody hurt.

### Limits

The statement asks for analysis of the gains and names none of them. Practicing ways to settle a quarrel is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often see walking away or talking as losing or as weakness. Many count only the immediate result, such as who got the ball, and not the friendship afterwards. Some think peaceful ways work only when the other child is also calm.
