## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When a relative or a friend uses or misuses tobacco, alcohol or other drugs, the child puts several working ways of coping into practice.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child works with scene cards, such as a parent who smokes indoors or a friend's older brother who drinks. For each scene the child acts out a fitting response: going to another room, telling a trusted adult how it feels, doing something calming such as reading or playing outside. The child also says aloud that another person's drinking or smoking is not the child's fault.

### Limits

Standard 7 is practising health-enhancing behaviours: the child uses the strategies, where 4.1.2.A only asks the child to name them. The statement asks for more than one strategy and does not say which. It covers both use and misuse, by people close to the child.

### Common mistakes

Children often believe they caused an adult's use or can stop it by behaving well. Many rely on one strategy, usually hiding, for every situation. Some think telling a trusted adult is disloyal.
