## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child gives reasons that the same drug, or the same drink of alcohol, does not affect every person alike.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child compares two people on cards, such as a small child and a large adult, and says whose body would be affected more by the same amount and why. The child lists what changes the effect: body size, age, how much was taken, food in the stomach, other medicine, health.

### Limits

The statement names alcohol and drugs; tobacco is not in this item. It asks for reasons and lists none. No amounts or measures are given.

### Common mistakes

Children often conclude that a substance is safe because one person they know seemed fine after using it. Some think only the amount matters and not the person's size or age. Some believe a strong or sporty person cannot be harmed.
