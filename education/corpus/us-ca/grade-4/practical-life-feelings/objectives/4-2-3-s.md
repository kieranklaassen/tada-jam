## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child explains that most children and teenagers handle their difficulties without violence.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child thinks of ten classmates and counts how many settled their last disagreement by talking, walking away or asking an adult. The child compares that count with what television suggests. The child says the fact in their own words to a partner.

### Limits

The statement is a single fact to explain. It gives no figures and names no source for the fact.

### Common mistakes

Children often overestimate how common fighting is, because fights are talked about and shown on television and calm endings are not. Some conclude that hitting back is normal and expected. Many take "most" to mean "all" and think the fact is false when they know one child who fights.
