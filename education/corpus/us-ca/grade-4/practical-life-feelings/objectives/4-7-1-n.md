## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises being responsible, themselves, for eating food that is good for them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child packs part of their own lunch and puts in a fruit or a vegetable without being told. At the table the child serves their own portion and tries the vegetable. At a friend's house the child picks the snack and can say why.

### Limits

Standard 7 is practising health-enhancing behaviours: the child does the behaviour and does not only describe it. The statement does not say which foods, how often or where. Shopping and cooking are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Children often see food as the parent's decision and wait to be told. Many think one healthy item compensates for the rest of the meal. Some call a food healthy because the package says so.
