## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names what shapes the things a person decides to eat, both from inside the person and from outside.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child looks at yesterday's lunch and says why each item was chosen. Reasons from inside are hunger, taste, mood and habit; reasons from outside are what was in the cupboard, what a friend ate, an advert, the price, a family or cultural custom. The child writes the reasons in two columns.

### Limits

Standard 2 is analysing influences: the child names both kinds, and the statement lists none. Adverts are studied in the next item (4.2.2.N), and influences on activity are a separate item (4.2.3.N).

### Common mistakes

Children often say "I just like it" and do not see where the liking came from. Many do not count a feeling such as boredom or sadness as a reason for eating. Some think packaging sways other people and not them.
