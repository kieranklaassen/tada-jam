## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child helps other people choose well about what they eat and about being active.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child invites a classmate who is standing alone to join a game at break. The child offers to share cut fruit, praises a sibling for trying a new vegetable, or asks the family to go for a walk after dinner. The child speaks kindly and does not comment on anyone's body.

### Limits

Standard 8 is health promotion: the child acts toward other people. The statement does not say who those people are or what form the support takes.

### Common mistakes

Children often criticise another child's lunch, which shames that child and does not help. Many think support means telling others what to do. Some connect food and activity with body size and tease.
