## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child studies in detail the methods by which things to eat and drink are advertised and marketed.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child puts a cereal box on the table and circles the cartoon character, the bright colours, the free toy, and words such as "natural". The child compares the front of the box with the label on its side. The child says where in a shop such products stand and at whose eye level.

### Limits

The statement covers adverts and marketing more widely, for both food and drinks. It lists no techniques and names no media.

### Common mistakes

Children often believe a health claim on the front of a pack without checking the label. Many do not see a mascot, a toy or a sponsored video as selling. Some think adverts are aimed at adults only.
