## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names the steps people take ahead of time to be ready for a disaster. Three settings are covered: the community, the school and the family home.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lists what belongs in a family emergency kit: water, a flashlight, a first-aid box, a list of phone numbers. The child points out the classroom's exit route and the place where the class gathers outside. The child finds out where the town's shelter or meeting point is and marks it on a simple map.

### Limits

The statement asks the child to name the procedures and sets no number of them. It names three settings and no particular disaster. Rehearsing the procedures is a separate grade 4 statement that covers two of the settings.

### Common mistakes

Children mix up getting ready beforehand with what to do during the event. Many know the school drill but cannot say where their own family would meet. Some think a kit is only food and forget water, light and contact numbers.
