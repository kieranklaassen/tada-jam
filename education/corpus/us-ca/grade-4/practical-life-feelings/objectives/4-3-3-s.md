## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names the people and the places that are safe to turn to when they feel unsafe or someone is threatening them. Examples given are the fire department, the police department, and the counselor at school.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child draws the route from home to school and marks each safe stop on it: the school office, a fire station, a library, a known neighbor's house. The child writes down three people by name and how to reach each one. The child rehearses walking in and saying "I don't feel safe, can you help me?"

### Limits

The three examples are examples only. The statement asks for naming people and places and does not ask the child to rehearse going there.

### Common mistakes

Children often name only a parent and have no answer for when the parent is not there. Many think any adult in a uniform, or any friendly adult, is safe. Some name a place that is closed or empty at the time they would need it.
