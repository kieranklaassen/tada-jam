## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child makes their own promise to keep apart from people who take part in what gangs do.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes a short pledge in their own words and names what they will do after school in its place. The child plans a route and a group of friends to walk with. The child tells a trusted adult about the pledge and agrees on what to do if a gang member approaches.

### Limits

The statement is a goal the child sets for themselves and gives no wording for the pledge. Refusing an offer to join is a separate grade 4 statement.

### Common mistakes

Children often do not see how the pledge applies when the person is a cousin, a brother or a neighbor they like. Many think avoiding means being rude, and need words for staying polite while leaving. Some treat the pledge as a classroom exercise and connect it with no real place or person.
