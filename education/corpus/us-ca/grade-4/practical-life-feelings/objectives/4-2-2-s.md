## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child looks at how violent content met through technology and media can shape the way people act where their health is concerned.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child keeps a tally through one cartoon or one video game session of how often a fight solves a problem and whether anyone is shown hurt afterwards. The child compares that with what would happen to a real person. The child tells how they feel and act after watching: jumpy, wanting to copy a move, unable to sleep.

### Limits

The statement speaks of media and technology in general and names no particular program or device. It asks for examining an influence and sets no viewing rule.

### Common mistakes

Children often say that watching violence has no effect on them, only on younger children. Many do not notice that injuries and grief are left out of what they watch. Some conclude that because a character they like fights, fighting is how admired people solve problems.
