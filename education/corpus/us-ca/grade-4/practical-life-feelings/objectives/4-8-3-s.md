## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child prompts other people to act safely. Buckling a seat belt and putting on a helmet for cycling are the examples given.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reminds a younger brother or sister to buckle up before the car moves and hands a friend their helmet before a ride. The child wears their own gear first, so the reminder is believable. The child thanks a friend who does it, and does not nag or mock one who forgets.

### Limits

The two behaviors are examples; the statement is not limited to them. It is about what other people do, where a separate grade 4 statement covers the child's own gear.

### Common mistakes

Children often remind others in a bossy or mocking tone, which makes peers resist. Many tell others to buckle up while leaving their own belt off. Some stay silent with older children or adults because they think a child may not correct them.
