## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student reads data from maps, works out what it shows, and describes patterns in the features of the Earth. The clarification says the maps can include topographic maps of the land and of the sea floor, and maps showing where earthquakes, volcanoes, mountains and the edges of continents are.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student lays a tracing sheet over a world map and marks volcanoes in one colour and earthquakes in another, then looks for where the marks cluster. The student runs a finger along a mountain chain on a relief map and compares it with the marked sheet. The student says in a sentence where such features tend to be.

### Limits

The expectation states no assessment boundary. The clarification lists kinds of map that can be used. The expectation asks for patterns described from map data; it does not mention explaining what causes them.

### Common mistakes

Reading the colours of a topographic map as vegetation or temperature instead of height or depth. Listing single features and not stating a pattern. Assuming the ocean floor is flat.
