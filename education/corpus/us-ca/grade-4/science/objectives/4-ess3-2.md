## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student thinks of several solutions that lessen the harm natural processes of the Earth do to people, and compares them. The clarification's examples are better monitoring of volcanoes and a building designed to withstand earthquakes.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student builds two small towers from sticks and clay, one with diagonal braces and one without, shakes the table under each, and notes which stands longer. The student sketches a second, different kind of answer, such as a warning signal, and lists what each does well and badly.

### Limits

The assessment boundary names four hazards and no others: floods, earthquakes, volcanic eruptions and tsunamis. The expectation is marked as integrating engineering practices. The two solutions in the clarification are examples.

### Common mistakes

Proposing to stop the earthquake or eruption itself instead of reducing what it does to people. Offering one solution and not comparing it with another. Comparing by which looks strongest, with no test or stated reason.
