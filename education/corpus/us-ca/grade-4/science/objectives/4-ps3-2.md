## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student observes in order to give evidence that energy can move from one place to another, carried by electric currents, heat, light or sound.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student connects a battery, wires and a bulb and sees light appear away from the battery. The student holds a hand near a lamp and feels warmth arrive, and puts a metal spoon in warm water and feels the handle warm up. The student taps one end of a table while a partner, with an ear at the far end, hears it.

### Limits

The assessment boundary leaves out measuring energy in numbers. The expectation names four ways in which energy is carried from place to place.

### Common mistakes

Thinking the energy is used up and gone when the bulb lights, instead of having moved. Describing what happened (the bulb lit) without saying where the energy came from and where it went. Treating heat and temperature as the same word.
