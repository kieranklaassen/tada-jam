## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student asks questions and predicts what will happen to the energy of objects when they hit each other. The clarification puts the emphasis on energy changing because speed changes, and not on the forces between the objects.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before rolling one marble into a row of still marbles, the student says which will move and how fast, then tries it. The student asks "what if the first marble goes faster?" or "what if it hits two?", predicts, and tests. The student notices the click at each hit and asks where some of the energy went.

### Limits

The assessment boundary leaves out measuring energy in numbers. The clarification directs the work toward changes in speed and away from forces.

### Common mistakes

Explaining the collision by force ("it pushed harder") when the expectation is about energy. Thinking the energy disappears when both objects stop. Predicting without a reason, and not checking the prediction against the result.
