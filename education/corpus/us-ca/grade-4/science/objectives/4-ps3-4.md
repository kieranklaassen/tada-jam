## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student applies science ideas to design a device that changes energy from one form into another, tests it, and improves it. The clarification's example devices are a passive solar heater, which turns light into heat, and electric circuits that turn electrical energy into sound, light, or the motion of a vehicle; its example constraints are time, cost and the materials.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student wires a battery, a switch and a small motor to a cardboard car, runs it, and measures how far it goes. The student changes one thing, such as the wheel size or the wiring, and runs it again. The student keeps to a set list of parts and a time limit, and records what each change did.

### Limits

The assessment boundary keeps devices to two kinds: ones that turn motion energy into electric energy, and ones that use stored energy to make motion, light or sound. The expectation is marked as integrating engineering practices. The devices and constraints in the clarification are examples.

### Common mistakes

Saying the device "makes" energy instead of changing it from one form into another. Stopping after the first working version and skipping the testing and improving. Changing several things at once, so it is unclear which change helped.
