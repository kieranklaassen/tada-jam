## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student develops a model that describes how we come to see an object: light bounces off the object and then goes into the eye.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student draws a lamp, a book and an eye, and adds arrows from the lamp to the book and from the book to the eye. The student uses a flashlight, a mirror and a card with a slit to follow a beam as it bounces. The student blocks the path with a hand and notes that the object can no longer be seen.

### Limits

The assessment boundary leaves out which colours are reflected and seen, how the retina works, and what cells do in vision.

### Common mistakes

Drawing the arrows from the eye to the object, as if sight travels outward. Drawing light going from the lamp to the eye only, and leaving out the bounce off the object. Thinking only shiny things reflect light.
