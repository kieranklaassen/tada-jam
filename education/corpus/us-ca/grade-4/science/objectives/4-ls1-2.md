## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student uses a model to describe a chain: animals take in various kinds of information through their senses, the brain processes it, and the animal responds, in various ways. The clarification puts the emphasis on systems that pass information along.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student acts out or draws a flow from sense to brain to action: a hand catches a dropped ruler, or a cat hears a tin being opened and runs to the kitchen. The student labels each step with the sense used, the brain, and the response. The student compares how two animals respond to the same sound.

### Limits

The assessment boundary leaves out how sensory receptors work and how the brain keeps and retrieves what it has taken in. The verb is to use a model; the expectation does not say the student must make it.

### Common mistakes

Leaving the brain out and drawing the sense organ as causing the action directly. Thinking the eyes or ears themselves do the understanding. Assuming every animal responds to a signal in the same way.
