## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child brings together information from two texts about one topic so that they can speak or write about that subject with real knowledge of it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child reads a book chapter and a magazine article, both about owls. The child writes notes from each on cards, sorts the cards by subtopic (food, nests, hunting), and sets aside any fact that both texts give. The child then gives a two-minute talk or writes a paragraph that draws facts from both.

### Limits

The statement asks for two texts, both on the same topic. The result may be spoken or written. The statement does not say how long the texts or the child's product are.

### Common mistakes

Children report on one text and then on the other without combining them. They draw only on the text they read last. They do not notice when the two texts give different facts on the same point.
