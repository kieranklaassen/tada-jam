## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Helped and guided by adults and classmates, the child develops their writing and makes it stronger, as needed, through planning, revising and editing. A note adds that editing for conventions should show control of Language standards 1 to 3 through grade 4.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child fills in a planning sheet before writing a report, then writes a draft. A classmate reads the draft and puts a question mark where something is unclear; the child adds two sentences there and moves one paragraph. With a teacher's checklist the child then checks capital letters, commas and spelling line by line and writes a clean copy.

### Limits

The work is done with guidance and support from classmates and adults, not alone. Three activities are named: planning, revising and editing, done as needed. Editing is tied to Language standards 1 to 3, those of grade 4 and of the grades before it.

### Common mistakes

Children treat revising as copying the draft out neatly. They fix spelling and leave unclear or missing ideas untouched. They skip planning, start writing, and have nothing more to say after a few lines.
