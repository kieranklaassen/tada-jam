## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child gives a thorough description of an event, a setting or a character from a play or story, built on particular details in the text, for example what a character does, says or thinks.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child picks the grandfather in a novel and writes a paragraph about what kind of person he is. For each trait the child copies or points to something he says, does or thinks in the book. For a setting, the child draws the attic from its description and labels each part with the page where that detail appears.

### Limits

The texts are stories and dramas. The child describes a character, a setting or an event; the statement does not require all three at once. What a character thinks, says and does is given as an example of the details to draw on.

### Common mistakes

Children describe how a character looks and leave out what the character is like. They name a trait ('brave') with no detail from the text. They describe the character at one moment and miss that the character changes.
