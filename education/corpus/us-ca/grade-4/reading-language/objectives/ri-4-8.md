## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child explains how the author of a text backs up specific points with evidence and reasons.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child reads a short article arguing that schools should start later. The child underlines each of the author's points in one colour and each reason or piece of evidence in another, then draws a line from each reason to the point it supports. The child tells a partner which point has the most support and which has none.

### Limits

The statement is about the points an author makes and the reasons and evidence given for them. It does not ask the child to judge whether the evidence is good enough or to say whether they agree.

### Common mistakes

Children restate the author's point and call it a reason. They cannot tell a reason from a piece of evidence. They give their own reasons for agreeing with the author and not the ones in the text.
