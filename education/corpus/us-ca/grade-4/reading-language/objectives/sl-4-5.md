## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Where it suits the presentation, the child brings in sound recordings and visual displays so that its themes or main ideas are developed better.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child preparing a talk on frogs records a frog calling at a pond and draws a poster of the life cycle. During the talk the child plays the recording when describing the call and points to each stage on the poster when naming it. The child leaves out a second picture that had nothing to do with the main point.

### Limits

The additions are audio recordings and visual displays, brought in when appropriate, not in every presentation. Their purpose is to develop main ideas or themes. The statement names no tools or software.

### Common mistakes

Children add pictures or sounds because they like them, not because they support the point. They show the display and never refer to it while speaking, or read aloud from it with their back to the listeners. They put so much text on a poster that listeners read it and stop listening.
