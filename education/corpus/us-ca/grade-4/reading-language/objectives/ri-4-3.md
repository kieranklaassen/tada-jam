## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Drawing on specific information from a scientific, technical or historical text, the child explains its ideas, concepts, procedures or events, and the explanation covers both what took place and the reason for it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child reads an account of a bridge collapse and tells a partner what happened first, what happened next, and what caused the collapse, pointing to the paragraph for each. After reading instructions for building a circuit, the child explains why the wire must touch both ends of the battery. On paper the child draws arrows between boxes to show which event led to which.

### Limits

The texts are historical, scientific or technical. The explanation rests on information that is in the text. The statement does not say how long or how hard the text is; another standard of this strand sets the text complexity for the year.

### Common mistakes

Children list events in order and leave out why each one happened. They give a cause from their own knowledge that the text does not state. In a procedure they skip a step or reverse two steps.
