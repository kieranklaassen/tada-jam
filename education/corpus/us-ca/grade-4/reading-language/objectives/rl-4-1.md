## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Whether the child is explaining what a text says outright or inferring something it leaves unsaid, they back it with particular examples and details taken from the text.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child reads a chapter in which a girl hides a letter. Asked what she did with it, the child finds the line and reads it aloud. Asked how the girl feels, which the chapter never says, the child answers 'worried' and points to her shaking hands and the way she keeps checking the door.

### Limits

This record belongs to the literature strand; the informational text strand of this grade has a standard with the same wording. The statement covers both what is stated outright and what is inferred. It does not say how many details or examples to give, and it does not ask for exact quotation.

### Common mistakes

Children retell the whole story when asked for one supporting detail. They give a character a feeling or motive from their own experience with nothing in the text behind it. They treat an inference as something the text says outright.
