## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

From the details of a poem, play or story, the child works out a theme of it, and the child also gives a summary of the text.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child reads a folktale in which a slow, careful animal finishes a job that a fast one abandons. The child says what the tale shows about people in general and points to two events that show it. The child then retells the tale in a few sentences: who is in it, what the problem is, how it ends.

### Limits

The texts are stories, dramas and poems. The statement says 'a theme', so it does not require one single correct theme, and the theme has to come from details in the text. It does not say how long the summary is.

### Common mistakes

Children give the topic in one word ('friendship') and not a theme in a sentence. They state a moral they already know that the details do not support. Their summary retells every event or stops before the ending.
