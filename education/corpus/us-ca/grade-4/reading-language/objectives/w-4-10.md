## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Writing is a regular activity for the child, both over longer stretches that leave time to research, reflect and revise and over short ones such as one sitting or one or two days. The writing serves a variety of audiences, purposes and tasks tied to particular subjects.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In one week a child writes a ten-minute science observation in a notebook, a quick answer to a history question, and one more section of a report on rivers that has been planned, researched and revised over three weeks. The child keeps the pieces in a folder and can say who each one is for.

### Limits

Two time frames are named: extended ones that leave room to research, reflect and revise, and shorter ones of one sitting or one to two days. The statement gives no number of pieces or pages. The tasks are tied to subject areas and vary in purpose and audience.

### Common mistakes

Children treat every piece as a one-sitting task and do not go back to revise. On a long piece they forget the plan between sessions. They write the same way for every subject and reader.
