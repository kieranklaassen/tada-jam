## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

By year's end the child reads and understands informational texts with proficiency at the level of complexity set for grades 4 and 5, with support where needed for texts at the top of that range. The texts include technical texts, science texts, and history and social studies texts.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Over the year a child reads science chapters, history accounts and sets of technical instructions, each about as hard as the books of grades 4 and 5. For the hardest ones a teacher or partner helps, for example by reading the first section together or explaining three key words beforehand. After reading, the child can tell what the text explained and answer questions about it.

### Limits

This is an end-of-year expectation. Text difficulty is the complexity band for grades 4 and 5, which the statement names but does not define. Support, as needed, is mentioned for texts at the upper end of the band. The statement names no titles and no number of texts.

### Common mistakes

Children can read every word aloud and still not be able to say what the text explained. They give up on a hard text at the first unknown term. They read an informational text from start to finish as if it were a story and pass over headings, captions and diagrams.
