## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child takes a productive part in many kinds of shared discussion (in pairs, in groups and led by the teacher) with varied partners, about texts and topics of grade 4. In these the child adds to what others have said and states their own ideas clearly. The child arrives ready, with the required material read or studied, and openly uses that preparation, along with other things they know about the topic, to explore the ideas being discussed. The child keeps to the discussion rules the group has agreed, does the role they were given, asks and answers specific questions that clear up information or follow it up, and makes comments that add to the discussion and connect with what others said. The child also goes back over the main ideas people raised and says what they themselves think and understand, given what was discussed.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before a group talk about a chapter, a child reads it and writes two notes on sticky paper. In the group of four the child is the timekeeper, waits for a turn, and says 'I want to add to what Maya said' before giving an example from page 12. The child asks a classmate 'What did you mean by unfair?' and answers a question put to them. At the end the child says what they think now and which classmate's idea led them to think it.

### Limits

Discussions are about texts and topics of grade 4 and take three forms: led by the teacher, in groups, and one-on-one. The rules are ones the group has agreed and the roles are assigned; the statement lists neither. Preparation means required material read or studied beforehand. The statement gives no group size and no length of discussion.

### Common mistakes

Children wait for their turn to say what they planned and do not respond to what was just said. They come without having read the material and speak from general opinion. They ask a question that was already answered, or repeat a classmate's point as if it were new.
