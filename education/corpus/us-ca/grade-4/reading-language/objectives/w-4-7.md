## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child carries out brief research projects in which looking into several sides of one topic adds to what they know.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child picks sea otters and writes three questions: what they eat, where they live, and why their numbers fell. Over a few days the child reads a book section and an article for each question, writes notes under the three questions, and tells or writes what they found.

### Limits

The projects are short; the statement gives no number of days, sources or pages. The child looks into different aspects of one topic. How to gather information and take notes is covered by the next writing standard of this grade.

### Common mistakes

Children pick a topic so broad that they cannot finish, such as 'animals'. They collect facts about one aspect only. They copy whole sentences from a source and do not connect the facts to their questions.
