## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

By year's end the child reads and understands literature with proficiency at the level of complexity set for grades 4 and 5, with support where needed for texts at the top of that range. The literature includes poetry, dramas and stories.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Over the year a child reads novels, short plays and poems about as hard as those of grades 4 and 5. For the hardest ones an adult or partner helps, for example by reading the first chapter aloud together or talking through an old-fashioned phrase. After reading alone, the child can retell what happened in a story and say what a poem is about.

### Limits

This is an end-of-year expectation. Text difficulty is the complexity band for grades 4 and 5, which the statement names but does not define. Support, as needed, is mentioned for texts at the upper end of the band. The statement names no titles and no number of books.

### Common mistakes

Children read the words of a hard story fluently and can no longer say who is speaking or what happened. They pick only short or easy books and avoid the upper end of the range. They read a poem once at story speed and decide it means nothing.
