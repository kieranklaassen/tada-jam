## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

With some adult help and guidance, the child uses technology, the Internet included, to produce writing and publish it and also to work and communicate with other people. The child's keyboarding is good enough to type at least one page in one sitting.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child types a report in a word processor, with an adult nearby to help with saving and sharing. The child sends the draft to a classmate, reads the classmate's typed comments, and changes two sentences. The finished page is printed or posted on the class page. In one session at the keyboard the child types a full page.

### Limits

Adults give some guidance and support. The typing amount is stated: at least one page per sitting. No typing speed is given. The Internet is named as one kind of technology, and the statement names no program or device.

### Common mistakes

Children type with one or two fingers while looking at the keys, so a page takes far longer than one sitting. They spend the time on fonts and colours and not on the writing. They lose work by not saving it.
