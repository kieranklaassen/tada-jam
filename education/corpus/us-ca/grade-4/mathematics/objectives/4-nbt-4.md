## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

By the standard algorithm, the child fluently adds and subtracts whole numbers that have several digits.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes one number under the other with the places lined up, works from the ones column to the left, and records each regrouping (a small 1 carried over, a digit crossed out and renamed). The child does this at a steady pace without modelling each step with blocks, and checks a subtraction by adding back.

### Limits

A footnote to the base-ten domain (recorded with 4.NBT.1) keeps grade 4 work to whole numbers up to and including 1,000,000. Whole numbers only. One method is named, the standard algorithm, and fluency with it is expected. The cluster heading rests the arithmetic of this group on place value and on the properties of operations.

### Common mistakes

Subtracting the smaller digit from the larger one in every column, whichever number it belongs to (52 − 38 gives 26). Going wrong when regrouping across a zero, as in 4,003 − 1,267. Lining up the places wrongly when the two numbers differ in length, or forgetting to add the carried digit.
