## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child builds on what they already know about multiplication to work out a whole number times a fraction. In this part, the child solves word problems in which a whole number multiplies a fraction, for example by showing the problem with equations and with visual fraction models. The example given: 5 guests at a party each eat 3/8 pound of roast beef; the child finds how many pounds are needed and says which two whole numbers that amount falls between.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads "each of 6 plant pots gets 2/3 cup of water", lays out six groups of two 1/3 tiles or draws six bars with 2 of 3 parts shaded, counts 12 thirds, writes 6 × 2/3 = 12/3, and says "that is 4 cups". For a product such as 15/8 the child says that it is between 1 and 2.

### Limits

A footnote to the fractions domain (recorded with 4.NF.1) allows only these denominators in grade 4: 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, then 8, 10 and 12, and also 100. Sevenths, ninths and elevenths are not used. The problems multiply a whole number and a fraction; no fraction is multiplied by a fraction. Placing the answer between two whole numbers is part of the statement's example.

### Common mistakes

Adding the whole number and the fraction where the story needs multiplying (5 + 3/8). Multiplying both the numerator and the denominator by the whole number. Leaving the answer as a fraction greater than 1 without being able to say roughly how large it is.
