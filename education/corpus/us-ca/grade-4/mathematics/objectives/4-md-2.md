## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child solves word problems about money, intervals of time, distances, liquid volumes and the masses of objects, with addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. Some problems include simple fractions or decimals, and some need a measurement stated in a larger unit to be rewritten in a smaller one. The child shows measured amounts in diagrams, for example a number line that carries a measurement scale.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads "a film starts at 2:45 and runs for 1 hour 40 minutes", draws a number line marked in hours and quarter hours, and moves along it in jumps to find the end time. For "a 2 kg bag of flour, 350 g used" the child rewrites 2 kg as 2,000 g and subtracts. With play money the child lays out $3.75 and works out the change from $5.

### Limits

Five kinds of quantity are named: distance, time interval, liquid volume, mass and money. The fractions and decimals are called simple, and the statement does not define that; the neighbouring fraction statements of this grade add and subtract with like denominators and use decimals to hundredths. A change of unit goes from the larger unit to the smaller, as the cluster heading also says. No number range is set.

### Common mistakes

Calculating with the numbers as printed without first putting them in one unit (2 kg − 350 g treated as 350 − 2). Treating time as a base-ten number, so that 1 hour 40 minutes is written 1.40 or an hour is traded for 100 minutes. Reading a scale by counting marks without checking what one interval is worth.
