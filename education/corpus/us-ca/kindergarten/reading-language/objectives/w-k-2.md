## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child makes a text that informs or explains, with a mix of writing, dictating and drawing. In it the child says what the subject is and gives some information on it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child draws a ladybug, labels its spots and wings with letters for the sounds heard, and dictates "Ladybugs eat little bugs". The first line or the label on the picture says what the page is about. The child adds a second fact after looking at a book or a real insect.

### Limits

Writing, drawing and dictating may be combined. The piece names its topic and gives some information; the statement sets no number of facts or sentences. The information is about the named topic; opinion pieces and narratives have their own statements at this level.

### Common mistakes

Giving an opinion ("I love ladybugs") in place of information. Moving to a different subject partway through. Drawing the picture with no words or label that names the topic.
