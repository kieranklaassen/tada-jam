## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child takes an active part when a group reads together, knowing what the reading is for and following its meaning. The child recalls what they already know that relates to the events and information in the texts. The child also predicts things about a text from its pictures and from context.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Sitting with the group for a read-aloud about snow, the child says what they remember about a snowy day before the book starts. The child looks at the cover and the next picture and says what the book will tell about next. During the reading the child joins in on repeated lines, answers when asked, and looks at the page being read.

### Limits

The reading is done in a group; reading alone is not asked. California adds both lettered parts: recalling prior knowledge, and predicting from pictures and context. The statement sets no text level and does not say who reads aloud. This record belongs to informational text; a record with the same wording covers literature.

### Common mistakes

Sharing a memory that has nothing to do with the topic of the text. Making a prediction from a wish or from another book and not from the picture or the words so far. Keeping a prediction after the text has shown something else.
