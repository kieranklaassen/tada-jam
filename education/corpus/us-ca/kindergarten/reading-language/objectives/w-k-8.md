## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

With adults guiding and supporting, the child answers a question either by remembering information from things they have experienced or by collecting information from sources they have been given.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Asked "What do plants need?", the child remembers watering the class bean plant and says "water". The child then looks at two picture books the adult has set out, finds a picture of a plant in the sun, and adds "sun". The child draws or dictates the answer.

### Limits

This is done with adults guiding and supporting. There is one question to answer, and the sources are given to the child; finding sources is not asked. Information may come from the child's own experience or from those sources.

### Common mistakes

Answering a different question from the one asked. Giving a made-up or wished-for answer instead of something remembered or found. Looking through the source for interesting pictures and forgetting the question.
