## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

To give more detail, the child may support a description with a drawing or some other visual display; whether to do so is the child's choice.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While telling about their pet, the child draws it and points to the spots and the long tail on the drawing. The child may build a model from blocks or clay, or hold up a photo, to show what the words leave out.

### Limits

The words "as desired" make this the child's choice, not a requirement for every description. Drawings are named; other kinds of visual display are left open. The visual gives extra detail for a description and does not replace it.

### Common mistakes

Drawing something unrelated to what is being described. Making the drawing and then saying nothing, so the picture replaces the description. Adding decoration that gives no information about the thing described.
