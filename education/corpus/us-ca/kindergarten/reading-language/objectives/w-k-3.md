## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

With a mix of writing, dictating and drawing, the child tells the story of one event, or of a few events that are loosely connected. The telling follows the order in which things happened and includes a reaction to what took place.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child draws three boxes: going to the beach, building a sandcastle, a wave knocking it down. Under them the child writes or dictates a few words for each and ends with "I was sad" or "It was fun". The child tells the story aloud in order while pointing to each box.

### Limits

Writing, drawing and dictating may be combined. The story holds one event, or a few that are loosely connected; a plot with a problem and a solution is not asked. The order of telling matches the order of the events, and a reaction is required. The statement does not say whether the events are real or made up.

### Common mistakes

Telling events in the order the child thinks of them and not the order they happened. Leaving out the reaction, so the piece ends with the last event. Listing unrelated things ("I have a dog. I like pizza.") as if they were one story.
