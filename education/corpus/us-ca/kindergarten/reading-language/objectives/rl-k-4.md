## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child asks about words in a text that they do not know and answers questions about them. The statement refers to this grade's language standards 4 to 6 for further expectations.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Hearing "The hen was weary", the child asks what "weary" means. Asked what the word might mean, the child uses the picture of the tired hen or the rest of the sentence to make a guess. The child then tries the word in a sentence of their own.

### Limits

This statement does not mention prompting or support, unlike the matching statement for informational text. California adds to this statement (it carries the state's mark for an addition); the record does not show which words are the added ones. It refers to this grade's language standards 4 to 6 for more on word meaning. It sets no number or kind of words.

### Common mistakes

Not noticing that a word was unfamiliar, and so asking nothing. Taking a word with two meanings in the one sense already known (the "bark" of a tree heard as a dog's bark). Guessing from a word that sounds alike.
