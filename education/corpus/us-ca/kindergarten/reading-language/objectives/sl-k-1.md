## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child takes part in shared conversations on the texts and topics of kindergarten, with a range of partners, both children their age and adults, in small groups and in bigger ones. The child keeps to the rules the group has agreed for discussion, for example speaking in turn about the topic or text being discussed and listening while others speak. The child keeps a conversation going over several turns back and forth.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a circle of four children talking about a story, the child waits for the talking stick, looks at the speaker, and then adds something about that story. The child answers a partner's question and asks one back, so that each speaks several times. In a whole-class talk the child raises a hand before speaking.

### Limits

The topics and texts are kindergarten ones. Partners include both peers and adults, and groups are both small and larger. Listening and taking turns are given as examples of agreed rules, not as the full set. Part b asks for more than one exchange and sets no number.

### Common mistakes

Saying something unrelated when their turn comes, because they were waiting to speak and not listening. Answering once and then stopping, so the talk ends after one exchange. Speaking only to the adult and never to another child.
