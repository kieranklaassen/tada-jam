## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

With prompting and support, the child tells how the pictures of a story fit with the story, for example by saying which moment of the story a picture shows.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a picture of a wolf at a door and says "This is when he blows the house down". Given three pictures from a story just heard, the child puts each beside the part of the story it belongs to. The adult prompts with "What is happening in the story here?"

### Limits

The child does this with prompting and support. Saying which moment a picture shows is given as an example, not as the only relationship. The pictures are the ones in the story itself.

### Common mistakes

Describing what is in the picture (colours, animals) without linking it to an event in the story. Making up a new story from the picture and ignoring the words that were read. Matching a picture to the wrong moment because one character appears in both.
