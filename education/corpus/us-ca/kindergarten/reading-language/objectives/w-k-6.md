## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

With adults guiding and supporting, the child tries out several kinds of digital tools for making writing and publishing it, which includes working together with other children.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Next to an adult, the child types their name and a short sentence on a tablet or keyboard, finding each letter. The child records their voice telling a story, or takes a photo of a drawing and adds a typed label. Two children take turns adding a page to a class book, which the adult prints.

### Limits

This is done with adults guiding and supporting. The child explores; mastery of any tool is not asked, and no tool is named. Both producing and publishing writing are covered, and working with other children is included. Typing speed is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Pressing keys or buttons for the effect and forgetting what they meant to write. Holding a key down and filling the line with one letter. Not finding a letter on a keyboard marked with capitals because they are looking for the small form.
