## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

With prompting and support, the child tells how the pictures in a text go with its words, for example by saying which idea, thing, place or person from the text a picture shows.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The adult reads a sentence about a beaver's dam, and the child points to the dam in the picture and says "That's the dam it's talking about". The child matches a photo of a seed to the sentence that names it. Asked "What does this picture show us?", the child answers with something from the words just read.

### Limits

The child does this with prompting and support. Naming the idea, thing, place or person that a picture shows is the statement's example, not the only kind of relationship. The pictures are those printed with the text itself.

### Common mistakes

Describing the picture alone and never linking it to what the words said. Assuming the picture shows everything the text says and missing details given only in words. Pointing to the most noticeable item in the picture and not the one the sentence is about.
