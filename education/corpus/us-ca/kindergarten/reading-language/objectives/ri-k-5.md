## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell which part of a book is its title page, which is its back cover and which is its front cover.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child picks up a book, holds it the right way up and taps the front cover, then turns it over to show the back cover. The child opens the book and finds the page inside that gives the title again before the main text begins.

### Limits

Three parts are named: the title page and the two covers, front and back. No prompting or support is mentioned. The statement does not ask the child to read the title or to name other parts such as the spine or a contents page.

### Common mistakes

Mixing up the title page with the front cover, since both show the title. Holding the book upside down or back to front and calling the back cover the front. Pointing to the first page of the main text as the title page.
