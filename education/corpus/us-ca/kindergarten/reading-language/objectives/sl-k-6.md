## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child talks loudly enough to be heard and expresses ideas, feelings and thoughts in a way that others can follow.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells the group what they made and how they feel about it, in a voice that reaches the child sitting farthest away. The child faces the listeners, finishes the sentence, and says it again more slowly when someone did not hear it.

### Limits

Two things are asked: being loud enough to hear, and being clear. The statement sets no audience size, length of speech or topic, and mentions no prompting or support.

### Common mistakes

Speaking toward the floor or into a hand, or getting quieter before the end of the sentence. Speaking so fast that the words are not heard as separate. Saying "it" and "that thing" without saying what is meant, so the listener cannot follow.
