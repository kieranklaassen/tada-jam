## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells who wrote a story and who drew its pictures, by name, and explains the part the author and the illustrator each have in telling it, when prompted and supported.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The adult reads the two names on the cover and the child repeats them, saying which one wrote the words and which one made the pictures. The child points to the words on a page for the author and to the picture for the illustrator. The adult prompts with "Who drew this?"

### Limits

The child does this with prompting and support. Two roles are named, author and illustrator, and each is defined by its part in telling a story. The statement does not say how the child learns the two names.

### Common mistakes

Swapping the two roles. Thinking the adult who reads the story aloud made it up. Not knowing that one person can be both author and illustrator of one book.
