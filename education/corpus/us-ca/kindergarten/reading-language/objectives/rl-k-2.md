## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When prompted and supported, the child tells again a story they know well, keeping its important details in.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells "The Three Little Pigs" back using puppets or by moving picture cards into order: the straw house, the stick house, the brick house, the wolf at each door. When the child stops, the adult asks "What happened next?" The child includes who was in the story and how it ended.

### Limits

The child does this with prompting and support, and the stories are ones the child already knows, not new ones. Key details are asked for; a word-for-word recital is not. The statement sets no length of story and no number of details.

### Common mistakes

Telling only the start and the end and leaving out the middle. Telling events out of order. Listing the characters without saying what happened.
