## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child puts questions, and answers them, for three purposes: to get help, to find something out, or to make clear something they have not understood.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child asks an adult "Can you help me open this?" or asks a classmate "Where do the blocks go?" When a visitor explains how a tool works, the child asks "What does that part do?" The child answers when another child asks them the same kinds of questions.

### Limits

Three purposes are named: getting help, finding something out, and making clear what was not understood. No prompting or support is mentioned. The statement is about talk with other people and names no text or topic.

### Common mistakes

Making a statement when a question is needed ("I can't do it" instead of asking for help). Asking a question whose answer was just given. Using "why" for every question, whatever they want to know.
