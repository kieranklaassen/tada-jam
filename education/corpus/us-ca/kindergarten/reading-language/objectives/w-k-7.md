## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child takes part in research and writing projects that are done together with others. As an example, the statement gives looking through several books by a favourite author and saying what one thinks of them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The class gathers five books by one author, and the child looks through them, picks the one they like best and tells why. The child adds a drawing or a sentence to a class chart about the books. The adult writes down what the group found.

### Limits

The projects are shared, so the child takes part and is not asked to research or write alone. The author study is the statement's example, not a required project. No number of sources, length or product is set.

### Common mistakes

Giving an opinion about one book before looking at the others. Copying a neighbour's opinion instead of giving their own. Treating the project as free drawing and adding something unrelated to the question.
