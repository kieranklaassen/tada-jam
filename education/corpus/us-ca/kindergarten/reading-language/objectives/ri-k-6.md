## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child says who wrote a text and who made its pictures, and explains what part the author and the illustrator each have in presenting its ideas or information.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child finds the two names on the cover or title page and says them, or repeats them after an adult reads them out. The child explains that the author wrote the words and the illustrator drew or painted the pictures, and points to an example of each on a page.

### Limits

No prompting or support is mentioned here, unlike the matching statement for literature. The two roles are author and illustrator; photographers and other contributors are not mentioned. The roles are defined by how each presents ideas or information.

### Common mistakes

Swapping the two roles. Believing the pictures are only decoration and carry no information. Naming the person who reads the book aloud as its author.
