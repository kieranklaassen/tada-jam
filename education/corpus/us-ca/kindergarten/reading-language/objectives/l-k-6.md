## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child uses the vocabulary and expressions learned from talking with others, from reading or hearing someone read aloud, and from responding to texts.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After hearing a story about a storm, the child says "enormous" or "all of a sudden" while playing or while telling about their own day. In a talk about a book on frogs, the child uses "tadpole" and "pond" in their own sentences.

### Limits

The statement names where the vocabulary comes from: conversation, reading (by the child or aloud by someone else), and responding to texts. It gives no word list and no number of words. It does not say whether the child uses them in speech or in writing.

### Common mistakes

Repeating a new word as a sound without knowing what it means. Using a new word only inside the sentence it was first heard in. Using a new word for the wrong thing, such as calling any baby animal a "tadpole".
