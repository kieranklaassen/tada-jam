## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

With prompting and support, the child asks about words in a text that they do not know, and answers questions about such words. The statement points to language standards 4 to 6 of this grade for further expectations.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While an adult reads a book about the sea aloud, the child stops at "tide" and asks "What's a tide?" Asked what "enormous" might mean, the child looks at the picture of the whale and says "really big". The adult prompts by pausing at a new word and asking who knows it.

### Limits

The child does this with prompting and support. California adds to this statement (it carries the state's mark for an addition); the record does not show which words are the added ones. The statement refers to this grade's language standards 4 to 6 for more on word meaning. It belongs to informational text and sets no number or kind of words.

### Common mistakes

Not noticing that a word was unfamiliar, and so asking nothing. Guessing a meaning from a word that sounds alike. Answering with something about the picture and not about the word.
