## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

With adults guiding and supporting, the child responds to what other children ask and suggest about a piece of writing, and puts in more details where needed to make the writing stronger.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child shows a drawing with a sentence to a partner, who asks "What colour was the dog?" The child answers and then adds the colour to the picture or adds a word with an adult's help. The adult repeats the partner's suggestion and asks the child where the new detail should go.

### Limits

This is done with adults guiding and supporting. The questions and suggestions come from other children. Details are added as needed; the statement does not mention rewriting, removing text or correcting spelling.

### Common mistakes

Hearing a question about the work as criticism and refusing to change it. Answering the partner aloud but not putting the detail on the page. Adding something unrelated to what was asked.
