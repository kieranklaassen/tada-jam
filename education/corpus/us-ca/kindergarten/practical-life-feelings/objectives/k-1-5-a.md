## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child knows that smoke from tobacco hurts the body and that the right thing is to stay away from it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says that breathing cigarette smoke is bad for the lungs. When someone lights a cigarette nearby, the child moves away or asks a grown-up to move with them. They point out a no-smoking sign and tell what it means.

### Limits

The statement is about the smoke, so it includes smoke from another person's cigarette. It does not mention vaping, nicotine, or addiction, and it asks nothing about why people smoke.

### Common mistakes

A child may conclude that a relative who smokes is a bad person, or become frightened for that relative. Some think smoke only hurts the person holding the cigarette. Smoke from a campfire or a candle is easily confused with tobacco smoke.
