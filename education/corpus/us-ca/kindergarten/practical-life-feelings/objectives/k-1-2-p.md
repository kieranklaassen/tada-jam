## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell what people do to protect themselves from the sun.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before going out the child puts on a hat with a brim, rubs sunscreen on arms, face, and neck with help, and puts on sunglasses. They choose the shady side of the playground at midday and drink water. They say why: the sun can burn skin.

### Limits

The statement names no measures, no sunscreen strength, and no time of day. It asks the child to tell about them; nothing is said here about doing them alone.

### Common mistakes

Children think the sun cannot burn on a cloudy or cool day. One morning layer of sunscreen is assumed to last all day, including after swimming. Ears, neck, and the tops of feet are missed.
