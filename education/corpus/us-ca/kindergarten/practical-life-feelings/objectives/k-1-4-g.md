## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name grown-ups they can rely on who support growing and developing in a healthy way, for example a dentist, an eye doctor (optometrist), a nurse, or a physician.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells who checked their teeth, who tested their eyes, and who measured and weighed them. They pair a tool with the person who uses it: a small mouth mirror, an eye chart, a stethoscope, a scale. In pretend play they take the role of the nurse or dentist and say what that person does to keep a child well.

### Limits

The four professions are given as examples, so others may count. The statement asks who these people are, not what treatments they give. Other content areas of this lane cover adults who help in an emergency (K.3.1.S) and with feelings (K.3.1.M).

### Common mistakes

Children link these people with pain or injections and may not see them as helpers. Many think a doctor is only for when someone is ill, not for check-ups of a healthy child. The words optometrist and physician are unknown to most five-year-olds, who say eye doctor and doctor.
