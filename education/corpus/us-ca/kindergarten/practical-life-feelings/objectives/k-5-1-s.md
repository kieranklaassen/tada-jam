## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell in which cases a grown-up must be fetched or 9-1-1 dialled.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sorts picture cards three ways: I can handle it (a dropped crayon), get a grown-up (a classmate with a bleeding nose, an unknown person at the door), phone 9-1-1 (smoke in the house, an adult who will not wake up). They say why for each card.

### Limits

The statement is about deciding, not about doing; the asking and the phone call are shown under K.4.1.S. It lists no cases.

### Common mistakes

Children make both errors: they try to deal with a real danger alone, or they want 9-1-1 for a lost toy. They think 9-1-1 is only for fires. When the adult in charge is the one who is hurt, they do not know that they must then decide themselves.
