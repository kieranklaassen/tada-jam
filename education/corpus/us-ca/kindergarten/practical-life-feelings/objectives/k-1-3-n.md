## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell what good it does a person to keep their body moving.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After running or dancing, the child puts a hand on the chest and says the heart beats fast and they feel warm. They say what moving does: makes muscles and bones strong, helps sleep, feels good, lets them play longer. They compare how they feel after sitting a long time with how they feel after playing outside.

### Limits

The statement asks for benefits in general and names none. No amount of activity per day is stated. Ways of being active regularly are in K.5.1.N.

### Common mistakes

A fast heartbeat, sweating, or being out of breath is taken as a sign that something is wrong. Children think exercise means sport or lessons, so their own running and climbing do not count. Benefits far in the future mean little; they understand "feels good now".
