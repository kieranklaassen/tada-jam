## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name people whose job is in health care and who support good health habits.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child names the school nurse, the dentist, the doctor, the dental hygienist, the pharmacist. They say what each one teaches or checks: how to brush, when to wash hands, how tall they have grown. In pretend play they are the nurse who shows a doll how to cover a cough.

### Limits

The statement names no professions. It is about people who work in health care and about everyday habits, not emergencies (K.3.1.S). A similar statement with examples is K.1.4.G.

### Common mistakes

Children know the doctor and no one else. They think these workers only act when someone is ill or hurt. Parents and teachers get named; they help, but they are not health care workers.
