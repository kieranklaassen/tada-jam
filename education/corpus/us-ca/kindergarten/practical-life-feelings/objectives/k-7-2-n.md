## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child decides in advance what a breakfast that nourishes the body will consist of.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child chooses toy foods or picture cards and lays them on a plate: something made from grain, a fruit, and milk, egg, or yoghurt. They draw tomorrow's breakfast and tell a parent what is needed for it. They say why each item is on the plate.

### Limits

The product is a plan. The statement does not say the child prepares or eats the meal, and it does not define nutritious. Why breakfast matters is K.1.4.N.

### Common mistakes

Children plan what they like best (pancakes with syrup, sweet cereal) and call it healthy because it is breakfast food. They plan one item only, or a meal too large to eat. Drinks are forgotten.
