## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name habits that help the environment. The examples given are collecting litter, recycling, and not leaving water running or lamps on.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child flips the light switch on leaving a room and closes the tap while brushing teeth. They put a can, a sheet of paper, and a banana peel into the right bins. On the playground they pick up a wrapper, with gloves or tongs, and bin it.

### Limits

The actions listed are given as examples, so other habits may count. The child is asked to name them; the statement does not require doing them. It does not ask the child to explain why they help.

### Common mistakes

Children do the action as a rule without linking it to water, energy, or animals. Sorting is hard: any plastic or paper goes in recycling, including dirty items. "Environment" is an unknown word; they understand "outside", "nature", or "our park".
