## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child picks food that is good for the body wherever they are, across different places.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At the school lunch line the child takes the vegetable and the fruit. At a birthday party they eat cake and also take fruit and water. At a restaurant or a grandparent's house they ask for a side of vegetables or a glass of milk.

### Limits

The statement names no settings and no foods; home, school, a shop, a restaurant, and a party are all possible. It is a practising statement, so the child does the choosing. Naming healthy foods is K.1.1.N.

### Common mistakes

Children keep the rule in the place where it was taught (school) and not elsewhere. At parties and restaurants they think healthy choices do not apply. With many options in front of them they pick the first familiar item.
