## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child knows that anything at all can poison or hurt someone when it is handled in an unsafe way.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells how an everyday thing becomes dangerous: scissors when running, a plastic bag over the head, too many vitamins, a berry from a garden plant in the mouth. They ask a grown-up before tasting or using something they do not know. They show the careful way to carry scissors.

### Limits

The statement says anything, so it is wider than K.1.4.A, which is about some household products. It names no objects or substances.

### Common mistakes

Children sort the world into dangerous things and safe things and do not expect a safe thing to hurt. They think only items with a warning symbol are poison. Food-like things, such as berries, mushrooms, or vitamins, are assumed safe to eat.
