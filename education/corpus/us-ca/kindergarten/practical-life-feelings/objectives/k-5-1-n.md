## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell how they could, on a regular basis, join in lively play and in kinds of exercise they like.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lists what they like to do with their body: tag, climbing, dancing, riding a scooter, swimming, kicking a ball. They say when it fits into their days: at break, after school in the yard, at the park on Saturday. They choose one to do today and name who could do it with them.

### Limits

Three conditions are in the wording: it happens often, it is active, and the child enjoys it. No minutes per day and no list of activities are given. This is the decision-making standard, so the statement is about the child's own choice among options.

### Common mistakes

Children do not count ordinary play as physical activity and name only organised sport. They name things they did once, such as a holiday swim, as regular. Watching movement on a screen is mistaken for active play.
