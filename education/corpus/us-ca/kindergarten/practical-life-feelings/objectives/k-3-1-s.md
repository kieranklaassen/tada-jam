## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name grown-ups they rely on to turn to when there is an emergency.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child names who would help: a parent, a teacher, a neighbour the family knows, a firefighter, a police officer, a paramedic. They pair the helper with the trouble, such as fire with firefighter. They say where that person is found: in the classroom, in the house, or by phone.

### Limits

The statement asks for people, not for actions. It names no roles. How to reach them is K.4.1.S, and deciding when help is needed is K.5.1.S.

### Common mistakes

Children name only a parent and have no second person when the parent is the one who is hurt. Uniformed helpers can frighten them, so they may hide from a firefighter. Superheroes or older siblings get named as emergency helpers.
