## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can act out picking up a phone call safely.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child picks up a toy phone, says hello, and listens. They do not give their name or address and do not say that they are alone. They say "Just a moment, I'll get my mum" or "She can't come to the phone now" and fetch a grown-up or hang up.

### Limits

The statement does not say what answering safely consists of; families and schools set the details. It is about a phone call only. Messaging and video calls are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Children answer every question a caller asks, since answering adults truthfully is what they are taught. They say "Mum isn't home". Some nod or shake their head instead of speaking.
