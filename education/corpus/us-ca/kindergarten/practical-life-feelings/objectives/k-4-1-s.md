## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can show two ways of getting help: going to a grown-up they trust and saying what is needed, or phoning 9-1-1.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

On a toy or unplugged phone the child presses 9, 1, 1 in order. They say their name, what happened, and where they are, and they stay on the line. In a second role-play they go to an adult and say "I need help, my friend fell".

### Limits

Either route counts: an adult or the emergency number. It is a showing statement, so the child acts it out. It does not say what the child must tell the dispatcher; knowing when to call is K.5.1.S.

### Common mistakes

Children do not know their address or their parent's full name. They hang up as soon as they have spoken. Some say "nine eleven" and look for an 11 key.
