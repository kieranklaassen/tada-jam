## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child knows that it matters to start the day with a breakfast that is good for the body.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells what they ate this morning and how their body felt at mid-morning: full and ready, or hungry and tired. They build a breakfast from toy food, such as porridge, fruit, egg, toast, milk. They say that breakfast gives energy to play and learn until lunch.

### Limits

The statement is about knowing why breakfast matters. It does not say what such a breakfast contains. Planning one is K.7.2.N.

### Common mistakes

Children do not connect being tired or cross at ten o'clock with a missed breakfast. Any food eaten in the morning counts for them, including sweets or a sugary drink. Some think breakfast is only a certain food, such as cereal.
