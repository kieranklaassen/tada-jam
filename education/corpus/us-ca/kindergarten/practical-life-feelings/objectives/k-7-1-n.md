## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When there is a choice of snacks, the child picks ones that nourish the body.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Offered a tray with fruit, cheese, crackers, biscuits, and sweets, the child takes the fruit or cheese. At the shop or the kitchen cupboard they point to a banana or yoghurt for after school. They pour water for themselves.

### Limits

This is a practising statement: the child does the choosing. Knowing which snacks are healthy is K.1.2.N. It sets no rule about how often or how much.

### Common mistakes

Children know which snack is the healthy one and still take the sweet one when both are in reach. They choose by package and character. They take a healthy snack and leave it uneaten.
