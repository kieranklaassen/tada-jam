## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child lets their feelings out in ways that suit the moment and the people around them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says "I'm angry" instead of hitting, stamps their feet on the spot or squeezes a cushion, and takes slow breaths. They cry when sad and go to someone for a hug. They jump and cheer when glad without knocking into anyone.

### Limits

The statement does not say which ways are suitable. It is a practising statement, about what the child does. Naming feelings is K.1.1.M.

### Common mistakes

Children think the feeling itself is wrong, not the hitting, and try to hide it. They know the calm-down steps when calm and cannot use them when upset. Strong joy comes out as shouting or grabbing.
