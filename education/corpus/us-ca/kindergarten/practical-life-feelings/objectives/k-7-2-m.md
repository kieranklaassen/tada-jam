## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell good ways of letting other people know that one cares about them, thinks of them, and is concerned for them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says what they could do: ask "Are you okay?" when someone falls, fetch a tissue, make a card for a sick classmate, split a snack, let someone go first. They tell about a time someone did this for them. They act one out with a partner.

### Limits

Telling is enough here, though the statement sits under the practising standard. The ways must be positive ones. No list is given.

### Common mistakes

Children offer what would comfort themselves (their own teddy) without checking what the other person wants. They laugh when someone falls, out of surprise, and do not see how it looks. Care is limited to friends; a child they do not like is left out.
