## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell what they would say to people in their family to get a choice of food that is good for the body.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child practises sentences: "Can we buy grapes?", "Could I have water instead?", "May I have carrots in my lunch box?". They say when to ask: while the shopping list is written or before the snack is poured. They role-play asking a parent and a grandparent, politely and once.

### Limits

The child tells how; the statement is under the communication standard. It names family members as the ones asked. It does not say which foods.

### Common mistakes

Children ask by whining or at the checkout, and the answer is no. They ask for "something healthy" without naming a food, so nothing changes. Some think food choices belong to adults only and never ask.
