## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can act out requesting help with a problem that has to do with health.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child goes to the teacher and says what hurts and where: "My tummy hurts", "My tooth is wobbly and bleeding", "I feel dizzy". They point to the place. They practise saying when it started and answering "Does it hurt a lot or a little?".

### Limits

It is a showing statement. The wording does not say whom the child asks or which problems are meant. Emergencies and 9-1-1 are in the safety area (K.4.1.S).

### Common mistakes

Children say "I don't feel good" and cannot say where or how. A stomach ache is named for almost any discomfort, including worry. They wait and tell nobody, or tell only a classmate.
