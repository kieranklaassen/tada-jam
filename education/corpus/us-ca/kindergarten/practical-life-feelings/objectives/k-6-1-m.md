## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child sets out, ahead of time, what they will do in their own household to assist the people in their family.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child picks one job: set the table, feed the cat, put shoes by the door, carry their plate to the sink. They say or draw when they will do it and for whom. The next day they tell whether they did it.

### Limits

This is the goal-setting standard, so the product is a plan; the statement does not ask for proof that it was carried out. It names no tasks and no length of time. The people helped are family, and the place is the home.

### Common mistakes

Children choose something too big or vague ("I will help with everything"). They pick what they enjoy, not what the family needs. A plan with no set moment ("sometime") is forgotten.
