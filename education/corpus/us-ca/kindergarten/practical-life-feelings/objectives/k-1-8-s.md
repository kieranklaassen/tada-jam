## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name what keeps them from harm in three activities: play, cycling, and getting across a road.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child puts on a helmet and fastens the strap under the chin. At a curb they stop, hold an adult's hand, and look both ways. On the playground they say what is safe: one at a time on the slide, feet first, no pushing.

### Limits

Three activities are named and no specific rules are given. Showing the road crossing in action is a separate statement (K.7.2.S). Travelling as a passenger is K.1.3.S.

### Common mistakes

Children think that if they can see a car, the driver can see them. They look both ways as a ritual without noticing what is coming. A helmet is worn tipped back or unbuckled.
