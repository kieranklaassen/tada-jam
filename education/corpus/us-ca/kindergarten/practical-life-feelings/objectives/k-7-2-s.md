## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can perform a safe street crossing.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a curb, or a taped line on the floor, the child stops, takes an adult's hand, looks left, right, and left again, and listens. They walk straight across without running and keep looking. At a signal they wait for the walking figure.

### Limits

The statement gives no steps and does not say whether an adult is present. The child performs the crossing; naming the ways to be safe is K.1.8.S.

### Common mistakes

Children turn their head without registering what is coming. They cannot judge the speed or distance of a car and step out when one is close. They run across, or stop looking once they have started.
