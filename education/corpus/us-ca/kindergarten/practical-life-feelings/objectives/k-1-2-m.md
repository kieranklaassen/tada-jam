## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell what families are like and what features they have.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child draws or builds their family with figures and says who is in it and who lives where. They tell what the people do for each other: cook, help, play, look after someone who is ill. They hear about a classmate's family and say one thing that is the same and one that is not.

### Limits

The statement names no features and no family form. Its wording is plural, so it is about families in general and not only the child's own.

### Common mistakes

Children take their own family as the pattern and say another form is not a real family. They count only those who live in the house, or add pets and friends. Many think a family needs a certain number of parents or children.
