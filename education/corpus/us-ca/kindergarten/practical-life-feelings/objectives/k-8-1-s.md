## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can act out reporting to a grown-up they trust that a weapon has been found, whether the finder was the child or one of their friends.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In role-play the child sees a pretend object standing for a weapon, does not touch it, moves away, and goes straight to a teacher or parent. They say "I found a weapon" or "My friend found one", and where it is. They bring the friend along instead of staying to look.

### Limits

The case is finding a weapon, by the child or by a friend. The statement is about telling; it does not say what else the child does. A footnote cites the Education Code.

### Common mistakes

Children pick the object up to bring it to the adult. They stay silent to protect a friend. They wait until later in the day instead of going at once.
