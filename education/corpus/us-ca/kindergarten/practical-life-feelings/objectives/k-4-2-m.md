## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works together with the people around them and shares things.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children carry a box together, build one tower from a single set of blocks, or take turns with the one red crayon. The child hands over a toy when their turn ends and waits for it to come back. They split a pile of beads so each has some.

### Limits

The statement is two verbs and no conditions: it does not say with whom, with what, or for how long. It is under the communication standard, so talking while working together is part of it.

### Common mistakes

Children understand sharing as giving a thing away permanently, so they refuse. Taking turns fails because one minute of waiting feels very long at this age. In joint work each child builds their own part side by side without a common plan.
