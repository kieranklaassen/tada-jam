## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell that a medicine does good in some cases and does damage in others.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sorts picture cards of situations into two piles: a medicine that helped (a sick child given the right dose by a parent) and a medicine that hurt (a child who swallowed someone else's pills). They say why each card goes where it does.

### Limits

The statement gives both sides and no detail: it does not say when a medicine helps or what makes it dangerous. Who gives the medicine is the subject of the next statement (K.1.3.A).

### Common mistakes

Children of this age tend to sort things as all good or all bad, so one thing that is both is hard for them. Some decide medicine is always safe because a doctor or parent gives it. Others become afraid to take any.
