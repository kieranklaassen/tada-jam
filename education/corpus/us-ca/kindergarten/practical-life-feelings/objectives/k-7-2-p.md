## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can show actions that stop germs from passing between people, for example using a tissue or washing their hands.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child coughs and sneezes into the elbow or a tissue, throws the tissue away, and washes with soap. They wash before eating and after the toilet, playing outside, or blowing the nose. They keep their own cup and do not share a spoon.

### Limits

The two actions in brackets are examples, so others may count. It is a showing statement. Why the spread matters is K.1.4.P.

### Common mistakes

Children sneeze into the hand and then touch toys. They hold the tissue and put it back in a pocket or on the table. They wash only when hands look dirty.
