## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child carries out, in a way that works, the habits for looking after their teeth and keeping their body clean.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child wets their hands, soaps the palms, backs, between the fingers, and thumbs, rinses, and dries. They put a pea-sized amount of paste on the brush and brush every surface of the teeth, top and bottom, then spit. They blow their nose into a tissue, bin it, and comb their hair.

### Limits

Here the child does the habits; naming them is K.1.1.P. The way it is done must work, but no duration or technique is written down. Adult help is not mentioned either way.

### Common mistakes

Handwashing is too short and skips soap, thumbs, and drying. Brushing covers the front teeth only, with the paste swallowed. Children do it when told and do not start unprompted.
