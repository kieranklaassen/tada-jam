## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child knows that certain things kept around the house can hurt a person who swallows them or breathes them in.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child points to things at home that are not for eating, drinking, or sniffing: bleach, dishwasher tablets, spray cleaner, paint, glue. They say "not food" and leave the bottle closed, or fetch a grown-up when a container is open or spilled. They sort empty, clean packages into "for eating" and "never in the mouth or nose".

### Limits

The statement says some products, not all, and names none. It covers two routes only, the mouth and the breath; skin and eyes are not mentioned. A broader statement in the safety area (K.1.9.S) says anything can hurt when used wrongly.

### Common mistakes

Brightly coloured liquids and tablets look like juice or candy to a child of this age. Children judge by smell and colour, so a product that smells of lemon or flowers seems safe. Breathing something in is rarely thought of as a way to get hurt.
