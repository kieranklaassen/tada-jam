## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can act out a suitable way of telling people what they need or would like.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child speaks in a calm voice: "I need the toilet", "I'm thirsty", "Can I have a turn?", "I want to be alone for a bit". They look at the person and wait for an answer. They practise the same request to a friend and to a teacher.

### Limits

The statement does not define what is suitable, and it draws no line between a need and a wish. It is a showing statement about the child's own requests. Feelings are handled in K.7.1.M.

### Common mistakes

Children grab, whine, cry, or point instead of speaking. They state a wish as a demand ("Give me that"). They treat every wish as a need, or they hide a real need, such as the toilet, until it is too late.
