## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child knows the features that mark an act as bullying.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears a short story and says whether it is bullying: someone is hurt or left out on purpose, it happens again and again, and the one doing it is stronger, older, or in a group. They compare it with a single quarrel over a toy between two friends. They say how the child in the story feels.

### Limits

The statement does not list the signs. It asks the child to know the signs; what to do about bullying is not part of this statement.

### Common mistakes

Five-year-olds call any unkind act bullying, including one argument or an accident. They miss forms without hitting, such as leaving someone out or name-calling. They rarely notice that repetition is what separates bullying from a single fight.
