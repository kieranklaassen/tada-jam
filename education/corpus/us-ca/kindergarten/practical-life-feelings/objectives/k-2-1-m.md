## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name things that relatives and friends do that keep a person feeling good and healthy.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells who makes their breakfast, who reads to them at bedtime, who hugs them when they are sad, and who plays with them at break. They draw one of these moments. They say how they felt before and after the help.

### Limits

This sits under the standard on influences, so it is about what other people do for the child's health and mood. The statement lists no ways. Two groups are named: family and friends.

### Common mistakes

Children think of help as things given (toys, treats) and overlook care such as cooking, listening, or setting a bedtime. A rule that protects them, such as an early bedtime, is seen as unkind. Friends are named for fun only, not for comfort.
