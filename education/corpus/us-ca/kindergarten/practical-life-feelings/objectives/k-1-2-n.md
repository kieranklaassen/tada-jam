## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can point out several different snacks that are good for the body.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

From a tray of real or pretend items the child picks things to eat between meals: apple slices, a banana, carrot sticks, cheese, plain yoghurt, water. They name each and say it is a good choice. They look in their own lunch box and say which item is the snack.

### Limits

The statement is about snacks, not meals, and sets no number. It asks the child to know them; choosing them in practice is K.7.1.N.

### Common mistakes

Packaging with fruit pictures or cartoon characters is taken as a sign that the snack is healthy. Children think a snack means a sweet or crisps by definition. Juice and fruit-flavoured drinks are counted as fruit.
