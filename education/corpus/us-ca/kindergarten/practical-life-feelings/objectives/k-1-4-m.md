## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell what it is about a person that sets them apart from everyone else.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says things that are true of them alone or in their own combination: their name, their voice, their fingerprint, what they are good at, what they love, their family's stories. They make a thumbprint and compare it with a neighbour's. They say one special thing about a classmate.

### Limits

The statement is about every person, so it covers classmates and family as well as the child. It names no kinds of trait. Telling about one's own body is K.1.2.G, and comparing people is K.1.3.G.

### Common mistakes

Children name possessions ("I have a blue bike") in place of traits. They name something that many classmates share and do not notice. Some hear "different from everyone" as something bad and want to be the same.
