## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name grown-ups they can rely on, both in their school and where they live.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says who they would go to: a parent, a grandparent, or another carer; the teacher, the aide, the yard supervisor, the office worker. They draw one hand for each place and name a person for each finger. They say why they trust that person: "she listens", "he helps me".

### Limits

Two places are named, and the statement sets no number of adults. It asks who they are. Asking them for help is K.1.11.S, and turning to them about feelings is K.3.1.M.

### Common mistakes

Children name friends, pets, or siblings their own age. They name only one person, usually a parent, and nobody in the school building. Any familiar adult is treated as someone to rely on, without thinking about whether that person listens and helps.
