## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When other people act in ways that are healthy and safe, the child gives them encouragement.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells a classmate "Good, you put your helmet on" or "You washed your hands, me too". They give a thumbs-up to a friend who chose water or who waited at the curb. They invite another child to join in: "Come and wash with me".

### Limits

This is the health-promotion standard: the child's encouragement is directed at other people. The wording does not limit the encouragement to spoken words. The statement asks for support of good choices; it does not ask the child to correct bad ones.

### Common mistakes

Children tell on those who break a rule instead of praising those who keep it. Praise becomes an order ("You have to wear it!"). They praise only friends.
