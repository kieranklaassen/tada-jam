## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name habits that work for keeping teeth healthy and the body clean.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child picks out the things used for keeping clean, such as a toothbrush, toothpaste, floss, soap, a towel, a comb, and a tissue, and says what each is for. They tell when it is done: brush morning and night, wash hands before eating and after the toilet, bathe, wear clean clothes. They put the steps of brushing in order with picture cards.

### Limits

The statement asks the child to know the habits; doing them is K.7.1.P. It covers teeth and the rest of the body. It gives no list, no brushing time, and no frequency.

### Common mistakes

Children think a quick rinse of the fingers under water is washing. They brush only the front teeth, for a few seconds. Looking clean is taken as being clean, so hands that look fine are not washed.
