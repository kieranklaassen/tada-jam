## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can show the way they would go to a grown-up they trust and request help.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child walks up to a teacher or parent, gets their attention by name or a tap on the arm, and says what is wrong in a sentence: "I'm hurt", "Someone is bothering me", "I can't find my mum". They practise this in role-play with different problems. They try a second grown-up when the first one is busy.

### Limits

The child performs the asking and does not only talk about it. The statement does not say for which problems. Calling 9-1-1 is added in K.4.1.S.

### Common mistakes

Children stand near the adult and wait to be noticed, or cry without saying what is wrong. They give up after one try. Some do not ask because they think they will be in trouble.
