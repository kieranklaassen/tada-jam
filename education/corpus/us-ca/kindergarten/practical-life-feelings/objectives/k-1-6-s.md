## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell what rules the school has for how to get on with other people.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells the class rules in their own words: take turns, keep hands to yourself, use kind words, let everyone join. They show one with a classmate, such as passing the ball or waiting in line. They point to the picture of the rule on the classroom wall.

### Limits

Only rules of the school are asked for, and only those about getting on with other people. The wording does not narrow those people to classmates. The statement lists no rules. Recognising bullying is the next statement (K.1.7.S).

### Common mistakes

Children can say a rule and still not connect it to what they just did. They report every small breach by a classmate and see that as following the rule. Rules are understood as what the teacher wants, not as what makes play work for everyone.
