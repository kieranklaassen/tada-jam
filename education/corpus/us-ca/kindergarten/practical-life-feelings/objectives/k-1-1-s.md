## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell rules that keep people from harm in three places: at school, at home, and out in the community.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says a rule and where it belongs: walk in the hallway, do not touch the stove, hold a hand in the parking lot. They sort rule pictures onto three mats, one per place. They tell what could happen when the rule is broken.

### Limits

Three settings are named and no particular rules are listed. The statement asks the child to know the rules; following them is a separate statement (K.7.1.S).

### Common mistakes

Children recite a rule without knowing what it protects against. A rule learned in one place is not carried to another: no running indoors applies in class but is forgotten in a shop. "Community" is an unfamiliar word; children think of specific places such as the park or the store.
