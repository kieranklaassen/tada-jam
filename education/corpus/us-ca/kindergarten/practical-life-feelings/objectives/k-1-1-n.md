## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can list several different foods that are good for the body and say what makes eating them needed to stay well.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child picks real or toy foods from a basket and names them: apple, carrot, beans, bread, egg, milk, fish. For each they give a reason in their own words: "gives me energy to run", "makes my bones strong", "helps me grow". They sort a shopping bag into foods to eat often and foods to eat only sometimes.

### Limits

Two parts: naming and giving the reason. The wording asks for many kinds and sets no number and no food groups. Nutrients, portions, and labels are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Children sort food into good and bad and feel guilty about the bad pile. They judge by taste or colour: sweet fruit yoghurt and fruit-flavoured sweets are both "fruit". Reasons do not go beyond "because it's healthy", which repeats the word without explaining.
