## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can tell what keeps a passenger from getting hurt on a bus or in any other vehicle.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child climbs into a seat, pulls the belt across, and clicks the buckle, or shows this on a chair with a strap. They say the rules for the bus: sit down, face forward, keep hands and head inside, wait until it stops before standing. They act out waiting back from the curb until the door opens.

### Limits

A bus is the one vehicle named; all others are left open. The statement is about being a passenger. Walking across a road and cycling belong to K.1.8.S.

### Common mistakes

Children think a short trip or a slow speed means the belt is not needed. They wear the shoulder strap behind the back or under the arm. On a bus without belts they assume there are no rules for sitting.
