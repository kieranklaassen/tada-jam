## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When playing, the child keeps to the rules that prevent harm, and also does the set routines for safety.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lines up and walks out quietly in a fire drill. They wait their turn at the slide, go down feet first, and keep sand low. They hang up their bag so nobody trips, and they stop at the whistle.

### Limits

This is a doing statement: knowing the rules is K.1.1.S, and here the child acts on them. It lists no rules and no routines.

### Common mistakes

Children keep a rule while an adult watches and stop keeping it when excited or unobserved. In a drill they run, hide, or go back for a toy. They obey the words of a rule and miss its purpose, for example walking backwards along the pool edge because only running was forbidden.
