## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can name grown-ups, in their school and in their household, whom they rely on for help when feelings or worries are troubling them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says whom they go to when they are very sad, scared at night, or worried about something: a parent or grandparent, the teacher, the school counsellor. They practise the opening words: "I feel bad and I don't know why." They place a photo or drawing of those people on a "who helps me" page.

### Limits

The help is for the mind and feelings, not for injuries or emergencies (those are K.3.1.S). Two places are named. No professions are listed.

### Common mistakes

Children believe adults already know how they feel, so they do not tell. Worry shows as a stomach ache or as angry or restless behaviour, and the child does not connect it to a feeling someone could help with. They name a person who is fun and not a person who listens.
