## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can say what a weapon is and why weapons are dangerous.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says in simple words that a weapon is a thing made or used to hurt people, such as a gun or a knife. They tell what could happen: someone could be badly hurt or killed, and it cannot be undone. They say that a weapon is never a toy and that they do not touch one.

### Limits

The statement names no kinds of weapon. A footnote cites the Education Code. Telling an adult about a weapon is covered by K.1.13.S and K.8.1.S.

### Common mistakes

Children cannot reliably tell a real gun from a toy one. Screen stories teach them that a person who is shot gets up again. Many are sure they would not touch a gun they found, but many children who find one handle it anyway.
