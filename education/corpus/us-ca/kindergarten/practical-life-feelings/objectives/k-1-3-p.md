## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child can say what germs are.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says that germs are living things too small to see, that they are on hands and objects and in coughs and sneezes, and that some of them make people ill. They rub glitter or flour on their hands, shake hands with a friend, and see where it ends up. They look at their hands and say that germs can be there though the hands look clean.

### Limits

The statement asks only for a meaning of the word. How germs pass between people and why that matters is K.1.4.P; stopping the spread is K.7.2.P. Bacteria and viruses are not named.

### Common mistakes

Children picture germs as bugs or dirt that can be seen, so clean-looking hands have none. They think every germ makes people ill. Illness is blamed on cold weather or on being naughty, not on germs.
