## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child looks at two numerals in writing, each standing for a number from 1 to 10, and tells how the two numbers compare.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads two numeral cards, such as 4 and 9, and says which is more and which is less. To check, the child builds each number with counters and lines the two rows up, or finds both on a number track and sees which comes later.

### Limits

Both numbers lie from 1 to 10 and are given as numerals, with no objects shown. Two numbers are compared at a time. The statement does not mention the symbols <, > or =.

### Common mistakes

Not yet linking a numeral to its amount, and so guessing. Misreading look-alike numerals such as 6 and 9, and so comparing the wrong numbers. Knowing which is more but being unable to say which is less.
