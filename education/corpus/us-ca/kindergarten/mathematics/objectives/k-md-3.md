## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Given a set of categories, the child sorts objects into them, counts how many are in each, and then puts the categories in order by those counts.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child drops buttons into bowls, one bowl for each colour, counts the buttons in each bowl, and lines the bowls up from the fewest buttons to the most. The child says which bowl has the most and which has the fewest.

### Limits

A footnote limits this to ten or fewer objects in any category. The categories are supplied to the child, who is not asked to invent them. The statement does not mention graphs or charts.

### Common mistakes

Switching the sorting rule midway, starting by colour and drifting to shape. Miscounting a pile by counting one thing twice or missing one. Ordering the piles by how much space they take up, not by how many each holds.
