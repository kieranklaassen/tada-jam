## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child counts to find out how many things there are: up to 20 when they are set out in a row, in a grid of rows and columns, or in a ring, and up to 10 when they lie about with no pattern. Told a number from 1 to 20, the child also counts out a group of that size.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child counts pegs standing in a row, eggs in a tray, or chairs set in a ring, and says the total. From a basket of acorns the child takes out exactly the number asked for, such as twelve, and stops there.

### Limits

Up to 20 things when they are in a row, a rows-and-columns grid or a ring; up to 10 when they are scattered. The numbers given for counting out run from 1 to 20.

### Common mistakes

In a ring, forgetting where the count began and going round past the first thing. In a scattered group, counting some things twice and missing others because there is no path to follow. When counting out, carrying on past the number asked for, or grabbing a handful without counting.
