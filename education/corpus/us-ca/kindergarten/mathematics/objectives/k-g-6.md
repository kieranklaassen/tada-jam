## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child puts simple shapes together so that they make a bigger shape. The example given is joining two triangles along a whole side to get a rectangle.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child slides two triangle tiles together edge to edge and sees a rectangle or a bigger triangle appear. The child fills an outline with pattern blocks, turning and flipping pieces until they fit with no gaps.

### Limits

The statement names no shapes beyond its one example, two triangles making a rectangle. Taking a shape apart into smaller ones is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Joining pieces at a corner or along only part of a side, leaving gaps or overlaps. Not thinking to turn or flip a piece, and giving up when it does not fit as first placed. Not seeing the new whole: still saying "two triangles" and not recognising the rectangle they make.
