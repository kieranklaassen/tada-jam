## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child writes the numerals 0 through 20. Given a group of things, the child writes the numeral that tells how many there are, anywhere in that range, and uses 0 when there is nothing to count.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child counts a handful of buttons and writes the matching numeral on paper, in a sand tray or on a slate. Shown an empty plate, the child writes 0. The child also writes a numeral when someone says its number.

### Limits

Numerals run from 0 to 20 and no further. Zero is included and stands for an empty group. The statement asks for numerals, not for number words spelled out.

### Common mistakes

Writing numerals mirrored, most often 2, 3, 5, 7 and 9. Writing a teen number in the order it is heard, so that sixteen comes out as 61. Leaving out 0, or not accepting that an empty group has a number.
