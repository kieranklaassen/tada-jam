## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child adds and subtracts with ease and accuracy as long as every number involved, totals included, stays at 5 or below.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Shown two fingers and then one more, the child says "three" at once without counting each finger. Asked "four take away one", the child answers straight away or after a glance at a hand. The child does this for any two numbers whose total is five or less.

### Limits

Totals go no higher than 5, and taking away starts from 5 or less. This is the only statement at this level that asks for fluency; the neighbouring statements that reach 10 name objects and drawings as ways of working. The statement names no method and no time limit.

### Common mistakes

Still counting everything from one for facts as small as 2 and 1. Knowing the adding facts but being much slower at taking away. Being off by one from counting the starting number as the first step.
