## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child describes features of objects that can be measured, with length and weight given as examples. For one object, the child describes more than one such feature.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child picks up a stick and says it is long and light, then a rock and says it is short and heavy. Holding one bucket, the child talks about how tall it is, how heavy it is and how much it holds.

### Limits

Length and weight are examples; the statement gives no closed list of features. The child only describes: no units, numbers or measuring tools are mentioned. Comparing two objects is the next statement.

### Common mistakes

Using "big" for everything, without saying whether tall, long, wide or heavy is meant. Offering features that cannot be measured, such as colour. Assuming that the larger object must be the heavier one.
