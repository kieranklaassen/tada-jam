## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

Starting at any number the child is told, the child carries on counting upward from there without going back to 1 first. This applies inside the stretch of the number sequence the child already knows.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An adult says "seven" and the child answers "eight, nine, ten" straight away. With a row of things where the first six are already counted and covered by a cloth, the child continues "seven, eight" on the uncovered ones without lifting the cloth to start over.

### Limits

The statement sets no number range of its own: the starting number only has to lie inside the sequence the child can already say, which a neighbouring statement takes to 100. Counting goes forward only; counting backward is not asked here.

### Common mistakes

Whispering the whole count from 1 to reach the starting number. Saying the starting number again as if it were the next one, or skipping the number right after it. Managing from small starting numbers but losing the place when the start is in the teens or just before a new decade.
