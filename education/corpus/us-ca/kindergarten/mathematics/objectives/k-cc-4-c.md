## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child links counting to how many there are, as part of grasping how numbers and amounts go together. In this part, the child knows that moving on to the next number word in the count means an amount that is bigger by one.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child builds a staircase of cube towers, each one cube taller than the tower before it, and says the numbers while touching each tower in turn. When one more block is put on a pile of six, the child says "seven" without recounting the pile.

### Limits

This part sets no number range. It speaks of the step up to the next number; one fewer and going backward are not mentioned. Addition and subtraction as operations sit under a different heading at this level.

### Common mistakes

Recounting the whole group from one after a single thing is added. Treating the number words as a chant with no sizes attached, and so not knowing that eight is more than seven without lining things up. Knowing "one more" for small amounts but not carrying it past five or into the teens.
