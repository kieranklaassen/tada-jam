## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works out word problems that call for addition or subtraction, and adds and subtracts, staying within 10. Objects or drawings that show the problem are named as an example way of working.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears "four ducks are on the pond and three more come", sets out four counters, puts three more with them and counts them all. For "seven apples, two get eaten", the child lays out seven, slides two away and counts what is left. The child may draw dots and cross some out instead.

### Limits

The stated range is within 10. Objects and drawings are an example method, not the only one. The statement itself does not say which kinds of word problem are meant. The cluster heading names how the two operations are understood: addition as joining things and as putting more with a group, subtraction as splitting a group into parts and as removing some of it.

### Common mistakes

Adding whenever two numbers are heard, without attending to what happens in the story. When putting more on, counting the starting amount again as the first of the new ones and ending one short. In subtraction, counting the things removed instead of the ones left.
