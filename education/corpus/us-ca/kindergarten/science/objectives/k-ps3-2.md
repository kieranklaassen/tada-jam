## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

With tools and materials, the child designs and builds a structure that makes a patch of ground warm up less in the sun. The clarification's examples of such structures are tents, canopies and umbrellas.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child props a sheet of card on sticks over a patch of the playground, waits, and feels the ground under it and beside it. The child tries cloth, foil or paper as the cover and says which patch stays cooler.

### Limits

The expectation states no assessment boundary. It is marked as integrating engineering practices. The three structures named in the clarification are examples.

### Common mistakes

Building a cover that looks like a tent but does not keep the sun off the test spot. Thinking shade makes cold, instead of keeping sunlight away. Feeling the cover and not the ground beneath it.
