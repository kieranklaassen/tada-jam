## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child asks questions to find out what weather forecasts are for: helping people get ready for severe weather and act when it comes. The clarification puts the emphasis on the kinds of severe weather that happen where the child lives.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child listens to a forecast with an adult and asks questions: "How do they know a storm is coming?", "What do we do when it says strong wind?". The child helps pack what the family would need, such as a flashlight and water, and says which forecast would make them use it.

### Limits

The expectation states no assessment boundary. The clarification points to local kinds of severe weather. The expectation is marked as integrating engineering practices. The child's part is asking questions to get information; making a forecast is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Believing the forecast causes or controls the weather. Treating a forecast as certain. Asking only about the storm itself and never about what the forecast is for.
