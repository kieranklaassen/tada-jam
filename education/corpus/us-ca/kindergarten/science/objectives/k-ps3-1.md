## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child observes what sunlight does to the surface of the Earth. The clarification's examples of that surface are water, rocks, soil and sand.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child puts a hand on sand, a rock and a tray of water that have been in the sun, then on the same things kept in the shade, and says which feels warmer. The child comes back later in the day and feels them again.

### Limits

The assessment boundary keeps temperature to relative terms, and gives warmer and cooler as the kind of comparison meant. The surfaces in the clarification are examples.

### Common mistakes

Thinking the sun gives only light and does not warm things. Believing a rock is warm because of what it is made of, not because the sun shone on it. Comparing a sunny spot and a shaded spot at different times of day.
