## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The expectation is set for the close of grade 8: by then the child reads history and social studies texts on their own, with understanding and skill, at the level of complexity set for grades 6 to 8.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes a chapter of a history book for the grade, or a document from the period, and reads it alone, without an adult explaining it first. Afterwards the child retells the main points, answers questions about it, and points to the lines that support an answer.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6. Its target is to be reached when grade 8 finishes, so a grade 6 child is at the start of the band. The texts are history and social studies texts in the complexity band for grades 6 to 8. Reading is independent; no support is mentioned. The statement does not define the band with a number or a reading scale.

### Common mistakes

Children read every word of a dense history text accurately and still cannot say what it was about. They skip dates, captions and maps that give part of the meaning. They do not notice when they have stopped understanding, and keep reading instead of going back.
