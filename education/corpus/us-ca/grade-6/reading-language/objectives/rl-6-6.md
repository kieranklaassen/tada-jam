## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student explains the ways in which a writer builds up the point of view that a text's narrator or speaker has.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student reads a story told by a ten-year-old narrator and lists what the narrator notices, misunderstands and leaves out. The student marks the words that only this narrator would use. The student rewrites one paragraph as another character would tell it and says what had to change.

### Limits

The statement is about the viewpoint of the narrator or speaker inside the text, not about the author's own opinion. It asks how the viewpoint is built, so naming first or third person is not enough. It lists no techniques.

### Common mistakes

Students take the narrator's voice for the author's. They answer "first person" and stop. They trust everything a narrator says and do not notice when the narrator knows less than the reader.
