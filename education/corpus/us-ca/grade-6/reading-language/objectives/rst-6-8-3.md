## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child carries out a procedure of several steps exactly as written. This applies when the child does a technical task, measures something, or runs an experiment.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a six-step instruction sheet for testing which material keeps water warm longest. The child does each step in order, measures 200 millilitres where the sheet says 200, waits the stated two minutes, and ticks each step off. When a reading looks wrong, the child goes back to the sheet to find the step that was missed.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. The procedure has several steps; no number of steps is set. Three settings are named: experiments, measuring, and technical tasks. The child follows a procedure; the statement does not ask the child to design one.

### Common mistakes

Children skim the whole procedure and then work from memory, so a step is skipped or done out of order. They treat amounts and times as rough guides. They do not read ahead, and reach a step that needed something prepared earlier.
