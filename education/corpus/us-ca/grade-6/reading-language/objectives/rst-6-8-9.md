## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child sets what they learned from reading a text beside what they learned about the same topic from multimedia sources, video, simulations or experiments, and says where the two are alike and where they differ.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child rolls a marble down ramps of different heights and records how far it travels, then reads a textbook page on the same subject. The child lists what both showed, what only the experiment showed (the measurements varied from trial to trial), and what only the page gave (the name of the effect and the rule).

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. One side is a text the child reads; the other is one of four named kinds of source: experiment, simulation, video, multimedia. Both are about one topic. The statement does not say which source to trust when they differ.

### Common mistakes

Children describe the experiment and the text one after the other and never compare them. When their own result differs from the text, they decide that one of the two is simply wrong and do not look for the reason. They remember the video and forget what the text said.
