## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When presenting, the student adds visual displays and multimedia elements, for example sound, music, pictures or graphics, in order to make the information clearer.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

For a talk on the water cycle, the student draws a labelled diagram, chooses one photo of a cloud, and records ten seconds of rain sound. The student shows the diagram at the moment of explaining evaporation and points to the part being described. The student removes a decorative slide that explains nothing.

### Limits

Graphics, images, music and sound are named as examples of multimedia parts; visual displays are named beside them. Their stated purpose is to make information clear. The statement names no tools or software and sets no number of items.

### Common mistakes

Students add pictures, music or effects as decoration that explain nothing. They put full paragraphs on a slide and read them out. They show a chart and never refer to it.
