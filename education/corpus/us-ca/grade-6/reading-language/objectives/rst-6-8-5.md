## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child examines how an author has organised a text. This includes what each main section adds to the text as a whole and to understanding its topic.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes a lab report with its headings (question, materials, method, results, conclusion) and says what each section is for. The child covers one section and explains what a reader could no longer understand without it. The child puts the cut-up sections of an article back in order and gives a reason for the order.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. The focus is the major sections and what they add to the whole text and to understanding the topic. No particular structure is named.

### Common mistakes

Children list the headings and do not say what each section is for. They read the sections as separate pieces and miss that the results answer the question asked at the start. They skip the method section as unimportant.
