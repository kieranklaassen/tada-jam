## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student compares how two authors present events, saying what is alike and what differs. The example given is a biography about a person and a memoir by that same person.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student reads a chapter of an athlete's memoir and a passage of a biography about the same match. The student fills a two-column chart: what each writer includes, what each leaves out, and how each describes the deciding moment. The student writes a paragraph on why a person writing about themselves and an outsider would tell it differently, using lines from both.

### Limits

Two authors and their presentations of events are required. The memoir and biography pair is an example, not the only pairing. The statement is about how events are presented, which includes what is selected and stressed. The comparison statement in the literature strand, RL.6.9, is about forms and genres.

### Common mistakes

Students list facts from each text without comparing them. They note only differences or only similarities. They assume the first-person account is the true one and do not consider that each writer selects.
