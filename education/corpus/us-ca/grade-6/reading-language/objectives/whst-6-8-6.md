## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child writes and publishes with the help of technology, the Internet included. The child also uses it to show clearly and efficiently how ideas and information are connected.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child types a science report in a word processor, adds a chart made from the results, and links a statement to the web page it came from. The child arranges headings and a diagram so that a reader sees which evidence belongs to which idea. The child posts the finished piece to the class page.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. The Internet is named as one technology among others; no tool or program is named. No adult support is mentioned. The statement does not mention keyboarding or working with other people.

### Common mistakes

Children spend the time on fonts and decoration and not on the writing. They paste in a picture or link that does not connect to any point in the text. They treat publishing as the final click and do not check how the piece looks to a reader.
