## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When the child analyses a source in history or social studies, whether a primary source or a secondary one, the child points to particular places in the text that back up what they say about it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a letter written by a gold-rush miner and a textbook paragraph about the same year. Asked what life in the camp was like, the child underlines the two sentences in the letter that show it and reads them out when giving the answer. In a written answer the child names the document and quotes or retells the exact line.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. Both primary and secondary sources are covered. No number of pieces of evidence and no citation format is set.

### Common mistakes

Children answer from what they already know about the period instead of from the document. They quote a line that is on the topic but does not back the point they are making. They write "the text says" without showing where.
