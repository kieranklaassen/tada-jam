## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When analysing a science text or a technical text, the child backs up what they say by pointing to particular places in that text.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads an article on why bridges need expansion joints. Asked what happens to steel on a hot day, the child finds the sentence that says it and reads it out, or copies it with the paragraph number. In a lab write-up the child names the page of the manual that a statement comes from.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. The texts are science texts and technical texts. No number of pieces of evidence and no citation format is set.

### Common mistakes

Children answer from what they remember of the topic and do not go back to the text. They point to a whole page or to "the article" instead of the sentence or figure that holds the evidence. They cite a line that mentions the topic but does not support the point.
