## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When the child analyses, reflects or researches, the support comes from evidence the child takes out of informational texts.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes a paragraph explaining why a volcano erupted, and for each point goes back to the article and the data table to find the sentence or figure that backs it. In a reflection after a history unit, the child quotes a line from a source read in class to show what changed their thinking.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. The texts are informational texts; literary texts are not mentioned. Three uses of the evidence are named: analysis, reflection, research. No number of texts or pieces of evidence is set.

### Common mistakes

Children write what they think and add no evidence from the texts. They add a quotation and do not say what it shows. They use evidence from memory of a lesson and cannot say which text it came from.
