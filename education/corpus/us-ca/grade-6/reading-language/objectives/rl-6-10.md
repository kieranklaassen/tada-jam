## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

When the school year ends, the student reads literature (poems, plays and stories among it) at the complexity level set for grades 6 to 8 and understands it well. For texts at the upper end of that range the student may have support where needed.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student reads a whole novel over several weeks, keeps track of characters and events between sittings, and can say what a chapter added. The student reads a poem twice, once for the sense and once for how it is made, and reads a scene of a play aloud with classmates taking parts. For a harder text, the student works with a glossary, a teacher's questions, or a partner.

### Limits

The target is set for the end of the year. Stories, dramas and poems are named as included kinds of literature. The complexity range is the band for grades 6 to 8, and support is allowed for texts at the top of it. No titles, lengths or reading levels are named.

### Common mistakes

Students follow the plot and miss what is implied. They stop reading poetry and drama because the layout is unfamiliar. They read aloud accurately and cannot say what happened.
