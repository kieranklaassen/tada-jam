## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student changes the way they speak to fit different tasks and situations, and shows control of formal English when it is asked for or suits the situation. The statement points to Language standards 1 and 3 of this grade for the details.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student explains a science result to a friend at the table in everyday words, then explains the same result to the class in full sentences without slang. In a practice interview with an adult, the student greets the person formally and avoids "like" and "you know". The student says which of the two ways of speaking fits a spoken message to the principal.

### Limits

Formal English is required when it is asked for or fits the setting, not at all times. The statement refers to L.6.1 and L.6.3 for the specific expectations. It names no particular contexts or tasks.

### Common mistakes

Students speak to every audience as they do to friends. Others take formal to mean long words and stiff sentences and become hard to follow. They change vocabulary but keep informal grammar or fillers.
