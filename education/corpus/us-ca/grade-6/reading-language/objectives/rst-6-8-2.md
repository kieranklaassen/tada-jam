## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works out a text's main ideas or its conclusions. The child then gives a correct summary of it that leaves out their own opinions and anything they knew before reading.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a report of an experiment on plant growth and says in one sentence what the experimenters concluded. The child writes a short summary and checks each sentence against the report, striking out a fact that came from a lesson last year and not from the report.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. It sits in the strand for reading science and technical texts; its own wording does not name a kind of text. The summary holds only what the text says. No length is set.

### Common mistakes

Children give the topic ("it is about plants") in place of the conclusion. They mix in what they already believe about the subject. They copy the first and last sentences instead of summarising.
