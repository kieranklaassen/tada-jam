## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works out what a word or phrase means in the place where a text uses it. This includes the special vocabulary of history and social studies subjects.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child meets "cabinet" in a passage about a president and rereads the sentences around it to decide that it means a group of advisers, not a cupboard. The child says the meaning in their own words and tries it in the sentence. For a term such as "tariff" the child checks the glossary and then explains the sentence again.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. The meaning is the one the word has in the text at hand. The vocabulary of history and social studies is included by name. The statement gives no word list and names no strategy for working out a meaning.

### Common mistakes

Children take the everyday meaning of a word that has a special meaning in history ("party", "house", "bill"). They copy a glossary definition without checking that it fits the sentence. They skip an unknown term and lose the meaning of the paragraph.
