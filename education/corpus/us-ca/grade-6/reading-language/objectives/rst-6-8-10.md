## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The expectation is set for the close of grade 8: by then the child reads science and technical texts on their own, with understanding and skill, at the level of complexity set for grades 6 to 8.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes a science chapter for the grade, a set of technical instructions or an article with many figures, and reads it alone. Afterwards the child explains the main idea, reads a value from its table or diagram, and carries out or retells the steps correctly.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6. Its target is to be reached when grade 8 finishes, so a grade 6 child is at the start of the band. The texts are science and technical texts in the complexity band for grades 6 to 8. Reading is independent; no support is mentioned. The statement does not define the band with a number or a reading scale.

### Common mistakes

Children read the running text and skip the diagrams, tables and formulas that give much of the content. They read a technical text once from start to finish, as they would a story, and do not go back to a step or a definition. They keep reading after an unknown term has made the passage unclear.
