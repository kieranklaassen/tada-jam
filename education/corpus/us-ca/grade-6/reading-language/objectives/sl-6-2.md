## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student makes sense of information that comes in varied media and formats, for example spoken, in numbers or in pictures, and explains what it adds to the text, topic or issue being studied.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student watches a two-minute video clip about volcanoes, studies a table of eruption dates, and listens to a classmate's short talk. After each, the student says in a sentence what it showed. The student then says which part of the class's topic each one helped explain and what would be missing without it.

### Limits

Visual, quantitative and oral presentation are named as examples of formats. The student both interprets the information and says what it contributes to what is being studied. The statement does not set how many sources or which media. A related statement in the reading strand is RI.6.7.

### Common mistakes

Students describe what they saw or heard and do not say what it means. They misread the axes or units of a chart. They cannot say how a video or chart connects to the topic being studied.
