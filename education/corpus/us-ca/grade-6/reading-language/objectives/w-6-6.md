## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student uses technology, the Internet included, to create writing and publish it, and to work and communicate with other people. The student keyboards well enough to type at least three pages in one sitting.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student types a report in a word processor, uses the spelling tool, and adds a heading and an image. The student shares the file with a partner, reads the partner's comments in the margin and answers them. The student posts the finished piece to a class page. In one class period the student types three pages of a draft without stopping to write by hand.

### Limits

The keyboarding amount is stated: three pages at the least, typed in one sitting. The statement does not say how long a sitting is, how fast the student types, or what counts as a page. The Internet is named; no other tool or program is. This statement mentions no guidance or support from others, which W.6.5 does.

### Common mistakes

Students type with two fingers while looking at the keys and tire before three pages. They accept every suggestion of the spelling tool without reading it. When working in a shared file, they overwrite a partner's text or leave comments unanswered.
