## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student writes clearly and coherently, and the way a piece is developed, organised and styled suits the audience, the purpose and the task. A note in brackets says that what is expected of each writing type at this grade is set out in Writing standards 1 to 3.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Given the same event, a class trip, the student writes a thank-you letter to the museum guide and a report for the school newsletter. For each, the student decides who will read it and what it is for, and changes the opening, the order of information and the word choice to fit. The student reads each draft and checks that every paragraph belongs and follows from the one before.

### Limits

The statement names three things to fit (task, purpose, audience) and three things that must fit them (development, organisation, style). A note leaves the detail for each writing type to W.6.1, W.6.2 and W.6.3. The statement sets no length.

### Common mistakes

Students write every piece in the same voice whatever the reader. They write as if the reader already knows what they know and leave out needed background. Their paragraphs each make sense alone but do not connect.
