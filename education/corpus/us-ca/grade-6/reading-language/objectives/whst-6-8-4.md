## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child's writing is clear, and its parts connect. How it is developed, how it is organised and what style it uses all fit the audience, the purpose and the task.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes one science finding twice: once as a lab report for the teacher and once as a short explanation for a younger class. For the report the child uses headings, exact terms and measurements; for the younger readers the child picks simpler words, a shorter length and an everyday example. The child says who each piece is for and what changed because of that.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. Three features of the writing (development, organisation, style) are to suit three things (task, purpose, audience). No text type and no length is named.

### Common mistakes

Children write every piece in the same style, whoever the reader is. They leave out what the reader does not already know, because they know it themselves. They put ideas down in the order they thought of them, so the reader cannot follow how the parts connect.
