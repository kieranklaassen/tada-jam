## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works out what symbols, important terms and other subject-specific words and phrases mean in the particular scientific or technical setting where they appear. The texts and topics are those of grades 6 to 8.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a circuit diagram and says what each symbol stands for: the battery, the switch, the lamp. The child meets "force" in a physics passage and explains what it means there, as distinct from "forcing someone". The child reads "H2O" and "°C" in a table and says each in words.

### Limits

The standard is for the band of grades 6 to 8 and is recorded here at grade 6; it sets no separate level for grade 6. Symbols are included alongside words and phrases. The meaning is the one used in the scientific or technical setting at hand. No list of terms or symbols is given.

### Common mistakes

Children bring the everyday meaning of a word into science ("work", "theory", "cell"). They guess a symbol from its look (reading the "m" for metres as minutes). They can recite a definition and still not use the term correctly in a new sentence.
