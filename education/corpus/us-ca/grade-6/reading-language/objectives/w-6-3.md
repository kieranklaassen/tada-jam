## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student writes narratives about events or experiences, real or made up, with effective technique, well-ordered sequences of events and descriptive details that matter to the story. The opening interests the reader and shows them where they are: it sets up a context, brings in a narrator, characters or both, and starts a chain of events that moves forward in a natural, logical way. The student develops characters, events and experiences with narrative techniques such as description, pacing and dialogue. Varied transition words, phrases and clauses show the order of events and mark a move to a different setting or time frame, while exact words and phrases, sensory language and relevant descriptive detail convey the events and experiences. The story's conclusion follows from what has been told.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student writes a story about getting lost at a fair. In the first paragraph the student shows who is telling it, where and when. The student writes a short exchange of dialogue in place of a sentence of telling, slows the moment of realising by describing sounds and smells, and covers the uneventful walk home in one line. Revising, the student adds "an hour later" and "back at the gate" where the time or place changes, and replaces an ending of "then I woke up" with one that follows from what happened.

### Limits

The narrative may be real or imagined. Dialogue, pacing and description are named with "such as", so they are examples of technique. The narrator and the characters are joined by "and/or", so a piece may introduce either or both. The statement sets no length and no genre.

### Common mistakes

Students start with "One day" and list events at the same speed, with no part slowed down or shortened. They tell feelings ("I was scared") and do not show them through action, speech or the senses. They change time or place with no signal, and they end abruptly or with a dream.
