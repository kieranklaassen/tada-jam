## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student collects information and brings it together to show that sensory receptors react to stimuli by signalling the brain, and that the signals lead either to behaviour straight away or to a stored memory.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student tests reaction time by catching a dropped ruler and tests touch with two pencil points on a fingertip and on a forearm. The student reads two short texts and a diagram about receptors in the skin and the eye, and writes one summary that traces the path: stimulus, receptor, message to the brain, then a response or a memory.

### Limits

The record prints no clarification statement. The assessment boundary leaves out how the signals are carried from the receptor to the brain.

### Common mistakes

Thinking the sense organ itself does the perceiving, with no part for the brain. Thinking every response is a conscious decision. Copying from one source and not combining what several sources say.
