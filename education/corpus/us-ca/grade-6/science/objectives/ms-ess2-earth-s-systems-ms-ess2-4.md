## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student builds a model that shows how water circulates through Earth's systems, with the sun's energy and gravity as the drivers. The clarification stresses how water changes state along the many paths of the water cycle, and says a model may be physical or conceptual.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student seals water in a clear container with a lamp over one end and ice on the lid, watches drops form under the lid and run down, and draws the container with arrows named evaporation, condensation, precipitation and runoff. The student writes beside each arrow what drives it, the sun's heat or gravity. On a second diagram the student traces several routes one drop could take: through a cloud, a snowfield, a river, the ground or a plant.

### Limits

The assessment boundary leaves out a numerical treatment of latent heat, that is, of the heat water takes up or gives off when it evaporates, condenses, melts or freezes.

### Common mistakes

Drawing the cycle as a single loop from ocean to cloud to rain and back, with no groundwater, ice or plants. Thinking water evaporates only when it boils. Leaving gravity out as a driver, or taking clouds to be water vapour when they are droplets and ice crystals.
