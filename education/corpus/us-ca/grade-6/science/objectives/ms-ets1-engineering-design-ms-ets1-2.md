## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student judges rival solutions to a design problem by a systematic procedure, to find out how well each one satisfies the problem's criteria and stays within its constraints.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student draws a table with the designs in the rows and the criteria and constraints in the columns, fills each cell from test results or from a scale agreed in advance, and compares the rows. The student explains the ranking by pointing to the table.

### Limits

The record prints no clarification statement and no assessment boundary. This engineering design expectation holds for the whole grade 6 to 8 band, not for grade 6 alone.

### Common mistakes

Choosing a favourite design, often one's own, and finding reasons for it afterwards. Judging on one criterion and ignoring a constraint such as cost. Testing the designs in different ways, so that the comparison is unfair.
