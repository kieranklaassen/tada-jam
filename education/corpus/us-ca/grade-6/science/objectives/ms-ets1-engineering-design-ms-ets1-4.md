## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student builds a model that yields data, so that a proposed object, tool or process can be tested and changed in repeated rounds until the best design is reached.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The student builds a small cardboard version of a proposed ramp, times a ball on it, changes one feature such as the slope, times the ball again, and enters every round in a table. The student continues until further changes no longer improve the result.

### Limits

The record prints no clarification statement and no assessment boundary. This engineering design expectation holds for the whole grade 6 to 8 band, not for grade 6 alone.

### Common mistakes

Changing several features between two tests, so that nobody can tell which change mattered. Treating the model as the finished product and decorating it, without taking data from it. Stopping at the first version that works.
