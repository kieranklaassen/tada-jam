## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The student studies test data to see how several design solutions are alike and how they differ, and picks out the strongest features of each. Those features can then be joined in a new solution that meets the criteria for success better.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After three teams have loaded their paper bridges with weights, the student sets out the load held, the mass and the span of each bridge in one table. The student notes that one bridge held most because of its folded beams and another was lightest because of its triangle pattern, sketches a new bridge that uses both features, builds it and tests it.

### Limits

The record prints no clarification statement and no assessment boundary. This engineering design expectation holds for the whole grade 6 to 8 band, not for grade 6 alone.

### Common mistakes

Naming one winner and discarding the other designs without taking any feature from them. Drawing a conclusion from a single trial. Combining features without testing whether they work together.
