## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child understands that to solve an equation or an inequality is to answer a question: out of a stated set of values, which ones make it true, if any do. The child tests a given number from such a set by substituting it and seeing whether the equation or inequality then holds.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child is given x + 7 = 12 and the number cards 3, 4, 5 and 6, places each card on the x in turn, and sorts the cards into "makes it true" and "makes it false". The child does the same with x > 4 and finds that more than one card goes on the true pile. With the cards 1, 2 and 3 and the same equation, the child reports that no card in the set works.

### Limits

The values come from a set that is stated, and the answer may be one value, several values or none. Substitution is the method named. The statement sets no number range. The cluster heading adds that the equations and inequalities of this group have one variable. Writing equations for problems and solving them is covered by 6.EE.7.

### Common mistakes

Reading the equals sign as an instruction to work out an answer, not as a statement that can be true or false. Stopping at the first value that works for an inequality and not testing the others. Believing that every equation must have exactly one solution in the set.
