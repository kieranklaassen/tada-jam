## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works with expressions made only of numbers in which a power appears, the exponent being a whole number. The child both writes such expressions and works out their value.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes 5 × 5 × 5 in the short form 5³ and says "five to the third power", then expands 2⁴ into 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 and multiplies step by step to reach 16. The child builds a square of 3 by 3 tiles and a cube of 3 by 3 by 3 blocks and connects them with 3² and 3³. In a line such as 4 + 3², the child works out the power first and then adds.

### Limits

Only exponents that are whole numbers are in scope. The statement gives no range for the base or for the size of the exponent. The expressions here hold numbers only; working out expressions that contain letters is covered by 6.EE.2.c. The cluster heading adds that this work takes the arithmetic the child already knows and carries it over to algebraic expressions.

### Common mistakes

Reading 2³ as 2 × 3 and answering 6. Swapping base and exponent, so 3² is worked out as 2 × 2 × 2. Adding before taking the power in 4 + 3², which gives 49.
