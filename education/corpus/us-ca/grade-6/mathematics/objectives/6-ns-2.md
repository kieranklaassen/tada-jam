## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child divides numbers that have several digits by the standard algorithm, and can do it fluently.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sets out 7,548 ÷ 12 in the long-division layout and works digit by digit: estimates how many 12s fit, multiplies, subtracts, brings down the next digit, and repeats. The child says the place value of each quotient digit as it is written. The child checks the result by multiplying the quotient by the divisor.

### Limits

The statement says only that the numbers have several digits: it sets no limit on the length of the divisor or of the number divided. The method is the standard algorithm and the level asked is fluency. The statement does not say how a remainder is written. Decimals are covered by the neighbouring statement 6.NS.3. The cluster heading adds no further limit: it groups fluent calculation with multi-digit numbers together with common factors and multiples.

### Common mistakes

Leaving out a zero in the quotient when a brought-down digit gives a number smaller than the divisor (37 written for 307). Putting the first quotient digit in the wrong place, which shifts every place value after it. Making a subtraction error inside a step, or leaving a remainder that is larger than the divisor.
