## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows ways of getting away when a weapon or another dangerous object is there.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old acts out what they would do at a friend's house when the friend takes out a parent's gun: stop, do not touch, say "I have to go", leave the room and the house, and tell an adult. For a case in the street the child shows moving away from the person and towards other people or an open shop, without arguing and without stopping to film. The child points out the exits of the room they are in.

### Limits

Standard 4 is interpersonal communication, and the child shows the strategies and does not only list them. The statement covers weapons and other dangerous things and names none. It does not say what the strategies are. A footnote cites the Education Code.

### Common mistakes

Children stay to watch out of curiosity, or because leaving feels rude to a friend. Many think they should take the weapon away or calm the person by talking. Some stand still and have no words ready for leaving.
