## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works on persuading other people well, so that they stay away from tobacco, alcohol and other drugs.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A twelve-year-old prepares a one-minute talk or a poster for younger pupils with one clear message, one fact from a trusted source and one reason that matters at that age. In a role-play the child speaks to a friend who is thinking of vaping: states a worry, gives a reason, listens to the answer, and offers something else to do together.

### Limits

Standard 8 is health promotion: the child speaks up for other people's health and not only their own. The statement does not say who the others are and names no form (speech, poster, conversation). It asks for practice, not for a measured result.

### Common mistakes

Children lecture or try to frighten, and the listener stops listening. Many state an opinion with no fact or reason behind it. Some make an exaggerated claim, and lose trust when the listener knows it is not true.
