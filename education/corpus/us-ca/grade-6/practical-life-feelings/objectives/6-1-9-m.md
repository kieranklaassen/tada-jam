## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child talks over what harm comes from acting violently.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old discusses in a group what follows from a fight or from weeks of bullying: injury, fear of coming to school, lost friendships, punishment, a record with the school or the police. The child lists harm to the person hurt, to the one who did it, to those who watched, and to the class. The child tells which harm lasts longest.

### Limits

The statement gives no list of effects and does not say who is harmed. The kinds of violent behaviour are named in 6.1.8.M.

### Common mistakes

Children think of bruises only and leave out fear, shame and loneliness. Many see harm only to the victim, not to the one who acted or to bystanders. Some believe that words leave no lasting harm.
