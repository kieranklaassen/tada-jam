## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells what using violence to settle problems can do to personal goals.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old writes down two goals, such as making the school team or getting into a certain school programme. The child follows a story in which a student settles an argument with fists and traces what comes after: suspension, a lost place on the team, lost trust of teachers and friends, a record. The child draws the two paths side by side and marks where the goal can no longer be reached.

### Limits

The statement does not say who uses the violence or whose goals are affected, and it names no goals. The wider harm of violent behaviour is 6.1.9.M.

### Common mistakes

Children think only of the immediate punishment and not of what it makes impossible later. Many believe violence that 'works' once does them no harm. Some do not yet have goals in words, so the link stays abstract.
