## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works out what a person's choice to drink alcohol, use tobacco or take other drugs would do to that person's ties with family members and friends.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A twelve-year-old draws themselves in the middle of a page with the people close to them around it: a parent, a grandparent, a best friend, a team. For a made-up choice to start vaping, the child writes on each line what would change, such as lost trust, lying, arguments, or new friends who also vape. The child does the same for the choice not to start.

### Limits

Standard 5 is decision making: the child thinks a choice through and is not asked to carry one out. Only the effect on family and friends is in scope here. Results for health, feelings, social life and the law are a neighbouring statement (6.5.3.A).

### Common mistakes

Children think a choice about their own body affects nobody else. Many expect friends to stay the same and do not see that use changes who they spend time with. Some think family will not find out, and leave out the lying this needs.
