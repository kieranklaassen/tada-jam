## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child puts into practice fitting ways of showing respect to people unlike themselves and of bringing those people in.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old invites a classmate who sits alone to join the lunch table or the game, and changes the game's rules so that a classmate with a cast or a wheelchair can play. The child asks a classmate how they want to be helped before helping. In group work the child makes sure everyone gets a turn to speak, including a classmate who is learning English.

### Limits

The statement asks for practice and names no ways and no particular differences. Explaining why empathy matters is 6.1.5.M, and encouraging a respectful school is 6.8.1.M.

### Common mistakes

Children include someone once, when the teacher asks, and not again. Many help without asking and take over what the other can do alone. Some think including means treating a classmate as a guest and not as an equal member.
