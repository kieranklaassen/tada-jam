## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows by words and actions how a person can make their respect for human diversity known to others.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old learns to say a classmate's name the way the classmate says it, asks instead of assuming ('What do you call that holiday?'), and listens without interrupting or mocking. The child rewrites a remark that stereotypes into one that does not. The child shows with posture and face that they are listening, and invites someone new into the group.

### Limits

The statement asks the child to show ways of communicating and names none. It does not define which differences are meant.

### Common mistakes

Children think respect means saying nothing about differences at all. Many ask curious questions in a way that singles someone out in front of others. Some believe 'I was only joking' undoes the hurt.
