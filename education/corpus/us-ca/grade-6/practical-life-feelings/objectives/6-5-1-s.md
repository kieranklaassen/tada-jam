## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child goes through the steps of making a decision to work out what is safe to do when a situation carries risk.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A sixth grader takes a case, such as friends wanting to swim in a canal or a lift home offered by a driver they do not know, and works through it on paper: what is the choice, what are the options, what could happen with each, which option is safest, what do I do. The child says the decision aloud and, afterwards, whether they would choose the same again.

### Limits

Standard 5 is decision making, and the statement asks for a process with steps, not a quick answer. It does not name the steps or say how many there are. The situations are not listed.

### Common mistakes

Children choose the first option at once and list consequences only afterwards, to justify it. Many see two options only, go along or refuse, and miss a third such as phoning home. Some give more weight to what friends will think than to the physical risk.
