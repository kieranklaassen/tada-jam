## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child draws up their own plan with two aims: keeping stress from arising, and coping with it once it is there.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old writes down what stresses them most, such as tests, a crowded week or quarrels at home. For each the child writes one thing to do beforehand (start homework earlier, keep one afternoon free, go to bed on time) and one thing to do when the stress comes (slow breathing, a walk, talking to someone). The child puts the plan where they will see it and sets a day to check whether it works.

### Limits

The plan has two parts, prevention and management, and the statement names no strategies or time span. Carrying the strategies out is 6.7.2.M.

### Common mistakes

Children plan only for the moment of stress and leave out prevention. Many write vague steps such as 'relax more' that cannot be checked. Some list things that avoid the cause without solving it, such as staying home on a test day.
