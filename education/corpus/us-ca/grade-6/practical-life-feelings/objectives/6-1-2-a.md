## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names positive things a person can do in place of drinking alcohol, using tobacco or taking other drugs.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A sixth grader writes a list of what they do to have fun, to calm down and to feel part of a group: a sport, music, drawing, time with a friend, a talk with a parent. Next to each reason a young person gives for trying a substance (boredom, stress, wanting to belong) the child puts one activity from the list.

### Limits

Standard 1 is essential concepts: the child names the options here, and doing them is a separate statement (6.7.1.A). The statement gives no examples and no number of options.

### Common mistakes

Children list activities without linking them to a reason for use, so the list does not answer stress or pressure from friends. Some name only refusing, which is a different skill. Some name another risky activity as the replacement.
