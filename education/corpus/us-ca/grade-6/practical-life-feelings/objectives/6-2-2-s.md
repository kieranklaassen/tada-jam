## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works out what leads people to act safely and what leads them to act with violence.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A twelve-year-old takes a made-up pupil who hits someone at break and lists what may have contributed: friends who cheer fights, violent videos, anger the pupil has not learned to manage, what the pupil has seen at home, no adult nearby. For a pupil who walks away from a fight the child makes the same kind of list: calm friends, a trusted coach, clear rules, practice at cooling down. The child marks which items come from inside the person and which from outside.

### Limits

Standard 2 is analyzing influences. The statement asks about two kinds of conduct, the safe kind and the violent kind. It names no influence and does not say whose conduct is meant.

### Common mistakes

Children explain violence by one cause, most often "he is a bad person". Many look only for what pushes towards violence and leave out what supports safe conduct. Some believe that media and friends affect others and not themselves.
