## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child looks closely at the steps for staying safe on public transport and when riding in vehicles.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A sixth grader goes through a bus trip step by step: wait back from the kerb, let the bus stop fully, sit or hold on, keep bags out of the aisle, know the stop, and cross only where the driver can see them. For a car the child lists: seat belt on before the car moves, sit in the back, do not distract the driver, get out on the kerb side. The child gives the reason for each step.

### Limits

Standard 1 is essential concepts: the child studies the procedures and is not asked to show them. Two settings are named. The statement lists no procedure and does not mention walking or cycling.

### Common mistakes

Children think a short trip needs no seat belt. Many put the shoulder strap behind their back or under their arm. Some know the rules for a car and none for waiting at a stop or at a platform edge.
