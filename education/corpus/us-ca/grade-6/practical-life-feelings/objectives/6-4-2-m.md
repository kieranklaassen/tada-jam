## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells the ways in which violence can come from bias, prejudice and discrimination.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old follows a case step by step: a group is spoken of as 'them', jokes and name-calling follow, someone is left out or treated unfairly, and then someone is pushed or beaten. The child marks the point where a classmate could have spoken up. The child gives the difference between the three words with one example of each.

### Limits

The statement says these three may end in violence, not that they always do. It asks for description; showing respect for diversity is 6.4.3.M.

### Common mistakes

Children use the three words as if they meant the same. Many think prejudice is only held by bad people and do not see it in jokes they laugh at. Some see no connection between words and later physical harm.
