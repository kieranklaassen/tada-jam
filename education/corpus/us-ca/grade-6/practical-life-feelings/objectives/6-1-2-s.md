## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells what to do in an emergency and how to give simple first aid. This includes what to do when a tooth is knocked out or hurt in an accident.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A twelve-year-old says the steps in order: check that the place is safe, call an adult or the emergency number, say where you are and what happened. For a cut the child says to press with a clean cloth; for a burn, cool running water; for a nosebleed, lean forward and pinch the nose. For a knocked-out adult tooth the child says to hold it by the crown, keep it wet in milk or put it back in its socket, and get to a dentist at once.

### Limits

Standard 1 is essential concepts: the child describes the steps here, and carrying them out is a separate statement (6.7.4.S). The word "basic" is the only limit on scope, and teeth are the one case the statement names. It lists no other injury and no procedure.

### Common mistakes

Children want to treat first and forget to check for danger and call for help. Many would wrap a knocked-out tooth in dry tissue or scrub it, which damages it. Some think a nosebleed needs the head tipped back.
