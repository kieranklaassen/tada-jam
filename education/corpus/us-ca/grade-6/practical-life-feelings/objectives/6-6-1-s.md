## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child draws up their own plan for staying safe and not getting hurt.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A twelve-year-old writes a one-page plan about their own week: the trip to school, the sport they play, time at home alone, time online. For each the child writes the main risk, one thing they will do (helmet on every ride, mouth guard at training, phone away when crossing), and who they will tell or call when something goes wrong. The child shows the plan to a parent and changes it after a month.

### Limits

Standard 6 is goal setting: the child writes a plan of their own and is not asked to prove it was followed. The statement names no risks, no time span and no form for the plan.

### Common mistakes

Children write general promises ("I will be careful") with no action attached. Many copy rules that do not match their own week. Some plan only for rare events and leave out daily risks such as traffic and sport.
