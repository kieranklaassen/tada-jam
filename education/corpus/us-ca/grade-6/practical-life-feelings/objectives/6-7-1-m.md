## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child does, in a fitting way, what they are responsible for toward themselves and toward other people.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old keeps their own duties without being reminded: homework handed in, belongings looked after, enough sleep before a school day. In a group the child does their share of a project, keeps a promise to a friend, tells the truth about a mistake and helps a new classmate find the way. The child says which of their tasks are for themselves and which are for others.

### Limits

The statement names two kinds of responsibility and does not list them. It does not say what counts as appropriate.

### Common mistakes

Children do a duty only when an adult watches. Many see responsibility as chores and leave out what they owe to friends and classmates. Some take over a group task entirely and count that as being responsible.
