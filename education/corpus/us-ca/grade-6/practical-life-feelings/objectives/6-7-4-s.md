## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises simple first aid and the steps to take in an emergency.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

On a classmate who plays the casualty, an eleven-year-old presses a pad on a pretend cut and raises the arm, cools a pretend burn under running water, and turns a person who is breathing but not responding onto their side. The child makes a pretend emergency call to a teacher who plays the operator: name, place, what happened, how many are hurt, and stays on the line. The child repeats each drill until the order is right without prompting.

### Limits

Standard 7 is practicing health-enhancing behaviors: the child carries the steps out, in a drill or for real, where the matching concept statement (6.1.2.S) asks for a description. The word "basic" is the only limit on scope. No injury and no technique is named.

### Common mistakes

Children go straight to the injured person without checking that the place is safe. On the phone many forget to say where they are, or hang up early. Some remember the actions and do them in the wrong order.
