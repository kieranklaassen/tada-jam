## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works out in which situations with tobacco, alcohol or other drugs a grown-up ought to be asked for help.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A sixth grader reads short cases: a friend who passed out after drinking, an older pupil selling pills at school, a parent who drinks and then drives, a classmate who vapes every day, a bottle of unknown tablets found in a park. For each the child says whether an adult is needed, which adult, and how fast. The child says the first sentence they would say to that adult.

### Limits

Standard 5 is decision making: the child judges cases and is not asked to handle them. The statement gives no list of situations and does not say which adult.

### Common mistakes

Children think telling an adult is betraying a friend. Many try to care for a drunk or drugged friend themselves and wait too long. Some believe they will be punished for having been there and stay silent.
