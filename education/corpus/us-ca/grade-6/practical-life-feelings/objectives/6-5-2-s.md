## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child goes through the steps of making a decision to choose fitting ways of answering harassment and bullying.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old takes three cases: being called names every day, seeing a classmate removed from a group chat, and being touched in a way they do not want. For each the child lists options (tell the person to stop, walk away, stay with friends, save the messages, report to an adult), says what each would probably lead to, and picks one or two. The child says why the choice differs between the cases.

### Limits

Standard 5 is decision making: the child chooses a response through steps and is not handed one. Both bullying and harassment are covered, and the statement does not say whether the child is the target or a witness. It names no steps and no strategy.

### Common mistakes

Children pick one response for every case, most often "ignore it" or "hit back". Many see reporting as weakness and leave it off the list. Some see no part for themselves when they are a witness.
