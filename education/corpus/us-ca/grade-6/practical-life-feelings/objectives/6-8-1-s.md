## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child backs efforts to keep people from getting hurt in three places: the community, the home and the school.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A sixth grader reports a broken step or a loose railing to the school office, helps a younger pupil fasten a helmet, and makes a poster about crossing at the lights. At home the child checks the smoke-alarm battery with a parent and clears toys off the stairs. In the neighbourhood the child joins a clean-up or tells an adult about a broken swing in the park.

### Limits

Standard 8 is health promotion: the child acts for the safety of others and not only their own. Three settings are named. The statement does not say what form the support takes.

### Common mistakes

Children think preventing injuries is the job of adults and see no part for themselves. Many notice a hazard and assume someone else has reported it. Some give orders to other children, who then ignore them.
