## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child looks closely at plans that prepare a school and a household for a disaster.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old reads the school's earthquake and fire plan and walks the route from the classroom to the meeting point. At home the child asks where the family would meet, who to phone, where the water, torch and first-aid kit are, and what to do if the adults are not there. The child notes anything that is missing or out of date.

### Limits

Standard 1 is essential concepts: the child studies plans and is not asked to write one. Two places are named, home and school. No kind of disaster is named.

### Common mistakes

Children know the school drill by habit and cannot say what the plan is at home. Many think a plan is only about the moment of the event and leave out the meeting point and how to reach family afterwards. Some assume an adult will always be present to tell them what to do.
