## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises doing positive things in place of getting involved with a gang.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A twelve-year-old joins something that gives what a gang promises: a team, a youth club, a band, a faith group, a volunteer project. The child goes every week, gets to know an adult there, and brings a friend. The child spends free afternoons there, at a library or at a relative's house.

### Limits

Standard 7 is practicing health-enhancing behaviors, so this asks for taking part and not for a list. The statement names no activity. Saying no to an approach is a neighbouring statement (6.4.4.S).

### Common mistakes

Children name an activity and do not go, or stop after a few weeks. Many choose something that fills time and gives no group to belong to and no adult to ask for help. Some think this applies only to children who live where gangs are visible.
