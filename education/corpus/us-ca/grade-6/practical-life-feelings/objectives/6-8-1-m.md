## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child works to bring about a school where the ways people differ from one another are met with respect.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old proposes a class agreement about how people speak to each other and collects signatures. The child makes a display of the languages spoken in the class, thanks a classmate aloud for defending someone, and tells a teacher about a corner of the playground where children are mocked. The child speaks at a school meeting about what would make new students feel welcome.

### Limits

The statement concerns the school, not the wider community. It does not say what form the encouragement takes or which differences are meant.

### Common mistakes

Children think a respectful school is the adults' job. Many make a poster and do not change how they themselves speak. Some want respect for the differences of their own group only.
