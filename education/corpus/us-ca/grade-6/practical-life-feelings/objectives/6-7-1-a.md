## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises doing positive things in place of drinking alcohol, using tobacco or taking other drugs.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A sixth grader picks two activities that meet a real need, such as a run after a bad day or a club that gives a group to belong to, and does them for two weeks. The child keeps a short log of what they did and how they felt afterwards. When bored or stressed, the child goes to the list first.

### Limits

Standard 7 is practicing health-enhancing behaviors, so this asks for practising the activities and not for naming them. Naming such activities is a separate statement (6.1.2.A). No activity is named and no length of time is set.

### Common mistakes

Children treat this as a list to write and do not do the activities. Many choose an activity an adult suggested and drop it after a few days. Some choose only activities done alone, which do not answer the wish to belong to a group.
