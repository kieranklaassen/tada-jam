## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child describes the dangers that being involved with a gang brings.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A sixth grader lists what a gang member can lose or suffer: injury or death from violence, arrest and a criminal record, being made to commit crimes, leaving school, danger to family, and difficulty leaving. The child sets this list next to what a gang promises (protection, belonging, money) and says, for each promise, what happens in fact.

### Limits

Standard 1 is essential concepts: the child describes and is not asked to act. The statement lists none of the dangers. It does not define a gang or say what counts as being involved.

### Common mistakes

Children think a gang protects its members, when membership raises the chance of being hurt. Many believe a person can leave whenever they want. Some see the danger only for the member and leave out brothers, sisters and parents.
