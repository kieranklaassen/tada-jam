## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises ways of coping with stress.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old tries several methods on real occasions: slow breathing before a test, a run after a hard day, writing worries down, splitting a big assignment into parts, talking with someone. The child notes how tense they felt before and after on a scale of one to five. After two weeks the child says which methods help them and which do not.

### Limits

The statement names no strategies and no number of them. It asks for doing them; planning them is 6.6.1.M.

### Common mistakes

Children try a method once, feel no change and drop it. Many count distraction with a screen as dealing with stress though the cause stays. Some use a method only after the stress is already very high.
