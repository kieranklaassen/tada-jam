## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises going to adults they trust and requesting help with a problem of mental, emotional or social health.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old role-plays the request with a partner who plays a teacher, a parent or a counsellor: 'Can I talk to you? I haven't been sleeping and I feel sad most days.' The child chooses a time and place, says plainly what is wrong and what they need, and tries a second adult when the first one is busy or does not take it seriously. The child also practises asking on behalf of a friend.

### Limits

The statement asks for practice: a rehearsal counts, and so does asking for real. It names trusted adults as the people to go to and three kinds of health problem. Naming the sources of help is 6.3.1.M.

### Common mistakes

Children hint and hope the adult guesses, instead of saying what is wrong. Many give up after one adult does not respond. Some start with 'it's nothing, but' and make the problem sound smaller than it is.
