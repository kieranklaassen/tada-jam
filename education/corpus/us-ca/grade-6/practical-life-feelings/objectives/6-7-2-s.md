## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises using technology safely.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A sixth grader sets a strong password and keeps it from friends, sets accounts to private, and checks with an adult before installing an app or meeting anyone known only online. Before posting, the child asks whether the post gives away an address, a school or a location. The child puts the phone away when walking or cycling, and shows an adult any message that frightens them.

### Limits

Standard 7 is practicing health-enhancing behaviors: the child does these things and does not only talk about them; talking over what can go wrong online is a separate statement (6.1.7.S). The word "technology" is not narrowed to the Internet. No device and no rule is named.

### Common mistakes

Children know the rules and break them when a friend asks for a password or a photo. Many think a private account or a disappearing message cannot be copied. Some think of safety only as strangers and leave out screens in traffic.
