## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child gives their own promise to keep away from people, from places and from things to do that lead a person toward violence or delinquency.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old names, for themselves, the situations to stay out of: a group that plans a fight after school, a spot where older youths drink and damage things, a dare to shoplift. The child writes the promise in their own sentence, signs it, and adds what they will do instead and what they will say in order to leave. The child tells one adult about it.

### Limits

The statement asks for a commitment, which is a stated intention, and names three things to avoid without examples. It does not define delinquency.

### Common mistakes

Children write the promise in the teacher's words and do not connect it to people and places they know. Many believe they can go along and stay uninvolved. Some have no sentence ready for leaving and so stay.
