## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child goes through the steps of making a decision and uses them to arrive at a choice that improves health.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old takes a choice from their own life, such as whether to stay in a group chat that leaves them upset or what to do about too many activities, and writes the steps: state the decision, list options, think through what each would lead to for themselves and others, choose, act, look back. The child says the steps aloud for a second case with a partner. The child tells which consequence settled the choice.

### Limits

The statement does not spell out the steps or name the health decision; it stands under mental, emotional and social health. It sets no number of options.

### Common mistakes

Children decide first and fill in the steps afterwards. Many think only about what happens today. Some leave out the last step of looking back at how it went.
