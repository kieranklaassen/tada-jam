## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows that they can go to a grown-up they trust and ask for help when they feel in danger or under threat, online included.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a role-play a twelve-year-old picks an adult (a parent, a teacher, a counsellor), goes to them and says plainly what happened: "Someone online keeps asking me for photos and I am scared. I need help." The child shows the adult the messages without deleting them. If the first adult is busy or does not act, the child goes to a second adult.

### Limits

Standard 3 is accessing valid information; here the source of help is a person, and the child shows the asking and does not only describe it. The statement speaks of feeling threatened or unsafe, and names being online as one setting. It does not say who counts as a trusted adult.

### Common mistakes

Children wait because they fear losing their phone or being blamed. Many only hint ("something weird happened") and stop when the adult does not ask further. Some delete the messages first, out of shame, and the evidence is lost.
