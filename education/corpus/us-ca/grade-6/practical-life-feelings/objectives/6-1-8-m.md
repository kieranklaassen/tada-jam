## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells what different kinds of violent behaviour have in common; the examples given are fighting, verbal abuse, hazing and bullying.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old takes four short cases, one of a fight, one of name-calling, one of a team's initiation ritual and one of a classmate tormented week after week, and lists what is the same in all: someone is hurt, power is used against a person, it is done on purpose, and bystanders are affected. The child draws overlapping circles and writes the shared features in the middle. The child says why words can count as violence.

### Limits

The four behaviours in the statement are examples, not a complete list. It asks for what they share; their harm is 6.1.9.M.

### Common mistakes

Children count only hitting as violence. Many see hazing or teasing as a joke when the group laughs. Some describe how the behaviours differ and never reach what they share.
