## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

For stress, for loss and for depression, the child tells how each shows itself, what brings it on, and what it does to a person's health.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old fills in a chart with three columns for each of the three: how you can tell (headaches, trouble sleeping, crying, losing interest), what can bring it on (tests, a move, a death), and what it does to body and mind over time. The child explains the chart to a partner, using a character from a story.

### Limits

The statement names three subjects and three things to describe for each. It sets no depth for depression and asks for description, not diagnosis. When to seek help is 6.5.2.M.

### Common mistakes

Children use 'depressed' for any sad day. Many think stress is only in the mind and do not link it to stomach aches or poor sleep. Some believe loss means only death and leave out a divorce, a move or a lost friendship.
