## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child explains the part that the media and culture play in whether people drink alcohol or take other drugs.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

For one week an eleven-year-old notes where drinking appears: at a family celebration, in a song, in a film, on a sports shirt, in posts online. For each the child notes whether drinking is shown as normal, funny, grown-up or harmful. The child compares notes with a classmate whose family has different customs and says how each set of messages could shape what a young person expects.

### Limits

Standard 2 is analyzing influences. This statement names alcohol and then uses a general term for all other drugs. Tobacco is not named separately here, unlike in most statements of this content area, and the general term does not exclude it. It names no culture and no kind of media.

### Common mistakes

Children take what their own family or community does as what everyone does. Many think a film or a song only entertains and carries no message about drinking. Some hear "culture" as meaning only other countries.
