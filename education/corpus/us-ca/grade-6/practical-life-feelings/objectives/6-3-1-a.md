## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child names where trustworthy facts about tobacco, alcohol and other drugs, their use and their abuse, can be found.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A sixth grader gets six cards: a school nurse, a doctor, a government health website, a classmate's story, an advert, an anonymous video. The child sorts them into trusted and not trusted and gives a reason for each, such as who wrote it, whether they are trained and whether they are selling something. The child names one adult they would ask.

### Limits

Standard 3 is accessing valid information: the child names sources and is not asked to research a topic. The statement gives no list of sources and no test for validity.

### Common mistakes

Children trust a source because it is a friend or an older sibling. Many judge a website by how it looks and not by who made it. Some think one person's story is a fact that holds for everyone.
