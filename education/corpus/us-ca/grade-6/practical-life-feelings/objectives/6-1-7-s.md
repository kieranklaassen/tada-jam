## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child talks over what can go wrong for a person's safety when they are online.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group, an eleven-year-old takes part in a talk about cases: a stranger in a game chat who asks for a photo and an address, a cruel post shared about a classmate, a message with a link that asks for a password, a challenge video that could injure. The child says what the danger is in each, gives a case they have seen themselves, and listens to what others add.

### Limits

Standard 1 is essential concepts, and the verb is to discuss: the child talks about the dangers and is not asked to show safe habits here; that is a separate statement (6.7.2.S). No danger is listed, and no device or service is named.

### Common mistakes

Children think the only online danger is a stranger, and leave out people they know, scams and their own posts. Many believe a private message or a deleted post is gone permanently. Some trust an online friend because they have chatted for a long time.
