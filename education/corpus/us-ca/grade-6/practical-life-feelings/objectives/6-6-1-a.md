## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child sets goals of their own for staying away from drugs.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old writes one or two goals in their own words, such as "I will not vape in middle school, so that I keep up in swimming". Under each goal the child writes why it matters to them, what could get in the way, what they will do then, and who will help them. The child reads the goal to a classmate or a parent.

### Limits

Standard 6 is goal setting: the child writes goals and is not asked to prove they were kept. The statement lists no substances and sets no time span and no number of goals.

### Common mistakes

Children write a goal that is too general ("I will never do drugs") with no reason and no plan for a hard moment. Many copy a goal that sounds right and is not their own. Some leave out who can help.
