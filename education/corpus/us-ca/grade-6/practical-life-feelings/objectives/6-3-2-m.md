## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child talks about why turning to an adult they trust matters at times when help is called for.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old names three adults they would go to and says why they trust each. The child discusses cases in a group, such as a friend who talks about hurting themselves, weeks of feeling low, or a threat online, and says what could happen with and without an adult's help. The child explains why some problems cannot be solved by friends alone.

### Limits

The statement asks for discussion of why this matters and does not define the moments of need. It does not say who counts as a trusted adult.

### Common mistakes

Children think that at their age they must handle things alone, or that telling an adult betrays a friend. Many wait until a problem is very large. Some pick an adult by how nice they are, not by whether that adult can be relied on to act.
