## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child examines what affects how well a person is in mind, in feelings and with other people, looking both at what comes from within the person and at what comes from outside.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old maps what affected their mood and friendships in the past week: sleep, self-talk, hunger and temperament on the inside; family, friends, social media, school pressure and news on the outside. The child marks each as helping or harming and explains how it works, not only that it does. The child picks one outside influence and says how far they can change it.

### Limits

The statement asks for analysis, which is more than listing, and names no particular influences. It covers both those inside a person and those outside.

### Common mistakes

Children list influences without saying how they act. Many see friends and media as the only influences and overlook sleep, health and their own habits of thinking. Some treat every influence as something done to them that they cannot change.
