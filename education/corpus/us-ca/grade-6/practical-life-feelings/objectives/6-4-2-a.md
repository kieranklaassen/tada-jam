## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child shows how to say no, in words and in body language, when pushed to drink alcohol, use tobacco or take other drugs.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a role-play a partner holds out a pretend vape and says "everyone does it". An eleven-year-old stands straight, looks at the partner, says "No, I don't want it" in a firm voice, and shakes their head or steps back. When the partner asks a second and a third time, the child repeats the same words, suggests something else to do, and walks away.

### Limits

Standard 4 is interpersonal communication: the child shows the skill and does not only describe it. Both words and body language are asked for. The statement does not say who applies the pressure and lists no refusal techniques.

### Common mistakes

Children say no while smiling, looking down or laughing, and the body language contradicts the words. Many have one refusal ready and nothing for the second request. Some explain at length, which gives the other person something to argue with.
