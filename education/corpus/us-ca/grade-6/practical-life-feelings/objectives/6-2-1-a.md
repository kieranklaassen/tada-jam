## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells which things inside a person can push them towards tobacco, alcohol or other drugs, or keep them away.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A twelve-year-old reads a short story about a pupil offered a cigarette and underlines what is going on inside the pupil: curiosity, wanting to feel older, stress before a test, fear of being left out, or a firm belief that smoking is not for them. The child sorts these into what pushes towards use and what keeps the pupil from it, and adds one from their own experience.

### Limits

Standard 2 is analyzing influences. This statement is about influences from within the person only and gives no examples of them. Advertising, role models, culture and media are neighbouring statements (6.2.2.A, 6.2.4.A).

### Common mistakes

Children name outside pressures (friends, adverts) when asked for inside ones. Many see inner influences only as risks and leave out the ones that protect, such as their own values and goals. Some do not count a mood, such as boredom or sadness, as an influence.
