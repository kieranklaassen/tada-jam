## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child practises communicating well, so that dangerous situations do not arise or can be stayed out of.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In role-plays an eleven-year-old tries out sentences for real cases: telling a friend "I'm not climbing that fence, let's go round", asking a host "Will an adult be home?", phoning a parent with an agreed code word to be picked up, and telling a group "Stop, someone will get hurt". The child uses a clear voice, looks at the person, and checks that the other person understood.

### Limits

Standard 4 is interpersonal communication: the child does the communicating and does not only describe it. The statement does not name the risks or the people communicated with. Gangs and weapons have their own statements (6.4.4.S, 6.4.2.S, 6.4.3.S).

### Common mistakes

Children speak up too late, once the situation has already started. Many soften the message until it is unclear ("I don't know, maybe not"). Some stay silent in a group because they think nobody else is worried.
