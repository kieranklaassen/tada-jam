## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child speaks and acts for a school and a community where nobody is bullied.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A twelve-year-old greets and sits with a pupil who is left out, says "That's not funny, stop" when a classmate is mocked, and does not forward a cruel post. With classmates the child writes a class agreement, makes posters for the corridor, or presents at an assembly on how to report bullying. At the sports club or the park the child does the same and tells an adult when the bullying continues.

### Limits

Standard 8 is health promotion: the child works for others and for the group. Two settings are named, the school and the community. The statement does not say how the child promotes this or where within the two settings.

### Common mistakes

Children think not bullying is enough and do nothing as bystanders. Many laugh along to stay safe themselves, which the bully takes as support. Some answer bullying by turning as a group against the bully.
