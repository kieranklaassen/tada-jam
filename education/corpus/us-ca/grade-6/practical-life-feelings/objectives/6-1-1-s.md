## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child explains ways of bringing down violence, harassment and conflict.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

For a case of two pupils arguing over a seat, an eleven-year-old explains what calms it: a pause to cool down, each saying what they want without insults, listening, a compromise, or a mediator. For a case of repeated name-calling the child explains other steps: telling the person to stop, staying with friends, reporting to an adult. The child says which step fits which case and why.

### Limits

Standard 1 is essential concepts: the child explains the ways and is not asked to use them here; settling a disagreement in practice is a separate statement (6.7.1.S). The statement names three problems and lists no method.

### Common mistakes

Children treat the three as one thing and offer "talk it out" for harassment, where the target should not have to negotiate. Many think ignoring always works. Some see telling an adult as tattling and leave it out.
