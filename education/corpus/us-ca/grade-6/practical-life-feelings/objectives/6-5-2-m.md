## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells in which circumstances a person ought to look for help with stress, with a loss or with depression.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An eleven-year-old describes, in their own words, times when a person should look for help with stress, with a loss or with depression. The child may start from short cases, which are the pack's examples and not the statement's: nerves before a test, sadness after a pet has died, a friend who no longer wants to do anything. For each time when help is needed the child says whom the person could turn to, and whom they themselves would go to.

### Limits

The statement covers three things, stress, loss and depression, and gives no list of situations or warning signs. The signs and effects themselves are 6.1.1.M.

### Common mistakes

Children think help is only for emergencies. Many judge by the cause ('it was only a pet') and not by how the person is doing.
