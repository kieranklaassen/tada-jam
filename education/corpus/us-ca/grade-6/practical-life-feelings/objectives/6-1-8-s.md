## Summary

_The pack's summary, not the official text._

The child tells what dangers ice, water and the sun bring.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A twelve-year-old makes three columns and fills them. Under sun: sunburn, heat exhaustion, skin damage over years. Under water: drowning, rip currents, cold shock, diving where it is shallow. Under ice: falling through a frozen pond, slipping on a frozen path. For each the child says one warning sign to look for.

### Limits

Standard 1 is essential concepts: the child describes the dangers and is not asked to act on them. Three sources are named. No particular danger and no safety rule is listed.

### Common mistakes

Children think sunburn happens only on hot, clear days. Many believe a strong swimmer cannot drown, or that ice that held yesterday holds today. Some know the danger and cannot name a warning sign.
