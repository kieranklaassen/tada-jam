## Official wording

verkennen en omgaan met afspraken en regels

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring and dealing with agreements and rules

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child asks "why can only four play here?" and helps to think of a rule for the new climbing frame. It tries what happens when it runs inside, hears the rule again and walks. A child of 4 learns the rules of the group by doing and by being reminded; a child of 6 can say what a rule is for and suggests a rule itself.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement holds two parts: exploring agreements and rules, and dealing with them. It does not say which agreements or rules. Keeping to them is `Samenwerken met andere kinderen / 7`; that different places need different rules is `Zichzelf staande houden of aanpassen in een groep / 3`.

### Common mistakes

A child takes a rule as valid only for the moment and for the adult who said it. A child sees a rule as something adults want, with no reason behind it. A child treats a rule as fixed for ever and gets upset when the group changes it.
