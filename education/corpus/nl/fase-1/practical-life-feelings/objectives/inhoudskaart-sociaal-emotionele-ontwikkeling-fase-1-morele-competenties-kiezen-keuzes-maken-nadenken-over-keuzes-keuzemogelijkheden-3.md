## Official wording

uitleggen wat het gevolg is van een bepaalde keuze

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

explaining what the consequence of a particular choice is

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "if I choose painting now, the sand table will be full later" or "if I eat my biscuit now, I have none at break". After its choice it tells what happened because of it. A child of 4 tells the consequence after it has happened; a child of 6 predicts it before choosing.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for explaining and speaks of the consequence of one particular choice. That a choice has consequences in the short and/or long term is `Nadenken over keuzes/keuzemogelijkheden / 5`.

### Common mistakes

A child names the consequence it hopes for and leaves out the unwanted one. A child sees the consequence for itself only. A child cannot yet link a consequence that comes later to the choice.
