## Official wording

herkennen aan gedrag van kinderen of zij boos, bang, blij, verdrietig zijn

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising from the behaviour of children whether they are angry, scared, happy, sad

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sees a classmate stamp and throw a block and says "she is angry"; it sees a child hide behind the teacher and says "he is scared". It watches children on the playground and tells who looks happy and why it thinks so. A child of 4 recognises it from strong behaviour such as crying, shouting and jumping; a child of 6 also from quieter behaviour such as withdrawing or going silent.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names four feelings: angry, scared, happy and sad. The child recognises them from behaviour, and the others are children. It does not ask for the cause of the feeling.

### Common mistakes

A child takes withdrawing or silence for "nothing is wrong". A child takes a scared child's hitting or shouting for anger. A child reads one sign, such as tears, and misses that tears also come with anger or laughing.
