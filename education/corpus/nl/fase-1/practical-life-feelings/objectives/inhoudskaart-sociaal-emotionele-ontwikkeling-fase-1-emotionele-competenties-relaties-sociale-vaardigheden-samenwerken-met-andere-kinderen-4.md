## Official wording

ideeën/oplossingen aandragen passend bij het samenwerken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting forward ideas/solutions that fit the working together

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While building a bridge with two others a child says "let's put the long block across" when the bridge keeps falling. When two of them want to pour the water, it proposes "you pour first, then me". A child of 4 offers an idea about its own part; a child of 6 offers an idea for the shared work and listens to whether the others agree.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The ideas or solutions fit the working together; the wording does not say whether they are about the task or about how the children work together. It does not ask that the idea is used.

### Common mistakes

A child offers an idea that has nothing to do with the shared task. A child repeats its idea louder when the others do not respond to it. A child has an idea and carries it out without saying it.
