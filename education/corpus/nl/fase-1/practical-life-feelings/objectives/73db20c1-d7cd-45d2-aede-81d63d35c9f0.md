## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child waits for a turn on the slide, moves over on the bench, lowers his or her voice next to a child who is reading, and shares the glue. The child asks "do you want this one?" before taking the last piece and walks around another child's building instead of through it. A child of 4 does this when reminded and with one other child at a time; a child of 7 does it more often without a reminder and can say what the other child wanted.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is five words long in Dutch and names no situations, no behaviour and no persons. "The other" (*de ander*) is singular and general; the text does not say whether a classmate, an adult or anyone is meant.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out that the other child wants something too: they see only their own plan. They also take it to mean giving in every time, or they take account of a friend and not of other children.
