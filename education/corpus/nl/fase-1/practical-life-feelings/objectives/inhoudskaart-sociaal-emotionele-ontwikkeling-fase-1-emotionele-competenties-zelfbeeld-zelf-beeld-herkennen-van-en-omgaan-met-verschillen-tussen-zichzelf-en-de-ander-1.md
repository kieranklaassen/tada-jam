## Official wording

beschrijven van eigen zelfbeeld

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing one's own self-image

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child draws itself and tells: "this is me, I have brown hair, I am five, I can ride a bike and I like dogs". It fills a box with things that belong to it and says why. A child of 4 describes how it looks and what it has; a child of 6 adds what it can do, what it likes and how it is, such as "I am shy".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for describing. It does not say what a self-image holds; `Herkennen van en omgaan met verschillen tussen zichzelf en de ander / 2` gives examples of characteristics.

### Common mistakes

A child describes only what can be seen or owned. A child describes itself as best at everything. A child repeats what adults say about it ("I am naughty") as its own picture of itself.
