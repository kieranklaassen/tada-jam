## Official wording

ontdekken dat er verschillen bestaan in relaties

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that there are differences in relationships

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child draws the people it knows and tells who is family, who is a friend, who is the teacher and who is a neighbour. It notices that it hugs its grandmother and not the caretaker, and that it plays with a friend every day and with a cousin only on visits. A child of 4 sorts people into family and not family; a child of 6 tells how it behaves differently with a friend, a teacher and a stranger.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discovering. The statement names no kinds of relationship and does not say in what the differences lie.

### Common mistakes

A child calls every child it has played with once a friend. A child thinks a relationship is the same from both sides ("she is my best friend, so I am hers"). A child treats an unknown adult as it treats a familiar one.
