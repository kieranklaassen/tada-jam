## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child wants to tell its story now and waits with it until the other child has finished. It is hungry and waits until the whole group has its fruit, or wants the bicycle and plays something else until one is free. A child of 4 waits a short time with something to do or an adult's word; a child of 6 waits longer and can tell itself "after this it is my turn".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no need and no length of time. Waiting for one's turn is `Beheersen van eigen gevoelens / 9`.

### Common mistakes

A child takes "not now" for "never" and protests. A child keeps asking while it waits. A child can wait when it sees when its turn comes and not when the wait has no visible end.
