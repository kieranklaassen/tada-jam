## Official wording

ervaren dat door tegenstrijdige belangen conflicten kunnen ontstaan die je samen kunt oplossen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing that conflicting interests can give rise to conflicts that you can solve together

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children both want the one red shovel in the sandpit. With the teacher beside them they say what each wants and agree that one digs first while the other fills the bucket, and then they swap. A child of 4 goes through this with an adult who leads each step; a child of 6 sometimes proposes a swap or a turn itself.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is experiencing: the child lives through it, and the statement does not ask the child to explain it. It holds two parts: conflicts can arise from interests that clash, and you can solve them together. It does not say whether an adult helps, and it names no way of solving.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the "together": they expect the adult to decide who is right, or think a conflict ends only when one child gives up what it wanted. Many do not yet see that the other child also wanted something.
