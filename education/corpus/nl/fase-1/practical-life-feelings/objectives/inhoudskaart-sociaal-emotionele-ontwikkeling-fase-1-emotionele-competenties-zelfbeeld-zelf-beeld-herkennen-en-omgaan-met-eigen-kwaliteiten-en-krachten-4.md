## Official wording

kritiek ervaren en ervaren dat verzoeken kunnen worden geweigerd

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing criticism and experiencing that requests can be refused

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child hears from another child "that is not how the tower goes" or from the teacher "you forgot the edges", and goes on working. It asks "can I join?" and hears "not now", then looks for another child or waits. A child of 4 cries or gets angry at a "no"; a child of 6 can hear it, ask "when then?" and find something else to do.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb experiencing is used twice: for criticism, and for the fact that requests can be refused. The statement does not say whose criticism or whose refusal, and does not ask the child to give criticism or to refuse.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the difference between the remark and the person: a child hears a comment on its work as "you do not like me". A child hears one refusal as a refusal for always.
