## Official wording

zichzelf durven blijven in een groep

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

daring to remain oneself in a group

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child goes on wearing its favourite hat when another child laughs at it. It says "I do not want to do that" when the group runs off to tease someone, and picks the activity it likes when its friends pick another. A child of 4 follows the group or the adult in most things; a child of 6 sometimes keeps to its own choice and says so.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement says "daring" and does not say in which situations. It names no group. Holding one's own within one's own group is `Rekening houden met zichzelf binnen een groep / 1`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out their own preference when a friend chooses otherwise: they copy the choice, the opinion or the play of the child they want to belong with. Some take remaining oneself for never doing what the group does.
