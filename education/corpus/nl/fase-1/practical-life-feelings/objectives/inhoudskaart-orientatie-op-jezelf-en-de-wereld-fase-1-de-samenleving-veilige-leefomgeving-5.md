## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle a child says which of two songs he or she wants and why, and hears classmates choose the other. The child holds the talking stick while speaking and passes it on, so that each child who wants to can speak. A child of 4 says what he or she likes or does not like; a child of 6 says "I think ..., because ..." and can repeat what a classmate thinks even when it is different.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card marks this as an aspect of citizenship (b). The verb is discussing. The statement puts the term in quotation marks and names the right only; it does not mention the limits of that right, the law or other rights. It sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

Children think that having said what they want means it will happen. They take a different opinion as a quarrel. They say what the child before them said.
