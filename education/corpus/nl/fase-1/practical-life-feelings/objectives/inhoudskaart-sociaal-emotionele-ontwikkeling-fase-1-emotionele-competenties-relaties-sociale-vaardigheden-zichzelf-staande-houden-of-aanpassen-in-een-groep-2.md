## Official wording

accepteren van hulp

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

accepting help

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child who cannot get its boot on lets another child hold the boot. It says "yes please" when the teacher offers to hold the paper while it cuts. A child of 4 accepts help from a familiar adult; a child of 6 accepts help from a classmate and says which part it wants to do itself.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about accepting help; asking for help is not mentioned. It does not say from whom the help comes. Reacting to others' request for help is `Leren omgaan met een ander / 5`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out saying how much help they want: a child says "I do it myself" and refuses all help, or hands the whole task over. Some accept help only from one adult.
