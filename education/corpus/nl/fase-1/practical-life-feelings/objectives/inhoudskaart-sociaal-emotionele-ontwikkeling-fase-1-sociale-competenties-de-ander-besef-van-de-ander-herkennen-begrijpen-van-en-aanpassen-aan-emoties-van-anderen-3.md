## Official wording

verplaatsen in de gevoelens van anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

imagining oneself in the feelings of others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sees a classmate whose mother has just left, and says "she misses her mama, I had that too". In a story it says how the bear feels when nobody comes to its party. A child of 4 shares the feeling when it knows the situation from itself; a child of 6 can also imagine a feeling in a situation in which it would feel differently itself.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement does not say which feelings or which others. It is about imagining the feeling; taking it into account in one's own behaviour is `Herkennen, begrijpen van en aanpassen aan emoties van anderen / 4`.

### Common mistakes

A child assumes that the other feels what it would feel itself. A child gets as upset as the other and cannot help. A child comforts with what comforts itself, such as its own cuddly toy.
