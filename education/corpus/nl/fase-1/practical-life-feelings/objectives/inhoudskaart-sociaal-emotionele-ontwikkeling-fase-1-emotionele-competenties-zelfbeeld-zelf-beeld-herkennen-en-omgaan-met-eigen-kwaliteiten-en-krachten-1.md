describing one's own needs

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "I am thirsty", "I need to go to the toilet", "I want to sit alone for a while" or "it is too loud for me". It points at a picture card of a need when it cannot find the word. A child of 4 states bodily needs and says "I want"; a child of 6 also describes a need for rest, help or company and says what would help.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for describing and is about the child's own needs. It names no kind of need.

### Common mistakes

A child shows the need in behaviour, such as whining, pushing or hiding, and does not say it. A child mixes up a wish ("I want that toy") with a need. A child says the need late, when it can no longer wait.
