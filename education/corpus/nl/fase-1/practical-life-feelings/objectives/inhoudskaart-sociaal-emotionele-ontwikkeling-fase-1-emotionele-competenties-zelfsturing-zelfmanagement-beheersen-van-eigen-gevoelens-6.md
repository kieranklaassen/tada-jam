## Official wording

gepast reageren op een onverwachte situatie

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reacting appropriately to an unexpected situation

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The bell for the fire drill rings and a child stops, looks at the teacher and walks to the line. A cup of water falls over and the child fetches a cloth. A child of 4 looks to the adult and copies what the adult does; a child of 6 acts itself in a situation it understands and asks for help in one it does not.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement says "appropriately" and does not say what is appropriate, nor which kind of situation. Not being disconcerted by unexpected things is `Beheersen van eigen gevoelens / 10`.

### Common mistakes

A child stands still or cries and does nothing. A child reacts with a lot of noise or laughter. A child goes on as if nothing has happened.
