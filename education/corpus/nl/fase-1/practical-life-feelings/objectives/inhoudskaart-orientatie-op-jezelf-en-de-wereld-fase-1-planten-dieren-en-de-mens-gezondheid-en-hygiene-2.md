## Official wording

verkennen van gewoontes op het gebied van voeding en hygiëne

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring habits in the area of food and hygiene

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At fruit time a child names what he or she brought, tastes a piece of a fruit that is new, and tells what the family eats in the evening. The child washes hands before eating and tells when that happens at home. With pictures the child puts the steps of brushing teeth or washing hands in order. A child of 4 tells and shows his or her own habit; a child of 6 compares it with a classmate's and can say why hands are washed before food.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is exploring habits, not following rules or knowing which food is healthy. Two areas are named: food and hygiene. The statement names no foods and no hygiene habits, and does not use the word healthy.

### Common mistakes

Children call food they do not know "dirty" or "yucky" without tasting it. They think hands are clean when they look clean. They think everyone eats the same things at the same times as their family.
