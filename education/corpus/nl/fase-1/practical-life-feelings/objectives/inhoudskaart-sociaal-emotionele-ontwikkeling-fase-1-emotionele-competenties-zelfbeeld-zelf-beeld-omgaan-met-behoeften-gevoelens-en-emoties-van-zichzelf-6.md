## Official wording

aangeven wat de aanleiding of oorzaak van de eigen gevoelens is

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

indicating what the trigger or cause of one's own feelings is

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "I am sad because mama left" or "I am angry because my tower was knocked over". It points to the picture of what happened before it started to cry. A child of 4 names the thing that just happened; a child of 6 also names a cause from earlier, such as "I slept badly" or "I was already angry this morning".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks the child to indicate the trigger or cause of its own feelings. It names both a trigger and a cause and does not say how they differ. It names no feelings.

### Common mistakes

A child names the last thing that happened where the cause lies earlier. A child names a person as the cause ("because of him") and not what happened. A child cannot find a cause and invents one when asked.
