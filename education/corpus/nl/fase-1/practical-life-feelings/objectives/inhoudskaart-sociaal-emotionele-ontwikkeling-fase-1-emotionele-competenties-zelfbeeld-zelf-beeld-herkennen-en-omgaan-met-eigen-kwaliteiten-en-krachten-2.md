## Official wording

omgaan met sterke en minder sterke kwaliteiten van zichzelf

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with one's own strong and less strong qualities

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child who draws well shows another child how to draw a horse, and asks for help with the zip it cannot close yet. It chooses a hard puzzle, says "I can't do this yet" and tries again. A child of 4 says it can do everything or stops quickly; a child of 6 names one thing it is good at and one it still practises, and goes on with both.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says "less strong", not weak. The statement is about dealing with these qualities; it does not say which qualities or what dealing with them looks like.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the less strong side: a child avoids what it is not good at, or boasts about what it can do. Some conclude from one failed attempt that they "can't" and stop.
