## Official wording

zelfstandig uitvoeren van opdrachten of taken bijv. opruimen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

carrying out assignments or tasks independently, e.g. tidying up

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child hears "put the blocks away", sorts them into the right crates and puts the crates on the shelf without an adult beside it. It waters the plants when the task board shows its name. A child of 4 does a task of one step and may need a reminder to finish; a child of 6 does a task of several steps and checks whether it is done.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Tidying up is given as an example. The word "independently" bounds it. The statement does not say how many steps a task has or who gives it.

### Common mistakes

A child starts the task and begins to play with the things it tidies. A child does the first step and takes the task for done. A child waits for an adult to say each step.
