## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking care of and dealing respectfully with one's own health (physical and mental)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child drinks water after running, rests when tired, puts on a sun hat, and tells the teacher when something hurts. The child also says when he or she is worried or sad, and picks something that helps, such as sitting in a quiet corner or talking to an adult. A child of 4 says "it hurts" or "I am tired" and lets an adult decide what to do; a child of 7 notices the sign earlier and takes a first step without being told, such as getting a drink or asking for a break.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two things are named: taking care of one's own health and dealing with it respectfully; the brackets name physical and mental health. The statement names no behaviour, food, exercise or sleep, and sets no level of detail for mental health.

### Common mistakes

Children do not notice thirst, tiredness or cold while they play, and go on until they cry. They think health is only about the body and do not count worry or sadness. They think being healthy means never being ill, so that an illness is their own fault.
