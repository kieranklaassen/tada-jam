## Official wording

bewondering tonen voor elkaars vaardigheden en mogelijkheden (kwaliteiten)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

showing admiration for each other's skills and capabilities (qualities)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child watches a classmate hang upside down on the bar and says "wow, you dare that!". It claps when another child finishes a hard puzzle and asks "how did you do that?". A child of 4 admires what it sees and would like to do itself; a child of 6 also admires what another is good at and it is not, such as comforting or explaining.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets sum up skills and capabilities as qualities. The wording says "each other's": the admiration goes both ways. Giving and receiving compliments is `Leren omgaan met een ander / 4`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out admiration for children who are not their friends. A child may answer another's success with "I can do that too" or "mine is better".
