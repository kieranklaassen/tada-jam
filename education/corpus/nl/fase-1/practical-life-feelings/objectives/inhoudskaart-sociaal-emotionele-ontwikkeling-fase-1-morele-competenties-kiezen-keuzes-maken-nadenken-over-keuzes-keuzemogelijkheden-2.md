## Official wording

benoemen welke keuze gemaakt moet worden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

naming which choice has to be made

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child stands at the choice board and says "I have to choose between the sand table and painting". When two friends each ask it to play, it says "I have to pick who I play with first". A child of 4 names the choice when the options are in front of it; a child of 6 names a choice that is not visible, such as "tell or not tell".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for naming the choice; making a choice and carrying it out is `Nadenken over keuzes/keuzemogelijkheden / 4`. It names no kind of choice.

### Common mistakes

A child does not see that there is a choice and does what it always does. A child names what it wants and not the options. A child thinks it can have both and does not see that it has to choose.
