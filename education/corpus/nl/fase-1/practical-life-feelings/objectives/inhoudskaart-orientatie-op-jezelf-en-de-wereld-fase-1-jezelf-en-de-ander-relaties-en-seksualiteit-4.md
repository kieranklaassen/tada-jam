## Official wording

bespreken dat het aanraken van (bepaalde delen van) het lichaam positieve en negatieve gevoelens kan oproepen en hoe hiermee om te gaan

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discussing that touching (certain parts of) the body can call up positive and negative feelings, and how to deal with this

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle a child tells which touching he or she likes, such as sitting on a lap or a hand on the shoulder, and which not, such as rough pulling or tickling. The children talk together about what one can do when a touch gives an unpleasant feeling. A child of 4 says of one touch that he or she likes it or does not like it; a child of 6 says that the feeling about a touch can differ per person and per moment.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discussing, and two things are discussed: that touching the body, or certain parts of it (the brackets), can call up positive and negative feelings, and how to deal with this. The statement names no parts of the body, no situations and no ways of dealing with it, and sets no level of detail. It stands under the sub-heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*).

### Common mistakes

Children think that a touch they like themselves is liked by everyone. They think that a bad feeling about a touch is not something to talk about. They name the touch and not the feeling it gives.
