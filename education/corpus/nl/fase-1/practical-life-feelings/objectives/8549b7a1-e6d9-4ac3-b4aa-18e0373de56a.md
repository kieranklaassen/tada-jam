## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discussing that touching (certain parts of) the body can call up positive and negative feelings, and how to deal with this

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle a child says which touching he or she finds pleasant, such as a hug or holding hands, and which not, such as tickling or being pulled by the arm. The children hear that the same touch is pleasant for one child and unpleasant for another, and they talk about what one can do when a touch gives an unpleasant feeling. A child of 4 says of one touch that it feels nice or not nice; a child of 7 says that the same touch can feel pleasant at one moment and unpleasant at another, and adds a thought of his or her own to the talk about what one can do.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discussing, and two things are discussed: that touching the body, or certain parts of it (the brackets), can call up positive and negative feelings, and how to deal with this. The statement names no parts of the body, no situations and no ways of dealing with it, and sets no level of detail. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*). The content-line document prints a footnote on this cluster's heading: "Scholen zijn verplicht aandacht te besteden aan het thema relaties en seksualiteit, dat valt onder kerndoel 38, maar mogen zelf kiezen welke leerlijnen en tussendoelen per bouw zij formuleren." (in English: schools are obliged to give attention to the theme of relationships and sexuality, which falls under core goal 38, but may choose for themselves which learning lines and intermediate goals they formulate for each *bouw*, a stage of several school years).

### Common mistakes

Children have few words for the feeling a touch gives and say only that it is nice or stupid. They think that an unpleasant feeling about a touch is their own fault. They do not see that another child may dislike a touch that they themselves like.
