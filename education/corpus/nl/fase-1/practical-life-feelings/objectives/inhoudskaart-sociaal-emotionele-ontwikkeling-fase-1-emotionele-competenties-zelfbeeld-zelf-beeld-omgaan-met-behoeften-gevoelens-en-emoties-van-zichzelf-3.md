## Official wording

uiten van gevoelens op een acceptabele manier

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

expressing feelings in an acceptable way

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An angry child says "stop, I do not want that" or stamps its feet in the quiet corner, where earlier it would have hit. A happy child jumps and tells; a sad child goes to the teacher and says so or asks for a hug. A child of 4 manages this with an adult who offers the words or the place; a child of 6 does it itself.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement says "acceptable" and does not say what is acceptable. It names no feelings. Controlling intense feelings is `Beheersen van eigen gevoelens / 1`.

### Common mistakes

A child expresses anger with the body (hitting, biting, throwing) before words come. A child hides the feeling at school and shows it strongly at home. A child thinks the feeling itself is not allowed, where only the way of showing it is in question.
