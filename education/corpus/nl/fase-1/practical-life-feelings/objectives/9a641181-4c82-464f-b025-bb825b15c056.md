## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle children tell who belongs to their family and who else they are close to, and a child hears that this differs between classmates. The child draws the people he or she lives with, or places dolls or figures to show them, and looks at a picture book in which people live together in different ways. A child of 4 names the people at home; a child of 7 can name more than one kind of bond between people, such as family, friends and a couple, and says which he or she has seen.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discussing. The statement says "different forms of relationship" and names none; it sets no level of detail. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*). The content-line document prints a footnote on this cluster's heading: "Scholen zijn verplicht aandacht te besteden aan het thema relaties en seksualiteit, dat valt onder kerndoel 38, maar mogen zelf kiezen welke leerlijnen en tussendoelen per bouw zij formuleren." (in English: schools are obliged to give attention to the theme of relationships and sexuality, which falls under core goal 38, but may choose for themselves which learning lines and intermediate goals they formulate for each *bouw*, a stage of several school years).

### Common mistakes

Children assume every family is built like their own and call another form "not real". They mix up the bond and the word for it: every adult woman at a classmate's home is "the mother". They think that people who do not live in one house are not family.
