## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child washes and dries hands after the toilet and before eating, blows his or her nose in a tissue and throws it away, and puts on a coat when it is cold. The child brushes the teeth of a model or doll and then shows how he or she brushes at home. A child of 4 does these steps with reminders and with help for taps, zips and sleeves; a child of 7 does them without help and mostly without a reminder.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no parts of care (such as washing, teeth, hair, clothing or sleep) and no level of independence. Habits around food and hygiene are the subject of `ojw/pdm/2/02/fase1`.

### Common mistakes

Children wet their hands and skip the soap or the drying. They wash only what looks dirty and do not think of what cannot be seen. They brush the front teeth and leave out the back teeth and the inner sides.
