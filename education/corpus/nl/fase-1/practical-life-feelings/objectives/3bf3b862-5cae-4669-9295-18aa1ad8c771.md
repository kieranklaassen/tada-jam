## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child washes hands before cutting fruit, carries scissors with the points down, and wipes up spilled water before someone slips. The child puts the lid back on the glue, hands a tool to another child by the handle, and says what could happen otherwise. A child of 4 follows these steps when shown and reminded; a child of 7 can say why a step is there and points out a hazard, such as a bag in the walkway, before an adult does.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discovering that a safe environment calls for this, so the statement is about the link between how people act and safety. It names four ways of acting: hygienic, precise, safe and caring. The content-line PDF prints an example in brackets after the goal (*bijv.*), which the open data leaves out: handling objects, materials and tools with care and good sense, and seeing dangers; it is an example, not a list. The statement names no particular tools or dangers and states no safety condition such as adult supervision.

### Common mistakes

Children think a thing is dangerous only if it has hurt them before. They follow a rule in the place where they learned it and not in a new place, for example washing hands at school and not at a picnic. They think being quick and being careful go together, and hurry while carrying scissors or a full cup.
