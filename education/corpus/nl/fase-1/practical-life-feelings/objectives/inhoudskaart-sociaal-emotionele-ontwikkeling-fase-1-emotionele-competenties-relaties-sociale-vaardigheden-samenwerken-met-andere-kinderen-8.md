## Official wording

bewust zijn dat jezelf en anderen, samen verantwoordelijk zijn (in het proces)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

being aware that you yourself and others, together, are responsible (in the process)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After building together, the children who built put the blocks away together, and a child says "we all have to help". When water is spilled on the shared painting, the children talk about what each did. A child of 4 feels responsible for its own piece; a child of 6 says "we made this" and helps finish a part that is not its own.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets add "in the process": the responsibility is for how the work goes, and the statement sits under working together with other children. Being aware is asked; the wording names no task and no action.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out their own share: when something goes wrong they point to another child, and when it goes well they say "I did it". Some think that the child who started, or the teacher, is the only one responsible.
