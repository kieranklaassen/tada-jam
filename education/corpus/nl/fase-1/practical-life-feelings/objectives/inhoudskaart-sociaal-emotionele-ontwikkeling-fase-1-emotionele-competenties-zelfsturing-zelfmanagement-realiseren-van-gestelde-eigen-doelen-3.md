## Official wording

proberen om iets af te maken, ook als het niet direct lukt (nieuwe dingen proberen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

trying to finish something, even if it does not succeed straight away (trying new things)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child's beads slide off the thread, and it knots the end and starts again. A puzzle piece does not fit and it turns the piece, tries another place and goes on until the puzzle is done. A child of 4 tries once or twice more and then asks for help; a child of 6 tries another way and finishes, sometimes the next day.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement says trying: it does not ask that the thing is finished. The brackets add trying new things. It names no task and no number of attempts.

### Common mistakes

A child repeats the same attempt in the same way. A child stops at the first failure and says "I can't". A child starts something new each time and finishes nothing.
