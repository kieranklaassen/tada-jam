## Official wording

beheersen van eigen (heftige) gevoelens, emoties en stress (reguleren)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

controlling one's own (intense) feelings, emotions and stress (regulating)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child whose tower is knocked over clenches its hands, breathes out slowly, and goes to the quiet corner or to the teacher, where earlier it would have hit. It squeezes a cushion or counts with an adult until it is calm. A child of 4 calms down with an adult who stays close; a child of 6 uses a way it has practised and comes back to the group.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets add "intense" and "regulating": intense feelings are included, and controlling is explained as regulating. The statement names feelings, emotions and stress. It does not say whether an adult helps and names no way of calming down.

### Common mistakes

A child acts on the feeling before it can use what it has practised. A child thinks that controlling a feeling means not having it or not showing it. A child can do it when it is a little upset and not when it is very upset.
