## Official wording

beschrijven van de verschillen en overeenkomsten van elkaar tussen zichzelf de ander(en)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing each other's differences and similarities, between oneself and the other(s)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children sit opposite each other and tell "we both have a brother, you have glasses and I do not, we both like football". They draw themselves side by side and point out what is the same and what differs. A child of 4 describes what it sees, mostly differences; a child of 6 describes similarities and differences in looks, in what each likes and in what each can do.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The source prints the last words without a joining word (*tussen zichzelf de ander(en)*); "between oneself and the other(s)" is plainly meant, and the gloss supplies the "and". The source also says both "of each other" and "between" for the one comparison. The statement asks for describing and names both differences and similarities. The "(s)" leaves open whether one other or several are meant, and no kind of difference is named.

### Common mistakes

A child describes differences and finds no similarities. A child describes a difference as better or worse. A child describes the other and leaves itself out of the comparison.
