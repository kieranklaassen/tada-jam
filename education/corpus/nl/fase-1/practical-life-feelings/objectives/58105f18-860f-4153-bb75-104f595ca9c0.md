## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a circle a child hears a question without one right answer, such as "can a stone be happy?" or "is a drawing of a dog a dog?", and says what he or she thinks. The child gives a reason, hears another child's reason, and says "I agree, because ..." or "I think something else". A child of 4 gives an answer and sometimes a reason from his or her own life; a child of 7 can react to what another child said and can change his or her mind aloud.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording is gaining experience (the Dutch has the plural, *ervaringen*), so what is described is taking part, not a level of reasoning. The conversations are called simple; the statement names no questions, topics, length or group size.

### Common mistakes

Children look at the teacher for the right answer and stop thinking when no answer is confirmed. They tell a story about themselves that has nothing to do with the question. They repeat the previous speaker's answer in place of giving their own.
