## Official wording

benoemen van verschillen tussen kinderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

naming differences between children

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children stand side by side at the mirror and say "you have curly hair, I have straight hair" and "you are taller". In the circle a child says "she likes climbing and I like drawing". A child of 4 names what it can see, such as hair, height and clothes; a child of 6 also names differences in what children like, can do or do at home.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for naming and is about differences only; similarities are not mentioned here. It names no kind of difference. `Herkennen van en omgaan met verschillen tussen zichzelf en de ander / 2` gives examples of kinds of difference and similarity.

### Common mistakes

A child names a difference as better or worse ("my hair is nicer"). A child names only what is visible. A child points at a child and names a difference in a way that hurts, without knowing that it does.
