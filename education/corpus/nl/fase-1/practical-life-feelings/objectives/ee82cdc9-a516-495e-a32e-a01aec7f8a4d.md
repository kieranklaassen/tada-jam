## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

becoming aware that humans have an influence on the natural environment

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child picks up litter in the schoolyard and sees how much there is. The child sees that trampled grass by the gate does not grow and that a watered plant does, and says who did that. On a walk the child points at things people made or changed, such as a ditch, a mown verge or a felled tree. A child of 4 notices one thing somebody did and its result at that spot; a child of 7 can say that the same act by many people changes a whole place.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is becoming aware, not explaining or acting. The statement does not say whether the influence is harmful or helpful, and names no particular kind of influence, no examples, no scale and no place.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out their own part: they see litter as something other people did. They also take "influence" to mean only damage, and do not count planting, feeding or tidying as influence.
