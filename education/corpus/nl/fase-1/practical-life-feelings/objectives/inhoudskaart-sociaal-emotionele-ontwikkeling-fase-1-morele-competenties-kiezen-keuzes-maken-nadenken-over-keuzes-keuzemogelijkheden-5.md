## Official wording

beschrijven dat een keuze gevolgen heeft, vaak op korte en/of lange termijn

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing that a choice has consequences, often in the short and/or long term

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child tells "I left my coat inside, so I was cold outside" and "we planted the bulbs in autumn, so there are flowers now". It lays picture cards in order: the choice, what happened at once, what happened later. A child of 4 describes what follows at once; a child of 6 also describes something that follows hours or days later.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says "often" and "and/or": a consequence may come soon, later, or both. The statement asks for describing. It does not say how long the long term is.

### Common mistakes

A child sees only the consequence that comes at once. A child does not link a later consequence to its choice. A child thinks a consequence is a punishment given by an adult.
