## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child goes to the toilet alone, flushes, and washes hands with soap, singing a short song until the foam is rinsed off. The child wipes his or her nose, puts the tissue in the bin, and puts on a coat and zips it before going outside. A child of 4 needs help with buttons, the tap and wiping; a child of 6 does the whole routine alone and notices when hands or face need washing.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no parts of care (such as washing, teeth, hair, clothing or sleep) and no level of independence.

### Common mistakes

Children hold their hands under the tap for a second and skip the soap. They forget to wash hands after the toilet when play is waiting. They put on a coat without closing it or put shoes on the wrong feet and do not notice.
