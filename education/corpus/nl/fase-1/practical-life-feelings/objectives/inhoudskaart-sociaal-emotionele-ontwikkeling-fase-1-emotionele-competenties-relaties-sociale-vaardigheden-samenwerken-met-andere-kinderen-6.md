## Official wording

rekening houden met elkaar (de ander te respecteren)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking each other into account (respecting the other)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child moves its chair so that another child can also see the book. It walks around another child's building and lowers its voice when a child nearby is working. A child of 4 does this when reminded; a child of 6 does it more often unprompted and asks "is this in your way?".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets explain taking each other into account as respecting the other. The statement sits under working together with other children. It names no situation and no behaviour.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out looking around before they act: they step on another child's work or take the space because they did not notice the other. Many take friends into account and not the other children.
