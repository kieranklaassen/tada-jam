## Official wording

zichzelf handhaven binnen de eigen groep

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

holding one's own within one's own group

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child goes on with its building when other children crowd into the corner, and keeps its place in the circle when another child pushes up beside it. It stays in the group's play when the play changes, and keeps the part it had. A child of 4 stays where it is and keeps hold of what it has; a child of 6 keeps its place and its part in the group's play when the others do not make room at once.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement speaks of the child's own group and names no situation. It does not say how the child holds its own. Daring to remain oneself in a group is `Rekening houden met zichzelf binnen een groep / 7`.

### Common mistakes

A child holds its place by pushing or shouting. A child gives up its place or its play as soon as another child presses. A child leaves the group and plays alone when it is not given room.
