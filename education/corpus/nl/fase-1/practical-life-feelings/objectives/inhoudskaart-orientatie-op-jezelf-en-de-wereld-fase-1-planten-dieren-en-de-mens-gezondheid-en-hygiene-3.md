## Official wording

kennismaken en ervaringen delen met onderdelen van de gezondheidszorg

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting to know parts of health care and sharing experiences of them

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a hospital corner a child bandages a doll's arm, listens with a toy stethoscope and gives a pretend injection, and a classmate plays the patient. The child tells in the circle about the dentist, the school doctor or a stay in hospital and shows a plaster or a scar. A visitor such as a nurse or dental hygienist shows the tools, and the child holds a mouth mirror. A child of 4 plays one act he or she remembers; a child of 6 tells a visit in order and names who did what.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two things are named: getting to know, and sharing experiences. "Parts of health care" (*onderdelen van de gezondheidszorg*) is not filled in: no professions, places or treatments are named. The statement asks for no knowledge of illness.

### Common mistakes

Children expect every visit to hurt, because the injection is what they remember. They think the doctor makes you ill, or that medicine is a sweet. They think getting ill is a punishment.
