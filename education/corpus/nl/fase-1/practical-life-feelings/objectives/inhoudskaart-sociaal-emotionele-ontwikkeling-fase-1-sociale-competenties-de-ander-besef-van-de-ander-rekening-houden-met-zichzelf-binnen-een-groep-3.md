## Official wording

sluiten van vriendschappen (vriendjes maken)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

forming friendships (making friends)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child asks the same classmate to play several days in a row, saves a seat for that child and waits for it at the door. It shares its building blocks and says "you are my friend". A child of 4 calls whoever it plays with at the moment its friend; a child of 6 has one or more lasting friends and says what it likes about them.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets say the same in the words children use (*vriendjes maken*). The statement does not say how many friendships or how long they last. Explaining how you can enter into a relationship is `Leren omgaan met een ander / 9`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the other's side: a child claims a friend for itself and keeps other children away, or says "you are not my friend any more" after a small quarrel. Some wait to be chosen and do not approach anyone.
