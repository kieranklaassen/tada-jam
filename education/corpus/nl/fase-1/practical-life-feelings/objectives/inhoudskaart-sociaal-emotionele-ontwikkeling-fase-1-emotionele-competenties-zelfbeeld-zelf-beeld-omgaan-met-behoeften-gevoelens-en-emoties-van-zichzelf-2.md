## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "I am happy because grandpa is coming" or "I am scared of the thunder". It picks a feelings card and says the word that goes with it. A child of 4 uses a few words, such as happy, angry, sad and scared; a child of 6 uses more words, such as proud, jealous, disappointed and nervous.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for naming and does not say which feelings or how many words. The sub-heading shows that the child's own feelings are meant.

### Common mistakes

A child uses "good" and "not good" for every feeling. A child names the feeling the adult suggests. A child says what happened ("he hit me") where it is asked what it feels.
