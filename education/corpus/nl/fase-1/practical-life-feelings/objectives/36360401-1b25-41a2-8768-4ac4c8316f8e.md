## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child plays doctor in the home corner: listens to a doll's chest with a toy stethoscope, puts on a plaster, and writes a note. In the circle the child tells about a visit to the dentist, the family doctor or the hospital and what happened there. The child handles real things such as a bandage or a tongue depressor. A child of 4 acts out one thing he or she has been through, such as an injection; a child of 7 can name several places and people of health care and what each does.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two things are named: getting to know, and sharing experiences. "Parts of health care" (*onderdelen van de gezondheidszorg*) is not filled in: no professions, places or treatments are named. The statement asks for no knowledge of illness.

### Common mistakes

Children think a doctor or dentist is there to hurt, because they remember the injection and not the reason for it. They call every health worker "the doctor". They think illness or a treatment is a punishment for something they did.
