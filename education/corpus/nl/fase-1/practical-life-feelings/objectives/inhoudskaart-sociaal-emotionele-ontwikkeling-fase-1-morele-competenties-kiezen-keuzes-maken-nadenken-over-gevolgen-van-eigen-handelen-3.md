## Official wording

ontdekken dat de oorzaak van een moeilijke situatie bij zichzelf kan liggen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that the cause of a difficult situation can lie with oneself

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a quarrel a child retells with the teacher what happened step by step and says "I took the car first". With hand puppets it replays the scene and sees at which moment it went wrong. A child of 4 gets there with an adult's calm questions and without blame; a child of 6 sometimes says its own part unasked.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discovering, and the wording says "can lie": the cause is not always with the child. The statement does not ask the child to repair anything.

### Common mistakes

A child names the other child as the cause each time. A child takes all the blame and concludes that it is bad. A child admits its part to end the talk, without seeing it.
