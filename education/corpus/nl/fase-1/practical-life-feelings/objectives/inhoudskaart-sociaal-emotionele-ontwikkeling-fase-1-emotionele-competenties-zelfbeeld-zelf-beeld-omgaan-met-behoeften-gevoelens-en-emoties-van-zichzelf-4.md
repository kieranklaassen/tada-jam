## Official wording

bewust omgaan met eigen gevoelens, wensen en opvattingen en deze voor anderen begrijpelijk kunnen uiten

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

consciously dealing with one's own feelings, wishes and views and being able to express these in a way that others can understand

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "I do not want to play tag, I want to build, because I am tired". In a talk about what the group will do it says what it thinks and why. A child of 4 says what it wants in a few words and shows the feeling; a child of 6 says the feeling, the wish and a reason so that the other child understands.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement holds two parts: dealing consciously with one's own feelings, wishes and views, and being able to express them so that others understand. It names three things: feelings, wishes and views. It does not say which others.

### Common mistakes

A child expects others to know what it wants without saying it. A child states a wish as a demand ("I must"). A child says what it wants and leaves out the reason, so the other does not understand.
