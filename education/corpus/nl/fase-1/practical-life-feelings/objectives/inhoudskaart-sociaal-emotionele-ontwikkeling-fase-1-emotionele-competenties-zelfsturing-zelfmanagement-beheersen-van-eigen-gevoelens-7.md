## Official wording

eerst nadenken voordat er gereageerd wordt op een (lastige) situatie

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

thinking first before reacting to a (difficult) situation

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child whose place in the line is taken stops, puts its hands behind its back and says "I was standing there", where earlier it would have pushed. With a picture of a traffic light it practises stop, think, do. A child of 4 manages the stopping with an adult who says "wait"; a child of 6 stops itself in calm moments and thinks of two things it could do.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets add "difficult": the statement covers any situation and names difficult ones in particular. It does not say how long the child thinks or about what. Thinking of solutions is `Nadenken over gevolgen van eigen handelen / 2`.

### Common mistakes

A child reacts first and thinks afterwards. A child can say the steps when calm and cannot use them when upset. A child stops and then does what it was going to do, without having thought of another way.
