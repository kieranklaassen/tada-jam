## Official wording

herkennen van (alledaagse) problemen en conflicten

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising (everyday) problems and conflicts

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sees that two children both pull at the same doll and says "they are fighting". It notices that the block tower fell because someone walked into it, and tells the teacher that there is a problem. A child of 4 notices a conflict when it is loud or when it is part of it; a child of 6 also notices a quiet one, such as a child who is left out of play.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets add "everyday" before "problems": any problem is covered and an everyday one is named in particular; the wording does not settle whether the bracketed word also goes with the conflicts. The statement is about recognising; telling causes and consequences is `Herkennen, praten over en oplossen van conflicten / 2`. Beyond that word it names no kind of problem and does not say whether the child is part of the conflict or watches it.

### Common mistakes

A child sees a conflict only when someone cries or shouts. A child calls every "no" from another child a fight. A child sees the other child's part in a conflict and not its own.
