## Official wording

ervaren van de verschillen en overeenkomsten tussen zichzelf en de andere kinderen in de groep: bijvoorbeeld in lichaamskenmerken, karaktereigenschappen, voorkeuren/afkeuren, afkomst en traditie

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing the differences and similarities between oneself and the other children in the group: for example in physical characteristics, character traits, likes/dislikes, origin and tradition

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Children stand in a row by height, put their hands side by side, and find out who else likes pancakes or has a baby brother. A child tells which feast its family celebrates and hears what another family does. A child of 4 notices what it sees, such as hair and height; a child of 6 also notices differences in character and in what families do, and finds things it shares with a child who looks different.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is experiencing. The list after "for example" gives examples: physical characteristics, character traits, likes and dislikes, origin and tradition. The comparison is with the other children in the group, and both differences and similarities are named.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the similarities and attend to the differences. A child may take its own way as the normal one and call the other's strange.
