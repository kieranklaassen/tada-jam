## Official wording

reageren op de hulpvraag van anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reacting to others' request for help

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child hears "can you help me with my coat?" and holds the sleeve or pulls up the zip. When a child cannot reach the paper, it fetches a sheet for it. A child of 4 helps when asked directly and when it is easy; a child of 6 also hears a less direct request such as "I can't do this" and asks what the other needs.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about a request that others make; it does not say that the child offers help unasked. It names no kind of help and does not say that the help must succeed. Accepting help is `Zichzelf staande houden of aanpassen in een groep / 2`.

### Common mistakes

A child does the whole task itself, where the other wanted help with one step. A child hears the request and goes on with its own play. A child helps a friend and ignores the same request from another child.
