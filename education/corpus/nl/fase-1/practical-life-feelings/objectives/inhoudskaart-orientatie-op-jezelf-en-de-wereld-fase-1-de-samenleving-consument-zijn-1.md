## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

developing self-reliant (*redzaam*, able to manage for oneself) behaviour as a consumer

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the shop corner a child takes a basket, picks items from the shelf, puts them on the counter and pays the classmate at the till with play coins. On a class trip to the bakery the child asks for a loaf, hands over the money and waits for the bag. A child of 4 hands over any coin and takes the item; a child of 6 asks what it costs, counts out a few coins of one kind, and chooses between two items when the coins are enough for one.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is developing. The statement names no shop, product or way of paying and no amount of money. This statement states no number range.

### Common mistakes

Children think that a larger coin or a larger number of coins is worth more. They think the shopkeeper hands out money, because they see change come back. They treat things taken from a shelf as theirs before paying.
