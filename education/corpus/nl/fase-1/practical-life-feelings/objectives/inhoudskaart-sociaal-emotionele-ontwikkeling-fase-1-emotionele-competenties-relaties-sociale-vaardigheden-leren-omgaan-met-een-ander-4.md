## Official wording

geven en ontvangen van complimenten

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

giving and receiving compliments

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child looks at another child's drawing and says "nice colours" or "you built that high". When it gets a compliment itself it looks at the giver and smiles or says "thank you". A child of 4 gives general praise such as "nice", often copying the teacher; a child of 6 says what exactly it finds good and can take a compliment without denying it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement holds two parts: giving and receiving. It does not say what a compliment is about or how many are given. Showing admiration for each other's skills is a separate statement, `Herkennen, begrijpen van en aanpassen aan emoties van anderen / 5`.

### Common mistakes

A child gives compliments only to its friends. A child answers a compliment by denying it ("no, it is ugly") or by looking away and saying nothing. A child says "nice" without having looked at the thing.
