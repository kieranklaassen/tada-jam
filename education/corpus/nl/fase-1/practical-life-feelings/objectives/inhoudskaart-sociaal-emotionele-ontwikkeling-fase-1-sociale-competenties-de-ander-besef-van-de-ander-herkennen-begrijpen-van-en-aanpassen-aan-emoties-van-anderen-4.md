## Official wording

rekening houden in het eigen gedrag met de gevoelens en de situatie van anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking the feelings and the situation of others into account in one's own behaviour

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child plays more quietly next to a child who has a headache, and makes room for a child with its arm in a cast. It waits with showing its new toy when a child nearby has just lost one. A child of 4 adjusts when an adult points out the other's state; a child of 6 notices it and adjusts unprompted in familiar cases.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names two things to take into account: the feelings and the situation of others. It is about the child's own behaviour and names no behaviour.

### Common mistakes

A child knows how the other feels and goes on as before. A child adjusts for a moment and forgets. A child avoids the other child altogether because it does not know what to do.
