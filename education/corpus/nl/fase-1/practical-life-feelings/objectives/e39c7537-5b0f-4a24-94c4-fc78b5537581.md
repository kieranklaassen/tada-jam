## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring habits in the area of food and hygiene

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Children show what is in their lunch boxes and tell when and where they eat at home, and a child sorts play food or pictures into what he or she eats often and seldom. The child tells when hands are washed and teeth are brushed at home, and tries out the class habit of washing hands before fruit time. A child of 4 tells what he or she eats and does at home; a child of 7 compares the habits of different children and can give a reason for one, such as washing hands before eating.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is exploring habits, not following rules or knowing which food is healthy. Two areas are named: food and hygiene. The statement names no foods and no hygiene habits, and does not use the word healthy.

### Common mistakes

Children sort food into "good" and "bad" by whether they like it. They think the habits of their own home are the only ones. They think clean means looking clean, so that hands with no visible dirt need no washing.
