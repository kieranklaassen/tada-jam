## Official wording

beseffen dat de leefomgeving wordt beïnvloed door techniek (wetenschap en technologie)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising that the living environment is influenced by technology (*techniek*) (science and technology)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child takes an old alarm clock or torch apart at the discovery table and looks at what is inside. On a walk through the building the child finds things that help people, such as the tap, the door handle, the heating and the lift, and says what each does. A child of 4 presses, turns and opens such things and names them; a child of 6 can say what people would have to do if the thing were not there.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is realising, not explaining how a thing works. The Dutch *techniek* covers made things and the skill of making and using them; the card's own brackets name science and technology. The statement names no devices and does not say whether the influence is good or bad.

### Common mistakes

Children count only things with a screen or a plug as technology. They think a made thing grew or was always there, and do not think of a maker. They know what a thing does and not what it changes for people.
