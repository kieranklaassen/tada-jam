## Official wording

beschrijven van (heftige) gevoelens, emoties en stress aan anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing (intense) feelings, emotions and stress to others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After it has calmed down a child tells the teacher "I was so angry that my tummy hurt". It draws how the feeling looked or shows on a row of faces how strong it was. A child of 4 says one word or points; a child of 6 tells what it felt, where in its body and how strong.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets add "intense". The statement asks for describing to others and does not say to whom or when. It names feelings, emotions and stress.

### Common mistakes

A child cannot find words while the feeling is strong. A child tells what happened and not what it felt. A child says "nothing" when asked, and shows the feeling in its behaviour.
