## Official wording

verkennen van de invloed van digitalisering op de eigen leefwereld (impact, veiligheid, gevaren)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring the influence of digitalisation on one's own everyday world (impact, safety, dangers)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child tells in the circle what he or she does on a tablet or phone and with whom: watching a film, calling a grandparent, taking photos. In the home corner the child plays with an old phone or keyboard and acts out a call. The class talks about what to do when a film or picture is frightening: put the device down and go to an adult. A child of 4 names the devices at home and what he or she watches; a child of 6 can name something useful about a device and something that can go wrong, and knows to ask an adult first.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is exploring. The brackets name impact, safety and dangers. The statement names no devices, apps or rules, sets no level of detail for the dangers, and does not ask that a child uses a device.

### Common mistakes

Children do not tell a film or play on a screen apart from what is real. They think that tapping or swiping cannot cause anything outside the screen, such as a purchase or a sent photo. They hide that something frightened them, because they fear the device will be taken away.
