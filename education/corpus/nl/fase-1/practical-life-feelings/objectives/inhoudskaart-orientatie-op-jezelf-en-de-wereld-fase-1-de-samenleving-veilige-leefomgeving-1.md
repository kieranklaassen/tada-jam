## Official wording

zorgdragen voor de eigen leefomgeving (thuis, klas, school)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking care of one's own living environment (home, classroom, school)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At tidy-up time a child puts the puzzle pieces in the box and the box on its shelf, pushes in a chair and hangs an apron on its hook. As helper of the day the child waters the plants, wipes the snack table or sweeps under the sand table, and tells what he or she helps with at home. A child of 4 tidies with an adult alongside and with pictures on the shelves that show where things go; a child of 6 finishes a tidying task without help and notices a thing that lies in the wrong place.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name home, classroom and school. The statement names no tasks and no level of independence. It does not mention the neighbourhood or nature.

### Common mistakes

Children stop tidying when their own toy is put away and leave the shared mess. They move things out of sight, for example under a cupboard, in place of putting them back. They start to play again with what they are tidying.
