## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Three children get one sheet of paper and paint one large picture of the playground, or they set the table for the group together. They talk about who does what and look at the result together. A child of 4 does its own piece of the task next to the others; a child of 6 thinks of the shared result and adjusts its piece to it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The assignment is shared: one task for several children. The statement does not say who gives the assignment, how many children take part, or how long it takes.

### Common mistakes

A child treats the shared sheet as its own and fills it. A child finishes its own piece and leaves. A child changes or removes what another child made without asking.
