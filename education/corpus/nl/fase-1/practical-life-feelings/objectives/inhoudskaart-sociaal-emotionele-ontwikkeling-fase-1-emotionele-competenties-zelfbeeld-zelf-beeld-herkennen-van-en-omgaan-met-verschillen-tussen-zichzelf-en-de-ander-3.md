## Official wording

herkennen van situaties die gevoelens, emoties en reacties oproepen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising situations that evoke feelings, emotions and reactions

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child looks at a picture of a child whose ice cream fell and says "then you get sad". It tells "when someone takes my blocks I get angry" and "when the dog barks I am scared". A child of 4 links a clear situation to one feeling; a child of 6 knows which situations bring a strong reaction in itself and says so beforehand.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for recognising the situations, not for controlling the reaction. It names feelings, emotions and reactions and does not say which, or whose.

### Common mistakes

A child thinks a situation gives everyone the same feeling. A child names the reaction ("I hit") and not the situation that came before it. A child links a feeling to the last thing that happened and not to its cause.
