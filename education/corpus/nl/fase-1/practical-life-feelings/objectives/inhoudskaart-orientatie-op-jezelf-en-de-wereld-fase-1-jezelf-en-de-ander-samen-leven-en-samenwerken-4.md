## Official wording

ervaren dat door tegenstrijdige belangen conflicten kunnen ontstaan die je samen kunt oplossen (b)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing that conflicting interests can give rise to conflicts that you can resolve together (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children pull at one doll's pram. With the teacher beside them each says what he or she wanted, and they pick a solution: one pushes the pram to the door and the other pushes it back. Two children at one easel agree who paints first and turn a sand timer. A child of 4 says what he or she wants and accepts a solution an adult offers; a child of 6 hears what the other wants and proposes turns or sharing.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card marks this as an aspect of citizenship (b). The verb is experiencing, and two things are experienced: that conflicts can arise from conflicting interests, and that you can resolve them together. The statement names no kinds of conflict and no method for resolving one, and does not say whether an adult helps.

### Common mistakes

Children think the one who had the toy first, or the one who cries loudest, is in the right. They think a quarrel means the other child is no longer a friend. They say yes to a solution and take the toy again a minute later.
