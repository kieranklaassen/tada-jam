## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

behaving safely in the street and as a (walking and cycling) participant in traffic

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child stops at the kerb, looks left, right and left again, listens, and crosses in a straight line beside an adult. On the playground the child rides a balance bike or bicycle along a chalk road, brakes at a line, and lets a walking child pass first. A child of 4 stops at the kerb when told and walks on the side of the pavement away from the road; a child of 7 looks and says when it is clear to cross while the adult checks, and cycles in a straight line beside an adult.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names the street and taking part in traffic, and the brackets name walking and cycling. It names no traffic rules, signs or kinds of road and states no safety condition such as adult supervision. It does not mention being a passenger in a car or on public transport.

### Common mistakes

Children think that if they can see a car, the driver can see them. They turn the head to look and step off the kerb without waiting for what they see. They judge how far away a car is but not how fast it comes, and they run across to be quick.
