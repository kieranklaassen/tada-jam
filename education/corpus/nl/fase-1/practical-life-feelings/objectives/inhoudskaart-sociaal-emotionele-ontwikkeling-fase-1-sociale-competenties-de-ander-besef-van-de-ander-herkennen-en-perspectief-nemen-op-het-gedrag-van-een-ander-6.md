## Official wording

uitleggen wanneer een situatie met een ander onveilig is en er over praten wat je er aan kan doen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

explaining when a situation with another person is unsafe and talking about what you can do about it

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a circle talk a child explains that a situation is unsafe when a bigger child pushes it or does not stop when asked to. The children talk about what one can do then, and more than one idea is named. A child of 4 says of one moment, such as being pushed on the climbing frame, that it was unsafe; a child of 6 explains what made the situation unsafe and brings an idea of its own to the talk about what one can do.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement holds two parts: explaining when a situation with another person is unsafe, and talking about what you can do about it. It sets no level of detail, does not say who the other is, and names no kind of unsafe situation. Recognising safe and unsafe situations is `Zichzelf staande houden of aanpassen in een groep / 6`.

### Common mistakes

A child thinks that telling an adult about another child is not allowed. A child cannot say what made the situation unsafe and says only that the other was "mean". A child knows what to do when asked and does not do it in the moment.
