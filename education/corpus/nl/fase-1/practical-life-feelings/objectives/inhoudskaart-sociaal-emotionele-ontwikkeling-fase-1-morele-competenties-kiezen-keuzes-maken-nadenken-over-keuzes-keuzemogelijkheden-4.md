## Official wording

maken van een keuze en deze uitvoeren

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

making a choice and carrying it out

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child hangs its name card at the building corner, goes there and builds until the work period ends. It says "I choose the red paper", takes it and makes its card with it. A child of 4 chooses and often wants to switch after a short time; a child of 6 stays with its choice or says why it changes.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement holds two parts: making the choice and carrying it out. It names no kind of choice and does not say how long the child keeps to it.

### Common mistakes

A child chooses and then goes where its friend goes. A child regrets the choice as soon as it sees another option. A child cannot choose and waits until an adult chooses for it.
