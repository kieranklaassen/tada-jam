## Official wording

nieuwsgierig naar het onbekende (gaat over tot actie)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

curious about the unknown (proceeds to action)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sees a new box of magnets on the table, picks them up and tries what sticks. It asks "what is that?" about a snail on the path, crouches and watches. A child of 4 explores by touching and trying; a child of 6 also asks questions and tries out an idea of its own.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The source prints this statement without a verb at its start, and the words in brackets are in the third person. The brackets show that the curiosity leads to doing something. The statement names no subject of the curiosity.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the step to action: a cautious child watches for a long time and needs an adult or another child to go first. Other children act at once and do not look first.
