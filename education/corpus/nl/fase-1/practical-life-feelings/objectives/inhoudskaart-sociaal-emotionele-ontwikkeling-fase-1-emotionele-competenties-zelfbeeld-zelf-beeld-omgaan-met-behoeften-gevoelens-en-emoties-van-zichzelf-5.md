## Official wording

inschatten van eigen gevoelens, gedachten, motieven

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

assessing one's own feelings, thoughts, motives

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child puts a peg on a feelings thermometer to show how angry it is: a little or very. Asked why it knocked over the tower, it thinks and says "I wanted to join and they said no". A child of 4 says how big the feeling is with an adult's help; a child of 6 also says what it thought and why it did something.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names three things: feelings, thoughts and motives, all the child's own. *Inschatten* means assessing or estimating; the wording does not say what about them is assessed.

### Common mistakes

A child rates every feeling as very big. A child answers "why did you do that?" with "I don't know" or with what the other child did. A child gives the reason it thinks the adult wants to hear.
