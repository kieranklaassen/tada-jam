## Official wording

actief luisteren naar anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

actively listening to others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle a child looks at the child who is talking, keeps its hands still and waits until the other has finished. Afterwards it asks a question about what was told or says one thing it heard. A child of 4 listens for a short time and to one speaker; a child of 6 listens through a longer story and reacts to what was said.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The word "actively" bounds it: more than being quiet is meant. The statement does not say what active listening consists of, how long the child listens, or to how many others.

### Common mistakes

A child sits still and looks at the speaker and takes in nothing of what is said. A child interrupts with its own story as soon as a word reminds it of something. A child listens to the teacher and not to other children.
