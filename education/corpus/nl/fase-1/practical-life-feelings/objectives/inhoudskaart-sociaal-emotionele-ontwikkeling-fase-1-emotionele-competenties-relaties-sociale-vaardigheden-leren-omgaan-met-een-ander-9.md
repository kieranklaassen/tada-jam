## Official wording

uitleggen hoe je een relatie kunt aangaan

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

explaining how you can enter into a relationship

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child tells how it got to know a new classmate: "I asked her name and then we played in the house corner". With two dolls it shows how one asks the other to play. A child of 4 shows it by doing or says one step such as "ask to play"; a child of 6 gives several ways, such as saying your name, asking a question or sharing something.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for explaining, so the child puts into words how it is done; forming friendships itself is `Rekening houden met zichzelf binnen een groep / 3`. It does not say which kind of relationship. How a relationship is made to last is `Leren omgaan met een ander / 10`.

### Common mistakes

A child thinks that giving a present or a sweet is the way to get a friend. A child says "you just are friends" and cannot name a step. A child explains joining in as starting to play along without asking.
