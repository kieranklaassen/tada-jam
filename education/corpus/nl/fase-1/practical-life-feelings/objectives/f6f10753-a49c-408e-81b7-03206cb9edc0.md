## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child puts the blocks back in their box, hangs up a coat, wipes a table after painting and waters the class plant. The child sweeps sand back into the sandpit and tells what he or she does at home, such as setting the table or feeding a pet. A child of 4 tidies what he or she has used when the teacher asks; a child of 7 sees without being asked what needs doing and does a fixed class task for a week.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name home, classroom and school. The statement names no tasks and no level of independence. It does not mention the neighbourhood or nature.

### Common mistakes

Children tidy only what they themselves used and walk past things others left. They put things anywhere out of sight instead of in their place. They take care of a place while an adult watches and stop when the adult leaves.
