## Official wording

ontdekken dat de wijze van interactie (mimiek, lichaamstaal en toon van de stem) de onderlinge relatie kan beïnvloeden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that the manner of interaction (facial expression, body language and tone of voice) can influence the mutual relationship

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "give me that" once with an angry face and a hard voice and once with a friendly face and a soft voice, and sees how the other child reacts each time. It notices that a child who stands with crossed arms and a turned back is not asked to play. A child of 4 notices a loud or angry voice and reacts to it; a child of 6 can say that the same words sound friendly or unfriendly and tries the other way.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name facial expression, body language and tone of voice. The verb is discovering, and the wording says "can influence": no fixed rule is stated. The statement does not ask the child to control its own expression.

### Common mistakes

A child attends to the words only and does not notice its own face or voice. A child thinks that loud always means angry. A child does not link the other child's walking away to how it spoke itself.
