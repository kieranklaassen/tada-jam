ontdekken dat iedereen deel uitmaakt van verschillende groepen waarin verschillende rollen worden vervuld (b)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that everyone is part of different groups in which different roles are filled (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sticks his or her photo on sheets for "my family", "my class" and "my club" and sees it appear on several. The child says what he or she is in each place: big sister at home, helper of the day in class, beginner at swimming. In role play the child is a parent in one scene and a customer in the next. A child of 4 names the family and the class; a child of 6 names more groups and says that the teacher is also somebody's parent or child.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card marks this as an aspect of citizenship (b). The verb is discovering. The statement says "everyone", so it covers the child and other people. It names no groups and no roles.

### Common mistakes

Children think the teacher lives at school and are startled to meet him or her in a shop. They think a person can be one thing only: a mother cannot also be a doctor. They keep a role from one group in another, for example deciding everything in class because they do so with a little brother at home.
