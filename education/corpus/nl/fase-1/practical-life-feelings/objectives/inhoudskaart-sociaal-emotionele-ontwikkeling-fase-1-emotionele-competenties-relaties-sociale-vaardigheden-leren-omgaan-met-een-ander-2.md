## Official wording

contact zoeken met anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

seeking contact with others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child walks up to a child in the building corner, watches, and says "can I play?" or holds out a block. It calls a child by name, sits down next to someone at the table, or shows the teacher what it made. A child of 4 seeks contact without words, by standing close or handing something over; a child of 6 asks, proposes a play idea and seeks out particular children.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no way of seeking contact and does not say whether the others are children or adults. It is about seeking contact; how a relationship is made to last is `Leren omgaan met een ander / 10`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out a clear opening: they stand near and wait to be asked, or they join by taking a toy or stepping into the play uninvited. Some seek contact only with adults or with one fixed child.
