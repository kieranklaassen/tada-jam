## Official wording

vertrouwen hebben in eigen kunnen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

having trust in one's own ability

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "I can do that" and climbs onto the balance beam, or starts cutting along the line without first asking for help. When something fails it says "I will try again". A child of 4 is sure of what it has done often; a child of 6 also starts something it has not done before and judges whether it needs help.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no task and no situation. Developing self-confidence is `Herkennen en omgaan met eigen kwaliteiten en krachten / 3`, and trust in another is `Leren omgaan met een ander / 6`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out a fair view of what they can do: one child says it can do everything, and another says "I can't" before it has tried.
