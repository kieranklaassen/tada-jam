## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child builds a den with two classmates, agrees who fetches the cloths and who holds the pegs, and plays in it with them. The child works at one large drawing with a child who speaks another language at home, shows by pointing what he or she means, and learns a word from the other child. A child of 4 plays next to and with one or two children and needs help to share out the roles; a child of 7 plays and works in a group of three or four, also with children he or she did not choose, and settles small disagreements without an adult.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording ends with the printed marker (b), the curriculum institute's sign for an aspect of citizenship (*burgerschap*). Three things are named: playing together, working together and interacting with one another. The brackets add diversity, with social and cultural backgrounds as an example (*bijv.*); no other kind of diversity is named. The statement names no group size and no tasks.

### Common mistakes

Children choose the same one or two playmates each time and leave out children who speak or look different. They take over the whole task instead of dividing it, or they leave it to the others. They take an unfamiliar habit of another child, such as different food or clothing, as a reason not to play.
