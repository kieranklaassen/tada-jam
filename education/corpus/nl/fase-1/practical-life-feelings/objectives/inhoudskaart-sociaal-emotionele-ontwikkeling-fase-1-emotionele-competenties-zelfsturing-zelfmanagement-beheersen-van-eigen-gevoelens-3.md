## Official wording

geven van eigen mening

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

giving one's own opinion

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The group chooses between two books and a child says "I want the one about the bear, because it is funny". Looking at a classmate's building it says what it thinks of it. A child of 4 says "I like it" or "I don't" and follows the child before it; a child of 6 gives an opinion that differs from its friend's and adds a reason.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no subject and does not ask for a reason. The card places it under controlling one's own feelings. That views differ is `Rekening houden met zichzelf binnen een groep / 6`.

### Common mistakes

A child repeats the opinion of the child before it or of a friend. A child takes its opinion for a fact ("that is ugly"). A child says nothing when it thinks its opinion differs from the group's.
