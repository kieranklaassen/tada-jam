## Official wording

(her)kennen en interpreteren van eenvoudige emoties en gevoelens bij anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

knowing/recognising and interpreting simple emotions and feelings in others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child looks at a classmate with a red face and tight fists and says "he is angry, I think because his tower fell". It matches photos of faces to the words happy, angry, sad and scared. A child of 4 recognises a clear face or crying; a child of 6 also reads posture and voice and says why the other might feel so.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The word "simple" bounds it. The statement does not list the emotions; `Herkennen en perspectief nemen op het gedrag van een ander / 1` names angry, scared, happy and sad. The Dutch *(her)kennen* joins knowing and recognising in one word. Interpreting is asked as well.

### Common mistakes

A child mixes up scared and angry, or sad and tired, in a face. A child reads the other's feeling as the feeling it would have itself in that situation. A child reads only the face and misses what happened before.
