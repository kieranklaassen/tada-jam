## Official wording

zichzelf kunnen redden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

being able to manage by oneself

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child puts on its coat and shoes, opens its lunch box, goes to the toilet and washes its hands without help. When it cannot find the scissors it looks in the usual places and then asks. A child of 4 manages the daily routines with some help for zips and laces; a child of 6 manages them alone and solves small problems itself.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording is general: it names no task and no situation. Carrying out assignments or tasks independently is `Realiseren van gestelde (eigen) doelen / 4`.

### Common mistakes

A child waits for an adult to do it, because that is how it goes at home. A child refuses all help and cannot go on. A child asks for help before it has tried.
