## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing similarities and differences in people's views and *gewoonten* (habits or customs)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle children tell what they eat for breakfast, how they celebrate a birthday and what they do at bedtime, and a child hears what is the same as at home and what is not. The child stands by a picture of the thing he or she likes best and sees who else stands there. A child of 4 says "we do that too" or "we don't"; a child of 7 can name a view or habit of a classmate that differs from his or her own and asks why the other family does it that way.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is experiencing: meeting similarities and differences, not judging or explaining them. The statement names views (*opvattingen*) and *gewoonten*, a word that covers both personal habits and the customs of a group; it names no examples, cultures or religions.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often take what happens at home to be what everyone does, and call a different habit "wrong" or "strange". They notice differences and leave out similarities. They also treat a view as a fact ("peas are horrible").
