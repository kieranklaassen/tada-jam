## Official wording

zich niet uit het veld laten slaan door onverwachte dingen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

not letting oneself be disconcerted by unexpected things

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The gym lesson is cancelled, the glue is used up or it starts to rain during outdoor play, and a child grumbles briefly and then picks something else. Its drawing tears and it tapes the tear and goes on. A child of 4 picks something else once an adult has named the change; a child of 6 recovers by itself from a small change.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no kind of unexpected thing. It is about staying steady; reacting appropriately to an unexpected situation is `Beheersen van eigen gevoelens / 6`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the next step: a child stays with what should have happened ("but we were going to do gym") and cannot start something else. A small change late in the day upsets a child more than the same change in the morning.
