## Official wording

oog hebben voor je eigen en andermans behoeften, wensen, kwaliteiten, zwakheden en uitdagingen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

being attentive to your own and other people's needs, wishes, qualities, weaknesses and challenges

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While the children divide the tasks for a shared building a child says "you are good at drawing, you make the sign; I find cutting hard, will you help me?". It notices that a classmate needs more time and waits. A child of 4 notices its own wishes and a visible need of another, such as a child who cannot reach; a child of 6 names what it and another are good at and find hard.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names five things: needs, wishes, qualities, weaknesses and challenges, for the child itself and for others. It asks for attention to them and names no action.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out one side: they attend to their own needs and miss the other's, or the reverse. Many name weaknesses of others more easily than their own.
