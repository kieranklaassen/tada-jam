## Official wording

hebben van kennis over de anderen in de groep (kent alle kinderen bij naam)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

having knowledge about the others in the group (knows all the children by name)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child hands out the cups and says each child's name. It rolls a ball across the circle to a child and calls the name first, and tells who is absent today. A child of 4 who has just started school knows the names of a few children it plays with; a child of 6 knows every name and also things about the children, such as who has a baby sister.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name one thing: knowing all the children by name. The wording before the brackets is wider, knowledge about the others in the group, and does not say what knowledge. The size of the group is not stated.

### Common mistakes

A child knows the names of its friends and not those of quiet children. A child mixes up two children who look alike or whose names sound alike. A child knows a classmate by face and has no name for that child.
