## Official wording

ervaren van overeenkomsten en verschillen in gedrag van mensen

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing similarities and differences in people's behaviour

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sees that one classmate jumps into the gym hall and another waits at the door, and that both then join in. In the circle children take turns to show how they say hello or how they act when they are glad, and the others copy it. A child of 4 copies and notices "same as me"; a child of 6 can say how two children did it differently and that both ways are a greeting.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is experiencing, not judging or explaining. The statement names no kinds of behaviour and no groups of people.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often report only what is different and miss what is alike. They judge a different way of acting as naughty. They copy the behaviour of the child they admire and think that way is the right one.
