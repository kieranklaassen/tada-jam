## Official wording

beschrijven wat makkelijke en moeilijke situaties zijn

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing what easy and difficult situations are

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child tells "saying good morning is easy for me, and asking if I can join is hard". It sorts pictures of situations, such as sharing a toy, losing at a board game and saying sorry, into easy and hard for itself. A child of 4 calls a situation hard when it made it cry or angry; a child of 6 says what makes it hard and that another child may find it easy.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for describing and names both easy and difficult situations. It does not say for whom they are easy or difficult, and names no kind of situation. It sits under thinking about the consequences of one's own actions.

### Common mistakes

A child calls a situation it does not like "hard". A child thinks that what is hard for it is hard for everyone. A child describes what happened and not what made it hard.
