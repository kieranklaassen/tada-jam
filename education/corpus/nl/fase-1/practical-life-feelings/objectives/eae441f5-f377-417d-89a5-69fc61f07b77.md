## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child tells where screens and devices are in his or her day: a video call with a grandparent, a film on a tablet, a parent's phone, the scanner in the shop. In the circle the child talks about what to do when something on a screen is frightening or when a stranger asks something, and practises telling an adult. A child of 4 names the devices he or she uses and who is there at the time; a child of 7 can name something that goes well with a device and something that can go wrong, and says whom to ask for help.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is exploring. The brackets name impact, safety and dangers. The statement names no devices, apps or rules, sets no level of detail for the dangers, and does not ask that a child uses a device.

### Common mistakes

Children think what is on a screen is always true and that a person on a screen is who they say they are. They do not see that a photo or message, once sent, stays with the other person. They keep something frightening on a screen to themselves because they expect to lose screen time.
