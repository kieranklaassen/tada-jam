## Official wording

ontdekken dat er verschillen in opvattingen bestaan

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that differences in views exist

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The teacher asks "is a spider scary or nice?" and the children stand on two sides of a line; a child sees that its friend stands on the other side. It hears that one child thinks rain is fun and another does not, and that both may think so. A child of 4 is surprised that someone thinks differently; a child of 6 says "you think that, I think this" and goes on playing with the other.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discovering. The statement names no subject on which views differ and does not ask the child to weigh the views. Giving one's own opinion is `Beheersen van eigen gevoelens / 3`.

### Common mistakes

A child thinks there is one right view and the other child is wrong. A child changes its view to that of its friend. A child takes a different view as a sign that the other does not like it.
