## Official wording

omgaan met zichzelf (zelfsturing)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with oneself (self-direction)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child chooses an activity from the choice board, fetches what it needs, works, and puts the things back. When it notices that it is getting restless it asks for a break. A child of 4 does this with a fixed routine and reminders; a child of 6 plans a small task, stays with it and corrects itself.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording is general: the brackets name self-direction and nothing more is said. The neighbouring statements under this sub-heading, such as `Beheersen van eigen gevoelens / 7` and `Beheersen van eigen gevoelens / 8`, name parts of it.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out stopping to check: a child goes from one thing to the next without finishing, or waits for an adult to say what to do.
