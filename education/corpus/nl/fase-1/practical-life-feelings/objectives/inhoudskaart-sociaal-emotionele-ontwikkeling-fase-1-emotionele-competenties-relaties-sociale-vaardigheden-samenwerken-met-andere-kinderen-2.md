## Official wording

initiatief nemen tot samenwerken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking the initiative to work together

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "shall we build the garage together?" or picks up the other end of a box without being asked. It invites a child who stands watching: "you can do the roof". A child of 4 starts working together by joining in or handing something over; a child of 6 asks, proposes a plan and says who could do what.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about starting: the child is the one who proposes. It does not say with how many children or for what task.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the asking: they wait until the teacher pairs them, or they start by telling others what to do. Some take the initiative only towards one fixed friend.
