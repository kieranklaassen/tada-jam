## Official wording

openstaan voor gevoelens en wensen van anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

being open to feelings and wishes of others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child stops its play and looks when another child says "I want to tell you something". It lets a child explain why it is sad and does not walk away, and it listens when a friend wants to play something else. A child of 4 is open to another's feeling when it is shown clearly and when it wants nothing itself at that moment; a child of 6 also listens when the other wants something different from what it wants.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about being open; taking feelings and wishes of others into account is `Herkennen en perspectief nemen op het gedrag van een ander / 3`. It names feelings and wishes of others and does not say which others.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out attention for the other when their own wish is strong. A child may be open to a friend and not to a child it plays with less.
