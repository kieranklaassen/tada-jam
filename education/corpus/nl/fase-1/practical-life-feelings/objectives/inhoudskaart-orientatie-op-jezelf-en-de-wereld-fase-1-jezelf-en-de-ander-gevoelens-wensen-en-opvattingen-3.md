## Official wording

ervaren van overeenkomsten en verschillen in opvattingen en gewoonten van mensen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing similarities and differences in people's views and *gewoonten* (habits or customs)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Children bring a photo or an object from home and tell about a meal, a feast day or a bedtime habit. A child puts his or her picture with those of children who do the same and sees who is there too and who is somewhere else. At snack time the child looks at and tastes what others brought. A child of 4 says what happens at home; a child of 6 says "at my home too" or "at my home it is different" and asks a question about it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is experiencing: meeting similarities and differences, not judging or explaining them. The statement names views (*opvattingen*) and *gewoonten*, a word that covers both personal habits and the customs of a group; it names no examples, cultures or religions.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often think that every home is like theirs, and say "that is not how you do it". They state a liking as a fact everyone shares. They see the differences and overlook what is the same.
