## Official wording

ontdekken dat een veilige leefomgeving vraagt om hygiënisch, nauwkeurig, veilig en zorgzaam handelen (bijv. met zorg en verstand omgaan met voorwerpen, materialen en gereedschappen en gevaren zien)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that a safe living environment calls for acting hygienically, precisely, safely and with care (for example handling objects, materials and tools with care and good sense, and seeing dangers)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At the workbench a child clamps the wood before sawing, keeps fingers away from the nail and puts the hammer back on its hook. The child washes hands before baking, wipes up spilled paint water, and tells the teacher about broken glass in the sandpit without picking it up. A child of 4 does this when shown each time; a child of 6 remembers the steps for a known tool and points out a danger, such as a wet floor, to others.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discovering that a safe environment calls for this. The statement names four ways of acting: hygienic, precise, safe and caring. The brackets give an example (*bijv.*): handling objects, materials and tools with care and good sense, and seeing dangers. The statement names no particular tools or dangers and states no safety condition such as adult supervision.

### Common mistakes

Children think a tool is safe because they used it once without harm. They see a danger for themselves and not for others, for example they leave open scissors on a chair. They wash or wipe quickly to be done, and leave out the soap or leave the floor wet.
