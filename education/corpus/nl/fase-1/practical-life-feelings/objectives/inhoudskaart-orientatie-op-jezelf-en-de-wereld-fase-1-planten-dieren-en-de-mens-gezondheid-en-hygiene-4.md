## Official wording

zorg dragen voor en respectvol omgaan met de eigen gezondheid (lichamelijke en psychische)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking care of and dealing respectfully with one's own health (physical and mental)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child stops running to drink, takes off a jumper when hot, and sits down when out of breath. The child tells the teacher "my tummy hurts" or "I feel sad" and chooses the quiet corner, a cuddly toy or a talk. A child of 4 shows discomfort by crying or going quiet and needs an adult to find out what it is; a child of 6 says what is wrong and names one thing that helps.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two things are named: taking care of one's own health and dealing with it respectfully; the brackets name physical and mental health. The statement names no behaviour, food, exercise or sleep, and sets no level of detail for mental health.

### Common mistakes

Children play on while thirsty, cold or in need of the toilet, and notice too late. They say "tummy ache" for worry, hunger and real pain alike. They think only the body can be unwell and do not mention feeling sad or afraid.
