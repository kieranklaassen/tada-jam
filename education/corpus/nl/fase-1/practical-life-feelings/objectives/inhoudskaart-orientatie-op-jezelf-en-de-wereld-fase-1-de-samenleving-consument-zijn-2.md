## Official wording

verkennen van de rol van geld in onze samenleving (sparen, ruilen, lenen en kopen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring the role of money in our society (saving, swapping, *lenen* (borrowing or lending) and buying)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child drops coins into a class money box for a shared goal, such as seeds for the garden, and looks in to see it fill. The child swaps a sticker for a marble with a classmate, borrows a book from the class library and brings it back, and buys play fruit in the shop corner. A child of 4 does the swapping and paying in play; a child of 6 uses the words saving, swapping, borrowing and buying and can say which one he or she just did.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is exploring. The brackets name saving, swapping, *lenen* and buying; the Dutch *lenen* means both borrowing and lending, and the statement does not choose. It names no coins, notes or amounts and no way of paying. This statement states no number range.

### Common mistakes

Children treat a borrowed thing as a gift and are upset when it has to go back. They want a swap undone after it is made, or think a swap is fair whenever each child gets one thing. They think money is always there: a parent gets more from a machine or a card.
