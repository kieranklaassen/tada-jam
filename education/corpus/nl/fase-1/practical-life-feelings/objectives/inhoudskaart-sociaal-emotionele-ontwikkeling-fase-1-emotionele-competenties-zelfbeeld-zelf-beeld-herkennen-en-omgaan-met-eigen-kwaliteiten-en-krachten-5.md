## Official wording

gebruik maken (op originele wijze) van verworven vaardigheden en inzichten

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

making use (in an original way) of acquired skills and insights

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child who has learned to fold a paper boat folds a hat from the same folds. It uses the counting it knows to share out the cups, or builds a ramp for marbles with what it found out about rolling cars. A child of 4 repeats what it learned in the same form; a child of 6 uses it in a new place or combines two things it can do.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets add "in an original way": the child uses what it has learned in a way of its own. The statement does not say which skills or insights.

### Common mistakes

A child uses a skill only in the situation where it learned it. A child copies another child's idea exactly. A child stops when its own idea does not work the first time.
