## Official wording

samen spelen, samenwerken en omgaan met elkaar (met diversiteit in bijv. sociaal-culturele achtergronden) (b)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

playing together, working together and interacting with one another (with diversity in, for example, social and cultural backgrounds) (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child lays a floor puzzle with two classmates and hands over a piece that fits on the other side. In the home corner the child cooks with a classmate who shows a dish from home, and they set the table for both dishes. In a circle activity the child waits while each child gets a turn. A child of 4 plays beside others and joins in for a short time; a child of 6 plays a shared plan with two or three children, including children he or she seldom chooses, and waits for a turn.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card marks this as an aspect of citizenship (b). Three things are named: playing together, working together and interacting with one another. The brackets add diversity, with social and cultural backgrounds as an example (*bijv.*); no other kind of diversity is named. The statement names no group size and no tasks.

### Common mistakes

Children play only with the same friend and say "you can't join". They take all the pieces or the main role and leave nothing for the other child. They stay away from a child whose language or habits they do not know.
