## Official wording

herkennen wat iemand leuk/niet leuk vindt

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising what someone likes/does not like

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sees that its friend pulls back when tickled and stops. It picks the dinosaur book for a classmate "because he always takes that one" and notices that another child frowns at loud singing. A child of 4 knows what a close friend likes from having seen it often; a child of 6 reads from face and reaction in the moment whether someone likes what is happening.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for recognising and names both liking and not liking. It does not say how the child recognises it or who the someone is.

### Common mistakes

A child assumes that others like what it likes itself. A child takes laughing for liking, also when the other laughs out of unease. A child does not hear a soft "no" or see a turning away as not liking.
