## Official wording

herkennen van veilige en onveilige situaties

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising safe and unsafe situations

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sees scissors lying open on the floor and tells the teacher, or stops at the top of the slide because a child still sits at the bottom. It points out on the playground where it may climb and where not. A child of 4 recognises dangers it was told about, such as hot, sharp and high; a child of 6 also judges a new situation and says why it is unsafe.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for recognising and names both safe and unsafe. It names no kind of situation and states no safety condition. An unsafe situation with another person is `Herkennen en perspectief nemen op het gedrag van een ander / 6`.

### Common mistakes

A child takes a situation for safe because an adult is near or because nothing went wrong before. A child knows the rule ("do not run with scissors") and does not recognise the danger in a new form. A child takes everything unfamiliar for unsafe.
