## Official wording

ervaren dat mensen in een groep van elkaar afhankelijk zijn en elkaar nodig hebben (onder andere samen spelen en samenwerken) (b)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing that people in a group depend on one another and need one another (among other things playing together and working together) (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the gym a child holds one side of a mat that needs four carriers, and the mat does not move when one lets go. In the home corner a child who plays the baker needs someone to buy the bread, and a child with the train track needs the classmate who holds the bridge pieces. A child of 4 feels that it does not work alone and calls for someone; a child of 6 says "we did it together" and can name what each one did.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card marks this as an aspect of citizenship (b). The verb is experiencing. The brackets name playing together and working together, introduced by "among other things" (*onder andere*), so they are not a complete list. The statement names no group size and no tasks.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often say "I did it" and leave out the others. They want to carry, build or decide alone and get angry when it fails. They think only the child who leads the play matters and that the other roles are not needed.
