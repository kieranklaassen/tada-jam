## Official wording

rekening houden met gevoelens en wensen van anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking feelings and wishes of others into account

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child asks "which colour do you want?" before it hands out the cups. It agrees to play what the other child wants first and what it wants itself afterwards, and stops chasing a child who says it is scared. A child of 4 does so when reminded and when it costs little; a child of 6 gives up part of its own wish to meet the other's.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names feelings and wishes. Being open to them is `Herkennen, begrijpen van en aanpassen aan emoties van anderen / 1`; this statement is about taking them into account. It does not say that the child sets its own wishes aside.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out asking: they guess what the other wants from what they want themselves. Some always do what the other wants and leave out their own wishes.
