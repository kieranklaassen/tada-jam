## Official wording

aanpassen aan nieuwe situaties

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

adapting to new situations

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child gets a substitute teacher, a new place in the circle or a different gym hall, looks at how things go and joins in. On the first school trip it stays with its partner and follows the new routine. A child of 4 watches first from beside a familiar adult and then joins in; a child of 6 asks what will happen and joins in.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no kind of new situation and does not say whether an adult prepares the child. Unexpected situations and things are covered by `Beheersen van eigen gevoelens / 6` and `Beheersen van eigen gevoelens / 10`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out watching first: a child refuses to take part or cries, or it behaves as in the old situation where the new one asks for something else. A child may seem to adapt during the day and react with tiredness or anger afterwards.
