## Official wording

bewust worden dat de mens invloed heeft op de natuurlijke omgeving

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

becoming aware that humans have an influence on the natural environment (*natuurlijke omgeving*, natural surroundings)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the schoolyard a child sees a bare strip of earth across the grass where children walk, and grass still growing beside it. With the class the child pushes flower bulbs into the soil and later finds the plants standing where they were put, or hangs up bird food and watches the birds that come to it. A child of 4 points to the birds on the feeding table and tells that the class put the food there; a child of 6 stands by the bare strip and tells that this is where the children walk.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is becoming aware, not explaining and not acting. The wording says only that humans have an influence: it names no kind of influence, no cause and no example, and does not say whether the influence helps or harms. Its subject is *de mens*, humans in general; it does not single out the child or any group. The Dutch *natuurlijke omgeving* can be read as the natural surroundings or as the natural environment, and the wording names no place and no scale.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out what nobody did on purpose: they count planting and mowing as something people do, and not the grass that wears away where people walk. Many do not tell apart what people made or planted and what came without people, and say that people made a hill or a river, or that a hedge grew in a straight row by itself.
