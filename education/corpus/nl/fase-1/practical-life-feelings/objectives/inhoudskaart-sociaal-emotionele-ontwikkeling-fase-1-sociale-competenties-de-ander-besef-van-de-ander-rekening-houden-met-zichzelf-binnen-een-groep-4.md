## Official wording

ontdekken dat mensen deel uitmaken van verschillende groepen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that people are part of different groups

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child lists the groups it belongs to: its family, its class, its swimming lesson, its street. The children stand up when a group is called ("everyone who has a sister", "everyone who walks to school") and see that the groups overlap. A child of 4 knows its family and its class; a child of 6 sees that one person is in several groups at once and that the teacher is also someone's mother.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discovering. The statement says people, not only the child. It names no groups.

### Common mistakes

A child thinks a person belongs to one group only, for example that the teacher lives at school. A child takes a group for fixed and thinks that nobody can join or leave. A child sees its own groups and not that others have other groups.
