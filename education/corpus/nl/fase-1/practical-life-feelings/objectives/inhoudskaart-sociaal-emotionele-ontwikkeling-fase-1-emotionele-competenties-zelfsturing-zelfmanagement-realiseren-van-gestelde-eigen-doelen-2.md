## Official wording

geconcentreerd bezig zijn bij uitvoeren van een taak of bij het spelen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

being occupied in a concentrated way while carrying out a task or while playing

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child threads beads in a pattern and does not look up when another child walks past. In the house corner it stays in its play for a long time and brings its story to an end. A child of 4 stays with something it chose itself; a child of 6 stays with a given task and returns to it after an interruption.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names two settings: carrying out a task and playing. It names no length of time.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out returning to the work after a disturbance. A child who concentrates well in play it chose may not do so in a task it was given.
