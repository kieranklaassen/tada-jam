## Official wording

verzamelen van informatie om keuze te (kunnen) maken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gathering information in order to (be able to) make a choice

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before it chooses an activity a child looks at the choice board to see where there is room, walks past the tables to see what is laid out, and asks "what are you making?". It tastes two kinds of fruit before it says which it wants. A child of 4 looks at what is in front of it and chooses; a child of 6 asks a question or looks at more options first.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The word in brackets widens it: gathering information to make a choice, or to be able to make one. The statement names no kind of choice and no source of information.

### Common mistakes

A child picks the first thing it sees. A child chooses what its friend chooses without looking. A child keeps looking and asking and does not come to a choice.
