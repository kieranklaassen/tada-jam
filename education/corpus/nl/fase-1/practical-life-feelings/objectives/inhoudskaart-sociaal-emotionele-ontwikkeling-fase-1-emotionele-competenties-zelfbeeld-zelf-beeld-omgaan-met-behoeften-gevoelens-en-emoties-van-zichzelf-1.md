## Official wording

(her)kennen van eigen emoties en gevoelens

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

knowing/recognising one's own emotions and feelings

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child feels its fists tighten and its face get hot and notices "I am angry". It points at the face on a feelings chart that fits how it feels this morning. A child of 4 recognises happy, angry, sad and scared in itself when an adult helps it look; a child of 6 notices the feeling itself and tells apart, for example, angry and disappointed.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The Dutch *(her)kennen* joins knowing and recognising in one word. The statement is about the child's own emotions and feelings and names none. Naming feelings is `Omgaan met behoeften, gevoelens en emoties van zichzelf / 2`.

### Common mistakes

A child feels the body signal, such as a stomach ache or tears, and does not link it to a feeling. A child takes every unpleasant feeling for anger. A child notices the feeling only after it has acted on it.
