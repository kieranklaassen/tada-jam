## Official wording

luisteren naar elkaar, bijv. om de gevoelens en het perspectief van anderen te bepalen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to each other, e.g. in order to determine the feelings and the perspective of others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children who each want to play something different tell in turn what they want and why, and each repeats what the other said. A child asks "are you sad?" and waits for the answer. A child of 4 listens to one sentence and reacts to the feeling it hears; a child of 6 can say back what the other wants and that it differs from what it wants itself.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The part after "e.g." is an example of what the listening is for: finding out what others feel and how they see something. The statement sits under working together with other children. It does not say how long or to how many others.

### Common mistakes

A child hears the other and takes its own wish for the other's ("he wants that too"). A child waits for its turn to speak without taking in what was said. A child attends to the words and misses the feeling in the voice.
