gaining experience with simple philosophical conversations

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a picture book the teacher asks a question such as "is a cuddly toy alive?" and a child says yes or no and tries to tell why. The child listens to a classmate who says the opposite and sometimes changes sides. The child walks to the "yes" side or the "no" side of the circle to show what he or she thinks. A child of 4 answers from what he or she has seen ("mine sleeps in my bed"); a child of 6 gives a reason and reacts to another child's reason.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording is gaining experience, so what is described is taking part, not a level of reasoning. The conversations are called simple; the statement names no questions, topics, length or group size.

### Common mistakes

Children wait for the teacher to say who is right. They answer with a story about home that does not answer the question. They change their answer to match a friend's, not because of a reason.
