## Official wording

(samen)werken met andere kinderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

working (together) with other children

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children build one road of blocks, one fetching and one laying. At the table several children each do their own puzzle side by side and pass pieces to each other. A child of 4 works beside other children on its own piece; a child of 6 divides the work with one or two others and talks about what comes next.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets make the statement cover both working with other children and working together with them. It names no group size and no kind of task. The other statements under this sub-heading name parts of working together, such as `Samenwerken met andere kinderen / 3` on a shared assignment.

### Common mistakes

A child directs the others and decides everything. A child works on its own part and does not look at what the others make. A child lets the others do the work and watches.
