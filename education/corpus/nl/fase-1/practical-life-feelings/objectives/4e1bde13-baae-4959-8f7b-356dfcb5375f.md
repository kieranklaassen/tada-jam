## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring the role of money in our society (saving, swapping, *lenen* (borrowing or lending) and buying)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child puts coins in a money box and counts whether there is enough for something wanted. The child swaps one toy card for another, borrows a pencil and gives it back, and pays with play money in the class shop. A child of 4 swaps and pays in play and knows that a borrowed thing goes back; a child of 7 can tell the four apart and says that saving means waiting to buy later.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is exploring. The brackets name saving, swapping, *lenen* and buying; the Dutch *lenen* means both borrowing and lending, and the statement does not choose. It names no amounts, coins or notes and no way of paying. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

Children mix up borrowing and getting: a borrowed thing is treated as theirs. They think a swap is fair when the number of things is equal, whatever the things are worth. They think money in a money box grows by itself or that a bank card holds money without end.
