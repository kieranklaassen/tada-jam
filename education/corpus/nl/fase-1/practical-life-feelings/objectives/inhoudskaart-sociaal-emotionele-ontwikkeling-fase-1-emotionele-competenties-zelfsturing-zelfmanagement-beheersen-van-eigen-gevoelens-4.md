## Official wording

reageren op verbaal en non-verbaal geuite gevoelens van anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reacting to verbally and non-verbally expressed feelings of others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child hears "I am sad" and sits down next to the other, or sees a child with a bent head and tears and fetches the teacher. It stops tickling when the other says "I do not like this" or pulls back. A child of 4 reacts to clear signs such as crying and to words said to it; a child of 6 also reacts to a face or posture and asks what is the matter.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names two ways in which feelings are expressed: in words and without words. It asks for a reaction and does not say which. The card places it under controlling one's own feelings.

### Common mistakes

A child reacts to words and misses a face or posture. A child goes on with what it does after the other said "stop". A child laughs or stares at a crying child because it does not know what to do.
