## Official wording

benoemen hoe je een relatie laat voortduren

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

naming how you make a relationship last

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says what friends do to stay friends: "you play together again", "you say sorry after a fight", "you share". It makes a drawing for a friend who was ill and tells why. A child of 4 names one thing, mostly playing together; a child of 6 also names making peace after a quarrel and keeping a promise.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks for naming, not for doing. It does not say which kind of relationship and names no ways. Entering into a relationship is `Leren omgaan met een ander / 9`.

### Common mistakes

A child thinks a friendship is over after one quarrel ("you are not my friend any more"). A child thinks staying friends means always doing what the friend wants. A child names only being together and leaves out making peace after a quarrel.
