## Official wording

inzien dat thuis, op school en op straat verschillende regels nodig zijn

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

understanding that different rules are needed at home, at school and in the street

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child tells that at home it may eat on the couch and at school it eats at the table, and that in the street it holds a hand and stops at the kerb. It sorts pictures of rules by place and says why one fits there. A child of 4 knows that the rules differ; a child of 6 gives a reason, such as "there are cars in the street" or "there are many children at school".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names three places: home, school and the street. It says that different rules are "needed" (*nodig*), not only that the rules differ, and it does not ask the child to give a reason for a rule. It does not say which rules.

### Common mistakes

A child brings the rule of home to school ("at home I may"). A child thinks that one of the places has the wrong rules.
