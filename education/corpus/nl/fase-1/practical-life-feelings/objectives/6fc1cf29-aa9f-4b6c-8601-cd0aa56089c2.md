## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing that people in a group depend on one another and need one another (among other things playing together and working together) (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children carry a bench that one cannot lift, and four hold the corners of a parachute cloth to keep a ball on it. In shop play a child finds that a shopkeeper needs a customer, and at tidy-up time that the room is ready sooner when everyone takes a part. A child of 4 feels this in the doing, for example that a seesaw needs two; a child of 7 can say who did which part and that the result needed all of them.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording ends with the printed marker (b), the curriculum institute's sign for an aspect of citizenship (*burgerschap*). The verb is experiencing. The brackets name playing together and working together, introduced by "among other things" (*onder andere*), so they are not a complete list. The statement names no group size and no tasks.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out the parts others did and say "I did it". They think needing help means not being able to do something, and refuse help. They count only friends as people they need.
