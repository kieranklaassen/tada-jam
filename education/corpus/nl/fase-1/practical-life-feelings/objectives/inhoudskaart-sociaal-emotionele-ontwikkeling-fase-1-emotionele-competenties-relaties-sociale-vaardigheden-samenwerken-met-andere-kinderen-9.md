## Official wording

ervaren dat mensen in een groep van elkaar afhankelijk zijn en elkaar nodig hebben

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - fase 1 (kleuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing that people in a group depend on each other and need each other

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Four children carry a heavy mat that none of them can lift alone. In the circle the ball stays on the parachute cloth only when every child holds an edge. A child of 4 experiences it in the moment, when something works only with help; a child of 6 can say afterwards "we needed everyone for that".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is experiencing: the child lives through it and is not asked to explain it. The statement says people in a group; it names no group and no size.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out what the others added: a child sees its own part as the reason that it worked. A child who wants to do everything alone refuses help that the task needs.
