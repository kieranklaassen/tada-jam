## Official wording

reageren op tekst met blijk van herkenning, waardering en eigen mening

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/LE/03/03; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau 817ea38a-e404-4276-8048-65fc49192c2e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

responding to text with signs of recognition, appreciation and an opinion of one's own

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

With an information book about pets the child says "we have a rabbit too, and it also eats carrots", "I like this page with the baby rabbits", and "I think rabbits should not live in a small cage". A child of 4 responds with a short call or by pointing to something they know; a child of 7 says what they recognise, what they like about the text and what they think of it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Three kinds of response are named: recognition, appreciation and an opinion of one's own. The goal's cluster is about non-fiction texts; the wording itself says only "text". It does not say whether the text is read aloud or read by the child, and it does not ask for reasons.

### Common mistakes

Telling a long experience of their own that no longer has to do with the text. Saying only "nice" or "boring". Repeating the opinion of a classmate.
