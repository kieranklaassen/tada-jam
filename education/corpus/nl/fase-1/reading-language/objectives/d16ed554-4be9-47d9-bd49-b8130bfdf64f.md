## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before a story is read aloud, the adult asks "What are we going to do, and what do you need for it?"; the child says "listen to a story", sits where the book can be seen and puts down what they are holding. The child looks at the cover and says what is already known about the subject. The child repeats what to listen for, for example "where the cat hides". A child of 4 gets ready with the adult's prompts, one step at a time; a child of 7 says beforehand what the task is and what they will do to follow it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start; the content-line document prints it at the end of the sentence. The statement does not say who helps or how. It does not say what the orientation consists of. NE/MT/02/09/02/fase1 is the goal about listening strategies and NE/MT/02/09/03/fase1 the goal about reflecting on the task.

### Common mistakes

Hearing "we are going to listen" as "sit still" and not finding out what to listen for. Starting a story of their own about the cover picture and missing the task. Having forgotten the listening question by the time the text starts.
