drawing and decorating words (*groep* 1/2, the first two years of primary school)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child copies their name or a word such as "mama" in large letters as a drawing: with a thick marker, in several colours, with dots, stripes or small flowers on the letters. The child lays a word with beads, clay or sticks. A child of 4 draws the shapes of a few letters of their own name; a child of 6 draws a whole word with its letters in order and decorates each letter without changing its shape.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do; the wording itself names the years, *groep* 1 and 2 (about ages 4 to 6). The open data ends the wording with "(groep 1/2)"; the content-line document prints the label "groep 1/2:" on a line above the statement instead. The object is words; the wording names no words and no letters. The verbs are drawing and decorating: the goal does not ask for letters written with a set starting point and direction, and it does not mention reading the words. For *groep* 3 the neighbouring goal NE/SCH/02/06/02/fase1 names dividing up the page and making illustrations.

### Common mistakes

Drawing letters mirrored or turned. Changing the order of the letters or leaving one out when copying. Covering a letter with decoration so that it can no longer be recognised.
