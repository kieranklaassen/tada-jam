## Official wording

kennismaken met eenvoudige technieken om informatie te onthouden (herlezen, onderstrepen, woordspin)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting to know simple techniques for remembering information (rereading, underlining, word web (*woordspin*, a word with related words around it))

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) reads a short text a second time and draws a line under a word they want to remember. The child writes or draws a word in the middle of a sheet and puts words that belong to it around it, joined by lines. Afterwards the child tells from the sheet what the text said.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The wording says getting to know: a first meeting with the techniques, not using them alone. The brackets name rereading, underlining and the word web. The statement calls the techniques simple and names no kind or length of text.

### Common mistakes

Underlining almost every word. Rereading by saying the words again without attending to what they say. Filling a word web with words that come to mind and are not in the text.
