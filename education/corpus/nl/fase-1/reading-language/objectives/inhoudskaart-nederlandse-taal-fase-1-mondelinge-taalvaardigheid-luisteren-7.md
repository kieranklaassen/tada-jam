## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

naming the theme/topic of spoken text

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a short story or an explanation the child answers "what was it about?" with a word or a short sentence: "about a bear who can't sleep", "about the fire brigade". The child picks, from a few objects or pictures on the table, the one that fits the story. A child of 4 names a character or an event; a child of 6 says what the whole text was about.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says theme/topic with a slash and does not separate the two. It is about spoken text and sets no kind or length of text. The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Naming the last or the most striking event in place of what the whole text was about. Retelling the full story when asked for the subject. Naming something from a picture that the spoken text did not deal with.
