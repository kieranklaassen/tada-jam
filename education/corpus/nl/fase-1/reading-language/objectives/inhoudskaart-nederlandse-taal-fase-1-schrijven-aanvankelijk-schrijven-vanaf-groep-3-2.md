## Official wording

ervaren dat schrijven is onder woorden brengen van gedachten, gevoelens en meningen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing that writing is putting thoughts, feelings and opinions into words

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) draws something that happened at the weekend and writes a sentence about what they thought of it. The child writes what they think of a book, starting with *ik vind* (I think), and reads it back to a classmate.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The wording says experiencing. It names three things that are put into words: thoughts, feelings and opinions. It sets no length or kind of text and does not mention spelling or handwriting; *ik vind* above is the pack's example.

### Common mistakes

Writing only the words they are sure how to spell, so that the text says less than they mean. Writing what happened and leaving out what they thought or felt about it. Copying a model sentence in place of their own thought.
