## Official wording

spelen met lettertekens (benoemen en schrijven)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

playing with letter characters (*lettertekens*) (naming and writing)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stamps letters, shapes them from clay, draws them in sand with a finger and picks magnetic letters from a box, saying which letter each one is. The child writes some of them with a thick pencil or chalk. A child of 4 names and copies the first letter of their own name; a child of 6 names and writes more letters, among them the letters of their own name.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says playing. The brackets name two activities: naming and writing. The statement does not say which letters or how many, capitals or small letters, or printed or joined writing, and it does not say whether naming is by the letter's name or by its sound. It does not mention how a letter is formed.

### Common mistakes

Writing a letter in mirror image. Mixing up letters that differ only in direction, such as b, d, p and q. Mixing capitals, small letters and numerals in one row as if they were one set.
