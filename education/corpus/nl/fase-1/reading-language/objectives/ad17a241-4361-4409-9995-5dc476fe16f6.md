## Official wording

(met hulp) reflecteren op de uitvoering van de luistertaak

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/MT/02/09; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau ad17a241-4361-4409-9995-5dc476fe16f6.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) reflecting on the carrying out of the listening task

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After an audio story the adult asks "Could you follow it? What helped you?", and the child says "yes, I looked at the pictures" or "no, it went too fast". The child shows with a thumb up, sideways or down how well the listening went and gives one reason. The child says whether the listening question was answered ("I know where the cat hid"). A child of 4 answers yes or no and names one thing after a prompt; a child of 7 says what made listening easy or hard and what to do next time, such as sitting closer or asking sooner.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start; the content-line document prints it at the end of the sentence. The statement does not say who helps or how. The reflection is on how the listening task was carried out, not on the content of the text; forming an opinion about a text is the subject of NE/MT/02/08/01/fase1. The statement names no points to reflect on and no form of reflection.

### Common mistakes

Saying whether the story was nice when asked how the listening went. Answering "good" every time, without an example. Naming only what others did ("they were noisy") and nothing about one's own listening.
