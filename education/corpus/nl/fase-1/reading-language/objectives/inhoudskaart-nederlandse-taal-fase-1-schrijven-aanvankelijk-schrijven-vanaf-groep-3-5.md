## Official wording

ervaring opdoen met het schrijven van korte zinnen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experience with writing short sentences

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) says a sentence aloud, counts its words on their fingers, and writes it word by word with a space after each word. The child reads the sentence back while pointing to each word. The child writes such a sentence under a drawing of their own.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The wording says gaining experience, and short sentences. It sets no number of words and does not mention capital letters, full stops or correct spelling; *de* under Common mistakes is the pack's example.

### Common mistakes

Writing the words joined together without spaces. Leaving out a word, most often a short one such as *de* (the). Forgetting the rest of the sentence while sounding out one word.
