## Official wording

kennis ontwikkelen over de opbouw van een tekst (met behulp van leesstrategieën)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

developing knowledge about the structure of a text (with the help of reading strategies)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) looks at a short text before reading it and points to the title, the pictures and the separate blocks of text. After reading, the child says what came first, what came next and how the text ended. The child lays sentence strips of a short story in order.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The wording says developing knowledge about the structure of a text, and the brackets say this happens with the help of reading strategies. It names no strategy and no kind of text; predicting, rereading and underlining are named in `Aanvankelijk lezen (vanaf GROEP 3) / 8`. The statement does not list terms for parts of a text that a child should know.

### Common mistakes

Treating a text as a row of separate sentences with no beginning, middle or end. Skipping the title and the pictures and starting at the first word. Telling the last thing read when asked how the text began.
