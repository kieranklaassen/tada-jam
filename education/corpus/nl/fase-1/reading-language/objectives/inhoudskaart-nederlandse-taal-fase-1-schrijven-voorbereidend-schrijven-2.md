## Official wording

schrijven van woorden met grote eenheden (bijv. eigen naam, logo's en merknamen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

writing words in large units (*met grote eenheden*) (e.g. own name, logos and brand names)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes their own name from memory as one known whole, and copies the name of a shop or a brand from a packet. A child of 4 writes some letters of their name, not always in order; a child of 6 writes their name and a few other known words as wholes, without sounding them out.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card does not explain "large units". The examples it gives are the child's own name, logos and brand names: words a child knows as a whole. They are examples, and the statement sets no number of words. It does not mention sounding out or correct spelling.

### Common mistakes

Leaving out letters of the name, or writing them in another order. Writing the name from right to left or with mirrored letters. Knowing a brand name only in its own colours and letter shapes and not in plain letters.
