## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before reading a page aloud the adult says "Listen for the name of the dog", and the child raises a hand when the name is heard. For an instruction of three steps the child listens to the end and repeats the steps on three fingers. For a story read for enjoyment the child sits back and listens without a task. A child of 4 can listen for one named thing in a short text; a child of 7 listens in different ways to a story, an instruction and an item of information, and says which way fits.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is "practising", so mastery is not stated. The statement names no ways of listening and no purposes; NE/MT/02/04/01/fase1 is the goal about becoming aware of listening purposes. It does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Calling out at the first word that resembles the one to listen for, without checking. Trying to remember every detail of a story meant for enjoyment, or listening to an instruction as to a story and missing the steps. Forgetting the listening question once the text becomes exciting.
