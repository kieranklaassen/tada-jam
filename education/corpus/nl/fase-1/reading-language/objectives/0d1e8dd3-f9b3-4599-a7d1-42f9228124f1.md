## Official wording

bladspiegel verdelen, illustraties bij tekst maken (groep 3)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/SCH/02/06; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau 0d1e8dd3-f9b3-4599-a7d1-42f9228124f1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dividing up the page (*bladspiegel*, the layout of a page), making illustrations to go with text (*groep* 3, the third year of primary school)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before writing, a child in *groep* 3 decides where the title, the text and the drawing will go on the sheet, and may mark the places with light pencil lines. The child writes from left to right on the lines, starts a new line when one is full, and makes a drawing that shows what the text says.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do; the wording itself names the year, *groep* 3 (about ages 6 to 7). The open data ends the wording with "(groep 3)"; the content-line document prints the label "groep 3:" on a line above the statement instead. Two things are named: dividing up the page, and making illustrations with a text. The wording does not say what a good division is and names no parts of a page, such as margin, title or lines. It does not mention handwriting or letter size.

### Common mistakes

Starting with large letters and having no room left, so the last words are written very small or up the side of the sheet. Filling the sheet with the drawing and leaving no room for the text. Making a drawing that does not match the text.
