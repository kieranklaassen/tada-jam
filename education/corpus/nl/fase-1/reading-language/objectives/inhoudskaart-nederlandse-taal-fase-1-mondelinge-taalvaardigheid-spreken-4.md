## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells about the weekend in full sentences with a subject and a verb: "I went to the swimming pool". The child joins two parts with "and", "but" or "because", and turns a statement into a question. A child of 4 speaks mostly in short main sentences; a child of 6 joins sentences and uses a sub-clause with "because" or "when".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording is two words and a term in brackets, syntax. It sets no sentence length and names no sentence types or rules of word order. It stands under Spreken (speaking); writing is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Using the word order of a main sentence in a Dutch sub-clause (*omdat ik ben ziek* for *omdat ik ziek ben*, because I am ill). Leaving out small words such as articles. Joining clause after clause with *en toen* (and then), so that no sentence ends.
