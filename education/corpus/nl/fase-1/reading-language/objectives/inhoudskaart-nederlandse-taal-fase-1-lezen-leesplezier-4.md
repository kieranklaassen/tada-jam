## Official wording

meeleven met personages in een op de leeftijd afgestemd boek

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

feeling along with characters in a book suited to the age

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While a book is read aloud, the child looks worried when the character is lost and glad when it is found. The child says how the character feels and plays the character afterwards with a doll or in the home corner. A child of 4 shows the feeling in face and body and names it; a child of 6 says why the character feels that way and what they would do in its place.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about characters in a book, and the book is one suited to the age. It names no books, no characters and no feelings. It does not say whether the book is read aloud or read by the child.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the characters beside the main one. Many name their own feeling about the story where the character's feeling is asked.
