## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After listening to a story the child answers "Who was it about?", "Where were they?" and "What happened?". The child picks the puppet that stands for the main character and puts it at the right place on a play mat, such as the forest or the house. From two or three picture cards of events the child points to the one that happened in the story. A child of 4 recognises the main character and one place with pictures within reach; a child of 7 recognises several characters, the places the story moves to and the main events, without pictures.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Three elements are named: person, place and event, each in the singular. The verb is "recognising"; labelling with words is the subject of NE/MT/02/06/01/fase1. The text is one that is listened to, since the open data lists the goal under listening (NE/MT/02). Time, cause and the feelings of characters are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Confusing two characters, or giving one character's action to another. Naming a place from the pictures or from the child's own life instead of the place in the story. Knowing who and where, and not being able to say what happened.
