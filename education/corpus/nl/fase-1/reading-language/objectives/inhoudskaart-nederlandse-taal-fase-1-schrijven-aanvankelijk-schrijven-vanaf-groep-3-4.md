## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

coming to enjoy writing

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In free time a child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) chooses to write: notes, lists, small folded books and letters for the class letterbox. The child shows what they wrote to others and reads it aloud.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The statement names enjoyment only. It names no kind of text and no amount of writing, and it does not mention spelling or handwriting.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often take writing to mean forming letters neatly and spelling without errors, and stop writing when they make a mistake.
