## Official wording

ontwikkelen van kennis van de functies van schrijven

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

developing knowledge of the functions of writing

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes a list in the play shop, puts their name on a drawing so that others know whose it is, posts a "letter" in the class letterbox and makes a sign for a block building. A child of 4 makes scribbles and says "this is a list"; a child of 6 writes their own name and some letters or words, and says what a list, a card or a sign is for.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says developing knowledge of the functions of writing. It lists no functions; the ones above are the pack's examples. It does not ask that the child writes real letters or words.

### Common mistakes

Seeing writing as making letter shapes and not as putting down a message. Telling a different message each time they are asked what their note says.
