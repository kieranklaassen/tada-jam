## Official wording

luisteren naar en begrijpen van de mening van anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to and understanding the opinion of others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears a classmate say that the book was scary and that he did not like it, and can say afterwards what that classmate thought. In a talk about which game to play outside, the child listens to two different wishes before giving their own. A child of 4 hears that another child likes or dislikes something; a child of 6 repeats another child's opinion with the reason that child gave.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording is about listening to and understanding what others think; forming an own opinion about a spoken text is a separate statement of the card (Luisteren / 11). It does not mention agreeing, disagreeing or responding. It names no topics.

### Common mistakes

Assuming that others think the same as they do, and being surprised or upset when they do not. Hearing a different opinion as a rejection of themselves. Taking an opinion for a fact ("it is a stupid book", because a friend said so).
