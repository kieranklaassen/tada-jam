## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) getting an orientation on the purpose of the reading (*groep* 3, the third year of primary school)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before reading, the teacher asks "why are we going to read this?" and a child in *groep* 3 answers: "to find out what hedgehogs eat", "to know how to fold the boat", or "because it is a nice story". The child then looks for that in the text and says afterwards whether they found it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do; the wording itself names the year, *groep* 3 (about ages 6 to 7). The open data ends the wording with "(groep 3)"; the content-line document prints no such tag and carries the year in the heading of the cluster, "Zakelijke teksten: aanpak (vanaf groep 3)" (from *groep* 3). The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start; the content-line document prints it after the verb. The goal names no reading purposes; those above are the pack's examples. Its cluster is about non-fiction texts. It does not say that the child chooses a way of reading to fit the purpose.

### Common mistakes

Starting to read at once without thinking about why. Forgetting the question during the reading and telling afterwards about a detail they liked. Giving "because the teacher said so" as the only purpose.
