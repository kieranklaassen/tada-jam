## Official wording

luisteren naar en begrijpen van prentenboeken, verhalen, liedjes, gedichten, (toneel)voorstellingen, belevenissen van volwassene of kind, informatie over concrete onderwerpen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to and understanding picture books, stories, songs, poems, (theatre) performances, experiences of an adult or a child, information about concrete topics

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sits with the group while a picture book is read aloud, looks at the pictures, and afterwards says what happened to the main character. The child joins in a song with the matching movements, watches a puppet show and tells who was in it, and listens to a classmate telling about a visit to grandmother. A child of 4 follows a short story with pictures and reacts to single events; a child of 6 follows a longer story told without pictures and retells it in order.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording lists what is listened to: picture books, stories, songs, poems, performances ("theatre" stands in brackets), things an adult or a child has experienced, and information about concrete topics. The list has no "e.g."; the statement does not say that it is complete. It sets no length or difficulty of text. The information named is about concrete topics; abstract topics are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Following the pictures and not the spoken text, so that what the text adds is missed. Remembering separate events without the link between them (what caused what). Taking a joke or a non-literal expression in a poem or song literally.
