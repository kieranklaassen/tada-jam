developing knowledge of the functions of writing

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sees the teacher write a list so as not to forget, a name on a drawing to show whose it is, and a letter to tell something to someone who is not there. The child does the same in play: a shopping list, a ticket, a sign for the hut. A child of 4 knows that writing "says something" and asks an adult to write things down; a child of 7 says what a text is for (to remember, to tell, to ask) and chooses to write for those uses.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names no functions of writing; those above are the pack's examples. It is about knowledge of what writing is for; it does not mention forming letters or spelling. Experiencing that written language has communicative purposes is worded separately in NE/SCH/02/01/01/fase1.

### Common mistakes

Thinking that writing and drawing are the same thing. Thinking that what is written changes when another person reads it or when it is read on another day. Seeing writing only as a school task and not as a way to tell or remember something.
