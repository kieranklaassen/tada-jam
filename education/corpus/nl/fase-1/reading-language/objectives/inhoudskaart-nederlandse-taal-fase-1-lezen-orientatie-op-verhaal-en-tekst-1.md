## Official wording

ontwikkelen van kennis over de functies van lezen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

developing knowledge about the functions of reading

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sees an adult read a recipe before baking, a list in the shop, a card from a grandparent and a book for pleasure. In play the child uses print the same way: "reads" the menu in the play restaurant and looks for their name above a coat hook. A child of 4 knows that the label on a drawer tells whose it is; a child of 6 says what a person reads a recipe, a letter or a story book for.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says developing knowledge about the functions of reading. It lists no functions; the ones above are the pack's examples. It does not ask that the child can read.

### Common mistakes

Thinking that the story comes from the pictures and not from the print. Thinking that reading is only reading a story book aloud. Not seeing print outside books, such as on signs and packets, as something that is read.
