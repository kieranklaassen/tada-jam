## Official wording

ontwikkelen van leesplezier vanuit een rijke leesomgeving

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

developing enjoyment of reading from a rich reading environment

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child goes to the book corner, picks a book, turns the pages and looks at the pictures. The child "reads" the book to a doll or a classmate and asks an adult to read it again. A child of 4 looks at the pictures and names what is in them; a child of 6 follows the lines with a finger and reads their own name and a few known words.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says developing enjoyment of reading, from a rich reading environment. It does not say what makes the environment rich, and it names no books, no kinds of text and no amount of reading. It does not ask that the child can read.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out variety: many take the same book every time, or turn the pages quickly without looking at them.
