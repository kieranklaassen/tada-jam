## Official wording

deelnemen aan woord- en klankspelletjes

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking part in word games and sound games

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle the child joins in spoken games: finding something in the room that begins with a given sound, clapping the parts of each child's name, saying which of three words sounds different, and making up nonsense words that rhyme. A child of 4 joins in by repeating and clapping; a child of 6 finds a word with a given first sound and says which sound a word ends with.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says taking part. It names word games and sound games and does not say which; the games above are the pack's examples. The statement does not mention letters or written words.

### Common mistakes

Answering by meaning and not by sound, for example giving another animal where a word with the same first sound is asked. Naming a thing in sight without checking its first sound. Repeating the answer of the child before.
