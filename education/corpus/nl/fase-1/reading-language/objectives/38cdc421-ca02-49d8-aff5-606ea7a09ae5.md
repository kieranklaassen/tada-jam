## Official wording

(met hulp) aanpassen van tekst n.a.v. reacties van publiek (klasgenootjes)(groep 3)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/SCH/02/04; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau 38cdc421-ca02-49d8-aff5-606ea7a09ae5.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) adapting a text in response to reactions from the audience (classmates) (*groep* 3, the third year of primary school)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 reads their short text aloud to two classmates, who ask "who is 'he'?" and "what happened then?". With the teacher's help the child adds the name and one more sentence, and reads the text to them again.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do; the wording itself names the year, *groep* 3 (about ages 6 to 7). The open data ends the wording with "(groep 3)"; the content-line document prints the label "groep 3:" on a line above the statement instead. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start; the content-line document prints "met hulp" at the end of the sentence, without brackets. The audience is named in brackets: classmates. The wording does not say what is adapted, content or form, and sets no number of changes. Experimenting with fitting a text to an audience is worded for *groep* 1/2 in NE/SCH/02/04/01/fase1.

### Common mistakes

Answering the classmate's question aloud and leaving the text as it was. Hearing a question as disapproval and not wanting to change anything. Changing only how the text looks (neater letters) and not what it says.
