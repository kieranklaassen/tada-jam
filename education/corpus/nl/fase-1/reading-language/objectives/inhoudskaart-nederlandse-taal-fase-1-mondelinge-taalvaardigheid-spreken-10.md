## Official wording

(met hulp) herkennen van en afstemmen op het publiek

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) recognising and adjusting to the audience

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before telling, the child answers the teacher's question "who are you telling this to, and do they know your grandfather?". The child tells the same event once to a friend who was there and once to classmates who were not, and with help adds for the second group who the people are. A child of 4 tells in the same way to everyone; a child of 6, with a prompt, explains more to listeners who were not there.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording puts "with help" in brackets at the front. It has two verbs: recognising the audience and adjusting to it. It does not say who the audience is; the card's statement Spreken / 1 speaks of a familiar audience. It does not say what is adjusted (content, words or voice).

### Common mistakes

Telling as if every listener had been present, with "he" and "there" left unexplained. Using the same volume for one friend and for a whole group. Naming, with help, who the listeners are, but changing nothing in the telling.
