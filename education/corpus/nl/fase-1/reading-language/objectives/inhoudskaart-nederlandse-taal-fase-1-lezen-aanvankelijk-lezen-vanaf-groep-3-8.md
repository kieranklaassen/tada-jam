## Official wording

voorspellen waar de tekst over gaat en herlezen en onderstrepen, met name om nog een keer te genieten van de tekst en om te onthouden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

predicting what the text is about and rereading and underlining, in particular to enjoy the text once more and to remember

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before reading, a child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) looks at the title and the picture and says what they think the text will be about; after reading, the child says whether that was right. The child reads a part they liked a second time. The child draws a line under a word or a sentence they want to remember.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The statement names three actions: predicting what the text is about, rereading and underlining. It gives two purposes, introduced by "in particular": enjoying the text once more, and remembering. It names no kind or length of text and does not say what is underlined.

### Common mistakes

Predicting from the picture alone and keeping to that idea after the text says something else. Saying what they would like the text to be about, without looking at the title. Underlining a great deal without being able to say why.
