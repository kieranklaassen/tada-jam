## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Telling the class about a fish that was caught, the child shows its length between two hands. Retelling a story, the child makes the giant's angry face and stamps a foot at the right moment. Explaining the way to the gym, the child points left and right while saying the words. A child of 4 acts things out with the whole body and sometimes leaves the words out; a child of 7 continues talking while hands and face add size, direction and feeling.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Gestures and facial expression are named, and their role is to support the message, not to replace it. The open data lists the goal under speaking (NE/MT/03); NE/MT/01/07/01/fase1 is the matching goal for conversations. The statement does not say which gestures or how often.

### Common mistakes

Showing something with the hands and saying only "like this", so that the words are missing. Standing stiffly with the hands behind the back or in front of the mouth when speaking to a group. Making a face that does not match what is told, such as laughing from nervousness while telling something sad.
