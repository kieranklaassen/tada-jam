## Official wording

naspelen of navertellen van een voorgelezen verhaal (met of zonder illustraties)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

acting out or retelling a story that has been read aloud (with or without illustrations)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child plays the story with dolls or dressing-up clothes, takes a role and says the words of a character. Or the child tells the story again while turning the pages of the book. A child of 4 plays a scene, or tells what is on each picture; a child of 6 retells the beginning, the middle and the end in order without the book.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names acting out or retelling, either one, of a story that has been read aloud. The brackets say with or without illustrations. It sets no length for the story and does not say how complete the retelling is.

### Common mistakes

Telling only the part they liked most, or only the end. Mixing up the order of the events. Naming what is on each picture without linking one event to the next.
