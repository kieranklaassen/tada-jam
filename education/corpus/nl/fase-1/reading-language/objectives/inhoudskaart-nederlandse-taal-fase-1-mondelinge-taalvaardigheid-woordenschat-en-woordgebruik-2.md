## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child uses a word that was new last week while playing in the shop corner: "I need the scales". The child names objects, actions and properties when telling about a drawing, and tries a new word in a sentence of their own. A child of 4 uses new words mostly right after hearing them and with the object present; a child of 6 uses them days later, in a new sentence and without the object.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Productive vocabulary is the words a child uses when speaking; the words a child understands are a separate statement of the card (Woordenschat en woordgebruik / 1). The wording sets no number of words and names no word list or themes.

### Common mistakes

Understanding far more words than the child uses, and saying "that" and "thing" where a known word would fit. Using a new word with a wrong or too wide meaning, for example "scales" for the till. Saying a new long word with a syllable missing or swapped, so that listeners do not recognise it.
