## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child talks with the teacher, with a classmate at the sand table, with an older pupil who comes to read aloud, and with a visitor such as a parent who tells about their work. The child asks the school caretaker for a broom and says thank you. A child of 4 talks most easily with one familiar adult or one friend and may stay silent with a stranger; a child of 7 talks with children from other classes and with unfamiliar adults, and greets and addresses them in a way that fits.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording is "coming into contact with": the school brings the child together with different partners, and the statement does not say what the child does in those conversations. It does not name the partners (children or adults, familiar or unfamiliar) or say how many.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What children most often leave out is adjusting to the partner: they speak to an unfamiliar adult as to a friend, they assume the partner knows the people and places they mention ("then Tim did it", without saying who Tim is), or they say nothing to someone they do not know.
