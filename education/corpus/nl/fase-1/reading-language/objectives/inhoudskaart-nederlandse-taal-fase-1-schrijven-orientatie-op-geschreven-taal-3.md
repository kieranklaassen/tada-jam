## Official wording

ontdekken van het verschil tussen 'schrijven' en 'lezen'

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering the difference between 'writing' and 'reading'

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child makes a note in their own signs and gives it to someone else to read. In post office play one child writes and another reads. A child of 4 says "I am writing" when making marks and "I am reading" when looking in a book; a child of 6 says that writing is putting something on paper and reading is finding out what someone put there.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says discovering, and it puts the two words in quotation marks. It does not say what the difference is. It does not ask that the child can read or write.

### Common mistakes

Using the words "reading" and "writing" for the same activity. Saying "I am reading" while telling a story without looking at the book. Giving their scribbles to an adult and asking what they wrote.
