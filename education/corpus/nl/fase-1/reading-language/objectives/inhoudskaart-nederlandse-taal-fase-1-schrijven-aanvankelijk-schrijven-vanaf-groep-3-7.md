## Official wording

schrijven over concrete onderwerpen uit eigen leefwereld. (onder andere over het hier en nu of over de nabije omgeving, de nabije toekomst/verleden)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

writing about concrete topics from one's own world (*leefwereld*). (among other things about the here and now or about the nearby surroundings, the near future/past)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) writes about their pet, about what lies on the table in front of them, about what they did yesterday or about their birthday next week. The child starts from a drawing, a photo or an object they brought from home.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The statement names concrete topics from the child's own world. The brackets open with "among other things" and name the here and now, the nearby surroundings, and the near future or past; the list is open. The source prints a full stop before the brackets. The statement sets no length or kind of text.

### Common mistakes

Leaving out when and where something happened, because the writer knows it. Mixing present and past forms in one short text. Listing things without telling anything about them.
