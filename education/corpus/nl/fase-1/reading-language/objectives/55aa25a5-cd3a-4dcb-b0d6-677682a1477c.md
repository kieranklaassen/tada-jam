## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After telling about a pet, the child answers the adult's questions: "Did you tell what you planned? Could everyone hear you?". The child looks at the three reminder pictures and points to the one that was forgotten. Classmates name one thing they liked, and the child names one thing to do the same way or differently next time. A child of 4 says whether it was nice to do and names one thing after a prompt; a child of 7 compares the telling with the plan and names one point to improve, such as speaking louder or looking at the listeners.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start; the content-line document prints it at the end of the sentence. The statement does not say who helps or how. The reflection is on how the speaking task was carried out. The statement names no points to reflect on and no form of reflection. NE/MT/03/09/01/fase1 is the goal about preparing the task.

### Common mistakes

Answering "good" or "I don't know" to every question, without an example. Telling the content again instead of saying how the telling went. Judging only by whether the listeners laughed or were quiet.
