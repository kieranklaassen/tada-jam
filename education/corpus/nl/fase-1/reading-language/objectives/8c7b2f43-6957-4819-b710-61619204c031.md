## Official wording

uiten van gevoelens/mening over een voorgelezen verhaal of rijmpje

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

expressing feelings/an opinion about a story or rhyme that was read aloud

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After the reading the child says "that was scary" or "I liked the rhyme about the frog", laughs, or asks for it again. The child shows with a face, a thumb up or down, or a drawing how the story made them feel. A child of 4 shows the feeling with the body and one or two words; a child of 7 says in a sentence what they felt or thought, and at which part.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The text is a story or a rhyme that was read aloud to the child. The slash between "feelings" and "opinion" is the source's; the wording does not say whether both are meant or either. It does not say that a reason is given. Giving an opinion about a story, verse or song is a separate goal, NE/LE/07/03/01/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Saying what the teacher or the previous child said. Saying only "nice" about every text. Telling what happened in the story when asked what they felt or thought about it.
