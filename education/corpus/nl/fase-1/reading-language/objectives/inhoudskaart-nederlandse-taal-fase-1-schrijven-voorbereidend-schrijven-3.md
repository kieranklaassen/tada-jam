## English gloss

drawing and decorating words

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child draws the letters of their own name large, colours them in and adds patterns. The child makes a name card or a birthday card with a decorated word on it. A child of 4 colours and decorates a word that an adult wrote; a child of 6 draws the letters of a word themselves and decorates them.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names two activities: drawing words and decorating words. It does not say which words, and it does not ask that the child can read or spell the word.

### Common mistakes

Decorating a letter so much that it can no longer be recognised. Copying the letters of a word in another order, or in mirror image. Leaving out a letter when copying a word.
