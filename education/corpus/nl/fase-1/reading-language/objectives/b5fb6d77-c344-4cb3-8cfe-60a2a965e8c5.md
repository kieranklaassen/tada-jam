## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

responding to each other, asking and answering questions

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A classmate shows a shell, and the child says "I have one too, mine is white" or asks "Where did you find it?". Asked "What did you make?", the child answers with what it is and what it is for. In a pair, two children take turns to ask and answer about a picture. A child of 4 responds mostly to the adult and asks "what" and "why" questions; a child of 7 responds directly to other children, asks a second question about the answer and answers in a full sentence.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names three things: responding to each other, asking questions and answering them. It sets no kind of question, no number of turns and no group size. It does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Saying a statement when asked to ask a question ("I have a dog too" instead of a question about the dog). Answering a different question from the one asked, often a "what" answer to a "why" or "how" question. Responding only to the adult and not to what another child said.
