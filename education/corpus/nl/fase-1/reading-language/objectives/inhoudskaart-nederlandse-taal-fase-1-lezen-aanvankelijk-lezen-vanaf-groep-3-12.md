## Official wording

zelfstandig begrijpen van een eenvoudige tekst door het onderwerp en enkele kenmerken te benoemen en de titel te interpreteren

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

independently understanding a simple text by naming the topic and a few features and interpreting the title

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) reads a short, simple text alone and then says what it is about. The child names a few things they notice about the text, for example that it has a title and a picture, or that it tells a story. The child says what the title tells them about the text.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The wording says independently, and a simple text. It names three things the child does: naming the topic, naming a few features, and interpreting the title. It does not say which features are meant and sets no length for the text.

### Common mistakes

Giving one detail or the last sentence as the topic. Retelling the whole text when asked what it is about. Reading the title as a separate line and not linking it to the text.
