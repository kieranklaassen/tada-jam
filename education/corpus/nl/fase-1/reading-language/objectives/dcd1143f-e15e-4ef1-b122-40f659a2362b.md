## Official wording

herkennen van lettercombinaties en spellingpatronen in niet-klankzuivere woorden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_cluster NE/LE/09; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau dcd1143f-e15e-4ef1-b122-40f659a2362b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising letter combinations and spelling patterns in words that are not phonetically regular (*niet-klankzuivere woorden*, words not written as they sound)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 sees a group of letters in a word and reads it as one piece, not letter by letter: for example "sch" in "school", "ng" in "ring", or "eer" in "beer", where the vowel sounds different before the r. The child circles the same letter group in a row of words and reads the row. With practice the child reads a number of such letter groups at once in short words.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do; the content-line document names the year in the heading of the cluster, "Technisch lezen (vanaf groep 3)" (technical reading, from *groep* 3), which within fase 1 is *groep* 3, about ages 6 to 7. The open data titles the cluster "Technisch lezen" and carries no year. The goal is about words that are not written as they sound, and about recognising letter combinations and spelling patterns in them. It names no letter combinations and no patterns, and sets no number; those above are the pack's examples, not the goal's. It stands under technical reading: it does not say that the child spells these words.

### Common mistakes

Sounding out every letter, so the word comes out wrong: "beer" said with the vowel of "been". Saying a final d as it is written and then not recognising the word: "hond". Reading "ng" or "sch" as separate letters.
