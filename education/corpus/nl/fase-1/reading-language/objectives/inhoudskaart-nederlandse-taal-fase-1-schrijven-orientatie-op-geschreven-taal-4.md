## Official wording

achterhalen van de betekenis van geschreven taal

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

finding out the meaning of written language

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child points to the print on a packet, on a street sign or under a picture and asks what it says. The child guesses from the logo, the picture or the first letter and checks the guess with an adult. A child of 4 "reads" a familiar logo and their own name; a child of 6 uses the letters they know and the place where the text stands to work out a word.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names finding out the meaning of written language. It does not say by what means, and it names no texts, letters or words. It does not ask for sounding out.

### Common mistakes

Guessing from the picture or the place alone, for example saying "milk" for any word on the carton. Taking any word that starts with their own first letter for their name. Thinking that a long word must stand for a big thing.
