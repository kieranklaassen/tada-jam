## Official wording

uiten van gevoelens/mening over een voorgelezen verhaal of rijmpje

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

expressing feelings/an opinion about a story or rhyme that has been read aloud

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After the reading the child says what they thought of it: "I liked it", "that was scary", "the wolf was mean". The child points to the page they liked most. A child of 4 says or shows with their face whether they liked it; a child of 6 gives a reason taken from the story.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names feelings or an opinion, joined by a slash, about a story or a rhyme that has been read aloud. It does not say in what form the child expresses them and does not ask for reasons.

### Common mistakes

Repeating what the child before said. Retelling a part of the story when asked what they thought of it. Saying "nice" about every story without anything more.
