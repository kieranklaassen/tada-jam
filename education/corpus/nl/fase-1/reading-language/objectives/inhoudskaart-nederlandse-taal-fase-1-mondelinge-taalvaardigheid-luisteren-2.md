## Official wording

luisteren naar en begrijpen van instructies (bijv. bij activiteiten in de groep)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to and understanding instructions (e.g. for activities in the group)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears "put your coat on the hook and come and sit in the circle" and does both. During a movement activity in the group the child does what is called out: stand still, hop, sit down. A child of 4 hangs up the coat when the teacher says it and points to the hook; a child of 6 carries out the same instruction in the order given without having it shown.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Activities in the group are given as an example of when instructions occur. The wording sets no number of steps in an instruction and no length. The statement does not say whether the instruction is given to one child or to the whole group.

### Common mistakes

Carrying out only the first or only the last part of an instruction that has several parts. Not taking an instruction addressed to the whole group as meant for themselves. Mixing up the words for order and place in the instruction, such as "before", "after" and "behind".
