## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

telling about material that one has brought along oneself

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child brings a shell, a soft toy or a holiday photo from home, holds it up in the circle and tells what it is, where it comes from and why it was chosen. The child passes the object round or points at a part of it while telling. Classmates ask a question, and the child answers while holding the object. A child of 4 names the object and says one or two things about it after the adult's questions; a child of 7 tells several things in order and uses the object to show what is meant.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The material is brought along by the child; the statement does not say what kind of material. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/03/08, titled *Visuele ondersteuning* (visual support). The statement sets no length and names no audience, and does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Holding the object up and saying nothing, or only its name, and waiting for questions. Holding the object so that the group cannot see it, or looking only at the object while talking. Telling about something else that the object reminds the child of, and not about the object.
