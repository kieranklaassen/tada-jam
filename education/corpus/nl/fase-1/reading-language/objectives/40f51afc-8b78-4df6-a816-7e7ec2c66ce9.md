## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While listening to an explanation of how bees make honey, the child looks at the pictures the adult shows and links them to what is said. The child raises a hand when a word or a part was not understood and listens to it once more. Afterwards the child tells in a few words what was heard, helped by the adult's questions "Who? Where? What happened?". A child of 4 does one of these when the adult prompts it at that moment; a child of 7 does them after a reminder beforehand and asks for a repeat without a prompt.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start; the content-line document prints it at the end of the sentence. The statement does not say who helps or how. It names no listening strategies and no number of them. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/02/09, titled *Aanpak* (approach), between NE/MT/02/09/01/fase1 (orientation on the listening task) and NE/MT/02/09/03/fase1 (reflection on it); a goal with the same wording, NE/MT/02/04/03/fase1, is listed under *Luisterdoelen en -strategieën* (listening purposes and strategies).

### Common mistakes

Sitting still and looking at the speaker without following, and not showing it. Asking for everything to be repeated instead of saying which part was unclear. Using a strategy only at the moment the adult names it and not at the next listening task.
