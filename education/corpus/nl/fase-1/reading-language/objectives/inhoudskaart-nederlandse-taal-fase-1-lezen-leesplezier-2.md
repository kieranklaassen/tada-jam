## Official wording

plezier tonen in voorlezen en boeken (leesbeleving)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

showing enjoyment in reading aloud (*voorlezen*) and books (reading experience)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child comes to sit close when a book is read aloud, looks at the pages, and says the repeated lines together with the adult. The child laughs, goes quiet at a tense part, and asks for the book again. A child of 4 points at the pictures and reacts with face and body; a child of 6 asks for a book by its title and says beforehand what will happen in a book they know.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says showing enjoyment, in reading aloud and in books; the brackets give the term reading experience (*leesbeleving*). It does not say who reads aloud. The child's own reading and own reading aloud are named in `Leesplezier / 3`. The statement names no books.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the showing: a child who sits still and says nothing can enjoy the book as much as a child who laughs and joins in.
