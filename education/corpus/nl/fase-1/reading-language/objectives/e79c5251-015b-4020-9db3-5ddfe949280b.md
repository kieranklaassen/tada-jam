## Official wording

reageren op signalen van bekende luisteraars en aanpassen van de inhoud aan de groep

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

responding to signals from familiar listeners and adapting the content to the group

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells about a holiday; a classmate frowns and asks "Who is Oma Bep?", and the child says "She is my grandmother". When listeners at the far side call that they cannot hear, the child speaks louder or holds the object higher. Telling the class about judo, the child first says what it is, because not everyone knows it. A child of 4 responds to a direct question or to the adult's prompt; a child of 7 sees a puzzled face, explains again in other words, and leaves something out or adds something for this group.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement has two parts: responding to signals from listeners the child knows, and adapting the content to the group. Perceiving the signals is the subject of NE/MT/03/04/01/fase1. The statement does not say which signals or how the content is adapted, and does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Continuing the telling as planned after a listener shows that something was not understood. Repeating the same sentence more loudly when the listener needed another word or an explanation. Answering one listener's question with a long unrelated story, after which the other listeners no longer follow.
