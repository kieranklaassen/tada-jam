## Official wording

ontdekken welke onderwerpen men leuk of niet leuk/saai vindt om over te lezen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering which topics one finds fun or not fun/boring to read about

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) picks books on different topics from the book corner or the library box, reads in them, and says afterwards which they liked and which bored them. The child puts a book back after a few pages and takes another. The child tells a classmate which book about animals, football or knights they would read again.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The wording says discovering, and it names both sides: topics the child finds fun and topics the child finds not fun or boring. It names no topics; the ones above are the pack's examples. It is about topics to read about, not about kinds of text.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the second half: they do not say that a book bored them, and finish it or keep it without reading. Many choose by the cover or by what a friend chose, and do not yet link their choice to a topic.
