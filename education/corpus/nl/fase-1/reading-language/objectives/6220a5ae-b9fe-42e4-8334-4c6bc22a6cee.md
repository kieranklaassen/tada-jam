## Official wording

meeleven met personages in een op de leeftijd afgestemd boek

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

feeling with characters in a book suited to the age

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While listening or reading, the child says "oh no" when the character loses something, says aloud that they hope it ends well, and is glad at the end. The child tells what they would do in the character's place, or strokes the sad character in the picture. A child of 4 reacts to what happens to the main character in the pictures; a child of 7 says what a character feels and wants, also when the text does not say it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The condition is a book suited to the child's age; the goal does not say what makes a book suited, and it names no kind of book. It does not say whether the book is read aloud or read by the child. Experiencing emotions in a story is worded as a separate goal, NE/LE/07/02/02/fase1.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often feel only with the main character and do not think about what the other characters feel. A young child may become so afraid for the character that they do not want to hear the rest.
