## Official wording

(met hulp) herkennen van en afstemmen op spreekdoel

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) recognising and adjusting to the purpose of speaking

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before speaking, the child says with the teacher what the telling is for: to explain how the marble run works, to make the others laugh, or to tell what happened in the playground. When explaining, the child shows the steps in order; when telling a funny story, the child uses voices and keeps the surprise for the end. A child of 4 speaks without a stated purpose and follows the adult's questions; a child of 6 says "I'm going to explain it" and, with a reminder, keeps to explaining.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording puts "with help" in brackets at the front. It has two verbs: recognising the purpose of speaking and adjusting to it. It names no purposes; the card's statement Spreken / 1 names giving an explanation, telling or retelling a story, and telling about an event or an own experience.

### Common mistakes

Turning an explanation into a story about themselves ("I once made a marble run and then ..."). Telling an event as a list of facts when the aim was to amuse, or the reverse. Not knowing what "explain" asks for, and naming the object instead of saying how it works.
