## Official wording

kennismaken met eenvoudige technieken om informatie te onthouden (herlezen, onderstrepen, woordspin) (groep 3)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting to know simple techniques for remembering information (rereading, underlining, word web (*woordspin*, a word with related words around it)) (*groep* 3, the third year of primary school)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 reads a short text about the cow a second time and underlines the three words that matter most. With the teacher the child writes "koe" in the middle of a sheet and draws lines from it to "melk", "gras" and "stal". The next day the child uses the sheet to tell what the text said.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do; the wording itself names the year, *groep* 3 (about ages 6 to 7). The open data ends the wording with "(groep 3)"; the content-line document prints no such tag and carries the year in the heading of the cluster, "Zakelijke teksten: studievaardigheden (vanaf groep 3)" (from *groep* 3). The brackets name three techniques: rereading, underlining and the word web. The verb is getting to know: the goal does not say that the child uses the techniques alone. The goal's cluster is about study skills with non-fiction texts. It sets no length for the text.

### Common mistakes

Underlining whole lines or nearly everything. Putting words in the word web that the child thinks of with the subject but that are not in the text. Rereading quickly without attention to what is to be remembered.
