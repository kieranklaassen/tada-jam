## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before reading, a child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) talks with the teacher about why they are going to read this text: to find something out, to be able to make something, or for pleasure. The child says in their own words what they want to know from the text and looks for it while reading.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The brackets at the start say that the child does this with help. It names the reading goal and lists no goals; the three reasons above are the pack's examples. It names no kind of text.

### Common mistakes

Thinking that the goal of reading is always to say the words aloud without errors. Forgetting the question during the reading. Naming the topic of the text when asked why they are reading it.
