(groep 3) voorspellen waar de tekst over gaat en herlezen en onderstrepen, met name om nog een keer te genieten van de tekst en om te onthouden

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(*groep* 3, the third year of primary school) predicting what the text is about, and rereading and underlining, in particular to enjoy the text once more and to remember

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 looks at the title and the picture and says what the text will be about, then reads to see whether that was right. The child reads a text they liked a second time, and reads a short information text again to remember what it says. With a pencil the child underlines the word or sentence that is to be remembered.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do; the wording itself names the year, *groep* 3 (about ages 6 to 7). Three actions are named: predicting, rereading and underlining. Two purposes follow "met name" (in particular): enjoying the text once more and remembering; the wording does not say which action goes with which purpose, and "in particular" leaves room for other purposes. The code of this record is the label "PO Kerndoel 06: Informatie ordenen", not a code of the same form as those of its neighbours in cluster NE/LE/05.

### Common mistakes

Keeping to the prediction when the text says something else. Underlining nearly every line, so that nothing is marked as more important. "Rereading" by saying the remembered text from memory without looking at the words.
