## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes one step or lays one block for each word of a spoken sentence, and points to the words of a written sentence one by one while it is read aloud. The child cuts a written sentence into its words and lays them back in order, and sees the white spaces between the words. A child of 4 hears that a sentence is made of separate words and that a long sentence has more of them; a child of 7 points out a sentence in a text, counts its words and says where it starts and stops.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name three levels: text, sentence and word. Syllables, sounds and letters are not named in this goal; other goals of cluster NE/LE/08 are about sounds and letters, and NE/LE/09/04/fase1 is about syllables. The goal does not mention the capital letter or the full stop, and it does not ask for word classes or parts of a sentence.

### Common mistakes

Taking a syllable for a word, so "ta-fel" gets two blocks. Taking words that are said together for one word: "de hond" as one block. Thinking that the word for a big thing must be a long word, so "trein" is called longer than "lieveheersbeestje" (ladybird).
