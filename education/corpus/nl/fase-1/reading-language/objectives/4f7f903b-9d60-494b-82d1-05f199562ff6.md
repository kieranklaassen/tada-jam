## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child goes to the writing corner without being asked, fills a sheet with scribbles, letters or words, and brings it to someone as a letter. The child writes name cards, tickets and menus for play. A child of 4 "writes" with lines and a few letters and tells what it says; a child of 7 writes short notes and stories with words that others can read.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about pleasure; it sets no kind of writing, no length and no correctness. The wording does not say whether "writing" means composing a text, forming letters by hand, or both.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often stop when they are afraid of writing a word wrongly or when every mistake is corrected. Some copy the same known words again and again and do not try new ones.
