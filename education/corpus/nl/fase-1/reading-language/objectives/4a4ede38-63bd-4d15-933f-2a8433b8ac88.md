## Official wording

benoemen van het onderwerp en verbinding leggen tussen onderwerp, gebeurtenissen, personen in een verhaal en de eigen werkelijkheid

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

naming the subject and making a connection between subject, events, persons in a story and one's own reality

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a story about a child who is afraid in the dark, the child says "it is about being afraid" and tells about a time of being afraid in bed. The child says which person in the story is like someone at home, and which event has also happened to them. A child of 4 mostly tells an experience of their own that the story reminds them of; a child of 7 names the subject in a few words and says in what way the story is like or unlike their own life.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal has two parts: naming the subject, and linking three things in a story (subject, events, persons) to the child's own reality. It does not say whether the story is read aloud or read by the child, and it names no kind of story. The code of this record ends in "fase2", but the data place the goal at fase 1.

### Common mistakes

Retelling the whole story when asked what it is about. Telling an experience of their own that is linked to one detail (the dog in a picture) and not to the subject. Naming a person as the subject ("it is about Tim") without saying what happens to him.
