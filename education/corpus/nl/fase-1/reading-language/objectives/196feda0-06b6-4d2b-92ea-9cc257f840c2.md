## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The adult says "Hang your coat on the hook and come and sit in the circle", and the child does both in that order. In the gym the child hears "Walk to the bench, climb over it and sit on the mat" and does it. The child hears an instruction given to the whole class and understands that it applies to them too. A child of 4 carries out an instruction of one or two parts in familiar words; a child of 7 carries out an instruction of three or more parts, including words such as "first", "before" and "next to".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Activities in the group follow "e.g." and are an example; "the group" (*groep*) here is the class. The statement sets no number of parts in an instruction and no kind of instruction. It asks for understanding and does not say how that is shown.

### Common mistakes

Doing only the part heard last of an instruction with several parts. Doing the parts in the order they were said when a word such as "before" or "after" reverses the order ("Before you sit down, wash your hands"). Not reacting to an instruction given to the whole class because the child's own name was not said.
