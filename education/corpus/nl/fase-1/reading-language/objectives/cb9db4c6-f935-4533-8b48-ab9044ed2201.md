## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

practising writing (scribbles, own name and words (e.g. with drawings)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child fills a sheet with scribbles in lines from left to right and says it is a letter. The child writes their own name on a drawing, first by copying from a name card and later without it, and writes a word with the drawing, such as "poes" under a cat. A child of 4 scribbles and writes some letters of their name; a child of 7 writes their name and short words alone, with a letter or letter pair for each sound.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The source prints the wording with one closing bracket missing, and the gloss leaves it as printed. The brackets name three things: scribbles, the child's own name, and words. Since the source does not show where the first bracket closes, "(e.g. with drawings)" can be read as an example for the words only or for the whole of the practising (scribbles, own name and words); the wording does not settle it. Sentences are not named in this goal; words or short sentences are named in NE/SCH/02/02/01/fase1. The goal does not mention correct spelling or a way of forming letters.

### Common mistakes

Writing the name with letters mirrored or in a changed order. Writing from right to left, or starting anywhere on the sheet. Writing a word with only its first sound or its consonants: "ps" for "poes".
