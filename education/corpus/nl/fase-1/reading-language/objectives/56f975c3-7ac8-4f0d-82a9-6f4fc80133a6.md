## Official wording

schrijven: - verhalende teksten (bijv. briefje, kaartje); - eenvoudige informatieve teksten (bijv. bericht of antwoorden op vragen); - eenvoudige instructieve teksten (bijv. recept of korte gebruiksaanwijzing); - schrijven van woorden of korte (2 woords)zinnen (bijv. eigen naam op etiket, korte zinnen bij zelfgemaakte tekeningen)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

writing: - narrative texts (e.g. a note, a card); - simple informative texts (e.g. a message or answers to questions); - simple instructive texts (e.g. a recipe or short instructions for use); - writing words or short (2-word) sentences (e.g. own name on a label, short sentences with self-made drawings)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes their own name on a label for the coat hook, writes "oma lief" (sweet grandma) on a card, and writes a word or a short sentence under a drawing. With the group or alone the child writes the steps of a recipe with a drawing for each step, or an answer to a question about the class pet. A child of 4 writes their name and a few letters or words and dictates the rest; a child of 7 writes a card, a message or a few steps in short sentences.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Four items are listed: narrative texts, simple informative texts, simple instructive texts, and words or short sentences. Everything after "bijv." in brackets is an example. The only size stated is in the fourth item: words or short sentences, with "2 woords" (two-word) in brackets. The source gives a note and a card as its examples of narrative texts. The goal does not mention spelling, handwriting or punctuation.

### Common mistakes

Writing words without spaces between them. Writing only the sounds heard most clearly, so vowels or middle sounds are missing. In an instruction, leaving out a step or writing the steps in the order in which the child thinks of them.
