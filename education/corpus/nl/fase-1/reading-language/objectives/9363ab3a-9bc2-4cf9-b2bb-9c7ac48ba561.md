## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle the child talks about words: which word is funny, which words sound alike, what a word means, and how a classmate says it in another language at home. The child talks about how one speaks to a friend and how to the head teacher. A child of 4 notices and says that a word sounds odd or that someone "says it wrong"; a child of 7 compares words and ways of saying something and gives a reason.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names two subjects of conversation, language and language use, and nothing more exact: no terms, no aspects of language and no rules. The form is conversation; the goal asks for no written work.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often talk about the thing a word names and not about the word itself: asked about the word "trein", they tell about trains. Many children believe there is only one right way to say something and call another dialect or language wrong.
