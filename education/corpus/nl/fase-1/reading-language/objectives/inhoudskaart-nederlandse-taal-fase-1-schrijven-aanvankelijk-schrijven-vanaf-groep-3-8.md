## Official wording

experimenteren met het afstemmen van tekst op publiek

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experimenting with fitting a text to an audience

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) writes an invitation for parents and then one for the children of *groep* 1, with fewer words and more drawings. The child tries a different opening for a note to the teacher and a note to a friend. The child gives the text to the reader it was meant for and sees whether that reader understands it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The wording says experimenting: trying out, not mastering. It names no audience; classmates are named in `Aanvankelijk schrijven (vanaf GROEP 3) / 9`. It does not say what in the text is fitted to the audience.

### Common mistakes

Writing as if the reader was there when it happened. Writing the same text for every reader. Thinking that fitting a text to a reader means only writing neatly.
