## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears a story begin with "er was eens" (once upon a time) and says "this is a fairy tale". The child points out what is usual in fairy tales: a king or princess, a wolf or witch, magic, something that happens three times, a good ending. A child of 4 recognises a fairy tale heard before, such as *Roodkapje* (Little Red Riding Hood), by its figures; a child of 7 can say of a story not heard before why it is or is not a fairy tale.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about recognising only: it does not ask the child to tell or write a fairy tale or to name its features. It names no fairy tales and no features; the opening words and figures above are the pack's examples. It does not say whether the fairy tale is read aloud or read by the child.

### Common mistakes

Calling every story with a talking animal or a princess a fairy tale. Calling every story that cannot be real a fairy tale, including a fantasy picture book. Knowing a fairy tale only from one film version and not recognising it in a book.
