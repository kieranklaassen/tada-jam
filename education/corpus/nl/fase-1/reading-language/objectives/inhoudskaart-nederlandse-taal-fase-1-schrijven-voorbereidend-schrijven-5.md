## Official wording

ervaring opdoen met het schrijven van woorden: - losse woorden (bijv. eigen naam op etiket of label, lijstjes) - korte (2 woords)zinnen (bijv. korte zinnen bij zelfgemaakte tekeningen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experience with writing words: - single words (e.g. own name on a sticker or label, lists) - short (2-word) sentences (e.g. short sentences with drawings the child made)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes their name on a label for their drawer and a list for the play shop. Under a drawing of their own the child writes two words that tell what it shows, such as *ik loop* (I walk). A child of 4 writes their name, or its first letter, on a label; a child of 6 writes a list of words spelled by their sounds and a two-word sentence under a drawing.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says gaining experience. It has two sub-points: single words, and short sentences, with "2-word" in brackets in the source. The examples for single words are the child's own name on a sticker or label, and lists; the example for short sentences is short sentences with drawings the child made. Correct spelling is not named, and the statement names no letters and sets no number of words beyond the two of the sentence; *ik loop* above is the pack's example.

### Common mistakes

Writing only the first sound of a word, or only its consonants. Writing two words joined together without a space. Leaving out letters of their own name, or writing them in another order.
