## Official wording

(met hulp) inzetten van schrijfstrategieën: - verkennen van het onderwerp; - 'verzamelen' van informatie

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) putting writing strategies to use: - exploring the subject; - 'collecting' information

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before writing about the guinea pig in the classroom, the child talks with the teacher and the group about what they know of it, watches it, and looks at a book about guinea pigs. The teacher or the child puts words or small drawings on a sheet: what it eats, where it sleeps. A child of 4 collects by looking, talking and drawing; a child of 7 notes a few words alone before writing sentences.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets at the start say that the child does this with help. The content-line document prints "onder begeleiding" (under guidance) where the open data has "(met hulp)" (with help). Two strategies are named: exploring the subject and collecting information; the quotation marks around 'collecting' are the source's. Three more strategies are named in NE/SCH/02/05/03/fase1. The goal does not say how the information is collected or noted.

### Common mistakes

Starting to write at once and stopping after one line because they know nothing more to write. Collecting only what they already know, without looking or asking. Not using what was collected once the writing starts.
