## Official wording

verzorgen van de lay-out van de tekst, bladspiegel verdelen en illustraties bij de tekst maken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking care of the layout of the text, dividing up the page (*bladspiegel*, the written area of the page) and making illustrations to go with the text

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) decides where on the sheet the title, the text and the drawing go before starting. The child leaves a margin and space between the lines. The child makes a drawing that shows what the text says.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The statement names three things: taking care of the layout of the text, dividing up the page, and making illustrations to go with the text. It gives no rule for layout and does not say whether the text is written by hand or typed.

### Common mistakes

Starting with letters that are too large and having no room left at the edge or the foot of the page. Filling the page with the drawing and leaving no room for the text. Making a drawing that shows something other than what the text says.
