## Official wording

deelnemen aan geplande en ongeplande gesprekken: - om informatie uit te wisselen (bijv. over gebeurtenissen, n.a.v. prentenboeken, of om elkaar te helpen) - voor discussie en overleg (bijv. over spel en ervaringen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking part in planned and unplanned conversations: - to exchange information (e.g. about events, prompted by picture books, or to help each other) - for discussion and consultation (*overleg*, talking something over to agree on it) (e.g. about play and experiences)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells the class in the morning circle what happened at home, and answers when another child asks about it. While building or playing shop, the child talks with a partner about who does what and which block goes where. After a picture book is read aloud, the child says what a character did and hears what others noticed. A child of 4 adds a remark about their own experience; a child of 6 talks a plan over with another child until they agree who does what.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording names two purposes: exchanging information, and discussion and talking something over. The topics in brackets (events, picture books, helping each other, play and experiences) are examples. The statement does not say how long a conversation lasts, how many children take part, or whether an adult leads it.

### Common mistakes

Talking about their own subject without linking it to what the previous speaker said. Staying silent in a large group while talking freely with one friend. Treating a disagreement about play as a quarrel and walking away, instead of talking it over.
