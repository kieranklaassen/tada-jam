## Official wording

geven van een eigen mening over een verhaal, versje of liedje

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/LE/07/03; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau c833c42b-f997-4fe9-9f26-dfad29f79ad0.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

giving an opinion of one's own about a story, verse or song

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a story, verse or song, the child says whether they liked it and says one thing about it: "I liked the part where the cat fell in the water" or "the song is too slow". The child picks which of two books should be read again and says why. A child of 4 says "nice" or "not nice" and points to a picture; a child of 7 gives a reason that is about the story, the verse or the song itself.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Three kinds of text are named: a story, a verse and a song. The goal does not say that the opinion needs a reason, and it does not say whether the text is heard or read by the child. The code of this record ends in "fase3", but the data place the goal at fase 1.

### Common mistakes

Repeating the opinion of the child who spoke first. Retelling a part of the story when asked for an opinion. Giving a reason that is not about the text: "because I like it", "because my sister has it".
