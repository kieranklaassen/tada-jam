## Official wording

gebruiken van eenvoudige signaalwoorden (bijv. en toen, omdat) voor samenhang in een verhaal

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using simple signal words (*signaalwoorden*, words that show how parts connect) (e.g. and then (*en toen*), because (*omdat*)) for coherence in a story

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child retells a story and links the events: "the bear woke up and then he went outside, because he was hungry". With three picture cards in a row, the child tells the story from one card to the next and says "first", "then" and "because". A child of 4 joins nearly everything with "and then"; a child of 6 also uses "because", "but" and "when" to say why and when something happened.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording speaks of simple signal words and gives *en toen* (and then) and *omdat* (because) as examples; it gives no list. Their purpose is stated: coherence in a story. The statement sets no number of such words.

### Common mistakes

Using "and then" between all events, which gives an order but no reasons. Saying "because" and then giving something that is not a reason ("he cried because he cried", or the event that came next). Reversing cause and effect around "because".
