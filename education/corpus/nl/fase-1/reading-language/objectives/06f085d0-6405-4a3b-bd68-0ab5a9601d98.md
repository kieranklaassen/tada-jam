## Official wording

samenvatten door het onderscheiden van hoofdelementen van een tekst (wie, wat, waar) met behulp van picto's (groep 3)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/LE/04/01; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau 06f085d0-6405-4a3b-bd68-0ab5a9601d98.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

summarising by distinguishing the main elements of a text (who, what, where) with the help of pictograms (*groep* 3, the third year of primary school)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After reading a short text, a child in *groep* 3 lays out three pictogram cards, one for who, one for what and one for where, and says or writes one answer with each: "the farmer", "milks the cows", "in the barn". The child then says the three answers as one sentence.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do; the wording itself names the year, *groep* 3 (about ages 6 to 7). The open data ends the wording with "(groep 3)"; the content-line document prints no such tag and carries the year in the heading of the cluster, "Zakelijke teksten: studievaardigheden (vanaf groep 3)" (from *groep* 3). The brackets name three main elements: who, what and where. "When" is not among them in this goal; it is named in NE/LE/07/01/01/fase1, which is about stories. The aid is named: pictograms. The goal's cluster is about study skills with non-fiction texts. It sets no length for the text or for the summary.

### Common mistakes

Retelling the whole text. Filling in "what" with a detail and not with the main event or main point. Mixing up the pictograms, for example answering "where" with a person.
