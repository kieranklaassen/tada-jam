## Official wording

reageren op signalen van (bekende) luisteraars en aanpassen van de inhoud aan de groep

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

responding to signals from (familiar) listeners and adapting the content to the group

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

When a classmate asks "who is Sem?", the child stops and explains that Sem is a cousin. Seeing puzzled faces, the child says a part again or shows it with the hands; seeing that the group does not know the game, the child first tells how it is played. A child of 4 answers a direct question and then continues as before; a child of 6 adds an explanation unasked when listeners look puzzled and leaves out what everyone already knows.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording has two parts: responding to signals from listeners (with "familiar" in brackets), and adapting the content to the group. It speaks of adapting content; it does not mention adapting word choice, volume or speed. The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Repeating the same words more loudly when not understood, instead of explaining in another way. Assuming that the group knows the people and places in the story. Ignoring a question as an interruption, or not being able to continue the story after answering it.
