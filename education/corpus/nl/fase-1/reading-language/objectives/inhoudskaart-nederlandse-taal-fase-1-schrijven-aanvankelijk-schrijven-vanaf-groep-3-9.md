## Official wording

(met hulp) aanpassen van tekst n.a.v. reacties van publiek (klasgenootjes)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) changing a text in response to reactions from the audience (classmates)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) reads their text to a classmate, who asks "who is Max?". With the teacher's help the child adds a word or a sentence that answers the question. The child crosses out a part and writes it again.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The brackets at the start say that the child does this with help. The audience named in brackets is classmates. The statement does not say what kind of change is made and does not mention spelling.

### Common mistakes

Answering the classmate's question aloud and leaving the text as it was. Seeing the text as finished once it is written. Changing only the spelling or the neatness where the reaction was about the content.
