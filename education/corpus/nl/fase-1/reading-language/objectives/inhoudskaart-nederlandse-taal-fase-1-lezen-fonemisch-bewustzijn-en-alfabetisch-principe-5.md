## Official wording

herkennen en gebruiken van rijmwoorden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising and using rhyming words

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears two words and says whether they sound alike at the end. The child finishes a line of a verse with a word that rhymes and makes up rhymes of their own, nonsense words included. A child of 4 fills in the rhyming word of a verse they know when the adult stops before it; a child of 6 thinks of a new word that rhymes with a word they are given.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names two things: recognising rhyming words and using them. It does not say whether the words are spoken or written, names no kind of rhyme, and sets no number or kind of words.

### Common mistakes

Giving a word that goes with the meaning and not with the sound, such as "dog" for "cat". Giving a word that starts the same where one that ends the same is asked. Saying the rhyming word of a known verse from memory without hearing that it rhymes.
