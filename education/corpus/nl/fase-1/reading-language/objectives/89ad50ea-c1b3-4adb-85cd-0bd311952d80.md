## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting to know and using elementary conversation rules (e.g. waiting for one's turn and listening to each other)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle the child raises a hand or waits until a classmate has finished before speaking. The child looks at the speaker while listening. In a pair, the child lets the other finish a sentence and then says something. A child of 4 needs a visible sign of whose turn it is, such as an object that is passed around, and reminders from the adult; a child of 7 waits without such a sign in a small group and reminds others of the rule.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The rules are called elementary, and the two in brackets, waiting for one's turn and listening to each other, follow "e.g." and are examples. The statement covers both getting to know the rules and using them. It gives no full list of rules and says nothing about group size.

### Common mistakes

Calling out an answer or a thought at once, for fear of forgetting it while waiting. Waiting in silence for a turn without listening, so that the child repeats what was just said or starts on something unrelated. Keeping the rule in the circle with the adult and dropping it in play with other children.
