## Official wording

ontdekken van de structuur van taal (tekst, zin, woord)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering the structure of language (text, sentence, word)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks along in a large book while an adult reads and points to each word. The child lays one block for each word of a short spoken sentence and points to where a sentence on the page begins and ends. A child of 4 sees that the print on a page is made of separate groups of letters with spaces between them; a child of 6 counts the words of a short sentence and points to the first and the last word of a written sentence.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says discovering. The brackets name three levels: text, sentence and word. Syllables, sounds and letters are not named in this statement, and it does not ask the child to use the terms; *de* and *een* under Common mistakes are the pack's examples.

### Common mistakes

Counting syllables or beats where words are asked. Leaving out short words such as *de* (the) and *een* (a) when counting the words of a sentence. Taking each line on the page for one sentence.
