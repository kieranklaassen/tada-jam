## English gloss

recognising and using rhyming words

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears "kat, mat, boom" and says which word does not belong. The child lays picture cards in rhyming pairs, "huis" with "muis" and "pan" with "kan", and finishes a line of a verse with a word that rhymes. A child of 4 joins in with known rhymes and hears whether two words rhyme; a child of 7 thinks of rhyming words alone, also made-up ones, and uses them in a verse of their own.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal has two parts: recognising rhyming words and using them. It names no words and no kind of rhyme; the words above are the pack's examples. It does not say whether the words are heard or read, and it does not mention the written form of rhyming words, such as the same letters at the end.

### Common mistakes

Giving a word that starts with the same sound ("kat, kip") where one that ends the same is asked. Giving a word that goes with the meaning: "kat, hond". Finishing a rhyme with the word that fits the sentence and not the one that rhymes.
