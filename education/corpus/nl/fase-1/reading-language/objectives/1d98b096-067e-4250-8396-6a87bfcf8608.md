## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The day before telling about a pet, the child decides with the adult what to tell: its name, what it eats and one thing it did. The child draws three small pictures as a reminder and chooses whether to bring a photo or the food bowl. The child tells it once to the adult or to one classmate as a trial. A child of 4 chooses the object and one thing to say about it together with the adult; a child of 7 prepares three or four points in order, with pictures or key words, and decides how to begin.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start; the content-line document prints "met hulp" at the end of the sentence, without brackets. The statement does not say who helps or how. The brackets name two things to prepare: content and approach. The statement does not say what the speaking task is, how long it is or what the preparation consists of. NE/MT/03/09/02/fase1 is the goal about reflecting on the task.

### Common mistakes

Preparing only the object to bring and not what to say about it. Planning to tell everything and choosing nothing, so that the telling has no order. Memorising the adult's wording and stopping when one word of it is forgotten.
