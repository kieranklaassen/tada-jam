## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child fetches a book and asks an adult to read it, sits close, looks at the pictures and joins in with lines they know. The child asks for the same book again and goes on looking at it alone afterwards. A child of 4 listens to a short picture book and points and calls out; a child of 7 follows a longer story read in parts over several days and asks what happens next.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about pleasure in being read to, not about understanding or retelling. It names no kind of book and no length of time. Pleasure in reading alone is a separate goal, NE/LE/01/02/fase1.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often stop listening when the text is too long for them or holds many words they do not know. A child who is asked many questions during the reading may start to see it as a task.
