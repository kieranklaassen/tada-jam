## Official wording

opzoeken en kennismaken met opzet van informatiebronnen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

looking up and getting to know the set-up of information sources

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) takes an information book about an animal, looks at the cover, the contents page and the headings, and turns to the page where the answer to a question could be. With an adult the child looks at how a picture dictionary or a children's website is ordered and where to start in it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The wording does not say what is looked up: it can be read as looking things up in information sources or as looking up the sources themselves. Getting to know the set-up is a first meeting, not independent use. The statement names no kind of source and does not mention alphabetical order.

### Common mistakes

Turning the pages one by one from the front and not using the contents page or the headings. Looking only at the pictures and not at the text beside them. Stopping at the first page on the subject, whether or not it answers the question.
