## Official wording

schrijven korte zinnen, meestal enkelvoudig, grammaticaal eenvoudig. (spelfouten en grammaticale fouten kunnen worden gemaakt)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudslijn po - Nederlands: Schrijven, page 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

writing short sentences, mostly single-clause (*enkelvoudig*), grammatically simple. (spelling mistakes and grammatical mistakes can be made)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 writes sentences such as "ik ga naar oma" and "de hond rent weg" under a drawing or in a short text; each sentence says one thing. The child reads each sentence back aloud to hear whether a word is missing. The child writes "wech" for "weg" or "ik loopte" for "ik liep" and goes on writing.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do; the document itself names the year, *groep* 3 (about ages 6 to 7). The content-line document prints the goal under the label "groep 3:"; the open data lacks this sub-cluster of writing ("inhoud/vorm", content/form), so the record is read from that document. The sentences are short, "meestal" (mostly) single-clause, and grammatically simple; "short" is not given a number of words. The sentence in brackets says that spelling mistakes and grammatical mistakes can be made, so the goal does not ask for sentences without errors. The source prints the verb in front of its object (*schrijven korte zinnen*), the word order of a sentence whose subject is left out; the gloss reads it in the form of the lane's other goals. The source also prints a full stop before the brackets and none after them. Texts are the subject of the first goal under the same label.

### Common mistakes

Joining everything with "en toen" (and then) into one long sentence. Leaving out a small word, such as "de" or "is". Writing words together without spaces, so that the sentence is hard to read back.
