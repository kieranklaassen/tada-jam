## Official wording

zelfstandig begrijpen van een eenvoudige tekst door het onderwerp en enkele kenmerken te benoemen en de titel te interpreteren

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/LE/03/02; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau 12778633-408d-4b7e-925f-3f8436dcebe1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

independently understanding a simple text by naming the subject and some features and interpreting the title

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a short text alone, for example six lines about the hedgehog, and says what it is about and two or three things the text says about it: it has spines, it sleeps in winter. The child reads the title first and says what it tells about the text. A child of 4 names the subject of a short text that was read aloud, without being prompted; a child of 7 reads a short text alone and names the subject, some features and what the title means.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. This goal says "independently", where its neighbour NE/LE/03/02/01/fase1 says "with help". The text is a simple text; the wording does not say what makes it simple and sets no length. Three things show the understanding: naming the subject, naming some features, and interpreting the title. "Features" (*kenmerken*) is not explained: it can mean features of the subject or features of the text, and the wording does not choose. The goal's cluster is about non-fiction texts.

### Common mistakes

Naming one detail as the subject. Giving features the child already knew that are not in the text. Reading the title as a separate line and not linking it to what follows.
