## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle the child plays "ik zie, ik zie wat jij niet ziet" (I spy) with the first sound of a word, claps the parts of a name, or finishes a rhyme that the teacher starts. The child makes a new word by putting two words together, "voet" and "bal", or by leaving a part off. A child of 4 joins in with clapping, rhyming and guessing words; a child of 7 plays with the separate sounds, such as saying a word without its first sound.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about taking part; it does not say what the child must be able to do in the games. It names no games, sounds or words; the games above are the pack's examples. It does not say whether letters are used: a word game or sound game can be played with spoken language alone.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often answer with the meaning of a word where the game asks for its sound: asked for a word that sounds like "kat", the child says "hond". Some children wait and repeat what a classmate says, and so do not listen for the sound themselves.
