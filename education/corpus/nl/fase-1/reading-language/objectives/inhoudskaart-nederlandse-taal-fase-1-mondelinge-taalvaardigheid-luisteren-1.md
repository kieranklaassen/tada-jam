## Official wording

luisteren naar en begrijpen van uitleg over activiteiten en taken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to and understanding explanation about activities and tasks

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child listens as the teacher explains what is done at the craft table today, then goes there and starts with the right materials. Asked by a classmate, the child says in their own words what to do. A child of 4 follows an explanation that is given close by and shown with the materials; a child of 6 follows an explanation given to the whole group and starts on the task without having it shown.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording covers explanation about activities and tasks; instructions are a separate statement of the card (Luisteren / 2). It sets no length of explanation and no number of steps. The statement does not say whether the explanation is supported by showing.

### Common mistakes

Remembering only the last thing said and starting there. Starting before the explanation has ended. Watching what other children do and copying them, which hides that the explanation was not understood.
