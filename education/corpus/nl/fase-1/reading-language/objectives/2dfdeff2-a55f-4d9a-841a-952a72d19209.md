## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes letters from a box of wooden or magnetic letters, names the ones they know, and lays their name with them. The child stamps letters, traces them in sand with a finger, and writes them with chalk or a thick pencil. A child of 4 names and writes one or a few letters, most often the first of their own name; a child of 7 names and writes all the letters.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name two things done with letters: naming and writing. The verb is playing. The goal names no letters and sets no number, and it does not say whether capitals, lower-case letters or both are meant. "Naming" (*benoemen*) is not explained: the wording does not say whether the child says the letter's sound or its name. It does not mention joined handwriting or the direction of the strokes.

### Common mistakes

Writing letters mirrored, most often letters with a direction such as s, j and z. Mixing up letters that differ only in direction: b, d and p. Naming a letter by a word or name that starts with it ("that is mama") and not by its sound.
