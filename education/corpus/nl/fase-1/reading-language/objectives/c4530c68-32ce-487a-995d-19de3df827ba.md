## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes a note and gives it to a classmate, who reads it back; then they change roles. The child notices that the writer chooses the words and puts down the signs, and that the reader looks at the signs and says the words. A child of 4 says "I am writing" when making marks and "I am reading" when looking in a book; a child of 7 says that writing goes from a word one thinks of to letters on paper, and reading from the letters back to the word.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording does not say which differences are meant; the quotation marks around the two words are the source's. The verb is discovering. The goal names no letters, words or texts.

### Common mistakes

Using "reading" and "writing" for the same activity. Thinking that someone who can read a word can also write it, or the other way round. Calling drawing "writing".
