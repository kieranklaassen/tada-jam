## Official wording

afleiden van de betekenis van onbekende woorden met behulp van de kaft en afbeeldingen bij een tekst (groep 3)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

working out the meaning of unknown words with the help of the cover and the pictures that go with a text (*groep* 3, the third year of primary school)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child in *groep* 3 reads the word "snavel" (beak) next to a picture of a bird, does not know it, looks at the picture and says "that is its mouth". Before reading, the child looks at the cover and says which things in the picture will probably be named in the text. The child points to the part of the picture that shows the new word.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do; the wording itself names the year, *groep* 3 (about ages 6 to 7). The open data ends the wording with "(groep 3)"; the content-line document prints no such tag and carries the year in the heading of the cluster, "Zakelijke teksten: aanpak (vanaf groep 3)" (from *groep* 3). Two aids are named: the cover and the pictures with a text. The goal does not mention working out a word from the sentence around it, from parts of the word, or from a dictionary. It names no words and sets no number. Its cluster is about non-fiction texts.

### Common mistakes

Reading on after an unknown word without noticing that it is unknown. Linking the word to the largest thing in the picture and not to the part it names. Reading the word wrongly as a known word that looks like it, and so not looking for its meaning.
