## Official wording

(met hulp) inzetten van schrijfstrategieën: verkennen van het onderwerp, 'verzamelen' , selecteren en ordenen van informatie, reflecteren (vooral op inhoud, minder op vorm)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - jonge kind (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) putting writing strategies to use: exploring the topic, 'collecting', selecting and ordering information, reflecting (mainly on content, less on form)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before writing, a child in *groep* 3 (about 6 or 7) talks with the teacher about the topic and puts words or small drawings about it on a sheet. The child chooses which of them go into the text and numbers them in order. After writing, the child reads the text back and says whether it says what they meant.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card prints this statement under a heading that says from *groep* 3 on (about ages 6 to 7). The brackets at the start say that the child does this with help. After the colon it names the strategies: exploring the topic, 'collecting' (in quotation marks in the source, which prints a space before the comma that follows), selecting and ordering information, and reflecting. The brackets say that the reflecting is mainly on content and less on form.

### Common mistakes

Starting to write at once without first thinking about what to tell. Writing down everything that was collected without choosing. Looking only at neatness and spelling when reading the text back.
