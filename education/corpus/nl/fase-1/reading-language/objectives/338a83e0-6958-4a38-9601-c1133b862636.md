## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Telling about a visit to the dentist, the child says "the drill" and "the waiting room", words learned in that week's theme, instead of "that thing" and "there". The child stops, searches for the word and says "a ... magnifying glass". When the first word does not fit, the child replaces it: "a car, no, a van". A child of 4 chooses from everyday words and uses a new one while holding the object; a child of 7 chooses the more exact of two words and uses words from earlier themes and from books.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. "New" stands in brackets, so the words may be new ones or ones the child already knows. The open data lists the goal under speaking (NE/MT/03), in the sub-cluster titled *Woordgebruik en woordenschat* (word use and vocabulary); unlike NE/MT/03/05/02/fase1 and NE/MT/01/05/01/fase1, it does not say "with help". The statement names no words, no themes and no number of words.

### Common mistakes

Using filler words ("thing", "that", "do") where the child knows the specific word. Using a newly learned word where it does not fit, because only part of its meaning is known (calling every doctor "the dentist"). Making up a word from known parts ("tooth doctor") and keeping it after the usual word has been offered.
