## Official wording

waarnemen en verkennen van elementaire fasen in groei, ontwikkeling (bloei) en voortplanting van planten, van dieren en van de mens

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

observing and exploring elementary stages in growth, development (*bloei*, flowering or flourishing) and reproduction of plants, of animals and of humans

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child pushes a bean into wet soil or cotton wool, waters it and looks every day for the root and the first leaves. The child looks at frogspawn, a caterpillar or chicks in class and tells what is different from last week. The child puts three pictures in order, such as seed, small plant and plant with a flower, or baby, child and adult. A child of 4 says that something has become bigger; a child of 6 orders three or four pictures and tells the steps in words.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The word *elementaire* limits this to basic stages, and the bracket puts *bloei* next to development; the Dutch word means both flowering and flourishing, and the wording does not separate them. The wording covers growth, development and reproduction, for plants, for animals and for humans, and asks for observing and exploring, not explaining. On reproduction the text sets no level of detail. It does not say which plants or animals, or how many stages.

### Common mistakes

Thinking a seed is not alive, or that a small complete plant is already inside it. Thinking growing means only getting bigger, and not seeing that the shape changes, as from caterpillar to butterfly or from flower to fruit. Putting the stages in a row that stops at the adult, without seeing that the adult makes seeds, eggs or young again.
