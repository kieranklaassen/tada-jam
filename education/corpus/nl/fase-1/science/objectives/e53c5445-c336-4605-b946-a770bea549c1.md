## English gloss

realising the influence of weather and seasons on humans

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at the weather before going out and picks the clothes that fit: boots and a raincoat for rain, a hat and mittens for frost, a sun hat when it is hot. The child tells what people do differently by weather and season: playing inside in a storm, swimming in summer, turning on lights and heating in winter. The child sorts clothes or pictures of activities by the season they fit. A child of 4 picks the coat for today's weather; a child of 7 can say why, and can name ways the season changes food, play and work.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording asks for a realisation that weather and seasons affect humans; it does not say in which ways. Clothing, food, play, work and health are not named, so none of them is set as content. The statement is about humans only: how trees and other plants change with the seasons is in another statement, `ojw/pdm/4/04/fase1`.

### Common mistakes

Choosing clothes by what the child likes or by what was worn yesterday, not by looking at the weather. Thinking that a coat itself gives warmth, not that it keeps the body's warmth in. Thinking that people cause the season by what they do, for example that it is summer because people go swimming.
