## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Outside, the child feels bark, moss and wet grass with the hands, smells soil and a crushed leaf, listens with closed eyes and counts the different sounds, and looks under a log with a magnifying glass. The child walks barefoot over sand, grass and stones and says how each feels. The child tastes only what an adult gives, such as a blackberry or a herb from the school garden. A child of 4 touches, smells and listens when invited; a child of 7 chooses a sense on purpose and describes the result in words such as rough, damp, sour or high.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says "all your senses", so it is not limited to looking, and it names none of the senses. Its object is the surroundings (*omgeving*); the cluster it stands in is about dealing with nature, but the wording itself does not say natural surroundings only. It asks for observing and exploring, not for recording or explaining, and it states no condition for tasting or touching.

### Common mistakes

Using only the eyes and naming what a thing is, without touching, smelling or listening. Saying "nice" or "dirty" instead of describing what is sensed, such as rough, cold or sweet. Putting things in the mouth, or touching plants and animals that sting or prick, because the child does not yet tell safe from unsafe.
