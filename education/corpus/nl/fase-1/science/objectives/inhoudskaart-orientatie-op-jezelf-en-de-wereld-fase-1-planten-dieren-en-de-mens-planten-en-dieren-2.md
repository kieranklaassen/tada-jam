## Official wording

verkennen van uiterlijke kenmerken van planten en van dieren zoals vorm, kleur, bouw en beharing

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring outward characteristics of plants and of animals such as shape, colour, build and hairiness

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child strokes a hairy leaf and a smooth one, feels the fur of a rabbit and a feather, and says how each feels. The child lays leaves, flowers or toy animals in groups by colour or by shape, and tells what an animal looks like: big, small, spotted, with a long tail. A child of 4 names the colour and says big or small; a child of 6 also names shape and covering, such as round leaves or an animal with fur, and sorts by a feature the child chose.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording is limited to outward characteristics, those that can be seen or felt from outside, of plants and of animals. "Such as" (*zoals*) introduces four examples: shape, colour, build and hairiness; the list is not given as complete. The statement does not ask for naming species or for a fixed way of sorting.

### Common mistakes

Changing the feature halfway through sorting, for example starting by colour and going on by size. Describing what an animal does, or whether the child likes it, instead of how it looks. Looking at one striking feature only, so that two animals of the same colour are called the same.
