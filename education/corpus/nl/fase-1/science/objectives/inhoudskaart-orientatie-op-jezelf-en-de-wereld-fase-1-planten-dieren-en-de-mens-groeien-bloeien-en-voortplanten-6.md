## Official wording

bespreken dat mensen en sommige dieren levend baren en dat de geboorte wordt voorafgegaan door een periode van gedragen worden door de moeder

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discussing that people and some animals give birth to live young and that birth is preceded by a period of being carried by the mother

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the circle the child tells about a baby that is expected at home or about a pet that had young, and says that the baby was in the mother's belly first. The child looks at a picture book about a new baby or about a cat with kittens and points to the mother with the big belly and to the young after the birth. The child asks what the child wants to know and listens to the answer. A child of 4 says that a baby comes from the mother's belly; a child of 6 can also say that a dog, a cat and a cow carry their young.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discussing (*bespreken*): the wording describes a conversation, not an investigation. It covers people and "some animals", names no animals, and holds two parts: live birth, and a period before it in which the mother carries the young. This statement touches on human reproduction, and the text sets no level of detail: it does not say how a pregnancy starts, how long it lasts, or how the birth goes.

### Common mistakes

Thinking the baby is in the stomach, where the food goes. Thinking the baby was always there, or was fetched or bought complete, with no period of growing. Thinking all animals carry their young in the belly, or that all animals lay eggs.
