## Official wording

beseffen van de invloed van weer en seizoenen op de mens

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising the influence of weather and seasons on humans

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stands at the coat rack, looks outside and decides whether a coat, boots or a sun hat is needed, and puts them on. In the doll corner the child dresses a doll for rain or for a hot day. The child tells what the family does when it is very cold or very warm, such as skating, staying in, or eating ice cream. A child of 4 puts on what an adult names and feels the difference outside; a child of 6 chooses by looking at the weather and gives the reason.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording asks for a realisation that weather and seasons affect humans; it does not say in which ways. Clothing, food, play, work and health are not named, so none of them is set as content. The statement is about humans only: how trees and other plants change with the seasons is in another statement of the card, Omgaan met de natuur / 4.

### Common mistakes

Choosing clothes by what the child likes or by what was worn yesterday, not by looking at the weather. Thinking that a coat itself gives warmth, not that it keeps the body's warmth in. Thinking that people cause the season by what they do, for example that it is summer because people go swimming.
