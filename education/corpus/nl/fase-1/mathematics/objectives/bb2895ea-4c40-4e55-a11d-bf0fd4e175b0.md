## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with opposites between concepts around length and perimeter (*omtrek*, the distance around something)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child finds a long and a short stick, a high and a low chair, and a thick and a thin book, and puts each pair together. When one ribbon is longer the child says that the other is shorter. A child of 4 gives "small" as the opposite of "big" and "short" as the opposite of "long"; a child of 7 gives the opposite of each word, also "low" and "thin", and turns a comparison round.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal lists no opposites; the words around length and perimeter are in rw/m/1/02/fase1. No units are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child gives "small" as the opposite of every word for size. The child gives "not long" where "short" is meant. The child does not see that the same stick is the long one in one pair and the short one in another.
