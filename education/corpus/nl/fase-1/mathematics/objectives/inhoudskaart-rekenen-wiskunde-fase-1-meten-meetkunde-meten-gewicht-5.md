## Official wording

meten met informele 'weeginstrumenten' zoals met wip, balans en handen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

measuring with informal 'weighing instruments' such as with a seesaw, a balance and hands

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sits on a seesaw with a classmate and sees who goes down, then tries with two children on one side. The child puts things in the two pans of a balance and adds blocks to one pan until the beam is level. The child weighs in the hands by holding one thing in each hand and moving them up and down. A child of 4 feels with the hands and watches the seesaw; a child of 6 uses a balance to check a guess and counts how many blocks weigh the same as an apple.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The instruments are informal; the seesaw, the balance and the hands come after "such as" and are examples. The card puts the word weighing instruments in quotation marks. No standard units and no scale with numerals are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child reads the balance the wrong way round and calls the higher pan the heavier one. The child does not notice that sitting nearer the middle of the seesaw changes who goes down. When counting blocks against an object the child uses blocks of different sizes.
