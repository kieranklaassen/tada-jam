## Official wording

plaatsen van gebeurtenissen in tijdsvolgorde

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

placing events in order of time

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child puts three or four picture cards of a story or of getting dressed in order and tells what happens first, next and last. The child tells in order what they did this morning, and lays photos of a baby, a toddler and a schoolchild from youngest to oldest. The child puts the pictures of the school day in order on the day chart. A child of 4 orders three pictures of a familiar routine; a child of 6 orders four or more pictures, also of a new story, and uses first, then, after that and last.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no events and sets no number of events. It does not say whether the events are of one day, of a story or of a longer time, and it mentions no clock times or dates.

### Common mistakes

The child puts the most striking picture first instead of the earliest. The child gets the first and the last picture right and mixes up the middle ones. The child tells the story in the right order and lays the cards from right to left or in a heap, so the cards do not show what the child knows.
