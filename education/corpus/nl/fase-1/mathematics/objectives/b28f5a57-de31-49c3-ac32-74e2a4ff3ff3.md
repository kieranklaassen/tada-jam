## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing and ordering by weight such as 'by eye', with hands and weighing with a balance

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at two parcels and says which will be heavier, then holds one in each hand to feel it, then puts them on the two pans of a balance. The child puts three or four bags in order from lightest to heaviest by comparing two at a time. A child of 4 compares two things that differ a lot with the hands; a child of 7 uses the balance for things that feel alike and orders several things.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The three ways after "zoals" are examples: by eye, with the hands, and weighing with a balance. Units and scales with numbers are not mentioned, and no number of objects is stated.

### Common mistakes

The child judges by size, and keeps to that after the hands or the balance show otherwise. The child reads the balance the wrong way round and calls the pan that is up the heavier one. When ordering three things, the child compares the first with the second and with the third, and not the second with the third.
