## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child covers the table top with sheets of paper and counts how many are needed, lays tiles over a drawn shape until it is full, and lays a cloth over the doll's bed to see whether it is large enough. A child of 4 lays things on a surface and sees whether it is covered; a child of 7 covers with equal tiles without gaps or overlaps, counts them and says "the table is twelve sheets big".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name the way: by means of covering. The goal names no units, no shapes and no material to cover with. Calculating an area is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child leaves gaps, or lets the tiles overlap or stick out over the edge. The child covers along the edges and leaves the middle open. The child uses tiles of different sizes and counts them together.
