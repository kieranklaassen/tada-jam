## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with ordinal number words (*rangtelwoorden*) such as first, second… tenth, as far as you can; and exploring when you use them

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The children stand in a line at the door and the child says who is first, second and third, and who is last. The child points to the third bead on a string, the second drawer of a cupboard and the fifth page of a book. A child of 4 uses "first" and "last" and sometimes "second"; a child of 7 uses the ordinal words to tenth and further and knows that they tell a place in an order and not how many.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. First, second and so on to tenth follow "zoals" and are examples, with the addition "as far as you can": no end is set. The goal also includes exploring when ordinal words are used. It does not say from which end of a row the counting starts.

### Common mistakes

The child gives a quantity when a place is asked, and takes three beads when asked for the third bead. The child forms the irregular Dutch words by rule, such as *eenste* for *eerste* and *driede* for *derde*. The child does not realise that the place of an object depends on the end from which the counting starts.
