## Official wording

vergelijken en ordenen op lengte en omtrek

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing and ordering by length and perimeter (*omtrek*, the distance around something)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child holds two sticks side by side with one end level and says which is longer. The child puts five strips in a row from shortest to longest, and stands classmates in a line by height. The child puts a string round each of two tree trunks and compares the two lengths of string. A child of 4 compares two things directly; a child of 6 orders several things, and compares two things that cannot be put side by side by measuring both with the same string or stick.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two actions are named, comparing and ordering, for both length and perimeter. The statement sets no number of objects and names no instruments or units. Informal instruments and measures for length are the card's statement Lengte en omtrek / 5.

### Common mistakes

The child compares two things without putting one end of each level, and judges by which end sticks out further. The child judges a bent or coiled rope as shorter than a straight one of the same length. When ordering several strips the child gets the longest and the shortest right and places the middle ones without comparing them.
