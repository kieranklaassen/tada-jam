## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with concepts around the counting sequence such as further (*verder*), on (*door*), back (*terug*), next to (*naast*), in between (*tussenin*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

With number cards 1 to 10 laid in a row, the child points to the numbers that lie next to 6 and to the number in between 4 and 6. The child counts "*verder*" from where another child stopped and counts "*terug*" when asked. A child of 4 follows "*verder*" and "*terug*" while walking along numbered tiles; a child of 7 uses "*naast*" and "*tussenin*" for numbers up to 20 without a row in front of them.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The five words after "zoals" are examples: *verder*, *door*, *terug*, *naast*, *tussenin*. This goal states no number range. The words here are about places in the counting sequence; geometric concepts such as in front of, behind and next to are in rw/mk/1/01/fase1.

### Common mistakes

For "*naast*" the child names only the number after and forgets the number before. The child takes "*terug*" as starting again at one and not as counting backwards. The child takes "*tussenin*" as any number near the two and not as a number that lies between them.
