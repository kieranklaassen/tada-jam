## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

playing with light and shadow of shapes and figures in sunlight or torchlight

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Outside in the sun the child makes their shadow long, short, wide and narrow, and another child draws round it with chalk. Inside, the child holds a block, a ball and a hand between a torch and the wall, moves them nearer and further, and turns them to see how the shadow changes. A child of 4 makes shadows and follows them; a child of 7 says beforehand which shadow an object will give and makes a shadow larger or smaller on purpose.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The Dutch names two sources of light, joined by "or": sun or torch (*zaklamp*). The verb is playing. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: making long and short shadows ("lange en korte schaduwen maken"); it is an example, not a limit. The long and short shadows under "In a child's hands" follow this example. The goal names no particular shapes and does not ask for an explanation of how a shadow comes about.

### Common mistakes

The child expects the shadow to show the colours or the face of the object. The child looks for the shadow on the same side as the light. The child thinks an object has one fixed shadow and does not expect a turned block to give another shape.
