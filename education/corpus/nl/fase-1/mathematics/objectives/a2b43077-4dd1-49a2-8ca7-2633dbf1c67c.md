## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning about handing out (*uitdelen*) and sharing out (*verdelen*) of quantities in suitable problem situations and conflict situations (*conflictsituaties*: an outcome against expectation, or a disagreement)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Three children have 10 stickers to share and one sticker is left; they talk about what to do with it. A child sees that one tray holds more than another after the sharing and explains how to put it right. A child of 4 says "that is not fair" and points; a child of 7 gives a reason with numbers, such as that each child has three and one is left.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. This goal states no number range; rw/bew/3/01/fase1 states "onder ten minste 20", and large quantities, for its own content. The text does not explain *conflictsituaties* (conflict situations): the word can mean a situation in which the outcome goes against what a child expects, or a disagreement between children, and the wording does not choose. It does not say which situations are suitable. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: a question such as "Can you share out 10 loose euros among three?" ("Kun je 10 losse euro's verdelen met z'n drieën?"); it is an example, not a limit. The first activity under "In a child's hands" uses the numbers of this example.

### Common mistakes

The child reasons from who should get more (the oldest, a friend) and not from the quantities. The child expects every sharing to come out without a remainder and keeps recounting when one object is left. The child says that the shares are equal without checking.
