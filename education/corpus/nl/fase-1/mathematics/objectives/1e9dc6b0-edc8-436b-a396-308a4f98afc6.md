## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child turns a sand timer and tidies up until the sand has run through, and tests how many blocks they can stack in that time. The child counts aloud at an even pace while another child hops to the fence, and watches the second hand go once round the clock. A child of 4 watches the sand run and knows that the time is over when the top is empty; a child of 7 compares two activities by counts or by turns of the sand timer and says which took longer.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The three time measurers after "zoals" are examples: sand timer, counting, second hand. The word "informal" stands in brackets in the wording. Seconds and minutes are not named as units, although a second hand is among the examples.

### Common mistakes

The child counts faster when in a hurry, so that the same time gets another number. The child starts the count or the sand timer after the activity has begun. The child thinks a larger sand timer always runs longer.
