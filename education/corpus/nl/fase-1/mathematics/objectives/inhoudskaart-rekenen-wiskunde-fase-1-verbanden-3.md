## Official wording

aflezen van informatie uit grafische voorstellingen (beelddiagram, staafdiagram)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reading off information from graphical representations (picture graph [*beelddiagram*, a chart built from pictures], bar chart)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a chart of the children's pets on the wall and says how many children have a cat by counting the pictures in that column. The child says which column has the most, which has the fewest and which two are equal. A child of 4 points at the longest column and names what it stands for; a child of 6 reads the number for each column and answers a question such as how many more children have a dog than a fish.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name two graphical representations: picture graph and bar chart. The statement sets no number range and does not say which information is read off. It does not mention a numbered axis or a scale.

### Common mistakes

The child tells what the pictures show instead of how many there are. The child reads off the tallest column only and ignores the others. The child miscounts a column by counting its label picture as one of the items.
