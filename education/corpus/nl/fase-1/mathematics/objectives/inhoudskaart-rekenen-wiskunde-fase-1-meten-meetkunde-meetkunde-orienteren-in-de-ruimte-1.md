## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child puts the bear in front of, behind, next to, on and under the chair when asked, and says where it is. The child follows and gives directions with words such as forwards, backwards, near, far, inside and outside. In the gym the child walks around the bench, crawls through the hoop and stands between two cones. A child of 4 uses in, on, under and next to correctly; a child of 6 also uses in front of, behind and between and begins to use left and right.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no concepts and gives no examples; every position word above is the pack's example. It stands under the sub-heading on orientation in space, with statements about the place of objects, viewpoints and routes; which concepts are meant is not said.

### Common mistakes

The child mixes up in front of and behind, most often with objects that have no front of their own, such as a ball or a tree. The child mixes up left and right. The child describes a place only from where they stand and does not see that it is different for someone who stands opposite.
