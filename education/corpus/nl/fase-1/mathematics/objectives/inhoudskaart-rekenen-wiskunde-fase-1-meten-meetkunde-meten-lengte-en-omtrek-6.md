## Official wording

verkennen en meten van maten van het eigen lichaam

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring and measuring measurements of one's own body

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stands against a strip of paper on the wall while a classmate marks the height, and compares the mark with the last one and with those of other children. The child draws round their own hand and foot, cuts them out and compares them with a classmate's. The child puts a string round their head, waist or wrist and lays the string next to another child's. A child of 4 compares body parts directly, hand against hand and back to back; a child of 6 measures height, foot length and the distance round the head with strips, strings or blocks and says a result.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about the child's own body; it names no body parts and no instruments or units. It stands under the sub-heading for length and perimeter, so weight is not part of it.

### Common mistakes

The child stands on tiptoe or does not stand against the wall when the height is marked. The child pulls the string too tight or lets it hang loose around the head or waist. The child thinks the older child is always the taller one.
