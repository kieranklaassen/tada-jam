## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experimenting with shapes and figures in mirrors and mirror effects

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stands a small mirror on half a drawing of a butterfly and sees the whole, and moves the mirror to make a shape wider or narrower. The child puts a block in front of two mirrors that stand at an angle and counts how many blocks are seen, and makes a print with two equal halves by folding painted paper. A child of 4 moves the mirror and tells what they see; a child of 7 says beforehand what the mirror will show and builds the second half of a figure so that it matches the mirror image.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is experimenting. The goal names no shapes and does not explain "mirror effects". The words symmetry and line of symmetry are not in the wording.

### Common mistakes

The child expects the mirror image to be the same way round as the original, and builds the second half as a copy moved sideways. The child places the second half at another distance from the mirror line than the first half. The child thinks the things seen in the mirror are behind it and looks there.
