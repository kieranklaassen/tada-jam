## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning about (qualitative) proportions (*verhoudingen*, how sizes or amounts relate) in suitable problem situations and conflict situations (*conflictsituaties*: an outcome against expectation, or a disagreement)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A doll's coat is far too large for the small doll, and the child says why it does not fit and which doll it would fit. A drawing shows a child who is taller than the house beside them, and the child says what is odd about it. A child of 4 says "too big" or "does not fit" and tries another; a child of 7 explains with a comparison, such as "the door must be taller than the person".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The word "qualitative" stands in brackets in the wording: proportions without numbers. This goal states no number range. The text does not explain *conflictsituaties* (conflict situations): the word can mean a situation in which the outcome goes against what a child expects, or a disagreement between children, and the wording does not choose. It does not say which situations are suitable. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: seeing that in a drawing a head is drawn too large in relation to the rest of the body ("zien dat in een tekening een hoofd te groot is getekend ten opzichte van de rest van het lijf"); it is an example, not a limit.

### Common mistakes

The child sees nothing odd in a picture where the sizes do not fit together, because each thing looks right by itself. The child gives a reason about one thing only ("it is big") and does not compare it with another. The child thinks that things drawn small because they are far away are small in reality.
