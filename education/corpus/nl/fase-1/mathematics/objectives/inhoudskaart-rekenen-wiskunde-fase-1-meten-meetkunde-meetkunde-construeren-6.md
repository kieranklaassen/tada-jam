## Official wording

redeneren over eenvoudige meetkundige problemen rond construeren

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning about simple geometric problems around constructing

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child's bridge falls down, and the child says why they think it fell and what they will change. The child predicts whether a round block can go at the bottom of a tower, tries it and explains what happened. Before building, the child says which box is big enough to be the garage for a toy car. A child of 4 tries again and says "it fell because it was too high"; a child of 6 gives a reason beforehand, such as "the wide block must go underneath, otherwise it tips over".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The problems are to be simple and to concern constructing; none is named. The statement does not say how the child shows the reasoning: in words, by pointing or by doing. The wording does not mention conflict situations, which the reasoning statements under Oriënteren in de ruimte and Opereren met vormen en figuren do.

### Common mistakes

The child builds the same thing again without changing anything and expects another result. The child describes what happened and gives no reason. The child names a cause that has nothing to do with the building, such as "the block did not want to".
