## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The price tag says 7 euros and the child lays the amount with play money: a note of 5 and a coin of 2, or seven coins of 1. The child lays the same amount in another way and swaps two coins of 1 euro for one coin of 2. A child of 4 lays one coin of 1 euro for each euro of a small price; a child of 7 combines notes of 5 and 10 with coins of 1 and 2 for amounts above ten euros.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The amounts are in whole euros: cents are not part of this goal. The amounts are to be simple; the statement itself states no range of amounts. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: making up 6 euros with coins and notes ("6 euro samenstellen met munten en briefgeld"); it is an example, not a limit. Giving change is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child counts the number of coins and not their value, and lays any three coins for 3 euros. The child counts a coin of 2 euros as one when counting on. The child believes an amount can be laid in one way only.
