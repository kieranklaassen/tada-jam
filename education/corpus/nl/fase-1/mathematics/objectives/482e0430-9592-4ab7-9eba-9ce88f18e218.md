## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child matches three bears of different sizes with three chairs and three bowls, the biggest with the biggest. The child puts a set of nesting dolls in order and chooses which toy car fits in which garage. A child of 4 pairs big with big and small with small for two or three things; a child of 7 orders two sets side by side and says that one thing is too big or too small for another.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. This goal states no number range. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: when you get bigger, your clothes must be bigger too ("als je groter wordt, moeten je kleren ook groter zijn"); it is an example, not a limit. The goal mentions no numbers and no calculating; proportions in numbers are in rw/verh/2/02/fase1.

### Common mistakes

The child compares one object with one other and not the whole set, so that the middle one gets the wrong partner. The child judges a toy by the real thing and calls a small toy elephant bigger than a large cuddly mouse. The child looks at height only and ignores width.
