## Official wording

doortellen en terugtellen vanaf willekeurige getallen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

counting on and counting back from arbitrary numbers

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An adult or another child names a number and the child counts on from it aloud, without starting at one. The child counts back from a given number, for example before a rocket of blocks is launched or while stepping backwards along number tiles. A child of 4 counts on from a small number and counts back from three or five; a child of 6 counts on and back from any number to about twenty.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The sub-heading above this statement on the card gives the range "up to at least 20" (*tot tenminste 20*): no upper bound is set, and the Dutch *tot* leaves open whether 20 itself is included. The statement says "arbitrary numbers" (*willekeurige getallen*) and does not say how far the child counts on or back.

### Common mistakes

The child goes back to one, aloud or in a whisper, before counting on from the given number. When counting back the child skips a number or changes to counting forwards. When counting back from a number above ten, the child does not know which word comes before ten or before a number between ten and twenty.
