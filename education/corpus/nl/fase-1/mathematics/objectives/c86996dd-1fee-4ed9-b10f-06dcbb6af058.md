## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating what is and what is not visible from certain viewpoints

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sits on one side of a block building and says what they see, then walks round or swaps places with the child opposite and looks again. The child crouches behind a screen to test whether a doll on the other side can see the toy, and chooses which of several photos was taken from the place where they sit. A child of 4 hides behind something and checks by looking; a child of 7 says without moving what someone on the other side can and cannot see.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal says "certain viewpoints" and names none, and it names no objects. Views from the front, the side and above are not named.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks another person sees exactly what they see themselves. The child thinks they cannot be seen when they cannot see the other person. The child does not allow for a tall object hiding what stands behind it.
