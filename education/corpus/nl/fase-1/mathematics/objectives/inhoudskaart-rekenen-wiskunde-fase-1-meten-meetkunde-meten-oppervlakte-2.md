## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child strokes the top of the table with a flat hand and hears that this is its surface, and says which of two sheets is bigger, which covers more and which fits on top of the other. The child uses big, small, bigger, smaller and as big as, and says that a cloth is too small to cover the table. The child hears and uses the word area for how much room a flat thing takes up. A child of 4 uses big and small for flat things; a child of 6 uses bigger, smaller and as big as for two surfaces and shows the area of a thing by moving a hand over all of it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The word area is in quotation marks: the statement includes dealing with this term itself. The other concepts are not named. Opposites are the card's statement Oppervlakte / 3. No units are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child shows the edge of a thing when asked for its area. The child says long for big and judges a long narrow strip as bigger than a wide square. The child says big without making clear whether height, length or area is meant.
