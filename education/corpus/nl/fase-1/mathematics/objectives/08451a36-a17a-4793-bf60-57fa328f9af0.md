## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with concepts around the division of time (*tijdsindeling*) such as names of the days of the week, parts of the day, seasons and names of the months

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Each morning the child hangs the card for today on the week strip, says the name of the day and says whether it is morning or afternoon. The child puts pictures of the four seasons with the clothes that fit, and finds the month of their birthday on the birthday calendar. A child of 4 knows morning, afternoon, evening and night and some names of days; a child of 7 says the days of the week in order, names the seasons and knows most names of the months.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The four kinds of words after "zoals" are examples: names of the days of the week, parts of the day, seasons, names of the months. The goal does not say that the names are to be known in order. Dates and clock times are not mentioned; clocks are in rw/m/6/05/fase1 and rw/m/6/06/fase1.

### Common mistakes

The child says the days as a rhyme and does not know which day it is today or which comes after it. The child mixes names of days with names of months, or seasons with months. The child ties a season to the weather of the day and calls a cold day in spring winter.
