## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

playing with light and shadow of shapes and figures in sun or torchlight

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stands in the sun in the playground, looks at their own shadow, and makes it move, shrink and join with another child's shadow. With a torch the child shines on a block, a hand or a cut-out shape and watches the shadow on the wall grow when the object comes nearer to the torch. The child turns a block in the light and sees its shadow change shape. A child of 4 makes shadows and tries to step on one; a child of 6 predicts where the shadow will fall and says which object made a given shadow.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is playing: the statement asks for no explanation of how a shadow forms. Two light sources are named, the sun and a torch. No shapes or figures are named, and measuring a shadow is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child takes the shadow for a part of the object and does not connect it with the light. The child expects the shadow on the same side as the light. The child expects the shadow to show the colours and details of the object.
