## English gloss

building a copy (*nabouwen*) of an example/photo/step-by-step plan with suitable construction material

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a tower of five blocks that the teacher built and builds the same tower beside it. The child builds a block bridge from a photo, or follows the pictures of a step-by-step plan one after the other with bricks that click together. A child of 4 copies a small building that stands in front of them; a child of 7 builds from a photo or from a plan of several steps and works out where blocks are hidden in the picture.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording joins three things to build from with slashes: an example, a photo and a step-by-step plan. The material is to be suitable. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: a small Lego car, a building of blocks ("Lego-autootje, blokkenbouwsel"); it is an example, not a limit. No number of blocks or steps is stated.

### Common mistakes

The child uses the right blocks in the wrong place or turned the wrong way, most often with left and right swapped. The child counts only the blocks that the photo shows and leaves out those behind or underneath. The child skips a step of the plan or loses their place in it.
