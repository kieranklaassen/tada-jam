## Official wording

verkennen en ervaren van de begrippen warm en koud in verschillende situaties

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring and experiencing the concepts warm and cold in different situations

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child feels warm and cold water in two bowls, holds an ice cube until it melts and feels the radiator and the window in winter. The child says that it is cold outside and puts on a coat, and that the soup is too hot to eat yet. The child sorts things or pictures into warm and cold: ice cream, tea, snow, a bath. A child of 4 says warm or cold for what they feel; a child of 6 compares two things as warmer and colder and connects the words with situations such as the season, clothing and food.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verbs are exploring and experiencing. Two concepts are named, warm and cold, in different situations; the situations are not named. The statement does not mention a thermometer, degrees or measuring. It is the only statement under Temperatuur on the card.

### Common mistakes

The child judges by the hand, which can mislead: after holding ice, lukewarm water feels hot. The child thinks a coat or a blanket makes warmth by itself, also around a cold object. The child treats warm and cold as two separate kinds and does not yet place lukewarm between them.
