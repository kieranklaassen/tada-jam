## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stacks blocks and says where each one goes: on top, underneath, next to, in between. The child asks for a long plank or a round block and says that the tower is crooked or that the bridge needs a block in the middle. A child of 4 uses words such as on, under and high while building; a child of 6 gives a partner a spoken instruction such as "put the long one across the two short ones".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no concepts and gives no examples. Building itself is the subject of the card's statements Construeren / 2 to Construeren / 5; this statement is about the concepts used while doing so.

### Common mistakes

The child points and says "this one there" instead of using a position word. The child mixes up position words, such as behind and next to. The child uses "big" for long, high and wide alike.
