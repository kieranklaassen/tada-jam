## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks briefly at a handful of marbles on the table, says about how many there are, and then counts to check. The child says whether a jar holds nearer to 5 or nearer to 20 chestnuts. A child of 4 says "many" or "a few", or names a number that has no relation to the pile; a child of 7 gives a number close to the count for piles up to 20 and compares with a group of five or ten that they know.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. The statement itself does not say how close an estimate should be. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: more or less than 10 ("meer of minder dan 10"); it is an example, not a limit.

### Common mistakes

The child counts one by one and does not estimate. The child names a favourite number or a very large number without looking at the quantity. The child judges a pile that lies spread out as much more than the same number lying close together.
