## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Each child puts one cube on the tower for the fruit they like best, and the towers stand side by side on a strip with a picture under each. The child looks which tower is highest and which lowest, and says which fruit was chosen most and which two were chosen equally often. A child of 4 adds their own cube and points to the highest tower; a child of 7 reads the number of each bar, puts the bars in order and says how many more one has than another.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names bar charts. This goal states no number range and no scale for the bars. Making a chart is in rw/verb/1/02/fase1 and reading information from one in rw/verb/1/03/fase1. The code of this record, rw/verb/1/01/fsae1, carries a typing error of the open data ("fsae1" for "fase1") and is kept as the data has it.

### Common mistakes

The child compares bars that do not start on the same line or whose blocks differ in size. The child looks only at the highest bar and can say nothing about the others. The child takes the bar for the thing itself and thinks the highest bar shows the biggest fruit, not the number of choices.
