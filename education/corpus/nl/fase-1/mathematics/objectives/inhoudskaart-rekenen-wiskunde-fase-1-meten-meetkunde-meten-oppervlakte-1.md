## Official wording

ontdekken en ervaren van het meten van oppervlakte

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering and experiencing the measuring of area

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child covers a table with sheets of paper or a mat with tiles and sees how many are needed to cover it. The child lays one sheet on top of another to see whether it sticks out. The child paints a large and a small sheet and notices which takes more paint. A child of 4 covers things and lays one thing on another; a child of 6 covers a surface with equal tiles without gaps and counts them.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verbs are discovering and experiencing: the statement asks for no result. It names no materials and no units. Under Oppervlakte the card has no statement about measuring with informal means: it has one about informal instruments and measures under length (Lengte en omtrek / 5) and one under volume (Inhoud / 5), and one about informal weighing instruments under weight (Gewicht / 5).

### Common mistakes

The child leaves gaps between the tiles or lets them overlap. The child covers only the edge or one row and stops. The child takes area for length and compares only the longest sides.
