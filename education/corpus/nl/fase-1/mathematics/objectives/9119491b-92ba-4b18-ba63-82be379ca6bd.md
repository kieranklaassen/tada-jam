## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with concepts around quantities such as more/fewer, most/fewest, the same number (*evenveel*), few/many, all/nothing, about (*ongeveer*), how many (*hoeveel*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At fruit time the child says who has more pieces of apple and who has fewer, and moves pieces until two plates hold "*evenveel*". The child answers "how many?" with a number and "about how many?" with an estimate, and says when the dish holds nothing. A child of 4 uses "more", "many" and "all gone"; a child of 7 uses "fewer", "fewest", "*evenveel*" and "*ongeveer*" correctly for quantities of objects.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The words after "zoals" are examples: four pairs (*meer/minder*, *meeste/minste*, *weinig/veel*, *alles/niets*) and the words *evenveel*, *ongeveer* and *hoeveel*. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child knows "more" long before "fewer", and points to the larger pile when asked which has fewer. The child uses "*evenveel*" for piles that look the same size without comparing their number. The child takes "*ongeveer*" as "exactly" and starts to count.
