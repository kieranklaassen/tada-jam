## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

hands-on (*handelend*, by acting with real things) adding and subtracting under at least 20 in the context of the counting sequence (*telrij*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stands on the 6 of a number path chalked on the ground, takes 3 steps forward and says where they are now, then takes 2 steps back. The child moves a pawn along a row of numbered squares by the throw of a die, or slides a peg along a string of 20 beads. A child of 4 steps forward on a path to 10 and counts each step aloud; a child of 7 jumps forward and back on a path to 20 or further and says the number they will reach before checking.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "onder ten minste 20" (under at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not make clear whether 20 itself is included. Adding and subtracting are tied here to the counting sequence, that is, to going forward and back along numbers in order. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: 10 little ducks, of which one goes away each time ("10 kleine eendjes; steeds gaat er eentje weg"); it is an example, not a limit. Splitting is not named in this goal; it is named in rw/bew/1/01/fase1. Written sums and signs are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child counts the square they stand on as the first step, which gives a result that is one too low when adding and one too high when subtracting. The child loses track of how many steps have been taken while also saying the numbers. The child steps forward when the task is to go back.
