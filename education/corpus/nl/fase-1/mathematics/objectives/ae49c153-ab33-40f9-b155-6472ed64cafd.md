## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reading a meaningful simple table such as a day plan and week plan of one's own group (*groep*, the class)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at the week plan on the wall, finds the column of today and says what comes after lunch. The child finds the row with their name and sees on which day they water the plants. A child of 4 follows the pictures of the day plan from top to bottom with a finger; a child of 7 finds one cell by combining a row and a column, such as their name and Thursday.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The day plan and week plan of the child's own group follow "zoals" and are an example. The table is to be meaningful and simple; nothing more is said about its size. The goal does not say whether the table holds pictures or words, and it does not mention making a table.

### Common mistakes

The child reads along a row or down a column and does not combine the two to find one cell. The child loses the row while moving a finger across. The child reads the week plan as one list in order and does not notice that the columns stand for different days.
