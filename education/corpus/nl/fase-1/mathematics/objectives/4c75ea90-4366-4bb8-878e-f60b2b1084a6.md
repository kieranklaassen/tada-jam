## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with the rhythm of the day (*dagritme*), the rhythm of the week and the rhythm of the year and cyclical indications of time

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child follows the picture cards of the day from arrival to going home and turns over each card when it is done. The child knows that gym is on a fixed day of the week and that after Sunday the week starts again with Monday, and lays the seasons or the birthdays of the class in a circle. A child of 4 knows what comes next in the school day; a child of 7 says what returns every day, every week and every year, and that after December comes January again.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names three rhythms (day, week, year) and cyclical indications of time, and lists nothing further. The names of days and months are in rw/m/6/01/fase1.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks the row of days or months ends at the last one and does not start again. The child mixes what returns every week with what returns every year, and asks whether it is their birthday again next week. The child knows the order of the day's activities and does not link them to a part of the day.
