## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child finds their own photo on the birthday calendar and says in which month their birthday is and whose birthday comes before theirs. The child looks on the week planner to see on which day gym is, and moves the marker to today. A child of 4 finds their own picture and the place of today with help; a child of 7 looks up what happens on which day, counts the days until an event, and reads the names of days and months.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The birthday calendar and the week planner follow "zoals" and are examples. The calendars are to be simple. The goal names no other kinds of calendar and does not say what makes a calendar simple.

### Common mistakes

When counting the days until an event, the child counts today as well, which gives one too many. The child loses the column of today on the week planner. The child expects the birthday calendar to start with their own birthday.
