## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child counts pairs of shoes by twos: two, four, six. The child counts the fingers on several children's hands by fives and counts bundles of ten sticks by tens. The child jumps along a number path and lands on every second tile. A child of 4 says "two, four, six" as a rhyme or whispers the numbers in between; a child of 7 counts in twos, fives and tens to 20 and further and knows that the last word tells how many there are.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. The brackets name jumps of 2, 5 and 10. The goal does not say at which number the jumps start, and it names no direction of counting, forward or back.

### Common mistakes

The child knows the row of twos as a rhyme and does not link it to pairs of objects, so the words and the objects do not match. The child mixes the rows and goes from "five, ten" to "eleven, twelve". The child says one word of the row of twos for each single object, which gives twice the real number.
