## Official wording

vergelijken en ordenen van getallen in de telrij tot ten minste 20 en verkennen of ze ver of dicht bij elkaar in de buurt liggen

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing and ordering numbers in the counting sequence up to at least 20 and exploring whether they lie far from or close to each other

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child gets three number cards, says which comes earliest and which latest when counting, and lays them in order. The child hangs the cards on a washing line and sees that 9 and 10 hang next to each other and that 3 and 18 hang far apart. A child of 4 says which of two numbers under 10 comes later when counting; a child of 7 orders several numbers up to 20 and further and says of two numbers whether they are close together or far apart.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. The numbers are compared by their place in the counting sequence. "Far" and "close" are explored; working out a difference is not asked. The signs for greater and less are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child has to count from one each time to decide which number comes later. The child judges by the separate digits and says that 9 comes later than 12. The child thinks 19 and 20 lie far apart because the words and the digits look so different.
