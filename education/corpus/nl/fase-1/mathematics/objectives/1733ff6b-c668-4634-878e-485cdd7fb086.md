## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering and experiencing the measuring of length and perimeter (*omtrek*, the distance around something)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child walks along the edge of the sandpit heel to toe and counts the steps, lays a rope along the bench, and lays a ribbon around a tree trunk or around their own head and then holds it out straight. A child of 4 lays things alongside and shows "this long" with the hands; a child of 7 counts steps or sticks for a length and knows that the ribbon around the tree shows the distance around it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names length and perimeter and no instruments or units; informal instruments and measures for length are named in rw/m/1/05/fase1. Metres and centimetres are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child leaves gaps between the steps or lets them overlap. The child does not start at the beginning of the thing that is measured. For the distance around, the child lays the ribbon loosely or does not end where they started.
