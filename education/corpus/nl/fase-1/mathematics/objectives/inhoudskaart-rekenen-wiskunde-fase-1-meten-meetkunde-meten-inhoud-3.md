## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with opposites between concepts around volume (*inhoud*, also capacity: how much fits inside)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child fills one cup to the rim, leaves another empty and names the pair: full and empty. The child pours some out and says that the cup now holds less and the bowl more. The child finds a container that holds a lot and one that holds a little, and says the opposite when the teacher says one word of a pair. A child of 4 names full and empty; a child of 6 uses pairs such as more and less, a lot and a little, and says that if the jug holds more than the cup, the cup holds less than the jug.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about opposites between volume concepts; it names no pairs. The card gives no example of a pair; full and empty is the pack's example. No units are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child knows full and says "not full" for every other state, including empty and half full. The child treats full and empty as the only two states. The child cannot yet turn a comparison round, from "holds more than" to "holds less than".
