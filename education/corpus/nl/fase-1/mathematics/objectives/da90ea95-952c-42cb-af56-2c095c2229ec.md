## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

linking number words, label numbers (*nummers*, numbers that name or mark a place), quantities and numbers (*getallen*) under at least 20

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears "eight", takes eight shells and lays the card with 8 beside them. The child reads 12 on a card and claps twelve times, or finds coat peg number 12 in the corridor. A child of 4 links the words and digits up to five to fingers and small piles; a child of 7 goes between the word, the written number and the quantity for any number under 20 and further.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "onder ten minste 20" (under at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not make clear whether 20 itself is included. The goal names four things that are linked: number words, *nummers*, quantities and *getallen*. Dutch uses *nummer* for a number that names something or marks a place, and *getal* for a number as a value; the text itself does not explain the difference.

### Common mistakes

The child reads the digit correctly and counts out a wrong quantity, because the counting does not stop at the right word. The child links 13 to *dertig* (thirty) and 14 to *veertig* (forty), words that sound alike in Dutch. The child takes a label number as a quantity and expects twelve coats at peg 12.
