## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning about quantities up to at least 20 in suitable problem situations and conflict situations (*conflictsituaties*: an outcome against expectation, or a disagreement)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two rows of 8 counters lie side by side and one row is pushed further apart; the children discuss whether that row now has more. There are 14 children and 12 chairs, and the child says what the problem is and how they know. A child of 4 judges by what they see and may say the longer row has more; a child of 7 counts or pairs to settle it and explains that moving objects does not change how many there are.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. The text does not explain *conflictsituaties* (conflict situations): the word can mean a situation in which the outcome goes against what a child expects, or a disagreement between children, and the wording does not choose. It does not say which situations are suitable. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: the questions "Is five a lot? When is it, and when not?" ("Is vijf veel? Wanneer wel, wanneer niet?"); it is an example, not a limit.

### Common mistakes

The child holds that the row that is spread out has more, even after counting both. The child settles a disagreement by who speaks loudest and not by counting or pairing. The child counts again and again without saying what the counts show.
