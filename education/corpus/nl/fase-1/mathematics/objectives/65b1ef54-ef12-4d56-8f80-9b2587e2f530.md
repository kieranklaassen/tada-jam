## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating number relations up to at least 20 such as neighbour numbers (*buurgetallen*, the numbers just before and after), large or small difference

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes a number card and names its two neighbour numbers, the one before and the one after. The child stands towers of 6 and 7 cubes side by side, and towers of 3 and 15, and says where the difference is small and where it is large. A child of 4 names the number that comes after a number under 10; a child of 7 names both neighbours of numbers up to 20 and says of two numbers whether their difference is large or small.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. The two relations after "zoals" are examples: neighbour numbers, and a large or small difference. The difference is called large or small; working it out exactly is not asked. Other relations, such as double and half, are not named.

### Common mistakes

The child names only the number after and not the number before. The child finds the number before only by counting up from one. The child calls a difference large because the numbers themselves are large, as with 18 and 19.
