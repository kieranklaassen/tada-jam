## Official wording

vergelijken en ordenen van getallen in de getallenrij

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing and ordering numbers in the sequence of numbers (*getallenrij*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes two number cards, finds both on a number line and says which comes earlier and which later. The child puts four or five loose number cards, which do not follow each other directly, in order from small to large. A child of 4 compares two numbers below 5 or 6 with the help of objects; a child of 6 orders several numbers from the range and gives the place on the number line as the reason.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The sub-heading above this statement on the card gives the range "up to at least 20" (*tot tenminste 20*): no upper bound is set, and the Dutch *tot* leaves open whether 20 itself is included. The statement places comparing and ordering in the sequence of numbers (*getallenrij*); the card's statement Telrij / 5 names the same two actions for the counting sequence (*telrij*). The statement does not mention the signs < and >.

### Common mistakes

The child orders cards correctly when the numbers follow each other directly, but not when numbers are missing between them. The child calls the number that comes later in the row the smaller one. The child compares only the last digit, so that 15 is placed before 8.
