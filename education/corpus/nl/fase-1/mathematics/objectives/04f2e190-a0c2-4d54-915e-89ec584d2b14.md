## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning about numbers up to at least 20 in suitable problem situations and conflict situations (*conflictsituaties*: an outcome against expectation, or a disagreement)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children disagree whether the card shows 12 or 21, and the child explains how to tell. In the play shop one price tag reads 9 and another 11, and the child says which toy costs more and why. A child of 4 shows the answer with fingers or blocks; a child of 7 gives a reason in words, such as "11 comes after 10 and 9 comes before it".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. The text does not explain *conflictsituaties* (conflict situations): the word can mean a situation in which the outcome goes against what a child expects, or a disagreement between children, and the wording does not choose. It does not say which situations are suitable. The content-line document prints this goal twice, each time with an example: the questions "Is bus line 8 older than bus line 5? Why are the digits of a PIN code not in the 'right' order of the counting sequence?" ("Is buslijn 8 ouder dan buslijn 5? Waarom staan de cijfers in een pincode niet in de 'goede' telrijvolgorde?"), and the question "Imagine that there are no numbers: what could happen then?" ("Stel je voor dat er geen getallen zijn, wat zou er dan allemaal kunnen gebeuren?"); they are examples, not limits.

### Common mistakes

The child reasons from how the digits look and not from the value, and says that 9 is larger than 11. The child is sure of an answer and cannot give a reason. The child changes a correct answer as soon as someone doubts it.
