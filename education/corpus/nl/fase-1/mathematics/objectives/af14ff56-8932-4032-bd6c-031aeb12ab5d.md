## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

counting quantities (resultative counting, *resultatief tellen*, counting to find how many) up to at least 20 and learning the rules of counting

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child counts the children in the circle, the cups on the table or a handful of buttons, and touches or moves each object once while saying one number word. At the end the child says how many there are. A child of 4 counts up to about ten objects in a row and may count again when asked "how many?"; a child of 7 counts 20 objects and more, also when they lie scattered, and gives the last word as the answer.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. The goal does not list the rules of counting. Saying the sequence without objects is in rw/gb/1/01/fase1.

### Common mistakes

The child counts an object twice or skips one, most often when the objects lie scattered. The child says the number words faster or slower than the finger moves, so that word and object do not match one to one. The child does not know that the last word said tells how many, and counts again when asked.
