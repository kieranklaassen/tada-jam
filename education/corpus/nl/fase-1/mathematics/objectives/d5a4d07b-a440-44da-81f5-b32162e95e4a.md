## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child puts an apple on one pan of a balance and adds marbles to the other pan until the beam is level, and says "the apple weighs as much as eight marbles". Two children sit on a seesaw, and a third child gets on or one moves along until it is level. A child of 4 feels with the hands and watches the seesaw tip; a child of 7 counts how many equal blocks balance a thing and compares two things by those numbers.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The three instruments after "zoals" are examples: seesaw, balance and hands. The instruments are called informal: grams, kilograms and scales with numbers are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child uses objects of unequal weight as the measure, such as large and small blocks together. The child stops adding when the pan begins to move and not when the beam is level. On a seesaw the child does not notice that the place where someone sits changes the result.
