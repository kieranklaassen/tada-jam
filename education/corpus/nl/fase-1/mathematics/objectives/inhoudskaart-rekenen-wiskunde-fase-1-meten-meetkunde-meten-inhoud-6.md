## Official wording

redeneren over inhouden in passende probleem- en conflictsituaties

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning about volumes (*inhouden*, also capacities: how much fits inside) in suitable problem and conflict situations (*conflictsituaties*: a clash of ideas, or a disagreement)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child pours the same cup of water into a tall narrow glass and into a wide bowl and says whether there is now more, less or the same, and why. Two children disagree about whose glass holds more lemonade; they pour both into equal cups to find out. The child predicts whether all the sand from the bucket will fit in the box and afterwards explains the guess. A child of 4 says the higher level is more and is surprised when pouring shows something else; a child of 6 explains that nothing was added or taken away, so the amount is the same.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card does not explain *conflictsituaties*: the word can mean a situation in which what a child expects and what the child finds do not agree, or a disagreement between children. No situation is named. The statement mentions no units.

### Common mistakes

The child says the amount has changed after pouring into a container of another shape, because the level is higher or lower. The child attends to height and not to width. The child tells what happened when pouring and gives no reason.
