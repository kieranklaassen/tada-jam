## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

On a walk the child looks for numbers and says what each is for: a house number, the number on a bus, a price, a shoe size, the candles on a cake, the time on a clock. The child sorts photos of numbers by what they tell: how many, which place in a row, a name for something, a measurement. A child of 4 points out numbers and tells where they have seen them; a child of 7 says of a number whether it tells how many, which place, or only names something.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. This goal states no number range. It does not list the meanings and functions. The verb is exploring.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks every number tells how many, so that bus 12 means twelve buses. The child thinks a higher number is always better or bigger, so that the house at number 20 is bigger than the house at number 4. The child does not see that the same number has a different function in different places (5 years old, 5 euros, bus 5).
