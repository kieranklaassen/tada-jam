## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the play shop the child uses the words that go with money: buying, paying, price, expensive, cheap, change, euro. The child asks "how much is it?", says that something costs too much and says that the money is used up. The child tells the difference between having money, spending it and saving it in a money box. A child of 4 uses buy, pay and money in play; a child of 6 uses expensive and cheap to compare two prices and knows the words euro and change.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no concepts and gives no examples. The card's other statements under Geld are about the values of coins and notes (Geld / 1), buying, selling and paying (Geld / 3) and amounts in whole euros (Geld / 4). Cents are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child uses expensive for anything they want and may not have. The child calls every coin "a euro". The child thinks that getting change means getting more money.
