## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating and describing the place of objects in space with respect to each other and with respect to yourself

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says where things are from where they stand: "the door is behind me, the window is on my left". The child describes things in relation to each other, such as "the bin is under the table, next to the cupboard", then turns round and says what has changed. A child of 4 says where a thing is in relation to their own body or to one thing nearby; a child of 7 describes a place so that another child can find a hidden object, and allows for the other child facing another way.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names two points of reference: the objects with respect to each other, and with respect to yourself. The source prints "t.o.v. van elkaar", in which "van" is doubled, because the abbreviation already contains it; the gloss gives what is meant. The words for places are not listed here; examples are in rw/mk/1/01/fase1.

### Common mistakes

The child describes from their own point of view to someone who faces them, so that left and right are reversed for the listener. The child gives a place that is too vague to find ("over there"). The child does not notice that "behind me" changes when they turn and that "under the table" does not.
