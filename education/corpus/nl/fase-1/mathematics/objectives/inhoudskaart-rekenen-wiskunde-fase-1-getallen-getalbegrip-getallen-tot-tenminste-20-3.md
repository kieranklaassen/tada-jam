## Official wording

oefenen met de vaste volgorde van getallen in de getallenrij

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

practising with the fixed order of numbers in the sequence of numbers (*getallenrij*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays shuffled number cards in order on the floor or pegs them on a line. The child finds the card that is missing from a row, or puts back a card that an adult has taken out or swapped. A child of 4 orders the cards 1 to 5 by counting from 1 each time; a child of 6 orders a longer row and says which number comes before and after a given number without starting at 1.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The sub-heading above this statement on the card gives the range "up to at least 20" (*tot tenminste 20*): no upper bound is set, and the Dutch *tot* leaves open whether 20 itself is included. The card uses *getallenrij* (sequence of numbers) here and *telrij* (counting sequence) under another sub-heading, and does not say how the two differ. The statement says practising; it does not mention counting backwards.

### Common mistakes

The child can recite the sequence aloud but cannot put the written numbers in order. The child swaps written numbers between 11 and 20. The child has to start at 1 every time to find where a number belongs.
