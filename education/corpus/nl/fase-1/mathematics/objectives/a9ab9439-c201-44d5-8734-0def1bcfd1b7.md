## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

sorting objects on the basis of one or more characteristics

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sorts a box of buttons by colour into dishes, then tips them together and sorts them by the number of holes. The child sorts blocks with two hoops that overlap: red in one, round in the other, red and round in the part they share. A child of 4 sorts by one characteristic that is easy to see, such as colour; a child of 7 sorts by two characteristics at once and says the rule behind another child's sorting.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal says "one or more" characteristics and names no particular ones. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: collecting all the red triangles ("alle rode driehoeken verzamelen"); it is an example, not a limit. The goal states no number of objects or groups.

### Common mistakes

The child changes the rule halfway, starting by colour and going on by shape. With two characteristics, the child attends to one and ignores the other. The child leaves out an object that fits no group or fits two groups.
