## Official wording

vergelijken en ordenen van de getallen in de getallenrij tot ten minste 20

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing and ordering the numbers in the number sequence (*getallenrij*) up to at least 20

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes three number cards from a bag and lays them from smallest to largest. Two children compare the numbers on their cards and say whose number comes later in the row. The child puts numbered boxes back on the shelf in order. A child of 4 orders two or three cards under 10 with a number row on the wall as help; a child of 7 orders five or more numbers up to 20 and further without help.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. This goal stands in the cluster on numbers (rw/gb/3); comparing and ordering in the counting sequence is in rw/gb/1/05/fase1. The goal does not say how many numbers are ordered at once, and it does not mention the signs for greater and less.

### Common mistakes

The child compares the separate digits and says that 8 is larger than 15. The child orders from largest to smallest when smallest to largest was asked. The child puts two cards in the right order and cannot fit a third card between them.
