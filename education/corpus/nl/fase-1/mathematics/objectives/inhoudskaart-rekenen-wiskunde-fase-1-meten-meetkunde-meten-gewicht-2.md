## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lifts things and uses the words for what they feel: heavy, light, heavier, lighter, as heavy as, the heaviest. The child says "this bag is heavier than that one" and, after trying both on a balance, "they weigh the same". The child uses the words weigh and balance in the play shop when selling apples. A child of 4 uses heavy and light; a child of 6 uses heavier, lighter, heaviest, lightest and as heavy as.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no concepts and gives no examples. Opposites between these concepts are the card's statement Gewicht / 3, and comparing and ordering by weight is Gewicht / 4. No units are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child says big for heavy and small for light. The child uses heavy for anything that is hard to lift, also when it is only awkward to hold. The child says heavier without saying heavier than what.
