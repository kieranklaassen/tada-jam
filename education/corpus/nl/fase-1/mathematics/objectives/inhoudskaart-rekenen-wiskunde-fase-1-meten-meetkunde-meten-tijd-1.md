omgaan met begrippen rond tijdsindeling zoals namen van de dagen van de week, delen van de dag, seizoenen en namen van de maanden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with concepts around the division of time such as names of the days of the week, parts of the day, seasons and names of the months

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the morning circle the child says which day it is, moves the marker on the week chart and says which day was yesterday and which comes tomorrow. The child says the days of the week in a rhyme or song, names morning, afternoon, evening and night and says what happens in each. The child names the season, says what the weather and the trees are like in it, and says in which month their birthday falls. A child of 4 names morning, evening and night and some days of the week; a child of 6 says the days in order, names the four seasons and knows several months.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Four kinds of concept come after "such as" and are examples: the names of the days of the week, the parts of the day, the seasons and the names of the months. The statement does not mention saying these in order, dates, or reading or writing the names. The rhythms of the day, the week and the year are the card's statement Tijd / 3.

### Common mistakes

The child recites the days of the week as a rhyme and cannot say which day comes after a given day without starting from the beginning. The child mixes up yesterday and tomorrow. The child confuses the kinds of name and answers "summer" or "June" when asked what day it is.
