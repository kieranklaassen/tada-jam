## Official wording

nabouwen van een voorbeeld/foto/stappenplan met passend constructiemateriaal

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

building a copy of an example/photo/step-by-step plan (*stappenplan*) with suitable construction material

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a block building that stands on the table and builds the same one beside it, block by block. The child builds from a photo of a building or follows a card with numbered steps, and first lays out the pieces that are needed. The child checks by comparing the copy with the example from the front and from the side. A child of 4 copies a small building of a few blocks that stands next to them; a child of 6 builds from a photo or a step-by-step plan and counts how many blocks are in each layer.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The slashes name three things to build from: an example, a photo and a step-by-step plan. The material is to be suitable; no material is named. The statement sets no number of pieces and does not say whether the example has blocks that cannot be seen.

### Common mistakes

The child copies the front of the building and leaves out the blocks that cannot be seen in the photo. The child builds a mirror image of the example. The child uses the right number of blocks in the wrong positions, or the right shape with blocks of another size.
