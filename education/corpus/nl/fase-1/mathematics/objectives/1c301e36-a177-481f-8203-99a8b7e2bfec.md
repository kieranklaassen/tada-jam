## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

constructing with paper (e.g. folding, folding a copy, building, cutting) and on paper (e.g. designing patterns)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child folds a sheet in half and in half again, folds a boat by copying a folded example step by step, and cuts into a folded sheet to make a shape that is the same on both sides when opened. The child builds a small tower from rolled and folded paper, and on squared paper colours a border of their own design in which two colours take turns. A child of 4 folds once, cuts along a line and colours a row of squares; a child of 7 folds edge to edge and corner to corner in several steps and designs a pattern that repeats.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal has two parts: constructing with paper and constructing on paper. Each has examples in brackets after "bijv.": folding, folding a copy of an example (*navouwen*), building and cutting with paper; designing patterns on paper. These are examples. The goal names no shapes, no kind of pattern and no result.

### Common mistakes

The child folds without laying edge on edge, so that the two halves are unequal. The child cuts along the fold line, so that the shape comes apart in two pieces. The child starts a pattern and does not keep to its rule, such as two colours that take turns followed by the same colour twice.
