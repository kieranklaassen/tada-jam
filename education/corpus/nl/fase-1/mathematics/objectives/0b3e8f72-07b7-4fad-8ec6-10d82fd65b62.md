## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child shows how many children sit at the table with fingers, with one block for each child, with tally marks on paper or with a drawing of dots. The child then shows the same quantity in a second way, such as beads on a string for the marks on the paper. A child of 4 holds up fingers or lays one block for each object for small quantities; a child of 7 draws marks in groups of five or writes the number for quantities up to 20.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: with fingers, pictures, small blocks, (number) symbols ("met vingers, plaatjes, blokjes, (getal-)symbolen"); it is an example, not a limit.

### Common mistakes

The child draws the objects themselves in detail and loses count, so that the drawing shows another number. The child makes marks without matching one mark to one object. The child shows a quantity above ten with fingers and loses the first ten.
