## Official wording

eenvoudige geldbedragen samenstellen in hele euro's

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

making up simple amounts of money in whole euros

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays down coins for a price on a tag: three 1-euro coins for 3 euros, or one 2-euro coin and one 1-euro coin. The child counts the euros aloud while putting them down and checks the total. The child makes the same amount in two ways and exchanges two 1-euro coins for one 2-euro coin. A child of 4 lays down one 1-euro coin for each euro of a small price; a child of 6 combines 1-euro and 2-euro coins and a 5-euro note and counts on from the larger value.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The amounts are simple and in whole euros; cents are not part of the wording. The statement gives no highest amount and does not say which coins or notes are used. It does not mention giving change.

### Common mistakes

The child counts a 2-euro coin as one euro, because the child counts coins instead of values. The child loses the total when going from a 2-euro coin to 1-euro coins while counting. The child thinks an amount can be made in one way only.
