## Official wording

construeren met papier (vouwen, navouwen, bouwen, knippen) en op papier (patronen ontwerpen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

constructing with paper (folding, folding after an example (*navouwen*), building, cutting) and on paper (designing patterns)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child folds a sheet in half, corner to corner or edge to edge, and follows a folding example step by step to make a hat or a boat. The child cuts along a line, cuts out shapes and glues strips and rolls of paper into a standing building. On paper the child draws or stamps a repeating border. A child of 4 folds a sheet once and cuts along a straight line; a child of 6 follows several folds from an example and cuts into a folded sheet to get a shape with two equal halves.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The first brackets name four actions with paper: folding, folding after an example, building and cutting. The second brackets name one action on paper: designing patterns. The statement names no folds, no models and no shapes.

### Common mistakes

The child folds without lining up the corners, so the two halves do not match. The child skips a step of the folding example or folds in the wrong direction. The child cuts through the folded edge that has to hold the shape together.
