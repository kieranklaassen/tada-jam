## English gloss

exploring the role of money in buying, selling and paying such as with coins and with a debit card (*pinpas*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the play shop one child is the customer and one the shopkeeper: the customer chooses, hears the price, hands over coins or holds a card against the play terminal, and takes the goods. The shopkeeper puts the coins in the till and hands over the goods, and then the two swap roles. A child of 4 acts out giving money and getting things; a child of 7 knows that paying by card also uses up money.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Paying with coins and with a debit card follow "zoals" and are examples. The verb is exploring. No amounts are stated, and change, saving and banks are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks a card gives money without end, because no coins are handed over. The child sees change as money that the shop gives away. The child thinks that a customer who gets several coins back as change has more money than before.
