## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

problem solving, critical thinking and reasoning about adding, counting together (*bij elkaar tellen*), subtracting, splitting with quantities in meaningful situations

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sets the table for six, finds four plates in the cupboard and works out with the plates how many are missing. Two children each have some marbles and work out how many they have together, and one explains why the other's answer cannot be right. A child of 4 solves it by fetching and counting the objects; a child of 7 first says a way of working it out and then checks it with the objects or on fingers.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names adding, counting together, subtracting and splitting, all with quantities and in meaningful situations. This goal states no number range; rw/bew/1/01/fase1 and rw/bew/1/03/fase1 state "onder ten minste 20" for their own content. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: a question such as "How can you throw 7 with two dice?" ("Hoe kun je 7 gooien met twee dobbelstenen?"); it is an example, not a limit. The goal does not say which situations count as meaningful.

### Common mistakes

The child adds the two numbers they hear without asking whether the situation is about joining or about taking away. The child gives an answer and cannot say how it was found. The child does not notice that an answer cannot be right, for example more objects left over than there were at the start.
