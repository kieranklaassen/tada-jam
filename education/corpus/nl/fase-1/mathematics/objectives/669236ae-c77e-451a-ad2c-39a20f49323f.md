## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

continuing, and designing oneself, *ketting* patterns (*ketting*: a chain, or a necklace of beads) and mosaic patterns

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child threads beads red, blue, red, blue and carries the order on to the end of the string. The child lays coloured tiles in a repeating pattern on a board and thinks up a pattern of their own for another child to continue. A child of 4 continues a pattern of two colours in turn; a child of 7 continues patterns with a repeating part of three or four, or with shape and colour together, and designs a mosaic that repeats in rows.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names two kinds of pattern and two actions, continuing and designing. It does not say how long the repeating part is or which properties (colour, shape, size) change.

### Common mistakes

The child continues correctly for a few beads and then changes to a favourite colour. The child looks only at the last bead and not at the whole repeating part, so that red, blue, blue is continued as red, blue. When designing, the child makes a row of colours in which nothing repeats.
