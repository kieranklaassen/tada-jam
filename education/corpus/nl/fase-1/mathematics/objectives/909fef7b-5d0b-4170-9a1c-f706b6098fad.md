## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

hands-on (*handelend*, by acting with real things) handing out (*uitdelen*) and sharing out (*verdelen*) of quantities under at least 20 but also with large quantities where it is about the action

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hands out 12 grapes to 3 dolls, one to each in turn until the bowl is empty, and then looks whether each doll has the same number. The child shares a large bucket of chestnuts over four trays by putting one in each tray in turn, without counting the total. A child of 4 hands out one each and stops when everyone has something; a child of 7 hands out until nothing is left, checks that the shares are equal and says how many each got.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "onder ten minste 20" (under at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not make clear whether 20 itself is included. For large quantities the goal says that the action is what matters; the statement itself sets no size for "large". The content-line document prints an example with this goal: sharing out 100 loose euros among four ("100 losse euro's verdelen met z'n vieren"); it is an example, not a limit. The goal does not say that the shares must be equal; words such as fair and the same number are in rw/bew/3/02/fase1. Multiplying, division sums and their signs are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child does not hand out one each in turn, and gives several objects to the first child and too few to the last. The child stops when everyone has something, although objects are left. The child judges the shares as equal by how the piles look and not by their number.
