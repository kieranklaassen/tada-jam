## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children hold the same plan of the playground and point to different spots for the hidden object; they turn the plan and find out who is right. A child explains why the doll cannot see the car behind the box. A child of 4 goes and looks; a child of 7 reasons from the position before checking, such as "you sit on the other side, so for you it is on the left".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The words in quotation marks are the name of this cluster (rw/mk/1), whose other goals are about words for places, the place of objects, drawings and plans, viewpoints and routes. The text does not explain *conflictsituaties* (conflict situations): the word can mean a situation in which the outcome goes against what a child expects, or a disagreement between children, and the wording does not choose. It does not say which situations are suitable.

### Common mistakes

The child holds that their own view is the only one. The child checks by walking there and does not connect the result with a reason. The child gives a description in place of a reason ("because it is there").
