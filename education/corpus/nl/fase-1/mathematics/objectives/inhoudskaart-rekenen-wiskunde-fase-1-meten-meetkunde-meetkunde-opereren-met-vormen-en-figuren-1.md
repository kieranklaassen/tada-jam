## Official wording

sorteren van voorwerpen op basis van één of meer kenmerken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

sorting objects on the basis of one or more attributes

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child puts buttons, blocks or leaves into trays: all the red ones together, or all the round ones together. The child then sorts the same set again in another way, by size instead of colour, and says what belongs in each tray. With two attributes the child looks for the things that are both big and blue. A child of 4 sorts by one attribute, most often colour; a child of 6 sorts by two attributes at once and names the rule of a set that someone else sorted.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording says one or more attributes; it names no attributes and no objects, and it sets no highest number of attributes or of objects. The statement stands under the sub-heading about shapes and figures, but the wording is not limited to shape.

### Common mistakes

The child starts sorting by colour and changes to shape partway through. The child cannot sort the same set again by another attribute and keeps to the first rule. With two attributes the child attends to one, and puts every blue thing with the big blue things.
