## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

practising with the fixed order of numbers in the number sequence (*getallenrij*) up to at least 20

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays number cards 1 to 20 in a row in order, fills the empty places on a number line with the missing cards, and puts back cards that were swapped. The child joins numbered dots in order to make a drawing. A child of 4 lays the cards 1 to 5 or 1 to 10 in order while saying the counting sequence; a child of 7 lays 1 to 20 and further and puts a single card in its place without starting at 1.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. The verb is practising. This goal stands in the cluster on numbers (rw/gb/3) and speaks of the *getallenrij*; reciting the counting sequence is in rw/gb/1/01/fase1.

### Common mistakes

The child swaps 12 and 21, or 13 and 31, in the row. The child lays the cards to ten in order and then places the numbers above ten at random. The child has to start from 1 to find the place of any card.
