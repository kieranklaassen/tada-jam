## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

constructing a picture graph (*beelddiagram*, a chart built from pictures) or bar chart

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Each child sticks a picture of how they came to school in the right column, one above the other, and the child checks that every picture takes up one place. The child stacks one block or colours one square for each object counted, and gives each column a picture or object as its label. A child of 4 adds their own picture to a chart an adult has started; a child of 6 sorts a small collection, sets up the columns on a common base line and fills them in.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names two kinds of chart, a picture graph and a bar chart, joined by "or". It sets no number range and no number of columns, and does not say who collects what is charted. It does not mention a numbered axis.

### Common mistakes

The child places pictures or squares with gaps or in unequal sizes, so the height of a column does not match its number. The child does not start the columns on the same line. The child puts in more than one mark for one object, or forgets objects.
