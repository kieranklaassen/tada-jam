## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child eats the last grape from a dish and says how many are left. The child counts back from five and ends at zero, finds the 0 on a ruler or on a lift button, and talks about whether zero is a number. A child of 4 says "nothing" or "all gone" for an empty dish; a child of 7 says and writes zero and places it before 1 in the number row.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is thinking about. This goal states no number range, and it does not mention calculating with zero.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks zero is not a number because there is nothing to count. The child starts a count of objects at zero, or leaves zero out of the number row. The child reads the 0 in 10 or 20 as "nothing" and takes 10 to be the same as 1.
