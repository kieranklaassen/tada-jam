## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

constructing a picture graph (*beelddiagram*, a chart built from pictures) or bar chart

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Each child sticks a picture of how they came to school (on foot, by bicycle, by car) in the right column on a large sheet, one above the other from a base line. The child colours one square in a column for each tally mark on a list about pets. A child of 4 places their own picture in the right column with help; a child of 7 collects the answers, draws the base line, builds the columns from squares of equal size and puts a picture or word under each.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names two kinds of chart, joined by "or": a picture graph or a bar chart. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: for the favourite fruit of all the children, with the help of pictures ("voor het lievelingsfruit van alle kinderen m.b.v. plaatjes"); it is an example, not a limit. This goal states no number range, no number of columns and no scale, and it does not say who collects the data.

### Common mistakes

The child places pictures of different sizes or with gaps between them, so that the height of a column no longer shows the number. The child does not start every column on the same line. The child puts their picture in the column a friend chose and not in the one that fits their own answer.
