## Official wording

ontdekken en ervaren van het meten van inhoud

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering and experiencing the measuring of volume (*inhoud*, also capacity: how much fits inside)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At the water table or in the sandpit the child fills a cup, pours it into a bucket and fills it again until the bucket is full. The child pours water from a jug into a tall glass and sees it overflow or stay below the rim. The child packs blocks into a box and finds out how many fit. A child of 4 fills, pours and empties; a child of 6 counts how many cups go into the jug and says that this tells how much the jug holds.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verbs are discovering and experiencing: the statement asks for no result. It names no materials, instruments or units; informal instruments and measures for volume are named in the card's statement Inhoud / 5.

### Common mistakes

The child does not fill the cup to the same height each time, or spills, and still counts each cup. The child loses count of the cups poured. The child thinks the taller container always holds more.
