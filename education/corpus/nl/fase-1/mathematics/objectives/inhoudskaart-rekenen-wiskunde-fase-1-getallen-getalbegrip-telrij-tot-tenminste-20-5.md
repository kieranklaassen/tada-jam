## Official wording

vergelijken en ordenen van getallen in de telrij en verkennen of ze ver of dicht bij elkaar in de buurt liggen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing and ordering numbers in the counting sequence (*telrij*) and exploring whether they lie far from or close to each other

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hangs number cards on a line in order and says which of two numbers comes first when counting. The child stands on one number tile and a partner on another, and they count the steps between them to see whether the numbers are close together or far apart. A child of 4 says which of two small numbers comes later when counting; a child of 6 orders several numbers and says, for example, that 9 is close to 10 and far from 2.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The sub-heading above this statement on the card gives the range "up to at least 20" (*tot tenminste 20*): no upper bound is set, and the Dutch *tot* leaves open whether 20 itself is included. The second part says exploring whether numbers lie far from or close to each other; it does not ask for the distance as a number. The card's statement Getallen / 4 names comparing and ordering in the sequence of numbers (*getallenrij*).

### Common mistakes

The child has to recite from one to decide which of two numbers comes later. The child judges closeness by how alike the digits look, and calls 12 and 21 close. The child counts both end tiles when counting the steps between two numbers and gets one too many.
