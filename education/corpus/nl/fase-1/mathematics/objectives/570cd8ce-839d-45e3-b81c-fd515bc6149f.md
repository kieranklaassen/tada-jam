## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing flexibly with the relation between the counting sequence, quantities and numbers under at least 20

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child has 9 blocks, gets one more and says "ten" without counting again, because ten comes after nine. The child sees the cards 15 and 14 and says which pile will be larger before the piles are laid out. A child of 4 knows that the last word of a count tells how many and counts again after any change; a child of 7 uses the counting sequence to say what one more or one fewer is, and goes between counting, quantity and written number as needed.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "onder ten minste 20" (under at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not make clear whether 20 itself is included. The goal names three things: the counting sequence, quantities and numbers. The statement itself does not say what "flexibly" asks. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: if a tin has 10 on it, you know that 10 are in it; if you have counted six apples, you can say that there are 'six' ("als op een blik 10 staat, weet je dat er 10 inzitten; als je zes appels hebt geteld, kun je zeggen dat het er 'zes' zijn"); it is an example, not a limit. Number words and *nummers* are named in rw/gb/3/08/fase1.

### Common mistakes

The child counts the whole pile again after one object is added. The child knows which number comes later in the sequence and does not know that it stands for the larger quantity. The child answers the question "how many?" by counting again and does not give the last word of the count.
