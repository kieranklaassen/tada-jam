## Official wording

doortellen en terugtellen vanaf willekeurige getallen tot ten minste 20

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

counting on and counting back from arbitrary numbers up to at least 20

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Someone says "seven" and the child goes on: eight, nine, ten. Someone says "fifteen" and the child counts back: fourteen, thirteen, twelve. The child also does this while stepping forward and back along a row of numbered tiles. A child of 4 counts on from a small number and often has to start at one first; a child of 7 counts on and back from any number up to 20 and further without starting again at one.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states its range as "tot ten minste 20" (up to at least 20): the offer reaches 20 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not say whether 20 itself is included. The wording leaves open whether the range applies to the starting numbers, to how far the counting goes, or to both. Counting in steps larger than one is not named here; jumps are in rw/gb/1/06/fase1.

### Common mistakes

The child has to begin at one to find the starting number. When counting back, the child skips a number or goes forward again after a few words, most often between thirteen and ten (*dertien*, *twaalf*, *elf*, *tien*). The child says the starting number again as the first word of the count.
