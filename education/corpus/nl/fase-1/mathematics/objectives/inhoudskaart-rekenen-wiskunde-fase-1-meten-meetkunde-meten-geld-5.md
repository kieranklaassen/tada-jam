## Official wording

redeneren over geld in passende probleem- en conflictsituaties

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning about money in suitable problem and conflict situations (*conflictsituaties*: a clash of ideas, or a disagreement)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child has 5 play euros and wants two things; the child works out whether the money is enough and says why or why not. Two children disagree about who has more, one with four 1-euro coins and one with a single 5-euro note, and they lay the money out to compare. The child says what to do when the money is not enough: choose something cheaper, or put one thing back. A child of 4 says "I have a lot" and compares by the number of coins; a child of 6 compares by value and explains that one note can be worth more than several coins.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The card does not explain *conflictsituaties*: the word can mean a situation in which what a child expects and what the child finds do not agree, or a disagreement between children. No situation and no amount is named. The card's statement on making up amounts (Geld / 4) keeps to whole euros.

### Common mistakes

The child judges who has more by the number of coins instead of their value. The child adds up what they want and forgets that the money spent on the first thing is no longer there for the second. The child does not check whether the money is enough before choosing.
