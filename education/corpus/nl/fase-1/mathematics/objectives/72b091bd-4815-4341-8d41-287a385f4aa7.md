## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child walks from the classroom to the gym and tells the way in steps: out of the door, left, past the coats, down the stairs. The child lays a route of arrows on the floor for another child to follow, and draws the way a toy car takes through a town of blocks. A child of 4 follows a short route of arrows or a rope and walks a way they know; a child of 7 designs a route with several turns, gives it in words or arrows, and checks it by letting someone else walk it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The routes are to be simple; the source puts the word routes in quotation marks. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: the walking route from classroom to gym ("de looproute van klaslokaal naar gymzaal"); it is an example, not a limit. The first activity under "In a child's hands" is this example. The goal does not say how a route is given (in words, with arrows, in a drawing or on a plan), and it does not mention a grid or counting steps.

### Common mistakes

The child leaves out a turn or a step that seems obvious to them. The child gives left and right as seen from the starting point and not as seen in the direction of walking. The child names the places that are passed in the wrong order.
