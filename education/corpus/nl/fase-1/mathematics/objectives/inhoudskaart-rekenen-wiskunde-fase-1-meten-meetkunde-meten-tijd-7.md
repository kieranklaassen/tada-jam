## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tears off or turns the day on a day calendar and finds today on the week chart. On the birthday calendar the child finds their own name, and the child crosses off the days until a party or an outing and counts the nights that remain. The child puts a picture of the weather or of a coming event on the right day of the week calendar. A child of 4 moves the marker to today with help and points to tomorrow; a child of 6 finds a day on a week or month calendar, counts the days until an event and says which day it will be.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The calendars are to be simple; no kind of calendar is named. The statement does not mention reading or writing dates and sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child counts today as one of the days still to wait, or leaves out the last day, and miscounts by one. On a month calendar the child goes from the end of one row to the box below it instead of to the start of the next row. The child thinks a day that has been crossed off is gone and does not expect that day of the week to come again next week.
