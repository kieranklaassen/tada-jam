## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with concepts around constructing such as straight (*recht*), slanting (*schuin*), double (*dubbel*), line (*lijn*), *hoek* (corner, or angle), point (*punt*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While folding, the child folds the sheet "*dubbel*", presses a straight fold line and folds a corner to the middle. While building, the child sets a plank straight or slanting against a tower and points to the point of a roof. A child of 4 follows the words when an adult shows the action with them; a child of 7 uses the words to tell another child how to fold or build something.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The six words after "zoals" are examples: *recht*, *schuin*, *dubbel*, *lijn*, *hoek*, *punt*. Several have more than one meaning and the text does not choose: *recht* is straight and also upright, *hoek* is corner and also angle, *punt* is a point, a tip or a dot. Measuring angles and using a ruler are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child mixes "straight" in the sense of not bent with "upright", and calls a straight line that slants not straight. The child folds "double" without laying the edges on each other. The child mixes corner and point (*hoek* and *punt*).
