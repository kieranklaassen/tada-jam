## Official wording

onderzoeken en omschrijven van de plaats van voorwerpen in de ruimte t.o.v. van elkaar en t.o.v. jezelf

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating and describing the place of objects in space relative to each other and relative to yourself

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks round the room and says where things are: the bin is next to the door, the ball is under the table, the cupboard is behind me. The child places toys as told, "put the cow in front of the barn and the horse behind it", and tells another child where a hidden object is. The child turns round and notices that what was in front is now behind. A child of 4 says where one thing is in relation to themselves or to one other thing; a child of 6 describes a place with two relations, such as "on the shelf, next to the books".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two reference points are named: the objects relative to each other and relative to the child. The source prints "t.o.v. van elkaar", with the word "van" doubled; the gloss gives what is plainly meant. The statement names no position words and no number of objects.

### Common mistakes

The child describes every place from their own position and cannot yet describe it from the position of an object or of another person. The child says "there" and points instead of using a position word. The child mixes up in front of and behind for a thing that has a front of its own and faces the child, such as a doll or a toy car.
