## Official wording

verkennen van de rol van geld bij kopen, verkopen en betalen zoals met munten en met pinpas

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring the role of money in buying, selling and paying, such as with coins and with a debit card (*pinpas*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child plays shop: one child is the customer and hands over coins, the other is the seller, takes the money and gives the goods. The child pays at the toy till by holding a play card against the reader, and talks about what happens to the money when a parent pays by card. The child finds out that the seller gets the money, that the buyer gets the thing, and that money is gone after paying. A child of 4 hands over a handful of coins and takes the goods; a child of 6 looks at the price, pays that many euros and knows that paying by card also takes money away.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is exploring. Three activities are named: buying, selling and paying. Coins and the debit card come after "such as" and are examples of ways to pay. The statement sets no amounts. Banknotes are not among its examples of ways to pay (the card names them in its statement Geld / 1), and the statement names no use of money other than buying, selling and paying.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks that a card never runs out and that paying by card costs nothing. The child thinks the shop gives money as well as goods, because the customer gets change. The child pays with any number of coins without looking at the price.
