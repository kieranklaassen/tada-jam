## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays pictures of getting up, having breakfast and going to school in order and tells the story from them. The child lays photos of a seed growing into a plant in a row, and tells what happened first, next and last on the class outing. A child of 4 orders two or three pictures of a routine they know; a child of 7 orders five or more events, also events spread over days or weeks, and uses "first", "then", "after that" and "last".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal states no number of events and no span of time, and it names no means such as pictures.

### Common mistakes

The child starts with the event that mattered most to them and not with the first one. The child gets the first and the last event right and mixes the ones in the middle. The child lays the pictures in a heap or in no fixed direction, so that the order cannot be read.
