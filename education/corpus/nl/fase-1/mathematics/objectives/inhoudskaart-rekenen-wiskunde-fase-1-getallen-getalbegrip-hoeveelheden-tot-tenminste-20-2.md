## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a handful of chestnuts or a jar of marbles and says about how many there are before anyone counts. Afterwards the child counts them and says whether the estimate was too high, too low or close. A child of 4 says "a lot" or names any number they know; a child of 6 names a number near the real one for a group of up to about twenty and uses a known group, such as five, to judge by.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The sub-heading above this statement on the card gives the range "up to at least 20" (*tot tenminste 20*): no upper bound is set, and the Dutch *tot* leaves open whether 20 itself is included. The statement does not say how close an estimate should be or how the quantities are presented. The word "about" (*ongeveer*) is among the examples of the card's statement Hoeveelheden / 4.

### Common mistakes

The child starts counting instead of estimating. The child names a very large number for any group that cannot be taken in at one look. The child treats an estimate that is not exactly the counted number as wrong.
