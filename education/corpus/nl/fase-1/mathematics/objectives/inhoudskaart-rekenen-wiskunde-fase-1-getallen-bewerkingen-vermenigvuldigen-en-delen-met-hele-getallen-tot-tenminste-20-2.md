## Official wording

handelend omgaan met begrippen rond delen zoals eerlijk, oneerlijk, delen, verdelen, over, evenveel

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing hands-on (*handelend*, through the child's own actions) with concepts around *delen* (dividing or sharing) such as fair (*eerlijk*), unfair (*oneerlijk*), *delen*, sharing out (*verdelen*), left over (*over*), the same number (*evenveel*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While sharing out fruit the child says "that is not fair, she has more" and moves pieces until both plates hold the same number. The child deals out blocks, holds up the block that cannot be shared and says it is left over. A child of 4 uses "share" and "fair"; a child of 6 also uses "unfair", "the same number" and "left over" and shows with the objects what each word means.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The sub-heading above this statement on the card gives the range "up to at least 20" (*tot tenminste 20*): no upper bound is set, and the Dutch *tot* leaves open whether 20 itself is included. The six words after "such as" (*zoals*) are examples: *eerlijk*, *oneerlijk*, *delen*, *verdelen*, *over*, *evenveel*. The Dutch *delen* means both dividing and sharing, and the statement does not choose. The statement is about words used while acting; the word half is not among its examples, and it does not mention the division sign.

### Common mistakes

The child calls a division fair when everyone has something, also when the numbers differ. The child judges "the same number" by how much space a share takes up, not by counting. The child calls a share unfair because its objects are smaller or have a less liked colour, not because of the number.
