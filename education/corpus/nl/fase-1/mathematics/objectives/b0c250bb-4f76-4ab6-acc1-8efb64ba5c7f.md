## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at the class chart of birthdays per season and says in which season most children have their birthday. The child counts the pictures in a column to say how many children come by bicycle, and finds the column with none. A child of 4 points to the column with the most and the column with the fewest; a child of 7 says the number for each column and compares two columns.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name picture graph and bar chart. This goal states no number range. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: reading off which fruit most children in the group like best, or how many children like banana best ("aflezen welk fruit de meeste kinderen in de groep het lekkerste vinden of hoeveel kinderen banaan het lekkerste vinden"); it is an example, not a limit. Reading a simple table is in rw/verb/1/04/fase1.

### Common mistakes

The child reads the height of a bar without looking at what the column stands for. The child counts the pictures in a column wrongly, by skipping one or by counting the label picture at the bottom as well. The child answers from their own preference and not from the chart.
