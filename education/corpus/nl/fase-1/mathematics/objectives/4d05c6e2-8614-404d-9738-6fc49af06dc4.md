## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A block bridge keeps falling in, and the child says why and what to change: a longer plank, or the pillars closer together. Two children compare their buildings with the photo and say what differs. A child of 4 tries another block and tells what happened; a child of 7 says beforehand what will work and why, such as "the wide block must go at the bottom".

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal speaks of suitable geometric problems around constructing. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: a question such as "How can you build firmly?" ("Hoe kun je stevig bouwen?"); it is an example, not a limit. Unlike rw/mk/3/06/fase1 and rw/mk/1/06/fase1, its wording does not mention conflict situations.

### Common mistakes

The child tries again in the same way without changing anything. The child gives chance or another child as the cause, and not the way the thing was built. The child sees that two buildings differ and cannot say where.
