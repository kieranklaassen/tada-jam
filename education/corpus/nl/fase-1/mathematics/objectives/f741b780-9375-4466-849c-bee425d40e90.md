## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring and experiencing the concepts warm and cold in different situations (indoors and outdoors, warm and cold water)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child puts one hand in a bowl of cold water and the other in lukewarm water and says which is which. The child steps outside without a coat for a moment in winter and comes back in, feels a window sill in the sun and one in the shade, and holds an ice cube until it melts. A child of 4 says "warm" and "cold" for what they feel; a child of 7 compares warmer and colder and puts three bowls of water in order from coldest to warmest.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1 (*groep* 1 to 3, about ages 4 to 7), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name indoors and outdoors, and warm and cold water. This is the only fase 1 goal of the cluster on temperature (rw/m/5). A thermometer, degrees and measuring are not mentioned. The text states no safety condition for the warm water.

### Common mistakes

The child judges with a hand that was in cold water just before, so that lukewarm water feels hot. The child thinks a coat or a blanket gives off warmth by itself. The child treats warm and cold as fixed properties and does not see that the same water feels warm after ice and cold after a warm bath.
