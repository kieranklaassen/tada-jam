## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with 'length' and 'perimeter' (*omtrek*, the distance around something) and concepts around length and perimeter

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child uses the words long, short, high, low, wide, narrow, thick, thin, far and near for the right kind of thing, and says longer than, shorter than and as long as. The child hears and uses the words length and perimeter themselves: "the length of the table", "the perimeter of the sandpit is how far it is all the way round". The child moves a finger along an edge to show what each word means. A child of 4 uses long, short, high and low; a child of 6 uses longer, shortest, wide and narrow and shows what the length and the perimeter of an object are.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The words length and perimeter are in quotation marks: the statement includes dealing with these two terms themselves. The other concepts are not named. Opposites are the card's statement Lengte en omtrek / 3. No units are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child uses big for long, high, wide and thick alike. The child mixes up high and long for a thing that can stand or lie. The child shows one side of an object when asked for its perimeter.
