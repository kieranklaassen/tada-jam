## Official wording

flexibel omgaan met de relatie tussen telrij, hoeveelheden en getallen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - kleuters (fase 1), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing flexibly with the relation between the counting sequence (*telrij*), quantities and numbers

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child is given a number card and lays that many objects, is given objects and finds the card, or is given a place on the number line and says how many objects belong to it. The child sees that one more object means the next number in the counting sequence, and one object fewer the number before. A child of 4 goes from a small group to its number word; a child of 6 goes in every direction between counting, group and written number without starting again at one.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 1, for the youngest children (*groep* 1 and 2, about ages 4 to 6), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The sub-heading above this statement on the card gives the range "up to at least 20" (*tot tenminste 20*): no upper bound is set, and the Dutch *tot* leaves open whether 20 itself is included. The statement does not say what "flexibly" (*flexibel*) covers. Linking number words, labels, quantities and numbers is the card's statement Relaties tussen telrij, hoeveelheden en getallen / 1.

### Common mistakes

The child can go from objects to a number but not from a number to objects, and keeps laying objects past the number asked for. The child does not see that adding one object gives the next number, and counts everything again. The child treats the place of a number in the row and the amount it stands for as unrelated.
