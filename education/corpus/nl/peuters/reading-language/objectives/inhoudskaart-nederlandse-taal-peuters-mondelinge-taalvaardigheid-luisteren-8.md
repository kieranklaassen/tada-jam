## Official wording

samenvatten van wat wordt verteld (bijv. in het kort een belangrijke gebeurtenis uit een prentenboek navertellen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

summarising what is told (e.g. briefly retelling an important event from a picture book)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After the reading, the adult asks what happened to the duck, and the child says "duck fell in the water". The child turns back to the page and points at it while telling. A child of 2 gives one thing from the story with a word or by pointing in the book; a child of 4 tells one important event in one or two sentences.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. Briefly retelling an important event from a picture book is given as an example: one event, told briefly. The wording does not ask for the whole story or for the order of events. It does not say whether the book is open while the child tells.

### Common mistakes

Telling the last thing they heard or a favourite detail and not an important event. Telling everything they remember, in the order in which they remember it. Telling about something that happened to themselves in place of the story.
