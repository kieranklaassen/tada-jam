## Official wording

luisteren naar en begrijpen van uitleg over activiteiten en taken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to and understanding explanation about activities and tasks

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An adult shows and tells how to stick a leaf on paper: first the glue, then press the leaf on. The child watches, listens and then does it. A child of 2 follows when the adult shows each step while saying it; a child of 4 listens to this short explanation to its end and then starts.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording is about explanation of activities and tasks. It sets no length and no number of steps, and it does not say whether the explanation is shown as well as spoken. Instructions are the subject of `Luisteren / 3`.

### Common mistakes

Starting before the explanation has ended. Remembering only the last step. Copying what the child next to them does and not what was said.
