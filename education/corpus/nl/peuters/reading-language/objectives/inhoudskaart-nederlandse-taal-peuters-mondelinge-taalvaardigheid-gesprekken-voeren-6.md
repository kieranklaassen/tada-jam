## Official wording

gebruiken van gebaren en mimiek om gespreksbijdragen te ondersteunen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using gestures and facial expression to support contributions to a conversation

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says "big!" and spreads both arms, shakes their head while saying "no", and points at the thing they are talking about. The child makes a sad face while telling that they fell. A child of 2 uses the gesture in place of the word, pointing and saying "that"; a child of 4 says a sentence and adds a gesture for size, place or feeling.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The gestures and facial expression support what the child says in a conversation. The wording names no particular gestures and no sign system. A statement with almost the same words, `Spreken / non-verbale communicatie en visuele ondersteuning / 1`, is about messages.

### Common mistakes

Letting the gesture replace the word, so that a listener who is not looking does not understand. Pointing at something the listener cannot see.
