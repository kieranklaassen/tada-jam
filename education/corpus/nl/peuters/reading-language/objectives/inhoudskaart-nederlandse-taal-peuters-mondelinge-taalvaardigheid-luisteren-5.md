## Official wording

luisteren naar liedjes en versjes

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to songs and short verses

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sits in the circle and listens while an adult sings, sways, and makes the hand movements that belong to the song. The child joins in on some words. A child of 2 listens, moves and says single words at the ends of lines; a child of 4 sings most of a familiar song along and says so when the adult sings a wrong word.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording is about listening. The Dutch uses the diminutive for both the songs and the verses. Singing a song or reciting a verse oneself is the example in `Spreken / vloeiendheid, verstaanbaarheid en grammaticale beheersing / 6`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the words: they follow the melody and the movements, and replace a word they do not know with a known word that sounds like it.
