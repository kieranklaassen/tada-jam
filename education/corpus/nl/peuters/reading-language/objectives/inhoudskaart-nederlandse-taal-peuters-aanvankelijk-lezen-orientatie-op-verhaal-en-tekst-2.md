## Official wording

ontdekken dat illustraties ook een verhaal vormen (begrijpend luisteren)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that illustrations also form a story (listening comprehension)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at the pictures while an adult reads and points at what is happening in them. With a familiar book the child turns the pages and tells from the pictures what happens. A child of 2 names single things on a page, such as "dog" or "ball"; a child of 4 tells what happens across several pages by looking at the pictures in order.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says discovering, and gives listening comprehension (*begrijpend luisteren*) in brackets. The word "also" sets the illustrations beside another way the story is told; the wording does not say whether the book has text. The statement does not ask the child to retell the whole story.

### Common mistakes

Naming separate things on each page without linking one page to the next. Not seeing that the figure on the next page is the same character as on the page before.
