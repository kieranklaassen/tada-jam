## Official wording

uitbreiden van de receptieve woordenschat

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

expanding the receptive vocabulary

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears an adult name the things they are both handling, such as the whisk and the ladle. The child points to a named thing in a book, or fetches the bucket when asked for it. A child of 2 points to or fetches familiar things when they are named; a child of 4 also follows words for properties, places and actions, such as "empty", "behind" and "pour".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. Receptive vocabulary is the words a child understands on hearing them. The wording sets no number of words and names no themes or word list. The words a child says are the subject of `Woordenschat en woordgebruik / 2`.

### Common mistakes

Understanding a word only in the situation in which they learned it. Taking a new word for the name of the whole object when it names a part or a property. Seeming to understand a word while in fact following a gesture or the usual routine.
