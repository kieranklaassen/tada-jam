## Official wording

luisteren naar eenvoudige voorstellingen , belevenissen van volwassene of kind

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to simple *voorstellingen* (performances or presentations), experiences of an adult or child

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sits and watches while an adult plays a short scene with a hand puppet. The child listens while another child tells what they did at their grandmother's, or while the adult tells about her cat. A child of 2 watches and listens for a short while and responds to what they see; a child of 4 listens until a short account has ended and then asks or says something about it.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. *Voorstellingen* can mean performances (such as a puppet show) or presentations, and the wording does not choose. The source prints the two parts with only a comma between them, and a space before the comma, so it is not clear whether the experiences of an adult or child are a second thing to listen to or say what the *voorstellingen* are about. "Simple" is the only bound; no length is stated.

### Common mistakes

Interrupting with a story of their own as soon as something reminds them of it. Watching the puppet or the object the speaker holds without following the words.
