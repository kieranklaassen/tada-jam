## Official wording

spelen met letters en woorden (versieren en kleuren van letters en woorden)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

playing with letters and words (decorating and colouring letters and words)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child colours in a large outline of the first letter of their name and puts stickers on it. The child moves magnetic or wooden letters around and lays them in rows. A child of 2 scribbles over the letter shape and handles loose letters like any other object; a child of 4 colours inside the outline and says whose letter it is.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The brackets name decorating and colouring letters and words. The wording does not ask the child to name letters, say their sounds or write them. It names no letters or words.

### Common mistakes

Turning a letter upside down or mirroring it without noticing. Seeing the letter only as a shape to colour and not as a sign. Colouring over the whole sheet so that the shape of the letter can no longer be seen.
