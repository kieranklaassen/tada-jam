## Official wording

meeleven met prentenboeken (leesbeleving)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

feeling along with picture books (reading experience)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While an adult reads a picture book aloud, the child looks worried when a character is lost, laughs at a funny page and covers their eyes at a frightening one. The child points at the character and calls out to it. A child of 2 copies the face or the sound of a character; a child of 4 says how the character feels and what they hope will happen.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names picture books and gives the term reading experience (*leesbeleving*) in brackets. It does not say which books or which feelings. It does not ask the child to retell or explain the story.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out a character's feeling when it differs from their own feeling at that moment, and many show what they feel with face and body and not in words.
