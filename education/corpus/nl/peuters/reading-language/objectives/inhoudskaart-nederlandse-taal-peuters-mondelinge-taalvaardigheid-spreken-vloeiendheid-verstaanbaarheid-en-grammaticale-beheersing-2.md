## Official wording

klanken vormen met het Nederlandse klanksysteem (fonologie)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

forming sounds with the Dutch sound system (phonology)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child talks, sings and copies words an adult says. The child makes animal and vehicle sounds, blows, hums and growls, and watches their own mouth in a mirror while doing so. A child of 2 says words with the sounds they can already make, and familiar adults understand them best; a child of 4 says many more words the way adults do, and some sounds still differ from adult speech.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names the Dutch sound system and gives the term phonology in brackets. It lists no sounds and no order in which they come. It does not mention other languages a child speaks.

### Common mistakes

Replacing a sound with one learned earlier, such as t for k (*toe* for *koe*, cow) or l, j or w for r. Saying s with the tongue between the teeth. For a child who speaks another language at home, using the nearest sound of that language.
