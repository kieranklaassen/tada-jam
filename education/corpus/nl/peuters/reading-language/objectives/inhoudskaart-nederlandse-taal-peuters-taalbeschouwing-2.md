## Official wording

bewust worden van het eigen taalgebruik

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

becoming aware of one's own language use

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child whispers to the sleeping doll and shouts outside, and speaks in a higher voice to a baby. The child stops in the middle of a sentence and says a word again in another way. A child of 2 whispers or speaks loudly when asked to; a child of 4 corrects a word they said and remarks that another child uses a different word for grandmother than they do.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. This is one of three statements that stand directly under the heading *Taalbeschouwing* (reflection on language). The wording says becoming aware and does not say of which sides of language use.

### Common mistakes

Not hearing their own error: when an adult repeats the word in the right form, the child believes they said the same. Thinking that everyone knows the words that only their own family uses.
