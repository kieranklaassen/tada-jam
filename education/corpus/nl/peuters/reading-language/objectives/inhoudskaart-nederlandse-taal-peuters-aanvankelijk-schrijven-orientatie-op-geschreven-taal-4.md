## Official wording

naschrijven, stempelen of typen van letters en woorden (bijv. eigen naam, logo's, merknamen, namen van producten)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

copying in writing, stamping or typing letters and words (e.g. own name, logos, brand names, names of products)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child presses letter stamps onto paper and presses the keys of a keyboard and watches letters appear. The child copies letters of their name from a name card with a thick pencil, or with a finger in sand. A child of 2 stamps and presses keys without choosing letters; a child of 4 finds some letters of their own name and stamps or copies them from the card.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names three ways joined by "or": copying in writing, stamping and typing. Each works from a model or with a tool; writing from memory and spelling are not mentioned. The examples are the child's own name, logos, brand names and names of products. The statement sets no number of letters and does not say capitals or small letters.

### Common mistakes

Writing letters mirrored, or from right to left. Leaving out letters of a name or putting them in another order while copying. Copying a letter as a drawing, with the strokes in any order and direction.
