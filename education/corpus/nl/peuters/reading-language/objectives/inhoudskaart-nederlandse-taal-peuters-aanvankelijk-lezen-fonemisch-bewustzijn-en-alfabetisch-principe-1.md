## Official wording

spelen met klanken (fonemen) en symbolen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

playing with sounds (phonemes) and symbols

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child repeats strings of sounds after an adult, makes the sound of a snake or a car, and claps while doing so. The child handles letter blocks, stamps and picture cards and lays them in rows. A child of 2 copies animal and vehicle sounds; a child of 4 repeats the first sound of a word many times for fun and picks out the sign that stands for their own name.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says playing. It names sounds, explained in brackets as phonemes, and symbols, and it does not say which sounds or which symbols. Letters are one kind of symbol; they are also named separately, in the statement `Fonemisch bewustzijn en alfabetisch principe / 4`.

### Common mistakes

Hearing a word only as a whole and not as something made of smaller sounds. Treating a symbol as a picture to look at and not as a sign that stands for something. Calling every letter or sign "my name" once they know the first letter of their own name.
