## Official wording

uiten van een mening en deze vergelijken met de mening van anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

expressing an opinion and comparing it with the opinion of others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At fruit time the child says "I like banana, not pear" and hears that another child prefers pear. The child chooses one of two books and sees which children chose the other one. A child of 2 shows a preference by pointing, pushing something away or saying "no" or "nice"; a child of 4 says "I think it is scary and she thinks it is funny".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording has two parts: expressing an opinion, and comparing it with the opinion of others. It does not ask for reasons. The statement names no subject for the opinion.

### Common mistakes

Thinking that everyone likes what they like. Repeating the opinion of the child who spoke before them. Treating a different opinion as wrong or as a quarrel.
