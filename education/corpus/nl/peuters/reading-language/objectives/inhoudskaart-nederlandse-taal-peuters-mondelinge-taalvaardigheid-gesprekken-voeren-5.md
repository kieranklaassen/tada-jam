## Official wording

kennismaken met enkele gespreksregels (bijv. op de beurt wachten en naar elkaar luisteren)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting to know some conversation rules (e.g. waiting for one's turn and listening to each other)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a small circle the child holds an object that is passed to the one whose turn it is to speak, and gives it to the next child afterwards. The child waits while another child speaks and looks at that child. A child of 2 waits for a short moment with an adult's help; a child of 4 waits for the object or raises a hand and keeps quiet until one other child has finished a short turn.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says getting to know, and it says some rules. Waiting for one's turn and listening to each other are given as examples. The statement does not say that the child keeps to the rules without help.

### Common mistakes

Calling out as soon as they know something. Forgetting what they wanted to say while waiting. Keeping quiet during another child's turn without hearing what that child said.
