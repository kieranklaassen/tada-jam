## Official wording

bladeren door (prenten-)boeken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

leafing through (picture) books

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child holds a book, turns it the right way up, turns the pages, lifts flaps and looks at each picture. The child goes back to a favourite page and stays there. A child of 2 turns thick cardboard pages, often several at once, and starts anywhere in the book; a child of 4 starts at the front, turns thin pages one at a time and keeps the book the right way up.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording is about leafing through books. "Picture" stands in brackets, so the wording covers books with and without pictures. The statement does not mention reading the text or going from front to back.

### Common mistakes

Holding the book upside down or starting at the back. Turning several pages at once and so missing part of the book. Tearing thin paper pages while turning them.
