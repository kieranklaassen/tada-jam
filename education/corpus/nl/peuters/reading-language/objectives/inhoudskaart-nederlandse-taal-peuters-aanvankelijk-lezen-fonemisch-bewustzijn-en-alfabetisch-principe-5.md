## Official wording

ervaringen opdoen in rijmen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experience in rhyming

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child listens to nursery rhymes and says the last word of a line together with the adult. The child claps, sways or bounces on an adult's knee in time with the verse. A child of 2 says the final word of a line in a verse they know well when the adult stops before it; a child of 4 notices that two words sound alike at the end and adds a nonsense word that sounds alike too.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says gaining experience; it sets no level, so it does not say that the child can make a rhyme or can tell whether two words rhyme, and it does not say what the experience consists of (hearing, joining in, making). The statement sets no number of rhymes and names no kind of rhyme.

### Common mistakes

Giving a word that goes with the meaning and not with the sound, such as "dog" for "cat". Giving a word that starts the same where one that ends the same is asked. Saying the rhyming word of a known verse from memory without hearing that it rhymes.
