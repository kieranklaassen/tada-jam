## Official wording

ontwikkelen van leesplezier vanuit een rijke leesomgeving

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

developing enjoyment of reading from a rich reading environment

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child goes to the book corner, takes a book from a low shelf or a basket, and sits down with it or climbs onto an adult's lap with it. The child carries a favourite book around and asks for it to be read again. A child of 2 picks the same book many times and points at its pictures; a child of 4 chooses among several books, says which one they want, and tells the story from memory to a doll while turning the pages.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names a rich reading environment as the place the enjoyment comes from and does not say what such an environment holds. It does not ask the child to read. The statement sets no number or kind of books.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out choosing for themselves when an adult always picks the book, and they leave out books they have not yet heard read aloud.
