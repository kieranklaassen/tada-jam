## Official wording

evalueren van wat wordt verteld (bijv. kort reageren op de inhoud in antwoord op vragen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

evaluating what is told (e.g. briefly responding to the content in answer to questions)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The adult asks "was it kind of the fox to take the egg?" or "did you like the story?". The child says "no, naughty", nods, or shakes their head. A child of 2 answers with "yes" or "no" or shows it with their face; a child of 4 says what they thought of it, sometimes with a short reason.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. Briefly responding to the content in answer to questions is given as an example. The wording does not ask for reasons. It does not say what is told or by whom, and it does not say who asks the questions.

### Common mistakes

Saying "yes" to every question. Judging the whole by its ending or by one picture. Repeating the answer of the child who spoke before them.
