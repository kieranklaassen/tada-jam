## Official wording

uitbreiden van productieve woordenschat

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

expanding productive vocabulary

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child names the things they handle and see, repeats a new word after an adult, and uses it on a later day without help. A child of 2 says the names of familiar things and people and a few words for actions; a child of 4 also uses words for properties, feelings and places.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. Productive vocabulary is the words a child says. The wording sets no number of words and names no themes or word list. The words a child understands are the subject of `Woordenschat en woordgebruik / 1`.

### Common mistakes

Using one word for too many things, such as "dog" for every animal with four legs. Using a word for one single thing only, such as "shoe" for their own shoes alone. Pointing and saying "that" where they do not know the word.
