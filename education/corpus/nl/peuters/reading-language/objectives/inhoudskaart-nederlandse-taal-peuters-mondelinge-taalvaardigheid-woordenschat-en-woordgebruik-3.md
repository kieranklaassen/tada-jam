## Official wording

gebruiken van nieuwe woorden in andere situaties (betekenisuitbreiding, semantiek)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using new words in other situations (extension of meaning, semantics)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child who learned *glad* (slippery) with wet soap says it about ice outside. The child who learned *leeg* (empty) with a cup says it about a box. A child of 2 uses a new name for another thing of the same kind, such as a second dog; a child of 4 uses a word for a property or an action in a quite different setting.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording is about new words and about situations other than the one in which the word was learned. The brackets name extension of meaning and semantics. The statement names no words and no situations.

### Common mistakes

Keeping the word for the first object or place only. Using the new word for everything that was present when they learned it, such as "slippery" for all soap. Using the word far outside its meaning.
