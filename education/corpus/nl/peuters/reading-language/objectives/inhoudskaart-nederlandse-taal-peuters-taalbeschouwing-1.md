## Official wording

op speelse wijze bezig zijn met taal

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

being occupied with language in a playful way

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child makes up nonsense words, changes a word in a song, and speaks in another voice for a puppet. A child of 2 repeats strange sounds and words and laughs at them; a child of 4 changes words on purpose, for example calling the cat a dog as a joke, and makes up nonsense names for things.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. This is one of three statements that stand directly under the heading *Taalbeschouwing* (reflection on language). The wording says in a playful way and names no activity. It does not mention terms such as word, sentence or letter.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the form of language: they attend to what is meant, and some object that a changed word or a wrong name "is not right" and do not join in.
