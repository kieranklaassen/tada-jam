## Official wording

herkennen van (bekende) veelvoorkomende pictogrammen (bijv. handen wassen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising (familiar) common pictograms (e.g. washing hands)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sees the hand-washing picture next to the sink and goes to wash their hands. The child looks at the picture cards for the parts of the day and says "eating" or "outside" at the right card. A child of 2 links one or two cards that are used every day to the action when an adult points to them; a child of 4 says without help what each familiar card in the room means.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording is about pictograms that occur often, and "familiar" stands in brackets. Washing hands is given as an example. The statement sets no number of pictograms and does not mention written words.

### Common mistakes

Saying what is drawn and not the action the pictogram stands for, such as "tap" for washing hands. Recognising a pictogram only in its usual place. Mixing up two pictograms that look alike.
