## Official wording

vertellen aan de hand van (zelf meegenomen) materiaal

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

telling with the help of material (that one has brought along oneself)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child holds a shell, a soft toy or a photo, shows it to the others and tells about it: "from the beach", "my bear". The other children look at it and touch it. A child of 2 holds up the object and says its name; a child of 4 tells in a few sentences where it comes from and what they did with it.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The material supports the telling. "That one has brought along oneself" stands in brackets, so the wording also covers other material. The statement does not say what material, to whom the child tells or for how long.

### Common mistakes

Showing the object without telling anything. Saying the name of the object and then stopping. Using "there", "that" and "he" for places and people the listeners do not know.
