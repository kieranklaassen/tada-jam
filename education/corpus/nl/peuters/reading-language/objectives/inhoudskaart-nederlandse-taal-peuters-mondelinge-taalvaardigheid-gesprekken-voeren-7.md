## Official wording

(spontaan)reageren op een ander kind of de leidster (bijv. door iets over zichzelf te vertellen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(spontaneously) responding to another child or the childcare worker (*leidster*, the woman who leads the group) (e.g. by telling something about oneself)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Another child says "I have new shoes" and the child answers "me too, red ones". The adult tells about her dog and the child says "grandma has a dog". A child of 2 responds with a look, a point or one word, or by holding up their own shoe; a child of 4 responds with a sentence about themselves that fits what was said.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. "Spontaneously" stands in brackets, so the wording also covers a response after a prompt. Telling something about oneself is given as an example. The statement sets no length for the response.

### Common mistakes

Responding with something that has nothing to do with what was said. Responding to adults only and not to other children. Repeating the other's words without adding anything.
