## Official wording

luisteren naar en begrijpen van instructie(-s) (bijv. bij activiteiten in de groep)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to and understanding instruction(s) (e.g. during activities in the group)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The adult says "get your coat", "put the blocks in the box" or "come and sit on the mat", and the child does it. A child of 2 follows such an instruction when it is about something in sight and is said to them personally; a child of 4 also follows a longer one, for example "get your coat and wait by the door", when it is given to the whole group.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The brackets around the plural leave open whether one instruction or several are meant. Activities in the group are given as an example of where instructions occur. The statement sets no number of steps.

### Common mistakes

Doing only the last part of an instruction of two steps. Not responding to an instruction given to the whole group, because they do not know it is meant for them too. Doing what usually happens at that moment of the day and not what was said.
