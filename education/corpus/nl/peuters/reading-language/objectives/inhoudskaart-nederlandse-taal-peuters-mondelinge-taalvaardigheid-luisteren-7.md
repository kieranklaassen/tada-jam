## Official wording

luisteren en begrijpen van gesproken tekst op radio, tv en internet (bijv. (educatieve) series of programma's, digitale prentenboeken en luisterboeken)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to and understanding spoken text on radio, TV and internet (e.g. (educational) series or programmes, digital picture books and audiobooks)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sits next to an adult and listens to a recorded story or a picture book that is read by a recorded voice. Afterwards the child says who was in it, and sings along with a song from a children's programme. A child of 2 repeats single words and sounds and joins in a song; a child of 4 tells one thing that happened in the story they heard.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names spoken text from three media: radio, TV and internet. Series or programmes (with "educational" in brackets), digital picture books and audiobooks are given as examples. The source prints *luisteren en begrijpen van* without the word *naar* ("to"); the gloss reads it as "listening to and understanding". The statement sets no length of time and does not say whether an adult listens with the child.

### Common mistakes

Following the pictures and the music and not the spoken words. Repeating phrases from a programme without knowing what they mean.
