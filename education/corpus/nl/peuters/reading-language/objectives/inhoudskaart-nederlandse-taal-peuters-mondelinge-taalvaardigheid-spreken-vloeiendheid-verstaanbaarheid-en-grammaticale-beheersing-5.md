## Official wording

beschrijven van iets of iemand uit de eigen omgeving met gebruik van losse woorden of eenvoudige zinnen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing something or someone from one's own surroundings using separate words or simple sentences

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child holds their soft toy and says "soft" and "long ears". Asked about grandpa, the child says "big" and "glasses". A child of 2 gives one or two separate words, such as the name and "big" or a colour; a child of 4 says a few simple sentences about how the thing or person looks and what it does.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The subject is something or someone from the child's own surroundings. The form is separate words or simple sentences, so separate words alone are covered by the wording. The statement sets no number of features to mention.

### Common mistakes

Saying the name of the thing and not describing it. Saying what they do with it, or that it is theirs, and not how it looks. Using one word such as "big" for tall, long, wide and many.
