## Official wording

vertellen op een begrijpelijke en verstaanbare wijze

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

telling in an understandable and intelligible (*verstaanbaar*, the words can be made out) way

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tells an adult what happened in the garden, looks at the adult while speaking and speaks loudly enough to be heard. When the adult does not understand, the child says it again. A child of 2 is mostly understood by people who know them well and who can see what the child is talking about; a child of 4 is understood by an adult they do not know for most of what they say.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names two qualities: the listener can follow what is told, and the listener can make out the words. It names no listener, no subject and no length.

### Common mistakes

Speaking too softly or with the face turned away. Leaving out who it was about and where it happened, so that the listener cannot follow. Speaking very fast when excited, so that the words are not separated.
