## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child finds their own coat hook or drawer by the picture sticker on it. The child points to the sign on the toilet door and says what room it is, and recognises the logo of a familiar shop on a bag. A child of 2 recognises their own sticker or one favourite logo; a child of 4 says what several signs in the room and in the street stand for.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names symbols and does not say which or how many. Pictograms are one kind of symbol; they are also named separately, in the statement `Oriëntatie op verhaal en tekst / 3`.

### Common mistakes

Recognising a symbol only in its usual place, colour or size and not when it appears somewhere else. Saying what the picture shows and not what it stands for, for example "hands" for the hand-washing sign.
