## Official wording

interesse tonen in geschreven taal

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

showing interest in written language

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child points at the letters on a cereal box or a street sign and looks at the adult. The child watches an adult write, asks for their own name on a drawing, and looks at the print in a book as well as the pictures. A child of 2 points at print and waits for the adult to say something; a child of 4 asks "what does that say?" and "where is my name?".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording is about showing interest. It names no texts and no letters, and it does not ask the child to read or write.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out print that is not tied to something they care about: their own name, food packets and familiar signs get attention first, and in books many children look at the pictures only.
