## Official wording

uitvoeren van eenvoudige, korte spreektaken met het doel om te amuseren of informeren (bijv. zingen van een kort eenvoudig liedje of opzeggen van een versje)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

carrying out simple, short speaking tasks with the aim of amusing or informing (e.g. singing a short simple song or reciting a short verse)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sings a short song for the group or for a parent and makes the movements that belong to it. The child says a short verse, or tells the group which fruit is in the bowl today. A child of 2 sings parts of a song along with the adult and makes the movements; a child of 4 sings a short song or says a short verse alone or with one other child while others listen.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The tasks are simple and short, and their aim is to amuse or to inform. Singing a short simple song and reciting a verse (in the diminutive, glossed as "short verse") are given as examples. The statement names no audience.

### Common mistakes

Stopping when others look at them. Singing the melody with only some of the words. Saying a familiar text very fast or too softly to be understood.
