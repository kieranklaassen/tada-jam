## Official wording

gebruiken van gebaren en mimiek om boodschappen te ondersteunen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using gestures and facial expression to support messages

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child holds up both arms and says "up", points at the cup and says "drink", and nods or shakes their head. The child shows a grazed knee with a sad face. A child of 2 uses a gesture with one word to make clear what they want; a child of 4 mostly uses words and adds gestures for size, place or feeling.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The gestures and facial expression support a message; the card places this under speaking, under its small heading for non-verbal communication and visual support. The wording names no particular gestures and no sign system. The statement `Gesprekken voeren / 6` uses almost the same words for contributions to a conversation.

### Common mistakes

Relying on the gesture alone, so that someone who is not looking does not understand. Repeating the same gesture with more force when not understood, without trying a word or another gesture.
