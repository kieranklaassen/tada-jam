forming sentences (syntax)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child points at the car and says "papa car", says "I want apple too", and tells what the doll is doing while playing with it. A child of 2 puts words together without the small words between them; a child of 4 says longer sentences with a verb and sometimes links sentences with *en* (and).

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording consists of "forming sentences" and the term syntax in brackets. It sets no sentence length and names no kinds of sentence. It does not say that the sentences are correct.

### Common mistakes

Leaving out small words such as articles and auxiliary verbs. Putting the verb at the end in its infinitive form, as in *papa auto rijden* ("papa car drive"). Keeping the word order of a statement in a question.
