## Official wording

gebruiken van steeds meer verschillende en complexere woorden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using more and more different and more complex words

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While playing, the child says "boot" and "sandal" where they first said "shoe", and says "huge" and "tiny" next to "big" and "small". The child says long compound words such as *brandweerauto* (fire engine). A child of 2 mostly uses general names; a child of 4 uses more exact names, words for groups such as "animals", and longer compound words.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording describes growth ("more and more") and sets no end point. It sets no number of words and does not say what makes a word complex.

### Common mistakes

Using a general word where they know an exact one. Shortening a long word or changing its sounds. Using a newly learned difficult word where it does not fit, because they do not yet know what it means.
