## Official wording

(mondeling) reageren op 'tekst' met blijk van herkenning, waardering en eigen mening

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(orally) responding to 'text' with signs of recognition, appreciation and one's own opinion

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

During or after a picture book the child points at something they know and says "I have that too". The child says "again" or "nice", or says that they do not like the wolf. A child of 2 shows recognition by pointing and saying one word, and shows appreciation by asking for the book again; a child of 4 says in a sentence what they liked or did not like.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. "Orally" stands in brackets, so the wording also covers a response that is not spoken. The source puts 'text' in quotation marks and does not explain it. The wording names three things the response shows: recognition, appreciation and an opinion of the child's own; it does not ask for reasons.

### Common mistakes

Repeating the opinion of the adult or of the child who spoke first. Responding to one picture or detail and not to the text as a whole. Saying "yes" to every question such as "did you like it?".
