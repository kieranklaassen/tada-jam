## Official wording

bewust worden dat je over taal kunt praten

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

becoming aware that you can talk about language

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child asks "what is that called?" and "what does that mean?". The child says that something is a funny word, or tells how a thing is called in the language spoken at home. A child of 2 asks "what is that?" to hear the name; a child of 4 asks what a word means and remarks on words they hear.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. This is one of three statements that stand directly under the heading *Taalbeschouwing* (reflection on language). The wording says becoming aware. It does not ask the child to use terms such as word, sentence or letter.

### Common mistakes

Mixing up the word and the thing, for example saying that "train" is a long word because a train is long. Thinking that the name is part of the thing and that a thing cannot be called anything else.
