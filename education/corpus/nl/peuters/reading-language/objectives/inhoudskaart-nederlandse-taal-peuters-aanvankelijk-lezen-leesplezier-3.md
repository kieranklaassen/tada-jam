## Official wording

genieten van voorgelezen versjes, gedichtjes, rijmpjes of verhalen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

enjoying short verses, short poems, short rhymes or stories that are read aloud

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sits with an adult, listens, and asks for the same verse or story again. The child says the repeated lines together with the adult and makes the hand movements that belong to a verse. A child of 2 moves along with the sound and rhythm; a child of 4 asks for one particular story and says its repeated line at the right moment.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording lists four kinds of text joined by "or", and all are read aloud by someone else. The Dutch uses the diminutive for the verses, poems and rhymes, glossed here as "short"; no length is stated beyond that. The statement does not ask the child to understand or retell the text; understanding a story is the subject of `Luisteren / 4`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out sitting through a whole text: many walk away and come back, which does not show that they do not enjoy it. Many also want the same text many times and not a new one.
