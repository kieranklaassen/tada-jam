## Official wording

ontdekken dat er verschillende soorten schriftelijke bronnen zijn

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Nederlandse taal: Ontluikende en beginnende geletterdheid - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that there are different kinds of written sources

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child handles a picture book, a shop leaflet, a recipe, a birthday card and a food packet. The child sees an adult use each one: reading a story from the book, looking in the recipe before cooking, reading out who sent the card. A child of 2 holds and looks at them; a child of 4 says what one is for, such as "that is for cooking" or "that is from grandma".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says discovering. It does not name the kinds of written sources or say how many. It does not ask the child to read them.

### Common mistakes

Calling every written thing "a book" or "a letter". Sorting written things by how the object looks (size, colour, pictures) and not by what it is used for.
