## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

constructing with paper (folding) and on paper (designing patterns, drawing)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child folds a sheet of paper in two and presses the fold flat with a hand, or folds a napkin into a triangle with help. With a thick crayon the child draws lines, dots and closed round shapes, or stamps a row of prints across the sheet. A child of 2 crumples, folds roughly and scribbles; a child of 4 folds edge to edge with some care and draws a simple repeated row.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The brackets name folding for working with paper, and designing patterns and drawing for working on paper. The statement does not mention cutting or gluing. It names no shapes and no folded figures.

### Common mistakes

The child does not bring the edges together, so the fold is slanted. The child does not press the fold, so the paper opens again. When drawing a pattern the child fills the sheet without a repeat.
