## Official wording

nadenken over de functie van tellen en de betekenis van telwoorden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

thinking about the function of counting and the meaning of number words

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child is asked "are there enough cups for everyone?" and counts children and cups to find out. With an adult the child talks about why they count: to know how many plates to fetch, or whether a toy animal is missing from the set. A child of 2 joins in counting as a routine with an adult; a child of 4 starts counting without being asked to answer a "how many" question and gives the last number as the answer.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names thinking about two things: what counting is for, and what number words mean. It sets no number range and names no situation. It does not say what the child is to conclude.

### Common mistakes

The child treats counting as a rhyme to recite and does not use it to find out how many. The child thinks the last number word is the name of the last object, not the size of the whole group. Asked to fetch three, the child grabs some without counting.
