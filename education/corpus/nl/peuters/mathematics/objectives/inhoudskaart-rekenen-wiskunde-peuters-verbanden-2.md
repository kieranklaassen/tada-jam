## Official wording

samen experimenteren met het maken van een beelddiagram (bijv. om weer te geven hoeveel kinderen lopend naar huis gaan, met de fiets naar huis gaan of met de auto)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experimenting together with making a picture graph (*beelddiagram*, a chart built from pictures) (e.g. to show how many children go home on foot, go home by bicycle or by car)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Each child gets a card with their own photograph and sticks it in the column under the picture of feet, a bicycle or a car. Together with the adult the children look at which column is highest and which has the fewest cards. A child of 2 places their own card with help; a child of 4 says which column has the most and counts a short column.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says "together" and "experimenting"; it does not mention a finished or correct graph. The way children go home, in three groups (on foot, by bicycle, by car), is an example. No numerals, axes or tally marks are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child does not see the card as standing for themselves and wants to keep it. The child places cards with gaps or overlapping, so the tallest column is not the one with the most cards. The child puts the card under the picture they like, not under the way they go home.
