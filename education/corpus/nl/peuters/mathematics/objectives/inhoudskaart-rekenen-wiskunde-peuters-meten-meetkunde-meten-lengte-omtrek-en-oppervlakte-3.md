## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with concepts around measuring such as big, small, long, short, high, low, thick, thin

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child builds a high tower and a low one and says which is which. The child rolls a thick and a thin roll of clay, finds a long and a short ribbon, and hears and uses the word for each. A child of 2 uses "big" and "small"; a child of 4 also uses "long", "short", "high", "low", "thick" and "thin" for the right kind of thing.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The eight words after "such as" are examples: *groot*, *klein*, *lang*, *kort*, *hoog*, *laag*, *dik*, *dun*. All eight are basic forms; forms such as bigger and biggest are among the examples of Lengte, omtrek en oppervlakte / 4. No units are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child uses "big" for every kind of size: long, high and thick. The child mixes up "high" and "long" for something that stands upright. The child knows the first word of a pair, such as "big" or "long", and not yet its opposite.
