retelling events in the right order

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After the walk to the petting farm the child tells what happened: first coats on, then the walk, then feeding the goats. The child lays three photographs of baking biscuits in order and tells what happened with them. A child of 2 names one thing that happened; a child of 4 tells two or three events in order with "first" and "then".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement sets no number of events and does not say whether they are the child's own experiences or events from a story. It names no time words; the card's statement Tijd / 3 names some.

### Common mistakes

The child starts with the event that impressed them most, not the first one. The child leaves out a step in the middle. The child joins everything with "and then" in an order that is not the real one.
