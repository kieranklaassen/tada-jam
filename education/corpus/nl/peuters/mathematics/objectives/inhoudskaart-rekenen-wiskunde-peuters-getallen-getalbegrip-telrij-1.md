## Official wording

noemen van namen van telwoorden (bijv. in liedjes en prentenboeken)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

saying the names of number words (e.g. in songs and picture books)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sings along with a counting song and says the number words that the song contains. With a picture book on the lap the child says "one, two" along with the adult who points at the pictures. A child of 2 says one or two number words, often the same one at any point; a child of 4 says several number words and knows that they belong to counting.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement is about saying number words; it does not mention a fixed order or counting objects. Songs and picture books are given as examples. The statement sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child says number words in no fixed order and repeats some. The child uses one favourite number word for everything. The child mixes number words with other words from the song.
