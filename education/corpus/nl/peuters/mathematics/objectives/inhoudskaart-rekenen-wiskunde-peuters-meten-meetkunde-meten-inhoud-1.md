## Official wording

vergelijken op inhoud (bijv. via 'op het oog', naast elkaar houden, overgieten, uitgieten)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing by capacity (*inhoud*, how much something holds) (e.g. 'by eye', by holding side by side, by pouring from one into the other, by pouring out)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At the water table the child looks at a tall cup and a wide bowl and says which holds more. The child fills the cup and pours it into the bowl to see whether the bowl is full or has room left. A child of 2 pours from one container into another and watches it overflow; a child of 4 pours to check a guess and says which container holds more.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The four ways in brackets are examples: by eye, holding side by side, pouring from one container into another, and pouring out. The comparison is direct, between containers; no measuring cup, no counting of scoops and no litres are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child says the taller container holds more, also when it is much narrower. The child thinks there is more water after it has been poured into a narrow glass, because the level is higher. The child does not fill the first container to the rim, so the pouring shows nothing about which holds more.
