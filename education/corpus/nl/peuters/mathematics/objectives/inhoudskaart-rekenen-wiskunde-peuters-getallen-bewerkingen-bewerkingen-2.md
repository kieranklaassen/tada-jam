## Official wording

ervaring opdoen met het 'eerlijk' verdelen (bijv. voorwerpen verdelen over verschillende kinderen/bakjes of in groepjes)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experience with 'fair' sharing (e.g. sharing objects out among several children/small containers or into small groups)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hands out crackers at the table, one to each child, and goes round again while there are crackers left. The child drops beads one at a time into two or three small containers and looks whether each holds as many as the others. A child of 2 gives each child one thing; a child of 4 deals out a pile in turns and says when one container has more than another.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The inverted commas around 'fair' are in the wording. The examples name sharing among children, among small containers, and into small groups. The statement sets no number of objects or of sharers, and does not mention a remainder or halves.

### Common mistakes

The child gives everyone a share but not the same number, and calls it fair because everyone has something. The child judges fairness by how big or how spread out a share looks, not by the number. The child keeps the largest share and sees no problem in it.
