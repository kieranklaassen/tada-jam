## Official wording

omgaan met symbolen en pictogrammen in een dagplanning (bijv. Kijk eens op het bord, wat gaan we nu doen?)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with symbols and pictograms in a plan of the day (e.g. Have a look at the board, what are we going to do now?)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child walks to the board with the day's picture cards, points at the card with the apple and says "eating fruit". After an activity the child turns its card over or moves the marker to the next card. A child of 2 recognises one or two cards, such as the one for going outside; a child of 4 looks at the row and says what comes now and what comes after it.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The symbols and pictograms are those of a plan of the day; the example is an adult's question at the board about what comes now. The statement does not say how many pictograms there are or in which direction they are read. No clock times are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child takes a pictogram for a picture of one particular thing, for example this apple, and not for the activity. The child does not know which card is "now" and names a card they like. The child names the cards of the row in no fixed order.
