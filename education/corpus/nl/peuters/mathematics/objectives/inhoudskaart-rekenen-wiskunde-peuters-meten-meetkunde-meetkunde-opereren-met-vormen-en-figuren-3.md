## Official wording

spelen/experimenteren rond zon en schaduw met vormen en figuren

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

playing/experimenting around sun and shadow with shapes and figures

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Outside on a sunny day the child stands still and looks at their shadow on the ground, then lifts an arm and watches the shadow do the same. The child holds a cut-out shape or a toy animal in the sun, looks at the shape of its shadow on the wall, and turns the object to see the shadow change. A child of 2 notices the shadow and tries to step on it; a child of 4 moves an object on purpose to make the shadow bigger, smaller or of another shape.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names sun and shadow; a lamp or another light source is not mentioned. It does not say which shapes or figures. It states no explanation that the child should reach.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks the shadow is a thing of its own that belongs to the object, not something that appears where the light is blocked. The child expects a shadow to show the colours or the face of the object. The child does not connect the place where the shadow falls with the place of the sun.
