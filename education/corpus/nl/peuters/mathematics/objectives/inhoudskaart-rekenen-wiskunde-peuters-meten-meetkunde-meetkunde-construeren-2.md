## Official wording

construeren met meetkundig constructiemateriaal (bijv. bouwen met blokken) en ook nabouwen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

constructing with geometric construction material (e.g. building with blocks) and also building a copy of an example (*nabouwen*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stacks blocks into a tower, lays them in a row as a road, or puts a long block across two upright ones as a bridge. An adult builds a small structure of three or four blocks and the child builds the same beside it, looking from the example to their own blocks and back. A child of 2 stacks and lines up; a child of 4 builds enclosures and bridges and copies a small example one block at a time.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. Blocks are given as the example of geometric construction material. The statement adds copying an example (*nabouwen*); it does not say whether the example is a built structure or a picture, or how many blocks it has. The statement does not mention shape names.

### Common mistakes

The child copies with the right blocks but in a different position, for example lying where the example has standing. The child copies the front of the example and leaves out blocks that are behind or underneath. The child puts a block on a base that is too narrow and the structure falls.
