## Official wording

ontdekken en onderzoeken van wat zwaar, heel zwaar en licht is en deze begrippen herkennen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering and investigating what is heavy, very heavy and light, and recognising these concepts

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tries to lift a bucket of wet sand, a full watering can and an empty box, and says which one they cannot lift. The child carries a feather or a balloon in one hand and pushes a crate of blocks across the floor with the whole body. A child of 2 strains at a heavy thing and says "heavy"; a child of 4 points out the heavy and the light thing when the words are said and uses "very heavy" for what they cannot move.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. Three terms are named: heavy, very heavy and light; the child investigates them and recognises the words. Comparing objects with the words heavier and lighter is the card's statement Gewicht / 2. No units and no scales are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child expects a big thing to be heavy and a small thing to be light, and is surprised by a large empty box or a small stone. The child calls something heavy because it is awkward to hold. The child uses "heavy" for "big".
