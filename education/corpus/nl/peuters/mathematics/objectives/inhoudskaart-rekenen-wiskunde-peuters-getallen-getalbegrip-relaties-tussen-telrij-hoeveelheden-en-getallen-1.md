## Official wording

ontdekken dat getalsymbolen, tellen, telwoorden en hoeveelheden iets met elkaar te maken hebben

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that number symbols, counting, number words and quantities have something to do with each other

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child counts three candles on a cake, hears "three", and sees the digit 3 on the card beside it. The child puts the number card with 2 next to two toy bears, or holds up two fingers when an adult points at the 2. A child of 2 connects the word "two" with two things; a child of 4 connects a few digits, their number words and small groups of things.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says only that the four "have something to do with each other"; it does not say which numbers, and it does not mention matching them correctly. The statement sets no number range. Recognising and naming a few digits is the card's statement Getallen / 1.

### Common mistakes

The child names a digit correctly but cannot put that many objects with it. The child counts up to a number in a rhyme without linking the last word to a quantity. The child thinks a digit printed larger stands for more.
