## Official wording

verkennen van cijfers en getallen die in het dagelijks leven gebruikt worden (bijv. voor je leeftijd, op klokken, bussen, tablet, mobiele telefoon)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring digits and numbers that are used in daily life (e.g. for your age, on clocks, buses, tablet, mobile phone)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child holds up fingers for their age and looks at the number on the birthday crown. On a walk the child points at the number on a bus or a front door, and in the home corner presses the keys of an old telephone and says numbers. A child of 2 notices the signs and points; a child of 4 says "that is a number", names some, and asks what a number is for.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The five places in brackets are examples: for your age, on clocks, on buses, on a tablet, on a mobile phone. The statement is about meeting numbers in use; it sets no range and does not mention reading them or telling the time.

### Common mistakes

The child treats a number on a bus or a door as decoration and not as a sign that tells which bus or which house it is. The child thinks every number tells how many there are. The child reads a number of two digits as two separate digits.
