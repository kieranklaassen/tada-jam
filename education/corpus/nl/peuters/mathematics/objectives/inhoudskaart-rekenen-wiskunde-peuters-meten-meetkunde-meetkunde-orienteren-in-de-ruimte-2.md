## Official wording

vertellen over situaties en voorwerpen en waar deze zich bevinden (bijv. de knuffel is achter mijn rug)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

telling about situations and objects and where they are (e.g. the cuddly toy is behind my back)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hides a cuddly toy behind their back and tells the group where it is. After tidying up the child tells an adult where things have gone: the cars are in the box, the book is on the shelf. A child of 2 points and says one word, such as "there" or "under"; a child of 4 says a short sentence with the object and its place.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement is about the child telling, that is, putting a place into words. The sentence about the cuddly toy is an example. No list of position words is given here; Oriënteren in de ruimte / 1 gives one.

### Common mistakes

The child says "there" and points instead of naming the place. The child names the place from their own position and does not notice that the listener sees it from the other side. The child chooses a position word that is close but wrong, such as "on" for "in".
