## Official wording

omgaan met begrippen rond optellen, aftrekken en verdelen zoals erbij, eraf, weg, samen, evenveel, overhouden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with concepts around adding, subtracting and sharing such as added (*erbij*), taken off (*eraf*), gone, together, the same number (*evenveel*), having left over (*overhouden*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears and says these words while handling things: "one more" when a block goes on the tower, "gone" when the last piece of apple is eaten, "together" when two piles are pushed into one. After sharing out cups the child holds up the cup that nobody got and says it is left over. A child of 2 uses "gone" and "more"; a child of 4 also uses "together", "the same number" and "left over" in a short sentence.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The six words after "such as" are examples: *erbij*, *eraf*, *weg*, *samen*, *evenveel*, *overhouden*. The statement is about words in use; it sets no number range and does not mention written signs.

### Common mistakes

The child uses "more" for any change, also when something is taken away. The child takes "the same number" to mean the same kind of thing. The child understands "together" as being near each other, not as one joined amount.
