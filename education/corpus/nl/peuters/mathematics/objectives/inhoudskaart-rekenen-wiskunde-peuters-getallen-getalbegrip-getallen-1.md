## Official wording

herkennen en benoemen van enkele cijfers en getallen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising and naming a few digits and numbers

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child points at the 3 on a birthday card and says "three", or finds the digit of their own age among number cards on the table. The child picks a wooden digit out of a box and says its name, or says "that is a number" without knowing which one. A child of 2 tells a digit apart from a picture; a child of 4 names a few digits, most often the one for their own age.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says "a few" (*enkele*) and names no digits or numbers; it sets no range. It does not mention writing digits. The link between number symbols and quantities is the subject of another statement of the card, Relaties tussen telrij, hoeveelheden en getallen / 1.

### Common mistakes

The child calls every digit by the one name they know, usually their age. The child mixes up digits and letters. The child confuses digits that look alike, such as 6 and 9 or 2 and 5.
