## Official wording

kennismaken met geld (munten en biljetten) en ervaren dat het een betaalmiddel is (geld geven en spullen terugkrijgen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting to know money (coins and banknotes) and experiencing that it is a means of payment (giving money and getting things in return)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child handles play coins and notes, sorts them into the compartments of a till, and looks at real coins with an adult. At the bakery the child hands the coin to the shop assistant and receives the bread roll. A child of 2 holds a coin and gives it when asked; a child of 4 says that you need money to buy something and that you give it at the till.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The brackets name coins and banknotes for the money, and giving money and getting things in return for the experience. The statement does not mention the values of coins or notes, amounts, or change. Paying by card or by telephone is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks more coins is always more money, whatever the coins are. The child thinks the shop gives money away when change comes back. The child thinks one coin buys anything, whatever the thing costs.
