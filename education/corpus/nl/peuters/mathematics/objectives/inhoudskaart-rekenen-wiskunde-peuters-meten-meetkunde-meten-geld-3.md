## Official wording

ervaring opdoen met begrippen rond geld: duur, goedkoop, 'kost veel'

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experience with concepts around money: expensive, cheap, 'costs a lot'

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the shop corner the seller says a toy car is "expensive" and asks for many coins, and says an apple is "cheap" and asks for one. At a real shop the child hears an adult say that something costs a lot and sees it put back. A child of 2 hears the words; a child of 4 uses "expensive" for something that needs a lot of money and "cheap" for the opposite.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. Three terms follow the colon: *duur*, *goedkoop* and *'kost veel'*; the wording does not mark them as examples. No prices or amounts are stated.

### Common mistakes

The child calls big things expensive and small things cheap. The child uses "expensive" for anything they are not allowed to have. The child takes "cheap" to mean bad or not nice.
