## Official wording

meten van de eigen lichaamslengte (bijv. met behulp van een ander en met streepjes op de muur)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

measuring one's own body height (e.g. with the help of someone else and with marks on the wall)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stands with heels and back against the wall or the door post while an adult draws a line above the head. The child turns round, looks at the line, and compares it with an earlier line or with a friend's line. A child of 2 stands still for the line and looks at it; a child of 4 says which line is higher and that they have grown since the lower one.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The help of another person and marks on the wall are given as an example. The statement mentions no centimetres, no measuring tape and no numbers. It is about the child's own height only.

### Common mistakes

The child stands on tiptoe or looks up, which moves the line. The child thinks the higher line must belong to the older child. The child does not see the line as their own height once they have stepped away from the wall.
