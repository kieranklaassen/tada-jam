## Official wording

ontwerpen van mozaïek- en kettingpatronen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

designing mosaic patterns and *ketting* patterns (*ketting*: a chain, or a necklace of beads)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays coloured tiles or pegs on a board and fills a row or an area with colours of their own choosing. The child threads large beads on a cord, for example red, blue, red, blue, and looks at the string. A child of 2 lays or threads pieces in any order; a child of 4 continues an alternation of two colours for several pieces and says what comes next.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names two kinds of pattern: mosaic patterns and *ketting* patterns; *ketting* can mean a chain or a necklace of beads, and the wording does not say which. The verb is designing: the child makes a pattern of their own; copying or continuing a given pattern is not mentioned. No rule for the pattern, such as a repeat of two colours, is stated.

### Common mistakes

The child starts a repeat and loses it after a few pieces. The child chooses each piece by what they like at that moment, so there is no rule. The child attends to colour and overlooks that shape or size also changes.
