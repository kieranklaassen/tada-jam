## Official wording

omgaan met (en imiteren van) kopen, verkopen en betalen (bijv. winkeltje spelen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with (and imitating) buying, selling and paying (e.g. playing shop)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the shop corner the child puts play fruit in a basket, carries it to the counter and hands over play coins. Another child stands behind the counter, presses the keys of the toy till and hands the basket back. A child of 2 carries things in a bag and hands something over; a child of 4 plays buyer or seller with the steps in order: choosing, paying, taking the things along.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names buying, selling and paying, as things the child deals with and imitates; playing shop is an example. It mentions no prices, amounts or change. Getting to know coins and banknotes is the card's statement Geld / 2.

### Common mistakes

The child takes the things without paying, or pays and leaves the things behind. As seller the child gives the money back together with the things. The child hands over any number of coins, because the number has no meaning for them yet.
