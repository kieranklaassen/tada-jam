## Official wording

voorwerpen sorteren op basis van lengte (bijv. Zoek alle lange schelpen bij elkaar)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

sorting objects on the basis of length (e.g. Find all the long shells and put them together)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child goes through a tray of shells and puts all the long ones in one dish. The child sorts sticks from the garden into a pile of long ones and a pile of short ones. A child of 2 picks out a few clearly long things; a child of 4 sorts a whole collection into long and short and moves a doubtful one after holding it next to another.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The sorting is on the basis of length only. The example is an instruction to gather all the long shells; it names one group, the long ones. Putting objects in order from shortest to longest is not mentioned. No number of objects is stated.

### Common mistakes

The child sorts by another property that is easier to see, such as colour, or by which shells they like. The child puts a big wide shell with the long ones because it is big. The child has no fixed idea of "long" and judges each object against the one picked up just before.
