## Official wording

tellen van kleine hoeveelheden

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

counting small quantities

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child touches each toy car in a short row and says a number word at each. The child takes three spoons from the drawer when asked for three, or counts the children at the table by pointing. A child of 2 says number words while touching things without the two matching; a child of 4 counts a few objects correctly and gives the last word as the answer.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says "small" and gives no number; the statement sets no number range. Hoeveelheden / 6 names groups of 2 or 3 for recognising without counting; that number is that statement's, not this one's. The statement does not mention digits.

### Common mistakes

The child counts an object twice or skips one. The child says the number words faster or slower than the finger moves. Asked "how many?" after counting, the child counts again instead of saying the last number.
