## Official wording

ontdekken van de functie van een klok (bijv. de klok waarop je aan de wijzer ziet dat het later wordt en hoe laat het is)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering the function of a clock (e.g. the clock on which you see by the hand that it is getting later and what time it is)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at the wall clock with an adult who says "when the big hand is at the top, we go outside", and looks again later to see that the hand has moved. The child turns the hands of a toy clock and says "it is time to eat". A child of 2 points at the clock and listens to it tick; a child of 4 says that a clock tells what time it is and watches the hand to see whether it is time yet.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement is about what a clock is for. The example is a clock with a hand, on which one sees that it is getting later and what time it is. The statement does not mention the child reading the time, and names no hours or half hours. A digital clock is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks the hands do not move, because the movement is too slow to see. The child says any number as "the time". The child thinks the clock makes things happen, for example that turning the hands brings lunch.
