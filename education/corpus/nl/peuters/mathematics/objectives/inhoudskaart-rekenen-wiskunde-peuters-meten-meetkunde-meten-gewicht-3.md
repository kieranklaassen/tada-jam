## Official wording

ervaring opdoen met meten van gewicht met informele 'weeginstrumenten' en maten (wip, balans, handen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experience with measuring weight with informal 'weighing instruments' and measures (seesaw, balance, hands)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children sit on the seesaw and see which side goes down; then a third child joins one side. The child puts a block in one pan of a balance and chestnuts in the other and watches which pan sinks, then adds chestnuts until the pans are level. A child of 2 loads the pans and watches them move; a child of 4 says beforehand which side will go down and says that the low side has the heavier load.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The brackets name a seesaw, a balance and hands. The instruments and measures are informal: no grams, kilograms or scales with numbers are mentioned.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks the side that goes up holds the heavier thing. The child thinks the pan with more objects must go down, whatever the objects are. The child pushes a pan down by hand and takes that as the result.
