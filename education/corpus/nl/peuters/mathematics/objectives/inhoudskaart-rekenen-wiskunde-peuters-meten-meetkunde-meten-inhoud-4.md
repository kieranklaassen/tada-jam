## Official wording

vergelijken en ontdekken van tegenstellingen en herkennen van begrippen zoals grootste/kleinste, meeste/minste

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing and discovering opposites and recognising concepts such as biggest/smallest, most/least

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sets out three buckets and points at the biggest and the smallest. The child looks at three equal cups with different amounts of water and points at the one with the most and the one with the least. A child of 2 picks out the big one from a pair; a child of 4 picks the biggest and the smallest from three or more, and the one with the most.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The card prints this statement under the sub-heading Inhoud (capacity). The two word pairs after "such as" are examples: *grootste/kleinste* and *meeste/minste*. The wording speaks of recognising the words and does not mention saying them. Another statement of the card, Lengte, omtrek en oppervlakte / 4, opens with the same words.

### Common mistakes

The child picks a big one but not the biggest when there are more than two. The child finds the biggest and then points at the next biggest as "the smallest". The child takes the biggest container to be the one with the most in it, whatever the level.
