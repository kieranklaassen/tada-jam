## Official wording

construeren met vrij constructie materiaal (bijv. met klei, brooddeeg, zand, dozen, touw)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

constructing with free construction material (e.g. with clay, bread dough, sand, boxes, string)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child rolls clay into a sausage and a ball, presses them together and calls it a snail. In the sandpit the child fills a bucket, turns it over and makes a row of sand heaps; with boxes the child stacks a tower or makes a house to sit in. A child of 2 squeezes, pokes and piles the material; a child of 4 says beforehand what they are going to make and joins several parts.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The five materials in brackets are examples: clay, bread dough, sand, boxes and string. The wording does not explain "free"; the card's statement Construeren / 2 is about geometric construction material, with blocks as its example. The statement names no shapes to be made and no result.

### Common mistakes

The child makes parts too thin or too heavy at the top, so the work falls over. The child cannot yet join two pieces of clay so that they stay together. The child names the work only afterwards, by what it turns out to look like.
