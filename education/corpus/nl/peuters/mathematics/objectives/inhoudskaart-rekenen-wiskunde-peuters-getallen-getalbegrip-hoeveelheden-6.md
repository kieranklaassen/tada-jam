## Official wording

herkennen van kleine groepjes (2 of 3) zonder tellen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Rekenen-wiskunde - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising small groups (2 or 3) without counting

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a plate with two biscuits and says "two" at once, without pointing at each. The child sees three ducks in a picture book and holds up three fingers. A child of 2 sees "two" at once for some things; a child of 4 sees two and three at once, also when the things lie in different arrangements.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The brackets name 2 or 3; the statement names no larger group. "Without counting" excludes pointing and saying the number words one by one. The statement does not say how the objects are arranged.

### Common mistakes

The child counts the two or three objects one by one. The child says "three" for any small group that is not two. The child recognises three only in a known arrangement, such as a row, and not when the objects lie apart.
