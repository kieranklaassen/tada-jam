## Official wording

praten over de invloed van weer en seizoenen op de mens

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

talking about the influence of weather and seasons on people

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child takes boots and a raincoat when it rains, or takes off a jumper when it is warm, and says why. The child talks about what people do in which weather: an umbrella, sun cream, a sledge, staying inside in a storm. A child of 2 fetches the boots when an adult says it is raining; a child of 4 says "it is cold, so I need my gloves".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The activity is talking. The statement names people (*de mens*) only; the appearance of plants and trees through the seasons is in `Omgaan met de natuur / 4`. It gives no examples of the influence and names no seasons.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age choose clothes by what they like, not by the weather, and do not link the two without an adult's question. They judge the weather by what they see through the window: sun means warm, also on a freezing day. They often turn the reason around: "it is winter because I wear my coat".
