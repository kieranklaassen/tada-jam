## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

observing and exploring the surroundings with all the senses

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child walks barefoot over grass and sand, squeezes mud, smells a herb leaf, listens for a bird and looks under a bush. Indoors the child knocks on things to hear the sound and feels what is rough, cold or wet. A child of 2 touches everything and still puts things in the mouth; a child of 4 stops to listen or smell when asked and says what they notice.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement says "all the senses" and does not list them. It stands under the sub-heading on dealing with nature (*Omgaan met de natuur*); "the surroundings" are not defined further. It does not ask a child to say what was noticed; naming is in `Natuurkundige verschijnselen / 1`.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age rely on looking and touching, and seldom use smell or hearing unless someone invites them. They do not yet keep the senses apart in words: "it smells nice" is said about a taste. They put things in the mouth that are not safe to eat.
