met zorg omgaan met planten en dieren

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

treating plants and animals with care

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child strokes a rabbit softly with a flat hand, holds a snail on an open palm, and puts it back where it was found. The child waters a plant with a small can, walks around the flower bed, and leaves the flowers on the plant. A child of 2 strokes softly with an adult's hand over their own; a child of 4 does it alone and tells another child to be gentle.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names plants and animals and gives no examples of care. It names no tasks such as feeding or watering, and no rules.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often cannot yet control their force: they squeeze, grab a tail, or hug an animal too hard without meaning harm. They pull off leaves and flowers out of interest. They do not yet notice the signs that an animal wants to be left alone.
