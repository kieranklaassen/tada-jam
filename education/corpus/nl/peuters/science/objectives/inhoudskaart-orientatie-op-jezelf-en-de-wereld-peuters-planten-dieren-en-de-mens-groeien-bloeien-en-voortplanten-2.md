## Official wording

bespreken dat baby's geboren worden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discussing that babies are born

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child talks with an adult about a new baby in the family or in the group, looks at the baby, and hears that the baby has just been born. The child looks at a photo of themselves as a baby and says "that is me". A child of 2 points and says "baby"; a child of 4 asks questions about the baby and says that they were a baby once.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The activity is discussing, and the content is only that babies are born. The text sets no level of detail: it does not say how a baby comes about or how a birth goes. Young animals are in `Groeien, bloeien en voortplanten / 3`.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age often do not believe that they were a baby themselves, or that an adult was one. They think a baby is bought or fetched somewhere, like other new things in the house. They expect a newborn baby to play with them at once.
