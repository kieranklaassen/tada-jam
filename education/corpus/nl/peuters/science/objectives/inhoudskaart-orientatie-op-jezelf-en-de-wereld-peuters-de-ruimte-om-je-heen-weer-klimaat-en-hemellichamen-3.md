## Official wording

aandacht hebben voor begrippen als zon, maan en sterren

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

paying attention to concepts such as sun, moon and stars

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child points at the sun or the moon in the sky or in a picture book and says its name. The child notices that it is light when the sun shines and that stars can be seen when it is dark, and hears a song or a story about the moon. A child of 2 points and says "moon"; a child of 4 says that the sun is there in the day and that stars can be seen when it is dark.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording is "paying attention to" (*aandacht hebben voor*): it names no action a child performs with these concepts. Sun, moon and stars are given as examples (*als*, "such as"). The statement does not mention the earth, planets, or why it gets dark.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age think the sun and the moon move along with them when they walk or ride in a car. They think the moon is only there at night and are surprised to see it by day. Many believe that the sun goes away or goes to sleep at night.
