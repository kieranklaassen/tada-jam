## Official wording

experimenteren met veilige materialen, stoffen en voorwerpen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experimenting with safe materials, substances and objects

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child pours water and sand from one cup into another, squeezes clay, drops things into a tub of water to see whether they sink, and mixes sand with water. The child stacks, rolls, shakes and knocks objects to find out what they do. A child of 2 repeats one action many times, such as filling and emptying; a child of 4 tries something on purpose and says what happened.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The word "safe" bounds the materials, substances and objects; the statement does not say which ones are safe and names none. It sets no question to answer and no result to reach. It states no further safety condition, such as supervision.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age repeat one action without changing anything, so they do not find out what causes the difference. They expect big things to sink and small things to float. They test a material by putting it in the mouth.
