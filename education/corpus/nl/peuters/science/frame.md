## What one record is

One record is one bullet (•) of the card. A sub-point (–) stays inside its bullet, and wording the card breaks over several lines is one statement. The card prints no codes. The code of a record is the pack's own: the sub-heading the bullet stands under, a slash and the position of the bullet under that sub-heading ("<sub-heading> / <position>"), or the position alone where the bullets stand directly under their bar. The lookup finds a record by that code, and the code scope names the card and the heading bar. The locator names the card, the heading bar, the sub-heading and the position of the bullet under it. The card is shared with practical life and feelings: each lane takes its own sub-headings.
