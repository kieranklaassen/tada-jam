## Official wording

zorgzaam omgaan met anderen, bijv. aaien over het hoofd van een baby

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

treating others with care, e.g. stroking a baby's head

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child strokes a baby's head softly with a flat hand, tucks a blanket around a doll, or brings a tissue to a child with a runny nose. It walks slowly around a child who lies on the floor. A child of 2 copies a caring gesture it has seen and needs an adult's hand to show how soft; a child of 4 adjusts its touch by itself and asks whether the other is all right.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. Stroking a baby's head is given as an example. "Others" is not limited to children or to babies. The statement does not mention care for animals or for things.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What children most often leave out is measuring their own force: a child means to stroke and pats too hard, or hugs so tightly that the other child cannot move. A child also continues when the other child shows that it wants this to stop.
