## Official wording

controle over zichzelf proberen te krijgen en behouden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

trying to gain and keep control over oneself

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child lifts its hand to hit, stops, and stamps its foot or calls the adult instead. It walks indoors although it wants to run, and keeps its hands on its knees while the adult hands out the fruit. A child of 2 stops when an adult says its name or holds its hand, and only for a moment; a child of 4 stops itself more often and keeps this up for the length of a short activity.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says trying: gaining and keeping control over oneself is the direction, and success is not stated. The sub-heading is about adjusting or stopping unwanted behaviour. It does not say which behaviour, for how long, or with how much help from an adult.

### Common mistakes

A child knows the rule and still acts on the impulse, because stopping an action that has started is hard at this age. A child controls itself for a while and then loses control over something small. A child controls itself in a calm moment and not when it is tired, hungry or excited.
