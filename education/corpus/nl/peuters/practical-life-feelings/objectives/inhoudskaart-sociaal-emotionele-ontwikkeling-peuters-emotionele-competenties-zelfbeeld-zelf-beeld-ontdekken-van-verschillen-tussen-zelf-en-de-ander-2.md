## Official wording

ontdekken van de verschillen en overeenkomsten tussen zichzelf en de andere kinderen in de groep: bijvoorbeeld in lichaamskenmerken, karaktereigenschappen, voorkeuren/afkeuren, afkomst en traditie

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering the differences and similarities between oneself and the other children in the group: for example in physical features, character traits, likes/dislikes, origin and tradition

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child puts its hand next to another child's hand and compares size and colour, or stands back to back with it to see who is taller. It says "you have curls, I don't", "we both like bananas" and "she doesn't like the slide". It hears that one child speaks another language at home or celebrates another feast, and tells what happens at its own home. A child of 2 notices differences it can see and touch; a child of 4 also talks about what others like and how things go in their family.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement covers both differences and similarities, between the child and the other children in the group. Physical features, character traits, likes and dislikes, origin and tradition are given as examples. It asks for discovering, and attaches no judgement to any difference.

### Common mistakes

A child notices differences and leaves out similarities. A child takes its own way (its food, its family, its language) as the normal one and calls another way "strange". A child says aloud what it sees, for example about skin colour or body size, without knowing that this can hurt.
