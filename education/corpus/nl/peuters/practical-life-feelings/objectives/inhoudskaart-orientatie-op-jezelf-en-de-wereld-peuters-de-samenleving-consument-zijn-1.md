## Official wording

verkennen van de rol van geld (kopen, verkopen, ruilen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring the role of money (buying, selling, swapping)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child plays shop: hands over a toy coin, gets an apple from the play till, and puts it in a basket. The child swaps a toy car for another child's toy, and watches an adult pay in a real shop. A child of 2 hands things over and takes things without linking this to paying; a child of 4 says "you have to pay" and waits for the coin before giving the apple.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The verb is exploring. The brackets name buying, selling and swapping. The statement names no coins, amounts or prices and no counting; it sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age think that more coins is more money, whatever the coins are worth. They think the shop gives money, because they see change come back, and that a bank card or a cash machine gives money without limit. After a swap they want their own thing back.
