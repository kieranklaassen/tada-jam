## Official wording

reageren vanuit intuïtief gedrag

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reacting from intuitive behaviour

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child acts at once on what it feels: it grabs the toy it wants, pushes when it is pushed, runs to what interests it, and hugs a child it likes. The action comes before any thought about a rule or about the other child. A child of 2 reacts this way most of the time; a child of 4 sometimes pauses first, mostly in familiar situations.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement describes how the child reacts: from intuitive behaviour. It stands under the sub-heading about adjusting or stopping unwanted behaviour, beside `Bijsturen/stoppen van ongewenst gedrag / 1` about trying to gain control over oneself. It does not say what the child does instead, and it does not call intuitive behaviour unwanted.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What children most often leave out is the pause between impulse and action: the child hits, grabs or runs off before it has looked at what follows, and is surprised or upset by the other child's reaction.
