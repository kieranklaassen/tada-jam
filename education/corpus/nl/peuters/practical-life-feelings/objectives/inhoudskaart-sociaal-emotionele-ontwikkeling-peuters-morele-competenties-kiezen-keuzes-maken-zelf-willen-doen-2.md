## Official wording

experimenteren om nieuwe dingen te ontdekken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experimenting in order to discover new things

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child pours water from a cup into a funnel, a sieve and a bottle and watches what comes out. It stacks blocks in a new way until they fall, mixes sand with water, or rolls different things down a slope. A child of 2 repeats one action many times with small changes; a child of 4 says what it is going to try and what it expects.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names experimenting with one purpose, discovering new things, and says nothing about what is discovered or with which materials. It stands under the sub-heading about wanting to do things oneself. It states no safety condition.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What children most often leave out is changing something: a child repeats exactly what worked once and does not try a variation. A child also stops when the first attempt gives no result, or waits for an adult to show what to do.
