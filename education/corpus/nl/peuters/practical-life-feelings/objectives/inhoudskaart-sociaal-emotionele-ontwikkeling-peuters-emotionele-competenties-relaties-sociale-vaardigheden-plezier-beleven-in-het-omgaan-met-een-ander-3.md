## Official wording

contact maken met andere kinderen (met of zonder taal)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

making contact with other children (with or without language)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child holds out a toy to another child, sits down beside it and copies what it does, or taps its arm and points. It says "hi", says the child's name, or says "look". A child of 2 makes contact mostly by looking, offering an object and imitating; a child of 4 mostly by speaking, for example "can I join?".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The brackets say with or without language, so contact by a look, a gesture or offering an object is included as much as words. The statement is about making contact, not about keeping play going. It names no number of children.

### Common mistakes

A child makes contact by taking the other child's toy or by pushing, because it has no other way yet. A child stands near and waits, without a signal the other child can notice. A child gives up after one attempt that got no answer.
