## Official wording

ervaren van verschillende rollen bij samen spelen en samenwerken (b)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing different roles when playing together and working together (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In pretend play a child is the doctor and then the patient, the shopkeeper and then the customer. In a shared task one child holds the dustpan and the other sweeps, and then they change over. A child of 2 takes a part by copying one action, such as stirring in a pan; a child of 4 says "I am the mother, you are the baby" and keeps the part for some time.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The card marks this as an aspect of citizenship (b). The verb is experiencing. The statement names two settings, playing together and working together, and names no roles. It does not say whether pretend roles, roles in a task or both are meant.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often want the same favourite part every time and refuse to change. They leave the part or change the play without telling the others. They decide the other child's part and do not ask.
