## Official wording

voor een kortdurende periode rustig werken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

working calmly or quietly (*rustig*) for a short period

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sits at a table and threads large beads, does a peg puzzle or draws, and stays with it for a short while or until it is finished. It keeps its voice low and stays in its place. A child of 2 stays with one activity only briefly, and longer when an adult sits beside it; a child of 4 stays with a task it chose for several minutes without an adult next to it.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording says a short period and gives no number of minutes. The Dutch *rustig* means both calmly and quietly. It does not say what kind of work, and it does not say whether the child chose the activity.

### Common mistakes

A child leaves the activity at the first distraction, such as a sound or another child walking past. A child stays seated and stops doing the task. A child works calmly on what it chose and not on what it was given.
