## Official wording

helpen van andere kinderen of de professional

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

helping other children or the professional

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child carries the cups to the table with the adult, hands over the cloth to wipe up spilled water, or holds the cupboard door open. It gives a younger child the shoe that fell off or pulls up a chair for it. A child of 2 helps by fetching and handing over one thing when asked; a child of 4 sees by itself that help is needed and offers it.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names two who are helped: other children or the professional, the card's word for the adult who works with the group. It names no tasks. It does not say whether the child helps when asked or by itself.

### Common mistakes

A child helps by doing the whole task for another child who wanted to do it alone. A child starts helping and walks away before the task is done. A child helps where no help was wanted, for example by tidying away a building another child is still making.
