## Official wording

zelf (graag) dingen willen doen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

wanting (gladly) to do things oneself

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "do it myself", pushes the adult's hand away, and puts on its own boots, pours its own drink or climbs onto the chair alone. It takes the cloth to wipe the table and wants to turn on the tap. A child of 2 insists on doing things itself that it cannot yet complete; a child of 4 does many of them alone and accepts help for the hard part.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. This is a statement about wanting: the child wants, and likes, to do things itself. It does not say that the child succeeds, and it names no actions. `Zelf willen doen / 4` is the statement about indicating when help is needed.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What children most often leave out is asking for help in time: a child insists on doing it alone, cannot go on and becomes angry. A child also refuses a task entirely once an adult has begun it, and wants to start again from the beginning.
