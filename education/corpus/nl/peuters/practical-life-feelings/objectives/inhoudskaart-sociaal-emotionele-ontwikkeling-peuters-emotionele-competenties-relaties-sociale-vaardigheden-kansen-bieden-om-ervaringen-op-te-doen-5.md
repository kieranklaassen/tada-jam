## Official wording

ontdekken dat er samen gewerkt kan worden om iets groters en/of leukers te bereiken

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that it is possible to work together in order to achieve something bigger and/or more fun

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children carry a large box that neither can lift alone. Several children put their blocks on one tower and it becomes taller than any tower one child built, or two children pull a cart with a third child in it. A child of 2 joins when an adult starts the shared action; a child of 4 asks another child to help or to join.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The verb is discovering: the statement is about the child finding out that working together is possible, not about working together well. "Bigger and/or more fun" is the only description of the result. It sets no group size, no task and no division of roles.

### Common mistakes

A child takes over the whole task instead of doing one part of it. A child works beside the other child on its own piece, so that no shared result is made. A child stops when the other child does something differently from what it had in mind.
