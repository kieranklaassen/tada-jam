## Official wording

basaal reageren op behoeften van anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reacting in a basic way to needs of others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child hands a dropped toy back to a baby, passes the bowl to a child who reaches for it, or fetches the adult when a child has fallen. It moves aside for a child who wants to pass. A child of 2 reacts with one simple act, often giving something or calling an adult; a child of 4 sometimes first asks "do you want this?".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The word "basic" bounds the statement: a simple reaction is meant, and nothing says that the child meets the need. It does not say which needs or whose. Recognising that others have needs is `Ontdekken van de eigen kwaliteiten en krachten / 3`.

### Common mistakes

A child gives what it would want itself, not what the other needs. A child sees the need and waits for an adult to act. A child reacts to the need of a child it likes and not to that of others.
