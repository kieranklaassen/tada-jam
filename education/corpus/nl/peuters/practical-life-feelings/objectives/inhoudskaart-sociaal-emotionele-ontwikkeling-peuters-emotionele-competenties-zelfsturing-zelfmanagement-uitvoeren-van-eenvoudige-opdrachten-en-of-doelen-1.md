## Official wording

(actief) opruimen van de gebruikte materialen, bijv. om naar een ander spel te gaan

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(actively) tidying up the materials used, e.g. in order to go to another game or play activity (*spel*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child puts the blocks back in the block box and the box on its shelf before it goes to the sand table. It carries the puzzle to the cupboard, hangs the apron on its hook and wipes the table with a cloth. A child of 2 puts a few things in a container while an adult tidies along and points; a child of 4 tidies what it used and knows where most things belong.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement is about the materials the child used. Going to another game or play activity is given as an example of a reason; the Dutch *spel* means both. The bracketed "actively" stresses that the child takes part itself. It does not say whether the child tidies alone or with others, or without being asked.

### Common mistakes

A child starts playing with the things it is putting away. A child puts everything into the nearest box, not where each thing belongs. A child tidies a few things and regards the task as done.
