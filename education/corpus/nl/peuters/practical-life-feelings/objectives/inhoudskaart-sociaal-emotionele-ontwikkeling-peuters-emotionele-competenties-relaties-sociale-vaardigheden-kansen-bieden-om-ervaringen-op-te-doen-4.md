## Official wording

uitstellen van eigen behoeften

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

postponing one's own needs

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child wants a drink and waits until everyone sits at the table. It wants the red bicycle, hears "after Sam", and plays with something else until the bicycle is free. A child of 2 waits a very short time and needs an adult who stays near and says what comes next; a child of 4 waits a few minutes and sometimes finds something to do in the meantime by itself.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names the child's own needs and no length of waiting. It does not say which needs. It does not say whether an adult helps.

### Common mistakes

A child takes "later" as "no" and reacts as if the wish is refused. A child can wait while it is busy with something and cannot when the wanted thing stays in view. A child asks again every few seconds because it has no sense yet of how long waiting lasts.
