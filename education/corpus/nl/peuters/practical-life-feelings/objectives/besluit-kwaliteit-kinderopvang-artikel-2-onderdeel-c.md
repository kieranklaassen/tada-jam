## Official wording

kinderen worden begeleid in hun interacties, waarbij hen spelenderwijs sociale kennis en vaardigheden worden bijgebracht, teneinde kinderen in staat te stellen steeds zelfstandiger relaties met anderen op te bouwen en te onderhouden, en

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kwaliteit kinderopvang, artikel 2, onderdeel c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

children are guided in their interactions, in which social knowledge and skills are taught to them through play, in order to enable children to build and maintain relationships with others more and more independently, and

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 2 to 4 in childcare plays next to and with other children, with an adult near who helps in a conflict: the adult gives words such as "may I have a turn?" and the child repeats them. The child takes turns, passes the fruit bowl on, and is friends again after a quarrel with the adult's help. A child of 2 plays alongside others and needs the adult in each conflict; a child of 4 asks to join, agrees with others on who does what, and settles some quarrels with words.

### Limits

This is a legal requirement on the childcare provider, not on the child: it says what the provider sees to, and sets nothing a child must be able to do. It is one lettered part of an article; the article's opening words, which are in this record's locator and not in its wording, say that the holder (*houder*) or the childminder (*gastouder*) sees to this in any case, taking account of the stage of development the children are in. The part names guidance in children's interactions and the teaching of social knowledge and skills through play (*spelenderwijs*); the aim is that children can build and maintain relationships with others more and more independently. It names no particular social skills and no ages. The wording ends with "and" because the next lettered part of the article follows it.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age take a thing they want without asking and do not see why the other child is upset. They do not know how to join a play that has started, so they disturb it or stay at the edge. After a quarrel they do not know how to start again without an adult.
