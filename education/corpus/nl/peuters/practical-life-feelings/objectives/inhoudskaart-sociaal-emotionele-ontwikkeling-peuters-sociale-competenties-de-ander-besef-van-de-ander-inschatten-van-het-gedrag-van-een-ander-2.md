## Official wording

duidelijk maken aan een ander kind van eigen gevoelens en emoties

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

making one's own feelings and emotions clear to another child

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "stop, I don't like that" to a child who pulls its sleeve, and holds up a hand. It says "I'm angry with you" or shows an angry face, or laughs and says "again!" when something they do together is fun. A child of 2 makes a feeling clear to another child with its body, a cry or "no"; a child of 4 uses a word for the feeling and says what caused it.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names who receives the message: another child, not the adult. It covers the child's own feelings and emotions and does not say whether words or gestures are used. Showing feelings in a non-verbal way is `Ontdekken van behoeften, gevoelens en emoties van zichzelf / 2`.

### Common mistakes

A child tells the adult and not the child concerned. A child shows the feeling by hitting or pushing, which the other child does not read as a message about a feeling. A child expects the other child to know how it feels without any signal.
