discovering features and expressions of friendship

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child chooses to sit next to the same child each day, shares a biscuit with them, holds their hand in the line, and asks "will you play with me?". The child says "you are my friend" and makes a drawing to give away. A child of 2 plays next to a familiar child and is glad to see them; a child of 4 names a friend and says what they do together.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The verb is discovering. The statement names no features and no expressions of friendship. It stands under the sub-heading on relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*); the statement itself is about friendship only.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age call whoever they are playing with at that moment their friend, and "you are not my friend any more" means only that they are angry now. They think a friend must want the same as they do. They think that giving a thing is the way to get a friend.
