## Official wording

omgaan met eigen gevoelens

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with one's own feelings

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child who is angry stamps, then goes to an adult or to a quiet corner, and comes back to play when calm. A sad child fetches their cuddly toy or asks for a hug; an excited child jumps and claps. A child of 2 needs an adult to hold and soothe them; a child of 4 sometimes uses a way they have learned, such as saying "stop, I do not like that" where they would have hit before.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement does not say what "dealing with" (*omgaan met*) covers, and names no feelings and no ways of calming down. It does not say that a child does this without an adult's help.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age show a strong feeling at once in what they do: they hit, bite, throw or scream, and cannot stop themselves. While they have a feeling, they think it will never end. They know a way to calm down when they are calm and cannot use it at the moment itself.
