## Official wording

waarnemen van gevoelens bij anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

perceiving feelings in others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child stops playing and looks when another child starts to cry. It looks from the crying child to the adult, becomes quiet, or points and says "she is crying". It laughs when others laugh. A child of 2 notices strong feelings shown with sound and movement; a child of 4 also notices a child who sits apart quietly.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The verb is perceiving: the statement asks that the child notices feelings in others, not that it names them or does something about them. It does not list the feelings. `Inschatten van het gedrag van een ander / 1` is the statement about assessing the expressions of simple feelings.

### Common mistakes

A child notices feelings that are shown loudly and misses those shown quietly. A child that is busy with its own play notices nothing around it. A child becomes upset itself by another child's crying and can no longer attend to that child.
