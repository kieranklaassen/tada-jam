## Official wording

Een houder van een kindercentrum of een voorziening voor gastouderopvang biedt verantwoorde kinderopvang, waaronder wordt verstaan het in een veilige en gezonde omgeving bieden van emotionele veiligheid aan kinderen, het bevorderen van de persoonlijke en sociale competentie van kinderen en de socialisatie van kinderen door overdracht van algemeen aanvaarde waarden en normen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Wet kinderopvang, artikel 1.49, eerste lid.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

A holder (*houder*, the operator) of a childcare centre or of a facility for childminder care (*gastouderopvang*) offers responsible childcare, which is understood to mean offering emotional safety to children in a safe and healthy environment, promoting the personal and social competence of children and the socialisation of children through the passing on of generally accepted values and norms.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 2 to 4 in childcare plays in rooms and outdoors that are safe and clean, and goes to a familiar adult for comfort. The child tries things alone, such as pouring, climbing and dressing, plays with other children, and takes part in the habits of the group: greeting, eating together at the table, tidying up. A child of 2 stays near the adult and joins in for a short time; a child of 4 follows the routine of the day mostly alone and plays with children they choose.

### Limits

This is a legal requirement on the holder of a childcare centre or of a facility for childminder care, not on the child: it says what responsible childcare is, and sets nothing a child must be able to do. The sentence names emotional safety in a safe and healthy environment, the personal and social competence of children, and their socialisation through the passing on of generally accepted values and norms. The Dutch leaves open whether "in a safe and healthy environment" belongs to emotional safety only or to all that follows; the gloss keeps this open. The sentence names no ages, activities or levels. Article 2 of the Besluit kwaliteit kinderopvang, whose lettered parts a to d are other records of this lane, opens with a reference to responsible childcare.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age in childcare most often find the parting from the parent hard, and a child who does not yet feel safe plays little and explores little. They need time to learn that the habits of the group differ from the habits at home.
