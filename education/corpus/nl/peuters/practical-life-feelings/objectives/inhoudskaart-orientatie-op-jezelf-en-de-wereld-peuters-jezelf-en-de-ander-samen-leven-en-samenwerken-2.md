## Official wording

rekening houden met anderen binnen de groep

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking others within the group into account

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child waits for a turn on the slide, moves along on the bench to make room, and hands a block to a child who needs one. The child speaks softly when a baby sleeps and steps around another child's building. A child of 2 does this when an adult asks and shows how; a child of 4 does it without being asked some of the time and says "you first".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names no behaviours such as sharing or waiting, and bounds it to others within the group. It does not say that a child does this without an adult's prompt.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age do not notice that another child wants something unless that child says or shows it clearly. They share a thing when they have finished with it, not while they still want it. They walk through another child's play without seeing it.
