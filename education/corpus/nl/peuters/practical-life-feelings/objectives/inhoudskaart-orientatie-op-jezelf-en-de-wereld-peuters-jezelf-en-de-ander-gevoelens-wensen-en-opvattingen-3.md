## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child looks at another child who cries and says "sad", or fetches that child's cuddly toy. The child points at faces in a picture book and says which one is happy and which one is angry. A child of 2 looks, stops playing and sometimes starts to cry as well; a child of 4 says what the other child feels and sometimes what happened.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The card marks this as an aspect of citizenship (b). The statement is about recognising; it names no feelings and does not ask a child to respond or to comfort.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age assume that another person feels what they feel themselves and likes what they like. They look only at the mouth of a face, so they mix up scared, surprised and angry. They go by the event and not by the face: someone at a party must be happy.
