## Official wording

aandacht hebben voor milieuzorg zoals 'afval in de afvalbak'

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

paying attention to environmental care (*milieuzorg*, care for the environment) such as 'rubbish in the rubbish bin'

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child holds an empty wrapper, walks to the rubbish bin and drops it in. The child watches an adult put an apple core in the bin, and carries a scrap of paper from the floor to the bin. A child of 2 lets a banana peel fall into the bin; a child of 4 puts an empty drink carton in the bin.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording is "paying attention to" (*aandacht hebben voor*): it names no action a child performs. 'Rubbish in the rubbish bin' is given as an example (*zoals*, "such as") of environmental care (*milieuzorg*); the card prints it in quotation marks, as a phrase without a verb. The wording names no other example and no kind of rubbish, and does not say what else environmental care covers.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often let a wrapper or a peel fall where they stand once the food is gone. They put things in the bin that are not rubbish, such as a spoon or a toy, and take things out of the bin again to look at them.
