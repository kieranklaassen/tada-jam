## Official wording

beseffen dat je elkaar in een groep nodig hebt en afhankelijk bent van elkaar (bijv. bij samen spelen) (b)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising that in a group you need each other and depend on each other (e.g. when playing together) (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children carry a long plank together, or one holds the tower steady while the other puts a block on top. A child finds that a seesaw or a play shop does not work alone and fetches another child. A child of 2 lets another child or an adult help; a child of 4 says "I need you for this" or "you hold, I pour".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The card marks this as an aspect of citizenship (b). The verb is realising. Playing together is given as an example (*bijv.*). The statement names no tasks and no group size.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age want to do everything themselves and see help as taking over. They give the other child no real part: the other may only watch or hand things over. They leave a shared task halfway and do not see that the other child cannot go on alone.
