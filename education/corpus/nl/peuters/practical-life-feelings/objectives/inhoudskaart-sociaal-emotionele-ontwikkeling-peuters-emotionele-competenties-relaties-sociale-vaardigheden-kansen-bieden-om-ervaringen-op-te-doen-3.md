## Official wording

behoeften van zichzelf en anderen herkennen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising needs of oneself and of others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child notices that it is thirsty and fetches its cup, or that it is tired and lies down on the cushion. It sees that another child reaches for a block it cannot get and passes it, or hears a baby cry and says "baby wants bottle". A child of 2 notices its own hunger, thirst or tiredness and shows it by acting; a child of 4 also says what another child needs.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording covers needs of the child itself and of others, and the verb is recognising, not acting on them. It names no particular needs. `Kansen bieden om ervaringen op te doen / 4` is the statement about postponing one's own needs.

### Common mistakes

A child assumes that another child wants what it wants itself. A child does not notice its own need until it is strong, for example needing the toilet or being tired, and then reacts with crying or anger. A child sees that another child is upset and cannot tell what that child needs.
