## Official wording

herkennen dat anderen ook behoeften hebben

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising that others also have needs

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sees that another child also wants a turn on the tricycle and gets off after its round. It notices that the adult is feeding a baby and waits beside her, or hands a cup to a child who has none. A child of 2 notices another's need when it is shown strongly, by crying or reaching; a child of 4 notices it from smaller signs and says "he wants one too".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The verb is recognising; the word "also" sets the needs of others beside the child's own. The statement asks for no action on those needs. `Open staan voor de emoties van een ander / 4` is the statement about reacting to needs of others.

### Common mistakes

A child assumes others need what it needs itself, and offers its own favourite thing. A child recognises another's need only when its own need is met. A child sees the need and treats it as less urgent than its own wish.
