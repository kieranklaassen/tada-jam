## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child washes and dries their hands, wipes their nose with a tissue, brushes their teeth before an adult brushes them again, and goes to the potty or the toilet. The child pulls up their own trousers and tells an adult when they are wet, cold or hurt. A child of 2 holds out their hands to be washed and tries the first steps; a child of 4 washes their hands and uses the toilet mostly alone, with reminders.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names no tasks: washing, teeth, the toilet, dressing, eating and rest are not named in it. It stands under the sub-heading on health and hygiene (*Gezondheid en hygiëne*). It does not say that a child does this without help.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age skip steps: they wet their hands without soap, or do not dry them. They brush only the front teeth, and only for a few seconds. They notice too late that they need the toilet, mostly when they are busy playing.
