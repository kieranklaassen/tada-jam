## Official wording

bespreken wat verliefdheid betekent en manieren waarop mensen hieraan uiting geven

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discussing what being in love means and ways in which people express it

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child hears the word in a picture book or about two people they know, and asks or tells something about it in the group. The child says whom they like very much. A child of 2 listens and looks at the pictures; a child of 4 uses the word and tells something they have seen people do.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The activity is discussing. The text sets no level of detail: it names no ways of expressing being in love and no kinds of relationship. It stands under the sub-heading on relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*); nothing else on that subject is in this statement.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age use "in love" for liking anyone very much, including a parent, a friend or a pet. They say that they will marry a parent, a brother or a sister. They giggle or tease with the word without knowing what it means.
