## Official wording

houden aan de regels van de groep

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

keeping to the rules of the group

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sits down on the mat when the group gathers, washes its hands before eating, and puts its cup back on the tray after drinking. It waits at the door until the adult opens it. A child of 2 follows a rule while the adult is near and reminds it; a child of 4 follows the familiar rules of the daily routine mostly without a reminder.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names the rules of the group and nothing more: it does not say which rules, how many, or whether the child follows them with or without a reminder. Telling what is allowed is a separate statement, `Ervaren wat het vraagt om in een groep deel te nemen / 1`.

### Common mistakes

A child follows a rule only while an adult is watching. A child forgets a rule when it is excited or busy with something it wants. A child keeps a rule in one place (on the mat) and does not apply it in another (at the table).
