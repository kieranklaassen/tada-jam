## Official wording

ontdekken wat de sterke en minder sterke kwaliteiten zijn

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering what the strong and less strong qualities are

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child finds out that it can climb the low frame by itself and cannot yet do the puzzle with many pieces. It says "I can do it" when it pours water without spilling and "can't" when the zip does not close. A child of 2 finds out by trying and shows pride or frustration; a child of 4 says what it is good at and what is still hard, and compares itself with others.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording names strong and less strong qualities; the sub-heading shows that the child's own qualities and strengths are meant. It names no kind of quality. It speaks of "less strong", not of weak or bad.

### Common mistakes

A child of this age rates itself high in everything and says it can do what it cannot yet do. A child concludes from one failed attempt that it "can't" and stops trying. A child takes what it likes for what it is good at.
