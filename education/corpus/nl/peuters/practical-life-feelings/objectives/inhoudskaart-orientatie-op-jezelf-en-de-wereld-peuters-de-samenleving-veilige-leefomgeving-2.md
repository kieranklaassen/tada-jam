## Official wording

ontdekken dat een veilige leefomgeving vraagt om hygiënisch, nauwkeurig, veilig en zorgzaam handelen (bijv. omgaan met voorwerpen, apparaten en gereedschappen en beseffen dat iets gevaarlijk kan zijn)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering that a safe living environment calls for hygienic, precise, safe and caring action (e.g. handling objects, appliances and tools and realising that something can be dangerous)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child washes their hands before eating, carries scissors with the points down, and puts a hammer back on the workbench after use. The child hears and repeats that the oven is hot and that a socket is not for fingers, and keeps away from both. A child of 2 stops when an adult says "hot" or "sharp"; a child of 4 says the warning to another child and cuts with child scissors while an adult watches.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The verb is discovering. The four words for the action are hygienic, precise (*nauwkeurig*), safe and caring. Handling objects, appliances and tools, and realising that something can be dangerous, are given as examples (*bijv.*). The statement names no particular appliances, tools or dangers, and states no safety condition such as supervision.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age know a warning as a word but do not act on it when they are curious: they say "hot" and still reach out. They see danger only in things that have hurt them before. They think a thing is safe because an adult uses it, and copy the adult with a knife or a plug.
