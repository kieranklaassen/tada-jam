## Official wording

ontdekken van patronen in het eigen gedrag en het gedrag van anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering patterns in one's own behaviour and the behaviour of others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child finds out that one child always cries when its tower falls and that another always wants the red cup. It notices about itself that it gets cross before the afternoon sleep, or says "I always cry when mummy goes". It says "he will be angry" before taking a toy from a certain child. A child of 2 expects fixed reactions from familiar people without putting this into words; a child of 4 says "always" or "every time" about someone's behaviour.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The verb is discovering, and the patterns are in the child's own behaviour and in the behaviour of others. The statement does not say which patterns or how the child shows that it has discovered one. It asks for no explanation of the behaviour.

### Common mistakes

A child sees patterns in others much sooner than in itself. A child makes a rule from one event: a child that pushed once is "the child who pushes". A child expects the usual reaction and is confused when the other person reacts differently.
