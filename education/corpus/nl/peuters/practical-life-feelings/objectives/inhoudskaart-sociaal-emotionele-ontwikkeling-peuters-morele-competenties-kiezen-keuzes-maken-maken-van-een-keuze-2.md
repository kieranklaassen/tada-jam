## Official wording

kiezen met wie of waarmee te willen spelen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

choosing with whom or with what one wants to play

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child walks to the shelf and takes the train, or goes to the home corner. It takes another child by the hand and says "come", or sits down beside the child it wants to play with. A child of 2 chooses mostly a thing or a place and plays beside whoever is there; a child of 4 also chooses a playmate by name.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names two choices joined by "or": with whom, or with what. It sets no number of options; `Maken van een keuze / 1` is the statement that speaks of two. It does not say that the chosen child has to agree.

### Common mistakes

A child chooses what another child is playing with at that moment, not what is free. A child chooses a playmate and does not ask or check whether that child wants to play. A child always chooses the same thing or the same child.
