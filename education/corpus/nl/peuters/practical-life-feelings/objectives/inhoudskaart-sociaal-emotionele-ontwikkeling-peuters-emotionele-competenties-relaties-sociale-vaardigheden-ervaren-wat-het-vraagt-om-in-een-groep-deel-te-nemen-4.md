## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing what is right and wrong

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "that is not nice" when a doll is thrown on the floor, or "good" when someone helps to carry the box. Looking at a picture book, it points to the child who takes a toy away and says that this is wrong. A child of 2 uses single words such as "naughty" or "ouch"; a child of 4 says in a short sentence what was wrong and sometimes why.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The verb is describing: the statement asks for words about right and wrong, not for acting on them. It does not say whose behaviour is described or in which situations. It names no reasons that the child gives.

### Common mistakes

A child judges by the result and not by the intention: a child who broke something by accident is called naughty. A child calls something wrong only because an adult got angry about it. A child calls what it does not like "wrong".
