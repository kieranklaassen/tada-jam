## Official wording

kijken naar andere kinderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

looking at other children

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child stands at the edge of the sandpit and watches what two other children do with the water. It stops its own play to look when a child laughs loudly or builds something tall, and later copies what it saw. A child of 2 watches for a long time without joining; a child of 4 watches briefly and then joins or imitates.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The wording asks only for looking at other children; joining, talking and playing together are not in it. The sub-heading is about taking pleasure in dealing with another person. It sets no duration.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. A child who stands and looks is engaged with the others, and this is easily taken for not taking part. What children of this age most often leave out is the step from looking to joining.
