## Official wording

aangeven wanneer hulp nodig is

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

indicating when help is needed

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child holds its shoe out to the adult, says "help" or "can't do it", or pulls the adult by the hand to the tap it cannot reach. It brings the jar it cannot open. A child of 2 shows that it needs help by holding the object out, by sounds or by crying; a child of 4 asks in words and says what the help is for, such as "can you do the zip?".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement is about indicating; it does not prescribe words, so a gesture is included. It does not say to whom the child turns, an adult or another child. It stands under the sub-heading about wanting to do things oneself, beside `Zelf willen doen / 1`.

### Common mistakes

A child goes on alone until it cries or throws the object, and does not ask. A child asks for help before trying. A child signals that something is wrong without making clear what help it wants.
