## Official wording

beseffen dat er overeenkomsten en verschillen zijn in gedrag van mensen (b)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld - peuters (pre-fase), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising that there are similarities and differences in people's behaviour (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child notices that one child likes to run and shout and another likes to sit with a book, and says so. The child sees that at a friend's house shoes come off at the door and at home they stay on, and asks why. A child of 2 watches what another child does and copies it; a child of 4 says "she does it differently" or "he likes that too".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The card marks this as an aspect of citizenship (b). The verb is realising. The statement gives no examples of behaviour and asks for no judgement of it.

### Common mistakes

Children of this age take their own way, or their family's way, for the only right way and call another way "wrong" or "naughty". They explain behaviour by what the child is ("he is naughty"), not by what happened. They notice differences more readily than similarities.
