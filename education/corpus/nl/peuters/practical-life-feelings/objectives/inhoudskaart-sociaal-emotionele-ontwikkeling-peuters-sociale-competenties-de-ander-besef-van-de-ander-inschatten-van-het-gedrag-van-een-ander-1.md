## Official wording

inschatten van de uitingen van eenvoudige gevoelens bij anderen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

assessing the expressions of simple feelings in others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sees another child cry and says "sad", or sees it laugh and laughs along. It looks at the adult's face before touching something and stops when the face is stern. It keeps a distance from a child that screams and stamps. A child of 2 reads clear, strong signals such as crying and laughing; a child of 4 also reads a frown or a quiet, turned-away posture and says what the other feels.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement is limited to the expressions of simple feelings; it does not list them. The sub-heading is about assessing another person's behaviour. Mixed or hidden feelings are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

A child reads crying as sadness in every case, also when the other child is angry or in pain. A child takes a loud, excited voice for anger. A child assumes the other feels what it would feel itself in that situation.
