## Official wording

kunnen kiezen tussen twee keuzemogelijkheden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

being able to choose between two options

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

An adult holds up an apple and a pear, and a child points to one, takes it, or says its name. The child chooses between the red and the blue cup, or between the sand table and the book corner. A child of 2 chooses by reaching for one of two things it can see; a child of 4 chooses between two things that are only named.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement sets the number of options at two. It does not say what the options are, whether the child can see them, or how the child shows its choice; making the choice clear in words is `Maken van een keuze / 3`. Choosing from more than two options is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

A child wants both and takes both. A child that is asked "this or that?" repeats the option named last without weighing the two. A child chooses, sees the other option go to someone else, and then wants that one.
