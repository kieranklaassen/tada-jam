## Official wording

gebruiken en begrijpen van het woord 'wij' (kent enkele namen)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using and understanding the word 'we' (knows some names)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "we are going outside" and "we made that", and gets up when the adult says "we are going to eat". It points to children in the circle and says their names, or brings a cup to "Sam" when asked. A child of 2 knows the names of the adult and one or two children and acts with the group when it hears "we"; a child of 4 says "we" itself and knows the names of several children.

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The statement names one word, "we" (*wij*), both used and understood. The brackets add that the child knows some names; they do not say whose names or how many. The sub-heading is about being part of a group.

### Common mistakes

A child says "I" or its own name where it means the group, or says "we" where it means only itself. A child does not count itself in when the adult says "we", and stays seated until it is spoken to by name. A child knows another child's face and not its name, and says "that boy".
