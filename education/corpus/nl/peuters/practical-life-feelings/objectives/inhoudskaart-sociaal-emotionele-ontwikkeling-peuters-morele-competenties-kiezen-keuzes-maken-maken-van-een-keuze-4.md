## Official wording

begrijpen van de consequenties van een keuze

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudskaart Sociaal-emotionele ontwikkeling - Jonge kind - pré-fase (peuters), page 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

understanding the consequences of a choice

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child chooses the pear and sees that the apple goes back in the bowl. It chooses to play outside and finds that it is then not at the painting table, or pours all its water into the sand and has none left to drink. A child of 2 experiences the result and protests when the other option is gone; a child of 4 sometimes says beforehand "then I can't have that one".

### Limits

This statement describes what is offered to children of about 2 to 4 before they start school, not what a child must be able to do. The verb is understanding, and the statement names consequences of a choice without saying which or how far ahead. It names no kind of choice. It does not say whether the child foresees the consequence or understands it afterwards.

### Common mistakes

A child expects to have the other option as well after choosing. A child does not connect a result that comes later with the choice it made earlier. A child takes the result as something an adult did to it, not as following from its own choice.
