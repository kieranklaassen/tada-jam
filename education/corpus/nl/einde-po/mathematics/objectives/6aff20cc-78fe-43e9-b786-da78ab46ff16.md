## Official wording

maken van grafische representaties van data en daaruit conclusies trekken;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 12 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

making graphical representations of data and drawing conclusions from them;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 takes a table of the rainfall in each month and draws a bar chart on squared paper: the child chooses the steps on the axis, labels both axes and draws the bars. Then the child writes down what the chart shows, such as the wettest month, or that the second half of the year was wetter than the first.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 12 A, which says that the pupil interprets and represents data; 12 A sits under core goal 12. The kinds of graphical representation are listed in item 12 A e: charts, graphs and infographics. The item does not say which data, how much data, or whether the work is done on paper or on a computer. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child puts unequal steps on an axis. A child joins separate categories, such as kinds of pet, with a line as if there were values in between. The conclusion only repeats one value, or says more than the data show.
