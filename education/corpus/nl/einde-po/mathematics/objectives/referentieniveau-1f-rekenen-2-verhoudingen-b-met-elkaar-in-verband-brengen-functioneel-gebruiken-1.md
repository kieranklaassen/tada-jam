## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing a part of a whole with a fraction

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at a pizza cut into 8 equal slices of which 3 are gone and says "3/8 of the pizza has been eaten". Of a group of 20 children in which 5 wear glasses the child says "5 of the 20, a quarter". The child cuts a quarter off a ribbon and names the two pieces as ¼ and ¾.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Verhoudingen* (ratios), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in row B, *Met elkaar in verband brengen* (relating to one another); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement does not say whether the whole is one thing or a group of things. It names no denominators, does not ask for simplifying, and sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child compares the part with the other part instead of with the whole: 3 slices eaten and 5 left is called 3/5. A child counts parts of unequal size as equal parts. A child swaps numerator and denominator and writes 8/3.
