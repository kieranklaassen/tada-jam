## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

choice to order information with table, graph, diagram

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 has the temperature of each day of a week and chooses a line graph, with the reason that it shows how the temperature changes from day to day. For the favourite sports of the class the child chooses a table or a bar chart, with the reason that these are separate groups to compare. The child says what the other form would have shown less well.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain *Verbanden* (relationships), under *weten waarom* (understanding why). It stands in part B, *Met elkaar in verband brengen* (relating things to each other). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The fragment is about the choice between forms for ordering information. It names three forms, table, graph (*grafiek*) and diagram, and no others; it names no kinds of graph or diagram. The Dutch *diagram* is the word used for charts such as the bar chart (*staafdiagram*). It has no verb; under this heading, understanding and giving reasons for the choice is presumably meant.

### Common mistakes

A child always chooses the same form. A child joins separate groups, such as favourite sports, with a line as if one changed into the next. A child gives no reason for the choice other than how it looks.
