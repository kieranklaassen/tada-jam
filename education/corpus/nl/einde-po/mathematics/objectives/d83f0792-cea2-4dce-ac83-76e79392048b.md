## Official wording

herkennen, beschrijven en voortzetten van patronen in rijen getallen en figuren;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 13 A a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising, describing and continuing patterns in rows of numbers and figures;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at 2, 6, 18, 54, says "times 3 each time" and writes the next two numbers. With square tiles the child lays the next figure in a row of growing L-shapes, draws the one after that on squared paper, and says in words what is added at each step.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 13 A, which says that the pupil reasons about patterns and relationships; 13 A sits under core goal 13. It names three actions: recognising, describing and continuing. It does not say which kinds of pattern are meant (repeating, growing by a fixed step, multiplying), and a formula for any place in the row is not mentioned. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child expects every row to grow by adding, finds +4 and then +12 in 2, 6, 18, and concludes there is no rule. In a row of figures a child counts the new pieces at one step and all the pieces at the next. A child continues a repeating pattern from the wrong place in the repeat.
