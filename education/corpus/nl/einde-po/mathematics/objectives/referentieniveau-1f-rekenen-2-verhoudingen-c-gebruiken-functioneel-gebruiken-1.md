## Official wording

eenvoudige verhoudingsproblemen (met mooie getallen) oplossen

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 2 Verhoudingen, niveau 1F, C Gebruiken / Functioneel gebruiken / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

solving simple ratio problems (*verhoudingsproblemen*; the Dutch *verhouding* covers both ratio and proportion) (with nice numbers)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a recipe for 4 people with 200 g of flour and works out 400 g for 8 people and 100 g for 2. For 3 pens that cost 6 euros the child says that 6 pens cost 12 euros. The child writes the pairs of numbers in a table with two rows.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Verhoudingen* (ratios), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in row C, *Gebruiken* (using); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The brackets say "met mooie getallen" (with nice numbers); the statement does not define them, and in Dutch arithmetic teaching the words usually mean numbers that are easy to calculate with. "Simple" is not defined either. Another statement of this row under the same kind is about problems in which the relation cannot be made directly. This statement sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child adds the same number to both amounts: from 4 people and 200 g to 8 people and 204 g. A child changes one amount and leaves the other as it was. A child puts numbers that do not belong together in one column of the table.
