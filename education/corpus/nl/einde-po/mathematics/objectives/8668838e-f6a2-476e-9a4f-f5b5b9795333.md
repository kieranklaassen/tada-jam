## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 shares 3 bars of chocolate equally among 4 children, finds that each child gets 3/4 of a bar, and writes 3 ÷ 4 = 3/4. In the other direction the child reads 12/4 as 12 divided by 4 and says 3. With a calculator the child keys 3 ÷ 4 and connects the 0.75 on the display with 3/4.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 10 B, which says that the pupil reasons and calculates with fractions as a number, a ratio and a division; 10 B sits under core goal 10. It names one link only; the links with decimal numbers, ratios and percentages are in item 10 B b. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child thinks a smaller number cannot be divided by a larger one, or turns 3 ÷ 4 into 4 ÷ 3. A child writes the answer to 3 ÷ 4 as 4/3. A child answers "0 remainder 3" and does not see that the remainder can be divided further.
