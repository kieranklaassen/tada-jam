## Official wording

selecteren van relevante kenmerken en weglaten van niet relevante kenmerken;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 15 B c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

selecting relevant features and leaving out features that are not relevant;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads: "Sam is 11, cycles 3 kilometres to school in 12 minutes and leaves at 8:10. At what time does Sam arrive?" The child underlines 12 minutes and 8:10, crosses out the age and the distance, and answers 8:22. To work out how much paint a wall needs, the child keeps the height and width of the wall and of the window in it, and leaves out the colour and the furniture.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 15 B, which says that the pupil makes and uses mathematical models; 15 B sits under core goal 15. It names two actions that belong together: selecting what is relevant and leaving out what is not. It does not say what kinds of features are meant or how a pupil decides which are relevant. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child uses every number in the text because it is there (11 + 3 + 12). A child leaves out something that matters, such as the window in the wall or the way back in a journey. A child keeps details of the story in the drawing and leaves out the quantities.
