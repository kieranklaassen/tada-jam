## Official wording

bij procenten mag je niet zomaar optellen en aftrekken (10% erbij 10% eraf)

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 2 Verhoudingen, niveau 1S, C Gebruiken / Weten waarom / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

with percentages you may not simply add and subtract (10% on 10% off)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 tries it with a price of 100 euros: 10% on gives 110 euros, and 10% off that price takes away 11 euros, so 99 euros is left, not 100. The child explains why: the second 10% is taken of a different whole, 110 and not 100. The child checks with 50 euros and finds 55 and then 49.50 euros.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain Verhoudingen (ratios and proportions), under *weten waarom* (understanding why). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. It stands under sub-heading C (using). The brackets name one case, 10% added and then 10% taken off; it illustrates the statement and is not a limit on the percentages. The wording is "not simply" (*niet zomaar*): the statement does not say that percentages may never be added or subtracted, and it does not say when they may. It names no amounts.

### Common mistakes

A child thinks that 10% on followed by 10% off brings back the starting price. A child adds two rises of 10% to 20%, where the total rise is 21%. A child thinks that 50% off followed by another 50% off makes an article free.
