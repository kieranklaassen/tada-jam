## Official wording

formules voor het berekenen van oppervlakte en inhoud verklaren

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 3 Meten en Meetkunde, niveau 1S, C Gebruiken / Weten waarom / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

explaining formulas for calculating area (*oppervlakte*) and *inhoud* (volume or capacity)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 covers a rectangle of 6 cm by 4 cm with centimetre squares, sees 4 rows of 6, and explains that length × width counts these 24 squares. The child fills a box of 4 cm by 3 cm by 2 cm with centimetre cubes: one layer holds 4 × 3 = 12 cubes, and 2 layers hold 24. The child says in words why multiplying gives the same number as counting.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain *Meten en Meetkunde* (measuring and geometry), under *weten waarom* (understanding why). It stands in part C, *Gebruiken* (using). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement asks for explaining formulas for area and for *inhoud*. It names no formula and no figure. *Inhoud* covers both volume and capacity. It stands under understanding why: the explanation is asked for, not the calculation. Using formulas to calculate the area and *inhoud* of simple figures is a separate statement in this part under *functioneel gebruiken*.

### Common mistakes

A child recites a formula and cannot say why it works. A child explains the area of a rectangle by adding the sides. A child uses length × width for every figure, also for one that is not a rectangle.
