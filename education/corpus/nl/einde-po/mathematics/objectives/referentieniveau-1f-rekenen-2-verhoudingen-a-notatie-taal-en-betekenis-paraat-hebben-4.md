geheel is 100%

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

whole is 100%

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says without working it out that the whole class is 100% of the class. Asked what 100% of a bottle of juice is, the child says at once that it is the whole bottle. The child says of a cake cut into two equal pieces that one half is 50% of the cake and the whole cake is 100%.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Verhoudingen* (ratios), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in row A, *Notatie, taal en betekenis* (notation, language and meaning); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement is three words and has no verb for what the pupil does. It does not mention percentages above 100%. It names no number other than 100%.

### Common mistakes

A child takes 100% for a hundred things. A child takes 100% for a great deal of something and not for all of it.
