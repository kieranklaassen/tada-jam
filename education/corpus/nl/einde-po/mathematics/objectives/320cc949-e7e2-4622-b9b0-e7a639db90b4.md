## Official wording

afstemmen hoe rekenaanpakken en andere wiskundige aanpakken bij verschillende leergebieden worden uitgevoerd;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 18 B c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

aligning how approaches to calculating (*rekenaanpakken*) and other mathematical approaches are carried out in different learning areas;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 finds 15% of 240 in a geography lesson with the same ratio table as in the mathematics lesson: 10% is 24, 5% is 12, together 36. In a craft lesson the child works out a scale in the way learned in the mathematics lesson and sets the work out in the same way. The child recognises the method and does not learn a second one for the same calculation.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 18 B, which says that the school supports the use of mathematics in different learning areas; 18 B sits under core goal 18. Like 18 B, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is aligning (*afstemmen*, making things agree with each other). The item does not say who aligns with whom (teachers, teaching materials and lessons are not named), and it names no approach and no learning area. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. When the same calculation is done in different ways in different lessons, children most often take the ways for different kinds of sum, or mix steps of two methods.
