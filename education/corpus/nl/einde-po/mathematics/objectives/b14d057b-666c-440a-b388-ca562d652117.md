## Official wording

redeneren met kijklijnen;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 14 A b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning with sight lines (*kijklijnen*, straight lines from a viewer's eye to what the viewer sees);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 holds a taut string from the eye past the edge of a cupboard to find out what is hidden behind it. On a drawing seen from above the child draws straight lines from a viewer past the corners of a building and shades the part the viewer cannot see. The child says where a photo was taken from, and why a small tree close by hides a tall tower far away.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 14 A, which says that the pupil reasons about geometric figures and determining position and carries out geometric transformations; 14 A sits under core goal 14. The item gives only these words. It names no situations; shadows and rays of light are not mentioned. The item states no sizes or number range.

### Common mistakes

A child thinks the taller thing can always be seen over the lower thing, whatever the distances are. In a drawing a child describes what is seen from the child's own place and not from the place of the viewer in the drawing. A child stops a sight line at the obstacle and does not draw it on past the edge, or draws it curved.
