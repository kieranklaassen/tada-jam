## Official wording

weergeven van patronen en verbanden in een beschrijving, tabel en grafiek, en deze weergaven in elkaar omzetten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 13 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

representing patterns and relationships (*verbanden*, the way one number or quantity goes with another) in a description, a table and a graph, and converting these representations into one another.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads "a taxi costs 3 euros to start and 2 euros for each kilometre", makes a table for 0 to 5 kilometres (3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 euros) and marks the points on squared paper. In the other direction the child takes a graph, fills in a table from it and says in words what the relationship is.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 13 A, which says that the pupil reasons about patterns and relationships; 13 A sits under core goal 13. It names three forms: a description, a table and a graph. A formula is not named. The words "into one another" ask for changing in every direction between the three. The item does not say which kinds of relationship are meant. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child plots the values with the two axes swapped. A child describes a graph as a picture ("the line goes up a hill") and not as a relationship between two quantities. In a table a child continues down one column (+2 each time) and loses the link between the columns, so the price for 20 kilometres cannot be found without writing every row.
