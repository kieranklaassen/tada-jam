## Official wording

oplossen van verhoudingsproblemen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 10 C e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

solving ratio problems (*verhoudingsproblemen*; the Dutch *verhouding* covers both ratio and proportion).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 works out the flour for 10 pancakes when the recipe for 4 pancakes uses 200 grams, going by way of 2 pancakes (100 grams) in a ratio table, and says 500 grams. The child works out that a trip of 150 kilometres at 100 kilometres per hour takes one and a half hours, and that a coat of 60 euros with 25% off costs 45 euros.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 10 C, which says that the pupil reasons and calculates with ratios; 10 C sits under core goal 10. It names no kinds of problem (item 10 C a lists the kinds of ratio covered) and no method. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child adds the same number to both amounts where both have to be multiplied: 4 pancakes with 200 grams becomes 10 pancakes with 206 grams. In a ratio table a child writes a value in the wrong row, so pancakes and grams are mixed. With a discount a child gives the amount taken off (15 euros) as the new price.
