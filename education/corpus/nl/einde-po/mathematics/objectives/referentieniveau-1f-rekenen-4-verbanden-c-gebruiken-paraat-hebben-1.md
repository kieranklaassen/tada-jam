Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 4 Verbanden, niveau 1F, C Gebruiken / Paraat hebben / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

making a simple bar chart on the basis of data

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 has the counts from a class survey on pets (8 dogs, 6 cats, 3 rabbits, 2 fish) and draws a bar chart of them on squared paper. The child draws two axes, writes the animals along one and the numbers 0 to 8 at equal steps along the other, draws a bar of the right height for each animal, and gives the chart a title. The child does this without an example to copy.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Verbanden* (relationships), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in part C, *Gebruiken* (using). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names one kind of graph, the bar chart, and names no other. The chart is made from data; the statement does not say where the data come from. It does not say how many bars, what step on the number axis or what number range makes a bar chart simple.

### Common mistakes

A child puts the numbers on the axis at unequal steps, or writes the data values themselves along the axis in the order in which they come. A child starts the bars at 1 instead of at 0, or draws bars of unequal width so that a wide bar looks like more. A child leaves out the labels of the axes, so the chart cannot be read.
