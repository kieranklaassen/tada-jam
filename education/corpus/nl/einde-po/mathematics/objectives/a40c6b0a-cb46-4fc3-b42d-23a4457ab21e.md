## Official wording

De leerlingen leren de rekenmachine met inzicht te gebruiken.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO (version in force until 31 July 2026), bijlage, kerndoel 31.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupils learn to use the calculator with insight.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 first says about how much 3 × €4.95 is (3 × 5 = 15), keys the sum in and reads 14.85 as 14 euros and 85 cents. After 10 ÷ 4 the child reads 2.5 and says what it means in the situation: two and a half metres, or €2.50. When the display is far from the estimate, the child keys the sum in again.

### Limits

This 2006 goal was replaced on 1 August 2026; schools may still use it until 1 August 2031. It is the last of six goals under the heading *Getallen en bewerkingen* (numbers and operations). The goal does not explain "met inzicht" (with insight). It does not say for which sums the calculator is used, nor which keys or functions are meant. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child accepts what the display shows without comparing it with an estimate. A child reads 2.5 hours on the display as 2 hours and 5 minutes or 2 hours and 50 minutes, where it is 2 hours and 30 minutes. A child keys in a sum with several steps in the order of the words in the text, where the order of the operations matters.
