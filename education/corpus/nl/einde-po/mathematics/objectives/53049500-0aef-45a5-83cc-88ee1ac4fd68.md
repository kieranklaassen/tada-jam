## Official wording

interpreteren van grafische representaties en beredeneren of daarbij gepresenteerde conclusies wel, niet of deels kloppen;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 12 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

interpreting graphical representations and reasoning whether the conclusions presented with them are correct, incorrect or partly correct;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a bar chart from a newspaper or an advertisement under the heading "Sales doubled". The child checks the axis, sees that it starts at 90 and not at 0, and reads that sales went from 100 to 110. The child says the heading is not correct, or correct only in part, and points to the place in the chart that shows it.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 12 A, which says that the pupil interprets and represents data; 12 A sits under core goal 12. It names three outcomes for a conclusion: correct, incorrect, or partly correct. The kinds of graphical representation are listed in item 12 A e. The item does not say where the representations come from. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child accepts a conclusion because it is printed with the chart. A child judges by the height of the bars without reading the numbers on the axis. A child answers only "right" or "wrong" and does not consider that a conclusion can be right in part.
