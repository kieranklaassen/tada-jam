## Official wording

berekenen en interpreteren van een gemiddelde;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 12 A b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

calculating and interpreting an average (the mean);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 adds five long-jump distances and divides by five. With five towers of cubes the child moves cubes from the tall towers to the short ones until all are equally high, and sees the average. The child says what the number means: "3 goals per match on average" does not mean 3 goals in every match.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 12 A, which says that the pupil interprets and represents data; 12 A sits under core goal 12. Only the average (*gemiddelde*, the mean) is named; the median and the mode are not mentioned. The item does not say how many values there are. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child adds the values and forgets to divide, or divides by the wrong number, for example by leaving out a value of 0. A child thinks the average has to be one of the values, or the most common one, or half-way between the lowest and the highest. A child rejects an average that is not a whole number: "2.4 children is not possible".
