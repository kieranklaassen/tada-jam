## Official wording

De leerling redeneert en rekent met verhoudingen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 10 C.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil reasons and calculates with ratios (*verhoudingen*, a word that covers both ratio and proportion).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 mixes syrup and water 1 to 5 and works out how much water goes with 3 cups of syrup. The child fills in a ratio table: 2 kilograms cost 3 euros, so 6 kilograms cost 9 euros. The child tastes or compares two mixtures and says whether they are equally strong and why.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 10, which is about numbers and ratios. Its items 10 C a to 10 C e name the kinds of ratio (among them percentages, scale and compound quantities), the links with percentages and fractions, and recognising, comparing and solving. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child adds where the situation asks for multiplying: from 2 to 3 the child goes on to 4 to 5 instead of 4 to 6. A child mixes up part to part with part of the whole: 1 part syrup to 5 parts water is read as 1/5 syrup, where the syrup is 1/6 of the drink.
