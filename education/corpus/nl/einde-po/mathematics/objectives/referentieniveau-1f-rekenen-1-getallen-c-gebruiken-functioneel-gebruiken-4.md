## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 keys in 8 × €1.95, reads 1560 and says "that cannot be right: 8 × 2 is 16", then finds that the decimal sign was not keyed in. The child checks 432 ÷ 12 = 36 by working out 36 × 12 = 432. The child asks whether a result fits the situation: a long jump of 35 metres at a sports day cannot be right.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in row C, *Gebruiken* (using); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names no way of judging (an estimate, the opposite operation, the situation). It does not say whose result is judged. It sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child trusts the result because it comes from a calculator or from a written procedure. A child checks by doing the same procedure again and repeats the same error. A child does not compare the result with the situation it belongs to.
