## Official wording

De leerlingen leren eenvoudige meetkundige problemen op te lossen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO (version in force until 31 July 2026), bijlage, kerndoel 32.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupils learn to solve simple geometric problems.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 builds a building of cubes from drawings of its front and its side, and folds a cut-out net into a box. On a map the child finds the place from which a photo was taken. The child shows that two shapes have the same area by cutting one up and laying the pieces on the other.

### Limits

This 2006 goal was replaced on 1 August 2026; schools may still use it until 1 August 2031. It is the first of two goals under the heading *Meten en meetkunde* (measuring and geometry); goal 33 is about measuring and units. The goal does not define "simple", and names no shapes, tools or kinds of problem. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

In a drawing of a building of cubes a child counts only the cubes that can be seen. A child mixes up left and right when taking the view of someone who stands on the other side. A child thinks a shape has become another shape after turning: a square that stands on a corner is no longer called a square.
