## Official wording

De leerling redeneert en rekent met breuken als getal, verhouding en deling.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 10 B.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil reasons and calculates with fractions as a number, as a ratio (*verhouding*, a word that covers both ratio and proportion) and as a division.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 marks 3/4 on a number line between 0 and 1 (the fraction as a number), says that 3 of every 4 children in the class cycle to school (as a ratio), and shares 3 pancakes equally among 4 children so that each gets 3/4 of a pancake (as a division). The child says which of the three meanings a situation uses.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 10, which is about numbers and ratios. Its items 10 B a to 10 B e name the kinds of fraction, the links with decimal numbers, ratios, percentages and division, the ordering, simplifying and comparing, and the calculating. Item 10 B e places calculating with fractions in concrete situations, supported by a model or by number relations; rules for bare fraction sums are not mentioned. This goal states no range of denominators.

### Common mistakes

A child sees a fraction only as a part of a pizza or a bar and cannot place it on a number line. A child reads the numerator and the denominator as two separate whole numbers, so 1/8 is called larger than 1/6 because 8 is more than 6.
