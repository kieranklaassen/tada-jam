## Official wording

globaal (benaderend) rekenen (schatten) als de context zich daartoe leent of als controle voor rekenen met de rekenmachine:
Is tien euro genoeg? € 2,95 + € 3,98 + € 4,10
1589 – 203 is ongeveer 1600 – 200

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

calculating roughly (approximately) (estimating, *schatten*) when the context lends itself to it or as a check on calculating with the calculator:
Is ten euros enough? €2.95 + €3.98 + €4.10
1589 – 203 is about 1600 – 200

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 stands in a shop with ten euros and three things priced €2.95, €3.98 and €4.10. The child adds 3 + 4 + 4 = 11 and says that ten euros is not enough. After keying 1589 − 203 into a calculator and reading 1386, the child compares it with 1600 − 200 = 1400 and accepts the result.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in row C, *Gebruiken* (using); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names two occasions for estimating: when the context lends itself to it, and as a check on the calculator. Dutch *schatten* has two readings, judging an amount by eye and calculating approximately; the statement's own brackets tie it here to approximate calculating. The decree prints two examples, a question about money and a subtraction; the record holds each on its own line. They are examples of the level, not a limit. The statement does not say how close an estimate has to be, and sets no number range. The decree prints the Dutch decimal comma (€ 2,95); the gloss writes the English decimal point.

### Common mistakes

A child cuts off the cents and adds 2 + 3 + 4 = 9, and so says that ten euros is enough. A child works out the exact sum where an estimate answers the question. A child accepts what the calculator shows and makes no estimate to compare it with.
