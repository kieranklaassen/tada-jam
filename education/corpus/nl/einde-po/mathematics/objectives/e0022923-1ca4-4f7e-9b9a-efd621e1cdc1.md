## Official wording

De leerling past wiskunde toe in bekende en nieuwe situaties.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 18.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil applies mathematics in familiar and new situations.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school works out the change from 20 euros in a shop, a familiar situation, and then uses the same knowledge in a situation not met before: comparing "3 for 5 euros" with "1.80 euros each" for 6 bottles (10 euros against 10.80 euros). In a geography lesson the child uses the scale of a map to find how far apart two towns are.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 18 itself. Two goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 18 A (the pupil recognises and uses mathematics in everyday situations and in situations in society) and 18 B (the school supports the use of mathematics in different learning areas). 18 B is addressed to the school. Core goal 18 is one of the two core goals of the domain *wiskunde en de wereld* (mathematics and the world); the other is core goal 17. The sentence names two kinds of situation, familiar and new; it names no particular situation or context and no unit, and the shop, the offer on bottles and the map above are the pack's examples. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child can do a sum in the mathematics lesson and does not recognise the same sum in a shop or in another subject. In a new situation a child waits to be told which operation to use.
