## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 matches at once the forms that say the same thing: "1 in 4", "a quarter of", ¼ and 25%. The child reads a symbol aloud in words ("is to" for the colon in 1 : 100, "per cent" for %) and writes spoken words in symbols.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain Verhoudingen (ratios and proportions), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. It stands under sub-heading A (notation, language and meaning). The brackets name symbols and words. The statement does not say which notations are meant (for ratios, fractions or percentages), prints no example and sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child treats "1 in 4" and 1 : 4 as the same, where 1 to 4 means 1 of 5 parts. A child reads "per cent" without linking it to "out of 100". A child writes a spoken ratio in the wrong order: "3 to 2" becomes 2 : 3.
