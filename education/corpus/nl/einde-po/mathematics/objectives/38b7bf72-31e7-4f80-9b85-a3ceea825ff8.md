## Official wording

De leerling redeneert en rekent met getallen en verhoudingen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil reasons and calculates with numbers and ratios (*verhoudingen*, a word that covers both ratio and proportion).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school works out a price, a share or an amount in a recipe and says aloud why the method is right. With a recipe for four people the child works out the amounts for six. The child compares two amounts, says which is more and how many times more, and checks the answer against an estimate made beforehand.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 10 itself. Three goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 10 A (whole and decimal numbers), 10 B (fractions) and 10 C (ratios). The sentence names reasoning as well as calculating. This goal states no number range. The reference levels 1F and 1S, which are other records of this lane (domains *Getallen*, numbers, and *Verhoudingen*, ratios), are worded in more detail.

### Common mistakes

Many children treat whole numbers, decimal numbers, fractions and percentages as separate subjects and do not see that 0.5, 1/2 and 50% are the same amount. In a ratio situation many children add the same number to both sides where they have to multiply both sides: they go from 2 to 3 on to 4 to 5 instead of 4 to 6.
