translating from a simple situation to a calculation

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads that three tickets of €7.50 are needed, says "that is 3 times 7.50" and writes 3 × 7.50. For 3 bags of 12 marbles shared by 4 children the child writes 3 × 12 = 36 and then 36 ÷ 4 = 9. The child says which operation fits the situation before calculating.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in row B, *Met elkaar in verband brengen* (relating to one another); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement does not define "simple" and does not say which operations or numbers are meant. Carrying out calculations is named in the statements of row C. This statement sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child chooses the operation from one word in the text ("more" means adding) where the situation asks for subtracting. A child uses every number in the text, also one that is not needed. A child writes a division the wrong way round, 4 ÷ 36 for 36 shared by 4.
