## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 sees "25% off" on a jumper of 48 euros, takes a quarter off (12 euros) and finds 36 euros. When 3 of the 5 children in a group cycle to school, the child says that this is 3/5 of the group, or 60%. The child chooses the form that makes the situation easier to work with.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain Verhoudingen (ratios and proportions), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. It stands under sub-heading B (relating to one another). The statement names both directions: fractions into percentages and percentages into fractions. It names no fractions and no percentages and sets no number range. Common conversions between percentages and fractions are a separate statement of this level (sub-heading B, *paraat hebben*).

### Common mistakes

A child uses the denominator as the percentage: ⅕ becomes 5% and ¼ becomes 4%. A child reads 20% as 1/20. A child guesses at a fraction with a less familiar denominator and writes ⅓ = 30%.
