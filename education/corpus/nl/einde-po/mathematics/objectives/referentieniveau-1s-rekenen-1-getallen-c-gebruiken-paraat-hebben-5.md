## Official wording

efficiënt rekenen ook met grotere getallen

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 1 Getallen, niveau 1S, C Gebruiken / Paraat hebben / 5.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

calculating efficiently also with larger numbers

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at the numbers before starting and chooses a short way. The child works out 1998 + 3567 as 2000 + 3567 − 2 = 5565, 25 × 48 as 100 × 12 = 1200, 4 × 37 × 25 as 37 × 100 = 3700, and 6000 − 2995 as 6000 − 3000 + 5 = 3005.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain Getallen (numbers), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. It stands under sub-heading C (using). "Larger numbers" is a comparison without a stated bound; the statement sets no number range. The word "also" (*ook*) shows that this is in addition to something the statement does not name. The statement names no strategies and does not say whether the calculating is mental or written; the strategies in the examples above are the pack's.

### Common mistakes

A child compensates in the wrong direction and works out 6000 − 2995 as 6000 − 3000 − 5 = 2995. A child uses the long written method for every calculation without looking at the numbers first. A child changes the order of the numbers where that is not allowed, in a subtraction or a division.
