## Official wording

eenvoudige relaties herkennen, bijvoorbeeld dat 50% nemen hetzelfde is als «de helft nemen» of hetzelfde als «delen door 2»

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 2 Verhoudingen, niveau 1F, B Met elkaar in verband brengen / Paraat hebben / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising simple relations, for example that taking 50% is the same as "taking half" or the same as "dividing by 2" (*delen*, dividing, also means sharing)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 sees "50% off" on a coat of 80 euros and says "half price, 40 euros", without working out a percentage. The child says that 50% of 80, half of 80 and 80 ÷ 2 are the same sum.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Verhoudingen* (ratios), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in row B, *Met elkaar in verband brengen* (relating to one another); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. "Bijvoorbeeld" (for example) introduces the one relation that is printed; it is an example of the level, not a limit. Other relations of this kind are not printed in this statement. It sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child calculates 50% of 80 as 80 − 50 or as 80 ÷ 50. A child makes a rule of the example and divides by 25 for 25% and by 20 for 20%. A child knows that 50% is a half but does not use it and starts a long calculation.
