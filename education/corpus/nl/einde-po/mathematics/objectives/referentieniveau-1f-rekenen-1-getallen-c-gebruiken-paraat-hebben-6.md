optellen en aftrekken (waaronder ook verschil bepalen) met gehele getallen en eenvoudige decimale getallen:

1268 – 385

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 1 Getallen, niveau 1F, C Gebruiken / Paraat hebben / 6.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

adding and subtracting (which also includes finding the difference) with whole numbers and simple decimal numbers:
235 + 349
1268 – 385
€2.50 + €1.25

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 works out 235 + 349 = 584 and 1268 − 385 = 883 and has a way ready for both. Asked how much more 1268 is than 385, the child sees a subtraction. The child adds prices of €2.50 and €1.25 to €3.75.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in row C, *Gebruiken* (using); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement does not say whether the work is done in the head or on paper; other statements of this row under the same kind say "in the head", and this one does not. The brackets "(which also includes finding the difference)" follow "adding and subtracting"; the wording does not say which of the two operations they belong to. The three examples are examples of the level, not a limit: numbers of three and four digits, and amounts of money with two decimal places. The record holds each on its own line. The statement sets no number range and does not define "simple" decimal numbers. The decree prints the Dutch decimal comma (€ 2,50); the gloss writes the English decimal point.

### Common mistakes

In a written subtraction a child takes the smaller digit from the larger in every column, so 1268 − 385 becomes 1123. A child forgets the ten that is carried, so 235 + 349 becomes 574. A child does not see "how much more" or "the difference between" as a subtraction and adds the two numbers.
