## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

determining, interpreting and judging the result.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads the scales and determines that a school bag weighs 3.4 kilograms, says what that means ("heavier than my own bag of 2.9 kilograms, by half a kilogram"), and judges whether it can be right by comparing it with the estimate of about 3 kilograms made beforehand. When a calculator shows 31.25 for 250 ÷ 8, the child says that this is 31 euros and 25 cents for each person, and checks with 8 × 30 = 240.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 16 B, which says that the pupil uses measuring instruments and other mathematical instruments; 16 B sits under core goal 16. It names three actions on the result of using an instrument: determining it, interpreting it and judging it. It does not say against what the result is judged; estimating beforehand is item 16 B b. This item states no precision and no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child reads a result and stops, without saying what it means in the situation. A child keeps a result that cannot be right, such as a pencil of 143 centimetres, because the instrument showed it. A child changes the measurement so that it matches the estimate.
