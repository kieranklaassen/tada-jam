## Official wording

beoordelen van het resultaat van een doorlopen algoritme;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 15 C c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

judging the result of an algorithm that has been run through (*doorlopen*, gone through step by step);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 has followed the steps of a written multiplication for 48 × 25, got 1200, and checks: 50 × 25 is 1250, so 1200 fits. A classmate got 336, and the child says that cannot be right, because 48 × 25 is more than 40 × 25 = 1000. After following written steps for putting five number cards in order, the child checks that every card is larger than the one before it.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 15 C, which says that the pupil devises and describes algorithms; 15 C sits under core goal 15. It is about the result after the steps have been carried out. It does not say against what the result is judged (an estimate, the purpose of the algorithm and a second run are not named), and it does not say who or what ran through the algorithm. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child trusts a result because the steps were followed, and does not compare it with an estimate. A child checks by doing the same steps again in the same way and repeats the same slip. A child sees that a result is wrong and cannot find the step where it went wrong.
