## Official wording

De leerling interpreteert en representeert data.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 12 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil interprets and represents data.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 takes a list of how the classmates travel to school, puts the counts in a table, and draws a bar chart on squared paper with a label on each axis. The child says what the chart shows. The child also reads a chart that someone else made and answers a question from it.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 12, which is about interpreting data; it is the only goal sentence of that goal. Its items 12 A a to 12 A e name tables, the average, making and reading graphical representations, and the kinds of representation. Core goal 12 and its items name interpreting and representing; collecting data is not mentioned in them. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child draws an axis with unequal steps (1, 2, 5, 10 at equal distances). A child leaves out the labels, so a reader cannot tell what the bars stand for. A child misreads a scale on which one square stands for 2 or 5.
