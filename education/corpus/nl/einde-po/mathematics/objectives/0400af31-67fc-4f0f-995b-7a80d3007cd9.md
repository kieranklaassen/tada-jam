## Official wording

rekenwijzen: rekenen met eigenschappen van getallen en bewerkingen, en met standaardprocedures.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 10 A e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

ways of calculating (*rekenwijzen*): calculating with properties of numbers and of operations, and with standard procedures.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 works out 4 × 99 as 4 × 100 minus 4, or 25 × 16 as 100 × 4, using what the numbers and the operation allow. The same child sets out a column addition or a long multiplication on paper, step by step in a fixed layout.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 10 A, which says that the pupil reasons and calculates with whole and decimal numbers; 10 A sits under core goal 10. It explains the term *rekenwijze* used in item 10 A c and names two ways. It does not say which properties are meant (for example that the order of two factors may be changed) or which standard procedures. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child adjusts in the wrong direction when using a round number: for 52 − 19 the child takes away 20 and then takes away 1 more instead of adding 1 back. A child uses a property where it does not hold and changes the order of the numbers in a subtraction or a division. In a standard procedure a child follows the steps without the place values and writes 7 × 30 as 21.
