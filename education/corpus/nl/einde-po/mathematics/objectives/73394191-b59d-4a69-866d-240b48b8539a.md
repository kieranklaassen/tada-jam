## Official wording

construeren en interpreteren van tweedimensionale representaties van driedimensionale figuren en relaties leggen tussen twee- en driedimensionale representaties van figuren;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 14 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

constructing and interpreting two-dimensional representations of three-dimensional figures, and making connections between two-dimensional and three-dimensional representations of figures;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 builds a block building from drawings of its front, side and top, and draws those three views of a building that stands on the table. The child folds a net into a cube and says beforehand which faces will meet. The child matches photos of a building with the places they were taken from.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 14 A, which says that the pupil reasons about geometric figures and determining position and carries out geometric transformations; 14 A sits under core goal 14. It asks for making and reading flat representations of solid figures, and for linking the flat and the solid. It names no particular kind of representation and no particular figure; the views, the net and the photos above are the pack's examples. The item states no sizes or number range.

### Common mistakes

A child counts only the cubes that can be seen in a drawing of a block building and forgets the hidden ones. A child swaps the view from the left with the view from the right. A child accepts any six joined squares as the net of a cube and cannot say which faces end up opposite each other.
