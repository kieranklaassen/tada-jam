## Official wording

De leerling herkent en gebruikt wiskunde in alledaagse en maatschappelijke situaties.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 18 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil recognises and uses mathematics in everyday situations and in situations in society (*maatschappelijke situaties*).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a bus timetable and works out that the bus of 8:47, with a journey of 25 minutes, arrives at 9:12. The child reads "25% off" on a jacket of 60 euros and works out 45 euros. In a news report about a town that builds 400 homes for 1000 people, the child works out that this is 2.5 people for each home.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 18, which is about applying mathematics in familiar and new situations. Its items 18 A a to 18 A e name using numbers and other concepts in concrete situations relevant to the pupil, using instruments when measuring and in other practical actions, using mathematics for decisions and problems, recognising that graphical representations carry a message, and using and judging mathematical information from society and the media. The sentence names two actions, recognising and using, and two kinds of situation; it names no situation, no unit and no context such as money or time. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child calculates with time as if an hour had 100 minutes, so 8:47 plus 25 minutes becomes 8:72. A child takes a percentage off as an amount, so 25% off 60 euros becomes 35 euros. A child does not see that a situation holds a mathematical question until someone states it as a sum.
