## Official wording

beredeneerd ordenen, vereenvoudigen en vergelijken van breuken;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 10 B d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

ordering, simplifying and comparing fractions, with reasons;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 lays fraction strips side by side to see that 2/3 is more than 3/5, and gives a reason without the strips: "3/4 is one quarter less than a whole and 5/6 is only one sixth less, so 5/6 is larger". The child puts cards with 3/8, 1/2 and 2/3 in order, and shows that 6/8 is the same as 3/4 by taking the eighths two at a time.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 10 B, which says that the pupil reasons and calculates with fractions as a number, a ratio and a division; 10 B sits under core goal 10. The word *beredeneerd* asks for a reason together with the answer. The item does not prescribe a method, such as making the denominators equal. This item states no range of denominators.

### Common mistakes

A child calls the fraction with the larger denominator the larger fraction: 1/8 above 1/6. A child compares numerators and denominators separately and says 3/5 is more than 2/3 because 3 is more than 2 and 5 is more than 3. A child simplifies by taking the same number off the numerator and the denominator (6/8 becomes 4/6), or thinks 3/4 is less than 6/8 because its numbers are smaller.
