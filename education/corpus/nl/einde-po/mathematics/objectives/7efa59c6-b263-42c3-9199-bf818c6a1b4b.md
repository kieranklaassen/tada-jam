## Official wording

De leerlingen leren praktische en formele reken-wiskundige problemen op te lossen en redeneringen helder weer te geven.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO (version in force until 31 July 2026), bijlage, kerndoel 24.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupils learn to solve practical and formal problems in arithmetic and mathematics (*reken-wiskundige problemen*) and to present lines of reasoning clearly.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 works out how many packs of 6 bottles are needed for 28 children: 28 ÷ 6 is 4 remainder 4, so 5 packs. The child also solves a bare sum such as 8 × 25 by seeing it as 4 × 50 = 200. Afterwards the child tells or writes the steps in order, with a sketch, a table or the sums, so that someone else can follow them.

### Limits

This 2006 goal was replaced on 1 August 2026; schools may still use it until 1 August 2031. It is the second of three goals under the heading *Wiskundig inzicht en handelen* (mathematical insight and acting). The goal names two kinds of problem, practical and formal, and defines neither; "formal" is usually read as a problem set in numbers and symbols without an everyday situation. It does not say in what form the reasoning is presented (spoken, written or drawn). This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child writes down only the answer, with no steps. A child takes the numbers out of the text and applies the operation practised most recently, without reading what the situation asks. A child leaves the result of the calculation as the answer ("4 remainder 4") and does not go back to the question (5 packs).
