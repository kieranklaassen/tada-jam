## Official wording

gegevens van meetinstrumenten interpreteren; 23,5 op een kilometerteller betekent ...

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 3 Meten en Meetkunde, niveau 1S, A Notatie, taal en betekenis / Functioneel gebruiken / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

interpreting data from measuring instruments; 23.5 on an odometer (*kilometerteller*, a counter of kilometres travelled) means ...

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at the counter on a bicycle or in a car that shows 23.5 and says that 23 and a half kilometres have been travelled. The child reads other instruments in the same way: looks at the display or the scale, says the number with its unit, and says what the digit after the decimal point stands for.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain *Meten en Meetkunde* (measuring and geometry), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in part A, *Notatie, taal en betekenis* (notation, language and meaning). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement asks for interpreting, in a practical situation, what a measuring instrument shows. It gives one example, a reading of 23.5 on an odometer, and the source ends the example with three dots: what the reading means is left for the pupil to say. The example is an example of the level, not a limit. The statement names no other instrument and prints no unit beside the 23.5; which instruments are meant is not said.

### Common mistakes

A child reads 23.5 as 23 kilometres and 5 metres, and does not see the 5 as five tenths of a kilometre. A child gives the number without a unit, or with a unit that does not belong to the instrument. On an instrument with a scale, a child counts every mark as 1 without checking what one step between two marks stands for.
