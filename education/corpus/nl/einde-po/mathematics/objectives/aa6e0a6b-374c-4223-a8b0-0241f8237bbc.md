## Official wording

invullen van tabellen bij data;

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

filling in tables for data;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 has a list of the pets of 28 classmates, or a sheet of tally marks, and writes the counts in a table with one row for each kind of pet. The child adds the counts and checks that the total matches the number of answers in the list.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 12 A, which says that the pupil interprets and represents data; 12 A sits under core goal 12. It asks for filling in tables; designing the layout of a table is not mentioned. It does not say how large the set of data is or how many rows and columns a table has. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child writes a value in the wrong row or column. A child counts one answer twice or misses one, so the total does not match the list, and does not check. A child counts the total row as one more category.
