## Official wording

De school ondersteunt het gebruik van wiskunde in verschillende leergebieden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 18 B.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The school supports the use of mathematics in different learning areas (*leergebieden*, the subject areas of the curriculum).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 uses mathematics outside the mathematics lesson: the child reads the scale of a map and a climate graph in geography, places years on a timeline in history, and measures the growth of a plant and records it in a table in a science lesson. The child works in the same way as in the mathematics lesson, for example with a ratio table.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 18, which is about applying mathematics in familiar and new situations. It is addressed to the school: it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. Its items 18 B a to 18 B d name offering concepts and ways of thinking and working in connection with one another, showing how learning areas use mathematical language and representations, aligning how approaches are carried out, and having pupils use models, instruments and algorithms. The sentence names no learning area. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

This sentence is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often do not use in another lesson what they can do in the mathematics lesson: a child who reads a line graph there does not recognise the same kind of graph in a geography book.
