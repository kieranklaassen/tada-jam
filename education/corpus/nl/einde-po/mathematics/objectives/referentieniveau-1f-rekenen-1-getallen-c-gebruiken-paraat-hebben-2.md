## Official wording

producten uit de tafels van vermenigvuldiging (tot en met 10) uit het hoofd kennen:
3 × 5
7 × 9

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 1 Getallen, niveau 1F, C Gebruiken / Paraat hebben / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

knowing by heart products from the multiplication tables (up to and including 10):
3 × 5
7 × 9

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says 3 × 5 = 15 and 7 × 9 = 63 at once, in any order and without reciting the table from the start. The child knows 9 × 7 as quickly as 7 × 9.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in row C, *Gebruiken* (using); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The brackets say "up to and including 10"; the statement does not spell out whether this means the tables of 1 to 10, the multipliers 1 to 10, or both. The two products are examples of the level, not a limit. The verb is knowing by heart.

### Common mistakes

A child has to recite the table from 1 × 9 to reach 7 × 9. A child mixes up products that lie close together, such as 7 × 8 = 56 and 6 × 9 = 54. A child knows 3 × 7 but has to work out 7 × 3.
