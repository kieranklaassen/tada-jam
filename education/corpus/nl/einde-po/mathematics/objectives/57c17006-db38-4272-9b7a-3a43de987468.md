## Official wording

herkennen en beschrijven dat met grafische representaties een bepaalde boodschap wordt overgebracht of benadrukt;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 18 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising and describing that a particular message is conveyed or emphasised with graphical representations;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at two bar charts of the same numbers, visitors going from 480 to 520, one with an axis from 0 and one with an axis from 450. The child says that in the second chart the second bar is more than twice as high as the first, although the rise is 40 visitors on 480, and that someone who wants to show strong growth would pick that chart. In an advertisement the child points out the large red bar for the maker's own product and says what the maker wants a reader to think.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 18 A, which says that the pupil recognises and uses mathematics in everyday situations and in situations in society; 18 A sits under core goal 18. It names recognising and describing; making such representations is not named here. It names no kind of graphical representation; item 12 A e, under core goal 12, lists kinds. The message is conveyed or emphasised: the item does not say that the message is false or misleading. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child reads a chart as a plain record of the facts and does not ask who made it and why. A child judges by the height of the bars without reading the numbers on the axis. A child who has learned that a chart can mislead calls every chart misleading.
