## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 in a shop says before calculating that 6 packs of €4.95 cost a little under 30 euros, "because 6 × 5 is 30". For 412 ÷ 8 the child says "about 50, because 8 × 50 is 400". The child says whether the exact result will be above or below the rounded one, and why.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in row B, *Met elkaar in verband brengen* (relating to one another); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names no numbers and no operations. A statement of row C, under *functioneel gebruiken*, is about approximate calculating and prints examples. This statement sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child calculates the exact answer first and calls that the estimate. A child does not notice that a result is ten times too large or too small. A child gives a rough answer but cannot say why it has to be about that much.
