## Official wording

relaties leggen tussen breuken, decimale getallen, verhoudingen en procenten;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 10 B b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

making connections between fractions, decimal numbers, ratios (*verhoudingen*, a word that covers both ratio and proportion) and percentages;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says that "1 out of 4", 1/4, 0.25 and 25% describe the same share, and puts cards with 3/4, 0.75, 75% and "3 of every 4" together as one set. The child changes from one form to another when that makes a sum easier: 25% off 80 euros becomes a quarter of 80.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 10 B, which says that the pupil reasons and calculates with fractions as a number, a ratio and a division; 10 B sits under core goal 10. It names four forms to connect. Item 10 C b, under the goal sentence on ratios, names a similar link between ratios, percentages and fractions, without decimal numbers. The item does not say which fractions or percentages are meant. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child writes 1/4 as 0.4 or as 1.4, and 5% as 0.5. A child takes 1/3 to be exactly 0.3 or 30%. A child treats a percentage as an amount ("25% is 25") without asking what it is a percentage of.
