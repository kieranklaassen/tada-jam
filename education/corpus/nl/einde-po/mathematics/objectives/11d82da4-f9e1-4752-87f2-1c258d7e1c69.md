## Official wording

De leerling redeneert over patronen en verbanden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 13 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil reasons about patterns and relationships (*verbanden*, the way one number or quantity goes with another).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 explains why a rule holds: "each new triangle needs only 2 matches, because it shares one side with the triangle before it". For the row 3, 7, 11, 15 the child says whether 100 will ever be in it and why not: every number in the row is 1 less than a number in the table of 4, and 100 is itself in the table of 4.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 13, which is about insight into patterns and relationships; it is the only goal sentence of that goal. Its items 13 A a to 13 A c name recognising, describing and continuing patterns, patterns in data sets, and changing between a description, a table and a graph. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child gives a rule from the first two numbers and does not check it against the rest of the row. A child describes what is seen ("it gets bigger") without saying by how much or why.
