## Official wording

beschrijven hoe een algoritme tot een vast resultaat leidt;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 15 C b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing how an algorithm leads to a fixed result (*vast resultaat*, which can be read as a definite result or as the same result every time);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 takes the steps "take a number, double it, add 6, halve the result, take away the number you started with" and tries 7, 12 and 100: the result is 3 each time. The child says why: doubling and halving undo each other, so what is left is half of the 6 that was added. For the written procedure for adding in columns, the child says why following the steps gives the sum of the two numbers, whichever numbers are taken.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 15 C, which says that the pupil devises and describes algorithms; 15 C sits under core goal 15. The words *vast resultaat* can be read in two ways, a definite result or the same result at every run, and the law does not say which. The item asks for describing; a proof is not mentioned. Item 15 C a names the kind of algorithm (a limited number of steps). This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child shows one worked example and takes it for the explanation. A child says "because those are the steps" and does not connect a step with what it does to the number. A child tests only a few small whole numbers and concludes that the result is always the same without saying why.
