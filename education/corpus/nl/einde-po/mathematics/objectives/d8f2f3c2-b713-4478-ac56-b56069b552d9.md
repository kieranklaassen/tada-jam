## Official wording

stimuleren van een onderzoekende en kritische houding ten aanzien van getallen en andere wiskundige informatie;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 17 A b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

stimulating an inquiring and critical attitude towards numbers and other mathematical information;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads "9 out of 10 children like this drink" on a package and asks how many children were asked and who asked them. The child reads that a town has "about 50,000 inhabitants" and says that the number is rounded. Told that "a person blinks 20,000 times a day", the child works out whether that can be right: at about 20 blinks a minute for 16 waking hours, 20 × 60 × 16 = 19,200, so it can.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 17 A, which says that the school stimulates the development of a mathematical attitude in pupils; 17 A sits under core goal 17. Like 17 A, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. It names two qualities of the attitude, inquiring and critical, and what the attitude is towards: numbers and other mathematical information. It does not say where the information comes from; item 18 A e, under core goal 18, names society and the media. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often accept a number because it is printed or because it looks exact. Some take "critical" to mean rejecting every number instead of checking it.
