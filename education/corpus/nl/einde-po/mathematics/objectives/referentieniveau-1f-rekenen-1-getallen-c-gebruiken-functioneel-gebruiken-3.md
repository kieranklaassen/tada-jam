## Official wording

verstandige keuze maken tussen zelf uitrekenen of rekenmachine gebruiken (zowel kaal als in eenvoudige dagelijkse contexten zoals geld- en meetsituaties)

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 1 Getallen, niveau 1F, C Gebruiken / Functioneel gebruiken / 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

making a sensible choice between working it out oneself or using the calculator (both bare (*kaal*, as a sum without a context) and in simple everyday contexts such as money and measuring situations)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 sees 25 × 8 and works it out in the head (200). For 37 hours of work at €4.85 per hour the child takes the calculator. For three planks of 2.35 m, 1.8 m and 0.65 m the child adds in the head: 2.35 and 0.65 make 3, and 1.8 more makes 4.8 m. The child says why each choice was made.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in row C, *Gebruiken* (using); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The choice is asked both for bare sums and in simple everyday contexts; "zoals" (such as) introduces money and measuring situations as examples. The statement does not say which sums are for the head and which for the calculator. It sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child takes the calculator for every sum, also for 100 × 10. A child keys in a sum that is quicker in the head and does not notice a keying error, because no estimate was made. A child never takes the calculator and loses much time on a sum with many digits.
