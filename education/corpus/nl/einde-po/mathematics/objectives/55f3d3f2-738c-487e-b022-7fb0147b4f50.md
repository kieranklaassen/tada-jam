## Official wording

De leerling redeneert over meetkundige figuren en plaatsbepalingen en voert meetkundige transformaties uit.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 14 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil reasons about geometric figures and about determining position (*plaatsbepalingen*, saying or finding where something is), and carries out geometric transformations.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 sorts cut-out quadrilaterals by their properties and says why a square is also a rectangle. The child finds a place on a map from its grid square, and tells a classmate where an object is in the room. On squared paper the child draws the mirror image of a shape, and turns a traced shape a quarter turn.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 14, which is about insight in geometric activity; it is the only goal sentence of that goal. Its items 14 A a to 14 A e name what is meant; item 14 A e lists the transformations: rotating, reflecting, enlarging and reducing. The sentence names no figures and no system for giving a position, such as coordinates. This goal states no sizes or number range.

### Common mistakes

A child draws a mirror image by sliding the shape to the other side without turning it over. A child turns a shape around the wrong point. In a grid a child swaps the two parts of a position and reads the row where the column is meant.
