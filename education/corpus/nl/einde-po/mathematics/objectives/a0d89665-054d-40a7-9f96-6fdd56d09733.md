## Official wording

De school stimuleert de ontwikkeling van een wiskundige attitude bij leerlingen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 17 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The school stimulates the development of a mathematical attitude in pupils.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 asks why a method works, tries a way of his or her own, and compares it with a classmate's way. The child sees mathematics being used, for example in a building plan or a train timetable, and questions a number read in a news report.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 17, which is about the pupil developing a mathematical attitude; it is the only goal sentence of that goal. It is addressed to the school: it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. Its items 17 A a to 17 A c name showing the usefulness and the power of mathematics, stimulating an inquiring and critical attitude towards numbers and other mathematical information, and having pupils reflect on ways of calculating and other mathematical activity. The sentence does not say how the school does this. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

This sentence is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. What children most often leave out is asking why: many accept a method or a number as given and do not ask whether it is right.
