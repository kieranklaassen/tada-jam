## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

operations: comparing, ordering, adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 lays number cards with whole and decimal numbers in order from smallest to largest, and says which of two is larger and why. The child adds and subtracts amounts of money, multiplies a price by a number of items, and divides an amount equally among several people.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 10 A, which says that the pupil reasons and calculates with whole and decimal numbers; 10 A sits under core goal 10. The item lists the operations that are meant, and it counts comparing and ordering among them. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

In a written subtraction a child takes the smaller digit from the larger in every column, whichever number it belongs to, so 52 − 37 becomes 25. In a division that does not come out evenly a child drops the remainder or cannot say what it means in the situation. A child orders decimal numbers by how many digits they have.
