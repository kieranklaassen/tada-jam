## Official wording

kwantitatieve informatie uit tabellen en grafieken gebruiken om eenvoudige berekeningen uit te voeren en conclusies te trekken, bijvoorbeeld: in welk jaar is het aantal auto’s verdubbeld t.o.v. het jaar daarvoor?

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 4 Verbanden, niveau 1F, C Gebruiken / Functioneel gebruiken / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using quantitative information from tables and graphs to carry out simple calculations and draw conclusions, for example: in which year did the number of cars double compared with the year before?

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads from a table that a village had 150 cars in 2019, 200 in 2020, 400 in 2021 and 450 in 2022. The child compares each year with the year before, sees that 400 is 2 times 200, and concludes that the number doubled in 2021. With a graph of the temperature over a week the child works out the difference between the warmest and the coldest day.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Verbanden* (relationships), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in part C, *Gebruiken* (using). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names two sources, tables and graphs, and two uses, simple calculations and conclusions. Its one example, after "for example", is the question in which year the number of cars doubled compared with the year before; it is an example of the level, not a limit. The statement names no kind of graph, no kind of calculation and no number range, and it does not define "simple".

### Common mistakes

A child reads "doubled" as "2 more" and looks for a rise of 2. A child compares each year with the first year instead of with the year before. A child reads a value from the wrong row of a table, or reads a point between two grid lines of a graph wrongly, and then calculates correctly with the wrong number.
