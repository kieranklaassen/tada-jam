## Official wording

bedenken en uitvoeren van een aanpak voor een niet-routinematig oplosbaar probleem;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 15 A a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

devising and carrying out an approach for a problem that cannot be solved in a routine way;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 gets a question that no practised method fits: "A farmer has chickens and rabbits, 10 animals with 28 legs in all. How many of each?" The child decides to make a list, starts with 10 chickens and 20 legs, changes one chicken into a rabbit at a time, sees the number of legs go up by 2 each time, and reaches 6 chickens and 4 rabbits. Another child draws 10 circles, gives each 2 legs, and hands out the 8 legs that are left two at a time.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 15 A, which says that the pupil solves mathematical problems and application problems; 15 A sits under core goal 15. It names two actions, devising an approach and carrying it out, and one kind of problem: one that cannot be solved in a routine way. The item does not say what makes a problem non-routine and gives no example. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child waits to be told which operation to use. A child starts calculating with the numbers at once and has no plan to go back to when the first attempt fails. A child tries values at random and does not write down what has been tried.
