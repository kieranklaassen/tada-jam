## Official wording

standaardprocedures met inzicht gebruiken binnen situaties waarin gehele getallen, breuken en decimale getallen voorkomen

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 1 Getallen, niveau 1S, C Gebruiken / Functioneel gebruiken / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using standard procedures with insight within situations in which whole numbers, fractions and decimal numbers occur

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 meets a practical situation, chooses a written method that fits and can say what each step does. For 2.5 kilograms of cheese at 11.80 euros per kilogram the child multiplies on paper, gets 29.50 euros and checks this against the estimate 2.5 × 12 = 30. To cut 4½ metres of ribbon into pieces of ¾ metre the child works out 4½ ÷ ¾ = 6 pieces.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain Getallen (numbers), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. It stands under sub-heading C (using). The statement names three kinds of number that occur in the situations: whole numbers, fractions and decimal numbers. It does not say which procedures count as standard procedures, it does not explain "with insight", and it sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child carries out the steps without thinking about what they mean and does not notice an answer that cannot be right, such as 295 euros for the cheese. A child lines up decimal numbers by their last digit and not by the decimal point. A child carries a rule for whole numbers over to fractions and adds numerators and denominators.
