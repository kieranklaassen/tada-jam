## Official wording

beredeneerd kiezen van een rekenvorm en rekenwijze en reflecteren op de keuze en uitvoering hiervan;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 10 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

choosing, with reasons, a form of calculation (*rekenvorm*) and a way of calculating (*rekenwijze*), and reflecting on this choice and on how it was carried out;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before starting, a child of 11 or 12 looks at the numbers and says how to do the sum: 1998 + 345 mentally, by taking 2000 and going back 2; 3476 × 28 on paper or with a calculator; "about how much is 49 × 21" by estimating. Afterwards the child says whether the choice worked, compares it with a classmate's way, and checks the answer.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 10 A, which says that the pupil reasons and calculates with whole and decimal numbers; 10 A sits under core goal 10. The two terms are explained by neighbouring items: 10 A d lists the forms of calculation (mental, estimating, written, calculator) and 10 A e the ways of calculating (using properties, or a standard procedure). The item does not say which form fits which sum: it asks for the choosing, the reasons, and the reflection afterwards. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child uses the written column procedure for every sum, also where the numbers allow a shorter way, such as 1000 − 998. A child accepts what the calculator shows without comparing it with an estimate. Asked to reflect, a child repeats the steps taken and does not say why this way was chosen or whether another way would have been shorter.
