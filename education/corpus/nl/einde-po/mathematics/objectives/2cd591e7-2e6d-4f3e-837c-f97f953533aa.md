## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht in patronen en verbanden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 13.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight into patterns and relationships (*verbanden*, the way one number or quantity goes with another).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school continues the row 3, 7, 11, 15 and says the rule: "4 more each time". The child lays triangles in a row with matches (3 matches, then 5, then 7) and says how many the tenth figure needs without laying it. The child says that the total price goes up by the same amount for each extra ticket.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 13 itself. One goal sentence, 13 A, and its items 13 A a to 13 A c say what it covers: patterns in rows of numbers and figures, patterns and relationships in data sets, and showing them as a description, a table and a graph. Formulas and letters for numbers are not mentioned in core goal 13. This goal states no number range. The reference levels 1F and 1S, which are other records of this lane (domain *Verbanden*, relationships), are worded in more detail.

### Common mistakes

A child can only go one step at a time and has to make every figure up to the tenth to know the tenth. A child thinks the tenth figure needs twice as many matches as the fifth, where the fifth needs 11 and the tenth 21.
