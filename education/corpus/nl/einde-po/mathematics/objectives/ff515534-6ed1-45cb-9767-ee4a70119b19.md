## Official wording

De leerlingen leren schattend tellen en rekenen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO (version in force until 31 July 2026), bijlage, kerndoel 28.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupils learn to count and calculate by estimating (*schattend*; Dutch *schatten* covers both judging an amount by eye and calculating approximately with rounded numbers).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at a hall with about 12 rows of about 20 chairs and says "about 240 seats". For 398 + 205 the child says "about 600", from 400 + 200. With a note of 20 euros the child checks three prices, €6.95, €8.10 and €3.99, by adding 7 + 8 + 4 = 19, and says the note is enough.

### Limits

This 2006 goal was replaced on 1 August 2026; schools may still use it until 1 August 2031. It is the third of six goals under the heading *Getallen en bewerkingen* (numbers and operations). The goal names estimating in counting and in calculating. It does not say how close an estimate has to be, and gives no rule for rounding. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child calculates the exact answer first and then rounds it, which is not estimating. A child gives a number with no reference amount behind it, for example without first counting one row or one handful. A child rounds every number in the same direction, so the estimate ends far from the exact result.
