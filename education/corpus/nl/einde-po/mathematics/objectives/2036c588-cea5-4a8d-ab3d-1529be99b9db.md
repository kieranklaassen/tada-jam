## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 thinks up steps for a task that has to work every time, such as finding the largest of a row of number cards by looking at one card at a time: "Hold the first card. Compare it with the next card and keep the larger one. Go on until the last card." The child tries the steps on 23, 8, 41, 17 (holds 23, keeps 23, takes 41, keeps 41), then on another row, and changes a step that did not work.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 15 C, which says that the pupil devises and describes algorithms; 15 C sits under core goal 15. Goal sentence 15 C itself already names devising; this item names it once more and adds nothing to it. It does not say for what task the algorithm is or in what form it is written down; item 15 C a names its size (a limited number of steps). This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child writes steps that fit only the one example tried ("take the third card"). A child leaves out what to do in a special case, such as two equal cards. A child does not say when to stop.
