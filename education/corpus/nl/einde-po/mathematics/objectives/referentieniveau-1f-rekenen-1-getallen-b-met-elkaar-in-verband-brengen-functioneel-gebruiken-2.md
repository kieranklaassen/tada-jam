## Official wording

afronden van gehele getallen op ronde getallen

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 1 Getallen, niveau 1F, B Met elkaar in verband brengen / Functioneel gebruiken / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

rounding whole numbers to round numbers

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads that 18,756 people came to a match and says "about 19,000". The child calls a price of 398 euros "about 400 euros", and rounds a distance of 1247 km to 1200 km or to 1250 km, depending on what the number is needed for.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in row B, *Met elkaar in verband brengen* (relating to one another); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement is about whole numbers; rounding decimal numbers is not mentioned here. It does not say what counts as a round number (tens, hundreds or thousands). It sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child cuts off the last digits and so always rounds down: 18,756 becomes 18,000. A child rounds in several steps, 1447 to 1450, then to 1500, then to 2000, where 1447 rounded to thousands is 1000. A child makes an error where a nine has to go over to the next place: 1996 rounded to tens becomes 1990 instead of 2000.
