## Official wording

construeren en interpreteren van plattegronden, routebeschrijvingen en wegwijzers;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 14 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

constructing and interpreting maps and plans (*plattegronden*, drawings of a place seen from above), route descriptions and signposts;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 draws a plan of the classroom, or of the way from home to school, as seen from above. The child writes a route description ("second street on the left, turn right at the bakery") that a classmate can follow, and follows someone else's description on a town map. The child reads a signpost with directions and distances and chooses the way, and makes a signpost for the school grounds.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 14 A, which says that the pupil reasons about geometric figures and determining position and carries out geometric transformations; 14 A sits under core goal 14. It asks for both making and reading, for all three. Scale is not mentioned here; it is named in item 10 C a, under the goal sentence on ratios. Compass directions and coordinates are not mentioned. The item states no distances or number range.

### Common mistakes

A child draws things in a plan from the side, such as the fronts of the houses along a street. A child mixes up left and right when the route on the map runs towards the reader. A child's route description leaves out a turn, or a landmark the writer assumes the reader knows.
