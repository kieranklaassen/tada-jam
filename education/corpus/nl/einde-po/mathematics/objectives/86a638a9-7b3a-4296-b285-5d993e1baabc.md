## Official wording

rekenen met breuken in concrete situaties, ondersteund met een model of met behulp van getalrelaties.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 10 B e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

calculating with fractions in concrete situations, supported by a model or with the help of number relations (*getalrelaties*, known links between numbers).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 works out how much is left of 3/4 litre of juice after 1/2 litre is poured out, with a drawn strip or a measuring jug marked in quarters. To find 2/3 of 24 biscuits the child lays them in three equal groups and takes two groups, or uses the known fact that 24 is 3 × 8. The child doubles a recipe that asks for 3/4 litre of milk and says 1 1/2 litres.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 10 B, which says that the pupil reasons and calculates with fractions as a number, a ratio and a division; 10 B sits under core goal 10. The item states two conditions: the calculating is in concrete situations, and it is supported by a model or by number relations. It does not name a particular model, and it does not say which operations are meant. Bare fraction sums and formal rules are not mentioned. This item states no range of denominators.

### Common mistakes

A child adds the numerators and adds the denominators: 1/2 + 1/4 = 2/6. For 2/3 of 24 a child divides by 3 and stops at 8. A child takes a fraction of the wrong whole: "half of what is left" is taken as half of the amount at the start.
