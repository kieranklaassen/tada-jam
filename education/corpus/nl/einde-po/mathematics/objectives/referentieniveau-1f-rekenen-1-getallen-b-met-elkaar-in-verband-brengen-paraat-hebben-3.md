## Official wording

getallenlijn met gehele getallen en eenvoudige decimale getallen

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 1 Getallen, niveau 1F, B Met elkaar in verband brengen / Paraat hebben / 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

number line with whole numbers and simple decimal numbers

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 puts 750 on a line from 0 to 1000, three quarters of the way along, and puts 2.5 half-way between 2 and 3. Asked where 3.7 belongs, the child says at once that it lies between 3 and 4 and nearer to 4.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in row B, *Met elkaar in verband brengen* (relating to one another); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names whole numbers and "simple" decimal numbers and does not define "simple". The Dutch *gehele getallen* can also be read as "integers"; the statement does not say whether negative numbers are meant. Fractions on the number line are named in a statement of row C. This statement sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child counts the marks and not the steps between them. A child takes every step on a scale for 1, also where a step is 10 or 0.1. A child puts 0.25 to the right of 0.5 because 25 is greater than 5.
