## Official wording

kiezen en bedenken van representaties om berekeningen en wiskundige redeneringen weer te geven en uit te wisselen;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 16 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

choosing and devising representations in order to represent and to exchange calculations and mathematical lines of reasoning;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 wants to show a classmate why 3/4 is more than 2/3 and chooses two strips of the same length, one divided into 4 parts and one into 3. Another child chooses to write both as twelfths, 9/12 and 8/12. For 6 × 48 a child makes up a drawing of a rectangle split into 6 × 40 and 6 × 8, with 240 and 48 written in the parts, and the children say what each representation shows best.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 16 A, which says that the pupil uses mathematical language and mathematical representations; 16 A sits under core goal 16. It names two actions, choosing a representation and devising one, and two purposes, representing and exchanging. It names no kind of representation and does not say with whom the pupil exchanges. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child uses the one representation learned most recently for everything, also where it does not fit, such as a circle for sevenths that cannot be drawn evenly by hand. A child makes a drawing without labels that only its maker can read. A child gives the answer when asked to show the reasoning.
