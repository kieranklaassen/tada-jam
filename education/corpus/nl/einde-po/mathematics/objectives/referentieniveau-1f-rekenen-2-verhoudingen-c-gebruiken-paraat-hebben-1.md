## Official wording

rekenen met eenvoudige percentages (10%, 50%, ...)

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 2 Verhoudingen, niveau 1F, C Gebruiken / Paraat hebben / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

calculating with simple percentages (10%, 50%, ...)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says at once that 10% of 250 is 25 and that 50% of 64 is 32. For 10% off a book of 30 euros the child says 3 euros off, so 27 euros.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Verhoudingen* (ratios), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in row C, *Gebruiken* (using); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The brackets name 10% and 50% and end in dots, so the list is open; the statement does not say which other percentages count as simple. It sets no number range for the amounts.

### Common mistakes

A child takes the percentage for an amount: 10% off 250 becomes 250 − 10 = 240. A child gives the amount of the discount, 3 euros, as the new price. A child divides by the percentage, so 50% of 64 becomes 64 ÷ 50.
