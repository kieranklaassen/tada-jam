## Official wording

wiskunde gebruiken bij het nemen van beslissingen en het oplossen van problemen;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 18 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using mathematics when making decisions and solving problems;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 has to choose between a pack of 6 cartons of juice for 4.20 euros and a pack of 10 for 6.50 euros, works out 70 cents and 65 cents for one carton, and decides, also asking whether 10 cartons are needed. For a trip with 4 people the child compares single bus tickets of 2.50 euros each, 10 euros in all, with a group ticket of 9 euros.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 18 A, which says that the pupil recognises and uses mathematics in everyday situations and in situations in society; 18 A sits under core goal 18. It names two uses: making decisions and solving problems. It does not say what kind of decision or problem is meant; money is not named. Solving mathematical problems and application problems is goal sentence 15 A, under core goal 15. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child decides by the lowest total price alone, without working out the price of one. A child takes the calculated answer for the decision and leaves out what the numbers do not show, such as whether the larger pack will be used up.
