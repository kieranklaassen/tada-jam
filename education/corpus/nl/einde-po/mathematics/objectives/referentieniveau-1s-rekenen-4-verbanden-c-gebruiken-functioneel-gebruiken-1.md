## Official wording

punten in een assenstelsel plaatsen en coördinaten aflezen (alleen positieve getallen)

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 4 Verbanden, niveau 1S, C Gebruiken / Functioneel gebruiken / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

placing points in a coordinate system and reading off coordinates (only positive numbers)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 marks the point (3, 5) on squared paper with two numbered axes: 3 along the horizontal axis and then 5 up. The child reads a marked point by going straight down to the horizontal axis and straight across to the vertical axis, and says its coordinates, (4, 2). The child uses this to find a place on a map with a grid or to plot measured values.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain *Verbanden* (relationships), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in part C, *Gebruiken* (using). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement names two actions, placing points and reading off coordinates, in practical use. The brackets limit it to positive numbers: no negative coordinates. The statement prints no notation for coordinates, and it does not say whether zero or decimal numbers are included.

### Common mistakes

A child swaps the two coordinates and goes up first. A child counts the squares and not the lines, or starts counting at 1 where the axis starts at 0, and so miscounts by one. A child puts the point in the middle of a square and not on the crossing of two lines.
