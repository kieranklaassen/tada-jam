## Official wording

getallenlijn, ook met decimale getallen en breuken

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 1 Getallen, niveau 1S, B Met elkaar in verband brengen / Paraat hebben / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

number line, also with decimal numbers and fractions

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 places 0.5, ¾, 1.25 and 2½ on a number line marked with whole numbers, quickly and without long working. The child reads off the number at a mark halfway between 3.4 and 3.5 as 3.45. The child says between which two whole numbers a number lies and which of them is nearer.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain Getallen (numbers), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. It stands under sub-heading B (relating to one another). The statement is a fragment without a verb: read with its sub-heading and kind, it concerns having the number line ready as a way of relating numbers to one another, also decimal numbers and fractions. The word "also" (*ook*) shows that these are in addition to other numbers, which the statement does not name. It sets no range for the line, no number of decimals and no denominators. Negative numbers are not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

A child counts the marks and not the steps between them, and so misreads the size of a step. A child places 0.25 beyond 0.5 because 25 is larger than 5. A child places ¾ between 3 and 4.
