## Official wording

een hectare is ongeveer 2 voetbalvelden

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 3 Meten en Meetkunde, niveau 1S, A Notatie, taal en betekenis / Functioneel gebruiken / 4.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

a hectare is approximately 2 football fields

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads that a park measures 6 hectares and says that this is about as large as 12 football fields. The child reads that a farm has 20 hectares of land and pictures about 40 football fields. The child says "about", because the comparison is rough.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain *Meten en Meetkunde* (measuring and geometry), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in part A, *Notatie, taal en betekenis* (notation, language and meaning). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement is one reference measure for use in practical situations: the hectare compared with football fields. The word "approximately" (*ongeveer*) belongs to it: the comparison is rough, and the statement gives no size for a football field. It names the hectare and no other unit, and it does not print the size of a hectare in square metres.

### Common mistakes

A child uses the comparison as an exact fact and calculates with it as if a football field were precisely half a hectare. A child reverses the comparison and says that one football field is 2 hectares. A child takes a hectare for a length.
