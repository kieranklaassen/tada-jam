## Official wording

herkennen en beschrijven van patronen en verbanden in datasets;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 13 A b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising and describing patterns and relationships (*verbanden*, the way one number or quantity goes with another) in data sets;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at a table with the height and the shoe size of each classmate, puts the rows in order of height, and says: "taller children mostly have larger shoes, but not always". In a table of the temperature in each month the child points out the rise towards summer and the fall after it.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 13 A, which says that the pupil reasons about patterns and relationships; 13 A sits under core goal 13. It names recognising and describing; continuing, which item 13 A a names for rows of numbers and figures, is not named here. The item does not say what kind of data set or how large. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child takes one case as the rule, or one exception as proof that there is no pattern. A child reads "these go together" as "this causes that". A child reads the rows one at a time and does not look down the columns.
