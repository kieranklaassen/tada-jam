## Official wording

laten zien van het nut en de kracht van wiskunde in uiteenlopende toepassingen;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 17 A a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

showing the usefulness and the power of mathematics in a variety of applications;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 sees and uses mathematics in different places: changing a recipe with 200 grams of flour for 4 people into 500 grams for 10 people, finding from a map with a scale how long a cycling route is, and comparing the cost of two phone subscriptions over a year. The child notices that one way of working, such as a ratio table, answers questions in all three.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 17 A, which says that the school stimulates the development of a mathematical attitude in pupils; 17 A sits under core goal 17. Like 17 A, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. It names two things to be shown, usefulness and power, and asks for a variety of applications; it names no application. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often see mathematics as the sums of the mathematics lesson and do not recognise it in a recipe, a map or a comparison of prices.
