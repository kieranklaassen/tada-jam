## Official wording

eenvoudige verhoudingen in procenten omzetten, bijvoorbeeld 40 op de 400

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 2 Verhoudingen, niveau 1F, B Met elkaar in verband brengen / Functioneel gebruiken / 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

converting simple ratios (*verhoudingen*, a word that covers both ratio and proportion) into percentages, for example 40 in 400

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads that 40 of the 400 pupils of a school come by bus, makes it 10 in 100 and says 10%. For 5 of the 20 children in a group the child makes 25 in 100 and says 25%.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Verhoudingen* (ratios), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in row B, *Met elkaar in verband brengen* (relating to one another); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. "Bijvoorbeeld" (for example) introduces 40 in 400 as an example of the level, not a limit; the statement does not print the percentage that goes with it. It does not define "simple" and sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child takes the first number for the percentage and calls 40 in 400 "40%". A child divides the wrong way round, 20 ÷ 5, and calls 5 in 20 "4%". A child thinks a percentage can only be given when the total is 100.
