## Official wording

De leerling gebruikt wiskundetaal en wiskundige representaties.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 16 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil uses mathematical language (*wiskundetaal*) and mathematical representations.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes 3/4 × 20 = 15, says "three quarters of twenty is fifteen", and shows it on a strip of 20 squares with 15 coloured. The child puts the same counts in a table and in a bar chart and tells a classmate what each of them shows.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 16, which is about using mathematical language and mathematical tools. Its items 16 A a to 16 A e name using symbols, notations and *begrippen*, representing calculations and approaches readably, choosing and devising representations, judging a representation critically, and connecting different representations of one concept. The sentence names no kind of representation. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child uses the equals sign as "the answer comes next" and writes chains such as 3 × 4 = 12 + 5 = 17. A child treats a drawing, a table and a sum of the same situation as three separate tasks.
