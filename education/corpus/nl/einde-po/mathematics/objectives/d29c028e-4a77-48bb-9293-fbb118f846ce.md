## Official wording

gebruiken van getallen en andere wiskundige concepten in concrete, voor de leerling relevante situaties;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 18 A a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using numbers and other mathematical concepts in concrete situations that are relevant to the pupil;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 has saved 12.50 euros, gets 3.50 euros of pocket money a week, and works out that a football of 30 euros can be bought after 5 more weeks (12.50 + 5 × 3.50 = 30). The child divides the class of 28 into teams of 4 for a tournament and gets 7 teams, and works out from a training plan how many minutes of practice there are in a week.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 18 A, which says that the pupil recognises and uses mathematics in everyday situations and in situations in society; 18 A sits under core goal 18. It sets two conditions on the situations: they are concrete, and they are relevant to the pupil. It names numbers and "other mathematical concepts" without listing them, and it names no situation. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child gives an answer that the situation does not allow, such as 7.5 teams. A child forgets what is already there and divides 30 by 3.50 without the 12.50 euros already saved. A child leaves out the unit, so the answer "5" can be euros or weeks.
