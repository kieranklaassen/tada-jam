## Official wording

De leerlingen leren schriftelijk optellen, aftrekken, vermenigvuldigen en delen volgens meer of minder verkorte standaardprocedures.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO (version in force until 31 July 2026), bijlage, kerndoel 30.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupils learn to add, subtract, multiply and divide (*delen*, which also means sharing) in writing (*schriftelijk*, on paper) according to more or less shortened standard procedures.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes 467 + 285 with the digits in columns, carries twice and gets 752. For 34 × 26 the child writes the partial products 6 × 34 = 204 and 20 × 34 = 680 under each other and adds them to 884. For 432 ÷ 12 the child takes away 30 × 12 = 360, sees that the 72 left is 6 × 12, and writes 36.

### Limits

This 2006 goal was replaced on 1 August 2026; schools may still use it until 1 August 2031. It is the fifth of six goals under the heading *Getallen en bewerkingen* (numbers and operations). "Meer of minder verkorte" (more or less shortened) leaves the length of the procedure open: a longer way that writes out every partial result and the short digit-by-digit procedure both fit. The goal names no particular procedure. It states no number range and no number of digits.

### Common mistakes

In a subtraction a child takes the smaller digit from the larger one in every column, so 52 − 38 becomes 26. In a multiplication a child writes the partial product of the tens without its zero, 68 instead of 680. In a division a child leaves out a zero in the result, so 612 ÷ 6 becomes 12 instead of 102.
