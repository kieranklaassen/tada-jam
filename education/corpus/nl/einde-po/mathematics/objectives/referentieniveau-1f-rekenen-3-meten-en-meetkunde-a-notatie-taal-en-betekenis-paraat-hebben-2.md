## Official wording

omtrek, oppervlakte en inhoud

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 3 Meten en Meetkunde, niveau 1F, A Notatie, taal en betekenis / Paraat hebben / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

perimeter (*omtrek*, also the circumference of a circle), area (*oppervlakte*) and *inhoud* (volume or capacity)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says without hesitation which of the three words fits: the length of the fence around a garden is its *omtrek*, the amount of grass inside the fence is its *oppervlakte*, and how much water goes into a paddling pool is its *inhoud*. The child runs a finger around the edge of a table top for the first, lays a flat hand on the top for the second, and points into an open box for the third.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Meten en Meetkunde* (measuring and geometry), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in part A, *Notatie, taal en betekenis* (notation, language and meaning). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names three terms and nothing else: no shapes, no units, no formulas and no calculation. The fragment has no verb; under this heading, knowing the three terms and what they mean is presumably meant. *Inhoud* covers both volume (the space a solid takes up) and capacity (what a container holds). *Omtrek* is the length around any flat figure, a circle included. Calculating perimeter and area of rectangular figures is a separate statement in part C, under *paraat hebben*.

### Common mistakes

A child mixes up *omtrek* and *oppervlakte* and gives the length around a figure when the space inside it is asked. A child thinks that a figure with a larger perimeter always has a larger area. A child links *inhoud* only to liquid in a container and not to the space inside a box.
