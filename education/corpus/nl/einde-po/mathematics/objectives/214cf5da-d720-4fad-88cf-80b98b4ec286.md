## Official wording

meetkundige transformaties: draaien, spiegelen, vergroten en verkleinen van figuren.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 14 A e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

geometric transformations: rotating, reflecting, enlarging and reducing figures.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 traces a shape on tracing paper, holds one point down with a pencil and turns the paper a quarter turn. The child stands a small mirror on a line and draws the mirror image on squared paper. The child copies a drawing on squared paper with every side twice as long, and again with every side half as long.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 14 A, which says that the pupil reasons about geometric figures and determining position and carries out geometric transformations; 14 A sits under core goal 14. It explains the term "geometric transformations" used in 14 A and names four. Sliding a figure (translation) is not in the list. The item states no angles of rotation, no factors for enlarging or reducing, and no number range.

### Common mistakes

For a mirror image a child slides the shape to the other side without turning it over, or draws it at the wrong distance from the mirror line, mostly when that line is slanted. When enlarging, a child adds the same amount to every side instead of multiplying, or doubles the width only, so the shape changes. A child thinks that doubling the sides doubles the area.
