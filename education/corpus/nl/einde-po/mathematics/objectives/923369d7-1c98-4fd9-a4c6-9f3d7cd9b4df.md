## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht bij meetkundig handelen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 14.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight in geometric activity (*meetkundig handelen*, doing things with shapes, positions and space).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school builds a block building from a drawing, folds a cut-out net into a box, and finds a route on a map of the neighbourhood. The child says why: which side of the building the drawing shows, which edges of the net meet, where a photo was taken from.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 14 itself. One goal sentence, 14 A, and its items 14 A a to 14 A e say what it covers: properties of figures, sight lines, maps, route descriptions and signposts, flat representations of solid figures, and transformations. Working out perimeter, area and volume is in item 11 A c, under another core goal. Core goal 14 names no particular shapes and states no sizes. The reference levels 1F and 1S, which are other records of this lane (domain *Meten en Meetkunde*, measurement and geometry), are worded in more detail.

### Common mistakes

A child mixes up left and right when the viewpoint is turned, for example on a map held with the south at the top. A child judges a shape by the position it usually has: a square standing on a corner is said not to be a square.
