## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising a simple pattern from a description in words

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 hears or reads "every week I put 2 euros in my money box" and says what the pattern is: the amount goes up by 2 each time. The child writes or lays out the first steps (2, 4, 6, 8) and explains how the words of the description show the rule.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Verbanden* (relationships), under *weten waarom* (understanding why). It stands in part A, *Notatie, taal en betekenis* (notation, language and meaning). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names a description in words as the starting point and a simple pattern as what is recognised. It names no kind of pattern, no number range, and no table, graph or formula. "Simple" is not defined.

### Common mistakes

A child picks the numbers out of the description but not the rule that links them, and cannot say what comes next. A child takes "2 more each time" as "2 times as much each time". A child leaves the starting amount out or counts it twice, so every later value is one step too early or too late.
