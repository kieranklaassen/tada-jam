## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

number sequence (*getallenrij*, the numbers in counting order)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 counts on and back from any number, also across a thousand: 2998, 2999, 3000, 3001. The child says at once which number comes just before 10,000, and counts on in steps of ten: 970, 980, 990, 1000, 1010.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in row B, *Met elkaar in verband brengen* (relating to one another); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement is one word, with no verb and no example. It does not say how far the sequence goes, in which steps, or whether decimal numbers are part of it. It sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

Counting back across a thousand, a child says 2900 after 3000, instead of 2999. Counting on in tens past 990, a child says 1000 and then 1100. A child says 2000 for the number after 1099.
