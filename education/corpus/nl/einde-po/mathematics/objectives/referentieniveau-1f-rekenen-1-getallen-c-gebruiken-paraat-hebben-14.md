## Official wording

in een betekenisvolle situatie een breuk vermenigvuldigen met een geheel getal

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 1 Getallen, niveau 1F, C Gebruiken / Paraat hebben / 14.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

in a meaningful situation, multiplying a fraction by a whole number

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 pours 6 glasses of ¼ litre each and says that this is 6 quarters, so 1½ litres. For 3 children who each eat ½ pizza the child says 3 halves, so 1½ pizzas.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in row C, *Gebruiken* (using); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The multiplying is tied to a meaningful situation. The statement prints no example and names no fractions. Multiplying a fraction by a fraction is not mentioned. It sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child multiplies the numerator and the denominator: 6 × ¼ becomes 6/24. A child leaves the result as 6/4 and does not see that it is 1½ litres. A child adds the whole number to the fraction and says 6¼.
