## Official wording

gebruiken en beoordelen van wiskundige informatie uit de samenleving en de media bij het vormen van een mening.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 18 A e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using and judging mathematical information from society and the media when forming an opinion.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads that "half of the children do not eat breakfast", looks up who was asked, and finds that it was 40 children at one school. The child says what that number can and cannot support, compares it with a second source, and then gives an opinion about a plan for breakfast at school and says which numbers support it.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 18 A, which says that the pupil recognises and uses mathematics in everyday situations and in situations in society; 18 A sits under core goal 18. It names two actions, using and judging, two sources, society and the media, and one purpose, forming an opinion. It names no particular medium and no kind of information such as percentages or graphs. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child takes a number as true because it is in the news, or picks only the numbers that fit the opinion already held. A child takes a result from a small group as true for everyone. A child mixes up a number and a share: 20 children in a school of 40 and 20 children in a school of 400 are read as the same.
