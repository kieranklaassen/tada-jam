## Official wording

breuken met noemer 2, 4, 10 omzetten in bijbehorende percentages

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 2 Verhoudingen, niveau 1F, B Met elkaar in verband brengen / Functioneel gebruiken / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

converting fractions with denominator 2, 4, 10 into the corresponding percentages

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads "a quarter off" on a shop sign and says "25% off". The child says ½ is 50%, ¾ is 75%, 1/10 is 10% and 3/10 is 30%, and reads a result such as "3 of the 10 children" as 30%.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Verhoudingen* (ratios), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). It stands in row B, *Met elkaar in verband brengen* (relating to one another); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The denominators 2, 4 and 10 are the stated range; other denominators (5, 100) are not named. The direction named is from fraction to percentage. The statement prints no example.

### Common mistakes

A child makes a percentage out of the digits: ¼ becomes 4% or 14%, and ¾ becomes 34%. A child takes 1/10 for 1%. A child knows that ¼ is 25% but cannot get from there to ¾.
