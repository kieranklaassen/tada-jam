algoritmes met een beperkt aantal stappen;

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

algorithms with a limited number of steps;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 follows or writes a short procedure, such as: "1. Write a number down. 2. If the number is even, halve it; if it is odd, add 1. 3. Write the new number down. 4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 until the number is 1." The child carries the steps out one at a time with 12 and writes 12, 6, 3, 4, 2, 1, and then with 7 and writes 7, 8, 4, 2, 1.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 15 C, which says that the pupil devises and describes algorithms; 15 C sits under core goal 15. The item is a noun phrase without a verb: it names the kind of algorithm that goal sentence 15 C is about. The stated limit is "a limited number of steps"; no number of steps is given, and the item does not say whether a repeated step is counted once or each time. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child puts several actions into one step. A child does not know which step comes next when a step sends the reader back to an earlier one. A child skips the check in a step with a condition ("if it is odd") and does what was done the time before.
