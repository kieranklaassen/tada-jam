## Official wording

afronden binnen gegeven situatie: 77,4 dozen berekend dus 78 dozen kopen

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 2, 1 Getallen, niveau 1S, B Met elkaar in verband brengen / Functioneel gebruiken / 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

rounding within a given situation: 77.4 boxes calculated, so buy 78 boxes

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 works out how many boxes, buses or tins are needed and then decides from the situation which way to round. For 250 eggs in boxes of 12 the calculation gives 20.83..., so the child says 21 boxes are needed. With 50 euros and tickets of 8 euros the calculation gives 6.25, and the child says 6 tickets can be bought, not 7.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1S for arithmetic, domain Getallen (numbers), under *functioneel gebruiken* (using in practical situations). 1S is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. It stands under sub-heading B (relating to one another). The example (77.4 boxes calculated, so buy 78 boxes) is an example of the level, not a limit; it rounds up where the usual rounding rule would give 77. The general wording "within a given situation" also covers rounding down because the situation asks for it, though no example of that is printed. The statement sets no number range. The source prints the number with a decimal comma (77,4); the gloss uses English notation.

### Common mistakes

A child applies the rounding rule without looking at the situation and buys 77 boxes. A child gives the unrounded result, 77.4 boxes, as the answer. A child always rounds up, also where the remainder cannot be used: from 250 eggs the child says 21 boxes of 12 can be filled, where only 20 are full.
