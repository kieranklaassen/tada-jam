## Official wording

reflecteren op aanpak, uitvoering en oplossing.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 15 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reflecting on the approach, the carrying out and the solution.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After solving a problem, a child of 11 or 12 considers three things. The child says whether the way chosen was a good one or whether a classmate's way was shorter, checks the steps for slips, and asks whether the answer can be right: 22 tables for 130 people fits, 220 tables does not. The child says what to do differently the next time.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 15 A, which says that the pupil solves mathematical problems and application problems; 15 A sits under core goal 15. It names three things to reflect on: the approach, the carrying out and the solution. It does not say in what form the pupil reflects (aloud, in writing or with others) or when. This item states no number range.

### Common mistakes

Asked to reflect, a child repeats the steps taken and gives no judgement of them. A child checks only the arithmetic and not whether the approach fitted the problem. A child reflects only when the answer was marked wrong.
