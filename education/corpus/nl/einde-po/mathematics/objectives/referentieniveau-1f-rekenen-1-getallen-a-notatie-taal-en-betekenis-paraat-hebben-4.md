## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes three quarters with the 3 above a horizontal bar and the 4 below it, and reads such a fraction aloud. The child sees that 3/4, written with a slash on a package, is the same fraction.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for arithmetic, domain *Getallen* (numbers), under *paraat hebben* (knowing by heart, having ready). It stands in row A, *Notatie, taal en betekenis* (notation, language and meaning); 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The decree prints an example at this place as an image: the fraction three quarters, with the 3 above a horizontal bar and the 4 below it. The wording holds the pack's transcription of the image in square brackets after the word "afbeelding", and the gloss writes "image" there. The transcription writes the fraction on one line with a slash (3/4), which is not the notation the statement names. The fraction is an example of the level, not a limit. The statement is about the notation and asks for no calculation. It names no denominators and sets no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child writes the two numbers the wrong way round, with the number of parts taken below the bar. A child reads ¾ as "three four" or as 3.4. A child sees the fraction as two separate whole numbers and not as one number.
