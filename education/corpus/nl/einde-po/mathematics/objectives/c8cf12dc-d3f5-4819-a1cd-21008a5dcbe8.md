## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht bij het handelen met grootheden en eenheden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel B, kerndoel 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight when working with quantities (*grootheden*, things that can be measured) and units.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school estimates first ("the door is about 2 metres high"), then takes a tape measure for the door and kitchen scales for flour, measures, and says the result with its unit. The child says that 1.5 litres and 1500 millilitres are the same amount, and that a length cannot be given in kilograms.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 11 itself. One goal sentence, 11 A, and its items 11 A a to 11 A e say what it covers: the quantities, measuring instruments, the perimeter, area and volume of rectangular figures, estimating with reference measures, and the links between quantities and units. The sentence names no units; the quantities are listed in item 11 A a. This goal states no number range. The reference levels 1F and 1S, which are other records of this lane (domain *Meten en Meetkunde*, measurement and geometry), are worded in more detail.

### Common mistakes

A child gives a number without a unit, or with a unit that does not fit the quantity, such as an area in centimetres. When changing units a child goes in the wrong direction and multiplies where the number has to become smaller.
