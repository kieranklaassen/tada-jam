## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht in en experimenteert met voorwerpen en technische systemen uit de leefomgeving.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 12.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight into and experiments with objects and technical systems from the living environment (*leefomgeving*, the surroundings one lives in).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at a bicycle and tries out what each part does: the child turns the pedals one full turn and counts how many times the rear wheel turns. The child takes a ballpoint pen with a push button apart, names the parts, and says what happens when the spring is left out. The child builds a small model with a construction set to try out an idea of its own.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 30, which is about insight into, and experimenting with, natural phenomena and technical systems. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Its items 30 A a to 30 A e name the kinds of technical system, how an object works and is built and from which materials, how parts contribute to the working of the whole, principles of transmission and of joining with constructions and profiles, and designing, making and repairing. "From the living environment" bounds the objects and systems; the sentence names none. It states no safety condition.

### Common mistakes

A child can use an object and cannot say how it works. A child names the parts and not what each one does. Many children think of technical systems as electronic devices only and leave out things such as a bicycle, a tap or a zip.
