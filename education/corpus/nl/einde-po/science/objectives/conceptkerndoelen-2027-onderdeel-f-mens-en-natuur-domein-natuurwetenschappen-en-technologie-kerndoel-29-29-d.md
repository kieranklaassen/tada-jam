## Official wording

De leerling verkent de aard van natuurwetenschappen en technologie.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil explores the nature of the natural sciences (*natuurwetenschappen*) and technology.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 compares its own result with that of another group, finds a difference, and looks with the others for the reason before measuring again. The child hears how an idea changed: people long thought that the sun moved around the earth, and observations led to the view that the earth moves around the sun. The child asks someone who works in science or technology what they do and what nobody knows yet.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 29, which is about exploring the world from a scientific and technological perspective. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Its items 29 D a to 29 D e name the activities and motives of scientists and technologists, how they work honestly, responsibly and carefully, weighing whether everything that can be done may be done, the difference between an opinion and a fact, and why scientists and technologists do not know everything. The verb is exploring (*verkennen*); the sentence sets no level of mastery.

### Common mistakes

Many children see science as a finished list of true facts. Many children picture a scientist as a man in a white coat who works alone in a laboratory. A child thinks that a result that differs from the book means that the experiment failed, and does not ask why it differs.
