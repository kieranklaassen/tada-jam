## Official wording

beschrijven hoe onderdelen bijdragen aan het functioneren van het voorwerp of systeem;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 12.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing how parts contribute to the functioning of the object or system;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 points to the parts of a bicycle in turn and says what each adds to the whole: the pedals and cranks take the push of the legs, the chain passes the movement on to the rear wheel, the brake slows the wheel by friction, and the frame holds everything in place and carries the rider. The child says what the bicycle can no longer do when one part is missing.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 30 A, which says that the pupil shows insight into and experiments with objects and technical systems from the living environment; 30 A sits under core goal 30. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is describing. "The object or system" points back to the objects and technical systems of goal sentence 30 A; item 30 A a lists the kinds of system. The item names no object, system or part.

### Common mistakes

A child lists the parts and leaves out what each contributes. A child gives every part a task of its own and does not say how one part acts on the next. A child thinks that a part that does not move, such as a frame or a casing, does nothing.
