## Official wording

experimenteren met ontwerpen, maken en repareren.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 12.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experimenting with designing, making and repairing.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 sketches a holder for pencils, makes it from cardboard and glue, tries it out and changes it where it does not work. For a repair the child first finds out what is broken and why: a bicycle chain that has come off, a loose screw in a chair, a torn page. The child then puts the chain back on, tightens the screw or mends the page, and checks that the thing works again.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 30 A, which says that the pupil shows insight into and experiments with objects and technical systems from the living environment; 30 A sits under core goal 30. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The item names three activities. "Experimenting with" points to trying out; the item sets no level of mastery and names no products, materials or tools. It states no safety condition. Item 29 C b names design steps and item 29 C c the use of tools and materials; both are under core goal 29.

### Common mistakes

A child starts a repair without finding out what is broken. A child wants to throw the whole thing away or replace it when one part has failed. When making, a child cuts before measuring, so the parts do not fit.
