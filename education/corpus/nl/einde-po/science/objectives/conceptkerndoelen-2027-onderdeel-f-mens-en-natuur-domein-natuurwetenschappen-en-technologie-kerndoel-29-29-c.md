## Official wording

De leerling gebruikt natuurwetenschappelijke en technologische werkwijzen bij het ontdekken en begrijpen van de wereld.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil uses scientific (*natuurwetenschappelijke*, of the natural sciences) and technological ways of working in discovering and understanding the world.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 asks whether a paper spinner with longer wings takes longer to fall. The child makes two spinners that differ only in the length of the wings, drops each several times from the same height, times each fall with a stopwatch and writes the times in a table. The child draws a conclusion from the table and says how sure it is.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 29, which is about exploring the world from a scientific and technological perspective. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Its items 29 C a to 29 C e name different ways to reach solutions and answers, steps of investigating and designing, the use of instruments, tools and materials, interpreting measurements and observations, and supporting conclusions with subject language. The sentence names no subject matter. Goal sentence 29 B is about ways of thinking.

### Common mistakes

A child changes several things at once, so the result cannot be linked to one of them. A child does not write measurements down and relies on memory. A child stops after one try.
