## Official wording

verbanden leggen tussen krachten en verandering in beweging;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 12.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

making connections between forces (*krachten*; *kracht* is read as force or as strength or power) and change in movement;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 pushes a toy car and says what each force does to the movement: a push sets the car moving, a harder push gives it more speed, a push from the side changes its direction, and a hand held against it stops it. The child lets the car roll until it stops on a smooth floor and on a carpet and sees that it stops sooner on the carpet, where the friction is larger. The child lets a ball roll down a ramp and sees that it goes faster and faster while gravity pulls it down the slope.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 30 C, which says that the pupil shows insight into and experiments with light, sound, energy and forces; 30 C sits under core goal 30. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The item speaks of change in movement; it names no particular kind of change and no particular force; starting, speeding up, turning and stopping, and friction and gravity, are the pack's examples. It states no unit, no instrument, no formula and no number.

### Common mistakes

Many children think that a thing keeps moving only while a force pushes it, and that it stops when the push is used up; it stops because friction acts against the movement. Many children think that no force acts on a thing that lies still; on a book on a table gravity pulls down and the table pushes up equally hard. Many children think that a heavy thing always falls much faster than a light one; two balls of the same size, one heavy and one light, dropped from the same height reach the floor at almost the same moment.
