## Official wording

ervaringen in de leefomgeving opdoen met het waarnemen en waarderen van natuur en technologie;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experiences in the living environment (*leefomgeving*, the surroundings one lives in) with observing and valuing (*waarderen*) nature and technology;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 sits by a ditch or under a tree for ten minutes and draws or writes down what it sees, hears and smells. The child watches a bridge that opens for a boat, or a crane on a building site, and follows which part moves which. Afterwards the child says what it found beautiful, clever, useful or annoying about what it observed.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 29 A, which says that the pupil explores the living environment and investigates issues from it; 29 A sits under core goal 29. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The item asks for experiences; it names no particular place within the living environment, no number of experiences and no knowledge to be gained from them. *Waarderen* can be read as appreciating and as judging the worth of something; the item does not choose.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often look for a short time and name only what they already know, so details that take time to notice are left out. Many children look at plants and animals only and leave out the made things around them.
