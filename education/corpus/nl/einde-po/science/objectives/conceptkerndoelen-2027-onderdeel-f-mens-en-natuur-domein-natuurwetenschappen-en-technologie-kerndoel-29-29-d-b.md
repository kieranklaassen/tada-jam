## Official wording

verkennen hoe wetenschappers en technologen eerlijk, verantwoordelijk en zorgvuldig werken;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring how scientists and technologists work honestly, responsibly and carefully;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes down every measurement, also the one that does not fit the prediction. The child notes what was done in such a way that another group can repeat it, asks a classmate to check a count, and says where a borrowed idea or picture came from. The child talks about why someone who tests a bridge or a medicine may not leave out a bad result.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 29 D, which says that the pupil explores the nature of the natural sciences and technology; 29 D sits under core goal 29. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is exploring (*verkennen*). The item names three words for how the work is done and no rules or procedures.

### Common mistakes

A child rubs out or changes a result that does not fit the prediction, because it sees that result as a wrong answer. A child copies the numbers of a neighbour. A child does not record what was done, so nobody can repeat it.
