redeneren met systemen en modellen;

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning with systems and models;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 draws a bicycle as a system: pedals, chain, rear wheel and brakes, with arrows for which part moves which. The child says what happens to the whole when one part fails: with the chain off, the pedals turn and the rear wheel is no longer driven. With a globe and a lamp as a model of the earth and the sun the child says what in the model matches reality and what does not, such as the sizes and the distance.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 29 B, which says that the pupil uses scientific and technological ways of thinking in discovering and explaining the world; 29 B sits under core goal 29. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The item names no system and no kind of model. Item 30 A a, under core goal 30, lists five kinds of technical system.

### Common mistakes

A child treats a model as the real thing and reads sizes or distances from it that the model does not show truly. A child lists the parts of a system and does not say how they act on each other. A child changes one part in its thinking and overlooks what that does to the rest.
