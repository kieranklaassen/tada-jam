## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht in en verkent systeem aarde.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 13.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight into and explores the system earth (*systeem aarde*, the earth seen as one connected system).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school keeps a weather log for some weeks, turns a globe beside a lamp to show day and night, and studies what lives in and around a ditch. The child says how these are connected: rain fills the ditch, the sun warms the water so that part of it evaporates, and the water plants need the light of the sun.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 32 itself. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Three goal sentences say what it covers, each with five items: 32 A (changes to the earth's surface through natural phenomena and human action), 32 B (the water cycle, weather and climate) and 32 C (ecosystems). The sentence has two verbs: showing insight and exploring. The Dutch prints *systeem aarde* without an article. The sentence names no place and no quantity.

### Common mistakes

Many children treat weather, landscape and living things as separate subjects and do not connect them. Many children think of the earth as unchanging: in their view the land, the coast and the climate have always been as they are now.
