## Official wording

De leerling verkent de wereld vanuit natuurwetenschappelijk en technologisch perspectief.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil explores the world from a scientific (*natuurwetenschappelijk*, of the natural sciences) and technological perspective.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 notices something in the surroundings, such as a puddle that is gone the next day or a bicycle lamp that works without a battery, and asks how that can be. The child says what it expects, checks by looking closely, measuring or trying something out, and says what was found. With a made thing the child asks which problem it solves and how it does that.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 29 itself. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Four goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 29 A (the living environment and issues from it), 29 B (ways of thinking), 29 C (ways of working) and 29 D (the nature of the natural sciences and technology). The sentence names no subject matter of its own. The verb is exploring (*verkennen*); the sentence sets no level of mastery. The lane's frame notes that this sentence is worded differently in the curriculum institute's open data; the record follows the draft decree.

### Common mistakes

A child gives an explanation and does not check it ("that is just how it is"). A child mixes what was seen with what is thought about it. Many children see science as a list of facts to learn and not as a way of finding things out.
