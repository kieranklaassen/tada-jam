## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht in organismen en hun gezondheid.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 13.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight into organisms and their health.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school sows beans, follows the plants for some weeks and says what they need to stay healthy. The child compares a plant, a pet and the child's own body: what each takes in, how each grows, and what shows that it is not well, such as limp leaves on a plant that has had no water.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 31 itself. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Two goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 31 A (organisms), which is in this lane, and 31 B (lifestyle, health and illness), which the pack records under practical life and feelings. The sentence says "organisms" and names no group of organisms; the items of 31 A name bacteria, fungi, plants, animals and humans. The sentence names no quantity and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

Many children use "organism" or "living thing" only for animals, and do not count plants, fungi or bacteria, because these do not move in a way the child can see. Many children think of health as something only people have, and do not see that a plant or an animal can also be healthy or ill.
