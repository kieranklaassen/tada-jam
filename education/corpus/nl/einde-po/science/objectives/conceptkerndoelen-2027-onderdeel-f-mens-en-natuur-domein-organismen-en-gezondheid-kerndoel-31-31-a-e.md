## Official wording

verkennen van rollen van organismen bij voedselproductie.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 13.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring roles of organisms in food production.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 mixes yeast into bread dough and sees the dough rise: yeast is a fungus, and it gives off a gas that makes the holes in bread. The child tastes yoghurt and tells that bacteria have turned the milk thick and sour, watches bees visit apple blossom, from which apples grow after pollination, and finds earthworms that loosen the soil of a vegetable bed. The child traces a slice of bread back to the wheat plant.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 31 A, which says that the pupil shows insight into organisms; 31 A sits under core goal 31. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is exploring (*verkennen*), where the items 31 A a, 31 A c and 31 A d ask for describing. The item names no organism, no role and no food, and no quantity; "roles" is in the plural. It is the last item of 31 A.

### Common mistakes

Many children think all bacteria and fungi are harmful and are surprised that bread, yoghurt and cheese are made with them. Many children know bees for honey only and leave out pollination, which fruit crops such as apple need to bear fruit. A child does not connect a food on the plate with the plant or animal it comes from.
