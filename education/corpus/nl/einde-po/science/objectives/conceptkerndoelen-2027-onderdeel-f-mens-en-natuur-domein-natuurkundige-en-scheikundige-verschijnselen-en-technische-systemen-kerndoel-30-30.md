## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht in en experimenteert met natuurverschijnselen en technische systemen.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 12.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight into and experiments with natural phenomena and technical systems.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says what it expects before trying something, and explains the result afterwards. The child dissolves salt in water and gets the salt back by letting the water evaporate from a saucer. The child takes a ballpoint pen with a push button apart, finds the spring, and says what the spring does when the button is pressed and released.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 30 itself. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Three goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 30 A (objects and technical systems from the living environment), 30 B (substances and the changes they can undergo) and 30 C (light, sound, energy and forces). The sentence has two verbs: showing insight and experimenting. It names no unit, no instrument and no number, and it states no safety condition for the experimenting. The heading of the domain speaks of physical and chemical phenomena; the sentence itself says "natural phenomena".

### Common mistakes

A child carries out an experiment as a list of instructions and does not say what it expects or what the result shows. A child describes what happened and gives no explanation, or gives the name of the phenomenon as its explanation ("it sticks because it is magnetic").
