## Official wording

ervaring opdoen met werkzaamheden van wetenschappers en technologen en hun motieven;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experience with the activities of scientists and technologists and their motives;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 does a piece of work of the kind a scientist or technologist does: counting the birds in the playground at a fixed time each week and recording the counts, or testing a self-made model and writing a short test report. The child asks a researcher, an engineer or a technician why they do their work, and hears motives such as curiosity, wanting to solve a problem or wanting to help people.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 29 D, which says that the pupil explores the nature of the natural sciences and technology; 29 D sits under core goal 29. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The item asks for experience; it names no activities, no occupations and no motives. *Technologen* (technologists) is the word of the text; it does not say which occupations it covers.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often report what a person does and leave out why. Many children think of laboratory work only, and leave out work outdoors, at a computer, or in building and repairing.
