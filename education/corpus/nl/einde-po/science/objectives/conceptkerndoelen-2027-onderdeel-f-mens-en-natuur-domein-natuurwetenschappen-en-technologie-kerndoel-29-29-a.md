## Official wording

De leerling verkent de leefomgeving en onderzoekt vraagstukken uit de leefomgeving.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil explores the living environment (*leefomgeving*, the surroundings one lives in) and investigates (*onderzoekt*, also read as examines) issues (*vraagstukken*, questions or problems to work on) from the living environment.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 walks through the street and the school grounds and notes what lives there and what has been built there. The child picks an issue from that place, such as the part of the playground that stays under water after rain, and finds out more: where the water collects, how long it stays and who is bothered by it. The child reports what was found and what could be done about it.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 29, which is about exploring the world from a scientific and technological perspective. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Its items 29 A a to 29 A e name gaining experience with observing and valuing nature and technology, the fields the issues are about, perspectives on an issue and its possible solutions, putting into words what an issue does to you, and occupations and role models. The sentence does not say how far the living environment reaches; it names no place and no distance.

### Common mistakes

A child chooses a question that is too large to investigate nearby ("how do we stop pollution?") instead of one that can be checked on the spot. A child proposes a solution before finding out what is going on. A child collects what others say and makes no observations of its own.
