## Official wording

De leerling verkent verschillende ecosystemen en verklaart interacties binnen het systeem.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 14.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil explores different ecosystems and explains interactions within the system.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 studies two places, for example a ditch and the edge of a wood: the child notes which plants, animals and fungi live there, and how the light, the damp and the soil differ. The child explains what the living things do to each other and to the place: the heron eats the frog, the reed gives cover to a bird's nest, and fungi break down the fallen leaves, which then feed the soil.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 32, which is about the system earth. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Its items 32 C a to 32 C e name where in a biotope plants, animals and fungi are found; the names of plants and animals and their classification; feeding relationships; how biotic and abiotic parts are connected; and how humans depend on ecosystems and influence biodiversity. The sentence has two verbs: exploring (different ecosystems) and explaining (interactions). It says "within the system" in the singular after "ecosystems" in the plural; the source prints it that way and the gloss follows it. The sentence names no ecosystem, no number of ecosystems and no quantity.

### Common mistakes

A child describes an ecosystem as a list of animals and leaves out the plants, the fungi and the non-living parts such as water, light and soil. A child sees eating and being eaten as the only interaction and leaves out shelter, pollination and the breaking down of dead material.
