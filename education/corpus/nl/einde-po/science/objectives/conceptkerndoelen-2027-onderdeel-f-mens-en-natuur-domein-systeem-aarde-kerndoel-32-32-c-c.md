## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 lays cards in a row and draws arrows from what is eaten to what eats it: oak leaf, caterpillar, great tit, sparrowhawk. With more cards and pieces of string the child joins several chains into a web, then takes one card away and tells which others are affected.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 32 C, which says that the pupil explores different ecosystems and explains interactions within the system; 32 C sits under core goal 32. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is representing (*weergeven*, showing in a form such as a drawing or a diagram). The item names no form: the words food chain, food web and food pyramid are not in it. It names no ecosystem, no organism, no number of links and no quantity.

### Common mistakes

A child draws the arrow from the eater to what it eats; in a food chain the arrow points from what is eaten to the eater. A child starts a chain with an animal and leaves out the plant at the start, and leaves out the fungi and bacteria that break down dead material. A child thinks a change in one link affects only the next link.
