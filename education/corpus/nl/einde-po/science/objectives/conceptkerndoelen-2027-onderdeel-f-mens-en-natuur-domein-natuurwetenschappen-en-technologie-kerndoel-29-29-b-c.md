## Official wording

redeneren met schaal, verhoudingen en hoeveelheden;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning with scale, ratios (*verhoudingen*, a word that covers both ratio and proportion) and quantities;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 measures 5 centimetres between two places on a map with scale 1 : 10 000 and says that this is 500 metres in reality. The child draws an ant ten times enlarged and writes the enlargement next to the drawing. The child measures a bean plant each week with a ruler, writes the height with its unit, and says in which week it grew twice as much as in the week before.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 29 B, which says that the pupil uses scientific and technological ways of thinking in discovering and explaining the world; 29 B sits under core goal 29. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The item names three terms to reason with and no subject matter. It states no unit, no instrument, no scale and no number range.

### Common mistakes

A child gives a number without a unit ("the plant is 12"). In a ratio a child adds the same amount to both sides where both have to be multiplied. A child converts a map distance with the scale and forgets to change the unit, so 5 centimetres on a map of 1 : 10 000 becomes 50 000 metres.
