## Official wording

Redelijk accuraat gebruik van eenvoudige zinsconstructies.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Spelling, interpunctie en grammatica / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Reasonably accurate use of simple sentence constructions.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes simple sentences in which the subject and the verb are both there and agree, such as the pack's example *Wij gingen gisteren naar het bos* (We went to the woods yesterday). Most of the child's sentences are correct, and some are not.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Spelling, interpunctie en grammatica* (spelling, punctuation and grammar). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the second of two statements in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement is a fragment without a verb and describes the writing. Two qualifiers set the level: the constructions are simple (*eenvoudige*), and their use is reasonably accurate (*redelijk accuraat*), so some errors remain. The statement gives no number of errors and does not say which constructions count as simple.

### Common mistakes

A child writes a sentence without a subject, such as *Ging toen naar huis* (Then went home). A child writes a singular verb with a plural subject, as in *De kinderen loopt*; correct is *De kinderen lopen* (The children walk). A child runs several sentences together without a full stop.
