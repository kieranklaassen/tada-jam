## Official wording

De leerling ontwikkelt een eigen leesvoorkeur.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil develops a reading preference of their own.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 stands at a bookcase, reads the back cover and the first page of three books, and chooses one. The child says which kinds of book the child usually picks and which the child puts back, with a reason taken from books already read. After finishing a book of an unfamiliar kind, the child says whether more of that kind will follow.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 8, which is about gaining experience with literature. Its items 8 A a to 8 A d name reading and listening to books and texts by different writers and of different literary genres, giving a value judgement, taking on new reading challenges, and putting a personal preference into words. The sentence does not say what the preference should be. It names no titles, no reading level and no number of books.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is the reason: a pupil names a favourite book or series and cannot say what it has in common with other books the pupil likes. Many pupils take over the preference of friends, or call a whole genre boring after one book.
