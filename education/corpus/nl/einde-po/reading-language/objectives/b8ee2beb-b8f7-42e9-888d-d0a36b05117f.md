## Official wording

De leerling reflecteert op het proces en evalueert het product van een taalactiviteit.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 5 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil reflects on the process and evaluates the product of a language activity.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who has written a report lays the plan, the first version and the final version side by side and says what changed and why. The child reads the final text against a list of points and marks which are met. After an interview the child says which questions led to full answers and which did not.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 5, which is about developing as a *bewuste* (aware, or deliberate) language user; it is the only goal sentence of that goal. Its items 5 A a to 5 A d name feedback, putting the process into words, assessing the product by provided criteria, and formulating learning goals. The sentence has two verbs: reflecting for the process and evaluating for the product. It does not say what a language activity is: no reading, writing, speaking or conversation task is named. The law sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child mixes the two up: asked how the work went, the child says whether the text is good. A child judges the product by its length or its neatness. A child names what went wrong and cannot say what caused it.
