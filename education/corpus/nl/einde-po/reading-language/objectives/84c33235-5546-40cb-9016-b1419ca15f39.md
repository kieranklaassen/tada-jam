## Official wording

accepteren of verwerpen van andermans ideeën met argumenten;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 B d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

accepting or rejecting other people's ideas with arguments;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says "Yes, let us use that idea, because it costs nothing and we already have the boxes", or "I would not do that, because we have only one afternoon." When a partner's argument is the stronger one, the child says so and gives up their own idea.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 4 B, which says that the pupil holds conversations to arrive at the building of knowledge, understanding or an approach; 4 B sits under core goal 4. The item names two outcomes, accepting or rejecting, and one condition: with arguments. It does not say how many arguments are needed or what makes an argument sound, and it sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child accepts an idea because a friend said it, and rejects one because of who said it. A child says "no" or "that is stupid" and gives no argument. A child agrees that the other argument is better and still keeps their own idea.
