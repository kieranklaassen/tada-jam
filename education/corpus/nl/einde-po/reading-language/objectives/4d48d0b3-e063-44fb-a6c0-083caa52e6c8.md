## Official wording

correct formuleren op woord-, zins- en tekstniveau;

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

phrasing correctly at word, sentence and text level;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a sentence of their own aloud, hears that *de meisje* is wrong and changes it to *het meisje* (the girl; the pack's example at word level). The child corrects a sentence in which the verb does not fit the subject, from *De kinderen loopt* to *De kinderen lopen* (the children walk; sentence level). In a text the child checks that *hij* (he) or *zij* (she) points back to someone already named, and joins two sentences with *omdat* (because) or *maar* (but) (text level).

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 6 B, which says that the pupil shows insight into rules and procedures for spelling, phrasing and punctuation; 6 B sits under core goal 6. The item names three levels: word, sentence and text. It does not say what correct phrasing is at each level, and it names no kind of error and no kind of text. Spelling and punctuation are not named in this item. It sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child writes as they speak and joins sentence after sentence with *en toen ... en toen* (and then ... and then) (the pack's example). A child uses *hij* or *zij* for someone the reader has not yet met. A child picks the wrong article, *de* or *het*, or a verb form that does not fit a plural subject.
