## Official wording

De leerling ontwikkelt zicht als bewuste taalgebruiker.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 5.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil develops as a *bewuste* (aware, or deliberate) language user.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school looks back at a letter they wrote or a talk they gave and says what went well and what did not. The child listens to what a classmate noticed, and picks one thing to do differently next time.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 5 itself. One goal sentence, 5 A, and its items 5 A a to 5 A d say what it covers: feedback, putting the process into words, assessing the product, and learning goals. The law prints *zicht* (sight, view) where *zich* is plainly meant (*ontwikkelt zich*, develops); the gloss gives what is plainly meant, and this lane's frame notes that the curriculum institute's own transcription prints *zich*. The Dutch *bewuste* can be read as aware or as deliberate; the sentence does not say which. The sentence names no language activity, and the law sets no level. Core goal 5 is one of four goals of the domain communication (*communicatie*), with core goals 2, 3 and 4.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Pupils most often leave out the looking back: a text or a talk is finished and put away. When they do look back, they judge only the result ("it was good") and leave out how they worked.
