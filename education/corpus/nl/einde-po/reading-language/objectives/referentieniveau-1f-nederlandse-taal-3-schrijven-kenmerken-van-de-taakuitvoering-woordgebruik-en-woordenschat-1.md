## Official wording

Gebruikt voornamelijk frequent voorkomende woorden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Woordgebruik en woordenschat / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Uses mainly frequently occurring words.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes with the words it uses every day, such as *leuk* (nice), *mooi* (beautiful), *gaan* (to go) and *doen* (to do). Now and then the child puts in a less common word it has learned, for example a word from a lesson about the subject it is writing on.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Woordgebruik en woordenschat* (word use and vocabulary). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement describes the words a pupil at this level writes with: mainly (*voornamelijk*) frequent ones. "Mainly" leaves room for other words. The statement gives no word list and no number of words, and it does not forbid less common words.

### Common mistakes

A child repeats one general word, such as *leuk* (nice) or *ding* (thing), many times in a short text. A child uses a less common word with the wrong meaning. A child writes filler words from speech, such as *nou* (well) and *of zo* (or something).
