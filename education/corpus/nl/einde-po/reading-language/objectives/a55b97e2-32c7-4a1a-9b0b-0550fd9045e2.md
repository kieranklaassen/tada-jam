## Official wording

benoemen van kenmerkende visuele elementen in literaire genres: illustraties, vormgeving.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 9 B c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

naming characteristic visual elements in literary genres: illustrations, design (*vormgeving*, layout, lettering and visual form).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 opens a picture book, a comic book and a book of poems and says what can be seen before a word is read: full-page pictures, panels with speech balloons, short lines with white around them. The child says what a picture adds that the words do not say, and points to letters made large or bold for a shout.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 9 B, which says that the pupil shows insight into genre features of literature; 9 B sits under core goal 9. After the colon the item names two, illustrations and design; the source joins them with a comma and no "and", and nothing says whether the list is complete. It asks for naming. It does not say in which genres, or which elements of design are meant.

### Common mistakes

A pupil treats pictures as decoration and skips them, and so misses the part of the story that only the pictures tell. A pupil describes what is in a picture and does not say what it adds to the text. A pupil does not see layout, such as white space or the size of letters, as something a maker chose.
