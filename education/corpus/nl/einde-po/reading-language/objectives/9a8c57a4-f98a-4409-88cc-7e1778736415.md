## Official wording

formuleren van leerdoelen voor proces en product bij toekomstige taalactiviteiten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 5 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

formulating learning goals for process and product for future language activities.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes two lines on a card for next time: "In my next report every paragraph starts with a sentence that says what it is about" (product) and "Next time I make a plan before I start to write" (process). The child takes the card out again when the next task starts.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 5 A, which says that the pupil reflects on the process and evaluates the product of a language activity; 5 A sits under core goal 5. The item names two things a learning goal is for, process and product, and says the goals are for future language activities. It does not say how many goals, for which activities, or who checks them. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child writes a goal that is too wide to act on: "write better", "make fewer mistakes". A child sets goals only for the product (neater, longer) and none for the way of working. A child writes the goal down and does not look at it again at the next task.
