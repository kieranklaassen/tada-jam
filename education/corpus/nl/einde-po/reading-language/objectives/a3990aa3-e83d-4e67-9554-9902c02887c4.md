## Official wording

flexibel toepassen van verschillende aanpakken en begripsverhogende strategieën.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 2 A f.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

flexibly applying different approaches and strategies that increase understanding (*begripsverhogende strategieën*).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at the title, the headings and the pictures before reading and says what the text will probably be about. While reading, the child notices that a passage was not understood, and rereads it, reads on to see whether it becomes clear, or looks up a word. In a timetable the child searches for one line, and a story the child reads from start to finish: the child chooses the way of reading that fits the text and the purpose.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 2 A, which says that the pupil shows understanding of non-fiction and literary texts; 2 A sits under core goal 2. The item gives no example of an approach or of a strategy; the examples above are the pack's. "Different" and "flexibly" say that the pupil has more than one and changes between them; the item does not say how many. The item does not limit the strategies to reading; item 2 A a names listening and watching too. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child reads every text in the same way, from the first word to the last, also when only one fact is needed. A child does not notice that the understanding has stopped, and reads on. A child carries out a strategy as a fixed step, for example underlining something in every paragraph, without using it to understand the text.
