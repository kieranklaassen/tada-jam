## Official wording

Gebruikt basisconventies bij een formele brief: Geachte/ Beste en Hoogachtend/Met vriendelijke groet.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op publiek / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Uses basic conventions in a formal letter: *Geachte*/*Beste* ("Dear", the formal and the less formal opening) and *Hoogachtend*/*Met vriendelijke groet* ("Yours faithfully"/"With kind regards").

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes a letter to the manager of a swimming pool to ask about lessons. In the pack's example the child opens with *Geachte heer De Vries,* on a line of its own (*Geachte* is followed by *heer* or *mevrouw* and the surname) and ends with *Met vriendelijke groet,* followed by its own name.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Afstemming op publiek* (fit to the audience). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the first of two statements in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement is about a formal letter and names four formulas: two openings, *Geachte* and *Beste*, and two closings, *Hoogachtend* and *Met vriendelijke groet*. It gives them as two pairs with a slash and does not say when each is used or which opening goes with which closing. The source prints a space after the first slash (*Geachte/ Beste*). The conventions are basic ones (*basisconventies*), and the statement names no others; where the date, address, salutation and signature stand is a statement under *Leesbaarheid*.

### Common mistakes

A child opens a formal letter with *Hoi* or *Hallo*. A child joins a formal opening to an informal closing, such as *Geachte mevrouw* at the top and *Groetjes* at the bottom. A child writes *Geachte* with a first name only.
