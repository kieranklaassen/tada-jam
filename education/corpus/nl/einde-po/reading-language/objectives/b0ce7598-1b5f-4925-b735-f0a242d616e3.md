## Official wording

ontwikkelen van spellingbewustzijn en spellinggeweten;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 6 B c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

developing spelling awareness (*spellingbewustzijn*) and a spelling conscience (*spellinggeweten*, the will to spell correctly);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While writing, a child of 11 or 12 stops at a word they are not sure of, puts a small mark by it and checks it later. Before handing over a card for a grandparent or a text for the school paper, the child reads it through once more for spelling alone, because they want the reader to get it without mistakes.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 6 B, which says that the pupil shows insight into rules and procedures for spelling, phrasing and punctuation; 6 B sits under core goal 6. The item names two things that are developed. *Spellingbewustzijn* (spelling awareness) is knowing while writing where a mistake is likely; *spellinggeweten* (spelling conscience) is wanting to spell correctly and making the effort to do so. These two explanations are the pack's; the law does not define the words. The item names no spelling rule or category and no kind of text, and it sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Pupils most often leave out the check: they spell with care in a dictation and without care in a text of their own, in a message to a friend or in work for another school subject. Many reread for the content only, or doubt no word at all.
