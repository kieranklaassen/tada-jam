## Official wording

verkennen van veranderingen in taalgebruik onder invloed van tijd, media en maatschappelijke ontwikkelingen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 7 B e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring changes in language use under the influence of time, media and developments in society.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a page from a children's book of a hundred years ago and marks the words and spellings that nobody uses now. The child asks a parent and a grandparent what they said for "very good" at the age of eleven, and compares the answers with their own word. The child lists words that came with new things, such as *appen* (to send a message with a chat app) and *vloggen* (to make a video diary) (the pack's examples).

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 7 B, which says that the pupil explores language variation and language change in the Dutch language area; 7 B sits under core goal 7. The item names three influences: time, media and developments in society. It gives no example of a change, names no period and no medium, and lists no facts to be known. The verb is exploring, and the item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child thinks that a change in the language means the language is getting worse. A child thinks only young people change the language and that the language of adults stays the same. A child thinks all new words come from English.
