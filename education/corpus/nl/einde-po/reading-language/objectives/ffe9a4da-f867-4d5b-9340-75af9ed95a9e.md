## Official wording

in eigen woorden verwerken van informatie uit verschillende bronnen tot een gestructureerde tekst met bronvermelding;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 3 A b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

working information from different sources, in one's own words, into a structured text with the sources named (*bronvermelding*);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who prepares a piece about wolves reads a book, an article and a web page, and notes the important points in short words of the child's own. The child sorts the notes into groups, writes a text with an opening, paragraphs and an ending, and lists at the bottom which book, article and page were used.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 3 A, which says that the pupil speaks and writes in a way adjusted to purpose, audience and context; 3 A sits under core goal 3. It sets four conditions: more than one source ("different sources"), the pupil's own words, a structured text, and the sources named. It does not say how many sources, in what form the sources are named, or how long the text is. The goal sentence covers speaking and writing; the item says "text" and does not say whether this text is written or spoken. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child copies whole sentences from a source and changes one or two words. A child puts the information in the order of the sources, one source after another, and not by subject. A child leaves the sources out or writes only "internet".
