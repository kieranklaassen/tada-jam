## Official wording

verwoorden van eigen ideeën, gedachten, ervaringen, gevoelens en fantasieën;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 3 B b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting into words one's own ideas, thoughts, experiences, feelings and fantasies;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 tells or writes about something that happened to the child, says how it felt and looks for the word that fits the feeling. The child writes down an idea for an invention, or describes a made-up world with its own animals and rules. The child chooses what to tell and in what form.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 3 B, which says that the pupil uses language in a creative way; 3 B sits under core goal 3. Five things are named, all of them the pupil's own: ideas, thoughts, experiences, feelings and fantasies. The item does not say whether they are put into words in speech or in writing, and names no form and no length. It sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child names a feeling with one general word and stops there (the pack's examples in Dutch: *leuk*, nice, and *stom*, stupid). A child lists events in order ("and then, and then") and leaves out what the child thought or felt. A child writes what the child expects an adult to want, and not the child's own idea.
