## Official wording

Kan in teksten met een eenvoudige lineaire structuur trouw blijven aan het doel van het schrijfproduct.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op doel / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can, in texts with a simple linear structure, keep to the purpose of the written product.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who writes at this level decides before writing what the text is for, for example to ask the head teacher for a longer break, and makes every paragraph serve that purpose. On rereading, the child crosses out a part that has moved to another subject, such as a story about last week's break in a letter that is a request.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, writing (*Schrijven*), row *Afstemming op doel* (matching the purpose). The row stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (characteristics of task performance). 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement is bounded to "texts with a simple linear structure"; it says nothing about texts with another structure. It names no purposes (such as informing, asking or persuading), no kinds of text and no length.

### Common mistakes

A child starts a request or an argument and ends in a retelling of events. A child never states what the text is for, so the reader has to guess what is asked. A child adds everything the child knows about the topic, whether or not it serves the purpose.
