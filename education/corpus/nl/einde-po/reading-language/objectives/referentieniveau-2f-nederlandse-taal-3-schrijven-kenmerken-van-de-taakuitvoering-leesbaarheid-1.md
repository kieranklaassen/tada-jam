## Official wording

Gebruikt titel en tekstkopjes. Heeft bij langere teksten (meer dan twee A4) ondersteuning nodig bij aanbrengen van de lay-out.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Leesbaarheid / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Uses a title and text headings. Needs support with applying the layout in longer texts (more than two A4).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who writes at this level puts a title above the text and a short heading above each part, so a reader sees what each part is about. For a project paper of four or five pages the child still needs help with the layout: where the table of contents goes, how the pages are numbered and how a picture is placed with its caption.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, writing (*Schrijven*), row *Leesbaarheid* (readability). The row stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (characteristics of task performance). 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The first sentence names two aids to readability: a title and text headings. The second states a limit of the level: for longer texts, which the brackets define as more than two A4 (the paper size), the pupil needs support with the layout. The statement does not say who gives the support or what it consists of. It does not state that texts of up to two A4 are laid out without support. It does not say whether the text is written by hand or typed.

### Common mistakes

A child writes a long text as one block, without a title or headings. A child writes a heading that does not match what stands under it. A child spends the effort on fonts and colours and gives the text no headings.
