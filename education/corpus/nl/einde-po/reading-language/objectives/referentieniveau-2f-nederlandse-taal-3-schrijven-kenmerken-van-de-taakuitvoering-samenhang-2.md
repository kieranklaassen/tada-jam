## Official wording

Kan alinea’s maken en inhoudelijke verbanden expliciet aangeven.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Samenhang / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can make paragraphs and indicate connections of content explicitly.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who writes at this level starts a new paragraph when a new point begins and keeps one point to a paragraph. The child writes the connection out, with words such as *daardoor* (as a result), *bijvoorbeeld* (for example), *daarna* (after that) or *aan de andere kant* (on the other hand), so the reader does not have to guess how two sentences or two paragraphs belong together.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, writing (*Schrijven*), row *Samenhang* (coherence). The row stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (characteristics of task performance); this is the second of the three paragraphs in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. Two things are asked: making paragraphs, and indicating connections of content "explicitly", that is, in words on the page. The statement does not say which connections (such as cause, order in time, contrast or example) and does not name the words used for them. It names no number or length of paragraphs.

### Common mistakes

A child starts a new line after every sentence, or writes the whole text without a new paragraph. A child puts two points in one paragraph or spreads one point over several. A child leaves the connection unsaid, for example writes two sentences one after the other where the second is the cause of the first.
