## Official wording

Kan een titel gebruiken.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Leesbaarheid / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can use a title.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes a title on a line of its own above a text, such as the name of an animal above a text about that animal. After writing, the child reads the text again and checks that the title still fits what it wrote.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Leesbaarheid* (readability). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the first of three statements in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names a title and nothing else. Headings inside the text are not named in this statement. It does not say what makes a title a good one.

### Common mistakes

A child leaves the title out. A child writes the title first and then writes about something else, so the title no longer fits. A child writes the kind of text as the title, such as *Verhaal* ("Story").
