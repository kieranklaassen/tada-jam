## Official wording

Kan relaties leggen tussen tekstuele informatie en eigen kennis en ervaringen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Interpreteren / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can make connections between textual information and their own knowledge and experiences.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 listens to a talk about hedgehogs and hears that they sleep through the winter under leaves. The pupil thinks of the pile of leaves in the garden at home where a hedgehog was seen last autumn, and says so afterwards. On hearing that hedgehogs should not be given milk, the pupil notices that this differs from what a grandparent always did.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), *Interpreteren* (interpreting). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). The row has one other statement at 1F, on interpreting information and opinions that are close to the pupil. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names two sides: information from the text, and the pupil's own knowledge and experiences. It does not say what kind of connection is made; recognising something and noticing a difference are the pack's examples. It does not say that the pupil puts the connection into words, and it names no topics.

### Common mistakes

A pupil reports afterwards what they already knew instead of what the text said. A pupil does not notice that the text says something different from what they believed, and keeps the old idea. A pupil treats the text as separate from everything they know and makes no connection.
