## Official wording

reviseren van de tekst met het oog op doelgerichte communicatie: taalgebruik en taalverzorging.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 3 A e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

revising the text with a view to purposeful communication: language use and language care (*taalverzorging*, the correct form of language, such as spelling and punctuation).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 rereads a finished draft and thinks of the reader, or reads it aloud to hear how it sounds. The child replaces a vague word with a precise one, removes a sentence that says the same thing twice, changes a tone that is too blunt for the reader, and then checks the spelling, the capitals and the full stops. The child writes out the improved version.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 3 A, which says that the pupil speaks and writes in a way adjusted to purpose, audience and context; 3 A sits under core goal 3. The revising has an aim, purposeful communication, and two things are named after the colon: language use and language care. The law does not say here what language care covers; the bracketed explanation in the gloss is the pack's, and the reference-level records of this lane that belong to the decree's section 4, *Begrippenlijst en Taalverzorging* (list of terms and language care), are filed under 4.1 *Begrippenlijst* and 4.4 *Moeilijkheid* (difficulty). The item says "the text"; the goal sentence covers speaking and writing, and the item does not say whether a spoken text is revised too. It names no spelling rules and no number of rounds of revising, and sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child corrects only the spelling and leaves the content and the wording as they are. A child reads what the child meant to write and not what is on the paper, so a missing word is not noticed. A child takes the first version as finished and changes nothing.
