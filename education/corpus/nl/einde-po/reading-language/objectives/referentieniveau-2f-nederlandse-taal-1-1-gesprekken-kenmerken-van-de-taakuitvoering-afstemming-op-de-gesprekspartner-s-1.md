## Official wording

Kan het spreekdoel van anderen herkennen en reacties schatten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op de gesprekspartner(s) / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can recognise the speaking purpose (*spreekdoel*) of others and estimate reactions.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 hears a classmate say "Are you using that ball?" and understands that the classmate wants to borrow it and is not asking for information. Before telling the group that their plan for the class party costs too much, the pupil judges that one classmate will be disappointed and another will agree.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), row *Afstemming op de gesprekspartner(s)* (attuning to the conversation partner or partners). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the first of three statements in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement names two things: recognising what others want to achieve by speaking, and estimating reactions. It does not say whose reactions or reactions to what, and the gloss leaves this as open as the text; the row heading points to the conversation partners. It names no speaking purposes and no kind of conversation, and does not say how the pupil shows the recognition.

### Common mistakes

A pupil takes an utterance literally and misses its purpose, for example answers "yes" to "Can you close the window?" and stays seated. A pupil does not recognise an attempt to persuade and treats it as plain information. A pupil expects others to react as he or she would, and is surprised when a joke offends someone.
