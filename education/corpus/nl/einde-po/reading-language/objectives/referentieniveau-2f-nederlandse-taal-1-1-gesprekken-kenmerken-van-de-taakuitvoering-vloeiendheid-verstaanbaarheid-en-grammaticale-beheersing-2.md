## Official wording

Vertoont een redelijke grammaticale beheersing.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Vloeiendheid, verstaanbaarheid en grammaticale beheersing / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Shows a reasonable command of grammar.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 tells the group what happened at the match on Saturday. Most sentences are built correctly, with the verb in the right place and in the right tense. A verb form or an article is sometimes wrong, and the listeners follow the account without effort.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), row *Vloeiendheid, verstaanbaarheid en grammaticale beheersing* (fluency, intelligibility and command of grammar). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the second of three statements in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. It is a fragment without a subject; the pupil is meant. The whole statement is one quality with one qualifier: a command of grammar that is *redelijk* (reasonable, fair). It does not ask for a full or faultless command, and it does not say which errors or how many still count as reasonable; the errors in the illustration are the pack's examples. It names no grammatical forms and no kind of conversation.

### Common mistakes

A pupil gives a strong verb a regular past form, such as *loopte* for *liep* (walked). A pupil uses the wrong article or the wrong word that refers back to a noun (*de* and *het*, *die* and *dat*). A pupil gets the word order wrong in a long sentence with a subordinate clause.
