## Official wording

Kan informatie en meningen interpreteren voor zover deze dicht bij de leerling staan.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 2.1 Zakelijke teksten, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Interpreteren / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can interpret information and opinions in so far as these are close to the pupil.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a piece in a school paper in which a pupil writes that the lunch break is too short, and tells a classmate what that opinion comes to: according to the writer, there is not enough time to eat and to play. The child reads that the sports hall is closed in the holiday week and works out that its own training will not take place that week.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Zakelijke teksten* (reading factual texts), *Interpreteren* (interpreting). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement has one bound: it covers information and opinions only in so far as they are close to the pupil (*dicht bij de leerling staan*). It does not say what counts as close. It names no kind of text and no length.

### Common mistakes

A child repeats the sentence from the text word for word and cannot say what it means. A child mixes its own opinion with what it read and reports that as what the writer thinks.
