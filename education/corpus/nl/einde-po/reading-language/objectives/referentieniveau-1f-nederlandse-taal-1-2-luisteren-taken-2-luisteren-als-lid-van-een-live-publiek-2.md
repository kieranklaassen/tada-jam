## Official wording

Kan een eenvoudig, voorgelezen of verteld verhaal begrijpen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 1F, Taken / 2. Luisteren als lid van een live publiek / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can understand a simple story that is read aloud or told.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: the teacher reads a chapter of a children's book aloud at the end of the day. A pupil of about 12 follows who the characters are, what happens and in what order, and can say afterwards why the main character ran away. On a school camp a group leader tells a story without a book, and the pupil follows it in the same way.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), task 2, *Luisteren als lid van een live publiek* (listening as a member of a live audience). It stands under *Taken* (tasks). The task has one other statement at 1F, on informative, *instructieve* and *betogende* texts. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The story is simple (*eenvoudig*), and it is read aloud or told. The statement does not say what makes a story simple, names no genres and no length, and does not say how the pupil shows the understanding.

### Common mistakes

A pupil follows the events but not why a character does something, when the reason is not said outright. A pupil no longer knows who is speaking in a dialogue that is read aloud. A pupil mixes up the order of events when the story goes back to something that happened earlier.
