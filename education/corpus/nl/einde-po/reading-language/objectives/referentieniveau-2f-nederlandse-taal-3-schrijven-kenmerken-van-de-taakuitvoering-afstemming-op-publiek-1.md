## Official wording

Past het woordgebruik en toon aan het publiek aan.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op publiek / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Adapts the word use and tone to the audience.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who writes at this level writes *Beste meneer Jansen* (Dear Mr Jansen) and the polite *u* (you) in a note to a neighbour the child hardly knows, and *Hoi* (Hi) and the familiar *je* (you) in a message to a friend. The child chooses plain words in a text for younger children and the subject's own terms in a text for readers who know them.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, writing (*Schrijven*), row *Afstemming op publiek* (matching the audience). The row stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (characteristics of task performance). 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement names two things that are adapted: word use and tone. It does not say which audiences are meant, and it names no kinds of text and no length.

### Common mistakes

A child writes to an adult the child does not know in the same words and tone as to a friend. A child starts formally with *u* and changes to *je* halfway. A child uses words the reader cannot know, such as a nickname or a word only the child's own group uses.
