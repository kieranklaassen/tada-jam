## Official wording

De leerlingen leren naar inhoud en vorm teksten te schrijven met verschillende functies, zoals: informeren, instrueren, overtuigen of plezier verschaffen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO (version in force until 31 July 2026), bijlage, kerndoel 5.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupils learn to write texts, as to content and form (*naar inhoud en vorm*, both what is written and how), with different functions, such as: informing, instructing, persuading or giving pleasure.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes a short piece about an animal for a school newspaper, the rules of a game that someone else must be able to play from the text, a letter that asks for a longer break and gives reasons, and a funny story or a poem. For each the child chooses the content and the form that fit what the text is for.

### Limits

This 2006 goal was replaced on 1 August 2026; schools may still use it until 1 August 2031. It is goal 5 of the core goals for Dutch, under the heading *Schriftelijk taalonderwijs* (written language education), goals 4 to 9. "Such as" (*zoals*) introduces four functions as examples: informing, instructing, persuading or giving pleasure. The goal asks for both content and form. It names no kinds of text, no length and no reader, and it sets no level. Kinds of text, spelling, handwriting and page layout are named in goal 8.

### Common mistakes

A pupil writes every text as a story of what happened, whatever the text is for. A pupil leaves out what a reader who was not there needs to know. In a text meant to persuade, a pupil states a wish and gives no reasons.
