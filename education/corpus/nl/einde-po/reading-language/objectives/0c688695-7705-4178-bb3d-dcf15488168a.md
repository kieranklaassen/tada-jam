## Official wording

De leerling doet ervaring op met literatuur.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil gains experience with literature.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school reads a novel for children over several weeks, listens to a story or a poem that is read aloud, and picks a next book from a shelf that holds many kinds of books. Afterwards the child tells someone what the reading was like, and what the book showed about people or places the child did not know.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 8 itself. Two goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 8 A (developing a reading preference of one's own) and 8 B (exploring the value of literature). The sentence asks for experience; insight into literature is core goal 9. The goal does not define literature, and it names no titles, no writers, no reading level and no number of books. The reference levels 1F and 2F, which are other records of this lane (section 2.2 *Fictionele, narratieve en literaire teksten*, fictional, narrative and literary texts), are worded in more detail.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is breadth: many read one series or one kind of book and nothing else. Many also stop at "I liked it" or "boring" and do not say what in the book caused that.
