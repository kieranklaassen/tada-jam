## Official wording

aangaan van nieuwe leesuitdagingen op basis van eerdere lees-, luister- en kijkervaringen;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking on new reading challenges on the basis of earlier reading, listening and viewing experiences;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who has seen a film made from a book picks up the book itself. A child who enjoyed a thin book by a writer chooses a thicker one by the same writer, or opens a first book of poems after hearing poems read aloud. The child says which earlier book, audiobook or film led to the choice.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 8 A, which says that the pupil develops a reading preference of their own; 8 A sits under core goal 8. The item ties the new challenge to what the pupil has read, listened to and viewed before. It does not say what makes a reading challenge new (a longer book, another genre, a harder text), what is viewed, or who chooses. It names no reading level and no number of books.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is the new challenge itself: a pupil keeps choosing books of the kind and length the pupil already knows. Other pupils choose a book far harder than anything read before, with no link to an earlier experience, and put it away after a few pages.
