## Official wording

reflecteren op hoe je overkomt op anderen op basis van keuzes in het eigen talige repertoire;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 7 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reflecting on how you come across to others on the basis of choices in one's own language repertoire (*talige repertoire*, all the language means a person has);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 listens back to a recording of a talk they gave, or asks a classmate "How did I sound when I said that?", and links the answer to a choice: a word, a tone, a gesture, a change to another variety. The child says "I used the words of my group of friends with the visitor, and she thought I was not serious", and thinks about what to choose next time.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 7 A, which says that the pupil explores how you express identity with language; 7 A sits under core goal 7. The item names what is reflected on: how you come across to others. It names the basis: choices in one's own language repertoire. It does not say how a pupil finds out how they come across, and it names no audience. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Pupils most often leave out the other person's side: they say how they meant to come across and not how they did come across. Many explain a reaction by the other person's character ("she is strict") and do not link it to a choice of their own.
