## Official wording

verwoorden van opgedane inzichten en kennis over de wereld en culturen op basis van literatuur.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8 B d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting into words insights and knowledge gained about the world and cultures on the basis of literature.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a story set in another country or another century and says what was new: what children there eat, how they go to school, what they celebrate, what was forbidden. The child compares it with daily life at home and says what is the same and what differs. The child finds the place on a map and asks which parts were really so and which the writer made up.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 8 B, which says that the pupil explores the value of literature; 8 B sits under core goal 8. The item names the world and cultures, in the plural, and does not say which countries, times or cultures. Items 8 B b and 8 B c say the same about oneself and about others. The item names no kinds of text and no titles.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is the comparison with what they already know: a pupil lists unfamiliar details and draws no insight from them. Many pupils also take one story as true for a whole country or people, or take invented parts of a story as fact.
