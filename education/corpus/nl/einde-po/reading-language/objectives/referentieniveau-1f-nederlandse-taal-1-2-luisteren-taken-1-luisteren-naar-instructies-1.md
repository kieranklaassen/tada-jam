## Official wording

Kan voldoende feitelijke informatie halen uit instructies en aanwijzingen om taken of handelingen uit te kunnen voeren.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 1F, Taken / 1. Luisteren naar instructies / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can get enough factual information from instructions and directions to be able to carry out tasks or actions.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: the teacher says, "Take your atlas, open it at the map of Europe, and write down the capitals of five countries that border Germany." A pupil of about 12 takes the atlas, finds the map and writes down five capitals. In the sports hall a trainer says where the mats go and in what order the exercises are done, and the pupil lays out the mats and starts with the first exercise.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), task 1, *Luisteren naar instructies* (listening to instructions). It stands under *Taken* (tasks). It is the only statement for this task at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The measure is practical: enough (*voldoende*) factual information to carry out the task or the action, not every word. The statement names instructions and directions (*aanwijzingen*), and tasks or actions in general. It names no number of steps, no kind of task and no speaker, and it does not say whether the instruction is heard once or may be repeated.

### Common mistakes

A pupil carries out the first step and no longer knows the second and third. A pupil starts before the instruction is finished and misses a condition at the end, such as "but only in pencil". A pupil does the steps in the wrong order when the order is given with words such as "before" and "after".
