## Official wording

Kan een kort gesprek beginnen, gaande houden en beëindigen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Beurten nemen en bijdragen aan samenhang / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can begin a short conversation, keep it going and end it.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 walks up to a child who is new at the sports club, says hello and asks which school the child goes to. The pupil reacts to the answer, asks further questions and tells something in return. When the trainer calls, the pupil says "See you after the training" and walks on.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), *Beurten nemen en bijdragen aan samenhang* (taking turns and contributing to coherence). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). It is the only statement in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement is about a short (*kort*) conversation and names three parts: beginning it, keeping it going and ending it. It does not say how short, with whom or about what. The row is named after taking turns, but the statement itself names no ways of taking or giving a turn.

### Common mistakes

A pupil keeps a conversation going only by answering, with short answers and no question or remark of their own, so the partner has to ask every question. A pupil interrupts or talks at the same time as the partner. A pupil ends a conversation by walking away or falling silent, without a closing word.
