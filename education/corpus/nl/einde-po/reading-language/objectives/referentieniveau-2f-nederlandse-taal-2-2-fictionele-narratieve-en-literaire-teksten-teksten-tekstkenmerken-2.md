## Official wording

Het verhaal heeft een dramatische verhaallijn waarin de spanning af en toe wordt onderbroken door gedachten of beschrijvingen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 2.2 Fictionele, narratieve en literaire teksten, niveau 2F, Teksten / Tekstkenmerken / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The story has a dramatic storyline in which the tension is now and then interrupted by thoughts or descriptions.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who reads at this level reads a story with a problem that grows and is resolved, and wants to know how it ends. Now and then the events stop for a moment: the main character thinks about what happened, or the text describes a room or a landscape. The child reads through such a passage and takes up the events again after it.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, reading fictional, narrative and literary texts (*Lezen fictionele, narratieve en literaire teksten*), row *Tekstkenmerken* (text characteristics). The row stands under *Teksten* (texts); this is the second of the three paragraphs in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement describes the stories read at this level, not what the pupil does. The interruptions are "now and then" (*af en toe*), which is the statement's own measure, and they are of two kinds: thoughts or descriptions. The statement does not say how long an interruption may be, and it names no genre, no length and no titles.

### Common mistakes

This statement describes texts, so it is not a skill with a typical error. A designer who chooses a story for this level looks for a storyline with tension in which thoughts or descriptions interrupt the events now and then. What pupils most often leave out are those passages: they skip the thoughts or descriptions.
