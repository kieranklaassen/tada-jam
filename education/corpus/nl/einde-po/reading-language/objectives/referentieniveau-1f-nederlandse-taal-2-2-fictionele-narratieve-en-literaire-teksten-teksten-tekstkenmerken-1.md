## Official wording

De structuur is eenvoudig. Het tempo waarin de spannende of dramatische gebeurtenissen elkaar opvolgen is hoog.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 2.2 Fictionele, narratieve en literaire teksten, niveau 1F, Teksten / Tekstkenmerken / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The structure is simple. The pace at which the exciting or dramatic events follow one another is high.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a story that is told in the order in which things happen and in which something exciting happens every few pages. The child keeps reading to find out what comes next.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Fictionele, narratieve en literaire teksten* (reading fictional, narrative and literary texts), *Tekstkenmerken* (features of the texts). It stands under *Teksten* (texts). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement describes the fictional texts a pupil at this level can read, not something the pupil does. It gives two features: a simple structure, and a high pace of exciting or dramatic events. It does not say what makes a structure simple, and it names no length, no titles, no authors and no genres.

### Common mistakes

This statement describes texts, so it is not a skill with a typical error. With a text that goes beyond this description, a child most often can no longer follow the story when it jumps in time or follows two characters in turn. A child also puts a book down when many pages pass without an event.
