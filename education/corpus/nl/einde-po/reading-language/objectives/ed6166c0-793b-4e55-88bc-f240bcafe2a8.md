## Official wording

actief deelnemen aan gesprekken;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 A b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking part actively in conversations;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group conversation a child of 11 or 12 brings in an idea of their own, asks another child a question, and reacts to what was said before. In a conversation with one other person the child continues it with a question or a remark and does not answer only "yes" or "no".

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 4 A, which says that the pupil holds conversations adjusted to purpose, partner or partners and context; 4 A sits under core goal 4. The item names nothing further: it does not say what counts as active, how often a pupil contributes, or in which kinds of conversation. Active does not have to mean contributing the most: item 4 A a names listening as a conversation skill. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child stays silent in a group and contributes only when asked by name. A child takes "active" to mean saying the most, and leaves others no turn. A child repeats what another child said and adds nothing to it.
