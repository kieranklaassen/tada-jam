## Official wording

Kan eenvoudige gesprekken voeren over vertrouwde onderwerpen in het dagelijks leven op en buiten school.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 1F, Algemene omschrijving Gesprekken / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can hold simple conversations about familiar topics in daily life at and outside school.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 talks with a classmate at the bicycle shed about what they did at the weekend, and asks the neighbour whether a ball may be fetched from the garden. The pupil says a few sentences, listens to the answer and says something that fits it. The talk stays simple and about things the pupil knows.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), *Algemene omschrijving* (general description). It is the whole general description of this skill at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. Three wordings set the level: the conversations are simple (*eenvoudige*), the topics are familiar (*vertrouwde*), and they come from daily life at and outside school. The statement names no topics, no conversation partners, no number of partners and no length of a conversation. What the conversations are for and how well they are held is worded in the other statements under *Gesprekken* at 1F, other records of this lane.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out in a simple conversation is their own part in keeping it going: they answer a question in one or two words and ask nothing back. Some pupils also leave out the opening or the close, and start or stop without a greeting or a last word.
