## Official wording

Kan een eenvoudige tekst beknopt samenvatten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 2.1 Zakelijke teksten, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Samenvatten / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can summarise a simple text concisely.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a simple informative text, marks the main point of each paragraph, and gives a summary of a few sentences in own words. The summary keeps the main points in the order of the text and leaves out examples, repetitions and details.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, reading factual texts (*Lezen zakelijke teksten*), row *Samenvatten* (summarising). The row stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (characteristics of task performance). 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. Two words bound the statement: the text is "simple" and the summary is "concise". It does not say how long the text or the summary is, or whether the summary is spoken or written, and it names no kind of text.

### Common mistakes

A child retells the text almost in full, so the summary is nearly as long as the text. A child copies the first sentence of each paragraph, whether or not it holds the main point. A child keeps a striking detail and leaves out the main point.
