## Official wording

De leerlingen leren bij de doelen onder «mondeling taalonderwijs» en «schriftelijk taalonderwijs» strategieën te herkennen, te verwoorden, te gebruiken en te beoordelen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO (version in force until 31 July 2026), bijlage, kerndoel 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupils learn, for the goals under "oral language education" (*mondeling taalonderwijs*) and "written language education" (*schriftelijk taalonderwijs*), to recognise strategies, to put them into words, to use them and to judge them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who is about to read a hard text first looks at the title, the headings and the pictures and says what the text will probably be about. Before writing the child makes a list of points; before giving a talk the child writes key words on a card. Afterwards the child says what was done ("I read the questions first") and whether it helped.

### Limits

This 2006 goal was replaced on 1 August 2026; schools may still use it until 1 August 2031. It is goal 10 of the core goals for Dutch, the first of three under the heading *Taalbeschouwing, waaronder strategieën* (reflection on language, including strategies), goals 10 to 12. The goal refers to the goals under the two other headings, goals 1 to 9, and asks four things of strategies: recognising them, putting them into words, using them and judging them. It names no strategy and gives no example, and it sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A pupil uses a strategy only when told to and does not choose one without being asked. A pupil can name a strategy as a learned word and does not use it while reading or writing. A pupil carries out a step, such as making a list of points, and then writes without looking at it.
