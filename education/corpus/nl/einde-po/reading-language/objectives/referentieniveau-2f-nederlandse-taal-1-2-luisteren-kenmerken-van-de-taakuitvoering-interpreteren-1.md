## Official wording

Kan informatie en meningen interpreteren.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Interpreteren / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can interpret information and opinions.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 listens to a radio item in which a reporter says that from next year a school in the town gives no more homework, and a head teacher says that homework "teaches children to plan". The pupil says what the information comes to: it is about one school in the town, and it starts next year. The pupil says what the head teacher means by "teaches children to plan": a child who has homework has to decide when to do it.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), row *Interpreteren* (interpreting). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the first of two statements in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The whole statement is one ability with two objects: information and opinions. It does not say what interpreting includes; saying what a piece of information or an opinion means, as in the illustration, is the pack's example. It names no kind of text or programme, no subject and no source of the information or opinions.

### Common mistakes

A pupil repeats what was said word for word and cannot say what it means. A pupil interprets everything in line with his or her own opinion and does not hear what the speaker meant.
