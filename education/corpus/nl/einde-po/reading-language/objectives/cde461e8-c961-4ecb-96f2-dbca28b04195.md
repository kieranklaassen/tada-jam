## Official wording

weergeven van hoofd- en bijzaken, indrukken en vragen bij gelezen, bekeken of beluisterde inhoud;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 3 C a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

giving main points and minor points, impressions and questions on content that has been read, watched or listened to;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 has read a chapter, watched a documentary or listened to a talk, and writes in a notebook: the two or three main points, a few details under each, what the child found surprising, and what the child still wants to know. The child uses the notes later to talk about the subject.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 3 C, which says that the pupil writes in order to build knowledge or reach understanding; 3 C sits under core goal 3. Four things are given: main points, minor points, impressions and questions. The content has been taken in in one of three ways: read, watched or listened to. The item names no form (notes, a list or a text) and no length, and sets no level. Item 2 A e, under understanding texts, also names main points and minor points and adds "in one's own words"; this item does not.

### Common mistakes

A child writes down everything and marks nothing as a main point. A child gives the content and leaves out the child's own impressions and questions. A child writes "no questions" where something was not understood.
