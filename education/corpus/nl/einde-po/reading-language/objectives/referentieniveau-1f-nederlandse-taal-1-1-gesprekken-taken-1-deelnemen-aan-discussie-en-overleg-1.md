## Official wording

Kan de hoofdpunten volgen en kan de eigen mening verwoorden en onderbouwen met argumenten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 1F, Taken / 1. Deelnemen aan discussie en overleg / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can follow the main points and can put their own opinion into words and support it with arguments.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: the class discusses whether mobile phones should be allowed in the playground. A pupil of about 12 follows the main things that are said for and against, and then says, "I think they should stay in the bag, because otherwise nobody plays together any more, and because phones get broken outside." The pupil gives the opinion and the reasons for it.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), task 1, *Deelnemen aan discussie en overleg* (taking part in discussion and in *overleg*, talking something over together). It stands under *Taken* (tasks). The task has one other statement at 1F, on listening critically to opinions and giving a response. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names three things: following the main points, putting one's own opinion into words, and supporting it with arguments. It asks for the main points, not for every detail. It names no topics, no size of group and no number of arguments, and it does not say that agreement is reached.

### Common mistakes

A pupil gives an opinion without a reason, or repeats the opinion in other words as if that were the reason ("I think it is bad because it is not good"). A pupil gives a personal preference or one single experience as an argument that should hold for everyone. A pupil thinks only about what they want to say and misses the main points of what the others said.
