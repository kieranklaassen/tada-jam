## Official wording

benoemen van tegenstrijdige en overeenkomstige inhoud binnen en tussen teksten;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 2 B c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

naming contradictory and corresponding content within and between texts;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads two texts about how much sleep a child needs and finds that one says nine hours and the other eleven; the child points to both sentences and says that they contradict each other. The child also finds what the two texts say alike. Within one text the child notices that the heading promises something that the text below it does not say.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 2 B, which says that the pupil evaluates and reflects on non-fiction and literary texts; 2 B sits under core goal 2. It names two kinds of content, contradictory and corresponding, in two places: inside one text and between texts. Item 2 B b is about differences and similarities in facts, opinions and perspectives; the law does not say how the two items are divided. This item does not ask the pupil to settle a contradiction. It sets no level and no number of texts.

### Common mistakes

A child calls two statements contradictory when they are about different things (nine hours for an adult, eleven for a young child). A child does not notice a contradiction because each sentence is understood on its own. A child finds the contradictions and leaves out what corresponds.
