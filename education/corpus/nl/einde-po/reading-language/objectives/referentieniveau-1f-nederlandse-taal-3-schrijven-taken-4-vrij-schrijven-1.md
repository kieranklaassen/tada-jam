## Official wording

Kan eigen ideeën, ervaringen, gebeurtenissen en fantasieën opschrijven in een verhaal, in een informatieve tekst of in een gedicht.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Taken / 4. Vrij schrijven / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can write down own ideas, experiences, events and fantasies in a story, in an informative text or in a poem.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes a story about a journey it has made up, an informative text about its own hobby, and a poem about the first snow of the winter. In each, the content is the child's own: something it thought of, lived through or imagined.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Vrij schrijven* (free writing). It is task 4 under *Taken* (tasks). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The content is the pupil's own: ideas, experiences, events and fantasies. The statement names three forms, joined by "or": a story, an informative text or a poem. It names no length, and it does not say what form the poem has, rhymed or not.

### Common mistakes

A child's story is a list of events joined with "and then". A child believes a poem must rhyme and chooses words for the rhyme that do not fit what it wants to say. A child puts made-up events into an informative text.
