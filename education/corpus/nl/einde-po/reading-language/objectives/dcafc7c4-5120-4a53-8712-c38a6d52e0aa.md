## Official wording

De leerling produceert teksten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil produces texts.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school writes a report of a class trip, a letter to the town council, a story and notes for the child's own use, and gives a short talk. Before starting, the child thinks about who will read or hear it and what it is for; afterwards the child rereads and improves it.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 3 itself. Three goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 3 A (speaking and writing adjusted to purpose, audience and context), 3 B (using language in a creative way) and 3 C (writing in order to build knowledge or reach understanding). The sentence does not say what counts as a text; 3 A names speaking as well as writing, so a text here can be spoken. The goal sets no level: it names no text length, no kinds of text to be produced and no standard for spelling. The reference levels 1F and 2F, which are other records of this lane (sections *Spreken*, speaking, and *Schrijven*, writing), are worded in more detail.

### Common mistakes

A child writes as the child speaks, in one long row of sentences joined by "and then". A child writes for a reader who already knows everything and leaves out what an outsider needs. A child hands in the first version without rereading it.
