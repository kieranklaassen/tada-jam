## Official wording

verwoorden van persoonlijke voorkeur op basis van reflectie op ervaringen met literatuur.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting personal preference into words on the basis of reflection on experiences with literature.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks back at the books of the past months, with a list or a pile of them, and sorts them into books the child would read again and books the child would not. The child then says or writes what the first group has in common: "I like books in which children solve something without adults", "I stop when a book has long descriptions".

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 8 A, which says that the pupil develops a reading preference of their own; 8 A sits under core goal 8. The item asks for the preference to be put into words, and for it to rest on reflection on the pupil's experiences with literature. It does not say whether in speech or in writing, or to whom. The value judgement about books and texts that have been read or listened to is item 8 A b. The item names no titles and no number of books.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is the looking back: a pupil names the last book read as the favourite. Many pupils name a title or a series where a preference is asked, and do not say what kind of story, character or language they like.
