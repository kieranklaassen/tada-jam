## Official wording

Kan formeel en informeel taalgebruik hanteren.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op publiek / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can use formal and informal language.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes *u* (the formal "you") in a letter to an adult it does not know and *je* (the informal "you") in a note to a friend. In the pack's example the child writes to the adult *Kunt u mij vertellen wanneer de les begint?* (Can you tell me when the lesson starts?) and to the friend *Weet jij wanneer de les begint?* (Do you know when the lesson starts?).

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Afstemming op publiek* (fit to the audience). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the second of two statements in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The Dutch has *taalgebruik* (language use) and the verb *hanteren* (using, handling). The statement names the two registers, formal and informal, and gives no examples and no situations for either. It does not say how a pupil chooses between them.

### Common mistakes

A child changes from *u* to *je* within one letter. A child writes words and short forms from chat messages in a letter to an adult. A child writes to a friend in the stiff wording of a formal letter.
