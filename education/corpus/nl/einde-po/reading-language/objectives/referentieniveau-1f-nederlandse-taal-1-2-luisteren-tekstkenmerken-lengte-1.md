## Official wording

Teksten zijn niet lang; luisterduur is 5–10 minuten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 1F, Tekstkenmerken / Lengte / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Texts are not long; listening time is 5–10 minutes.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 listens to a classmate's talk of about seven minutes on keeping guinea pigs, or to the teacher reading aloud for about ten minutes, and keeps listening from the beginning to the end. Afterwards the pupil can say what the beginning and the end were about.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), *Lengte* (length). It stands under *Tekstkenmerken* (text features), which describe the texts a pupil listens to at this level, not what the pupil does. It is the only statement in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement gives a listening time of 5–10 minutes and says the texts are "not long". It does not say whether the time is that of one text or of one stretch of listening, and it gives no number of words. Among the statements of the oral skills at 1F (*Gesprekken*, *Luisteren*, *Spreken*) this is the only one that gives a number; the statements under *Gesprekken* and *Spreken* give no duration in minutes, though one under *Gesprekken* speaks of a short (*kort*) conversation and one under *Spreken* of a short, prepared *verhaal* (a story, or an account or talk) or presentation.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error; it describes the text, not the pupil. In a listening text near the upper end of this time, pupils most often leave out the middle: they keep the beginning and the end. They also stop listening sooner when nothing in the text is repeated.
