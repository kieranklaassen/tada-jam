## Official wording

waarderen van creatief taalgebruik.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 3 B d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

*waarderen* (appreciating, or judging the worth of) creative use of language.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 hears a poem, a rap or a joke with a play on words and says what the child likes in it and why. The child listens to what a classmate has made and names one thing in it that works well. The child can say that a text is well made even when the child does not like it.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 3 B, which says that the pupil uses language in a creative way; 3 B sits under core goal 3. The Dutch verb *waarderen* has two readings: appreciating (enjoying and valuing) and judging the worth of something. The item does not show which is meant, and the pack does not choose. It does not say whose creative use of language is meant; item 3 B a names that of "others", and this item names nobody. It names no criteria and sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often say only "nice" or "not nice" and leave out what in the language makes it so. Many value only what rhymes or is funny.
