## Official wording

Kan informatie beoordelen en een reactie geven.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 1F, Taken / 2. Informatie uitwisselen / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can judge information and give a response.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a classmate says that the bus to the museum takes ten minutes. A pupil of about 12 remembers that the last trip took much longer and says, "I think that is not right, last time it took half an hour. Shall we look it up?" On hearing that the gym lesson has moved to Thursday, the pupil judges that this matters for packing the bag and says, "Then I will bring my gym things on Thursday."

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), task 2, *Informatie uitwisselen* (exchanging information). It stands under *Taken* (tasks). The task has one other statement at 1F, on giving and asking for information and listening critically to it. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names two things: judging information and giving a response. It does not say on what the information is judged; whether it is correct, complete or useful are the pack's examples of a judgement, not the decree's words. It names no kind of information and no form of response.

### Common mistakes

A pupil judges information by whether it is welcome, not by whether it can be right. A pupil notices that something does not fit and says nothing. A pupil rejects information outright ("That is nonsense") without saying what is wrong with it.
