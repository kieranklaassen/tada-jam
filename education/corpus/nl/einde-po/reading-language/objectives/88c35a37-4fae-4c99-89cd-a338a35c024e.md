## Official wording

samenvatten van inzichten, verwoorden van oplossingen of trekken van conclusies.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 B e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

summarising insights, putting solutions into words or drawing conclusions.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group conversation a child of 11 or 12 says: "So we found three reasons why the plant died: too little light, too much water and a pot that was too small." Or the child says what the group will do: "We solve it by taking turns with the tablet." Or the child concludes: "So the heavy ball does not fall faster."

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 4 B, which says that the pupil holds conversations to arrive at the building of knowledge, understanding or an approach; 4 B sits under core goal 4. The item names three actions joined by "or": summarising insights, putting solutions into words, and drawing conclusions. It does not say at what moment in a conversation this is done or who in a group does it. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child retells the whole conversation in order and does not pick out the main points. A child gives their own opinion as the conclusion of the group. A child draws a conclusion that goes further than what was found: from one example to "always".
