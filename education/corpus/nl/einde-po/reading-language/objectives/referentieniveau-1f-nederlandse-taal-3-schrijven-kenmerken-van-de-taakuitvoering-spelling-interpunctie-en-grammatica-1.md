## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

This statement is a cross-reference and names nothing that a child does. What it points to shows in a child's writing as words spelled correctly and sentences marked with a capital letter and a full stop.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Spelling, interpunctie en grammatica* (spelling, punctuation and grammar). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the first of two statements in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement refers to the level description for language care, in section 4 of the same annex of the decree, and states no level of its own. In this lane the 1F statements of that section are the records under *4.1 Begrippenlijst* and *4.4 Moeilijkheid*.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error: the statement is a reference. A reader most often overlooks that the level for spelling and punctuation in a pupil's writing is set in that other section and not here.
