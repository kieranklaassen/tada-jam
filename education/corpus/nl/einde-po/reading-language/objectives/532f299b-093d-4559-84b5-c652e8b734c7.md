## Official wording

De school stimuleert de taalontwikkeling van de leerling in alle leergebieden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 1 B.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The school stimulates the pupil's language development in all learning areas (*leergebieden*, the subject areas of the curriculum).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 reads a text in a history lesson, explains aloud in an arithmetic lesson how an answer was found, writes a report in a nature lesson, and learns the words of each subject while doing so. The child works on language in every subject and not only in the Dutch lesson.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 1, which is about the school stimulating the language competence of pupils. It is addressed to the school: it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. Its items 1 B a to 1 B e name stimulating activities in which school language, subject language and subject-specific language skills can be acquired, the use of rich texts, language production and interaction, attention to language care and language use, and offering a school-wide set of approaches and strategies. The sentence says "all learning areas" and does not list them. It says "the pupil" in the singular where the sentence of core goal 1 says "pupils". It sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This sentence is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. In other subjects children most often give an answer of one word and leave out the explanation, or copy a sentence from the book as their answer.
