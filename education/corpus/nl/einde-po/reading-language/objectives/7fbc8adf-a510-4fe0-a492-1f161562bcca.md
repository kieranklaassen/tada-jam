## Official wording

stimuleren van het gebruiken van rijke teksten over inhoudelijke thema’s in alle leergebieden;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 1 B b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

stimulating the use of rich texts about subject-matter themes (*inhoudelijke thema's*) in all learning areas (*leergebieden*);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 learns about a theme such as the Second World War or the climate from full texts: a chapter of an information book, a part of a diary, a newspaper article. The child reads them, talks about them and uses them to learn about the theme.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 1 B, which says that the school stimulates the pupil's language development in all learning areas; 1 B sits under core goal 1. Like 1 B, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is stimulating, and the item does not say who uses the texts. "Rich texts" is not explained: the item names no length, no difficulty and no kind of text. It names no themes. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. With a long text in another subject, children most often search for the answer to one question and skip the rest, or stop reading at the first hard paragraph.
