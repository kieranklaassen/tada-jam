## Official wording

De leerling verkent hoe je met taal uiting geeft aan identiteit.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 7 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil explores how you give expression to identity with language.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes down the words they use with friends and would not use with a grandparent, and says what those words show about the group they are in. The child notices that a football team, a family or a group of friends has words of its own, and that using them or not using them shows who belongs.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 7, which is about exploring the use of language. Its items 7 A a to 7 A d name exploring one's own language repertoire in relation to how you want to come across and which groups you want to belong to, exploring it in relation to audience, purpose and context, reflecting on how you come across to others, and *waarderen* (valuing, or judging the worth of) that repertoire. The sentence says "you" (*je*) in the general sense of "a person". It does not define identity and names no group, language or variety. The verb is exploring, and the law sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Pupils most often leave out that they choose at all: their own way of using language feels given, not chosen. Many link identity only to which language a person speaks and leave out word choice and tone.
