## Official wording

Kan relaties tussen en binnen teksten evalueren en beoordelen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 2.1 Zakelijke teksten, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Evalueren / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can evaluate and judge relations between and within texts.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads two short texts about the same topic, for example two pieces about a longer school day, and says where they agree and where they contradict each other. Within one text the child checks whether the conclusion follows from the reasons given and whether an example fits the claim it stands under. The child gives a judgement, for example that one reason does not support the conclusion, and points to the sentences.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, reading factual texts (*Lezen zakelijke teksten*), row *Evalueren* (evaluating). The row stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (characteristics of task performance). 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement names relations between texts and relations within a text, and two verbs: evaluating and judging. It does not say which relations are meant (for example cause and effect, claim and argument, agreement and contradiction), and it names no criteria for the judgement. It does not say how many texts are compared, and it names no length and no title.

### Common mistakes

A child gives an opinion about the topic ("I am against longer school days") where a judgement about the texts is asked. A child notes that two texts differ but does not say on which point. A child accepts a conclusion because it is stated firmly, without checking whether the reasons in the text lead to it.
