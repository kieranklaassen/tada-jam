## Official wording

Maakt eigen gedachtegang voor de luisteraar begrijpelijk, hoewel de structuur van de tekst nog niet altijd klopt.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.3 Spreken, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Samenhang / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Makes their own line of thought understandable for the listener, although the structure of the text is not yet always right.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 tells the class why the group chose the fire station for the class outing. The pupil gives the reasons, and the listeners understand how the group came to its choice. Halfway the pupil says, "Oh, and I forgot to say that we first wanted to go to the zoo", and adds it out of order.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Spreken* (speaking), *Samenhang* (coherence). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). It is the only statement in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement sets a measure and allows a shortfall: the listener can understand the pupil's line of thought, and the structure of the text is "not yet always" (*nog niet altijd*) right. "Text" here is what the pupil says. The statement does not say what a right structure is, and it names no parts of a text and no linking words.

### Common mistakes

A pupil tells things in the order in which they think of them, and adds a forgotten part later with "oh, and". A pupil leaves out the link between two thoughts, so the listener hears two statements and not that one is the reason for the other. A pupil says "he", "she" or "there" without having said who or where.
