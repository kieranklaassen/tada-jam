## Official wording

b) verkleinwoord na open klinker (parapluutje)

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 4.4 Moeilijkheid, niveau 2F, Spelling / 3. Morfologische spelling / Moeilijke gevallen / b).

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

b) diminutive after an open vowel (*parapluutje*, little umbrella)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 at this level makes the diminutive of a noun that ends in a single vowel letter with a long sound, and writes that letter twice before *-tje*, so the vowel is still read long: *paraplu* becomes *parapluutje*, *auto* becomes *autootje*, *oma* becomes *omaatje*.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, terms and language care, table of difficulty (*Moeilijkheid*), row b) of the difficult cases (*Moeilijke gevallen*) under *Spelling*, 3. *Morfologische spelling* (morphological spelling, the spelling of word forms such as plurals and diminutives). In this table (Table 2 of the decree) the row has a plus sign in the column for level 2F. The table's caption describes its levels as mastery: 75% of all pupils in the age group or level group have an 80% chance of getting it right. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The row names the diminutive after an open vowel and gives one word, *parapluutje*. It states no rule in words and does not say which final vowels are meant; "open vowel" is read here as a long vowel written with one letter at the end of the word, which is the pack's reading. The decree's running list of spelling rules in section 4.3.1, which carries no level and is not a record of the pack, gives *chocolaatje* (little chocolate) and *cafeetje* (little café) beside *parapluutje* for this case. The pack's further examples, all correct Dutch: *autootje* (little car), *omaatje* (little grandmother), *fotootje* (little photo), *menuutje* (little menu).

### Common mistakes

A child writes the vowel letter once (the wrong *paraplutje* and *autotje*), which would be read with a short vowel. A child borrows the apostrophe of the plural and writes the wrong *auto’tje*. A child keeps the accent and writes the wrong *cafétje* where *cafeetje* is right.
