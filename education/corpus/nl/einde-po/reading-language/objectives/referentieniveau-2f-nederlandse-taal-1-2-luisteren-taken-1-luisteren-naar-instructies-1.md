## Official wording

Kan uitleg en instructies over concrete onderwerpen begrijpen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 2F, Taken / 1. Luisteren naar instructies / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can understand explanation and *instructies* (directions for doing something, or teaching) about concrete subjects.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 listens while the teacher explains how a compass works and then tells the group how to find north in the playground: hold the compass flat, wait for the needle to stop, turn the ring. The pupil carries out the steps in order and can tell a classmate who was absent how it is done.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), row *Luisteren naar instructies* (listening to *instructies*). It stands under *Taken* (tasks), as task 1, and is the only statement in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. *Instructie* has two readings in Dutch: directions that say how to do something, and teaching given to a learner; the gloss keeps the Dutch. The statement names two things to be understood, explanation and *instructies*, and limits them to concrete subjects. It does not say what counts as concrete, names no subjects, no speaker and no number of steps, and does not say how the pupil shows the understanding; carrying out the steps is the pack's example.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is a step: they remember the first and the last part of a spoken instruction and forget a part in between. Many start to act before the explanation is finished and miss a condition that came at the end.
