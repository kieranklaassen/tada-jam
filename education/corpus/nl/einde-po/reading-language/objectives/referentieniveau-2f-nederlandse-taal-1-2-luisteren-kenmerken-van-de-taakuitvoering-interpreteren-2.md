## Official wording

Kan de bedoeling van de spreker(s) of het doel van de makers van een programma verwoorden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Interpreteren / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can put into words the intention of the speaker(s) or the purpose of the makers of a programme.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: after a talk in the classroom by someone from the fire brigade, a pupil of about 12 says: "She wanted us to check the smoke alarm at home." After a commercial for a soft drink that was shown in a lesson, the pupil says: "The makers want you to think that everyone who drinks it has fun, so that you buy it."

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), row *Interpreteren* (interpreting). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the second of two statements in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement names one ability, putting into words, and two objects joined by "or": the intention of the speaker or speakers, and the purpose of the makers of a programme. It names no intentions or purposes and no kinds of programme; the talk and the commercial are the pack's examples. It does not say whether the pupil says this aloud or writes it.

### Common mistakes

A pupil says what the talk or programme was about when asked what it was for. A pupil gives the same purpose for every programme ("to inform") and misses that a commercial is made to sell and a guest in a discussion wants to persuade. A pupil judges the purpose by the form: a commercial made to look like a news item is taken for news.
