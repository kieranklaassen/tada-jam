## Official wording

Kan doelgericht doorvragen om de gewenste informatie te verwerven.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op doel / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can ask follow-up questions purposefully (*doelgericht doorvragen*) to obtain the desired information.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 telephones the swimming pool to find out whether the class can come on Friday. The answer is "Friday is busy", so the pupil asks "Is there a time on Friday when a group of 28 can come?" and then "What does that cost for each pupil?" The pupil goes on asking until the day, the time and the price are known.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), row *Afstemming op doel* (attuning to the purpose). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the second of two statements in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement names one action, asking further questions with a purpose, and one aim, obtaining the information the pupil wants. It names no kind of conversation, no conversation partner and no subject, and sets no number of questions.

### Common mistakes

A pupil accepts the first answer although it does not give what was needed. A pupil asks a list of prepared questions in order and does not react to what the other person has just said. A pupil asks follow-up questions about something else and ends without the information he or she came for.
