## Official wording

verwoorden van opgedane inzichten en kennis over anderen op basis van literatuur;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8 B c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting into words insights and knowledge gained about others on the basis of literature;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a story told by a character who is very unlike the child, for example an old man, a child who has fled a war, or a bully, and says what that character wants and why the character acts that way. The child says what the story made clear about other people: "now I see why someone gets angry when nobody listens".

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 8 B, which says that the pupil explores the value of literature; 8 B sits under core goal 8. The item is about what the pupil has come to see or know about others. "Others" is not narrowed: the item does not say whether characters, people the pupil knows or people in general are meant. Items 8 B b and 8 B d say the same about oneself, and about the world and cultures. The item names no kinds of text and no titles.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is the other person's view: a pupil judges a character by what the pupil would do ("that is stupid, I would just tell the teacher") and does not ask why the character acts differently. Many pupils say what a character does and not what the character thinks or feels.
