## Official wording

Blijft trouw aan spreekdoel, soms met hulp van een ander.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.3 Spreken, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op doel / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Stays true to the purpose of speaking, sometimes with help from someone else.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 stands up to explain to the class how to look after a guinea pig. Halfway the pupil starts telling at length how their own guinea pig once escaped. The teacher asks, "And what does it need every day?", and the pupil goes back to the explanation and finishes it.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Spreken* (speaking), *Afstemming op doel* (fit to the purpose). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). It is the only statement in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The words "sometimes with help from someone else" (*soms met hulp van een ander*) are part of the level: at 1F a pupil may need another person in order to stay with the purpose, and only sometimes. The statement does not say who the other person is or what the help consists of. It names no purposes; the general description of *Spreken* at 1F, another record of this lane, names what a pupil does when speaking.

### Common mistakes

A pupil moves from the purpose into a side story and does not come back to it without a question from someone else. A pupil who is to explain something tells what happened to them instead, so the explanation becomes a story. A pupil stops when the prepared part is finished, although the purpose has not been reached, for example that the listeners can now do it themselves.
