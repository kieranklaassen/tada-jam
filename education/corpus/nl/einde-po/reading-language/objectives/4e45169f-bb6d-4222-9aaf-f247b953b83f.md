## Official wording

De school zorgt voor een rijke taal- en leesomgeving.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 1 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The school provides a rich language and reading environment.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 has books and other texts within reach, reads at a fixed time, hears a book read aloud, and talks and writes about real things: a letter that is sent, a talk that others listen to. The child tries out speaking and writing without fear of making mistakes.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 1, which is about the school stimulating the language competence of pupils. It is addressed to the school: it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. Its items 1 A a to 1 A e name offering language activities in meaningful contexts, offering literature for young readers within a fixed reading routine, offering the knowledge and skills of the learning area Dutch in connection with one another, stimulating reading motivation and the courage to speak and write, and giving room to pupils' different languages and language varieties. The sentence does not say what makes an environment "rich": it names no number of books and no amount of reading time. It sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This sentence is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. With many books within reach, children of this age most often keep to one series or one kind of book, or turn the pages of a book without reading it.
