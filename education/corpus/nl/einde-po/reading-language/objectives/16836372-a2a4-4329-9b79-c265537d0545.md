## Official wording

aanbieden van taalactiviteiten in betekenisvolle contexten;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 1 A a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

offering language activities in meaningful contexts;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 writes a letter that is posted, interviews a grandparent for a project, reads instructions in order to build something, and presents findings to another class. The reading, writing, speaking and listening have a use that the child can see.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 1 A, which says that the school provides a rich language and reading environment; 1 A sits under core goal 1. Like 1 A, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is offering. The item does not say what a language activity is or what makes a context meaningful, and it gives no example; the examples above are the pack's. It sets no level and does not say how often.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. In an activity with a real purpose, children most often put their attention on the product, such as the poster or the thing that is built, and leave out the language work: they copy a text onto the poster and do not rewrite it for their readers.
