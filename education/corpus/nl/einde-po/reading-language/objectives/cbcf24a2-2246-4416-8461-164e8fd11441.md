## Official wording

verwerven en inzetten van informatie afgestemd op kennis, achtergrond, standpunt en perspectief van de gesprekspartner(s);

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

acquiring and putting to use information adjusted to the knowledge, background, position (*standpunt*, the opinion someone holds) and perspective of the conversation partner(s);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who explains a computer game to a grandparent first asks what the grandparent already knows, and then leaves out the game's own words or explains them. Before a conversation with the caretaker about a broken goal on the playground the child finds out what happened and what a repair takes, and brings up the points that matter to the caretaker.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 4 A, which says that the pupil holds conversations adjusted to purpose, partner or partners and context; 4 A sits under core goal 4. The item names two actions, acquiring information and putting it to use, and four things about the partner or partners: knowledge, background, position and perspective. The Dutch leaves open whether "adjusted to" belongs to both actions or only to the using; the gloss keeps this open. The item does not say where the information comes from or how much is meant, and it sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child assumes the partner knows what the child knows, and uses names and words the partner has never heard. A child tells every fact they have and does not choose what this partner needs. A child argues only from their own position and brings no information that answers the partner's position.
