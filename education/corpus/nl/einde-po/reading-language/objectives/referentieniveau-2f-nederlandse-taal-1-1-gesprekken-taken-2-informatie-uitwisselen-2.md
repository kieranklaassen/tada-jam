## Official wording

Kan informatie verzamelen en verwerken via het houden van een vraaggesprek.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 2F, Taken / 2. Informatie uitwisselen / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can collect and process information by holding an interview (*vraaggesprek*, a conversation of questions and answers).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 interviews the school's caretaker for a piece about the jobs people do in the school. The pupil asks questions and notes the answers in key words. Afterwards the pupil sorts the notes and tells the class in a few sentences what the caretaker does in a day.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), row *Informatie uitwisselen* (exchanging information). It stands under *Taken* (tasks), as task 2, and is the second of two statements in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement names two actions, collecting information and processing it, and one means: holding an interview. It does not say who is interviewed or on what subject, and sets no length for the interview. It does not say what form the processing takes; the notes and the report to the class in the illustration are the pack's examples. It does not mention preparing questions.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is the processing: they come back with answers and do nothing to order or use them. During the interview many try to write down every word and do not listen to the answer, or ask only questions that can be answered with yes or no.
