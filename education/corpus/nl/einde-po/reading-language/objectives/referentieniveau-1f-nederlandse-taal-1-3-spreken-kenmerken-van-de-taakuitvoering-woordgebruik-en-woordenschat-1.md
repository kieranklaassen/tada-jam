## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration, taken from the statement this one refers to: a pupil of about 12 tells the class about a hobby and has enough words for it, because the subject is familiar. The pupil regularly searches for a word, and uses the same few words many times.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Spreken* (speaking), *Woordgebruik en woordenschat* (word use and vocabulary). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). It is the only statement in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement is a cross-reference and has no content of its own: it says that what is worded in the row of the same name under *Gesprekken* at 1F also holds for speaking. That row holds one statement, another record of this lane: enough words to talk about familiar situations and topics, with regular searching for words and little variation in word use. The source prints the reference without a full stop.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. The statement only refers to another row. A reader most often leaves out that row, and concludes that 1F says nothing about vocabulary in speaking. The typical shortfalls of pupils are noted in the record under *Gesprekken*.
