## Official wording

Kan het eigen gespreksdoel tot uitdrukking brengen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op doel / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can express one's own conversation purpose (*gespreksdoel*, what one wants from the conversation).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 goes to the teacher and says first: "I want to ask whether I may hand in my book report on Monday, because I am away on Friday." In a group task the pupil says: "I want us to decide today who makes the poster." The others know from these words what the pupil wants from the conversation.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), row *Afstemming op doel* (attuning to the purpose). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the first of two statements in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement asks that the pupil can express the purpose he or she has in a conversation. It names no purposes and no kinds of conversation, and does not say at what point in the conversation or in which words the purpose is expressed; the sentences in the illustration are the pack's examples.

### Common mistakes

A pupil tells a long account and does not say what he or she wants, so the other person has to guess. A pupil states the purpose only at the very end. A pupil has two purposes, for example asking permission and complaining, and expresses only one of them.
