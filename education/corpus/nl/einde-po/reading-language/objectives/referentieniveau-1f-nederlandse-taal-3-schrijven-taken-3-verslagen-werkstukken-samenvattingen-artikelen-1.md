## Official wording

Kan een verslag en of een werkstuk schrijven en daarbij stukjes informatie uit verscheidene bronnen samenvatten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Taken / 3. Verslagen, werkstukken, samenvattingen, artikelen / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can write a report and/or a project paper (*werkstuk*, a school paper on one subject) and in doing so summarise small pieces of information from several sources.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 makes a project paper about bees. The child reads a library book, a web page and a leaflet, writes down a few facts from each in its own words, and puts them together under headings such as what bees eat and how they live.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Verslagen, werkstukken, samenvattingen, artikelen* (reports, project papers, summaries, articles). It is task 3 under *Taken* (tasks). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names a report and a project paper; the source prints *en of* between them, and "and/or" is plainly meant. What is summarised is small pieces of information (*stukjes informatie*) from several sources (*verscheidene bronnen*). The statement gives no number of sources, no kinds of source and no length, and it does not ask for a list of sources. The row label names articles; this statement does not.

### Common mistakes

A child copies sentences from a source word for word instead of summarising. A child uses one source and no other. A child sets the facts down source by source, in the order in which it found them, and not by subject.
