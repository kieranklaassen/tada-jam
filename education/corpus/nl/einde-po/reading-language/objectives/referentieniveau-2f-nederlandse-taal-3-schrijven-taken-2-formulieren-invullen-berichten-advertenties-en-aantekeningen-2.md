## Official wording

Kan aantekeningen maken tijdens een uitleg of les.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 2F, Taken / 2. Formulieren invullen, berichten, advertenties en aantekeningen / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can take notes during an explanation or a lesson.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who writes at this level listens to an explanation about, for example, the water cycle and writes down key words and short phrases while the explanation goes on: the steps, a number, a new term. The child uses arrows, dashes or a small drawing instead of full sentences and can tell afterwards from the notes what the explanation was about.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, writing (*Schrijven*), row *2. Formulieren invullen, berichten, advertenties en aantekeningen* (filling in forms, messages, advertisements and notes). The row stands under *Taken* (tasks); this is the second of the two paragraphs in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The notes are made "during" an explanation or a lesson, so while it goes on. The statement does not say what form the notes take, how complete they are, or what they are used for afterwards. It names no length of the explanation or lesson.

### Common mistakes

A child tries to write down every word and misses what is said next. A child writes single words that no longer mean anything to the child a day later. A child notes the examples and misses the main point they belong to.
