## Official wording

Kan eenvoudige teksten lezen over alledaagse onderwerpen en over onderwerpen die aansluiten bij de leefwereld.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 2.1 Zakelijke teksten, niveau 1F, Algemene omschrijving Lezen zakelijke teksten / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can read simple texts about everyday subjects and about subjects that connect with the world of experience (*leefwereld*).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a short text about a subject from daily life, such as looking after a pet or the rules of a sport, and afterwards answers a question about something the text says, such as what the animal eats. The child reads a notice about a school trip and tells someone what to bring and when to be there.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Zakelijke teksten* (reading factual texts), *Algemene omschrijving* (general description); the row label in full is *Algemene omschrijving Lezen zakelijke teksten*. It is the whole general description of this skill at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement bounds the texts in two ways: they are simple (*eenvoudige*), and their subjects are everyday ones (*alledaagse*) or ones that connect with the *leefwereld*. The Dutch says *de leefwereld* without saying whose; the pupil's is plainly meant. The statement names no kinds of text, no length and no titles. The statements under *Taken* (tasks) name kinds of text, and the statement under *Tekstkenmerken* says what makes a text simple.

### Common mistakes

A child reads every word correctly and still cannot answer a question about something the text says. A child takes the content from the title or a picture and does not check it against the text. A child stops reading at the first unknown word instead of reading on.
