## Official wording

De leerling spreekt en schrijft afgestemd op doel, publiek en context.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 3 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil speaks and writes in a way that is adjusted to purpose, audience and context.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes an invitation to the school fair twice, once for the children of the youngest class and once for the neighbours of the school, and chooses other words, another greeting and other information each time. (The pack's example: *je* and *u* are two of the Dutch words for "you", and the child writes *je* to a classmate and *u* to an adult the child does not know.) The child tells the same event to a friend and then in a short talk to a group, and speaks more slowly and more fully the second time.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 3, which is about producing texts. Its items 3 A a to 3 A e name using a suitable approach, working information from different sources into a structured text that names its sources, knowledge about the form of texts, legible writing and intelligible speaking, and revising the text. The sentence names three things that the speaking and writing are adjusted to: purpose, audience and context. It names no purposes, audiences or kinds of text, and no length of a text or a talk. The sentence sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child uses the same words and tone for every reader or listener. A child knows the purpose at the start and forgets it while writing, so a letter that was meant to ask for something ends without the request. A child assumes that the audience knows the people and places that the child knows.
