## Official wording

aanbieden van een schoolbrede set van aanpakken en flexibel inzetbare strategieën bij het ondersteunen van taalactiviteiten in de andere leergebieden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 1 B e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

offering a school-wide set of approaches and flexibly usable strategies in supporting language activities in the other learning areas (*leergebieden*).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 finds the same ways of working in every class and subject: the same steps for a hard text, the same way of planning a piece of writing, the same way of preparing a talk. The child uses them in a geography or nature lesson without having to learn a new method there.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 1 B, which says that the school stimulates the pupil's language development in all learning areas; 1 B sits under core goal 1. Like 1 B, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is offering. "School-wide" says that there is one set for the whole school. The item names no approach and no strategy, and does not say who supports the language activities or who uses the strategies. "The other learning areas" are the learning areas other than Dutch, the learning area of this part of the law. Items 2 A f and 3 A a, which are addressed to the pupil, also name approaches. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often see a strategy as part of the lesson in which they learned it, and do not use it in another subject unless they are reminded.
