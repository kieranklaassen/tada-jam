## Official wording

Kan e-mails of informele brieven schrijven en daarbij meningen en gevoelens uitdrukken.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 2F, Taken / 1. Correspondentie / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can write e-mails or informal letters and express opinions and feelings in them.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who writes at this level writes an e-mail to an aunt or a letter to a friend who has moved, with a greeting, a few paragraphs and a closing. In it the child tells what happened and also what the child thinks and feels about it: that the new sports hall is too small, or that the child misses the friend.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, writing (*Schrijven*), row *1. Correspondentie* (correspondence). The row stands under *Taken* (tasks); this is the first of the two paragraphs in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The kinds of text are e-mails or informal letters; formal letters are a separate statement in the same row. The statement asks that opinions and feelings are expressed in them. It names no reader, no length and no conventions for a letter.

### Common mistakes

A child reports events and leaves out what the child thinks or feels about them. A child forgets the greeting or the closing, or does not say who is writing. A child writes as in a chat message, without full sentences or punctuation, where a letter is asked.
