## Official wording

verkennen van overtuigingen over verschillende talen en taalvariëteiten;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 7 B d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring beliefs about different languages and language varieties;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 listens to the same sentence spoken with different accents, writes down what they think about each speaker, and then asks where that thought comes from. The child collects statements that people make, such as "that dialect sounds friendly" or "you cannot use that language at school", and sorts them into what can be checked and what is an opinion.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 7 B, which says that the pupil explores language variation and language change in the Dutch language area; 7 B sits under core goal 7. The item names what is explored: beliefs (*overtuigingen*, what people hold to be true) about different languages and language varieties. It does not say whose beliefs are meant, the pupil's own or other people's. It names no language or variety, and it does not say which beliefs are right. The verb is exploring, and the item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Pupils most often leave out their own beliefs: they report what others think and do not notice their own first reaction to an accent or a language. Many take a belief for a fact, for example that one language is harder or more beautiful than another.
