## Official wording

Kan een collage, een krant of muurkrant maken.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 2F, Taken / 3. Verslagen, werkstukken, samenvattingen, artikelen / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can make a collage, a newspaper or a wall newspaper (*muurkrant*, a newspaper hung up as a poster).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 collects pictures, headlines and short texts about a topic, some of them written by the child, and arranges and glues them on a large sheet as a collage. For a newspaper or a wall newspaper the child writes short pieces with headlines, places them in columns with pictures, and writes the headlines large enough to be read from a distance.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, writing (*Schrijven*), row *3. Verslagen, werkstukken, samenvattingen, artikelen* (reports, project papers, summaries, articles). The row stands under *Taken* (tasks); this is the second of the two paragraphs in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement names three products: a collage, a newspaper or a wall newspaper. It does not say whether the pupil makes them alone or with others, or on paper or on a computer, and it names no size and no number of texts. It stands under the writing tasks; how much written text the product holds is not stated.

### Common mistakes

A child fills the sheet with pictures and adds little or no text of the child's own. A child writes texts too long or too small to be read on a wall. A child places pieces without order, so a reader cannot see what belongs together or where to begin.
