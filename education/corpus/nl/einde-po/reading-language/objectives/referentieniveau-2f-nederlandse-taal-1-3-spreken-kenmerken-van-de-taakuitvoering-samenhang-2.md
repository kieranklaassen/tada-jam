## Official wording

Gebruikt korte eenvoudige zinnen en verbindt deze door de juiste, eenvoudige voegwoorden en verbindingswoorden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.3 Spreken, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Samenhang / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Uses short simple sentences and connects these by the right, simple conjunctions and linking words.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 explains to the class how the school's paper collection works: "We collect the paper on Friday. First each class brings its box to the hall. Then two pupils weigh it, because we want to know which class has the most. But we do not count wet paper." The sentences are short, and the words "first", "then", "because" and "but" show how they belong together.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Spreken* (speaking), row *Samenhang* (coherence). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the second of two statements in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. It is a fragment without a subject; the pupil is meant. The statement names two things: short simple sentences, and connecting them with conjunctions (*voegwoorden*) and linking words (*verbindingswoorden*) that are both right and simple. It does not ask for long or complex sentences. It lists no words; "first", "then", "because" and "but" in the illustration are the pack's examples. It sets no sentence length.

### Common mistakes

A pupil joins every sentence to the next with "and then", also where "because" or "but" is meant. A pupil uses a linking word that states the wrong relation, for example "so" where a reason follows. A pupil joins many clauses into one long sentence and cannot finish its construction.
