## Official wording

Evalueert de tekst met emotieve argumenten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 2.2 Fictionele, narratieve en literaire teksten, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Evalueren / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Evaluates the text with emotive arguments.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says or writes what it thinks of a story and gives a feeling as the reason: "I think it is a good book because I had to laugh at the brother", or "I did not like the ending, it made me sad that the dog stayed lost".

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Fictionele, narratieve en literaire teksten* (reading fictional, narrative and literary texts), *Evalueren* (evaluating). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the first of two statements in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The arguments are emotive (*emotieve*): they come from what the reader felt. The statement names no other kind of argument, such as an argument about how the story is built or about whether it could really happen. It says "the text" and names no kind of text and no titles.

### Common mistakes

A child says only "nice" or "boring" and names no feeling and no moment in the text. A child retells the story instead of saying what it thinks of it. A child gives its feeling about the subject ("I love horses") and not about this text.
