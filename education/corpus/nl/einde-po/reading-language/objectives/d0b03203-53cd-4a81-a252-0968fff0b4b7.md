## Official wording

De leerling gebruikt taal op een creatieve manier.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 3 B.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil uses language in a creative way.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes a poem that does not rhyme, invents a word for a thing that has no name, tells a story from the point of view of a shoe, and makes a poster in which the letters take the shape of what they say. The child tries a sentence in three ways and keeps the one that sounds best.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 3, which is about producing texts. Its items 3 B a to 3 B d name exploring the creative use of language by others in literary and non-fiction texts, putting one's own ideas, thoughts, experiences, feelings and fantasies into words, experimenting with sounds, words, sentences, literary genres, language rules, language conventions and visual forms, and *waarderen* of creative use of language (appreciating it, or judging its worth). The sentence does not say what "creative" is, and does not say whether the language is spoken or written. It sets no level, no kinds of text and no standard for the result.

### Common mistakes

A child takes "creative" to mean that every result is good, and stops checking whether a reader can follow it. A child copies a known story or rhyme and changes only the names. A child avoids trying a new form for fear of making a mistake.
