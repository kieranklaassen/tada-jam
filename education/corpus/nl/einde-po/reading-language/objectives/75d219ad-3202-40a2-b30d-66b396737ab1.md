## Official wording

De leerling voert gesprekken om tot kennisopbouw, begrip of een aanpak te komen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 B.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil holds conversations in order to arrive at the building of knowledge, understanding or an approach (*aanpak*, a way of tackling something).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 sits with three others around the results of a test with plants, and the four talk through why one plant grew taller until the group has an explanation that each of them can repeat. Two children with different answers to a sum explain their ways of working to each other and find where they differ. A group that has to build a bridge of paper agrees in conversation who does what and in which order.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 4, which is about holding conversations. Its items 4 B a to 4 B e name what is done in such a conversation: putting knowledge, ideas and positions into words, asking, listening and calling into question, accepting or rejecting ideas, and summarising, wording solutions or concluding. The sentence names three outcomes joined by "or": the building of knowledge, understanding, or an approach. It names no subject for these conversations, no group size and no length, and the law sets no level.

### Common mistakes

Children state their ideas one after another without reacting to each other, so no one adds to what another said. A group accepts the first idea, or the idea of the most confident child, without testing it. The conversation stops without anyone saying what was found or agreed.
