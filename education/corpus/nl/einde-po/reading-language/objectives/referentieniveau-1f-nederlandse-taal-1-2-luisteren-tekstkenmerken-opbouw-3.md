## Official wording

Er wordt niet te veel (nieuwe) informatie gelijktijdig geïntroduceerd.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 1F, Tekstkenmerken / Opbouw / 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Not too much (new) information is introduced at the same time.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 listens to an explanation of a new board game. The speaker first tells how the pieces move, gives an example of a move, and only then tells how a piece is captured. The pupil hears each new thing used before the next one comes.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), *Opbouw* (structure, how a text is built). It stands under *Tekstkenmerken* (text features), which describe the texts a pupil listens to at this level, not what the pupil does. The row holds three statements at 1F; the other two are about a simple structure and about information density. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement is about how much information comes at the same time: not too much. The brackets around "new" (*nieuwe*) leave open whether the statement is about all information or about new information in particular; the gloss keeps the brackets. "Too much" is given no number or measure.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error; it describes the text, not the pupil. When several new things do come at once, pupils most often keep the first or the last and leave out the ones in between. A pupil who is still thinking about one new word or fact does not hear the next sentence.
