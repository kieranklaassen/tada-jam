## Official wording

inzetten en uitbreiden van vaardigheden om gesprekken constructief te laten verlopen: luisteren, herkennen van signalen en passend reageren op de gesprekspartner(s);

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 A a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting to use and expanding skills for making conversations go constructively: listening, recognising signals and responding fittingly to the conversation partner(s);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 lets the other person finish and shows with a nod or a short word that they follow. The child notices that a partner frowns, goes quiet or starts to fidget, and reacts to it: asks "Shall I explain it another way?", gives the other a turn, or stops and asks what is wrong.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 4 A, which says that the pupil holds conversations adjusted to purpose, partner or partners and context; 4 A sits under core goal 4. After the colon the item names three skills: listening, recognising signals, and responding fittingly to the partner or partners. It does not say which signals are meant or whose they are, and it does not say what counts as fitting. It asks for two things: using the skills a pupil has, and adding to them. The item names no kind of conversation and sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child hears the words and misses the signal: a partner who says "fine" in a flat voice is taken to agree. A child interrupts as soon as an idea comes up. A child notices that the other person is bored or upset and goes on in the same way.
