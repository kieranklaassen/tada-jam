## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration, taken from the statements this one refers to: a pupil of about 12 gives a short talk to the class. The pupil's pronunciation is clear enough for the class to follow, also with an accent and a hesitation here and there. The sentences are simple and most of them are built correctly.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Spreken* (speaking), *Vloeiendheid, verstaanbaarheid en grammaticale beheersing* (fluency, intelligibility and command of grammar). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement is a cross-reference followed by a plus sign, and it has no content of its own. The reference says that what is worded in the row of the same name under *Gesprekken* at 1F also holds for speaking. That row holds two statements, other records of this lane: one says that the pronunciation is clear enough for the speaker to be followed, and one names a reasonably accurate use of simple sentence constructions. The plus sign says that the statements that follow in this cell are added to them. Two statements follow, other records of this lane: one on posture, intonation and facial expression, and one on pauses, false starts and reformulations. The decree prints the plus sign as a paragraph of its own under the reference; the wording joins it to the reference and that paragraph is no record, so the records of this cell have the positions 1, 3 and 4. The source prints the reference without a full stop.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. The statement only refers to another row and joins the rest of its cell to that row. A reader most often leaves out that row, and takes the two statements after the plus sign for the whole of what 1F says about fluency, intelligibility and grammar in speaking. The typical shortfalls of pupils are noted in the two records under *Gesprekken*.
