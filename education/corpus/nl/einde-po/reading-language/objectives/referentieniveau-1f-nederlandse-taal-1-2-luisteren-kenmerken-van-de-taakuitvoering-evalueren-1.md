## Official wording

Kan een oordeel over een tekst(deel) of televisie- of radioprogramma (of fragment ervan) verwoorden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Evalueren / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can put into words a judgement about a text (or part of a text) or a television or radio programme (or a fragment of it).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: after an episode of a school television programme about recycling, a pupil of about 12 says, "I found it clear, and the part with the quiz went too fast for me." After a talk by a classmate the pupil says which part was interesting and which part was hard to follow.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), *Evalueren* (evaluating). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). It is the only statement in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement asks that the pupil puts a judgement into words. It does not ask for reasons for the judgement, and it names no grounds on which to judge. The brackets widen what is judged: a whole text or a part of it, a whole television or radio programme or a fragment of it. "A text" (*een tekst*) is a general term, and the statement names no particular kind of text; of programmes it names those on television and radio.

### Common mistakes

A pupil retells what the programme or text was about when asked for a judgement of it. A pupil gives only "nice" or "boring" and cannot say about which part. A pupil judges the subject ("I do not like football") instead of the text or the programme.
