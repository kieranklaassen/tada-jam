## Official wording

verwoorden van kennis, ideeën en standpunten met onderbouwing;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 B a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting knowledge, ideas and positions (*standpunten*, opinions held on a matter) into words, with supporting reasons (*onderbouwing*);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says: "I think the ice melted faster in the dark cup, because dark things take up more heat; we saw that with the black T-shirt in the sun." The child tells what they know about a subject, puts forward an idea for a plan or takes a position, and adds a reason, an example or something they read.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 4 B, which says that the pupil holds conversations to arrive at the building of knowledge, understanding or an approach; 4 B sits under core goal 4. The item names three things that are put into words (knowledge, ideas, positions) and one condition: with supporting reasons. It does not say what counts as support, how many reasons are needed or how strong they are, and it sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child gives an opinion without a reason, or repeats the opinion in other words as if that were a reason ("it is better because it is the best"). A child supports a claim with "everyone knows that" or with what a friend thinks. A child knows the reason and does not say it, because it seems obvious.
