## Official wording

Kan aantekeningen maken en overzichtelijk weergeven.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Taken / 2. Formulieren invullen, berichten, advertenties en aantekeningen / 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can make notes and set them out clearly.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 gathers information for a talk and writes down key words and short phrases, not whole sentences. Afterwards the child orders the notes: it puts them under a few headings or in a numbered list, so that it can find each point again.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Formulieren invullen, berichten, advertenties en aantekeningen* (filling in forms, messages, advertisements and notes). It is task 2 under *Taken* (tasks) and the third of three statements in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement has two parts: making notes, and presenting them in a well-ordered way (*overzichtelijk*). It does not say what the notes are made from, a text that is read or something that is heard, and it names no form such as a list or a scheme.

### Common mistakes

A child tries to write down everything word for word and cannot keep up. A child writes notes so short that it no longer understands them a day later. A child leaves the notes in the order in which they came and does not group them.
