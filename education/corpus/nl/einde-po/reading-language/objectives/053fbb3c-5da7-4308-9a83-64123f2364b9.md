## Official wording

inzetten en uitbreiden van kennis over de vorm van teksten: tekstsoorten, tekststructuren, verteltechnieken;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 3 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting to use and extending knowledge about the form of texts: kinds of text, text structures, narrative techniques;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who writes instructions numbers the steps and starts each step with a verb; in a letter the child writes a greeting, the message and a closing; for a story the child chooses who tells it and starts at an exciting moment. In an explanation the child uses linking words to show how the sentences connect (the pack's examples in Dutch: *omdat*, because, *daardoor*, as a result, *maar*, but).

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 3 A, which says that the pupil speaks and writes in a way adjusted to purpose, audience and context; 3 A sits under core goal 3. The item lists three things that knowledge about the form of texts covers: kinds of text, text structures and narrative techniques. Item 2 A c, for understanding texts, has the same list with literary genres added; this item does not name them. It names no particular kind of text, structure or technique, so this item requires none in particular. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child writes every text as a story in the order of time, also an explanation or a request. A child writes one block of text without paragraphs. A child changes the narrator or the tense halfway through a story without meaning to.
