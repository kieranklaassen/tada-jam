## Official wording

Kan bespreken wat er gedaan moet worden en bijdragen aan de planning.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 2F, Taken / 1. Deelnemen aan discussie en overleg / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can discuss what has to be done and contribute to the planning.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 sits with three classmates who have to make a class newspaper in two weeks. The pupil helps to list the jobs (interviews, photographs, layout), says which of them has to be finished first, and proposes: "If we do the interviews this week, we can write on Monday."

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), row *Deelnemen aan discussie en overleg* (taking part in discussion and in *overleg*). It stands under *Taken* (tasks), as task 1, and is the first of two statements in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. *Overleg* is a talk held to arrange or decide something together; "consultation" and "deliberation" each cover part of it. The statement names two things: discussing what has to be done, and contributing to the planning. It asks for a contribution to the planning, not for making the plan alone. It does not say with whom the pupil talks, what kind of work is planned or how many people take part.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is the planning: they talk about what has to be done and do not say who does it, in which order, or by when. Some pupils agree with every proposal and make none of their own.
