## Official wording

geven van een waardeoordeel over gelezen of beluisterde boeken en teksten op basis van literaire genres, eigen interesse en belevingswereld;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8 A b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

giving a value judgement about books and texts that have been read or listened to, on the basis of literary genres, own interest and world of experience (*belevingswereld*, the world as the pupil experiences it);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 finishes a book and tells someone whether it was good, with a reason: "for a detective story the solution came too early", "I ride horses myself, so I wanted to know how it ended", or "the family in the book lives like mine". The child compares the book with other books of the same genre that the child has read.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 8 A, which says that the pupil develops a reading preference of their own; 8 A sits under core goal 8. The item names three grounds for the judgement: literary genres, own interest and *belevingswereld*; it does not say whether one judgement uses all three. It covers books and texts the pupil has read or listened to. It does not say whether the judgement is spoken or written, and it names no titles and no reading level.

### Common mistakes

A pupil says "good" or "boring" and gives no ground. A pupil retells the story where a judgement is asked. A pupil judges a text by what its genre does not offer (a poem is called bad because it has no story).
