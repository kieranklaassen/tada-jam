## Official wording

Poëzie en liedjes hebben meestal een verhalende inhoud en een emotionele lading.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 2.2 Fictionele, narratieve en literaire teksten, niveau 2F, Teksten / Tekstkenmerken / 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Poetry and songs usually have a narrative content and an emotional charge.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who reads at this level reads or listens to a poem or a song text that tells something that happens, for example about a grandfather who moves to another town, and that carries a feeling such as missing someone or joy. The child says what happens in the poem and which feeling belongs to it, and points to the words that carry the feeling.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, reading fictional, narrative and literary texts (*Lezen fictionele, narratieve en literaire teksten*), row *Tekstkenmerken* (text characteristics). The row stands under *Teksten* (texts); this is the third of the three paragraphs in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement describes the poetry and songs read at this level, not what the pupil does. "Usually" (*meestal*) is the statement's own word: most of these poems and songs, not all, have a narrative content and an emotional charge. The statement names no forms of poetry, no rhyme or metre, no length and no titles.

### Common mistakes

This statement describes texts, so it is not a skill with a typical error. A designer who chooses a poem or a song for this level looks for one that tells something that happens and carries a feeling; "usually" leaves room for one that does not. What pupils most often leave out is one of the two: they look only for the rhyme and do not ask what the poem tells, or they name the events of a song text and not the feeling it carries.
