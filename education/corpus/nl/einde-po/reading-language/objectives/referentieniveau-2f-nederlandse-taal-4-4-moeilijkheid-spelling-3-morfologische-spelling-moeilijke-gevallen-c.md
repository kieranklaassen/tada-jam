## Official wording

c) ’s in ’s ochtends

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 4.4 Moeilijkheid, niveau 2F, Spelling / 3. Morfologische spelling / Moeilijke gevallen / c).

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

c) *’s* in *’s ochtends* (in the morning)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 at this level writes the time words *’s ochtends*, *’s middags*, *’s avonds* and *’s nachts* (in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening, at night) with an apostrophe before the first *s*, a space after it, and an *s* at the end of the word. At the start of a sentence the child keeps *’s* small and gives the next word the capital letter: *’s Avonds lees ik.* (In the evening I read.).

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, terms and language care, table of difficulty (*Moeilijkheid*), row c) of the difficult cases (*Moeilijke gevallen*) under *Spelling*, 3. *Morfologische spelling* (morphological spelling, the spelling of word forms such as plurals and diminutives). In this table (Table 2 of the decree) the row has a plus sign in the column for level 2F. The table's caption describes its levels as mastery: 75% of all pupils in the age group or level group have an 80% chance of getting it right. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The row names *’s* and gives one expression, *’s ochtends*. It states no rule in words and does not say which other expressions are meant. The decree's running list of spelling rules in section 4.3.1, which carries no level and is not a record of the pack, gives *’s nachts* (at night) and, for the start of a sentence, *’s Nachts*, with the capital letter on the word after *’s*. The pack's further examples, all correct Dutch: *’s middags* (in the afternoon), *’s avonds* (in the evening), *’s zomers* (in summer), *’s winters* (in winter); and at the start of a sentence, *’s Avonds lees ik een boek.* (In the evening I read a book.).

### Common mistakes

A child writes the expression as one word or puts the apostrophe in the wrong place (the wrong *sochtends*, *s’ochtends* and *’sochtends*). A child leaves off the final *s* (the wrong *’s ochtend*). A child writes a capital *S* at the start of a sentence (the wrong *’S avonds*), where *’s Avonds* is right.
