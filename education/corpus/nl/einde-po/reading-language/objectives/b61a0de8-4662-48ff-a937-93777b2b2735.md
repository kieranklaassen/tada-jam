## Official wording

verwoorden van het proces: de gemaakte keuzes in aanpak en strategieën tijdens en na de uitvoering van een taalactiviteit;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 5 A b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting the process into words: the choices made in approach and strategies during and after the carrying out of a language activity;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

While writing, a child of 11 or 12 says: "I am making a list of key words first, because I do not yet know what I want to say." Afterwards the child says: "I read the difficult paragraph twice and looked up two words; skipping it did not work."

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 5 A, which says that the pupil reflects on the process and evaluates the product of a language activity; 5 A sits under core goal 5. After the colon the item says what the process is: the choices made in approach and strategies. It names two moments, during and after the carrying out of a language activity; the Dutch leaves open whether these are the moments of the choices or the moments of putting them into words. It lists no approaches and no strategies, and it sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child retells what they did in order ("first I wrote, then I was done") and names no choice and no reason. A child cannot recall the approach afterwards, because they did not notice it while working. A child names a strategy by the label learned in class without having used it.
