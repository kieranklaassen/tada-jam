## Official wording

Kan redelijk vloeiend en helder ervaringen, gebeurtenissen, meningen, verwachtingen en gevoelens onder woorden brengen over onderwerpen uit de (beroeps)opleiding en van maatschappelijke aard.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.3 Spreken, niveau 2F, Algemene omschrijving Spreken / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can put experiences, events, opinions, expectations and feelings into words reasonably fluently and clearly, about subjects from (vocational) education (*(beroeps)opleiding*, schooling or training for an occupation) and of a societal nature.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 stands before the class and talks about the class's visit to a care home. The pupil tells what happened and what it was like to talk with the residents, says what he or she thinks about young and old people meeting more often, what he or she expects of the next visit and how the afternoon felt. The pupil speaks without long stops, and the class can follow the account.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Spreken* (speaking), row *Algemene omschrijving* (general description). It is the whole general description of the skill at this level; the task and the features of task performance are other records. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement names five things put into words: experiences, events, opinions, expectations and feelings. Its qualifier *redelijk* (reasonably) is the level: reasonably fluently, not without any stop. The Dutch leaves open whether *redelijk* also applies to "clearly", and the gloss keeps the same word order. The statement names two areas of subject: *(beroeps)opleiding*, and subjects *van maatschappelijke aard* (of a societal nature: that concern society). The brackets around *beroeps* (vocational) are in the source; the statement does not say what *opleiding* covers for a pupil in primary school. It lists no subjects, names no audience and sets no speaking time.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out are the expectations and the feelings: they tell what happened, give an opinion and stop there. On a subject of a societal nature many give an opinion and no experience or event to go with it.
