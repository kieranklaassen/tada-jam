## Official wording

De school stimuleert de taalcompetentie van leerlingen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The school stimulates the language competence of pupils.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child near the end of primary school reads, writes, speaks and listens through the whole school day and in every subject: the child reads a book of the child's own choice at a fixed time, discusses a history text, writes a report of an experiment and gives a talk. The child finds books in the classroom and gets time and encouragement to use language.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 1 itself. It is addressed to the school: it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. Two goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 1 A (a rich language and reading environment) and 1 B (the pupil's language development in all learning areas). The law places this goal in the overarching domain (*Domein: overkoepelend*) of the part on Dutch. The sentence does not say what language competence consists of or how the school stimulates it, and it sets no level. Core goals 2 to 9 are other records of this lane.

### Common mistakes

This sentence is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often use language fully in the Dutch lesson and leave it out elsewhere: they answer in single words in other subjects and read only when a task asks for it.
