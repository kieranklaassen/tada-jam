## Official wording

De leerling schrijft om tot kennisopbouw of begrip te komen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 3 C.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil writes in order to build knowledge or to reach understanding.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 keeps a notebook during lessons about the water cycle: the child writes down what is new, draws a diagram with arrows, notes a question, and afterwards writes in a few sentences what the child now understands. The child writes this in order to learn, and uses it later to explain the subject.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 3, which is about producing texts. Its items 3 C a to 3 C e name giving the main points and minor points of, impressions of and questions about content that has been read, watched or listened to, summarising content that has been read, putting into words, substantiating and ordering thoughts, insights and knowledge in a text or diagram, school language and subject language, and legible writing by hand and on a keyboard. The sentence names writing only; speaking is not named in 3 C. The purpose is joined by "or": building knowledge or reaching understanding. The sentence does not say in which subjects the pupil writes, and sets no level and no length of text.

### Common mistakes

A child copies from the board or the book word for word and takes that for learning. A child writes down facts and leaves out how they connect. A child writes only when a finished text is asked for, and makes no notes while reading or listening.
