## Official wording

Kan aantekeningen maken. Kan de informatie gestructureerd weergeven.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Samenvatten / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can make notes. Can set out the information in a structured way.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 listens to the teacher explaining the stages of the water cycle and writes a key word for each stage, with an arrow from each to the next. Afterwards the pupil uses the notes to tell a classmate who was absent what was explained, stage by stage in the same order.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), *Samenvatten* (summarising). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). It is the only statement in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The cell holds two short sentences: making notes, and setting out the information in a structured way. It does not say in what form the notes are made, whether the information is set out aloud or in writing, or what kind of structure is meant. The row is named after summarising, but the word "summary" is not in the statement. It sets no length of text; the statements under *Tekstkenmerken* (text features) of *Luisteren* at 1F, other records of this lane, describe the texts.

### Common mistakes

A pupil tries to write down whole sentences, writes too slowly to keep up, and misses what is said next. A pupil notes single words without showing how they belong together, and cannot use the notes afterwards. A pupil gives the information in the order in which they remember it, not in an order that a listener can follow.
