## Official wording

Besteedt aandacht aan de opmaak van de tekst (handschrift, bladspiegel, eventueel beeldende elementen en kleur).

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Leesbaarheid / 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Pays attention to the layout of the text (handwriting, page layout (*bladspiegel*, how the text is placed on the page), possibly visual elements and colour).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes so that each letter can be read and sets the text straight on the page, with the title above it. In a project paper the child adds a drawing next to the part it belongs to and writes the title in colour.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Leesbaarheid* (readability). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the third of three statements in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The brackets name handwriting (*handschrift*), page layout, and, after "possibly" (*eventueel*), visual elements and colour. The wording does not settle how far "possibly" reaches: over visual elements and colour together, or over visual elements only. Handwriting is named in the brackets; the statement does not say whether the text is written by hand or typed. The statement asks for attention to the layout and sets no standard for it.

### Common mistakes

A child's letters get smaller and closer together towards the end of a line or a page. A child fills the page from edge to edge. A child uses so many colours and drawings that the text is harder to read.
