## Official wording

verkennen hoe woordgebruik, stijlmiddelen en opbouw van teksten de betekenis beïnvloeden;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 6 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring how word use, stylistic devices (*stijlmiddelen*) and structure of texts influence the meaning;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 compares two sentences about the same house, one with *huisje* (little house) and one with *krot* (hovel), and says what each makes a reader think (the pack's example). The child finds a comparison or a repetition in a poem and says what it does there. The child cuts a short text into its paragraphs, lays them in another order, and says what changes for a reader.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 6 A, which says that the pupil considers the relation between the form and the meaning of language; 6 A sits under core goal 6. The item names three things that influence the meaning: word use, stylistic devices and the structure of texts. It lists no stylistic device (no comparison, metaphor or repetition is named), no kind of text and no text structure. The verb is exploring. The item sets no level and no text length.

### Common mistakes

A child takes figurative language literally. A child treats two words with nearly the same meaning as fully the same and does not notice the feeling that goes with a word. A child names a device ("that is a comparison") and cannot say what it does in the text.
