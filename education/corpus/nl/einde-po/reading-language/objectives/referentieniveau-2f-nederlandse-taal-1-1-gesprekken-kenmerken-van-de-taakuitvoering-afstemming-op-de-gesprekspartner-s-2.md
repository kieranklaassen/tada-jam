## Official wording

Kan het verschil tussen formele en informele situaties hanteren.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op de gesprekspartner(s) / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can handle (*hanteren*, to use or deal with) the difference between formal and informal situations.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 says to a friend in the playground "Hey, are you coming?" and a few minutes later says to the school's caretaker "Good morning, may I ask you something?", with *u* (the polite Dutch form of "you"). Talking with a visitor from the municipality who comes to the class, the pupil greets the visitor, uses *u* and leaves out playground words.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), row *Afstemming op de gesprekspartner(s)* (attuning to the conversation partner or partners). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the second of three statements in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement names the difference between formal and informal situations and asks that the pupil can handle it. It names no situations of either kind and no forms of address or words that belong to them; the polite form *u* in the illustration is the pack's example. A statement with the same wording stands under *Spreken*, row *Afstemming op publiek*, another record of this lane.

### Common mistakes

A pupil speaks to an adult he or she does not know as to a friend, with *je* (the informal "you") and playground words. A pupil is so formal with classmates in a group task that the talk becomes stiff. A pupil starts formally and goes back to informal words after a few sentences.
