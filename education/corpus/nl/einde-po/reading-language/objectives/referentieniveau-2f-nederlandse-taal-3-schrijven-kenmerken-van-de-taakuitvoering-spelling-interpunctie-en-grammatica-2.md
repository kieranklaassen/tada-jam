## Official wording

Vertoont een redelijke grammaticale beheersing.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Spelling, interpunctie en grammatica / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Shows a reasonable grammatical command.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who writes at this level writes sentences that are mostly built correctly: the verb agrees with the subject (*de kinderen lopen*, the children walk), the words stand in the Dutch order, and a clause with *omdat* (because) ends on its verb. Errors still occur, mostly in long sentences.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, writing (*Schrijven*), row *Spelling, interpunctie en grammatica* (spelling, punctuation and grammar). The row stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (characteristics of task performance); this is the second of the two paragraphs in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. "Reasonable" (*redelijke*) is the whole measure: the statement does not ask for full command, and it gives no list of constructions and no number of errors. It stands under the characteristics of task performance for writing, so it concerns grammar in the pupil's own written texts. Spelling and punctuation are covered by the cross-reference that is the other paragraph of this cell.

### Common mistakes

A child makes the verb agree with the nearest noun instead of the subject, as in the wrong *de lijst met namen liggen op tafel* where *de lijst met namen ligt op tafel* (the list of names lies on the table) is right. A child uses the wrong reference word for a *het*-word, for example the wrong *het huis die* where *het huis dat* (the house that) is right. A child writes a long sentence in which the verb of the main clause is missing or comes twice.
