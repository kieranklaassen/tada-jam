## Official wording

stimuleren van aandacht voor taalverzorging en taalgebruik in alle leergebieden;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 1 B d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

stimulating attention to language care (*taalverzorging*, the correct form of language, such as spelling and punctuation) and language use in all learning areas (*leergebieden*);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 rereads an answer in a history lesson or a report in a nature lesson before handing it in, corrects the spelling and the full stops, and checks that the wording is clear and fits the reader, as the child does in a Dutch lesson.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 1 B, which says that the school stimulates the pupil's language development in all learning areas; 1 B sits under core goal 1. Like 1 B, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil: the school stimulates attention, and the item sets no standard of correctness for the pupil. Two things are named: language care and language use; item 3 A e names the same pair for revising a text. The law does not say here what language care covers; the bracketed explanation in the gloss is the pack's. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often apply the rules of spelling and punctuation in the Dutch lesson and leave them out in other subjects, where they see the content as the only thing that matters.
