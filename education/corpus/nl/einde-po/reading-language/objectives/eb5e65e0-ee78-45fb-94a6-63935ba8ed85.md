## Official wording

beoordelen van het product van de taalactiviteit aan de hand van aangereikte criteria;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 5 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

assessing the product of the language activity by means of criteria that have been provided (*aangereikte criteria*);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 holds a finished letter next to a list of four points, for example "the purpose is in the first paragraph" and "each paragraph has one subject". The child ticks what is met and points at the place in the text that shows it. The child does the same with a recording of a talk.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 5 A, which says that the pupil reflects on the process and evaluates the product of a language activity; 5 A sits under core goal 5. The item sets one condition: the criteria are provided (*aangereikte*, handed to the pupil). It does not say by whom, how many criteria there are, or what they are about. Making criteria is not mentioned. The item does not say whose product is assessed, and it sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child ticks every criterion as met without checking it in the text. A child judges by a general impression, or by liking the subject, and ignores the criteria. A child reads a criterion narrowly: "has paragraphs" is ticked because there are blank lines, whether or not each paragraph has one subject.
