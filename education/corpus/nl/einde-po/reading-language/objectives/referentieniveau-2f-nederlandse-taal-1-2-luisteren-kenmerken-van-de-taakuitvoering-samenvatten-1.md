## Official wording

Kan een eenvoudige tekst beknopt samenvatten (voor zichzelf).

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Samenvatten / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can summarise a simple text concisely (for oneself).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 listens to a short, simple explanation by the teacher of how a lock in a canal lets a ship go up. Afterwards the pupil writes a short note in a notebook, or says it quietly, with the steps in order and without the side remarks. The summary is for the pupil's own use, to remember the explanation later.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), row *Samenvatten* (summarising). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance) and is the only statement in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement has three qualifiers: the text is *eenvoudig* (simple), the summary is *beknopt* (concise), and it is made *voor zichzelf* (for oneself), which stands in brackets in the source. The statement does not ask for a summary made for other people. The text is a spoken text, since the statement stands under listening. The statement does not say whether the summary is spoken, written or only thought, sets no length for the text or the summary, and names no kind of text.

### Common mistakes

A pupil retells the text from the start in full and stops before the end is reached. A pupil keeps the details and examples that were interesting and leaves out the main point. A pupil notes separate words heard in the text and cannot say afterwards how they belong together.
