## Official wording

ontvangen en geven van feedback;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 5 A a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

receiving and giving feedback;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a classmate's story and says one thing that works and one thing that is unclear, pointing at the place in the text. The child hears what a classmate says about their own text without arguing back, asks what is meant, and decides what to change.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 5 A, which says that the pupil reflects on the process and evaluates the product of a language activity; 5 A sits under core goal 5. The item names two directions, receiving and giving, and puts receiving first. It does not say who gives the feedback or who receives it, what the feedback is about, or in what form it is given, and it names no rules for feedback. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child gives feedback that is only praise ("nice") or only a verdict ("boring"), with nothing to point at. A child comments on spelling and handwriting and says nothing about the content. A child takes feedback as criticism of themselves and defends the text, or changes everything a classmate mentions without thinking about it.
