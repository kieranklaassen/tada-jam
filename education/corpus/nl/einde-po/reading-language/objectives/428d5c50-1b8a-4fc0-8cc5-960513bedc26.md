## Official wording

verwoorden van persoonlijke leeservaring en beleving na het lezen van literatuur;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8 B a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting into words personal reading experience and felt experience (*beleving*, how the reader lived through the text) after reading literature;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 closes a book and tells someone how the reading went: "I read the last three chapters in one evening", "I had to stop at the part with the dog", "I saw the island in my head". The child also writes it in a reading diary, or draws the scene the child remembers best and says why.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 8 B, which says that the pupil explores the value of literature; 8 B sits under core goal 8. The item is about what the pupil experienced, and it places the putting into words after the reading. It names reading only; listening is not mentioned in this item. It does not say whether in speech or in writing, or to whom. It names no kinds of text and no titles.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is their own experience: a pupil gives a summary of the story, or a mark out of ten, and says nothing about what the reading was like. Many pupils have few words for it beyond "nice", "exciting" and "boring".
