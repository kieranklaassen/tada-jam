## Official wording

Kan informatie en meningen interpreteren voor zover deze dicht bij de leerling staan.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.2 Luisteren, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Interpreteren / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can interpret information and opinions in so far as these are close to the pupil.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 hears a speaker from the municipality tell the class that the playing field next to the school will become a car park, and that "this is the best solution for everyone". The pupil works out what the information means close to home: no more football there after school. The pupil also tells a classmate what the speaker's opinion comes to: according to the speaker, a car park there is better for everyone than a field.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Luisteren* (listening), *Interpreteren* (interpreting). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). The row has one other statement at 1F, on connecting information from the text with own knowledge and experiences. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The condition "in so far as these are close to the pupil" (*voor zover deze dicht bij de leerling staan*) bounds the statement: it covers information and opinions near the pupil's own life, and it says nothing about information and opinions that are further away. The statement does not say what interpreting consists of; working out what a piece of information means for oneself and saying what an opinion comes to are the pack's examples. It names no topics.

### Common mistakes

A pupil repeats what the speaker said word for word and cannot say what it means. A pupil hears what was said and does not work out what it means, for example what an announced change means for their own week.
