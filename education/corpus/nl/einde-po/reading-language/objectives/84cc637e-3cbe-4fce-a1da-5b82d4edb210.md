## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht in genrekenmerken van literatuur.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 9 B.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight into genre features of literature.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 gets a poem, a page of a novel and a page of a play and sorts them, saying how each can be recognised: short lines with white around them, running text in paragraphs, names of speakers followed by what they say. The child points to a rhyme, to a comparison, and to a page of a picture book where the picture tells part of the story.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 9, which is about insight into literature. Its items 9 B a to 9 B c name distinguishing poetry, prose and drama texts on the basis of simple genre features, naming characteristic language use (figurative language, rhyme and rhythm), and naming characteristic visual elements (illustrations, design). The sentence itself lists no genres and no features. It names no titles and no level of text.

### Common mistakes

A pupil thinks every poem rhymes and that a text without rhyme is not a poem. A pupil uses "genre" for the subject only (a horse book, a football book) and does not look at the form of the text.
