## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht in literatuur.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 9.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight into literature.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school tells how a story is built: who the main character is, what that character wants, where and when it happens, and how the writer builds up suspense. The child takes a poem, a chapter of a novel and a scene of a play, says which is which, and says how that can be seen.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 9 itself. Two goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 9 A (narrative texts) and 9 B (genre features of literature). Experience with literature is core goal 8. The goal names no titles, no reading level and no number of texts, and it does not say how the insight is shown. The reference levels 1F and 2F, which are other records of this lane (section 2.2 *Fictionele, narratieve en literaire teksten*, fictional, narrative and literary texts), are worded in more detail.

### Common mistakes

A pupil retells the story from beginning to end where a description of how it is built is asked. A pupil knows a term such as main character or rhyme as a word to learn and cannot point to it in a text the pupil has just read.
