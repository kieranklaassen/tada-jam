## Official wording

lezen en beluisteren van boeken en teksten, van verschillende schrijvers en literaire genres;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8 A a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reading and listening to books and texts, by different writers and of different literary genres;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads an adventure novel alone, listens to a chapter of a historical story that is read aloud or played as an audiobook, and reads a few poems from a collection for children. Over some months the child has books by several writers in hand, and not only the next part of one series.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 8 A, which says that the pupil develops a reading preference of their own; 8 A sits under core goal 8. The item counts listening as well as reading, and texts as well as books. It asks for different writers and different literary genres and does not say which or how many. It names no titles and no reading level, and it does not say who reads aloud or in what form the pupil listens.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is the variety: a pupil stays with one writer or one series. Many pupils do not count listening to a book as an experience with books, and many leave poetry out altogether.
