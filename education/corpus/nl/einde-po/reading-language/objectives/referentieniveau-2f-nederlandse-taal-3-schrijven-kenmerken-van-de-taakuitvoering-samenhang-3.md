## Official wording

Maakt soms nog onduidelijke verwijzingen en fouten in de structuur van de tekst.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 2F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Samenhang / 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Still sometimes makes unclear references and errors in the structure of the text.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who writes at this level writes a text that is mostly coherent, but in places a reader has to stop: *hij* (he) can point to either of two boys named just before, or *daar* (there) points to a place not yet named. Sometimes a paragraph stands in the wrong place or a point returns after the conclusion.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, writing (*Schrijven*), row *Samenhang* (coherence). The row stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (characteristics of task performance); this is the third of the three paragraphs in its cell. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The statement describes errors that still occur at this level; it is not a task. "Sometimes" and "still" (*soms nog*) are the measure: the statement gives no number of errors. It names two kinds: unclear references, and errors in the structure of the text. It does not say that the pupil finds or repairs these errors.

### Common mistakes

A child uses *hij*, *zij* or *die* (he, she, that one) when two persons or things could be meant. A child refers with *dit* or *dat* (this, that) to something that was never written down, only thought. A child adds a new point after the conclusion.
