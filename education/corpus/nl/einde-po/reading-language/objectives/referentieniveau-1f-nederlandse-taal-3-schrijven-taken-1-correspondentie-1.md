## Official wording

Kan een briefje, kaart of e-mail schrijven om informatie te vragen, iemand te bedanken, te feliciteren, uit te nodigen e.d.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Taken / 1. Correspondentie / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can write a note (*briefje*, a short letter), card or e-mail to ask for information, to thank, congratulate or invite someone, and the like.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes a card to thank its grandmother for a present, an e-mail to a sports club to ask on which day training starts, and an invitation to a party with the date, the time and the address.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Correspondentie* (correspondence). It is task 1 under *Taken* (tasks). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names three forms, a note, a card or an e-mail, and four purposes: asking for information, thanking, congratulating and inviting. "And the like" (*e.d.*) leaves the list of purposes open. The statement names no length. The conventions of a letter are statements under *Afstemming op publiek* and *Leesbaarheid*.

### Common mistakes

A child leaves the date, the time or the place out of an invitation. A child does not say who the message is from. A child asks for information and does not say exactly what it wants to know.
