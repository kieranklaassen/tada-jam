## Official wording

Kan eenvoudige betogende teksten lezen, zoals voorkomend in schoolboeken voor taal- en zaakvakken, maar ook advertenties, reclames, huis- aan huisbladen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 2.1 Zakelijke teksten, niveau 1F, Taken / 3. Lezen van betogende teksten / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can read simple argumentative texts, such as occur in schoolbooks for language and for the subject lessons (*zaakvakken*: geography, history, nature study), but also advertisements, advertising, door-to-door papers (*huis-aan-huisbladen*, free local papers).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads a short text in a schoolbook that argues that children should cycle to school, and afterwards answers a question about something the text says. The child reads an advertising leaflet for a toy and tells what the leaflet says about the toy.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Zakelijke teksten* (reading factual texts), *Lezen van betogende teksten* (reading argumentative texts). It is task 3 under *Taken* (tasks). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The texts are simple (*eenvoudige*). After "such as" (*zoals*) the statement gives as examples argumentative texts as they occur in schoolbooks for language and for the subject lessons, "but also" advertisements (*advertenties*), advertising (*reclames*) and door-to-door papers. They are examples of the level, not a limit. The statement speaks of reading; it does not ask the pupil to weigh the arguments. The source prints *huis- aan huisbladen* with the hyphen and the space in that way; *huis-aan-huisbladen* is plainly meant. The statement names no length and no titles.

### Common mistakes

A child reads every sentence and still cannot answer a question about something the text says. A child reports its own opinion on the subject as if it were the writer's.
