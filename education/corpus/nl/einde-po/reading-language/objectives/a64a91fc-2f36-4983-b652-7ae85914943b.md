## Official wording

stimuleren van taalproductie en interactie in alle leergebieden;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 1 B c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

stimulating language production and interaction in all learning areas (*leergebieden*);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 discusses an arithmetic problem with a partner, writes down a prediction before an experiment, asks a question after a classmate's talk and answers in full sentences. In every subject the child speaks and writes, and reacts to what others say.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 1 B, which says that the school stimulates the pupil's language development in all learning areas; 1 B sits under core goal 1. Like 1 B, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. Two things are named: language production and interaction. The item does not say whether the production is spoken or written, and names no form and no amount. It sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often answer in a single word or wait for a classmate to answer; in group work they speak in turn without reacting to each other.
