## Official wording

vergelijkend beoordelen van bronnen op basis van hun betrouwbaarheid.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 2 C c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

judging sources by comparison on the basis of their reliability.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 puts two or three sources on the same question in order from most to least reliable and gives a reason for each place: who made it, how old it is, whether it says where its information comes from, and whether the other sources say the same.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 2 C, which says that the pupil explores the reliability of different sources; 2 C sits under core goal 2. The judging is by comparison (*vergelijkend*): sources are compared with each other, and the item does not ask for a verdict on one source alone. Reliability is the only ground named. This item names no criteria for reliability; item 2 C a names maker, kind of text and date of publication as features of a source. The goal sentence above it uses the verb "explores". The item sets no level and no number of sources.

### Common mistakes

A child orders the sources by which is most pleasant to read or most complete. A child gives an order and no reasons. A child takes it as proof that several sources say the same, without seeing that one has copied from another.
