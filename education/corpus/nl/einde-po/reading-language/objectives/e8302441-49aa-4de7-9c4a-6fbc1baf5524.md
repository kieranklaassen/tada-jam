## Official wording

reflecteren op de waarde, inhoud en vorm van teksten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 2 B e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reflecting on the value, content and form of texts.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 has finished a book or an article and says what it meant to the child, what the child thinks of what it says, and how it is made: the pictures, the layout, the short chapters, the rhyme. The child compares it with a text read earlier and says which of the two the child remembers better and why.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 2 B, which says that the pupil evaluates and reflects on non-fiction and literary texts; 2 B sits under core goal 2. Three things are named: value, content and form. The item does not say what kind of value is meant or for whom (for the reader or for others). It does not say whether the pupil reflects in speech or in writing. It sets no level and no text length, and names no kinds of text beyond the two groups named in 2 B.

### Common mistakes

A child says only whether the child liked the text. A child speaks about the content and leaves out the form. A child repeats another person's view of the text as the child's own.
