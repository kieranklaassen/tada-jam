## Official wording

De leerling verkent de waarde van literatuur.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8 B.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil explores the value of literature.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 finishes a story and tells someone what it was like to read: where it was sad, where it was funny, which character the child wanted to be. The child says what the story showed about the child's own life, about how other people feel, or about a country or a time the child did not know.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 8, which is about gaining experience with literature. Its items 8 B a to 8 B d name putting into words the personal reading experience, and the insights and knowledge gained about oneself, about others, and about the world and cultures. The verb is exploring. The sentence does not say what the value of literature is; the items name what the pupil puts into words. It names no titles and no kinds of text.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is the link between the book and themselves or other people: a pupil retells the events and stops there. Many pupils see reading only as practice for a test or as a way to find facts.
