## Official wording

Kan eenvoudige standaardformulieren invullen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Taken / 2. Formulieren invullen, berichten, advertenties en aantekeningen / 2.

Can fill in simple standard forms.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 fills in a form to join a library: surname, first name, date of birth, street and house number, postcode and town, each in its own box. The child reads the label of each box before writing in it.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Formulieren invullen, berichten, advertenties en aantekeningen* (filling in forms, messages, advertisements and notes). It is task 2 under *Taken* (tasks) and the second of three statements in this row at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The forms are simple (*eenvoudige*) and standard. The statement names no forms and no number of fields.

### Common mistakes

A child writes its first name in the box for the surname. A child writes today's date where the date of birth is asked. A child skips a box it does not understand and hands the form in incomplete.
