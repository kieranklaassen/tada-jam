## Official wording

toepassen van gespreks- en taalconventies, passend bij de gespreksvorm.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 A e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

applying conversation conventions and language conventions (*gespreks- en taalconventies*, the accepted ways of talking and wording), fitting the form of conversation.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says their name at the start of a telephone call and closes with a greeting. In a class meeting the child raises a hand and waits for the chairperson; in a debate the child keeps to the speaking time and answers the other side; in an interview the child thanks the guest at the close.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 4 A, which says that the pupil holds conversations adjusted to purpose, partner or partners and context; 4 A sits under core goal 4. The item names two kinds of convention, for conversation and for language, and ties them to the form of conversation (*gespreksvorm*). It lists neither the conventions nor the forms of conversation: no telephone call, debate, interview or meeting is named. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child uses the rules of one form in another: raises a hand in a conversation of two, or calls out in a meeting that has a chairperson. A child leaves out the opening or the closing of a telephone call or an interview. A child says the familiar *je* where the form asks for the polite *u* (the pack's example of a Dutch language convention).
