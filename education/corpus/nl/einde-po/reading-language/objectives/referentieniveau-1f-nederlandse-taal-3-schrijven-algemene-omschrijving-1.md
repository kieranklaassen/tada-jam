## Official wording

Kan korte, eenvoudige teksten schrijven over alledaagse onderwerpen of over onderwerpen uit de leefwereld.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 3 Schrijven, niveau 1F, Algemene omschrijving / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can write short, simple texts about everyday subjects or about subjects from the world of experience (*leefwereld*).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 writes a short text about its weekend, about its pet, or about the rules of a game it plays: several sentences that a reader who was not there can follow. The child reads the text over and adds who was there and where it happened.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Schrijven* (writing), *Algemene omschrijving* (general description). It is the whole general description of this skill at 1F. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The texts are short (*korte*) and simple (*eenvoudige*), and their subjects are everyday ones (*alledaagse*) or ones from the *leefwereld*. The Dutch says *de leefwereld* without saying whose; the pupil's is plainly meant. "Short" is not given as a number of words or lines. The statement names no kinds of text; the statements under *Taken* (tasks) do.

### Common mistakes

A child writes as it talks: one long sentence joined with *en toen* ("and then"). A child leaves out what the reader does not know, such as who the people are or where the events happened. A child stops when the page or the time is used up, in the middle of what it was telling.
