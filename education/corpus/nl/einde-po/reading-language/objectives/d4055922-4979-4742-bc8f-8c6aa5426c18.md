## Official wording

hanteren van een passende aanpak;

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using a suitable approach;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who has to write a report first notes what is already known, collects what is missing, puts the points in an order and only then writes; a short message the child writes at once and rereads. Before a talk the child makes cards with key words and practises aloud. The child chooses the way of working that fits the task.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 3 A, which says that the pupil speaks and writes in a way adjusted to purpose, audience and context; 3 A sits under core goal 3. The item gives no example of an approach; the examples above are the pack's. "Suitable" is not explained; the goal sentence above it names purpose, audience and context. The item names no steps and no number of versions, and sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child starts writing at once and finds halfway that the order does not work. A child uses one fixed plan for every task, also for a small one. A child makes a plan and does not look at it while writing.
