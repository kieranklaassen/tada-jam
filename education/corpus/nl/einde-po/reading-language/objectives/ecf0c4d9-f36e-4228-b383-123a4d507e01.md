## Official wording

oriënteren op kenmerken van aangereikte bronnen: maker, tekstsoort en verschijningsdatum;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 2 C a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting an orientation on features of provided sources: maker, kind of text and date of publication;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 is given a book, a newspaper cutting and a printed web page, and looks on each for who made it, what kind of text it is and when it appeared. The child finds the name on the cover or under the heading, finds the date at the front of the book or at the top of the page, and notes the three features for each source.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 2 C, which says that the pupil explores the reliability of different sources; 2 C sits under core goal 2. The sources are provided (*aangereikte bronnen*); the item does not say by whom, and finding sources is not mentioned. Three features are named after the colon: maker, kind of text and date of publication. The verb is getting an orientation on (*oriënteren op*); the item does not ask the pupil to draw a conclusion from the features. The item sets no level and no number of sources.

### Common mistakes

A child takes the website or the platform for the maker ("it is from the internet"). A child cannot find a date, or takes the date of printing or viewing for the date of publication. A child notes the three features and does not see what they have to do with trusting the source.
