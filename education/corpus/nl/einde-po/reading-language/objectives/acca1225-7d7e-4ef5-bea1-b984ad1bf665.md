## Official wording

vragen om toelichting, verklaring of bevestiging;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 B b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

asking for clarification (*toelichting*, more said about something), explanation or confirmation;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 asks "What do you mean by 'fair'?" to hear more, "Why does it sink, then?" to hear the cause, and "So you want to start with the poster, is that right?" to check what was understood. When the answer still leaves it unclear, the child asks once more in other words.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 4 B, which says that the pupil holds conversations to arrive at the building of knowledge, understanding or an approach; 4 B sits under core goal 4. The item names three things a pupil asks for, joined by "or": *toelichting* (more said about something), *verklaring* (an explanation of why or how something is so) and confirmation. It does not say whom the pupil asks, how the question is worded or how often, and it sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child does not understand and stays silent, or nods. A child asks "What?" and gets the same words again, where an explanation was needed. A child takes their own reading of what was said as certain and never checks it.
