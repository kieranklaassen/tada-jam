## Official wording

a) meervoud -s na klinker (meisjes, garages, fuchsia’s, cafés)

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 4.4 Moeilijkheid, niveau 2F, Spelling / 3. Morfologische spelling / Moeilijke gevallen / a).

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

a) plural -s after a vowel (*meisjes*, *garages*, *fuchsia’s*, *cafés*: girls, garages, fuchsias, cafés)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 at this level writes the plural of a noun that ends in a vowel and decides whether the *s* is written directly after the word or after an apostrophe. The child writes *meisjes* and *garages* with the *s* directly after the final *e*, *fuchsia’s* with an apostrophe after the final *a*, and *cafés* with the *s* directly after the *é*.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 2F for Dutch, terms and language care, table of difficulty (*Moeilijkheid*), row a) of the difficult cases (*Moeilijke gevallen*) under *Spelling*, 3. *Morfologische spelling* (morphological spelling, the spelling of word forms such as plurals and diminutives). In this table (Table 2 of the decree) the row has a plus sign in the column for level 2F. The table's caption describes its levels as mastery: 75% of all pupils in the age group or level group have an 80% chance of getting it right. 2F is the higher level that many pupils reach at the end of primary school, above the basic level 1F. The row names the plural in -s after a vowel and gives four words in brackets; the brackets do not say whether these are examples or the whole set. Two of them take the *s* directly after a final *e* (*meisjes*, *garages*), one takes an apostrophe (*fuchsia’s*) and one ends in *é* (*cafés*). The row states no rule in words. The pack's further examples, all correct Dutch: with an apostrophe, *auto’s* (cars), *taxi’s* (taxis), *menu’s* (menus), *baby’s* (babies), *opa’s* (grandfathers); without an apostrophe, *tantes* (aunts), *bureaus* (desks), *cadeaus* (presents).

### Common mistakes

A child writes an apostrophe in every plural in -s, also where none belongs (the wrong *meisje’s* and *tante’s*). A child leaves the apostrophe out after a long final vowel (the wrong *autos* and *babys*). A child writes the apostrophe after *é* or after a group of vowel letters such as *eau* (the wrong *café’s* and *cadeau’s*), where *cafés* and *cadeaus* are right.
