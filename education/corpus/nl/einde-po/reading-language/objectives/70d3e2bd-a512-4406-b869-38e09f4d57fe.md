## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht in verhalende teksten.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 9 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight into narrative texts.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 has read a story and draws or tells the course of the story: the problem at the start, what the main character tries, who helps and who works against, and how it ends. The child names where and when the story takes place. The child points to the page where the suspense rose and says what the writer did there.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 9, which is about insight into literature. Its items 9 A a to 9 A d name the problem or wish of a main character that drives the story and how the story develops, the characters and their traits, the narrative techniques for building up suspense, and where, when and under which circumstances a story takes place. The sentence says narrative texts and does not say how long they are or at what level. It names no titles.

### Common mistakes

A pupil retells every event in order ("and then, and then") and cannot say in one or two sentences what the story is about. A pupil mixes up the writer with the character who tells the story.
