## Official wording

verkennen van het eigen talige repertoire in relatie tot publiek, doel en context;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 7 A b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring one's own language repertoire (*talige repertoire*, all the language means a person has) in relation to audience, purpose and context;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 asks for help three times in three ways, as they would ask a friend, a teacher and a shop assistant they do not know, and writes down which words, tone and gestures change. The child sorts their own expressions on cards into "with friends", "in class" and "with people I do not know", and finds cards that fit in more than one place.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 7 A, which says that the pupil explores how you express identity with language; 7 A sits under core goal 7. The item names three things the repertoire is explored in relation to: audience, purpose and context. It does not list what the repertoire holds; item 7 A a does. The verb is exploring: the item does not itself ask the pupil to adjust language use. Adjusting conversations to purpose, partner or partners and context is in goal sentence 4 A. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Pupils most often leave out purpose: they see that they speak differently to different people, and do not see that they also speak differently when they want to convince, to comfort or to ask for something. Many sort language only into "neat" and "not neat".
