## Official wording

functioneel gebruiken van taalkundige begrippen en taalbeschouwingsstrategieën bij het denken en praten over spelling en grammatica.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 6 A e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

functionally using linguistic *begrippen* (concepts, or terms) and strategies for considering language (*taalbeschouwingsstrategieën*) when thinking and talking about spelling and grammar.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 who doubts between *hij vind* and *hij vindt* (he finds) says: "It is the verb, the subject is *hij*, so stem plus *t*: *vindt*" (the pack's example). The child tests the spelling by putting another verb in the same place: in *hij loopt* (he walks) a *t* is heard, so *vindt* gets a *t* as well. Two children who disagree about a sentence use the words for subject and verb to say where they differ.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 6 A, which says that the pupil considers the relation between the form and the meaning of language; 6 A sits under core goal 6. The item names two things that are used: linguistic *begrippen* (the Dutch word means both concepts and terms) and strategies for considering language. It says where they are used: when thinking and talking about spelling and grammar. *Functioneel* (functionally) ties the use to that thinking and talking; the item does not ask for terms to be known apart from it. It lists no terms and no strategies, and it sets no level. The reference levels 1F and 2F for Dutch, which are other records of this lane (section 4.1, *Begrippenlijst*, list of terms), are worded in more detail.

### Common mistakes

A child can name the parts in a practice sentence and does not use the terms when checking a text of their own. A child uses a term as a label without the idea behind it, and calls the first word of every sentence the subject. A child applies a spelling strategy without thinking, in a place where it does not fit.
