## Official wording

Past het taalgebruik aan de luisteraar(s) aan.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.3 Spreken, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op publiek / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Adapts the language use to the listener(s).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 explains the rules of dodgeball to children of six with short sentences and easy words. The same pupil explains the rules to a new classmate of the same age faster and with the usual words of the game.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Spreken* (speaking), *Afstemming op publiek* (fit to the audience). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). The row has one other statement at 1F, on supporting materials for a prepared presentation. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names the listener or listeners and nothing more about them. It does not say what is adapted; choice of words, sentence length and speed of speaking are the pack's examples. It sets no degree of adaptation.

### Common mistakes

A pupil explains something to much younger children with the same long sentences and hard words as to classmates. A pupil tells listeners who were not there about people and places as if they knew them. A pupil explains to classmates, step by step, what they already know.
