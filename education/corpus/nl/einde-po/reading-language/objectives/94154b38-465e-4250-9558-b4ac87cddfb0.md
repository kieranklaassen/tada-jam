## Official wording

verwoorden van opgedane inzichten en kennis over zichzelf op basis van literatuur;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 8 B b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting into words insights and knowledge gained about oneself on the basis of literature;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads about a character who is afraid to speak in front of a group and says: "I do that too, I wait until someone else has gone first." After a story about a quarrel between two friends the child says which of the two the child is more like, and what the child would have done. The child says it or writes it down.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 8 B, which says that the pupil explores the value of literature; 8 B sits under core goal 8. The item is about what the pupil has come to see or know about the pupil's own person. Items 8 B c and 8 B d say the same about others, and about the world and cultures. The item does not say which insights are meant, how personal they are, or to whom the pupil tells them. It names no kinds of text and no titles.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is themselves: a pupil describes the character ("she is shy") and says nothing about the pupil's own person. Other pupils say only "I am like that too" and name no part of the story that showed it.
