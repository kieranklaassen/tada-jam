## Official wording

Kan een oordeel over een tekst(deel) verwoorden.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 2.1 Zakelijke teksten, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Evalueren / 1.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can put a judgement about a text (or part of a text) into words.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 finishes a text about a new playground and says or writes what it thinks of the text: that the part about the costs was hard to follow, or that the text explains the plans clearly. Asked about one paragraph only, the child gives its judgement of that paragraph.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Zakelijke teksten* (reading factual texts), *Evalueren* (evaluating). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The judgement is about a text or a part of a text; the source writes this as *tekst(deel)*. The statement does not ask for reasons or arguments, sets no criteria for the judgement, and does not say whether the judgement is spoken or written. It names no kind of text.

### Common mistakes

A child retells what the text says instead of judging it. A child judges the subject ("I like playgrounds") instead of the text. A child gives a judgement of the whole text when asked about one part.
