## Official wording

waarderen van het eigen talige repertoire.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 7 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

*waarderen* (valuing, or judging the worth of) one's own language repertoire (*talige repertoire*, all the language means a person has).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 tells the class a saying or a rhyme from home, in the language or variety it belongs to, and says what it means. The child names something they can do with language, such as speaking with a grandparent in a regional variety, and says when it is useful or why it matters to them.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 7 A, which says that the pupil explores how you express identity with language; 7 A sits under core goal 7. *Waarderen* can be read as valuing (appreciating) or as judging the worth of something; the item does not say which, and the gloss keeps both. The item does not say what the repertoire holds; item 7 A a lists that. It names no language or variety, and it sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Pupils most often leave out the parts of their repertoire that are not used in lessons: a language or variety from home, or the language of their group of friends, is seen as not counting or as something to hide.
