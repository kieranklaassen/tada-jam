## Official wording

afstemmen van taalgebruik, stemgebruik en non-verbale communicatie op communicatief doel en context;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

adjusting language use, use of voice and non-verbal communication to communicative purpose and context;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says *u* to an adult they do not know and *je* to a classmate (the pack's example: Dutch has a polite and a familiar word for "you"). The child speaks more softly in the library, and more slowly and clearly on the telephone, where the other person cannot see gestures. To comfort a friend the child uses a calm voice and sits close; to convince the group the child stands up straight and stresses the main words.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 4 A, which says that the pupil holds conversations adjusted to purpose, partner or partners and context; 4 A sits under core goal 4. It names three things that are adjusted (language use, use of voice, non-verbal communication) and two things they are adjusted to (communicative purpose and context). The partner is not named in this item; item 4 A d names the partner. It gives no example of language use, voice or non-verbal communication, names no register such as formal or informal, and sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child adjusts the words and leaves the voice and the body the same: polite words in a bored tone. A child speaks at one volume and one speed everywhere. A child relies on a gesture or a facial expression where the other person cannot see it, such as in a telephone call.
