## Official wording

Kan gebruik maken van ondersteunende materialen om een voorbereide presentatie beter aan het publiek over te brengen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.3 Spreken, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op publiek / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can make use of supporting materials to convey a prepared presentation to the audience better.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 gives a prepared talk (*spreekbeurt*) about volcanoes. The pupil holds up a piece of lava stone, points on a drawing to where the magma rises, and shows three photographs while telling about them. The pupil says what each thing shows and then goes on with the talk.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Spreken* (speaking), *Afstemming op publiek* (fit to the audience). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). The row has one other statement at 1F, on adapting language use to the listener or listeners. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement is about a prepared (*voorbereide*) presentation, and the materials have a purpose: conveying the presentation to the audience better. It names no materials; an object, a drawing and photographs are the pack's examples. It does not say who makes the materials, how many are used or how long the presentation is.

### Common mistakes

A pupil reads the text on a poster or a slide aloud with their back to the audience. A pupil shows an object or a picture and does not say what it shows or why it is there. A pupil puts so much text on a slide or a poster that the audience reads instead of listening.
