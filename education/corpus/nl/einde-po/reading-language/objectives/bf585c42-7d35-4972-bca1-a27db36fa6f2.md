## Official wording

De leerling verkent het gebruik van taal.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 7.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil explores the use of language.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school pays attention to how they themselves use language at home, in class and with friends, and writes down the differences. The child collects words that a grandparent uses and they do not, listens to how a regional variety sounds, and tells what people think when they hear a certain way of speaking.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 7 itself. Two goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 7 A (how you express identity with language) and 7 B (language variation and language change in the Dutch language area). The verb is exploring, here and in both goal sentences: the goal does not ask the pupil to master a language variety, and the law sets no level. Apart from Dutch, the goal and its items name no language, no dialect and no regional language by name. Core goal 7 is one of two goals of the domain language (*taal*), with core goal 6.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Pupils most often leave out their own language use: they notice that others speak differently and take their own way of speaking as the normal one. Many also think there is one correct Dutch and that every other variety is wrong Dutch.
