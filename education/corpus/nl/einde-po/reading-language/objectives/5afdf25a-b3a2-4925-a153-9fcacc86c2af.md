## Official wording

aanbieden van kennis en vaardigheden uit het leergebied Nederlands in onderlinge samenhang;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 1 A c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

offering knowledge and skills from the learning area (*leergebied*, a subject area of the curriculum) Dutch in connection with one another;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 reads a text about a subject, talks about it, learns the new words from it, writes about the same subject and checks the spelling of what the child wrote, all in one piece of work on that subject.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 1 A, which says that the school provides a rich language and reading environment; 1 A sits under core goal 1. Like 1 A, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is offering. The item says that the knowledge and skills of the learning area Dutch are offered in connection with one another; it does not name them and does not say how they are connected. Goal sentence 1 B is about language development in all learning areas. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often keep apart what they have learned: they spell a word correctly in a spelling exercise and wrongly in a text of their own, and they do not use a word from a reading lesson when they write.
