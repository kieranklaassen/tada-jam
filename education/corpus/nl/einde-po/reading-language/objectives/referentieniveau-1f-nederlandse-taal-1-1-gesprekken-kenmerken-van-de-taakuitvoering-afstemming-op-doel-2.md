## Official wording

Herkent gesprekssituaties en kan passende routines gebruiken.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 1F, Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering / Afstemming op doel / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Recognises conversation situations and can use fitting routines.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a pupil of about 12 answers the telephone with a greeting and their name, and ends the call with a goodbye. At the bakery the pupil greets, says what is wanted and thanks the baker. In a class circle (*kringgesprek*) the pupil raises a hand and waits for a turn; with a friend in the playground the pupil just starts talking.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), *Afstemming op doel* (fit to the purpose). It stands under *Kenmerken van de taakuitvoering* (features of task performance). The row has one other statement at 1F, on what conversations are held for. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement has two parts: recognising the kind of conversation situation, and using routines that fit it. It names no situations and no routines; fixed ways of greeting, taking leave, thanking and asking for a turn are the pack's reading of "routines", not the decree's words. It does not say how many situations or routines a pupil knows.

### Common mistakes

A pupil uses the routine of one situation in another, for example saying *jij* (the informal "you") to an unfamiliar adult where *u* (the polite form) fits. A pupil leaves out the opening or closing routine on the telephone, and starts with the message or ends the call without a goodbye. A pupil knows the routine but does not recognise the situation in time, for example calling out in a class discussion where turns are given.
