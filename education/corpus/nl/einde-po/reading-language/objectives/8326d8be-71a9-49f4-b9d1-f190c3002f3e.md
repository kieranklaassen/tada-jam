## Official wording

vergelijken van de contexten waarin verschillende talen en taalvariëteiten worden gebruikt;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 7 B b.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing the contexts in which different languages and language varieties are used;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 keeps a list for one day: where they heard or read which language or variety, at home, in a shop, on the sports field, in a video, in a letter from the municipality. The child lays two of these contexts side by side and says what was used in each and why that fits there.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 7 B, which says that the pupil explores language variation and language change in the Dutch language area; 7 B sits under core goal 7. The verb is comparing, and what is compared is contexts. The item speaks of different languages and language varieties and names none. It names no contexts, and it does not say whether the contexts are the pupil's own. The item sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child lists who speaks what and leaves out where and when, so that people are compared and not contexts. A child thinks one person has one language, and does not see that the same person uses different varieties in different places. A child ranks contexts as better or worse and does not compare what is used in them.
