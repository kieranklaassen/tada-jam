## Official wording

stimuleren van de leesmotivatie en de durf om te spreken en te schrijven;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 1 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

stimulating reading motivation and the courage (*durf*) to speak and to write;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 chooses books, hears others talk with pleasure about what they read, and wants to go on reading. The child says something in a group, tries a hard word or a new kind of text, and shows a piece of writing to others without fear of being laughed at.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 1 A, which says that the school provides a rich language and reading environment; 1 A sits under core goal 1. Like 1 A, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil: the school stimulates, and the item does not require a pupil to be motivated or to dare. Two things are named: reading motivation, and the courage to speak and to write. Motivation is named for reading, and courage for speaking and writing. The item does not say how the school does this, names no measure and sets no level.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children of this age who doubt themselves most often stay silent in a group, write as little as possible, or choose only what they are sure to get right. A child who reads with difficulty often avoids reading.
