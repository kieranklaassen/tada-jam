## Official wording

Kan kritisch luisteren naar meningen en opvattingen en een reactie geven.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit referentieniveaus Nederlandse taal en rekenen, bijlage 1, 1.1 Gesprekken, niveau 1F, Taken / 1. Deelnemen aan discussie en overleg / 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

Can listen critically to opinions and views and give a response.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The pack's illustration: a group talks over where the evening game of the school camp should be held. A classmate says, "In the woods, because that is the most exciting." A pupil of about 12 listens, weighs the reason and answers, "Exciting, yes, but the youngest in our group are afraid in the dark, so I do not agree." The pupil responds to what was said, not to who said it.

### Limits

This is a statement of reference level 1F for Dutch, *Gesprekken* (conversations), task 1, *Deelnemen aan discussie en overleg* (taking part in discussion and in *overleg*, talking something over together). It stands under *Taken* (tasks). The task has one other statement at 1F, on following the main points and giving one's own opinion with arguments. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school. The statement names two things: listening critically to opinions and views, and giving a response. It does not say what listening critically consists of, names no grounds for judging an opinion, and does not say what form the response takes. It names no topics.

### Common mistakes

A pupil agrees or disagrees according to who is speaking, a friend or not, instead of according to what is said. A pupil responds with only "I agree" or "That is not true" and gives no reason. A pupil takes an opinion for a fact because it is said firmly.
