## Official wording

reflecteren op gemaakte keuzes in woorden en zinnen;

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 6 B d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reflecting on choices made in words and sentences;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads back a text and says why it has *rende* (ran) and not *liep* (walked), or why one long sentence was cut into two (the pack's example). The child tries another word in the same place, reads both versions aloud, and says which of the two fits the reader better.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 6 B, which says that the pupil shows insight into rules and procedures for spelling, phrasing and punctuation; 6 B sits under core goal 6. The item names choices at two levels: words and sentences. It does not say whose choices are meant (the Dutch has no word for "own") or in what kind of text. Choices at text level are not named in this item. It sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child cannot say why a word was chosen ("it just sounded good"). A child looks only for mistakes and does not compare two options that are both correct. A child defends the first version and changes nothing.
