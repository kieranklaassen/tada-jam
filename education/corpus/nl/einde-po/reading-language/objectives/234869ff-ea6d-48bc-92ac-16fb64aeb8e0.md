## Official wording

De leerling voert gesprekken afgestemd op doel, gesprekspartner(s) en context.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 4 A.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil holds conversations adjusted to purpose, conversation partner(s) and context.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 phones a shop to ask about the opening times and speaks politely and briefly; with a friend at break the same child jokes and uses short, loose sentences. In a group that plans a class party the child keeps to the subject, lets the others finish, and speaks so that everyone in the group can follow.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 4, which is about holding conversations. Its items 4 A a to 4 A e name the skills that make a conversation go constructively, taking part actively, the adjusting of language use, voice and non-verbal communication, the information that is acquired and used, and the conventions. The sentence names three things a conversation is adjusted to: purpose, partner or partners, and context. The bracketed "(s)" leaves open whether there is one partner or more. It names no kind of conversation, no purpose and no context, and the law sets no level.

### Common mistakes

A child uses the same words and tone with every partner, for example the language of the playground in a conversation with a visitor. A child gives too little background to a partner who was not there, or explains again what the partner already knows. A child forgets the purpose, and the conversation moves to another subject.
