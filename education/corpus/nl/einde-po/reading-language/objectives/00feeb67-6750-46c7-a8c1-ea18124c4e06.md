## Official wording

beschrijven waar, wanneer en onder welke omstandigheden een verhaal zich afspeelt.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit kerndoelen primair en speciaal onderwijs 2026, bijlage 1, onderdeel A, kerndoel 9 A d.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing where, when and under which circumstances a story takes place.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads the first pages of a story and says where it happens (a village by the sea, a flat in a city), when (long ago, now, in winter, during one night) and under which circumstances (there is a war, the family has no money, a storm has closed the road). The child points to the words in the text that show it: a horse and cart, a telephone with a cord, snow.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 9 A, which says that the pupil shows insight into narrative texts; 9 A sits under core goal 9. The item names three things: where, when and under which circumstances. It does not explain "circumstances" further. The effect of place, time and circumstances on the story or its characters is not mentioned. The item names no terms and no length or level of story.

### Common mistakes

A pupil gives only the place and leaves out the time and the circumstances. A pupil cannot tell the time of a story when the text gives no year, and does not use clues such as objects, clothes or ways of travelling. A pupil confuses when the story takes place with when the book was written.
