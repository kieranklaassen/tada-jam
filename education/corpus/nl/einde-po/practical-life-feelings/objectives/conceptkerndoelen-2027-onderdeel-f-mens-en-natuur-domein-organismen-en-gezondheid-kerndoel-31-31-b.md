## Official wording

De leerling toont inzicht in leefstijl, gezondheid en ziekte en verkent keuzes hierin.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 13.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil shows insight into lifestyle, health and illness and explores choices in these.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says how things people do each day, such as eating, sleeping and moving, go together with health, and how an illness can arise and can be prevented or treated. The child looks at a choice in this field, lists the options, and says what could follow from each.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 31, in the domain *organismen en gezondheid* (organisms and health); the sentence of core goal 31 and goal sentence 31 A are not records of this lane (the pack records them under science). This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Its items 31 B a to 31 B e name the influence of hygiene, food, sleep, nature, relationships, sexuality and movement on health; bacteria, fungi and viruses; how illnesses arise and can be prevented and treated; how unintended pregnancies can be prevented; and organisations for health and well-being. Two verbs: showing insight, and exploring choices. The sentence does not say which choices or whose, and does not say which choice is right. It names no illness, act or situation and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child takes health to be only not being ill, and leaves out mental health. A child gives one cause for an illness ("you get a cold from being cold") and leaves out that several things work together.
