## Official wording

verkennen van keuzes en hun gevolgen ten aanzien van ontvangsten, uitgaven, sparen, lenen en risico's in de eigen omgeving;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring choices and their consequences with regard to money received (*ontvangsten*), expenses, saving, *lenen* (borrowing or lending) and risks in one's own surroundings;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 keeps a record of money that comes in and goes out, for himself or herself or for a made-up household, and tries out choices: spending now or saving for a goal, borrowing from someone and paying back, or lending to someone and getting it back later. The child says for each choice what follows from it, and names a risk, such as losing money or not being able to pay something back.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 28 A, which says that the pupil explores economic choices of people and possible consequences; 28 A sits under core goal 28. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is exploring. Five subjects are named: money received, expenses, saving, *lenen* and risks. *Lenen* means both borrowing and lending, and the item does not choose. The wording leaves open whether "in one's own surroundings" belongs to risks only or to the whole list. The item states no amounts and no number range, names no product such as an account and no particular kind of loan, and does not say which choice is right. It names no situations beyond these five subjects and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child does not count small expenses and cannot say where the money went. A child takes borrowed money for extra money and leaves out paying back. A child treats money on a card or in an app as less real than coins.
