## Official wording

ervaringen opdoen met het voeren van dialogen, omgaan met conflicten en gelijkwaardige behandeling;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experience with holding dialogues, dealing with conflicts, and treatment as equals (*gelijkwaardige behandeling*);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 takes part in a dialogue in which each speaker finishes speaking and the aim is to understand one another. In a conflict with a classmate the child says what he or she wants, hears what the other wants, and looks for a way that both accept. The child takes part in a group in which the same holds for everyone, and notices the difference when it does not.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 20 A, which says that the pupil reasons about the importance of basic values of the democratic *rechtsstaat*; 20 A sits under core goal 20. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is gaining experience: the item asks that the pupil takes part, and names no result or level. Three things are named: holding dialogues, dealing with conflicts, and treatment as equals. The item does not say with whom, about what or how often, and names no way of dealing with a conflict. It does not say whether treatment as equals is something the pupil gives, receives or both. It names no situations and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out in a dialogue is listening: they wait for their turn and prepare their own answer. In a conflict many look for who is to blame and leave out what each side wants.
