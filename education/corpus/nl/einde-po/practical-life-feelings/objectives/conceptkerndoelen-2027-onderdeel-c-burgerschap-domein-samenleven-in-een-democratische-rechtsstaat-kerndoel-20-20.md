## Official wording

De leerling leert over samenleven in een democratische rechtsstaat.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil learns about living together in a democratic state under the rule of law (*democratische rechtsstaat*).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school says what freedom, equal worth and solidarity mean, with a case of each from the class, the street or the news. The child talks with classmates about a case in which two of these ask different things, describes ways in which people differ, and says how he or she can deal with such differences.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 20 itself. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Two goal sentences say what it covers, each with its own items: 20 A (the pupil reasons about the importance of basic values of the democratic *rechtsstaat*) and 20 B (the pupil explores how he or she can deal with diversity in society). The verb of the sentence is learning about; the goal sentences use reasoning and exploring. A *democratische rechtsstaat* is a democracy in which the government too is bound by law and the rights of persons are protected; the sentence does not define the term. It names no values, rights, institutions or situations and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

Many children take democracy to mean only that the majority decides, and leave out that rights also protect those who are in the minority. Many take a right or a value as something that holds for others, in the news, and do not connect it with their own life.
