## Official wording

aandacht schenken aan emoties en het omgaan met emoties;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

giving attention to emotions and to dealing with emotions;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 names what he or she feels with a more exact word than "good" or "bad", says what brought the feeling on, and says what he or she does with it, such as waiting before answering or telling someone. The child notices what a classmate feels and asks about it.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 28 D, which says that the school contributes to the social and emotional development of pupils and promotes respectful interaction with others; 28 D sits under core goal 28. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Like 28 D, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is giving attention to. Two things are named: emotions, and dealing with emotions. The item names no emotion, does not say whose emotions, and names no way of dealing with them. It names no situations and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often leave out the step between feeling and acting: they act on anger or shame at once and name the feeling only afterwards, or not at all. Many also believe that some feelings are not allowed.
