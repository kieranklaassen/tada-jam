## Official wording

veilig handelen in verkeerssituaties in de omgeving van de school;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

acting safely in traffic situations in the surroundings of the school;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 crosses the street by the school at the place meant for it, looks both ways also when others are already crossing, walks with the bicycle where it is crowded, and watches for cars that stop, park and pull out at the times when children arrive and leave.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 28 E, which says that the pupil takes part in traffic in a safe and responsible way; 28 E sits under core goal 28. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is acting. The item bounds the place: traffic situations in the surroundings of the school; it does not say how far these surroundings reach. It names no situation, role or act and does not say what acting safely is. It states no safety condition such as adult supervision, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child follows the group across the street without looking. A child pays less attention on a street he or she uses every day. A child steps out from between parked cars, where drivers cannot see him or her.
