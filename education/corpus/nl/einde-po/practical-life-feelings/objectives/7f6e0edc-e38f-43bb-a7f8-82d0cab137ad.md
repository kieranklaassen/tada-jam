## Official wording

De leerlingen leren met zorg om te gaan met het milieu.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO, bijlage, kerndoel 39.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupils learn to deal with the environment (*milieu*) with care.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school puts waste in the right bin and takes litter along, turns off the tap and the light when they are not needed, uses a thing again before throwing it away, and leaves plants and animals outdoors undisturbed.

### Limits

This 2006 goal is still in force. It is one numbered sentence of the decree, set for the end of primary school, under the heading *Mens en samenleving* (people and society). The verb is learning to deal with, and the manner is "with care". The goal does not say what counts as care. It names no subjects such as waste, water, energy or nature, no acts and no situations, and sets no level of detail. It is worded as behaviour; knowledge about the environment is not mentioned in this sentence.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is their own everyday use: they connect the environment with distant problems and not with their own waste, water and energy. Many also know what careful behaviour is and do not act on it when it takes effort.
