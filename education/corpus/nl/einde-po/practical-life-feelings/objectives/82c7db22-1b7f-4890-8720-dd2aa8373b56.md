## Official wording

De leerlingen leren zorg te dragen voor de lichamelijke en psychische gezondheid van henzelf en anderen.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO, bijlage, kerndoel 34.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupils learn to take care of the physical and mental (*psychische*) health of themselves and others.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school washes hands before eating, brushes teeth, goes to bed in time on a school night, and moves every day. The child notices when he or she or another person is sad, tense or left out, says so or asks about it, and fetches help when someone is hurt or unwell.

### Limits

This 2006 goal is still in force. It is one numbered sentence of the decree, set for the end of primary school, under the heading *Mens en samenleving* (people and society). The verb is learning to take care of. Two kinds of health are named, physical and mental, and two for whom: the pupils themselves and others. The goal does not say who the others are. It names no subjects such as food, hygiene or movement, no acts and no situations, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is mental health: they take caring for health to mean caring for the body only. Many also know what is healthy and do not act on it.
