## Official wording

aanbieden van activiteiten die sociale cohesie en actief burgerschap stimuleren.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

offering activities that stimulate social cohesion and active citizenship (*burgerschap*).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 takes part in an activity that the school offers and that pupils do together, for example preparing a school event or looking after a shared space. The child takes on a task in it and carries it out for the group.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 19 A, which says that the school stimulates social and societal competences of pupils; 19 A sits under core goal 19. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Like 19 A, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is offering. The activities are described only by what they stimulate: social cohesion and active citizenship. The item names no activity, does not say how many, how often or where, and does not define social cohesion or active citizenship. It names no situations and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often take part with their own friends only, and leave the tasks nobody chose to others.
