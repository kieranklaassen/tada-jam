## Official wording

verkennen hoe keuzes worden beïnvloed door beschikbare middelen en door andere mensen;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring how choices are influenced by available means and by other people;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 takes a choice he or she made, for example what to buy with pocket money, and says what set the limits (how much money or time there was) and who influenced it (a parent, a friend, a seller). The child compares this with the same choice made by someone with more or with less to spend.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 28 A, which says that the pupil explores economic choices of people and possible consequences; 28 A sits under core goal 28. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is exploring. Two influences are named: available means and other people. *Middelen* (means) is not narrowed to money; it can also cover time or goods. The item does not say whose choices or which other people are meant. It names no situations, states no amounts, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child believes that his or her choices are entirely his or her own and does not notice the influence of friends or sellers. A child takes means to be money only.
