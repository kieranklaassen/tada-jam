## Official wording

De school stimuleert sociale en maatschappelijke competenties van leerlingen.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The school stimulates social and societal (*maatschappelijke*, relating to society) competences of pupils.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 works with classmates on a shared task, asks what another person means before answering, and questions a claim before accepting it. The child takes part in an activity that the school offers for the group as a whole.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 19, which is about the school giving shape to the *democratische oefenplaats* (a place for practising democracy); it is the only goal sentence of that goal. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. It is addressed to the school: it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. Its items 19 A a to 19 A d name offering knowledge and skills about basic values, stimulating social skills and critical thinking skills, stimulating respectful communication, and offering activities. Dutch has two words here, *sociale* (between people) and *maatschappelijke* (relating to society); the sentence does not say which competences are meant by each. It names no situations or ways and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This sentence is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often show a social skill with their friends and leave it out with children outside their own group.
