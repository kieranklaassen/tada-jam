## Official wording

beschrijven van mogelijke gevolgen van consumptie voor jezelf, voor anderen en voor de leefomgeving.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing possible consequences of consumption for yourself, for others and for the living environment (*leefomgeving*).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 takes a thing he or she uses or buys, for example a T-shirt, a snack or a telephone, and describes what buying and using it can mean for himself or herself, for other people such as those who made it, and for the surroundings in which people live, such as the waste that is left.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 28 A, which says that the pupil explores economic choices of people and possible consequences; 28 A sits under core goal 28. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is describing. The item names three for whom or for what consumption can have consequences: yourself, others, and the living environment. It says "possible" consequences and does not say whether they are favourable or unfavourable. It names no products, consequences or situations, does not say what the pupil should buy or not buy, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child describes only consequences for himself or herself (the price, the pleasure) and leaves out the others and the surroundings. A child names only the waste and leaves out how a thing was made and by whom.
