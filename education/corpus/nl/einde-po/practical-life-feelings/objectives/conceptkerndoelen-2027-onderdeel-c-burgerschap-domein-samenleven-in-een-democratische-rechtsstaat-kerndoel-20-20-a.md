## Official wording

De leerling redeneert over het belang van basiswaarden van de democratische rechtsstaat.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil reasons about the importance of basic values of the democratic state under the rule of law (*democratische rechtsstaat*).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says why freedom, equal worth or solidarity matters and gives a reason: what would be different in the class or in the country if it did not hold. The child weighs a case in which two values ask different things, hears a classmate's reasoning, and says where he or she agrees.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 20, which is about living together in a democratic *rechtsstaat*. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Its items 20 A a to 20 A e name describing the meaning of the basic values, describing how the *Grondwet* (the Dutch Constitution), children's rights and human rights make them possible, reasoning about how the values can be in tension, gaining experience with dialogue, conflicts and treatment as equals, and recognising and naming the part the values play in society and in one's own life. The verb is reasoning, and what is reasoned about is the importance of the values. The sentence itself names no value; item 20 A a names three. It names no situations, does not say which conclusion the pupil reaches, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child states an opinion ("that is unfair") and gives no reason for it. A child treats a value as holding only where it serves himself or herself: freedom to say what he or she wants, and not another person's freedom to say something unwelcome.
