## Official wording

benoemen van het belang dat mensen elkaar gelijkwaardig behandelen;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 4.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

naming the importance of people treating one another as equals (*gelijkwaardig*, as being of equal worth);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 says why it matters that people treat one another as equals, and gives a reason from a case: what happens in a group when one child is treated as counting for less, and what is different when the same holds for everyone.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 20 B, which says that the pupil explores how he or she can deal with diversity in society; 20 B sits under core goal 20. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is naming. One thing is named: the importance of people treating one another as equals. *Gelijkwaardig* means as being of equal worth; it does not mean identically. The item gives no reason itself, names no persons, grounds or situations and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child takes treating as equals to mean treating everyone exactly the same, and calls it unfair when a classmate gets help that he or she does not need. A child repeats the rule ("you must not discriminate") and gives no reason why it matters.
