## Official wording

herkennen en benoemen van stereotypering, discriminatie en uitsluiting;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 4.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising and naming stereotyping, discrimination and exclusion;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 reads or hears a remark, a joke, a picture or an account of an event and says what it is: a fixed image of a whole group (stereotyping), unequal treatment of a person because of a group he or she belongs to (discrimination), or keeping someone out (exclusion). The child says by what he or she can tell.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 20 B, which says that the pupil explores how he or she can deal with diversity in society; 20 B sits under core goal 20. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Two verbs: recognising and naming. Three things are named: stereotyping, discrimination and exclusion. The item gives no definition of them, names no groups, grounds, examples or situations, and sets no level of detail. It asks for recognising and naming; it does not say what the pupil does next.

### Common mistakes

A child counts only open forms, such as name-calling, and does not recognise a joke or not being chosen. A child does not count a favourable image of a whole group as stereotyping. A child mixes up the three: stereotyping is an image, discrimination and exclusion are acts.
