## Official wording

verkennen van de eigen identiteit ten aanzien van aspecten van diversiteit;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 4.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring one's own identity with regard to aspects of diversity;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 considers aspects of diversity one by one and asks what each means for himself or herself: what he or she believes, where his or her family comes from, what he or she can and cannot do. The child may put this into words, a drawing or a text.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 20 B, which says that the pupil explores how he or she can deal with diversity in society; 20 B sits under core goal 20. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is exploring; the item names no result. The term aspects of diversity is the one that item 20 B a fills in with seven aspects. The item does not say which aspects the pupil explores and does not say that the pupil tells others about his or her identity. It names no situations or ways and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is that every person has a place on each aspect: a child who belongs to the majority sees himself or herself as "normal" and diversity as something of others. Many also describe themselves by one aspect only.
