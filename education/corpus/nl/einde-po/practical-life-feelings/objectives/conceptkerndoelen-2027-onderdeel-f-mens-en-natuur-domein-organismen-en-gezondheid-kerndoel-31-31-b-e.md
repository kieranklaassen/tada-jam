## Official wording

benoemen van verschillende gezondheids- en welzijnsorganisaties voor hulp, mentale steun en preventie voor jezelf en anderen.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 13.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

naming different health organisations and welfare organisations (*welzijnsorganisaties*) for help, mental support and prevention for yourself and others.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 names several organisations a person can turn to, such as the family doctor's practice, the municipal health service or a telephone helpline for children, and says for each what it is for: help, mental support or prevention. The child says that these are there for himself or herself and for others.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 31 B, which says that the pupil shows insight into lifestyle, health and illness and explores choices in these; 31 B sits under core goal 31. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is naming. The item asks for different organisations of two kinds, for health and for *welzijn* (well-being; in the Netherlands also the name of the sector of social work and support), and gives three purposes: help, mental support and prevention, for yourself and for others. It names no organisation and does not say how many. It asks for naming; it does not say that the pupil contacts one. It names no situations and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child knows only the doctor and the hospital and leaves out organisations for mental support and for prevention. A child takes such organisations to be for adults or for emergencies only, and not for himself or herself.
