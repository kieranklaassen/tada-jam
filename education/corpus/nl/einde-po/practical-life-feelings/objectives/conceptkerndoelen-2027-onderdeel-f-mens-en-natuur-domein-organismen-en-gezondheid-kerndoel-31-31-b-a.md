## Official wording

oriënteren op de invloed van hygiëne, voeding, slaap, natuur, relaties, seksualiteit en beweging op lichamelijke en mentale gezondheid;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 13.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting an orientation on the influence of hygiene, food, sleep, nature, relationships, sexuality and movement (*beweging*, physical activity) on physical and mental health;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 goes through the seven things the item names and says for each that it can affect the body and the mind: hygiene, what and when one eats, sleep, being in nature, relationships, sexuality, and moving. The child compares a day with little sleep or little movement with another day and says what he or she notices.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 31 B, which says that the pupil shows insight into lifestyle, health and illness and explores choices in these; 31 B sits under core goal 31. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is getting an orientation on (*oriënteren op*), which names no result and no level. Seven influences are named: hygiene, food, sleep, nature, relationships, sexuality and movement; and two kinds of health: physical and mental. The item does not say what the influence of each is or whether it is favourable, and gives no amounts (hours of sleep, minutes of movement, kinds of food). It names no acts or situations and sets no level of detail, for sexuality as for the other six.

### Common mistakes

A child connects the seven with the body only and leaves out mental health. A child takes healthy and unhealthy to be fixed labels on things ("sugar is bad") and leaves out how much and how often.
