## Official wording

aandacht schenken aan relationele en seksuele ontwikkeling;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

giving attention to relational and sexual development;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 takes part in lessons and talks in which relational development and sexual development are the subject, hears the words used for them, and asks the questions he or she has.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 28 D, which says that the school contributes to the social and emotional development of pupils and promotes respectful interaction with others; 28 D sits under core goal 28. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Like 28 D, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is giving attention to. Two kinds of development are named: relational and sexual. The item does not say whose development and does not say that a pupil tells about himself or herself. It names no subjects, acts, situations or parts of the body, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often leave out asking: they take what they heard from other children or saw in media for how it is, and do not check it.
