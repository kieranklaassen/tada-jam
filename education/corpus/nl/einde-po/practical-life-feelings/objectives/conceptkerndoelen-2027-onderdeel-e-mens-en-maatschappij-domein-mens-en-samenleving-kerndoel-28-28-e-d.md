## Official wording

rekening houden met andere verkeersdeelnemers.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

taking account of other road users.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at what others on the road are doing and are about to do, makes sure that he or she can be seen, signals a turn in time, leaves room for someone who is slower or less steady, and does not ride side by side where others cannot pass.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 28 E, which says that the pupil takes part in traffic in a safe and responsible way; 28 E sits under core goal 28. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. One thing is named: taking account of other road users. The item does not say which road users or how this is done, and names no situations, rules or acts. It states no safety condition such as adult supervision, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is that others cannot know what they are about to do: they turn or stop without a signal. Many also take account of cars and leave out pedestrians, small children and older people.
