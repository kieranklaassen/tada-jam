## Official wording

bevorderen van respectvolle omgang met seksualiteit en seksuele diversiteit.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

promoting a respectful way of dealing with sexuality and sexual diversity.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 speaks about sexuality and about sexual diversity in words that do not mock or insult, does not use a word for a sexual orientation as a term of abuse, and does not tease a person about it.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 28 D, which says that the school contributes to the social and emotional development of pupils and promotes respectful interaction with others; 28 D sits under core goal 28. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Like 28 D, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is promoting. One thing is promoted: a respectful way of dealing (*omgang*) with two subjects, sexuality and sexual diversity. The item does not say what makes it respectful. It names no acts, situations, groups or orientations, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often use words for sexuality or for a sexual orientation as terms of abuse without thinking of the people the word refers to. Many also answer the subject with giggling or jokes and so do not speak about it.
