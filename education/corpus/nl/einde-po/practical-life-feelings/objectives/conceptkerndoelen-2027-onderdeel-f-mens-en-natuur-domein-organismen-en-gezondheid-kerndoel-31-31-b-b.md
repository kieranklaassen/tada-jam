## Official wording

beschrijven hoe bacteriën, schimmels en virussen het functioneren van mensen positief en negatief kunnen beïnvloeden;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 13.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing how bacteria, fungi and viruses can influence the functioning of people positively and negatively;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 describes cases in both directions: bacteria in the gut that help to digest food, a virus that gives a cold, a fungus that makes the skin itch, bacteria that inflame a wound. The child says which of the three kinds plays a part in each case and whether it helps or harms.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 31 B, which says that the pupil shows insight into lifestyle, health and illness and explores choices in these; 31 B sits under core goal 31. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is describing. Three kinds are named: bacteria, fungi and viruses; and two directions of influence: positive and negative. The item says "can": it does not say that each of the three does both. It names no species, illness or example and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child takes all bacteria to be harmful. A child treats bacteria and viruses as the same thing. A child counts as fungi only mushrooms or the mould on food, and not something that can live on or in the body.
