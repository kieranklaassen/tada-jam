## Official wording

De leerling verkent hoe mensen met elkaar samenleven.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 9.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil explores how people live together with one another.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school looks at how people earn, spend and share, how they deal with one another, and how they use the road together, and says what he or she notices. The child asks people about their work or their choices and compares the answers.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 28 itself. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Of its goal sentences, three are records of this lane, each with its own items: 28 A (the pupil explores economic choices of people and possible consequences), 28 D (the school contributes to the social and emotional development of pupils and promotes respectful interaction with others) and 28 E (the pupil takes part in traffic in a safe and responsible way). Goal sentences 28 B and 28 C are not recorded in the pack. 28 D is addressed to the school. The verb of the sentence is exploring. The sentence names no situations, groups or ways of living together and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is that the way people around them live together is one way among others: they take the habits of their own home and street for how everyone lives.
