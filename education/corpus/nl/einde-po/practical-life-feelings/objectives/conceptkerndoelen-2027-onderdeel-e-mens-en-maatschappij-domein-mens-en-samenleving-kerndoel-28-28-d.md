## Official wording

De school draagt bij aan de sociale en emotionele ontwikkeling van leerlingen en bevordert respectvolle omgang met anderen.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The school contributes to the social and emotional development of pupils and promotes respectful interaction (*omgang*) with others.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 names what he or she feels, says what he or she wants and does not want, works with others, and settles a quarrel in words. The child treats others with respect and is treated with respect.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 28, which is about how people live together with one another. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. It is addressed to the school: it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. Its items 28 D a to 28 D e name giving attention to emotions; making wishes, boundaries, *mogelijkheden* (possibilities or abilities) and *beperkingen* (limitations or impairments) open to discussion; stimulating safe social interaction, *weerbaarheid* (being able to stand up for oneself, or resilience), cooperation and the handling of conflicts; giving attention to relational and sexual development; and promoting a respectful way of dealing with sexuality and sexual diversity. The sentence has two verbs: contributing to, and promoting. It does not say who the others are, names no situations, ways or programmes, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This sentence is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. What children most often leave out is the other person's side: they know what they themselves feel and want, and do not ask what the other feels or wants.
