## Official wording

beschrijven hoe onbedoelde zwangerschappen voorkomen kunnen worden;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 13.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

describing how unintended pregnancies can be prevented;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 describes, in speech, in writing or in a drawing with labels, how a pregnancy that is not intended can be prevented, and uses the words learned for it. The child's doing here is describing; the item asks for no other act.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 31 B, which says that the pupil shows insight into lifestyle, health and illness and explores choices in these; 31 B sits under core goal 31. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is describing. One thing is described: how unintended pregnancies can be prevented. The item names no means or method and does not say how many are described. It names no acts or situations and sets no level of detail. Here the source prints *voorkomen* without the accent it has in item 31 B c; the passive form shows that "prevented" is meant.

### Common mistakes

A child names a means without being able to say what it does, because he or she does not yet connect how a pregnancy comes about with how it is prevented. A child takes what he or she heard from other children for fact.
