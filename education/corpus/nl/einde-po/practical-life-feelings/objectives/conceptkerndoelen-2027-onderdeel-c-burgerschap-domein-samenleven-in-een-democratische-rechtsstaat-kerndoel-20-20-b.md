## Official wording

De leerling verkent hoe die kan omgaan met diversiteit in de samenleving.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 4.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil explores how he or she can deal with diversity in society.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 describes ways in which people differ, considers what holds for himself or herself, and tries out ways of dealing with a difference: asking about it, hearing another person's experience, waiting before judging. The child says which ways there are and what he or she noticed when using one.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 20, which is about living together in a democratic *rechtsstaat* (a state under the rule of law). This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Its items 20 B a to 20 B e name describing aspects of diversity, exploring one's own identity, recognising and naming stereotyping, discrimination and exclusion, naming the importance of people treating one another as equals, and gaining experience with the perspectives, experiences and behaviour of others. The verb is exploring. The Dutch uses *die* as the pronoun for the pupil, a form that names no sex; the gloss writes "he or she". The sentence says "can": it does not prescribe one way of dealing with diversity. It names no aspects of diversity (item 20 B a lists seven), no situations and no ways, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is their own part: they describe how others are different and do not see that they themselves differ from others too. Many also take dealing with diversity to mean not mentioning differences.
