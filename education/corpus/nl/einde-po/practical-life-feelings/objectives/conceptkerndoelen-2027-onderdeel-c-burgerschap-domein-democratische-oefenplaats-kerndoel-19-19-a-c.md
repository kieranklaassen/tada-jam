## Official wording

stimuleren van respectvolle communicatie, offline en online;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

stimulating respectful communication, offline and online;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 lets another person finish, disagrees with what was said and not with the person, and chooses words that do not insult. The child does the same in a message in a group chat or under a post: the child rereads the message before sending it and does not pass on a message that mocks someone.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 19 A, which says that the school stimulates social and societal competences of pupils; 19 A sits under core goal 19. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Like 19 A, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is stimulating. One thing is named, respectful communication, in two settings: offline and online. The item does not say what makes communication respectful, names no platforms, media, situations or rules, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often leave out that a written message comes without a tone of voice or a face: a remark meant as a joke is read as an insult. Many also write online what they would not say to a person who is present.
