## Official wording

De leerlingen leren zich redzaam te gedragen in sociaal opzicht, als verkeersdeelnemer en als consument.

Source: Rijksoverheid (Basiswettenbestand, KOOP), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen WPO, bijlage, kerndoel 35.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupils learn to behave in a self-reliant way (*redzaam*, able to manage for oneself) socially, as a road user and as a consumer.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child near the end of primary school asks a person he or she does not know for information or help, settles a disagreement with another child by himself or herself, cycles a known route by the traffic rules, and buys something with money after comparing prices and reading the label.

### Limits

This 2006 goal is still in force. It is one numbered sentence of the decree, set for the end of primary school, under the heading *Mens en samenleving* (people and society). *Redzaam* means able to manage for oneself. Three fields are named: social, as a road user, and as a consumer. The goal names no situations, rules, acts or purchases, states no amounts and no number range, and sets no level of detail. For the road user it does not say in which role (on foot, by bicycle) or on which roads, and it states no safety condition such as adult supervision.

### Common mistakes

A child can say what to do and waits for an adult to do it. On the road a child knows the rule and does not apply it when with friends. As a consumer a child takes what an advertisement says for information.
