## Official wording

aanbieden van kennis en vaardigheden ten aanzien van basiswaarden van de democratische rechtsstaat en de diverse samenleving, gericht op respectvolle omgang;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

offering knowledge and skills with regard to basic values of the democratic state under the rule of law (*democratische rechtsstaat*) and the diverse society, aimed at respectful interaction (*omgang*);

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 hears and reads what the basic values are and what a diverse society is, and practises skills that go with them: letting another person finish speaking, asking about a habit or a belief that he or she does not know, and disagreeing without insulting.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 19 A, which says that the school stimulates social and societal competences of pupils; 19 A sits under core goal 19. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Like 19 A, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is offering, and what is offered is knowledge and skills, with one stated aim: respectful interaction (*omgang*, how people deal with one another). The item does not say which basic values are meant; item 20 A a, under core goal 20, names three. The wording leaves open whether the basic values are those of both the democratic *rechtsstaat* and the diverse society, or whether the diverse society is a second subject beside the basic values. The item names no knowledge, skills or situations and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often know a value as a word and leave out what it asks of them: a child says that everyone is of equal worth and still laughs at a habit he or she does not know.
