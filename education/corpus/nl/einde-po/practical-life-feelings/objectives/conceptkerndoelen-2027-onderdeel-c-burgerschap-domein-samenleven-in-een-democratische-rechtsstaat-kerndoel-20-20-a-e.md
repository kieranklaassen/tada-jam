## Official wording

herkennen en benoemen hoe basiswaarden van de democratische rechtsstaat een rol spelen in de samenleving en het eigen leven.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising and naming how basic values of the democratic state under the rule of law (*democratische rechtsstaat*) play a part in society and in one's own life.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at a news item, a rule of the school or an event in his or her own day and says which basic value plays a part in it: freedom in choosing a club or a belief, equal worth in a rule that holds for all, solidarity in a collection for others. The child names one case from society and one from his or her own life.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 20 A, which says that the pupil reasons about the importance of basic values of the democratic *rechtsstaat*; 20 A sits under core goal 20. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Two verbs: recognising and naming. Two places are named: society and the pupil's own life. The item names no value; item 20 A a names three. It names no situations or examples and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child sees the values in the news and in politics and not in his or her own day. A child attaches the name of a value to a case without saying how the value plays a part in it.
