## Official wording

stimuleren van sociale vaardigheden en kritische denkvaardigheden;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

stimulating social skills and critical thinking skills;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 takes turns in a talk, asks for help and offers it, and settles with a classmate who does which part of a shared task. The child asks "how do you know that?" about a claim, looks for a second source, and says what speaks for and against a view before choosing.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 19 A, which says that the school stimulates social and societal competences of pupils; 19 A sits under core goal 19. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Like 19 A, it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. The verb is stimulating. Two kinds of skill are named: social skills and critical thinking skills. The item names no skill of either kind, no situations and no ways, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This item is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. Children most often take thinking critically to mean finding fault with another person's view, and leave out testing their own.
