## Official wording

ervaringen opdoen met het omgaan met verschillende perspectieven en ervaringen van anderen, overeenkomsten en verschillen, oordeel uitstellen, omgaan met mogelijkheden en gedrag van anderen.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 4.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experience with dealing with different perspectives and experiences of others, similarities and differences, postponing judgement, dealing with *mogelijkheden* (possibilities or abilities) and behaviour of others.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 hears a classmate tell how he or she sees or has lived something, asks a question about it, and says what is alike and what differs from his or her own view. The child waits with a judgement until the other has finished, and works with a classmate who can do other things or behaves otherwise than he or she expected.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 20 B, which says that the pupil explores how he or she can deal with diversity in society; 20 B sits under core goal 20. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is gaining experience: the item asks that the pupil takes part, and names no result or level. The list is loosely built: after "dealing with different perspectives and experiences of others" come "similarities and differences", "postponing judgement" and a second "dealing with"; the wording leaves open whether similarities and differences depend on "dealing with" or on "gaining experience with". The Dutch *mogelijkheden* can mean possibilities and can mean abilities. The item does not say who the others are, names no situations and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What pupils most often leave out is postponing judgement: they call another view wrong or strange before they have heard it. Many also notice the differences and leave out the similarities.
