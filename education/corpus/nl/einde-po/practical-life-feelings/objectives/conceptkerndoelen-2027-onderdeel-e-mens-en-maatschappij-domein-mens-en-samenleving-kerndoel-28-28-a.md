## Official wording

De leerling verkent economische keuzes van mensen en mogelijke gevolgen.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil explores economic choices of people and possible consequences.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 looks at a choice about money or goods, for example buying now or saving, or buying new or second-hand, and lists what could follow from each option. The child asks someone how he or she made such a choice and compares it with the choice of another person.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 28, which is about how people live together with one another. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Its items 28 A a to 28 A e name work and income, cooperation, what influences choices, choices about money received, expenses, saving, *lenen* (borrowing or lending) and risks, and consequences of consumption. The verb is exploring. The sentence says "possible" consequences: it does not say that a consequence follows. It names no choice or situation, does not say which choice is right, and sets no level of detail. This goal states no number range and no amounts.

### Common mistakes

A child sees only the consequence that comes at once and not the later one: money spent today is not there next month. A child takes it that people always choose freely and leaves out that some have less to choose from.
