## Official wording

verkennen hoe mensen met werk en inkomen in hun levensonderhoud voorzien;

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 10.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring how people provide for their livelihood with work and income;

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 asks adults what work they do, and lists what a household needs money for: housing, food, clothing, energy. The child compares kinds of work and notices that some income does not come from a job, for example a benefit or a pension.

### Limits

This is an item under goal sentence 28 A, which says that the pupil explores economic choices of people and possible consequences; 28 A sits under core goal 28. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. The verb is exploring. Two means are named, work and income, and one end: providing for one's livelihood (*levensonderhoud*, what a person needs to live on). The item names no occupations, kinds of income or situations, states no amounts, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child takes it that money comes from the bank or the card and does not connect it with work. A child counts only paid jobs as work and leaves out caring and voluntary work. A child thinks the whole income is free to spend and leaves out fixed costs.
