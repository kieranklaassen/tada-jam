## Official wording

De leerling neemt op een veilige en verantwoorde wijze deel aan het verkeer.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), pages 10-11.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The pupil takes part in traffic in a safe and responsible way.

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child of 11 or 12 walks and cycles on the street and rides along as a passenger. The child looks before crossing, signals a turn with the arm, gives way where the rule says so, and watches what other road users do.

### Limits

This is a goal sentence (*doelzin*, the sentence printed under a core goal with a capital letter) under core goal 28, which is about how people live together with one another. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. Its items 28 E a to 28 E d name the traffic rules for pedestrians, cyclists and passengers, choosing safe routes and avoiding dangerous situations, acting safely in traffic situations in the surroundings of the school, and taking account of other road users. The sentence gives two manners, safe and responsible, and defines neither. It does not say in which role, on which roads or how far from home or school the pupil takes part, and it states no safety condition such as adult supervision. It names no situations and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

A child knows the rule and does not apply it when cycling with friends or when in a hurry. A child assumes that drivers have seen him or her. A child misjudges the speed and distance of an approaching vehicle when crossing.
