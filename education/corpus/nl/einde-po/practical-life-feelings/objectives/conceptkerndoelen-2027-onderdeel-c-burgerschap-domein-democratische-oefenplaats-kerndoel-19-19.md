## Official wording

De school geeft vorm aan de democratische oefenplaats.

Source: Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (via internetconsultatie.nl), Besluit vernieuwde kerndoelen overige leergebieden (consultation draft), page 3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

The school gives shape to the democratic practice ground (*democratische oefenplaats*, a place for practising democracy).

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

At a school that does this, a child of 11 or 12 practises with classmates what living together in a democracy asks: saying what he or she thinks, listening to someone who thinks otherwise, and reaching a decision together about a matter of the group. The child takes part in what the group then carries out.

### Limits

This is the sentence of core goal 19 itself. This is a draft goal, not yet in force. It is addressed to the school: it says what the school does and is not a requirement on the pupil. One goal sentence, 19 A, and its items 19 A a to 19 A d say what it covers: the school stimulates social and societal competences of pupils, by offering knowledge and skills, stimulating skills and respectful communication, and offering activities. The record's code scope places the goal in the part of the draft on citizenship (*burgerschap*), in the domain *democratische oefenplaats*. The sentence does not define *democratische oefenplaats*. It names no activities, situations or ways of practising and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

This sentence is about what the school does, so it has no typical error of a child. What children most often leave out is listening: many say what they think and do not take in the view of a classmate who thinks otherwise. Many also take deciding together to mean that the largest group decides everything.
