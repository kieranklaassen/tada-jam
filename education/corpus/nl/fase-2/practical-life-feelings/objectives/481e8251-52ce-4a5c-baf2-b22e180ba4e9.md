## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child practises on another child or on a doll: rinsing a graze and putting on a plaster, holding a burn under running water, pressing a cloth on a bleeding wound, and staying with someone who has fallen while another child fetches an adult. The child practises what to say when calling for help: who he or she is, where, and what happened. A child of 7 fetches an adult, stays with the hurt child and puts on a plaster; a child of 10 carries out fixed steps in order and gives a clear report by telephone.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is getting to know, which describes a first meeting, not mastery. The skills are called simple; the statement names none, names no injuries or situations, and states no safety condition such as adult supervision. Dealing with simple health problems is the subject of `ojw/pdm/2/08/fase2`.

### Common mistakes

Children run to help and do not check first whether the place is safe for themselves. They start treating and forget to fetch or call an adult. On the telephone they say what happened and leave out where they are.
