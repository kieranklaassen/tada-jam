## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

becoming aware that people flee from their own country for different reasons (war, identity, natural disasters)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child listens to or reads the story of a child who had to leave home, and says what that child had to leave behind. The child sorts short stories or pictures by the reason for fleeing and finds the countries on a map or globe. A child of 7 says that some people have to leave because their home is not safe; a child of 10 names different reasons and says how fleeing differs from moving house by choice.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is becoming aware. The brackets name war, identity and natural disasters. The statement does not explain "identity", names no countries, groups or events, and sets no level of detail for what people who flee go through. The content-line document (the institute's PDF of the line De samenleving) underlines the three words in the brackets and refers for underlined terms to a separate elaboration of knowledge topics, which the pack does not hold. The statement is about the fact that people flee and the reasons for it, and names nothing of what follows the flight.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out the difference between fleeing and moving: they think people who flee chose to leave and can go back when they want. They also know war as the only reason, and they take what they hear about one group as true for everyone who flees.
