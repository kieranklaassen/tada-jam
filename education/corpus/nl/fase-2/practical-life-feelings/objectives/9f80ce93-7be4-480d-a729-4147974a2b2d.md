## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group talk a child hears what *integriteit*, sexual *integriteit* included, stands for, and says in his or her own words why it matters. A child of 7 says this when asked; a child of 10 brings it into the talk unasked.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is realising. The Dutch *integriteit* can mean that a person's body and person are not to be violated, and can mean acting uprightly; the statement does not choose. The word "sexual" stands in brackets, so the statement covers integrity in general and sexual integrity. It names no parts of the body, situations or acts and sets no level of detail. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*).

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out the other person: they see why it matters for themselves and not that it matters for others in the same way.
