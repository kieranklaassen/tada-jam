## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising that friendships can differ but always have trust and respect as their basis

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child tells about his or her friends and what is different between the friendships: one to play football with, one to talk with, one who lives far away. The child gives an example of trust and of respect from his or her own friendships. A child of 7 tells what he or she does with each friend and that a friend keeps a promise; a child of 10 uses the words trust and respect and says that each of the friendships rests on them.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is realising. The statement says two things: friendships can differ, and they always have trust and respect as their basis. It does not say in what friendships differ, does not define trust or respect, and does not mention making friends, quarrels or the end of a friendship. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*).

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out that friendships can differ: they think a real friend is one best friend who does everything with them, and take a friend's other friendship as a loss. They also think a friend must always agree with them.
