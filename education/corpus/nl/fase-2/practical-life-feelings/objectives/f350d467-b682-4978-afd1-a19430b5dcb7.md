## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

becoming aware of the digitalisation of society and the impact this has on one's own living environment

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child keeps a list for one day of every moment at home, at school and in the street where a screen, card or code is used: paying in the shop, checking in on the bus, a parent's phone, the digital board. The child asks a grandparent or an older neighbour how the same thing was done before, and compares. A child of 7 names the devices and what they are used for; a child of 10 names what has become easier and what has become harder or less safe, for example for a person who has no phone.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is becoming aware, of two things: the digitalisation of society, and the impact this has on one's own living environment. The statement does not say whether the impact is good or bad, names no devices, apps or rules, and does not ask that a child uses a device.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out what they do not hold in their hands: they count phones, tablets and game consoles and not the cash desk, the traffic light or the library desk. They also think it has always been this way, and they think what is on a screen is always true.
