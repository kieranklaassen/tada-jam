## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sets up a stall at a class market, puts prices on things, sells, gives change and counts the takings afterwards. The child compares the price of the same product in two shops or two sizes, and makes a savings plan for something he or she wants: how much each week and for how many weeks. The child asks at home how the family pays: with coins, a card or a phone. A child of 7 pays and gives change with coins and notes and saves in a money box; a child of 10 compares prices, makes a plan to save for something he or she wants and can say that money on a card is the same money as money in the hand.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is investigating. The brackets name buying, selling, paying and saving. The statement names no amounts, coins, notes or ways of paying. This goal states no number range. Advertising is the subject of `ojw/ds/2/02/fase2`.

### Common mistakes

Children think a shop keeps all the money it takes in, and do not think of what the shop itself paid. They think money on a card or phone is not real money or does not run out. They take the price as the worth of a thing, so that the dearer of two is always the better.
