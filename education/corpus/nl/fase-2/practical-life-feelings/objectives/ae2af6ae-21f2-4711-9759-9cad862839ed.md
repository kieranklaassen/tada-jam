## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

being aware that personal characteristics are not fixed, but develop under the influence of age, practice and social environment

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child compares work from the start of the year with work from now, or a baby photo with a recent one, and says what he or she can do now that was not possible then and what made it so. The child picks one thing to get better at, practises for some weeks and marks the progress on a chart. The child asks a parent or grandparent what he or she was like as a child and what changed. A child of 7 sees change in what he or she can do, such as reading and cycling; a child of 10 sees it in traits too, such as being shy or impatient, and names practice and the people around him or her as causes.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording is being aware. The statement names three influences: age, practice and social environment. It does not say which personal characteristics are meant, gives no examples, and does not say whose characteristics, so it covers the child and other people.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out practice: they take being good or bad at something as how they are ("I am just not a maths person"). They also judge another child by how he or she behaved years before, and they think change comes with age alone.
