## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

bearing responsibility for one's own living environment and making an active contribution to care for the environment

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child takes a turn at a task such as emptying the paper bin into the right container, watering the plants or turning off lights and taps when the room empties. With others the child clears litter from the schoolyard or a nearby street and separates paper, plastic and food waste. A child of 7 does an assigned task once reminded of it; a child of 10 sees that something needs doing, does it without being asked and proposes a task for the group.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two things are named: bearing responsibility for one's own living environment, and making an active contribution to care for the environment. The statement names no tasks and no topic such as waste, water or energy, and does not say what one's own living environment covers. It does not say whether the child acts alone or with others.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out what is not theirs: they clear up what they dropped themselves and leave the rest. They also care for a place while an adult watches and stop afterwards, and they take a single action day for the whole of the contribution.
