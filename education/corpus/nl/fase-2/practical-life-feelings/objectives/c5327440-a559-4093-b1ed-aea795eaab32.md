## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child washes hands with soap after the toilet, before eating and after blowing the nose, coughs into the elbow, and puts on clean clothes after sport. The child brushes teeth, washes and combs hair, and keeps nails short and clean. A child of 7 washes hands with soap before eating; a child of 10 showers and puts on clean clothes after sport.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is four words long in Dutch and names no parts of hygiene (such as hands, teeth, hair, clothing or the toilet), no frequency and no level of independence. The link between hygiene, food and health is the subject of `ojw/pdm/2/03/fase2`.

### Common mistakes

Children wet their hands and skip the soap or the drying. They wash only what looks dirty and do not think of what cannot be seen. They do the steps only when reminded and take the reminder as the reason, so the habit stops when nobody checks.
