## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

With a group a child takes part in a talk on a question such as "can you be brave and afraid at once?" or "is a copy of a drawing the same drawing?": the child gives an answer with a reason, asks another child what he or she means, and gives an example that does not fit someone's answer. At the end the child says what he or she now thinks and what is still open. A child of 7 answers from his or her own experience and listens to what another child answers; a child of 10 adds to or opposes what another said, changes his or her mind for a reason and accepts that the talk ends with the question still open.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is investigating, and the word "together" makes it a shared activity. The statement sets a condition on how it is done: no fixed answers are assumed, and none are expected at the end. It names no questions, topics, length or group size.

### Common mistakes

Children look to the teacher for the right answer and stop thinking when no answer is confirmed. They tell a story about themselves that has nothing to do with the question. They take a different answer as an attack and defend their own without asking what the other child means.
