## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child mends a torn book or a broken toy in place of throwing it away, brings a bottle that can be refilled, and takes part in a swap table for outgrown clothes and toys. With the group the child weighs how much paper or food is thrown away in a week, agrees on one change and weighs again. A child of 7 carries out an agreed habit, such as using both sides of a sheet; a child of 10 says why the habit matters for later and proposes a change for the class or school.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is five words long in Dutch. The verb is working on, so the statement describes doing, not knowing. It names no actions, topics or scale, does not say what sustainable (*duurzaam*) covers, and does not say whether the child works alone or with others. The content-line document (the institute's PDF of the line De ruimte om je heen) underlines *duurzame* and refers for underlined terms to a separate elaboration of knowledge topics, which the pack does not hold.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out the reason: they follow a habit such as separating waste as a rule and cannot say what it is for. They also think that recycling is enough and leave out using less, and they think only adults or governments can change anything.
