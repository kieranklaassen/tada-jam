## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child tells about a visit to the family doctor, the dentist, the school doctor, a hospital or a pharmacy: why he or she went, who was there, what was done and how it felt. The child listens to another child's account and asks a question, and brings an object or picture such as a plaster cast, a brace or a vaccination booklet. A child of 7 tells what happened and whether it hurt; a child of 10 says what each health worker is for, and why a treatment that was unpleasant was needed.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discussing experiences, so what is described is telling and listening, not knowledge of health care. The statement does not say whose experiences, names no professions, places or treatments, and sets no level of detail.

### Common mistakes

Children tell about the pain or the fear and leave out why the visit was needed. They call every health worker "the doctor". They take one bad experience as how it always goes, and expect every visit to hurt.
