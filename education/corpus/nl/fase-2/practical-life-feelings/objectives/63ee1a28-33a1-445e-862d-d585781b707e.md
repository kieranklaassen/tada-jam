## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

empathising with and taking into account the feelings, wishes and views of others (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before choosing an activity, dividing a task or planning a party a child asks the others what they want and adjusts the plan so that more of them can join. The child retells a quarrel or a story from the side of another person: what that person wanted, felt and thought. A child of 7 does this for someone who is present, and says what that person feels; a child of 10 does it too for someone who is absent or whom he or she does not like, and changes his or her own plan for that person.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording ends with the printed marker (b), the curriculum institute's sign for an aspect of citizenship (*burgerschap*). Two things are named: empathising with others, and taking them into account; and three things of others are named: feelings, wishes and views. The statement does not say who the others are and names no situations. It does not say how far a child's own wishes give way.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out that another person can want or think something else in the same situation: they offer what they themselves would like. They also take others into account when these are friends and not otherwise, or they take it to mean giving in every time.
