behaving safely in the street and as an (independent) participant in traffic

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child walks or cycles a practised route: stops at the kerb or at the give-way markings, looks both ways and behind, holds out an arm before turning and keeps to the right-hand side of the cycle path. On the playground the child practises braking, looking over the shoulder while riding in a straight line, and riding with one hand on the handlebars. A child of 7 does this on foot on a known route and cycles where there is little traffic; a child of 10 cycles a known route and gives way correctly at crossings.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names the street and taking part in traffic. The word "independent" (*zelfstandig*) stands in brackets, so the statement leaves open whether the child takes part independently; it does not say whether on foot, by bicycle or otherwise. It names no traffic rules, signs or kinds of road and states no safety condition such as adult supervision.

### Common mistakes

Children look and go at the same moment: they turn the head and ride on without taking in what they see. They hold out an arm and take that as the right to turn, without looking behind. They judge how far away a car is but not how fast it comes.
