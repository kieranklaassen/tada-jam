## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child reads the task on the card, fetches what is needed, starts without waiting to be told, and works on while the teacher is busy with other children. When stuck, the child rereads the instruction, tries another way or asks a neighbour before asking the teacher, and at the end checks the work and puts things away. A child of 7 carries out a task that was set and works on while the teacher is busy with others; a child of 10 chooses the order of the work himself or herself and records what is done.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement gives two phrasings joined by a slash: carrying out tasks independently, and working independently. It names no tasks, no length of time and no degree of help, and does not say what independent (*zelfstandig*) rules out. It stands under the heading living together and working together (*Samen leven en samenwerken*).

### Common mistakes

Children wait for help at the first difficulty and do nothing in the meantime. They take working independently to mean never asking anything, and go on in the wrong direction. They finish fast and do not check what they made.
