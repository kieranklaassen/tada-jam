## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

paying attention to body posture and movement, such as a sound (sitting) posture and rest/relaxation at suitable moments

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child sets the chair and table so that both feet rest on the floor and the back is supported, holds a book or tablet where the neck is not bent far, and lifts a heavy bag with bent knees. After a long time sitting the child stands up, stretches, walks or plays outside, and after effort the child sits or lies down quietly and breathes slowly. A child of 7 corrects posture when reminded; a child of 10 notices a stiff neck or tired eyes, changes posture without a reminder, and takes a break from sitting.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording is paying attention to, which describes an attitude, not a performance. Body posture and movement are the subject; a sound posture, with "sitting" in brackets, and rest or relaxation at suitable moments are given as examples (*zoals*). The statement names no exercises, sports or amounts of time.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out the signals of their own body: they sit bent over a book or a screen until something hurts. They also think rest is only for when one is ill, and they sit up straight when told and slump again soon after.
