## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing consciously with one's own feelings and views and expressing them in a way others can understand

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child says "I feel ... because ..." and "I think ... because ..." in a group talk or in a quarrel, in place of sulking, shouting or saying "never mind". The child draws or writes what happened and how it felt, and checks with a listener or reader whether it was understood. A child of 7 says a feeling and its cause when asked; a child of 10 separates what he or she feels from what he or she thinks about it, and chooses words and a moment so that the other person can follow.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two things are named: dealing consciously with one's own feelings and views, and expressing them so that others understand. The statement does not say how they are expressed (in words, in writing or otherwise) and does not say who the others are. It names no feelings, views or situations.

### Common mistakes

Children expect others to know what they feel without being told, and are angry when they do not. They state a feeling as blame ("you are mean"), so that the listener hears an attack and not the feeling. They mix up a feeling and a view: "I feel that it is unfair".
