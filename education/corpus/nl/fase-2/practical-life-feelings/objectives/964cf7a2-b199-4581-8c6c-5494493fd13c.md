## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising that people kiss each other, caress, touch each other or show another form of sexual behaviour because they love someone or because they find it pleasant

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group talk a child hears and says that people kiss, caress and touch each other because they love someone or because they find it pleasant. The child asks the questions he or she has about it. A child of 7 gives the reasons when asked; a child of 10 brings them into the talk unasked.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is realising. The statement names kissing, caressing and touching and adds "another form of sexual behaviour" without naming any; it gives two reasons, loving someone and finding it pleasant. It names no parts of the body, situations or ages and sets no level of detail. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*).

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out the reasons and react to the subject itself with giggling or disgust. They also take every kiss or touch to mean being in love, and do not tell it apart from a kiss from a parent or a friend.
