verkennen van de rol van planten en dieren bij technologische ontwikkelingen en uitvindingen (bijv. klittenband, neus hogesnelheidstrein, oppervlak zwempak)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring the role of plants and animals in technological developments and inventions (e.g. hook-and-loop fastener, the nose of the high-speed train, the surface of a swimsuit)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child presses a burr onto a woollen sleeve, looks at its hooks through a magnifying glass, and then looks at both sides of a strip of hook-and-loop fastener. The child folds a paper copy of a maple seed and drops it to see it spin. A child of 7 sees that the two look alike; a child of 10 says which problem the copied feature solves and draws an invention of their own that copies a plant or animal.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets give three examples: hook-and-loop fastener, the nose of the high-speed train and the surface of a swimsuit. The wording does not say which plant or animal each one is taken from.

### Common mistakes

Thinking the invention is made out of the plant or animal instead of copying how it works. Thinking the plant or animal has the feature in order to help people. Seeing that two things look alike without saying what the shared feature does.
