## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

setting up and carrying out a simple experiment with a physical phenomenon

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child picks a question about light, sound, heat, force, magnets or electricity, such as how many paper clips each of three magnets holds, and says what they expect. The child sets the test up, changes one thing, measures or counts, records and tells what the result was. A child of 7 predicts and observes within steps an adult has set; a child of 10 plans the test, repeats it and records the results in a table.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The phenomenon is not named in this statement; the other statements in this cluster, `ojw/nattech/2/01/fase2` to `ojw/nattech/2/07/fase2`, name light, sound, temperature, force, magnetism, electricity and energy. The experiment is to be simple, and the wording does not say what that means.

### Common mistakes

Changing several things at once. Measuring in a different way each time, for example from a different distance. Stopping after one trial.
