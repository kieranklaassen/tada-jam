## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising that weather and seasons have an influence on people, animals and plants

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child visits the same tree in each season and draws it, and notes which birds and insects are seen in which months. The child says what they wear and what they play outside in different weather. A child of 7 names direct links, such as cold weather and a coat; a child of 10 can follow a longer link, such as less food in winter and birds that leave or animals that sleep through the winter.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Three groups are named: people, animals and plants. The statement names no particular animals, plants or effects and gives no examples.

### Common mistakes

Believing that a tree is dead once it has dropped its leaves. Believing that all animals sleep through the winter. Explaining what plants and animals do as wishes, for example "the tree wants to rest".
