## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tallies a month of daily weather notes and says which weather occurred most often, then compares one day with that tally. The child compares this with a letter, photo or film from a child in a much warmer or much colder country. A child of 7 talks about what the weather is like today and what it is usually like here; a child of 10 reads a simple graph of temperature or rainfall per month.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement asks only for seeing the connection. It names no climates or climate zones, gives no definitions and does not mention climate change.

### Common mistakes

Using the words weather and climate as if they mean the same. Concluding from one cold day or week that the climate is cold. Thinking that climate is tomorrow's weather.
