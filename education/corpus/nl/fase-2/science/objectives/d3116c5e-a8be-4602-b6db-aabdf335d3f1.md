carrying out a working drawing or a manual step by step

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child builds a model from a construction set by its picture instructions, folds a paper figure from a diagram or follows a recipe. The child lays the parts out, does one step, compares the model with the picture and ticks the step off. A child of 7 builds a model from a construction set, following its picture instructions step by step; a child of 10 makes a wooden boat, following the working drawing step by step.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The child follows a working drawing or a manual, and does so step by step. The wording gives no kind, length or difficulty. The wording does not ask for making a drawing of one's own.

### Common mistakes

Skipping steps, or building from the picture of the finished object. Placing a part mirrored or turned compared with the drawing. Not checking after each step, so that an early error is found late.
