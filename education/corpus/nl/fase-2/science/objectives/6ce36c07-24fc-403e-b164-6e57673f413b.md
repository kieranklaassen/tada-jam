## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising the differences in weather across space (places in the Netherlands) and over time

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child compares the weather at school with what a relative or another class elsewhere in the country reports for the same day. On a weather map of the Netherlands the child points to where it rains and where it is dry. The child also compares today's weather notes with yesterday's and with last month's. A child of 7 compares today with yesterday at school; a child of 10 reads the symbols on a weather map.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. For differences across space the brackets name places in the Netherlands, which sets the area; no place outside the Netherlands and no particular place inside it is named. For differences over time the statement gives no span (hours, days or seasons).

### Common mistakes

Assuming the weather is the same everywhere at the same moment. Reading a weather map as the weather of the whole day instead of one moment. Thinking of differences over time only as the seasons, and missing the changes within a day or a week.
