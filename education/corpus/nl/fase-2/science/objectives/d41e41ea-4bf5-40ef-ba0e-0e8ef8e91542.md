## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

encountering, wondering at and realising the great variety of plants, animals and mushrooms (fungi)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child searches a square of grass, a hedge or the ground under a log, and counts how many different kinds of plants and small animals are there. The child draws or photographs them, and in autumn looks at mushrooms without picking them. A child of 7 finds and shows each new thing; a child of 10 groups the finds, counts the kinds and uses names.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Three groups are named: plants, animals, and mushrooms, with fungi in brackets. The verbs are encountering, wondering and realising; naming or classifying is not asked. No numbers or names of species are given.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the small animals: they count only large or furry animals as animals, so that insects, worms and snails are not counted. Many count mushrooms as plants, or count different kinds as one, for example calling every low green plant grass.
