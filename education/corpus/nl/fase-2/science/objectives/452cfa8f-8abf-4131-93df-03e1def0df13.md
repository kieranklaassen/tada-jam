## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring adaptations of plants and animals to temperature, drought, wind and enemies, through which they can survive in a particular environment

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at and carefully feels a cactus beside a plant with broad thin leaves, and finds the thick skin and the spines. The child looks at the thorns of a bramble, the shell of a snail and an insect that is hard to see on bark, and sorts pictures by what each feature protects against. A child of 7 names the feature and the danger; a child of 10 explains how the feature helps in that place.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Four things to which plants and animals are adapted are named: temperature, drought, wind and enemies. No environments or species are named.

### Common mistakes

Believing that one animal or plant changes itself on purpose because it needs to, for example that an animal grew thick fur because it wanted to be warm. Treating every feature of an animal or plant as an adaptation to something. Thinking that an animal or plant that is well adapted can live anywhere.
