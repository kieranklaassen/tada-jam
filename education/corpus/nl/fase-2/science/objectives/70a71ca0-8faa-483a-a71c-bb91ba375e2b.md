## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

observing the behaviour of an animal in relation to its surroundings

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child watches a blackbird in the school garden and notes where it looks for food and what it does when a cat comes by. The child watches birds at a feeding table and notes what they do when someone comes near. The child writes or draws what the animal did. A child of 7 watches the birds at the feeding table and tells what they did when someone came near; a child of 10 watches the snails in the school garden and notes that they come out when the ground is wet.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. It speaks of an animal (*een dier*, which sets no number) and names no species and no kind of surroundings. It asks for observing; it does not ask for an explanation of the behaviour.

### Common mistakes

Describing the behaviour as human feelings, such as "it is sad" or "it is shy". Disturbing the animal and then recording its reaction to the child. Drawing a conclusion from one short look.
