## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating that plants, animals and humans need food, water, light and the right temperature for growth and development

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child grows cress or beans in several pots: one without water, one in a dark cupboard, one in the fridge and one with everything, and draws and measures each pot every few days. The child helps care for a class animal and notes what it eats and drinks. A child of 7 waters the pots and looks; a child of 10 changes one condition per pot and measures in centimetres.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Three groups are named (plants, animals and humans) and four needs (food, water, light and the right temperature). Air is not among the four. No species are named.

### Common mistakes

Believing that plants eat soil the way animals eat food. Believing that more water or more light is always better. Changing several conditions in one pot, so that it cannot be said which one made the difference.
