experiencing the beauty and aesthetic value of nature for humans

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Outdoors the child chooses something they find beautiful, such as a leaf, a piece of bark, a shell or a spider's web with dew on it, and looks at it closely with a magnifying glass. The child draws it, or lays found leaves and stones in order of colour, and says what they like about it. A child of 7 collects and shows; a child of 10 can say why, and notices that classmates choose other things.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about experiencing. It names no knowledge, no skill and no kind of nature.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the small and ordinary things and look for beauty only in flowers and furry animals. Many pick a flower or take an animal along to keep it, instead of leaving it where it is.
