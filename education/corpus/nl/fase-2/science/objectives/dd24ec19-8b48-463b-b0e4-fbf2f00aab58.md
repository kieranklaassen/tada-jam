## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child waters the class plants and first feels the soil with a finger. The child feeds a class animal the right amount, cleans its cage, and holds it low above the table with two hands. The child puts an insect back where it was found. A child of 7 strokes the class animal gently and puts it back in its cage; a child of 10 waters the plants when it is their turn and lets an animal be when it moves away.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about how the child acts. It names no plants, animals or tasks and no knowledge.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out finding out what the plant or animal needs: they give too much water or too much food, or treat an animal as if it wanted what a person wants, for example giving it sweets. Many hold a small animal too tightly or for too long.
