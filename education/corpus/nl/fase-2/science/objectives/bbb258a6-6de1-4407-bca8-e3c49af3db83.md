onderscheiden en verkennen bij jezelf van 'de van buitenaf waarneembare' inwendige lichaamsdelen en organen (hart, bloedsomloop, longen, spieren, skelet, ademhaling en spijsverteringsstelsel) en verkennen wat hun functie is

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

distinguishing and exploring on yourself the internal body parts and organs 'that can be perceived from the outside' (heart, blood circulation, lungs, muscles, skeleton, breathing and digestive system) and exploring what their function is

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child feels the heartbeat at the chest and the pulse at the wrist, before and after running. The child lays both hands on the ribs while breathing in and out, feels an arm muscle harden when lifting, and feels bones under the skin at the knuckles, ribs and skull. A child of 7 feels and names; a child of 10 counts the pulse at rest and after exercise and says what each organ does.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. It is limited to the child's own body and to inner parts as far as they can be noticed from outside. The brackets name seven: heart, blood circulation, lungs, muscles, skeleton, breathing and digestive system; the wording names no others.

### Common mistakes

Placing the heart far to the left and drawing it as the heart shape printed on greeting cards. Thinking that breathed-in air goes to the stomach, or that food falls into an empty space inside the body. Thinking muscles are found only in the arms and legs.
