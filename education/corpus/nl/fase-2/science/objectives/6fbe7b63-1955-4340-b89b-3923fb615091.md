oriënteren op de voortplanting en de functie(s) van voortplantingsorganen bij mensen en de vergelijking maken met dieren

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting an orientation on reproduction and the function(s) of the reproductive organs in humans, and making the comparison with animals

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a book or follows a lesson about how a baby begins and grows in the womb, with pictures or a model of the organs involved. The child compares this with an animal that is expecting or has young, such as a guinea pig or a sheep. A child of 7 looks at a book about how a baby begins and compares it with how a lamb begins; a child of 10 follows a lesson about how a baby begins and compares it with how the young of a guinea pig begin.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is *oriënteren* (getting an orientation): it names no result and no level. The statement names reproduction, the function or functions of the reproductive organs in humans, and a comparison with animals. It sets no level of detail and no vocabulary, and it does not use the words puberty or relationships.

### Common mistakes

Believing that a baby grows in the stomach, where the food goes. Believing that only the mother has a part in making a baby.
