## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining experience with and investigating the phenomenon of sound: sound sources; properties of sound (sound vibration, loudness, pitch, timbre); the relationship with hearing and hearing damage

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child holds a ruler over the edge of a table and plucks it, shorter and longer, feels their own throat while humming, and sees grains of rice jump on a drum skin. The child stretches elastic bands over a box, tighter and looser, and with closed eyes names which classmate or instrument is sounding. A child of 7 makes and feels sounds and sorts them into loud and soft, high and low; a child of 10 links length and tightness to pitch and the force of the stroke to loudness, and can say that loud sound harms hearing.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Four properties are in the brackets: vibration, loudness, pitch and timbre; the wording names no others. Hearing and hearing damage are named. No units are given.

### Common mistakes

Mixing up high and low with loud and soft. Expecting a harder stroke to give a higher note. Believing that hearing damaged by loud sound recovers, or that sound does harm only when it hurts.
