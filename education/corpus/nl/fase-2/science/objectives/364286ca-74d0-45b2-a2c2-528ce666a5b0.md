making observations of the weather with suitable weather instruments

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a thermometer that hangs outside at the same time each day, reads and then empties a rain gauge, and holds up a wind vane or a ribbon to see where the wind comes from. The child writes the readings in a table and compares the days. A child of 7 reads the thermometer outside and writes the reading in the table; a child of 10 reads the rain gauge and holds up the wind vane to see where the wind comes from.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The instruments are to be suitable; none is named, and no units are given. Which aspects of the weather there are is listed in `ojw/rojh/7/01/fase2` (rain, hail and snow, wind, temperature, cloud cover), not in this statement.

### Common mistakes

Reading a thermometer that hangs in the sun or is held by the bulb. Misreading the unnumbered marks between two numbers on a scale. Naming the wind by where it blows to instead of where it comes from.
