## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child keeps a record of everything eaten and drunk in one day and sorts the items into groups such as fruit and vegetables, bread and grains, and dairy. The child prepares a simple snack from fruit or raw vegetables and talks about how a morning without breakfast feels. A child of 7 sorts real foods or pictures; a child of 10 looks at a whole day or week and compares it with advice on healthy eating.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no foods, food groups, nutrients or amounts and does not say what makes an eating pattern good. It stands in the cluster on growth and development.

### Common mistakes

Sorting foods only into "good" and "bad", so that a food is either always fine or never allowed. Looking at one meal instead of what is eaten over days. Leaving drinks out of the record.
