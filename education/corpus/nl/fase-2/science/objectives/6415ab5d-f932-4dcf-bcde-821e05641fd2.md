## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering similarities and differences in appearance, build and movement (and locomotion) between people, and between humans and mammals

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Classmates compare height, hand span, and hair and eye colour. The child compares their own arm and leg with the legs of a dog, cat or horse, using a pet, a picture or a skeleton, and finds the same parts: four limbs, a backbone, hair. The child walks on hands and feet as a dog does and compares that with walking on two legs. A child of 7 names what can be seen; a child of 10 can find the same joint in a different place, such as the knee and the heel of a dog's hind leg.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two comparisons are named: between people, and between humans and mammals. Three aspects are named: appearance, build and movement. The comparison with animals is with mammals; no other group of animals is named.

### Common mistakes

Not counting humans as animals or as mammals. Noticing only the differences and not mentioning what is the same. Mistaking the heel joint of a dog's or horse's hind leg for a knee that bends backwards.
