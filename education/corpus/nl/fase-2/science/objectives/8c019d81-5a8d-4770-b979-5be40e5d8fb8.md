## Official wording

beseffen dat mensen, dieren en planten geboren worden of ontkiemen, groeien en ontwikkelen, zich kunnen voortplanten en zullen doodgaan (levenscyclus)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising that humans, animals and plants are born or germinate, grow and develop, can reproduce and will die (life cycle)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child follows a bean from seed to plant, to flowers and new seeds, and sees the plant die. The child lays cards of a chicken, a frog and a human in the order of their lives, and finds and talks about a dead insect or dead leaves. A child of 7 orders pictures and tells the story; a child of 10 draws the cycle and can say that the young continue it while each individual dies.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Three groups are named: humans, animals and plants. Four steps are named: being born or germinating, growing and developing, being able to reproduce, and dying. The wording says "can" reproduce. No life spans or species are given.

### Common mistakes

Not counting plants or seeds as alive, so that they are not thought to be born or to die. Reading a drawn cycle as one individual that starts again after death. Believing that death comes only from an accident or illness and not to every living thing.
