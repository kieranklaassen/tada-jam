verkennen van waarneembare eigenschappen van materialen zoals geleiden en isoleren van warmte, geluid en/of elektriciteit, de invloed van vocht (roesten)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring observable properties of materials, such as conducting and insulating heat, sound and/or electricity, and the influence of moisture (rusting)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child stands a metal spoon and a wooden spoon in warm water and feels which handle gets warm. The child wraps cups of warm water in different materials and feels them again later, places objects in a circuit of a battery and a bulb to see which make the bulb light, and leaves one iron nail in a damp place and one in a dry place for a week. A child of 7 feels and sorts; a child of 10 measures with a thermometer and keeps everything else the same.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The properties follow "such as" and are examples: conducting and insulating heat, sound and/or electricity, and the influence of moisture, with rusting in brackets. "And/or" leaves open which of the three are offered. The properties are to be observable.

### Common mistakes

Believing that metal in a room is colder than wood in the same room, because it feels colder to the hand. Believing that wool or a blanket makes things warm, so that a wrapped ice cube would melt sooner. Believing that all metals rust.
