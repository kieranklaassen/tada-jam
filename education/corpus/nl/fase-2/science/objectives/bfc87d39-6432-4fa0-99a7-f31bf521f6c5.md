## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

experiencing and investigating what the *weerbeeld* (the overall picture of the weather) is in each season (in rain, hail and snow, wind, temperature and the amount of cloud cover)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child goes outside at the same time each day for a week or two in each season, feels the wind and the temperature, looks at the sky and records what falls, how hard the wind blows, how warm it is and how much cloud there is. The child lays an autumn record beside a spring record and says what differs. A child of 7 records with drawings and words such as warm, cold and windy; a child of 10 reads a thermometer and fills in a table.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name the aspects of the weather: rain, hail and snow, wind, temperature and cloud cover. The statement names no units and no instruments; observing with weather instruments is in `ojw/rojh/7/05/fase2`. It names no place.

### Common mistakes

Treating one day as the whole season, so that a cold day in July means "it is not summer". Confusing hail with snow. Expecting snow every winter and sunshine every summer day.
