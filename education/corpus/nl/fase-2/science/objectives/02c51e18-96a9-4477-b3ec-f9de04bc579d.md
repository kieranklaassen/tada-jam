investigating the outward shape and build of the human limbs and their function (form and function)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child bends and stretches an arm and feels where it bends and where it does not, and counts the joints of one finger. The child traces a hand and a foot on paper and compares them, and tries to pick up a pencil without using the thumb. A child of 7 names the parts and what they do; a child of 10 can say how a shape fits a use, such as the thumb standing opposite the fingers for gripping.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. It is limited to the limbs of humans, and to their outward shape and build: what can be seen and felt from outside. The inner parts of the body are in `ojw/pdm/1/02/fase2`.

### Common mistakes

Thinking an arm or leg can bend anywhere along its length instead of only at the joints. Naming the parts without saying what they are for. Thinking the palm of the hand and the sole of the foot hold no bones.
