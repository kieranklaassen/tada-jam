opzetten en uitvoeren van een eenvoudig experiment met materialen, stoffen of voorwerpen

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

setting up and carrying out a simple experiment with materials, substances or objects

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child asks a question, such as which kind of paper soaks up the most water, and says what they expect. The child decides what to change and what to keep the same, carries the test out, measures or counts, writes or draws the result and says what was found. A child of 7 predicts and observes within steps an adult has set; a child of 10 plans a fair test, repeats it and records the results in a table.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The experiment is to be simple; the wording does not say what that means and names no steps of a method. Its subject is materials, substances or objects. The clusters on physical phenomena and on technical principles each hold a statement on experimenting of their own: `ojw/nattech/2/08/fase2` and `ojw/nattech/3/09/fase2`.

### Common mistakes

Changing more than one thing at a time. Making no prediction, or changing the prediction afterwards to fit the result. Reporting what was done instead of what was found.
