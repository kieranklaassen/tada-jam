## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating what you use and need your senses for in your surroundings, consciously experiencing how they work, and exploring what animals use their senses for

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child names objects in a bag by touch alone, points with closed eyes to where a sound comes from, tastes pieces of fruit with the nose pinched shut, and walks a short route blindfolded with a partner as guide. The child watches a snail's tentacles or a dog sniffing and says which sense the animal is using. A child of 7 links each sense to a part of the body; a child of 10 tests one sense at a time, compares results with classmates and compares their own senses with those of animals.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Three parts are named: what the child uses and needs the senses for, consciously experiencing how they work, and what animals use their senses for. The senses are not listed or counted, and no animals are named.

### Common mistakes

Thinking taste comes from the tongue alone, with no part for smell. Thinking that seeing happens because something goes out from the eye. Assuming that animals sense the world just as people do.
