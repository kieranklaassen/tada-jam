## Official wording

hanteren van eenvoudige zoekkaarten van planten en van dieren

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using simple *zoekkaarten* (identification cards: single sheets with pictures and questions that lead to the name of a plant or animal) of plants and of animals

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays a leaf, or a tray with a small water animal, beside the card and follows the questions or pictures one step at a time: legs or no legs, how many, a shell or none. The child arrives at a name and checks the picture against the real thing. A child of 7 matches by looking along the pictures; a child of 10 follows a key of yes-and-no questions and goes back a step after a wrong choice.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The cards are to be simple, and they are of plants and of animals. No groups of plants or animals are named. The statement is about using a card; it does not ask for names known from memory.

### Common mistakes

Going straight to the picture that looks most alike without following the steps. Matching on colour or size alone. Stopping at the first near match without checking the other features.
