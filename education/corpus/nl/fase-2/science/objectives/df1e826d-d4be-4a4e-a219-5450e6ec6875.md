## Official wording

ontwerpen en maken van een object naar eigen idee en inzicht waarbij rekening wordt gehouden met het gebruik van materialen

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

designing and making an object according to one's own idea and understanding, taking account of the use of materials

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child decides what to make, such as a boat that floats or a feeder for birds, sketches it, chooses among card, wood, cloth, plastic and foil and says why. The child cuts and joins the parts, tries the object out and changes it. A child of 7 starts building at once and chooses whatever lies nearest; a child of 10 draws a plan first, chooses a material for what it must do and tests the object against its purpose.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The object is the child's own idea; no object, material or tool is named. One condition is stated: the use of materials is taken into account.

### Common mistakes

Choosing a material for its colour or because it is nearest, not for what it must do, for example paper for a part that gets wet. Skipping the design and being unable to say what the object is for. Making a part too large for the strength of the material.
