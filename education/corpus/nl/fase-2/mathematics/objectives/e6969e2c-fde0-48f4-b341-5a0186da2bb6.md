## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays or draws the next pieces of a pattern, for example a border of triangles and squares that repeats, and says which rule decides what comes next. The child also makes a pattern with a rule of its own and lets another person continue it. A child of 7 continues a repeating row and points out the part that repeats; a child of 10 designs a pattern that covers a surface or grows by a rule and explains why a given piece does or does not fit.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal says "continuing or designing" patterns that have a regularity, and reasoning about this. Its own wording names no kind of pattern, no shapes or materials and no length of pattern. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: mosaic, bead necklaces, simple tilings (*vlakvullingen*, patterns that fill a flat surface); it is an example, not a limit. The record sits in the geometry cluster *Construeren* (constructing). Goals about patterns under *Verbanden* (rw/verb/2) are other records of this lane.

### Common mistakes

The child repeats only the last piece instead of the whole part that repeats. The child follows one feature of the pattern, such as colour, and misses a second feature that also changes, such as direction or size. The child continues the pattern correctly but cannot say the rule, or gives a rule that fits only the last few pieces.
