## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

composing given amounts in different ways with coins and banknotes and in doing so investigating what handy ways (*handig*, clever and convenient) are and what the way with the smallest number of coins and/or banknotes is

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child is given an amount and lays it out with coins and banknotes, then lays out the same amount in another way. The child counts how many coins and banknotes each way uses and looks for the way with the fewest, starting with the largest banknote or coin that fits. A child of 7 lays an amount out and then lays the same amount out in another way; a child of 10 looks for the way with the fewest coins and banknotes and says why no way uses fewer.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The amounts are given and are composed in different ways with coins and banknotes. The goal names two questions to investigate: which ways are handy, and which way uses the smallest number of coins and/or banknotes. This goal states no number range and does not say which coins and banknotes are used.

### Common mistakes

The child stops after one way and does not look for another. The child thinks that the way with more coins is worth more money. When looking for the fewest coins, the child does not start with the largest value that fits and ends with more coins than needed.
