kritisch denken en redeneren over gegevens in eenvoudige tabellen, staaf-, en beelddiagrammen

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

thinking critically and reasoning about data in simple tables, bar charts, and picture charts (*beelddiagrammen*, charts built from small pictures)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a chart and says what can and cannot be concluded from it: "most children here come by bicycle, but the chart does not say why". The child asks who was counted and when, notices a bar that cannot be right, and compares two charts of the same data with different scales. The child gives reasons for an answer by pointing at the data. A child of 7 says which is most and whether that fits what they know; a child of 10 asks how the data were collected and what the chart leaves out.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The word "simple" (*eenvoudige*) stands before the list of tables, bar charts and picture charts; the wording does not say what counts as simple. The goal is about thinking critically and reasoning about data; the wording names no kind of problem. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child reads more into the chart than it holds, such as a cause, or a claim about all children from the data of one group. The child takes the tallest bar for "the best". The child goes by the look of the chart and does not check the scale.
