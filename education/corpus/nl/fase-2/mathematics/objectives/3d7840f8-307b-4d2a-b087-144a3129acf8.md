## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child bundles ten ones into a ten and ten tens into a hundred, with sticks, blocks or money, and says that each place is worth ten times the place to its right. The child builds 364 from 3 hundreds, 6 tens and 4 ones and says what each digit is worth: 300, 60 and 4. The child swaps two digits and says what changes, and says why 406 needs its zero. A child of 7 shows with bundles what each digit of a number is worth; a child of 10 explains in words why each place is worth ten times the place to its right.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. This goal states no number range. Two things are explained: the base-ten structure of the number system, and the place value of the digits in numbers. The wording names no material and no number of places. The source prints "tientallig structuur" for *tientallige structuur*; the gloss gives what is plainly meant.

### Common mistakes

The child reads a digit at its face value and says that the 6 in 364 is worth 6. The child can name the places but cannot say that 364 also holds 36 tens. The child gives the zero in 406 no value and thinks it can be left out.
