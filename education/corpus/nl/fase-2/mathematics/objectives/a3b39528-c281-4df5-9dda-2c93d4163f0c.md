## Official wording

uitvoeren van eenvoudige bewerkingen met gegevens uit tabellen, beeld- en staafdiagrammen

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_cluster rw/verb/1; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau a3b39528-c281-4df5-9dda-2c93d4163f0c.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

carrying out simple operations with data from tables, picture charts (*beelddiagrammen*, charts built from small pictures) and bar charts

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads two values from a chart and works with them: how many children chose apple or pear together, how many more chose apple than pear, how many answered in all. From a table of prices the child works out what two things cost together. The child says which numbers were read and which calculation was done. A child of 7 reads the values in a picture chart and compares or adds them by counting the pictures; a child of 10 reads the values from a scale and writes down the calculation that was done.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The operations are "simple" (*eenvoudige*); the wording does not say which operations. The data come from tables, picture charts and bar charts. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child reads a value wrongly and then calculates correctly with it. For "how many more" the child gives the larger value and not the difference. For the total the child counts the bars or rows and does not add the values.
