## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning about quantities up to at least 1000 and about the order of magnitude of quantities within different contexts

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child answers questions such as: are there about 30 or about 300 children in our school, and would 1000 steps take you across the playground or across the town. The child gives a reason from something known, "a class has about 25 children and there are 12 classes", and decides whether a number that is named can be right. The child compares one number in different settings: 500 grains of rice are few, 500 people in a hall are many. A child of 7 reasons about a quantity that can be seen and checks by counting; a child of 10 reasons about a quantity that cannot be counted directly and gives a reason from something that is known.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The range is stated as "up to at least 1000" (*tot ten minste 1000*): no upper bound is set, and the Dutch *tot* leaves open whether 1000 itself is included. The range is tied to the quantities; the wording gives no number for "order of magnitude" (*orde van grootte*). The contexts are "different" (*verschillende*); the wording names none.

### Common mistakes

The child cannot imagine how much 1000 is and uses "a thousand" for any large number. The child accepts any number given in a situation without asking whether it can be right. The child thinks a number is many or few in itself, apart from what is counted.
