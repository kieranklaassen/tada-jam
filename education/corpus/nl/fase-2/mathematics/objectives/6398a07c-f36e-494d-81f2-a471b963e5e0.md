## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a multiplication or a division the child tells or shows how it was done: "I did 6 × 30 and 6 × 4 and joined them", or "I knew 8 × 7 = 56, so 56 divided by 7 is 8". The child draws groups, rows or a rectangle, or points at the written steps, and answers the question why a step is allowed. The child follows the way of another child and says in what it differs from their own. A child of 7 retells the steps with objects or a drawing; a child of 10 also says why the steps are allowed and why this way was chosen.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. This goal states no number range. It sits in the cluster on multiplying and dividing with whole numbers; the same wording stands in the cluster on adding and subtracting with whole numbers as rw/bew/1/10/fase2. Three things are explained: the strategies used, the calculations made and the reasoning followed. The wording names no form for the explanation.

### Common mistakes

When asked how, the child only gives the answer again. The child says "I just knew it" for a fact that was worked out in steps. The child leaves out a step that was done in the head, so the explanation cannot be followed.
