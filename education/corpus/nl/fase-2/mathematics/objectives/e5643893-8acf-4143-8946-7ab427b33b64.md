## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

calculating or approximating the area of rectangles via informal approaches and explaining the solution method in their own words

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child covers a rectangle with square tiles and counts them one by one, or lays one row, counts the rows and adds the rows together or multiplies. When the tiles do not fit exactly the child gives an answer with "about", for example a little more than twelve tiles. The child tells in its own words how it found the answer. A child of 7 counts the tiles one by one or row by row; a child of 10 multiplies the number of rows by the number of tiles in a row and explains why this gives the same as counting.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about rectangles only. The area is calculated or approximated "via informal approaches", which the goal does not name, and the child explains its way of solving in its own words. The goal does not mention the formula length times width. It names no unit, and no goal of this cluster (rw/m/2) in this band names a standard measure for area such as cm² or m². This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child adds the length and the width, or gives the perimeter, instead of finding what covers the rectangle. The child counts only the tiles along the edge. When counting one by one the child skips tiles or counts tiles twice, most often at the corners.
