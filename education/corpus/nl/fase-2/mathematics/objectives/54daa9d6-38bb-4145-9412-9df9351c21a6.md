## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

folding figures on the basis of descriptions and/or step-by-step plans with examples

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes a sheet of paper and folds a figure, for example a boat or a small box, by following a description or a step-by-step plan one step at a time. After each fold the child compares the paper with the example for that step and turns the paper so that it lies the same way. A child of 7 follows a step-by-step plan in which each step has a picture; a child of 10 folds from a description in words and looks at the example to check.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The figures are folded from descriptions, from step-by-step plans, or from both ("and/or"). The wording leaves open whether "with examples" belongs only to the step-by-step plans or also to the descriptions. The goal does not name the figures that are folded, the material that is folded or the number of steps.

### Common mistakes

The child skips a step or does two steps in the wrong order. The child makes the right fold in the wrong direction or on the wrong side, because the paper lies differently from the example. The child folds inexactly, so that corners do not meet and later steps no longer fit.
