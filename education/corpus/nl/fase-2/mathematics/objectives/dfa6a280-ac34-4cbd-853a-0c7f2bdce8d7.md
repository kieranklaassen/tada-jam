## Official wording

kritisch denken en redeneren over patronen in rijen getallen en (geometrische) figuren

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

thinking critically and reasoning about patterns in rows of numbers and (geometric) figures

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says whether a number or a figure belongs in a row and why, for example whether 50 is in the row 4, 8, 12 and so on. The child finds a wrong term in a row and tests a rule against every term. The child predicts the tenth figure of a pattern that grows and explains how. A child of 7 checks a pattern by continuing it step by step; a child of 10 reasons from the rule and explains why a term fits or does not fit.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. This goal states no number range. The patterns are in rows of numbers and in figures, with "geometric" printed in brackets. The goal is about thinking critically and reasoning; the wording names no kind of problem.

### Common mistakes

The child accepts the first rule that fits the first two terms and does not test it further. The child predicts a far term by doubling a near one, and takes the tenth figure to have twice as many dots as the fifth. The child explains by showing the next term, not by giving the rule.
