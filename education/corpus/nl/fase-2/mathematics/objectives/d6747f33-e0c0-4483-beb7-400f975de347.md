## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After an addition or a subtraction the child tells or shows how it was done: "I first did 300 + 200, then 40 + 30, then I joined them." The child draws the jumps on a number line or points at the written steps while explaining, and answers the question why a step was taken. The child follows the way of another child and says in what it differs from their own. A child of 7 retells the steps in order and shows them with material; a child of 10 also says why the steps are allowed and why this way was chosen.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. This goal states no number range. It sits in the cluster on adding and subtracting with whole numbers; the same wording stands in the cluster on multiplying and dividing with whole numbers as rw/bew/3/12/fase2. Three things are explained: the strategies used, the calculations made and the reasoning followed. The wording names no form for the explanation.

### Common mistakes

When asked how, the child only gives the answer again. The child tells a tidy way that is not the way that was used. The child leaves out a step that was done in the head, so the explanation cannot be followed.
