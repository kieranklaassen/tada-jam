## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

rebuilding block constructions from drawings, recognising the front view, side view or top view of three-dimensional or drawn objects or constructions

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a drawing of a construction made of cubes, builds the same construction with loose blocks and checks it against the drawing. The child walks around an object or a construction, or bends down to look at it from the front, from the side and from above, and picks out the drawing that shows each view. A child of 7 rebuilds a construction in which every block can be seen in the drawing; a child of 10 works out where blocks are hidden behind or under other blocks and says which drawn view belongs to which side.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names two activities: rebuilding block constructions from drawings, and recognising a view. It names three views, joined by "or": the front view, the side view and the top view. The views are of objects or constructions that are three-dimensional or drawn. The goal names recognising views and does not mention drawing them. It states no number of blocks and no kind of drawing.

### Common mistakes

The child builds only the blocks that can be seen in the drawing and leaves out the hidden blocks that carry others. The child takes the view from the left for the view from the right, or a side view for the front view. The child reads a top view as if it showed how high each stack is.
