## English gloss

recognising, designing and drawing mirror images of figures

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child holds a small mirror beside a figure and looks at the image, and picks out from several drawings the one that is the mirror image of the figure. The child draws the mirror image of a figure on the other side of a line, counting for each corner how far it lies from the line. The child also designs a figure of its own together with its mirror image. A child of 7 finds the mirror image of a figure with a small mirror and picks out the drawing that shows it; a child of 10 draws the mirror image without a mirror.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names three activities: recognising, designing and drawing mirror images. Its own wording names no kind of figure, no direction for the mirror line and no aid such as a mirror or grid paper. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: mosaic figures, line figures, as figures of which mirror images are made; it is an example, not a limit. Axes of symmetry and mirror lines are the subject of `rw/mk/3/06/fase2`.

### Common mistakes

The child shifts the figure to the other side of the line without turning it over, so that it still faces the same way. The child draws the image at a different distance from the line than the original. When the line is not upright, the child still swaps left and right.
