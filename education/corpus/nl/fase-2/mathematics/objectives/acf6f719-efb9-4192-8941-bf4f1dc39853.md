## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

knowing and recognising (*(her)kennen*), understanding and using mathematical language for multiplying such as 'number of times' (*aantal keer*), 'times' (*keer*), 'times' (*maal*), 'repeated adding' (*herhaald optellen*), 'multiplying by' (*vermenigvuldigen met*) and the formal notation .. × .. = ..

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sees 4 bags with 6 apples each, says "4 times 6" or "6 + 6 + 6 + 6", and writes 4 × 6 = 24. The child reads a written multiplication aloud with the word "times" and lays it out with groups or rows of objects. The child picks, from several stories, the ones that are about a number of times the same amount. A child of 7 links "times" to equal groups and to repeated adding; a child of 10 also says "multiplying by" and writes the formal notation.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The words and the notation after "such as" (*zoals*) are examples: 'number of times', 'times' (two Dutch words, *keer* and *maal*), 'repeated adding', 'multiplying by', and the formal notation with the signs × and =. Three things are named: knowing and recognising the language, understanding it, and using it. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child cannot say which number is the size of a group and which is the number of groups, and lays 4 × 6 as a group of 4 and a group of 6. The child reads "times" as adding and gives 4 × 6 as 10. The child writes a repeated addition of unequal groups as a multiplication.
