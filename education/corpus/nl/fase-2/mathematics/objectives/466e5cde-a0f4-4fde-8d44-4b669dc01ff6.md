## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

estimating and precisely counting quantities up to at least 1000 by making use of given structures and by doing the structuring oneself (for example splitting or joining together in groups of 100, 10 and 1)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a large quantity, such as a jar of beans or a sheet of stamps, and says about how many there are. The child then counts precisely, by using a structure that is given, such as rows of 10 on the sheet or boxes of 100, or by making one: heaps of 10, ten heaps joined into a hundred, and the loose ones apart. The child compares the estimate with the count. A child of 7 counts a quantity by a structure that is given, such as the rows on a sheet; a child of 10 makes a structure for a loose heap and estimates the whole from a part that was counted.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The range is stated as "up to at least 1000" (*tot ten minste 1000*): no upper bound is set, and the Dutch *tot* leaves open whether 1000 itself is included. Two actions are named, estimating and precisely counting, and two ways: making use of given structures, and doing the structuring oneself. The brackets that close the statement give an example, introduced by "for example" (*bijvoorbeeld*): splitting or joining together in groups of 100, 10 and 1. The group sizes 100, 10 and 1 belong to that example and are not a limit. The wording names no kind of given structure. The open data lacks the bracketed part; the record holds the whole statement as the content-line document prints it.

### Common mistakes

The child counts a large quantity one by one and loses count. The child makes groups of unequal size, or reports the number of groups as the number of objects and says 3 for three heaps of 100. The child's estimate is a guess without a reference, and the child does not adjust it after a part has been counted.
