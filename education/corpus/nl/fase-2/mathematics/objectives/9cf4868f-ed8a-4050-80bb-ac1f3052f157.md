aflezen en interpreteren van legenda's bij eenvoudige kaarten en plattegronden

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reading off and interpreting legends (map keys) of simple maps and plans (*plattegronden*, floor plans or street maps)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sees a symbol or a colour on a map, finds it in the legend and says what it stands for. The child also does the reverse: it looks up in the legend how, for example, a playground is drawn and then finds every playground on the map. A child of 7 looks up a picture symbol on a plan of a place it knows; a child of 10 combines entries of a legend, such as a kind of line and a colour, to answer a question about the map.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names reading off and interpreting legends, and the maps and plans are "simple". It names no symbols or colours and no kind of map. It does not mention making a legend.

### Common mistakes

The child guesses the meaning of a symbol from how it looks and does not check the legend. The child mixes up two entries that look alike, for example two shades of a colour or two kinds of lines. The child takes the size of a symbol on the map for the real size of the thing.
