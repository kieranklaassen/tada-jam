## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears a short story, such as 240 children are in the hall and 35 more come in, and says that children are added, or that two groups are joined together. The child writes the story as 240 + 35 = 275 and reads a written addition aloud. The child tells a story of their own for a written addition. A child of 7 acts a story out with objects and says what happens in it; a child of 10 says whether a story is about something being added or about two amounts being joined, and writes it in the formal notation.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name two meanings of adding, something being added to an amount (*erbij komen/vermeerderen*) and two amounts being joined (*samenvoegen*), and the formal notation with the signs + and =. Three things are named: knowing and recognising the language, understanding it, and using it. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child reads the equals sign as "now write the answer" and not as "is the same as", so does not accept 275 = 240 + 35. The child links adding only to "more comes" and does not see the joining of two groups as adding. The child takes any story with the word "more" for an addition, also when it asks how many more one amount is than another.
