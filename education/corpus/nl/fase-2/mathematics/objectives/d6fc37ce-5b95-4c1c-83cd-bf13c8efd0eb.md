vergelijken en ordenen van getallen in de telrij tot ten minste 1000 en bepalen of getallen dicht bij elkaar of verder uit elkaar liggen; getallen globaal en precies plaatsen op een getallenlijn

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing and ordering numbers in the counting sequence (*telrij*) up to at least 1000 and determining whether numbers lie close to each other or further apart; placing numbers roughly and precisely on a number line

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child puts number cards such as 398, 405, 290 and 840 in order and says which lie close together and which far apart. On a number line from 0 to 1000 with only a few marks the child points to about where 740 belongs, a little before 750; on a line with a mark for every number the child puts a number on its exact place. A child of 7 puts a number card on its exact place on a line that shows the numbers; a child of 10 points to about the place of a number on a line on which only the ends are marked.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The range is stated as "up to at least 1000" (*tot ten minste 1000*): no upper bound is set, and the Dutch *tot* leaves open whether 1000 itself is included. Three parts are named: comparing and ordering; determining whether numbers lie close together or further apart; and placing numbers on a number line, both roughly (*globaal*) and precisely (*precies*). The wording does not say which marks the number line has, and does not mention the symbols for greater and less.

### Common mistakes

The child places numbers at equal distances in their order, not by their size, so that 100, 500 and 550 stand at equal steps. The child counts the marks from the start of the line without asking what one step is worth. The child judges closeness by the digits and calls 398 and 405 far apart because all their digits differ.
