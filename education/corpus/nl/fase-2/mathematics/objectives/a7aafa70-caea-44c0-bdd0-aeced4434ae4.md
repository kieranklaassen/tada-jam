## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child holds an object in the light of a lamp or of the sun, turns it, moves it towards and away from the lamp, and watches the shadow on the wall or the table change in shape and size. The child tries to make a given shadow, for example a round shadow and then a rectangular shadow with the same tin. A child of 7 finds that turning the object or moving the light changes the shadow; a child of 10 says beforehand which shadows an object can and cannot make and explains this from the direction of the light.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about discovering how different shadows of one figure can be made. It names no figure and no source of light, and it does not say whether the figure is flat or three-dimensional. It does not mention measuring a shadow.

### Common mistakes

The child expects the shadow to have the shape the object has from where the child stands, not the shape it has seen from the lamp. The child thinks that an object has one shadow shape. The child expects the shadow to become smaller when the object is moved towards the lamp, while it becomes larger.
