## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks up on a calendar on which day of the week a date falls and which date the second Tuesday of a month is. The child counts how many days or weeks it is until an outing, or how long a holiday lasts, by counting on the calendar or by going on in weeks of seven days. A child of 7 counts the days of a period one by one on the calendar; a child of 10 goes on in weeks of seven days and works out the date a number of weeks later.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names two activities: reading off information from calendars, and calculating with periods of time on the calendar. It names no kind of calendar and no units for the periods. It does not say whether a period runs past the end of a month or a year. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child counts both the first and the last day of a period, or neither, and is one day off. The child takes every month for 30 days or for four weeks when a period runs into the next month. The child reads the day of the week from the wrong column of the calendar.
