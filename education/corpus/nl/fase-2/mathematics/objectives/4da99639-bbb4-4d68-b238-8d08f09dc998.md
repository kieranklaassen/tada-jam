## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at the legend beside a chart and finds what each colour, symbol or picture stands for. The child uses it to read the chart: "blue is the dogs, red is the cats", or "one bicycle picture stands for 5 bicycles". The child covers the legend and finds that the chart can no longer be read. A child of 7 matches a colour or a picture to its meaning; a child of 10 uses a legend that gives a value for each symbol.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording names legends and does not say of what; the cluster it sits in is about relations in tables, diagrams and graphs. Two actions are named: reading off and interpreting. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child ignores the legend and guesses the meaning from the colour or the picture. The child counts every symbol as one when the legend gives a larger value for a symbol. The child mixes up two categories whose colours or symbols look alike.
