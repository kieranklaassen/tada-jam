## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

multiplying by estimation (*schattend*, with rounded numbers) with simple decimal numbers in context situations (amounts of money) under at least 1000

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child rounds a price to a whole number of euros before multiplying: for 4 notebooks of €2.95 each the child says "about 4 × 3, so about 12 euros". For 6 tickets of €9.90 each the child says "about 6 × 10, so about 60 euros". A child of 7 lays the rounded price in whole euros for each thing and counts the money; a child of 10 rounds the price in the head and multiplies.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names multiplying only, by estimation, with "simple decimal numbers" (*eenvoudige decimale getallen*), which it does not define. It names context situations, and the brackets name amounts of money; formal mathematical language is not named in this goal. The range is stated as "under at least 1000" (*onder ten minste 1000*): numbers below 1000 are the least that is offered, and no upper bound is set; read strictly, *onder* (under) leaves the bound itself out. The wording does not say whether the bound holds for the numbers in the calculation, for the outcome, or for both. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: 8 atlases of €49.95 (in Dutch notation € 49,95) cost about 8 × €50 = 400 euros in total; it is an example, not a limit. The open data files this goal under fase 3 and under the cluster for multiplying and dividing with whole numbers (rw/bew/3), which is why its code reads rw/bew/3/12/fase3. The content-line document prints it in the fase 2 column, under the cluster "Vermenigvuldigen en delen met decimale getallen" (multiplying and dividing with decimal numbers, rw/bew/4), and the pack follows the content-line document. It is the only fase 2 goal of that cluster. The code rw/bew/3/12/fase2 belongs to another goal of this lane.

### Common mistakes

The child drops the cents and rounds €2.95 down to €2, so that the estimate is far too low. The child multiplies exactly first and then rounds the answer. The child rounds the price and stops there, giving about 3 euros, the price of one notebook, as the estimate for all four.
