## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) getting an orientation on the carrying out of the listening task

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before a spoken text starts, the adult asks "What are you going to listen to, what for, and how will you go about it?", and the child answers each question. The child reads the title and the listening question, says what they already know of the subject and puts paper and a pencil ready for key words. A child of 7 says what the task is and what to listen for, in answer to the adult's questions; a child of 10, in answer to the adult's questions, says what the listening question is and what they will write down.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start; the content-line document prints it at the end of the sentence. The statement does not say who helps or how. It does not say what the orientation consists of. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/02/09, titled *Aanpak* (approach), with NE/MT/02/09/02/fase2, the goal about listening strategies, and NE/MT/02/09/03/fase2, the goal about reflecting on the task.

### Common mistakes

Hearing "we are going to listen" as "be quiet" and not finding out what to listen for. Having forgotten the listening question by the time the text starts. Thinking about the subject only and not about how to listen.
