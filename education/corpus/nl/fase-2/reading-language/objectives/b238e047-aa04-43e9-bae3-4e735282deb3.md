## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child starts on a text without first asking how every word is spelled, writes down an idea of their own, and lets someone else read it. The child reads their text aloud to others or pins it on the wall. A child of 7 dares to write words they are not sure how to spell; a child of 10 dares to write about their own thoughts or feelings and to show a first version that is not finished.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about daring; the goal itself sets no kind of text, no length and no correctness. The wording does not say whether "writing" means composing a text, forming letters by hand, or both. Under the heading of this cluster the content-line document prints a sentence that the lane's frame quotes: "Samenhangende teksten schrijven met een eenvoudige lineaire opbouw, over uiteenlopende (meestal vertrouwde) onderwerpen binnen school en de wereld om ons heen." (writing coherent texts with a simple linear build-up, about varied (mostly familiar) subjects within school and the world around us); it shows that the cluster is about composing texts. The goal names nothing in particular that the child should dare.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often write only words they are sure they can spell, so the text stays short and says less than they could tell. Some do not start until someone has told them what the first sentence should be.
