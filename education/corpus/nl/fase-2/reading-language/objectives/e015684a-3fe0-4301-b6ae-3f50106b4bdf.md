putting simple means to use (mind map, short video, image)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

During a talk about volcanoes the child holds up a large photo, points at the crater and tells what can be seen there. The child draws a mind map on a large sheet beforehand and uses it to show the audience the parts of the talk. The child plays a video of half a minute, says beforehand what to look for and afterwards links it to the talk. A child of 7 shows a picture and tells about it; a child of 10 chooses the means that fits a part of the talk, shows it at the right moment and keeps facing the audience while pointing.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The means are called simple, and the brackets name a mind map, a short video (*filmpje*) and an image. The wording does not say what the means are used for; the open data lists the goal under speaking, in sub-cluster NE/MT/03/08, titled *Visuele ondersteuning* (visual support). The statement sets no number of means and does not mention help. NE/MT/03/08/01/fase2 is the goal about being aware of the function of visual support.

### Common mistakes

Showing a picture too small or too briefly for the audience to see it. Turning the back to the audience and talking to the picture. Letting a video run for so long that it takes the place of the child's own telling.
