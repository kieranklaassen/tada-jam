## Official wording

opzoeken van informatie met behulp van zoekvaardigheden (inhoudsopgave, trefwoorden, alfabetische volgorde, hyperlinks)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/LE/04/02; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau 6ef3f143-2bc6-4267-8e87-d4a3ef621db3.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

looking up information with the help of search skills (table of contents, keywords (*trefwoorden*: search terms, or entries in an index), alphabetical order, hyperlinks)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child wants to know what a mole eats, opens an information book at the table of contents, finds the chapter and goes to the page. The child looks up a word in a children's dictionary or encyclopedia by the alphabet, thinks of a word to search with, and clicks a link on a web page to get to the part needed. A child of 7 finds a chapter in a table of contents and goes to its page; a child of 10 thinks of a search word and follows a link to the part needed.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name four things under search skills: table of contents, *trefwoorden*, alphabetical order and hyperlinks. *Trefwoorden* can mean the search terms a child thinks of or the entries of an index or encyclopedia; the wording does not choose. The goal names no sources (book, dictionary, website) and does not mention judging whether a source can be trusted. It does not say how far into a word the alphabetical order is applied. Its cluster is about study skills with non-fiction texts.

### Common mistakes

Ordering by the first letter only and not knowing how to go on when two words begin with the same letter. Searching with a whole question in place of one or two search words. Following link after link and forgetting the question.
