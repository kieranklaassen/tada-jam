## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears "The river rose over the dyke and the village was inundated" and says that "inundated" must mean flooded, because of the river and the dyke. The child hears the Dutch word *onbewoonbaar* and hears *on-*, *bewoon* and *-baar* in it: not fit to live in. The child checks the guess against the next sentences and changes it when it does not fit. A child of 7 infers a meaning from a picture or from the sentence the word is in; a child of 10 also uses the parts of the word and the wider text, and checks the guess.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no way of inferring (from the sentence, from parts of the word, from a picture) and gives no examples. The words are unknown words in a text that is listened to, since the open data lists the goal under listening, in sub-cluster NE/MT/02/06, titled *Woordenschat en -strategieën* (vocabulary and vocabulary strategies). NE/MT/02/06/01/fase2 is the goal about asking for a word's meaning. The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Guessing from another word that sounds similar. Keeping the first guess when the rest of the text contradicts it. Guessing from the child's own experience of the subject and not from the text.
