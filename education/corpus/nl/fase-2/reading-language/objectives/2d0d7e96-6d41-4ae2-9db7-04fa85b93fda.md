## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The adult explains how the afternoon's work in groups is arranged: which group starts where, when to change and what to hand in. The child goes to the right table, starts with the right task and tells a classmate who was out of the room what to do. When one part was unclear, the child asks about that part. A child of 7 follows the explanation with the steps in view on the board; a child of 10 follows a spoken explanation with a condition ("if you finish early, then ...") and remembers it during the task.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about explanation of activities and tasks; NE/MT/02/01/02/fase2 is the separate goal about instruction and explanation of the subject matter. The statement itself sets no number of steps and no length, and does not say whether the explanation is shown as well as spoken. The content-line document prints text features for listening at fase 2 (kinds of text, a length for informative and for narrative texts, structure), which the lane's frame quotes; the two lengths are "informatieve teksten: 5 tot 10 minuten" (informative texts: 5 to 10 minutes) and "verhalende teksten: tot 15 minuten" (narrative texts: up to 15 minutes). The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Remembering the first and the last step and not the ones between. Missing a condition or an exception ("only if", "except") and doing the general thing. Starting before the explanation has ended.
