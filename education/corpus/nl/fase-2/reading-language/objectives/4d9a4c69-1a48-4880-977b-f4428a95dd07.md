## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The group talks about a visit to the fire station, and the child adds something new about it: how heavy the hose was, or what a firefighter told about night shifts. The child brings in a related matter, "At the swimming pool they have an alarm too", and says how it is connected. The child asks a question about a part of the topic that was not yet discussed: "What do they do when there is no fire?". A child of 7 adds a fact or an experience of their own to the topic; a child of 10 brings in a related subject, a comparison or a consequence and makes clear how it belongs to the topic.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is one short phrase. It does not say whether expanding means adding more about the same topic or widening the talk to related topics, and it sets no limit to how far. It sets no length and does not mention help. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/01/03, titled *Gespreksregels en -vaardigheden* (conversation rules and skills).

### Common mistakes

Changing to another subject that is linked to the topic by one word only. Adding something without making the link clear, so that the others hear it as a change of subject. Saying again in other words what was already said, so that nothing is added.
