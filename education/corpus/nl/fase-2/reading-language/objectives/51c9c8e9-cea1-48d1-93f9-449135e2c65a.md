## Official wording

herkennen van lettercombinaties en spellingpatronen in niet-klankzuivere woorden

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising letter combinations and spelling patterns in words that are not phonetically regular (*niet-klankzuivere woorden*, words not written as they sound)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sees a group of letters in a word and reads it as one piece, not letter by letter: "-tie" in "politie", "-lijk" in "vrolijk", "-ig" in "veilig", the unstressed "be-" and "ge-" at the start of a word (the pack's examples). The child marks the same letter group in a row of words and reads the row, and sorts word cards by pattern. A child of 7 reads "-aai" in "saai" and "-eeuw" in "leeuw" as one piece; a child of 10 reads "-tie" in "politie" as one piece.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about words that are not written as they sound, and about recognising letter combinations and spelling patterns in them. The goal itself names no letter combinations and no patterns, and sets no number; those above are the pack's examples, not the goal's. Each of them stands in the spelling lists that the content-line document for *Taalbeschouwing* (reflecting on language) prints for *groep* 3-4 and *groep* 5-6, and none under "alfabetische spelling" (alphabetical spelling, which the document bases on a one-to-one relation between sound and sign); the lane's frame quotes those lists, which are about spelling, where this goal is about reading. The goal stands under technical reading: it does not say that the child spells these words. It sets no reading speed.

### Common mistakes

Sounding out every letter, so the word comes out wrong: "politie" read with "tie" as in "tien". Saying the ending "-lijk" with the full vowel of "kijk". Saying a final "d" or "b" as it is written and then not recognising the word: "hond", "web".
