## Official wording

herkennen van woorden door gebruik te maken van de betekenis van een woord

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising words by making use of the meaning of a word

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a hard word slowly, gets a result that sounds almost like a word they know, and corrects it to that word: "ka-me-le-on ... kameleon". The child sees a part with a known meaning inside a long word, such as "speel" in "speelgoedwinkel", and reads the rest from there. A child of 7 does this with a word they know from everyday speech; a child of 10 does it with a word from a school subject.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names one aid for recognising a word: the meaning of a word. Using the context of a word is a separate goal, NE/LE/09/07/fase2. The wording does not say how the meaning is used, and it names no words; the examples above are the pack's. It stands under technical reading and is about recognising the written word; it does not mention learning new word meanings. It sets no reading speed.

### Common mistakes

Changing a correctly decoded but unknown word into a known word that looks like it. Accepting a result that is not a word and reading on. Looking only at the first letters and then saying a known word that begins the same way.
