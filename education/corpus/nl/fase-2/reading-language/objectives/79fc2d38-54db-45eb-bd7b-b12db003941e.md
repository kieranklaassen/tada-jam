## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

writing several versions of a text (revising)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes a first version of a text, reads it again or hears a reader's questions about it, and writes a second version in which something has changed: a part added, a part moved, a sentence worded differently. The child keeps both versions and can show what is different. A child of 7 adds a sentence or replaces a word in a new version; a child of 10 changes the order or the content of a part in a new version.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal says "meerdere" (several) versions and sets no number. The brackets give the term: revising. The wording does not say what is changed from one version to the next (content, order, sentences or spelling) and does not say whether help is given; what revising looks at is named in NE/SCH/02/05/01/fase2. It sets no text length and names no kind of text. The source words this as a clause without its subject ("schrijven meerdere tekstversies"); writing several versions of a text is plainly meant.

### Common mistakes

Copying the first version out neatly and calling it the second. Changing only spelling and leaving content and order as they were. Starting a wholly new text and not improving the first.
