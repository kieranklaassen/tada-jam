## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before a spoken text about volcanoes, the child tells a partner what they already know and what they expect to hear (the strategies in this note are the pack's examples). While listening, the child writes down a few key words and, when the text says something unexpected, asks to hear that part again. A child of 7, after the adult's question, says from the title and a picture what the text will be about; a child of 10, after the adult's question, says what they already know of the subject and writes down key words while listening.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start and does not say how the child is helped. The content-line document prints other words: "inzetten van luisterstrategieën met hulp van de leraar" (putting listening strategies to use with help from the teacher), with the help at the end, without brackets, and the teacher named as the helper. The goal names no listening strategies and no number of them. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/02/04, titled *Luisterdoelen en -strategieën* (listening purposes and strategies); NE/MT/02/09/02/fase2, listed under *Aanpak* (approach), is identical in the open data and worded differently in the content-line document, which prints that goal as "inzetten van luisterstrategieën (met hulp)".

### Common mistakes

Keeping to a prediction after the text has continued differently. Writing so much while listening that the next part is missed. Naming a strategy beforehand and not using it once the text has started.
