## Official wording

zoekend en selectief teksten lezen om bepaalde informatie te vinden

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/LE/03/01; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau e61e75bf-52b0-4f51-ad97-e51827126d5a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reading texts in a searching and selective way in order to find particular information

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child has a question, such as what time the swimming pool opens on Saturday or what a hedgehog eats, and moves a finger or the eyes over the page looking for a heading, a word or a number that fits, without reading every sentence. When the place is found, the child reads that part with care and gives the answer. A child of 7 finds a name or an opening time in a list; a child of 10 uses the headings of a text to go to the part where the answer stands.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The purpose is stated: to find particular information. The manner is stated: searching and selective, that is, not reading everything. The goal names no kinds of text, no text length and no aids for searching; search skills such as the table of contents are in NE/LE/04/02/01/fase2. Its cluster is about non-fiction texts. It sets no speed.

### Common mistakes

Starting at the first word and reading everything in order. Stopping at the first place where the search word stands and giving that sentence as the answer without reading it. Looking for the exact word of the question and missing the answer when the text uses another word.
