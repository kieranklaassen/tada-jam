## Official wording

schrijfervaring opdoen met de basisstructuur inleiding-kern-slot

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudslijn po - Nederlands: Schrijven, page 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

gaining writing experience with the basic structure introduction-body-conclusion (*inleiding-kern-slot*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes a text in three parts: a beginning that says what the text is about, a middle part with what they want to tell, and an end that closes the text. Before writing, the child folds a sheet in three and notes a few key words for each part. A child of 7 writes about a day at the swimming pool with a beginning that says what the text is about, a middle and an end that closes it; a child of 10 writes about a hobby, again with a beginning, a middle and an end.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The institute's open data lacks this goal: the record is read from the content-line document (the PDF of the line Schrijven), and it has no code. The wording is gaining writing experience with the structure, not mastering it. One structure is named, with three parts: introduction, body and conclusion; the wording calls it the basic structure. The goal does not say what each part holds or how long it is; what the parts do in the note above is the pack's description. It sets no text length and no number of paragraphs, and the word *alinea* (paragraph) is not in it. It names no kind of text.

### Common mistakes

Starting with the first event or fact, with no opening that says what the text is about. Stopping when they can think of nothing more, or closing with the word "Einde" (The end; the pack's example) and nothing else. Telling everything in the opening, so that the middle part repeats it.
