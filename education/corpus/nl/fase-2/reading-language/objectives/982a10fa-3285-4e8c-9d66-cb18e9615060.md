(with help) matching the way of listening to the purpose

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before listening, the adult asks what the child needs from the text. For a route description the child listens for each turn and draws an arrow for it; for a story read for enjoyment the child listens without notes. To find one fact, such as the opening time in a spoken announcement of the swimming pool, the child waits for that part and does not try to remember the rest. A child of 7, after the adult asks what they need from a spoken recipe, listens for the steps in order; a child of 10, after the adult asks what they need from the weather report, listens for tomorrow's weather and lets the rest go by.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement begins with "with help" in brackets and does not say who helps or how. The statement names no ways of listening and no purposes. NE/MT/02/04/01/fase2 is the goal about recognising the listening purpose of a text. The source prints *manier luisteren* without *van* ("of") between the two words; the gloss reads it as "way of listening".

### Common mistakes

Trying to remember everything when one fact was needed, and missing that fact. Listening to an instruction as to a story, and missing a step. Forgetting the listening question once the text becomes interesting.
