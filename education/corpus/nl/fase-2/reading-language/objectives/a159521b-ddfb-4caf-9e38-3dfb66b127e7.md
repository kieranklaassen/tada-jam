## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child draws small arcs under the parts of a long written word, "ver-keers-lich-ten", "o-li-fant", reads it part by part and then as a whole. The child sees whether a syllable ends in a vowel or in a consonant and reads the vowel long or short to match: "bo-men", "bom-men". A child of 7 draws the arcs under a word and reads it part by part; a child of 10 reads a word part by part while reading on, without marking it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about syllables in written words; it does not name syllables in spoken words. It sets no number of syllables and names no words; the words above are the pack's examples. It stands under technical reading: it does not mention the rules for breaking a word at the end of a line, or the spelling of open and closed syllables.

### Common mistakes

Splitting at the wrong place, "bom-en" for "bo-men", which gives the wrong vowel sound. Reading each syllable with full stress and then not recognising the word. Skipping a syllable in a long word: "bibliotheek" read as "biblotheek".
