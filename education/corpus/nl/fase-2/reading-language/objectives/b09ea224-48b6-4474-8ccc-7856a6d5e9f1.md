## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) reflecting on the use of simple planning strategies and on the carrying out

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a talk the child lays the planning sheet and the key-word card on the table and answers the adult's questions: "Did you practise as planned? Did the card help? What went as you wanted?". The child compares the talk as given with the plan and points to the part that was left out. Classmates name one thing that went well, and the child names one thing to plan differently next time. A child of 7 says whether they practised and names something that went well; a child of 10 links the result to the preparation ("I halted because I had not practised aloud") and chooses a change in the planning.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start; the content-line document prints it after the verb. The statement does not say who helps or how. The reflection is on two things: the use of simple planning strategies, and the carrying out. The planning strategies are called simple and are not named. The wording does not say the carrying out of what; the open data lists the goal under speaking, in sub-cluster NE/MT/03/09, titled *Aanpak* (approach), so it is the carrying out of a speaking task. The statement names no form of reflection.

### Common mistakes

Answering "good" or "I was nervous" and saying nothing about the preparation. Judging the talk only by whether the audience laughed or was quiet. Telling the content of the talk again when asked how the preparing and the giving of it went.
