## English gloss

(with help) getting an orientation on and reflecting on the use of reading strategies

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before reading, the child says, with help, how they will go about this text: look at the headings first, or think about what they already know. After reading, the child says what they did when they did not understand something and whether it worked. A child of 7 says, in answer to a question, what they did at a part they did not understand; a child of 10 says which way of reading fitted this text and why.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start. The content-line document prints other words: "oriënteren en reflecteren op het gebruik van leesstrategieën (onder begeleiding van de leraar)", with brackets at the end that say "under the guidance of the teacher". Two verbs are used, both about the use of reading strategies: getting an orientation on it and reflecting on it. The goal names no strategies; specific ones are named in NE/LE/05/01/01/fase2. It is about looking at how one reads, not about understanding a particular text. Its cluster is about non-fiction texts.

### Common mistakes

Telling what the text was about when asked how they read it. Naming a strategy by its name ("I predicted") without having used it. Saying "I just read it" and not being able to name any step.
