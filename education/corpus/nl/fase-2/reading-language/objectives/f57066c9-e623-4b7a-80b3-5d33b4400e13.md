## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child listens to a chapter that is read aloud and afterwards tells why the main character lied. The child listens to a classmate who argues that the school should have a longer break, and says what the classmate wants and which reasons were given. After a spoken account of a subject in the news, such as a flood, the child tells what happened and where. A child of 7 follows a story that is read aloud and says what a classmate asks for in a plea; a child of 10 keeps the opinion and the reasons of an argument apart and understands a poem that says something indirectly.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement lists six things to listen to and understand: stories, poems, performances (with "theatre" in brackets), argumentative text from the teacher or from pupils, information about topical subjects, and the opinion of the teacher or of another person. The list is not introduced as examples. The statement itself sets no length or difficulty of text and does not mention help. The content-line document prints text features for listening at fase 2 (kinds of text, a length for informative and for narrative texts, structure), which the lane's frame quotes; the two lengths are "informatieve teksten: 5 tot 10 minuten" (informative texts: 5 to 10 minutes) and "verhalende teksten: tot 15 minuten" (narrative texts: up to 15 minutes). The open data lists the goal under sub-cluster NE/MT/02/02, titled *Luisteren als lid van een live publiek* (listening as a member of a live audience); NE/MT/02/03/01/fase2 is the goal about series, programmes and audiobooks.

### Common mistakes

Hearing an opinion as a fact, or as an error to be corrected. Remembering the speaker's most vivid example and not the point it was meant to support. In a longer story, remembering separate events and not how one led to the next.
