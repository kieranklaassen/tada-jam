## Official wording

geven van een mening in gesprek (met medeleerlingen) over een tekst en deze globaal toelichten

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

giving an opinion in conversation (with fellow pupils) about a text and explaining it in broad terms

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a small group of pupils who have read the same text, the child says what they think of it and adds a reason: "I found it exciting because you did not know who had taken the key." The child hears another pupil's opinion and says whether they think the same. A child of 7 says whether they liked the text and names a part of it; a child of 10 gives a reason about the story, the characters or the language, and responds to what another pupil said.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The setting is a conversation, and the brackets name fellow pupils as the partners. The opinion is about a text; the cluster is about fiction. "Globaal" (in broad terms) bounds the explanation: a general reason, not a detailed argument. "Deze" (it) most plausibly points to the opinion; in Dutch grammar it could also point to the text. The goal does not mention a written opinion.

### Common mistakes

Repeating the opinion of the pupil who spoke first. Giving a reason that is not about the text: "because I like horses". Retelling a part of the text when asked why.
