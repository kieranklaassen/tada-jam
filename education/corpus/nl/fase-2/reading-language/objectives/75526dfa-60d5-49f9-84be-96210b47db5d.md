## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes when nobody asks for it: a note to a friend, a comic with text, a list, a diary page, a story that goes on over several days. The child shows the text to someone and wants to hear what they think. A child of 7 writes a note or a card for someone, or a caption with a drawing; a child of 10 writes a story, or a text about a subject they know a lot about, and chooses the form.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about pleasure; the goal itself sets no kind of writing, no length and no correctness. The wording does not say whether "writing" means composing a text, forming letters by hand, or both. Under the heading of this cluster the content-line document prints a sentence that the lane's frame quotes: "Samenhangende teksten schrijven met een eenvoudige lineaire opbouw, over uiteenlopende (meestal vertrouwde) onderwerpen binnen school en de wereld om ons heen." (writing coherent texts with a simple linear build-up, about varied (mostly familiar) subjects within school and the world around us); it shows that the cluster is about composing texts.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often stop when they are afraid of writing a word wrongly or when every mistake is corrected. Some find it hard to think of what to write and say they have no ideas.
