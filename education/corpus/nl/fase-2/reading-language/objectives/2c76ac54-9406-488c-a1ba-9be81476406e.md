## Official wording

lezen van: - informatieve teksten (bijv. teksten uit zaakvakmethode, krantenbericht, kindertijdschrift, kinderencyclopedie, eenvoudige schematische overzichten, informatieve berichten op internet); - instructieve teksten (bijv. een speurtocht, bouwtekening, opdracht in lesboek); - betogende teksten (bijv. een boekrecensie, advertentie)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reading: - informative texts (e.g. texts from a textbook for the content subjects (*zaakvakmethode*; subjects such as geography and history), newspaper report, children's magazine, children's encyclopedia, simple schematic overviews, informative messages on the internet); - instructive texts (e.g. a scavenger hunt, building plan, assignment in a textbook); - persuasive texts (e.g. a book review, advertisement)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a page about the Romans in the history textbook, a short report in a newspaper for children and an entry in a children's encyclopedia, and reads a simple diagram or table that goes with it. The child follows written steps to build a model or to walk a scavenger hunt, and reads a book review or an advertisement and says what the writer wants the reader to do. A child of 7 reads the steps of a scavenger hunt and walks it; a child of 10 reads a text in the history textbook and uses the diagram that goes with it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is reading. Three groups of non-fiction text are named: informative, instructive and persuasive (*betogende*) texts. Everything in brackets follows "bijv." and is an example; none is required. The word "simple" appears once, with the schematic overviews, inside an example. The goal itself sets no text length, no reading level and no reading speed. The content-line document prints, after the goals of this cluster, what the non-fiction texts of fase 2 are like (their subjects, information density, structure and style), which the lane's frame quotes; none of those features is a text length.

### Common mistakes

Doing the steps of an instruction out of order, or starting before reading what is needed. Skipping the diagram, the table or the caption and reading only the running text. Taking an advertisement for information and not seeing that it wants to persuade.
