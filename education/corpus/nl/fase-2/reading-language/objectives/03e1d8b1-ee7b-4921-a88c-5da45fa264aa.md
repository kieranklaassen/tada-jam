(with help) putting body posture, eye contact, gestures to use while speaking

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Giving a talk, the child stands upright on both feet, facing the class, holds the card low so that the face can be seen and looks up at the listeners after each look at the card. The child shows with the hands how long the telescope is and points at the part of the poster that the talk is about at that moment. Beforehand the child practises with a partner, who gives an agreed sign when the child looks down for too long. A child of 7 looks up at the listeners and points at the poster; a child of 10 stands upright facing the class and shows with the hands how long the telescope is.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement begins with "with help" in brackets and does not say who helps or how. Three means are listed, without a linking word: body posture, eye contact and gestures. Facial expression is not named here. The statement places their use during speaking and does not say how often. NE/MT/03/07/01/fase2 is the goal about seeing the importance of non-verbal communication.

### Common mistakes

Looking at the card, the poster or the adult the whole time. Swaying, turning or fiddling with the card or with clothes. Making a practised gesture at the wrong moment, or large gestures that have nothing to do with what is said.
