## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After listening to a spoken text about hedgehogs, the child chooses from six sentence cards the three that say the most important things. Listening a second time, the child writes key words under headings that the adult has given: food, home, enemies. The child tells a partner the two things that someone who did not hear the text needs to know. A child of 7 picks the important information when the adult asks a guiding question and offers choices; a child of 10 selects it while listening, guided by a listening question, and can say why something is less important.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start; the content-line document prints it at the end of the sentence. The statement does not say who helps or how. It does not say for what purpose the information is important or who decides that, and it sets no amount. The information is from a text that is listened to, since the open data lists the goal under listening, in sub-cluster NE/MT/02/07, titled *Samenvatten* (summarising). NE/MT/02/07/02/fase2 is the goal about summarising the text in one's own words.

### Common mistakes

Choosing what is funny, frightening or new to the child instead of what the text is mainly about. Selecting so much that nearly everything counts as important. Choosing only from the beginning or the end of the text.
