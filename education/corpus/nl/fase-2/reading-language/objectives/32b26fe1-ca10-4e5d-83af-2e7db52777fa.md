## Official wording

(met hulp) bepalen van leerdoelen voor een volgend gesprek

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) determining learning goals for a next conversation

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After looking back on a group talk, the child chooses with the adult what to work on in the next talk, such as "I ask someone else a question" or "I let others finish". The child writes or draws it on a card, and the card lies on the table during the next talk. At the start of that talk the child reads the card to a partner. A child of 7 chooses with the adult to work on letting others finish in the next talk; a child of 10 chooses with the adult to work on asking someone else a question in the next talk.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start and does not say who helps or how. The content-line document prints other words: "bepalen met hulp leerdoelen voor een volgend gesprek", with "met hulp" after the verb, without brackets, and without the "van" that the open data has after the verb. The learning goals are for a next conversation; the statement does not say how many, what they are about or who words them. NE/MT/01/08/02/fase2 is the goal about reflecting on the child's own part in a conversation.

### Common mistakes

Choosing a goal that is too wide to act on ("talk better"). Choosing the goal that the adult or a friend named, without a link to how the child's own talk went. Having forgotten the goal by the next conversation.
