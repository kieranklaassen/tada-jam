## Official wording

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reading and discussing texts for age and target group

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a book, a poem or a magazine written for children of their age and talks about it with others: what it was about, what was funny or strange, and who else would like it. The child shows a page to another child and says why they chose it. A child of 7 talks about what happened and about the pictures; a child of 10 also talks about why a character did something and about what kind of reader the text suits.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two activities are named: reading and discussing. "Voor leeftijd en doelgroep" (for age and target group) is short: the wording does not say whose age or which target group, and the plain reading is texts meant for children of the pupils' own age and group. The goal names no kinds of text, no text length and no reading level, and it does not say with whom the texts are discussed. It stands under reading pleasure and reading motivation (NE/LE/01).

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often retell the text when asked to talk about it and say nothing about what they thought of it. Some say only "nice" or "boring" and stop there.
