becoming aware of the role of tone in conversations

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says "Come here" in a friendly, an angry and a pleading tone, and classmates say which was which. The child hears "Nice drawing" said in a mocking voice and says that the words were kind and the tone was not. After a quarrel the child tells, with the adult, what was said and how it sounded. A child of 7 hears a marked difference, such as angry against friendly; a child of 10 also hears mockery, impatience or doubt and can say what a tone did to the talk.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is "becoming aware": the statement does not ask the child to control their tone. It does not define tone and gives no examples. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/01/06, titled *Vloeiendheid, verstaanbaarheid en grammaticale beheersing* (fluency, intelligibility and grammatical control). The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. What children most often leave out is their own tone: they hear at once that another child speaks sharply and do not hear it in themselves ("I only said ..."). Many take the words to be the whole message and are surprised that a polite sentence caused offence.
