## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child watches an episode of a school television series about how bread is made and afterwards tells the steps to a partner. The child listens to a chapter of an audiobook through headphones and tells what the main character decided. The child watches a news broadcast for children and says what one item was about and why it is in the news. A child of 7 follows a programme in which the pictures show what is said; a child of 10 follows an item about a matter in society in which the pictures do not show what is said, and tells the main point.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement lists three things to understand: series or programmes (with "educational" in brackets), digital audiobooks, and topical and societal information. The list is not introduced as examples. The verb is "understanding" alone; the open data lists the goal under listening, in sub-cluster NE/MT/02/03, titled *Luisteren naar radio, televisie en gesproken tekst op internet* (listening to radio, television and spoken text on the internet). The statement itself sets no length and no amount of time with media, and does not mention help. The content-line document prints text features for listening at fase 2 (kinds of text, a length for informative and for narrative texts, structure), which the lane's frame quotes; the two lengths are "informatieve teksten: 5 tot 10 minuten" (informative texts: 5 to 10 minutes) and "verhalende teksten: tot 15 minuten" (narrative texts: up to 15 minutes).

### Common mistakes

Following the pictures and not the spoken text, so that the child can tell what was seen and not what was said. Missing a part when the speaker talks fast and listening on without it. Remembering a striking picture or detail of a news item and not what the item was about.
