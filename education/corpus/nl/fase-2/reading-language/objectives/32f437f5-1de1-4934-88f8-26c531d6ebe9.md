## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In the week before a book talk the child rereads part of the book, marks a fragment to read aloud and writes key words on a card: title, writer, main character, what it is about, opinion. The child practises the talk aloud for a partner, who says which part was unclear. A child of 7 prepares a short talk about a pet with a sheet of questions from the adult and practises it aloud for a partner; a child of 10 prepares a book talk with a sheet of questions from the adult and practises it aloud for a partner.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start and does not say who helps or how. The content-line document prints other words: "voorbereiden van een verhaal/praatje/boekbespreking (met ondersteuning)", with "(met ondersteuning)" (with support) at the end where the open data has "(met hulp)" at the start. Three speaking tasks are named, separated by slashes: a story, a short talk and a book talk. The statement does not say what the preparation consists of and sets no length. NE/MT/03/09/01/fase2 is the goal about determining content and approach, and NE/MT/03/02/03/fase2 the goal about memory aids.

### Common mistakes

Preparing the poster or the object and not what to say. Writing out the whole text and learning it by heart, and stopping when one word is forgotten. Not practising aloud, so that the talk turns out much shorter or longer than expected.
