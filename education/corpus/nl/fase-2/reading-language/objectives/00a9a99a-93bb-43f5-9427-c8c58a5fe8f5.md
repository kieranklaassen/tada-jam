## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child watches two classmates act out a short talk without words and says, from face, posture and gestures, what each felt and wanted. The child says "I am listening" once while looking at the partner and once while turned away, and the partner says how each felt. In a group the child sees from a raised finger and an open mouth that a classmate wants to speak. A child of 7 reads clear signs, such as a smile, a frown or a turned back; a child of 10 notices smaller signs and can say that the body can strengthen or contradict the words.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is "becoming aware": using eye contact and body posture is the subject of NE/MT/01/07/01/fase2. The statement does not say which forms of non-verbal communication are meant and gives no examples. NE/MT/03/07/01/fase2 is the matching goal for speaking, worded with another verb (*inzien*). The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often think that only the words count, and do not consider what their own face and posture show while they listen. They read strong signals of others well and miss a signal that contradicts the words, such as "fine" said with the face turned away.
