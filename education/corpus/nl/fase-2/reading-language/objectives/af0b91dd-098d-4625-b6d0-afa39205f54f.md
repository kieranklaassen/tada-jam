## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before a talk about a hobby, the child decides with the adult what will be in it and what will not: three sub-topics on slips of paper, and one slip put aside. The child decides how to go about it: when to practise, with whom, and whether to bring an object or a poster. The child writes these choices on a planning sheet. A child of 7 chooses what to tell about their rabbit and whether to bring a photo, guided by the adult's questions; a child of 10 chooses what to tell about their football club and when to practise the talk, guided by the adult's questions.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help) at the start and does not say how the child is helped. The content-line document prints other words: "bepalen van inhoud en aanpak met hulp van de leraar" (determining content and approach with help from the teacher), with the help at the end, without brackets, and the teacher named as the helper. Two things are determined: content and approach. The wording does not say of what; the open data lists the goal under speaking, in sub-cluster NE/MT/03/09, titled *Aanpak* (approach), so they are the content of and the approach to a speaking task. NE/MT/03/09/02/fase2 is the goal about preparing a story, short talk or book talk.

### Common mistakes

Wanting to tell everything about the subject and choosing nothing. Deciding on the object or poster first and on what to say last, or not at all. Choosing a subject that is too wide for a short talk ("animals").
