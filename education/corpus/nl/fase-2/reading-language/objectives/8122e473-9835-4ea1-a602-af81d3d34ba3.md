## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting to know word processing on the computer

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child types a short text of their own on a computer, uses the space bar, the shift key for a capital letter and the enter key for a new paragraph, and deletes and retypes a word. The child saves the text, opens it again later and changes something in it. A child of 7 types a sentence of their own and corrects a letter in it; a child of 10 moves a sentence to another place in a typed text and makes the title larger.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is getting to know, not mastering. The goal names the computer and word processing and nothing more exact: no program, no functions, no typing method and no typing speed. Its code places it in the same sub-cluster as the writing strategies and revising (NE/SCH/02/05/01/fase2 to NE/SCH/02/05/03/fase2); the wording itself does not link them.

### Common mistakes

Typing no space after a full stop, or several spaces between words. Pressing enter at the end of every line, as when starting a new line in handwriting. Spending the time on fonts and colours and little on the text.
