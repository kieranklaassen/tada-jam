## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Preparing a talk about rabbits, the child writes each thing they want to say on a slip of paper and lays the slips in groups: food, hutch, care. The child puts the groups in an order, chooses what comes first and last, and adds an opening sentence and a closing sentence. With the adult the child tries a second order and says which one a listener can follow better. A child of 7 puts pictures or slips in time order; a child of 10 groups the information under headings of their own, chooses an order that fits the purpose and names the parts at the start of the talk.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement begins with "with help" in brackets and does not say who helps or how. The statement has two parts, ordering information and adding structure, and names no kind of structure. The open data lists it under speaking, in sub-cluster NE/MT/03/02, titled *Samenhang* (coherence), so the information is for a spoken text. The statement sets no length.

### Common mistakes

Telling things in the order in which they come to mind. Making groups beforehand and then going back and forth between them while speaking. Having a beginning and a middle and ending without a close ("That was it").
