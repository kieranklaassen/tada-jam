## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child plays a scene twice: asking a friend for a pencil, and asking the head teacher for permission to use the hall. The child hears how a visitor from the town hall is welcomed (*goedemorgen*, *u*, *meneer* or *mevrouw*) and how classmates greet each other (*hoi*, *je*), and says which words differ. With a classmate the child says the same message, being late, once for a friend and once for the receptionist at the dentist's. A child of 7 hears the difference in the greeting and in *je* and *u*; a child of 10 also hears differences in word choice and sentence form and says which fits a given situation.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is "getting to know": the child meets both kinds of language use, and the statement does not ask for correct use of formal language. It names no contexts and gives no examples. NE/MT/01/04/03/fase2 is the goal about forms of address, and NE/MT/03/04/01/fase2 the goal for speaking that names formal and informal situations. The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often take formal language to be a greeting and *u* only, and leave out word choice. After a correction some treat informal words as wrong words, where the point is that each kind fits some situations and not others.
