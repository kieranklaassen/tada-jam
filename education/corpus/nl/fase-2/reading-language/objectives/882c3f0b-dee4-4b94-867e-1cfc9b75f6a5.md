## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting to know and practising specific strategies: activating prior knowledge, steering the reading process, selecting important information (as an extension of reading strategies)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before reading a text about bees, the child looks at the title and pictures and says or writes down what they already know about bees. While reading, the child stops after a paragraph, checks whether they still understand it, and rereads if not. Afterwards the child underlines the few sentences that say the most important things. A child of 7 says before reading what they already know about the subject; a child of 10 rereads a paragraph they did not understand and underlines the sentence in it that says the most important thing.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verbs are getting to know and practising, not mastering. Three strategies are named after the colon: activating prior knowledge, steering the reading process, and selecting important information; the wording does not explain them further. The brackets present them as an extension of reading strategies, without saying which strategies came before. The goal names no kind of text and no text length; its cluster is about non-fiction texts. It does not say whether the child is helped.

### Common mistakes

Saying everything they know about the subject before reading and not using it during the reading. Reading to the end without noticing that they stopped understanding. Marking almost every sentence as important, or marking what is interesting and not what is important.
