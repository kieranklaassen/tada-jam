## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

decoding phonetically regular words (*klankzuivere woorden*, words written exactly as they sound)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads words in which each letter or fixed letter pair has its usual sound, including longer ones with several consonants in a row, such as "sprong", "herfst" and "markt", and words never seen before. The child reads made-up words of this kind, such as "plorf", sound by sound and then as a whole. A child of 7 sounds out "herfst" and then says it as a whole; a child of 10 reads "herfst" at once.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is limited to *klankzuivere* words: words in which every letter, or fixed letter combination, is said with its usual sound. Words that are not written as they sound are in NE/LE/09/03/fase2. The goal names no words, no word length and no word structure, and it sets no reading speed; the words above are the pack's examples. Nothing in the wording says how long or how hard the words are in this band.

### Common mistakes

Dropping or moving a consonant where several stand together: "herfst" read as "hefst". Saying a known word that looks like it instead of decoding: "sprong" read as "spring". Sounding out correctly and then blending the sounds into a different word.
