## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes a book without being asked, reads on in it at a free moment, and takes it home. The child asks for the next book in a series and tells someone what they are reading. A child of 7 takes a book with pictures and reads it alone; a child of 10 finishes a book and chooses themselves what to read next.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about motivation, and "zelf" (oneself) says that the child is the one who reads. It sets no reading level, no kind of text, no amount of reading and no reading time. It names nothing that should motivate the child.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often choose a book that is too hard, cannot read many of its words and put it back. A child who compares their own reading with that of faster readers may stop choosing to read.
