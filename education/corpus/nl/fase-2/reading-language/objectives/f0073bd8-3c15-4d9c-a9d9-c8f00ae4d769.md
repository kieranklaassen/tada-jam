## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The adult shows at the board how to subtract with exchanging a ten and says each step aloud; the child follows the steps and then works a similar sum with blocks in the same way. The child tells a partner in their own words why the ten was exchanged. After an explanation of the water cycle the child lays picture cards of its steps in order. A child of 7 follows an instruction on a new step with the material at hand; a child of 10 follows an explanation with new terms, links it to what was learned before and notes a point to ask about.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 2 (*groep* 4 to 6, about ages 7 to 10), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about instruction and explanation of the subject matter, that is, what is taught in the lessons; NE/MT/02/01/01/fase2 is the separate goal about explanation of activities and tasks. The statement itself names no subject and sets no length. The content-line document prints text features for listening at fase 2 (kinds of text, a length for informative and for narrative texts, structure), which the lane's frame quotes; the two lengths are "informatieve teksten: 5 tot 10 minuten" (informative texts: 5 to 10 minutes) and "verhalende teksten: tot 15 minuten" (narrative texts: up to 15 minutes). The statement asks for understanding and does not say how that is shown. It does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Copying the steps that were shown without understanding why, so that the method fails when the numbers or the example change. No longer following after an unknown term, and not asking. Believing while listening that it was understood, and finding out otherwise when starting alone.
