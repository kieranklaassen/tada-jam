## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child separates waste, turns off lights and taps, repairs or passes on a thing in place of throwing it away, and walks or takes a bicycle for a short trip. With a group the child takes on a task for a period, such as clearing litter from a stretch of street or measuring and lowering the paper use of the class. A child of 10 carries out tasks that were agreed; a child of 12 proposes a measure, organises it with others and checks what the result was.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is four words long in Dutch and is about doing: taking care of the environment. It names no actions, no place and no scale, and does not say whether the child does this alone or with others.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out keeping it up: they join an action day and go back to the old habit the week after. They also count only what can be seen, such as litter, and leave out the use of energy and water.
