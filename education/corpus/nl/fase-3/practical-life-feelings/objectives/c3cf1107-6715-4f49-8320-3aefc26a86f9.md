## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

acting responsibly in traffic under different circumstances and weather conditions (both individually and in a group)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child cycles a route with crossings, a roundabout and a road with cars: looks behind, holds out an arm, takes the right place on the road and gives way correctly. The child does this in rain, wind or dusk or on a wet road, with lights on and at a lower speed, and rides in a group of children without swerving and two abreast only where there is room. A child of 10 does this on a known route in daylight and practises riding in a group; a child of 12 rides a longer route that is new to him or her, in different weather and in a group.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about acting, not about knowing rules. It names different circumstances and weather conditions without listing any, and the brackets name both individually and in a group. It does not say whether the child takes part on foot, by bicycle or otherwise, names no routes or kinds of road, and states no safety condition such as adult supervision. Laws and regulations concerning traffic are the subject of `ojw/ds/4/03/fase3`.

### Common mistakes

Children ride in rain or dusk at the same speed and distance as on a dry day. In a group they follow the child in front across a crossing without looking for themselves. When riding side by side they look at each other and not at the road.
