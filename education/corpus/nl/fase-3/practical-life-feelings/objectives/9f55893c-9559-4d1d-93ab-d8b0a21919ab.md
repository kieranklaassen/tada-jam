## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising that a combination of healthy food, movement, hygiene and rest promotes health and reduces the chance of illnesses

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child keeps a record for a week of four things: what he or she ate, how much he or she moved, hygiene habits and hours of sleep, and notes each day how fit he or she felt. The child compares days and reads or hears what each of the four does in the body. A child of 10 names the four and says for each one way in which it helps; a child of 12 says that the four work together, and that they lower the chance of illness without making illness impossible.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The source prints *bevorderd* where *bevordert* ("promotes") is plainly meant, and the gloss gives what is meant. The verb is realising. The statement names a combination of four things: healthy food, movement, hygiene and rest. It says that this promotes health and reduces the chance of illnesses, not that it prevents them. It names no foods, kinds of movement, hygiene habits or illnesses, and gives no amounts.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out the word "chance": they think someone who lives healthily never gets ill, so that an illness is the person's own fault. They also leave out rest, and count only food and sport.
