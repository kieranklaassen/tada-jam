## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising factors that influence one's own behaviour with regard to health and hygiene and bearing (shared) responsibility for one's own health and that of others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child keeps a record for a week of what he or she ate and drank and how long he or she slept, moved and sat at a screen, and writes beside it what led to each choice: habit at home, friends, an advertisement, taste, time, mood. The child picks one thing to change, does so for some weeks and notes what helped and what hindered. The child coughs into the elbow, does not share a bottle when he or she has a cold, and does not press another to eat, drink or dare something unhealthy. A child of 10 names influences from home and friends and takes on one task for his or her own health; a child of 12 also recognises influence from media and the group, and takes the health of others into account in what he or she does.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two things are named: recognising factors that influence the child's own behaviour with regard to health and hygiene, and bearing responsibility for one's own health and that of others. The part "shared" (*mede-*) stands in brackets, so the statement covers responsibility and shared responsibility; it does not say with whom it is shared. It names no factors and no behaviour.

### Common mistakes

Children name what others should do differently, such as the parents who buy the food, and leave out what they choose themselves. They believe that friends and advertising influence others and not them. They take responsibility for another's health to mean telling the other what to do.
