## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discussing together emotions that go with being in love and the relation with sexuality

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group talk a child names emotions that go with being in love, such as being glad, shy, nervous, jealous or sad, from stories, from films or from what he or she has seen or felt. The child hears in the talk how being in love and sexuality are related, and says in his or her own words what he or she has understood. A child of 10 mostly names the emotions and speaks about others; a child of 12 may also speak about himself or herself, and asks questions about the relation with sexuality.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discussing, and the statement says "together" (*samen*) without saying with whom. Two things are discussed: emotions that go with being in love, and the relation with sexuality. The statement names no emotions, does not say what the relation with sexuality is, names no acts or situations and sets no level of detail. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*).

### Common mistakes

Children think that being in love brings only pleasant emotions, and do not count jealousy, shame or sadness. They giggle or tease when the subject comes up, so that others stop speaking. They think that being in love and sexuality are the same thing, or that the two have nothing to do with each other.
