## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discussing the influence of social pressure from others and the media on sexual behaviour

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group talk a child looks at examples from media, such as an advertisement, a music video or a series, and says what picture of sexual behaviour they give. The child says what others of his or her age call normal, and how hard it is to do or think something else than the group. A child of 10 recognises pressure in an example that is given; a child of 12 finds examples in the media he or she uses, and says how such pressure can influence what a person does.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discussing, and what is discussed is an influence on sexual behaviour. The wording can be read as social pressure that comes from others and from the media, or as two influences, social pressure from others and the media; the gloss leaves this as open as the Dutch does. The statement names no media, persons, behaviour or situations, does not say whose behaviour is meant, and sets no level of detail. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*).

### Common mistakes

Children think that pressure exists only when someone says "you must", and do not count wanting to belong. They take what media show for how most people behave. They believe that others are influenced and that they themselves are not.
