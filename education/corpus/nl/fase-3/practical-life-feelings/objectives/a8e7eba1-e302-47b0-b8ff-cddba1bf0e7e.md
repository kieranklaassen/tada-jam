## Official wording

filosoferen over een idee zonder dit meteen om praktische redenen af te wijzen (gebruiken van gedachten experimenten)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_cluster ojw/ja/1; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau a8e7eba1-e302-47b0-b8ff-cddba1bf0e7e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

philosophising about an idea without rejecting it straight away for practical reasons (using thought experiments)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group talk a child takes a question such as "what if nobody could lie?" or "what if you woke up in someone else's body, would you still be you?" and reasons step by step about what would follow. When someone says "that cannot happen", the child answers "suppose it could" and goes on. A child of 10 follows a thought experiment that is given and names consequences; a child of 12 thinks up one of his or her own to test an idea, and says what the experiment shows about the idea.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The source prints *gedachten experimenten* as two words where *gedachte-experimenten* ("thought experiments") is plainly meant, and the gloss gives what is meant. The verb is philosophising, and its object is "an idea"; no ideas or questions are named. One condition is stated: the idea is not rejected straight away for practical reasons. The brackets name using thought experiments. The statement does not say whether this is done alone or with others, and does not mention reaching a conclusion.

### Common mistakes

Children end the thinking with "but that is impossible" or "that would cost too much". They treat the thought experiment as a story and add events to it in place of reasoning about what follows. They look for the one right answer and ask an adult to give it.
