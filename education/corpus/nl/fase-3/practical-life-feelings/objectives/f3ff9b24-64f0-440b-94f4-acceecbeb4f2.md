## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discussing what sexual abuse is and in what way help can be asked for

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group talk a child hears what sexual abuse is and says in his or her own words what he or she has understood. The child says in what ways help can be asked for. A child of 10 names a way of asking for help; a child of 12 also names ways of asking that need no face-to-face talk, such as writing or telephoning.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discussing, and two things are discussed: what sexual abuse is, and in what way help can be asked for. The statement names no acts, persons or situations, names nobody to ask for help, and sets no level of detail. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*).

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the second part: they say something about what sexual abuse is and do not get to the ways in which help can be asked for. They also think there is only one way to ask for help, which is to tell everything at once.
