## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising that (consciously) having children can be influenced by personal choice, religion, culture and income

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child hears or reads accounts of why people have many children, few or none, and brings them to a group talk. The child sorts the reasons given under the four things the statement names: a person's own choice, religion, culture and income. A child of 10 names one reason in an account; a child of 12 says that several of the four can work together and that they count differently for different people.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is realising. The word "consciously" (*bewust*) stands in brackets, so the statement covers having children in general and having children as a deliberate choice. Four influences are named: personal choice, religion, culture and income; the statement says having children "can be" influenced by them, not that it always is. It does not say how each works, names no religions or cultures, and sets no level of detail. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*).

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out that having no children, or having them later, can be a choice too: they think every adult couple has children as a matter of course. They also think of one cause only, usually what the parents wanted, and leave out income.
