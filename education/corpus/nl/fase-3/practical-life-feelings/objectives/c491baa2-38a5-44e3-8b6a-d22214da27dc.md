## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discussing that people can choose different forms of relationship and what consequences that has

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group talk a child tells which forms of relationship he or she knows from the adults around him or her and from stories, and hears about forms he or she does not know. The child says what a choice for a form means in daily life for the people in it, as far as he or she knows, and asks about what he or she does not know. A child of 10 describes the forms he or she knows and one thing that follows from each; a child of 12 compares forms and what follows from them, and does so without judging another child's family.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discussing, and two things are discussed: that people can choose different forms of relationship, and what consequences that has. The statement names no forms of relationship and no consequences, does not say for whom the consequences are, does not say which form is better, and sets no level of detail. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*).

### Common mistakes

Children assume every household is built like their own and call another form "not real". They hear "consequences" as "bad consequences" and name only problems. They mix up discussing a form of relationship with being allowed to judge a child whose family has that form.
