## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child visits a place nearby with many kinds of plants and animals and a place with few, counts what lives in each, and says what would be lost if the first became like the second. The child reads or hears about a plant or animal that has disappeared from an area and what changed because of it. A child of 10 says what he or she would miss and names an animal that depends on the place; a child of 12 gives reasons of several kinds, such as food, clean water, other living things and enjoyment.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is realising: the statement is about awareness of why preserving nature matters, not about an action. It does not say why it matters, what counts as nature or where, and names no plants, animals or areas. Contributing to preserving or restoring the natural balance is the subject of `ojw/rojh/6/03/fase3`, and taking care of the environment of `ojw/rojh/6/04/fase3`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out the nature close to them: they think of rainforests and polar bears and not of the ditch, verge or park nearby. They also give one reason only, that animals are sweet or beautiful, and leave out what people need from nature.
