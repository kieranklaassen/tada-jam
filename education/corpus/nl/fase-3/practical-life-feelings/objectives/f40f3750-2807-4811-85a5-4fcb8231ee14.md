## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

realising the influence of one's own feelings in dealings with others

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a day on which a child was unkind to someone, the child looks back and says what he or she felt beforehand and what that did to the way he or she spoke. For a week the child notes his or her mood and how contact with others went, and compares the two. A child of 10 links one feeling to one thing he or she said or did to another person; a child of 12 notices during the contact itself that a feeling from elsewhere, such as tiredness or worry, is shaping how he or she reacts.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is realising: the statement is about awareness, not about changing behaviour. It concerns the child's own feelings and their influence in dealings with others (*de omgang met anderen*); it names no feelings and no situations and does not say who the others are. That someone's behaviour can be the result of emotions, feelings, views and wishes is the subject of `ojw/ja/1/05/fase3`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out their own part: they explain a quarrel by what the other did and do not mention the mood they were in. They also think that a feeling they did not show had no influence.
