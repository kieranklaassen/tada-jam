## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

being familiar with bodies that give help and look after interests in the field of physical and mental health (for example *GGD*, *Riagg*, *Leger des Heils*, *Kindertelefoon*, *Longfonds*, *Diabetesfonds*)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child looks up for each of several bodies what it does, for whom, and how it can be reached, and makes a card for it. The child sorts the cards: bodies that give help and bodies that look after the interests of a group, such as people with one illness; bodies for the body and bodies for the mind. Given a short case, the child says where the person in it could turn. A child of 10 knows the bodies that children meet or can reach themselves, such as the school doctor and a helpline for children; a child of 12 knows more of them, tells giving help from looking after interests, and finds out how to reach one.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement speaks of being familiar with (*bekend zijn met*) such bodies, not of using them. The bodies are described by two tasks, giving help and looking after interests, in the field of physical and mental health. Six are given as examples (*bijv.*) and are kept as the goal names them; the goal gives the names only, and the explanations in brackets in this sentence are the pack's: GGD (the municipal public health service), Riagg (a name for regional institutions for outpatient mental health care, now largely replaced by other names), Leger des Heils (the Salvation Army), Kindertelefoon (a free helpline for children, by telephone or chat), Longfonds (a charity for lung diseases) and Diabetesfonds (a charity for diabetes). The statement does not say what a child knows about each.

### Common mistakes

Children know the doctor and the hospital and no other body. They do not know that a child can contact some of these bodies without a parent and without giving a name. They take a fund that collects money for an illness for a place where patients are treated.
