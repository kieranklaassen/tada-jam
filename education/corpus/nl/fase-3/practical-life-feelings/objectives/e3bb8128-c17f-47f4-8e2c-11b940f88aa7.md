## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child draws a food web of a pond or wood from what he or she found there, takes one kind away in thought and reasons through what happens to the others. The child keeps a closed jar or a small tank with water plants and small animals for some weeks and notes what stays in balance and what does not. The child takes one thing people use, such as wood or fish, and works out how much can be taken each year so that as much remains for later. A child of 10 follows a chain of who eats whom and names one effect of a change; a child of 12 follows effects through several steps, and links them to the question of what can go on for a long time.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement speaks of gaining insight (*inzicht*) and calls it beginning (*beginnend*), so it does not describe a complete understanding. The brackets name sustainable development, without *bijv.* or *zoals*. The statement does not define ecological insight or sustainable development and names no ecosystems, species or resources.

### Common mistakes

Children think of a food chain as a single line and not as a web, so that they see one effect of a change and no further ones. They think that when one kind disappears, only the animal that eats it is affected. They take sustainable to mean only "lasting long" or "made of natural material".
