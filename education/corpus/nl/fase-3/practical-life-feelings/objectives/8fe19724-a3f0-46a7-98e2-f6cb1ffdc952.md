## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

exploring why people flee from their own country (borders and identity, war and peace)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child reads or hears accounts of people who fled, from different times and countries, and notes for each why the person left and what he or she left behind. The child finds the countries and the borders that were crossed on a map, and looks up what was happening there at the time. A child of 10 names more than one reason and says how fleeing differs from moving by choice; a child of 12 links an account to what the brackets name (borders and identity, war and peace) and compares cases.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is exploring, and the question is why people flee from their own country. The brackets name two pairs: borders and identity, and war and peace, without *bijv.* or *zoals*. The statement does not say how borders and identity are connected with fleeing, names no countries, groups or events, and sets no level of detail for what people who flee go through. It does not mention the journey, asylum or arrival in another country.

### Common mistakes

Children think that people who flee chose to leave and can go back when they want. They know war as the only reason. They take what they hear about one group as true for everyone who flees.
