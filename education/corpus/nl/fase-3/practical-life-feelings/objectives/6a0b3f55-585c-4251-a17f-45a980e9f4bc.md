## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

recognising dangerous situations and dealing with them appropriately

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child looks at a place or a described situation, such as thin ice, a smell of gas, fireworks lying in the street or a group that dares someone to climb, and says what the danger is and what he or she would do. The child practises fixed steps for some of them: leaving the building at a fire alarm, and calling the emergency number and saying who, where and what. A child of 10 recognises the danger in situations that were discussed and follows the practised steps; a child of 12 recognises it in a new situation, also when friends say it is fine, and chooses between acting, leaving and fetching help.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two things are named: recognising dangerous situations, and dealing with them appropriately. The statement names no situations and no kinds of danger, does not say what makes a way of dealing appropriate, and states no safety condition such as adult supervision.

### Common mistakes

Children judge a situation as safe because nothing went wrong the last time. They see the danger and go along because the group does. They try to solve it themselves where the fitting step is to leave and fetch help.
