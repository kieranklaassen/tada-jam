## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a quarrel about a game, a seat or something that was said, a child stops, says what the quarrel is about, lets the other finish speaking and says what he or she wants. The child proposes a way to go on, or asks a third person to help when the two cannot settle it. A child of 10 does this in a quarrel between two, with steps that were practised; a child of 12 does it too where a group is involved or where the quarrel has lasted for days, and can help two others as a mediator.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording ends with the printed marker (b), the curriculum institute's sign for an aspect of citizenship (*burgerschap*). The statement names conflict situations in the child's own surroundings (*eigen omgeving*) and does not say which surroundings (home, school, the street or elsewhere). It names no kinds of conflict and no way of dealing with them, and does not say whether the child is a party to the conflict or a bystander. Dealing with such situations by finding solutions that suit all parties is the subject of `ojw/ja/3/08/fase3`.

### Common mistakes

Children take dealing with a conflict to mean winning it or avoiding the other person. They argue about who started it and do not get to what each wants now. They ask others to support their side, so that the conflict grows.
