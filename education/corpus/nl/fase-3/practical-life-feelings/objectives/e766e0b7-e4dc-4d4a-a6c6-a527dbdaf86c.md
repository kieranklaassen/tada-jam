## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

behaving from respect for generally accepted values and norms (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child lets others finish speaking, keeps agreements, leaves other people's things alone, tells truthfully what happened and treats people he or she does not like politely, also when no adult is looking. In a group talk the child says which value is behind a rule, such as honesty or fairness. A child of 10 acts on the value where no rule was given, such as helping a new child; a child of 12 does so too when the group does otherwise, and notices where norms differ between places.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording ends with the printed marker (b), the curriculum institute's sign for an aspect of citizenship (*burgerschap*). The statement is about behaving, and about where the behaviour comes from: respect for values and norms. It names no values or norms and does not say what "generally accepted" (*algemeen aanvaarde*) covers or who decides that.

### Common mistakes

Children keep to a norm while an adult is present and drop it among themselves. They apply a norm strictly to others and not to themselves. They take what their own group finds normal for generally accepted.
