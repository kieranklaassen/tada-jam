## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating what the influence on people's consumption is in the biological, commercial and social fields and the role you can take as a consumer

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child keeps a list of what he or she ate, bought or asked for in a week and writes beside each item why: hunger or thirst, an advertisement or offer, or because friends have it. The child studies advertisements, packaging and the layout of a shop for the means used to make people buy. A child of 10 sorts the reasons into the three fields and recognises an advertisement that is aimed at children; a child of 12 recognises hidden advertising, such as a paid recommendation in a video, and says what he or she can do as a buyer: compare, wait, refuse, complain or choose another product.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is investigating, and two things are investigated: the influence on people's consumption in three fields (biological, commercial and social), and the role a person can take as a consumer. The statement does not say what belongs to each field and names no influences, products or roles.

### Common mistakes

Children believe that advertising works on others and not on themselves. They recognise an advertisement on television and not a recommendation by someone they follow. They think a consumer can only buy or not buy, and leave out comparing, complaining and returning.
