## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

empathising with and dealing with the feelings, wishes and views of others (b)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child retells a disagreement from the side of another person: what that person felt, wanted and thought. In a group task the child asks what the others want, hears a view he or she does not share, and goes on working with that person. A child of 10 does this for people who are present and whom he or she knows; a child of 12 does it too for someone who is absent, someone he or she does not like or someone whose view he or she finds wrong, and stays polite while disagreeing.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording ends with the printed marker (b), the curriculum institute's sign for an aspect of citizenship (*burgerschap*). Two things are named: empathising with others, and dealing with what they feel, want and think; three things of others are named: feelings, wishes and views. The statement does not say who the others are, names no situations and does not say what dealing with these things consists of. The child's own emotions are the subject of `ojw/ja/1/02/fase3`.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children of this age most often leave out views: they feel with someone who is sad and do not try to follow someone who thinks differently. They also empathise with friends and not with others, or take dealing with another's wish to mean giving in to it.
