## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child follows the way of a patient with a broken arm or a lasting cough: whom the person sees first, who refers him or her, where the X-ray is made, where the medicine comes from and who pays. The child interviews someone who works in care, such as a doctor's assistant, a nurse, a pharmacist or a dentist, and draws a diagram of who does what. A child of 10 names the persons and places and what each does; a child of 12 explains the order, such as first the family doctor and then the hospital, and asks how the care is paid for.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is investigating, and the question is how health care functions. The statement names no persons, institutions, kinds of care or country, and sets no level of detail. Bodies that give help and look after interests in the field of physical and mental health are the subject of `ojw/pdm/2/11/fase3`.

### Common mistakes

Children think the hospital is where every sick person goes first. They know the doctor and the dentist and leave out others who work in care, such as the pharmacist, the physiotherapist and the home nurse. They think care costs nothing because no money is handed over at the visit.
