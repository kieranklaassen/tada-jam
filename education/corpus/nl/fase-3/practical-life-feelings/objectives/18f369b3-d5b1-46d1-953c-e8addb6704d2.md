## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discussing differences between friendship and being in love

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

In a group talk a child says what belongs to friendship, what belongs to being in love and what belongs to both, for example by sorting words such as trust, missing someone, being shy and doing things together. The child listens to what others say and tells how he or she sees it. A child of 10 names differences from stories and from what he or she sees in others; a child of 12 may also speak from his or her own experience, and names differences in what is felt and not only in what is done.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is discussing, and what is discussed is differences between friendship and being in love (*verliefdheid*). The statement names no differences, names no persons or situations and sets no level of detail. It does not say that a child tells about his or her own feelings. It stands under the heading relationships and sexuality (*Relaties en seksualiteit*). Emotions that go with being in love are the subject of `ojw/ja/2/02/fase3`.

### Common mistakes

Children think that a friendship between a boy and a girl must be being in love, and tease on that ground. They take being in love for a stronger friendship and not for something different. They think that everyone of their age is in love or ought to be.
