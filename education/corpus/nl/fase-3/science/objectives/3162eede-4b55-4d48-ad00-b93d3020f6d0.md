## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

acknowledging one's own responsibility for (healthy) food

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says which choices about food and drink in a normal week are their own, such as what to take from the fruit bowl, what to drink at school and what to buy with pocket money, and which choices others make for them. The child plans and prepares a lunch or a snack and explains the choice. A child of 10 names their own choices at home and at school; a child of 12 has more money of their own and more time away from home, and names the choices made there.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is *onderkennen*: acknowledging, seeing that something is so. The word healthy stands in brackets before food. The statement names no foods and no rule, and it does not say how far a child's own responsibility goes beside that of others.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out drinks and snacks between meals, where many of their own choices are made. Many place all responsibility with the adult who shops and cooks.
