experiencing the beauty and aesthetic value of nature for humans

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Outdoors the child chooses something they find beautiful, such as the pattern of veins in a leaf, lichen on a branch, a bird's song or the light on water, and takes time to look or listen. The child draws or photographs it, or writes a few lines about it, and says what makes it beautiful to them. A child of 10 shows and tells; a child of 12 can also say what a place in nature means to people, for example why people walk in a wood or by the sea to rest.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about experiencing. It names no knowledge, no skill and no kind of nature. The value named is beauty and aesthetic value for humans.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often look for beauty only in large or rare things, such as flowers, sunsets and furry animals, and leave out small and ordinary things. Many pick a flower or take an animal along to keep it, instead of leaving it where it is.
