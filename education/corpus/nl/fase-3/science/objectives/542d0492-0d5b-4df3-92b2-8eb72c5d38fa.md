## English gloss

investigating the behaviour of animals (e.g. soil animals, a pet)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child puts woodlice in a tray with a damp dark half and a dry light half, counts every minute how many are in each half, and repeats the test. The child watches a pet for ten minutes and tallies what it does: eating, washing itself, sleeping, moving about. The child puts the soil animals back where they were found. A child of 10 tallies and reports what was seen; a child of 12 writes a question beforehand, changes one condition and says whether the result answers the question.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets give two examples: soil animals and a pet. The statement names no behaviour and no method. It states no condition about care for the animals; care is the subject of `ojw/pdm/4/03/fase3` and a condition in `ojw/pdm/3/07/fase3`.

### Common mistakes

Describing the behaviour as human feelings, such as "it is bored" or "it is shy". Disturbing the animal and then recording its reaction to the child. Drawing a conclusion from one animal or one short look.
