## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating the basic rules of a healthy eating pattern and factors that influence eating behaviour and food choice

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child keeps a record of what they ate and drank over several days and lays it beside a published guide to healthy eating. The child reads the labels of two breakfast cereals or two drinks and compares the amount of sugar per 100 grams. The child lists what made them choose a snack that week: taste, price, an advertisement, what friends ate, what was in the house. A child of 10 sorts foods and reads one number from a label; a child of 12 compares products and names what influences their own choices, advertising included.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two parts are named: the basic rules of a healthy eating pattern, and factors that influence eating behaviour and food choice. Neither the rules nor the factors are listed, and no food group, nutrient or amount is named. The statement does not say which source the basic rules come from.

### Common mistakes

Sorting foods only into "good" and "bad", so that a food is either always fine or never allowed. Judging a product by the claim or picture on the front of the pack and not by the label. Naming taste as the only reason for a choice and leaving out price, habit, advertising and what others eat.
