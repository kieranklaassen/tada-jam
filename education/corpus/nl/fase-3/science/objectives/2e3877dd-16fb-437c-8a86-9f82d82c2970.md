## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating and functionally applying principles of movement and transmission (*snaar*: drive belt or cord), mechanisms for steering or control (*sturingsmechanismen*) and pneumatics

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child joins two wheels with an elastic band as a belt, turns one and counts the turns of the other, then crosses the band and changes the sizes of the wheels. The child joins two syringes with a tube, presses one plunger and sees the other move out, and uses this to lift a flap or tip a load in a model. The child builds a mechanism that sets a direction or a movement, such as a steering axle on a cart or a cam that lifts a lever, and gives it a job in a model. A child of 10 builds the mechanism from an example and describes what moves what; a child of 12 chooses the mechanism for the job, predicts the direction and speed of the driven wheel and adjusts the model after a test.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two verbs are joined: investigating and functionally applying. Three subjects are named: principles of movement and transmission, with *snaar* in brackets; *sturingsmechanismen*; and pneumatics. *Snaar* means a string or cord and, in a machine, a drive belt that passes turning from one wheel to another; *sturingsmechanismen* can be read as mechanisms for steering or for control; the wording does not choose in either case. No device, material or pressure is named, and hydraulics is not mentioned.

### Common mistakes

Expecting two wheels joined by an uncrossed belt to turn in opposite directions, as two meshing gear wheels do. Expecting a small wheel driven by a large one to turn more slowly, when it turns faster. Expecting the second syringe to move at once and just as far as the first, when the air between them is first pressed together.
