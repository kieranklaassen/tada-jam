onderzoeken van het verschijnsel licht: lichtbronnen; materialen die licht doorlaten (lichtbreking), licht terugkaatsen (schaduw/spiegelen) en licht absorberen (verwarmen); licht kan worden gesplitst in kleuren (prisma); relatie met zien; lichtsterkte in lumen

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating the phenomenon of light: light sources; materials that let light through (refraction of light), reflect light (shadow/mirroring) and absorb light (warming); light can be split into colours (prism); the relationship with seeing; strength of light (*lichtsterkte*) in lumen

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child shines a torch through glass, water, tracing paper and card and at a mirror, white paper and black paper, and sorts the materials by what the light does. The child stands a pencil in a glass of water and sees it look bent at the surface, sends a beam to a chosen spot with a mirror, and holds a prism in a beam of light to get a band of colours. The child compares the lumen figures on the packaging of lamps with how bright each lamp looks, and feels a black and a white card that have lain in the sun. A child of 10 tests, sorts and describes; a child of 12 draws the path of the light as straight lines from the source to the object and into the eye, and predicts where a reflected beam will go.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. After the colon the statement names light sources; materials that let light through, reflect it and absorb it, with refraction, shadow and mirroring, and warming in brackets; the splitting of light into colours, with the prism in brackets; the relationship with seeing; and the strength of light in lumen. Lumen is the only unit named. *Lichtsterkte* is the everyday word for how much light a lamp gives, which lamp packaging states in lumen; in physics the same word names another quantity with another unit, and the statement is glossed as written. The source puts shadow in the brackets after reflecting; a shadow forms behind any material that does not let the light through. The text states no safety condition for work with lamps or sunlight.

### Common mistakes

Believing that we see because something goes out from the eye to the object, instead of light going from the object into the eye. Believing that only shiny things reflect light; a white wall reflects light too, in all directions, and that is why it can be seen. Believing that the prism adds the colours, when the colours were already in the white light.
