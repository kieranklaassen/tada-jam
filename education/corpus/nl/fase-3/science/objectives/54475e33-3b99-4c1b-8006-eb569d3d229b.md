## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes two or three climate zones, such as the tropics, a desert and the polar region, looks up which plants and animals live in each and sets them side by side in a table with the temperature and the rainfall. The child compares features, such as thick fur and a layer of fat in the cold, a cactus that stores water, and large leaves in the wet tropics. A child of 10 matches plants and animals to a zone and names what differs; a child of 12 gives reasons from temperature and rainfall and checks them against a climate graph.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names flora, fauna and climate zones, without saying which zones or how many, and it names no plant or animal. It asks what the differences are. The influence of weather and climate on people, animals, plants and landscape is in `ojw/rojh/7/05/fase3`.

### Common mistakes

Placing polar bears and penguins together, though polar bears live in the Arctic and no penguins live there. Believing that nothing lives or grows in a desert or in the polar regions. Saying that an animal or plant chose or wanted its features in order to live in a climate.
