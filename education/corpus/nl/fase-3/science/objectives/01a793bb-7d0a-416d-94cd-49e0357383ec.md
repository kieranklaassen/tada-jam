## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes a turn in caring for the class plants, a school garden or a class animal: feeling the soil before watering, giving the right amount of food, cleaning the cage. Outdoors the child lifts a stone or a log to look at the animals under it and lays it back as it was. After a test with small animals the child puts them back where they were found. A child of 10 keeps to the tasks of a care rota; a child of 12 plans the care for a weekend or a holiday and notices signs that a plant or animal is not doing well.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about how the child acts. It names no plants, animals or tasks and no knowledge. Another statement, `ojw/pdm/3/07/fase3`, makes care for living organisms a condition for experiments.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out the care that is needed when nothing seems to be happening: in a weekend or a holiday, or after the first weeks with a new plant or animal. Many give too much water or food, or treat an animal as if it wanted what a person wants.
