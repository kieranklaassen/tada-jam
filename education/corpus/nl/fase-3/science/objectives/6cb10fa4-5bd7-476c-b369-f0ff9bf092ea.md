## Official wording

ontwerpen en maken van een object naar eigen idee en inzicht en bij de materiaalkeuze rekening houden met materiaaleigenschappen en de functie van het voorwerp

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

designing and making an object according to one's own idea and understanding, and in choosing the material taking account of the properties of materials and the function of the object

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child decides what to make, such as a lunch box that keeps a drink cool or a stand for a book, and writes down what it must do. The child draws the design, picks a material for each part by its properties, such as stiff, bendable, waterproof, light or insulating, and says why. The child makes the object, tests it against what it must do and changes a material where the object fails. A child of 10 chooses from the materials on the table and gives one reason; a child of 12 compares two materials in a test before choosing and justifies the choice by both property and function.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The object follows the child's own idea; no object, material or tool is named. One condition is stated: the choice of material takes account of the properties of materials and of the function of the object.

### Common mistakes

Choosing a material for its colour or because it lies nearest, not for what the part must do. Giving the reason for a material only after the object is made. Making the whole object from one material where the parts need different properties.
