## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating temperature (heat): heat sources and cooling appliances; substances change through a change of temperature (freezing/cooling leads to solidifying/shrinking/condensing, heating/boiling leads to melting/evaporating/expanding)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a thermometer in melting ice, in tap water and in warm water, and keeps a graph of the temperature of a cup of warm water as it cools. The child melts ice and lets water freeze, watches drops form on the outside of a cold glass and a puddle dry up, and sees the liquid in a thermometer rise in warmth and fall in cold. The child lists the heat sources and the cooling appliances at home and says what each does to the things in or near it. A child of 10 observes and names melting, solidifying, evaporating and condensing; a child of 12 measures, draws the graph and explains that the substance stays the same substance and that the change can be undone.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. After the colon two parts are named: heat sources and cooling appliances, and substances changing through a change of temperature. The brackets name solidifying, shrinking and condensing for freezing or cooling, and melting, evaporating and expanding for heating or boiling. No temperature, unit or instrument is named, and freezing and boiling points are not mentioned. The wording does not mention that water expands when it freezes. The text states no safety condition for heating or boiling.

### Common mistakes

Believing that the drops on a cold glass have come through the glass, when they are water vapour from the air that has condensed. Believing that the bubbles in boiling water are air, when they are water vapour. Thinking of cold as something that flows into an object, when heat goes out of it.
