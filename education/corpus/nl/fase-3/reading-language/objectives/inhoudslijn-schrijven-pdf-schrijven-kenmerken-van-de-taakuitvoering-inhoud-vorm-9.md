## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

ensuring variation in word choice

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads their text again and circles words that come back often, such as "zei", "leuk" or "en toen". The child replaces some of them with another word that fits the sentence: "riep", "fluisterde" or "antwoordde" for "zei", and "spannend" or "gezellig" for "leuk". A child of 10 replaces a word that comes back often; a child of 12 chooses between words that mean nearly the same and says which one fits the sentence.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The institute's open data lacks this goal: the record is read from the content-line document (the PDF of the line Schrijven), and it has no code. One feature of a text is named: variation in word choice. The goal gives no list of words, names no kinds of words and does not say how much variation; the Dutch words above are the pack's examples. It mentions no aid such as a dictionary, names no kind of text and sets no text length.

### Common mistakes

Replacing a word with one that means something else or does not fit the sentence. Using several words for the same thing in a text that informs, so that the reader thinks different things are meant. Taking variation to mean long or difficult words.
