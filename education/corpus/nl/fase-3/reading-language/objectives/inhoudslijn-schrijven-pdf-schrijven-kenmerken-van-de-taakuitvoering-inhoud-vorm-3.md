## Official wording

structureren/verbeteren de tekst zelfstandig zodanig dat het verband tussen zinnen en tussen passages binnen de tekst duidelijk(er) wordt

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), Inhoudslijn po - Nederlands: Schrijven, page 2.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

structuring/improving the text independently, in such a way that the connection between sentences and between passages within the text becomes clear(er)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads their own first version again and marks the places where a reader cannot follow: a sentence that does not follow from the one before, a part that stands in the wrong place, a new point that begins without a sign. Without being told what to change, the child moves parts, joins or splits sentences, and adds a sentence that leads from one part to the next. A child of 10 finds such places by reading the text aloud and moves a sentence or adds a linking word; a child of 12 changes the order of whole parts and rewrites the sentences that link them.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The institute's open data lacks this goal: the record is read from the content-line document (the PDF of the line Schrijven), and it has no code. Two verbs are joined by a slash, structuring and improving; the wording does not say whether both are meant or either. "Zelfstandig" (independently) says that the child does this alone; no help is mentioned. The aim is stated: the connection between sentences and between passages within the text becomes clear or clearer; the brackets in "duidelijk(er)" leave both open. "Passages" is not explained and no number of parts is set; placing information in one paragraph is another goal of the row *inhoud/vorm* (record 7 of the row), and the language means for coherence are listed in record 4 of the row. The goal sets no text length. The source words this as a clause without its subject ("structureren/verbeteren de tekst"); structuring or improving the text is plainly meant.

### Common mistakes

Finding their own text clear because they know what they meant. Improving only spelling and single words and leaving the order as it was. Adding a linking word where the sentences do not have that link, for example "daarom" (that is why; the pack's example) after a sentence that gives no reason.
