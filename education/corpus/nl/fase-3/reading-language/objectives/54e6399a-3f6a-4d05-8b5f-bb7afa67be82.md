## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

summarising with the help of strategies and determining and inferring the subject of a text

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a text of a page or two and says in a word or two what it is about, also where the title does not say it, by looking at what all the paragraphs have in common. The child then summarises: marks the core sentence of each paragraph, strikes through examples and repetitions, and writes what is left as a short text or a diagram. A child of 10 finds the subject from the title, the headings and words that come back, and shortens paragraph by paragraph; a child of 12 infers a subject that is stated nowhere and writes a summary in their own words that follows the structure of the text.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal has two parts: summarising with the help of strategies (the source abbreviates "met behulp van" to "m.b.v."), and determining and inferring the subject of a text. No strategies are named; those above are the pack's examples. Two verbs are used for the subject, determining and inferring; the wording does not say how they differ, and the plain reading is that a subject can be stated in the text or has to be worked out. The wording says "onderwerp" (subject); it does not mention a main idea. It sets no length for the text or for the summary and does not say whether the summary is spoken or written. Its cluster is about study skills with non-fiction texts.

### Common mistakes

Retelling everything in the same order, a little shorter. Giving a detail, or the subject of the first paragraph, as the subject of the whole text. Keeping what is interesting or surprising and leaving out the main point.
