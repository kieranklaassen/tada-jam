## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child meets an unknown word in a text and decides, without asking, what to do: read on if the word does not matter; look in the sentences around it for a description, an example or a synonym; take the word apart into parts they know; or look it up. The child then reads the sentence again with the meaning found, to check that it fits. A child of 10 does this in texts of a familiar kind; a child of 12 does it in school-subject texts with many new terms and picks the way that suits the word.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. "Zelfstandig" (independently) is the stated condition; the wording does not say how much help, if any, that still allows. The verb is applying. The goal names no strategies; those above are the pack's examples. "(Lees)" (reading) stands in brackets: the wording covers vocabulary strategies and reading-vocabulary strategies and does not say how the two differ. The goal names no words, no number of words and no kind of text; its cluster is about the approach to non-fiction texts.

### Common mistakes

Reading on at every unknown word, also one that the text depends on. Taking an unknown word for a known word that looks like it. Looking the word up, taking the first meaning given, and not checking whether it fits the sentence.
