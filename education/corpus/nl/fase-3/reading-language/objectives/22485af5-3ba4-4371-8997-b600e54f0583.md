determining learning goals for a next speaking task

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After looking back on a presentation, the child chooses one or two things to work on in the next speaking task, such as "I look up from my card after every sentence" or "I practise aloud three times with a timer". The child writes them on the planning sheet for the next talk and tells a partner, who watches for them. After that talk the child checks whether it was done. A child of 10 words a goal about the delivery, in general terms; a child of 12 words a goal as an action that can be observed, about the delivery or about the preparation.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The learning goals are for a next speaking task; the statement does not say how many, what they are about or who words them. It does not carry "with help", which NE/MT/03/09/03/fase3, the goal about reflecting on a speaking task, does. NE/MT/01/08/03/fase3 and NE/MT/02/09/04/fase3 are the matching goals for a conversation and a listening task.

### Common mistakes

Choosing a goal that is too wide to act on ("be less nervous"). Choosing a goal that the audience named, without a link to the child's own looking back. Having forgotten the goal by the time the next speaking task is prepared.
