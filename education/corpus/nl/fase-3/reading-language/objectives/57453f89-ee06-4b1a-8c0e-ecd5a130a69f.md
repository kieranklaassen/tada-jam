## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

A child who still reads slowly or misreads words reads rows of words and short texts again, aloud to someone who listens, until the words are read at once and correctly. The child reads a line again when a word did not fit, and practises long and unfamiliar words from a school-subject text before reading the text. A child of 10 who needs this practises words of several syllables and loanwords; a child of 12 who needs this practises with the texts of the school subjects, and a child who already reads fluently and accurately does none of it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. "(Indien nodig)" (if needed) makes the statement conditional: the attention is for where it is needed, and the wording does not say who decides that or by what measure. It also does not say whose attention is meant, the school's or the child's. Two qualities of word recognition are named: fluent and accurate. The goal sets no reading speed, no number of words per minute, no word length, no kinds of words and no text level. It is the only statement under technical reading (NE/LE/09) at this level in this lane, and it is about recognising written words, not about understanding a text.

### Common mistakes

Reading fast by guessing a long word from its first letters, so "verantwoordelijk" becomes "verantwoording". Reading every word correctly but one by one and so slowly that they no longer know what the sentence said. Skipping or changing small words such as "de", "het" and "er".
