determining learning goals for a next conversation

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After looking back on a discussion, the child chooses one or two things to work on next time, such as "I ask a further question before I give my view" or "I summarise once". The child writes them down and reads them again just before the next conversation. Afterwards the child checks with a partner whether it happened. A child of 10 words a goal from what went less well, in general terms; a child of 12 words a goal that can be observed, says in which kind of conversation they will practise it and says how they will know that it was reached.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The learning goals are for a next conversation; the statement does not say how many, what they are about or who words them. It does not carry "with help", which NE/MT/01/08/02/fase3, the goal about reflecting on the child's own part in a conversation, does. The content-line document prints this goal without "van" ("bepalen leerdoelen voor een volgend gesprek"); the record keeps the wording of the open data. NE/MT/02/09/04/fase3 and NE/MT/03/09/04/fase3 are the matching goals for a listening task and a speaking task.

### Common mistakes

Choosing a goal that is too wide to act on ("join in better"). Choosing the goal that a friend or the adult named, without a link to how the child's own conversation went. Having forgotten the goal by the next conversation.
