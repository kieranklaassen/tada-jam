## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two weeks before a class presentation the child gets a sheet of guidelines: the length, the parts the talk must have, the use of a key-word card and the points on which it will be assessed. The child spreads the work over the days, collects information, makes the card and practises aloud for a partner, who checks the talk against the guidelines. The child times the talk and shortens it. A child of 10 follows the guidelines point by point; a child of 12 uses the guidelines to plan and to judge their own draft, and decides themselves what to change after practising.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Three speaking tasks are named, separated by slashes: a story, a class presentation (*spreekbeurt*) and a presentation; the wording does not say how the last two differ. The preparation is done by means of guidelines (the source prints the abbreviation *m.b.v.*, for *met behulp van*, "with the help of"); the statement does not say what the guidelines contain or who gives them. It does not carry "with help", which NE/MT/03/09/03/fase3 in the same sub-cluster does. It sets no length.

### Common mistakes

Preparing the slides or the poster and not what to say. Writing out the whole text and learning it by heart, and stopping when one word is forgotten. Reading the guidelines once at the start and not checking the talk against them before giving it.
