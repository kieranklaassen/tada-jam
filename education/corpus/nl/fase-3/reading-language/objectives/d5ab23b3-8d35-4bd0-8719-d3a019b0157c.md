## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before reading a text for a school subject, the child, without being told, looks at the title, the headings and the pictures and thinks of what they already know about the subject. While reading, the child notices when they no longer follow, and reads the part again, reads on to see whether it becomes clear, or looks at the diagram; afterwards the child says in a sentence what the text said. A child of 10 does this with a text of a familiar kind; a child of 12 chooses the way of reading that fits the text and the purpose, for example searching for one fact or reading closely to learn.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. "Zelfstandig" (independently) is the stated condition; the wording does not say how much help, if any, that still allows. The verb is applying. The goal names no strategies; those above are the pack's examples. It names no kind of text and no text length; its cluster is about the approach to non-fiction texts. Reflecting on carrying out the reading task is in NE/LE/05/03/01/fase3, where the wording says "with help".

### Common mistakes

Naming a strategy when asked and not using it when reading alone. Reading every text in the same way, for example reading every word of a text in which one fact is sought. Reading to the end without noticing that they stopped understanding.
