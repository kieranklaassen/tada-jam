## Official wording

(met hulp) reflecteren op eigen aandeel in en aanpak van gesprek en openstaan voor reacties van gesprekspartners daarop

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/MT/01/08; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau e006738f-5cc1-4354-b4cb-0e21e74dab86.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) reflecting on one's own part in and approach to a conversation and being open to conversation partners' reactions to that

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a group discussion the child says how often they spoke, whether they responded to others and what they did to help the group decide. The partners then say how they experienced the child's part ("you interrupted me twice", "your summary helped"), and the child listens without defending, asks what they mean and says what they recognise. A list of questions, or an adult who asks them, guides the looking back. A child of 10 recalls what they said and hears a reaction without contradicting it at once; a child of 12 says what their contribution did to the conversation, asks the partners for a reaction and takes something from it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help), which the content-line document prints after the verb and without brackets ("reflecteren met hulp op eigen aandeel in ..."); it does not say who helps or how, and it leaves open whether the help also covers the second part. The statement has two parts: reflecting on one's own part in and approach to a conversation, and being open to the conversation partners' reactions to that. The wording leaves open whether "own" also applies to the approach, that is, whether the approach is the child's or the group's. The statement names no points to reflect on and no form of reflection. NE/MT/01/08/03/fase3 is the goal about determining learning goals for a next conversation.

### Common mistakes

Answering "it went well" to every question, without an example. Hearing a partner's reaction as an attack and answering with what the partner did wrong. Talking about what the group or a classmate did, and not about their own part.
