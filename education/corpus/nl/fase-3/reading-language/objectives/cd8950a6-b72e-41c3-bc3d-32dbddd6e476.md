## Official wording

(met hulp) oriënteren op aanpak en uitvoering van de luistertaak

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) getting an orientation on the approach to and carrying out of the listening task

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before a spoken text the child reads the task and says what has to be done with what is heard: answer questions, make a summary or form an opinion. The child decides how to go about it: read the questions first, draw a table for notes, and find out whether the recording will be heard once or twice. A list of points to think about, or an adult who asks them, guides this. A child of 10 says what the task asks and takes over an approach that is offered; a child of 12 chooses between approaches that are offered and says what could be hard in this task.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help), which the content-line document prints at the end ("... van de luistertaak (met hulp)"); it does not say who helps or how. The orientation is on two things: the approach to the listening task and the carrying out of it. The statement does not say when the orientation takes place and names no points to attend to. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/02/09, titled *Aanpak* (approach), with NE/MT/02/09/03/fase3, the goal about reflecting on the same two things.

### Common mistakes

Starting to listen without knowing what has to be done with the text. Reading the task and not thinking about how to carry it out, for example whether and how to take notes. Planning to remember everything without notes, in a task that asks for details.
