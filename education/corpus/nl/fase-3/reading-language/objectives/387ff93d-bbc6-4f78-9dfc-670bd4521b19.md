## Official wording

instructies bij opdrachten die ze op een ander moment moeten uitvoeren begrijpen

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

understanding instructions for assignments that they have to carry out at another moment

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

On Monday the child hears what has to be done for Thursday: read chapter 4, bring two pictures and prepare three questions for the guest. The child writes the instruction in a planner in their own words, asks at once about the part that is unclear and on Wednesday does the work without asking again. The child tells a classmate who was absent what was asked. A child of 10 carries out a delayed instruction when it was written down at once; a child of 12 notices while listening what they will need to know at the later moment and asks about it at once.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The condition is part of the statement: the assignments are carried out at another moment than the moment of the instruction, and the wording does not say how much later. The wording does not say who "they" (*ze*) are; it is read here as the pupils. The statement sets no number of parts, and does not mention writing the instruction down or help. NE/MT/02/01/01/fase3 is the goal about understanding explanation about activities and tasks.

### Common mistakes

Feeling sure while listening that the instruction is understood, and finding at the moment of doing that a part is missing. Remembering what to do and not when, where or for whom. Not asking at the moment of the instruction, because it only becomes clear later that a part was not understood.
