## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child tries different kinds of text, such as a historical story, a comic book, a book of poems and a magazine about science, and notices which they want more of. The child keeps a list of what they have read, marks what they would read again, and uses the list to choose the next text. A child of 10 knows which subjects and series they like; a child of 12 knows which kinds of book and which writers they prefer, and can say what they do not like and why.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about developing preferences; it does not say what the preferences should be, and it does not say what they are preferences for. It names no particular kind of preference; subjects, genres and writers are named in NE/LE/07/03/02/fase3. The goal names no texts and sets no number of texts read and no reading level. It stands under reading pleasure and reading motivation (NE/LE/01).

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often go on reading one series or one kind of book and leave out trying another, so there is nothing to compare the preference with. Many choose what a friend chooses and take that for their own preference.
