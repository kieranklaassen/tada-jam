## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes a book without being asked, reads on in it at a free moment, takes it home, and looks for the next one when it is finished. The child tells someone what they are reading, and puts aside a book they do not want to go on with to take another. A child of 10 reads a book of their own choice to the end; a child of 12 goes on through a slow chapter, and also reads to find out something they want to know.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about motivation, and "zelf" (oneself) says that the child is the one who reads. It sets no reading level, no kind of text, no amount of reading and no reading time. It names nothing that should motivate the child.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out reading in their own time: they read when it is asked and stop when the set time is over. Some choose a book that is too hard or too long for them and do not finish it.
