## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

For a poem to be recited the child practises aloud many times until they know it by heart; for an explanation of a sum the child thinks through the steps and tries them on a partner (the strategies in this note are the pack's examples). For an argued talk the child collects arguments, puts them in order and thinks of the objection a listener could raise. The child says beforehand which approach they chose for this task and why. A child of 10 knows that reciting, explaining and convincing ask for different preparation and takes over an approach that is suggested; a child of 12 chooses the approach from the task and the purpose and changes it when practising shows that it does not work.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. One thing is matched to two: the speaking strategy to the speaking task and to the speaking purpose. The statement names no strategies, no tasks and no purposes, and gives no examples. The source prints a dash where a hyphen is meant (*spreektaak en –doel*); the gloss reads it as "speaking task and speaking purpose". NE/MT/03/09/01/fase3 is the goal about preparing by means of guidelines. The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Preparing every speaking task in the same way, most often by writing out a text and learning it by heart. Choosing the approach by what is pleasant to do (making slides) and not by what the task asks. Practising silently for a task that needs to be practised aloud.
