## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears "After weeks without rain the soil was arid and nothing grew" and says that "arid" must mean very dry, because of the weeks without rain and the lack of growth. The child hears the Dutch word *onherkenbaar* and hears *on-*, *herken* and *-baar* in it: not able to be recognised. The child checks the guess against the next sentences and changes it when it does not fit. A child of 10 infers a meaning from the sentence the word is in or from a known part of the word; a child of 12 combines the wider text, the parts of the word and knowledge of the subject, and says how sure the guess is.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no way of inferring (from the sentence, from parts of the word, from knowledge of the subject) and gives no examples. The words are unknown words in a text that is listened to, since the open data lists the goal under listening, in sub-cluster NE/MT/02/06, titled *Woordenschat en -strategieën* (vocabulary and vocabulary strategies). NE/MT/02/06/01/fase3 is the goal about asking for a word's meaning. The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Guessing from another word that sounds similar. Keeping the first guess when the rest of the text contradicts it. Thinking so long about one word that the following sentences are missed.
