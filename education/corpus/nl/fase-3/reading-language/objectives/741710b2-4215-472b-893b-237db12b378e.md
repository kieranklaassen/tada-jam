## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child opens a word-processing program, types a text, saves it under a name, finds it again later, and changes it: selects a part, cuts it, pastes it elsewhere, undoes a step. The child sets headings, puts in a picture or a table, and prints the text or shares the file. A child of 10 types, saves and makes simple changes; a child of 12 uses the program for a whole text, from the first version to the final laid-out one.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. "Werken met" (working with) is a general verb: the wording names no functions, no program, no typing method and no typing speed; the functions above are the pack's examples. Its code places it with appropriate layout (NE/SCH/02/06/01/fase3) and editing for spelling and punctuation (NE/SCH/02/06/02/fase3); the wording itself does not link them. The means that such programs offer are named in NE/SCH/02/05/04/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Not saving the text, or saving it under a name or in a place where they cannot find it again. Pressing the enter key at the end of every line, as when starting a new line in handwriting. Trusting the spelling checker: accepting a suggestion that is a different word, or taking a text without marked words to be free of errors.
