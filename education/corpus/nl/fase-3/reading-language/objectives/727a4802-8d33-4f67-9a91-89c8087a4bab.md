## Official wording

(waar nodig met hulp) inzetten van schrijfstrategieën: - oriënteren met aandacht voor het doel en publiek; - plannen en maken van een opzet voor de tekst; - reflecteren en reviseren op inhoud (doel, publiek, opbouw van tekst) en vorm (opbouw van zinnen, spelling)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(where needed with help) putting writing strategies to use: - orienting, with attention to the purpose and audience; - planning and making an outline for the text; - reflecting and revising on content (purpose, audience, build-up of text) and form (build-up of sentences, spelling)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before writing, the child notes who the text is for and what it is for, collects ideas, and makes an outline: the parts in their order, with a few key words for each. After the first version, the child reads it again against the outline, the purpose and the reader, and changes things: adds an explanation that is missing, moves a paragraph, splits a sentence that is too long, corrects spelling. Where the child cannot go on, help is given. A child of 10 makes an outline and revises sentences and words; a child of 12 plans the parts of a text and revises content and order before spelling.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The record's wording carries "(waar nodig met hulp)" (where needed with help): the help is for where it is needed, and the wording does not say from whom or who decides. The content-line document prints other words, after the verb: "inzetten (waar nodig met begeleiding) van schrijfstrategieën:" (where needed with guidance). Three strategies are listed: orienting, with attention to purpose and audience; planning and making an outline; reflecting and revising. For the last, the brackets name what is looked at: for content the purpose, the audience and the build-up of the text, and for form the build-up of sentences and spelling. Punctuation is not among what the brackets name; it is in NE/SCH/02/06/02/fase3. The goal names no kind of text and no text length.

### Common mistakes

Starting to write at once and making the outline afterwards, or not at all. Taking revising to mean copying the text out neatly or correcting spelling only. Reading back what they meant and not what they wrote, so missing words and missing steps go unnoticed.
