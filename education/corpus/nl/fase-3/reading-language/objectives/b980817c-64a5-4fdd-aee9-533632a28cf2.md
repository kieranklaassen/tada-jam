## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening to and understanding: - stories with several storylines; - poems; - (theatre) performances; - more complex information or explanation; - information and opinions in an argumentative text

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child listens to a book read aloud in which the stories of two characters alternate, and tells afterwards what happened to each and where the two stories met. After a classmate's argued talk for a school without homework, the child says which parts were information and which were opinion. After a theatre performance the child tells what the main character wanted and how that was shown. A child of 10 follows the storylines when the change between them is marked and repeats a speaker's opinion with a reason; a child of 12 follows a change of storyline that is not marked, and keeps facts, opinions and arguments apart.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement lists five things to listen to and understand: stories with several storylines, poems, performances (with "theatre" in brackets), more complex information or explanation, and information and opinions in an argumentative text. The list is not introduced as examples. "More complex" is a comparative, and the wording does not say more complex than what; the statement sets no length or difficulty of text. The content-line document's text features for listening at this fase, which are no part of the goal and are quoted in the lane's frame, name the kinds of text ("informatieve, verhalende, instructieve en betogende teksten": informative, narrative, instructive and argumentative texts) and their length ("informatieve teksten: 10 tot 15 minuten", "verhalende teksten: tot 20 minuten": informative texts 10 to 15 minutes, narrative texts up to 20 minutes). The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/02/02, titled *Luisteren als lid van een live publiek* (listening as a member of a live audience); NE/MT/02/03/01/fase3 is the goal about news, societal subjects and discussion programmes. The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Mixing up two storylines: attributing an event to the wrong character or the wrong time. Hearing an opinion in an argumentative text as a fact. Remembering the speaker's most vivid example and not the point it was meant to support.
