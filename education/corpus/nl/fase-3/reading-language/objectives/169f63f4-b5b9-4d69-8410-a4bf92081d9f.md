## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

getting to know and using spelling categories and spelling rules

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child learns a group of words that share a spelling feature, sorts word cards into such groups, says the rule aloud and writes other words with it: verb forms such as "hij wordt" and "het is gebeurd", loanwords such as "bureau" and "station", words that end in "-isch" or "-teit", plurals such as "baby's" and "cadeaus". The child writes dictated sentences and says which rule decided a word they doubted. A child of 10 applies rules such as the stem plus t in the present tense and the doubling of a consonant; a child of 12 works out the spelling of past tenses and past participles by rule and writes common loanwords.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names no spelling categories and no rules, and sets no number; the categories and rules above, including the spelling of verbs and loanwords, are the pack's examples, and nothing in the wording says which belong to this band. A spelling category is a group of words that share a spelling feature; the wording uses the term without explaining it. Two verbs are used: getting to know and using. The goal sets no word length and no share of words spelled correctly, and it does not say whether the rules are used in dictated words or in the child's own texts. The content-line document prints spelling categories and rules by pair of *groepen*, each offered from the pair it stands under; they are no part of the goal, and the column for *groep* 7-8 is quoted in the lane's frame.

### Common mistakes

Writing a verb form as it sounds: "hij word", "het is gebeurt". Applying a rule where it does not belong: "ik vindt", with the t of "hij vindt". Knowing the rule in a spelling exercise and not using it when writing a text of their own.
