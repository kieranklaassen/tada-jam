determining learning goals for a next listening task

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After looking back on a listening task, the child chooses one or two things to work on in the next one, such as "I note key words, not sentences" or "I read the questions before the text starts". The child writes them at the top of the next note sheet. After that task the child checks whether it was done and whether it helped. A child of 10 words a goal from what went wrong, in general terms; a child of 12 words a goal that names an action, links it to a kind of listening task and says how they will check it.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The learning goals are for a next listening task; the statement does not say how many, what they are about or who words them. It does not carry "with help", which NE/MT/02/09/03/fase3, the goal about reflecting on the listening task, does. NE/MT/01/08/03/fase3 and NE/MT/03/09/04/fase3 are the matching goals for a conversation and a speaking task.

### Common mistakes

Choosing a goal that is too wide to act on ("concentrate more"). Choosing a goal about behaviour ("sit still") where the difficulty was in the approach to listening. Having forgotten the goal by the next listening task.
