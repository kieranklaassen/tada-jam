## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The group talks about plastic in the sea, and the child adds a new side of the topic: what happens to the plastic that is collected, or what a local shop does about packaging. The child brings in a related matter, "It is the same with clothes that are thrown away", and says how it is connected. The child asks a question about a part of the topic that was not yet discussed, such as who should pay for the cleaning. A child of 10 adds a fact, an experience or a related matter and makes the link clear; a child of 12 adds a cause, a consequence or an opposing view, and judges whether the addition helps the talk reach its purpose.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is one short phrase. It does not say whether expanding means adding more about the same topic or widening the talk to related topics, and it sets no limit to how far. It sets no length and does not mention help. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/01/03, titled *Gespreksregels en -vaardigheden* (conversation rules and skills).

### Common mistakes

Bringing in a matter that is linked to the topic by one word only, so that the talk changes subject. Adding something without making the link clear, so that the others hear it as a change of subject. Expanding so far, in a talk meant to reach a decision, that the decision is not reached.
