## Official wording

schrijven in toenemende mate zelfstandig op de computer en gebruiken de middelen die tekstverwerkingsprogramma's bieden (typografie, lay-out, kopjes/structuur,..)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/SCH/02/05; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau 301d22c0-e5a2-4aa6-8001-dee6b47f709a.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

writing on the computer, increasingly independently, and using the means that word-processing programs offer (typography, layout, headings/structure, ..)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child types a text of their own, gives it a title and headings with the heading styles of the program, chooses a typeface and a size, makes a list with bullets and places a picture. The child moves a paragraph by cutting and pasting, saves the text, and opens it again later to go on. A child of 10 types a text and makes the title and the headings larger or bold, and is shown where a function is; a child of 12 lays out a text of several pages with headings, page numbers and a table of contents alone.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. "In toenemende mate zelfstandig" (increasingly independently) gives a direction, not a level: the wording does not say how independently, or by when. The brackets name typography, layout and headings/structure and end with ",..", which shows that the list goes on; they carry no "bijv.". The goal names no program, no functions beyond the brackets, no typing method and no typing speed; the functions above are the pack's examples. The source words both parts as clauses without their subject ("schrijven ...", "gebruiken de middelen ...") and prints ",.." at the end of the brackets; writing on the computer and using these means is plainly meant. Working with word-processing programs is also named in NE/SCH/02/06/03/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Making each heading by hand, with bold or larger letters, so that the headings differ from one another. Pressing the enter key or the space bar many times to move text to a new page or to line things up. Spending the time on typefaces and colours and little on the text.
