## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before a spoken text about the water cycle the child looks at the title and tells a partner what they already know and what they expect to hear (the strategies in this note are the pack's examples). While listening, the child notes key words in a simple diagram and puts a question mark at a part that was not understood. Afterwards the child checks the expectation against what was said. A child of 10 uses a strategy that was practised just before, such as predicting or noting key words; a child of 12 chooses a strategy that fits the listening purpose and the kind of text, and combines strategies.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no listening strategies and no number of them, and does not mention help. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/02/04, titled *Luisterdoelen en -strategieën* (listening purposes and strategies), with the goals about a given listening purpose (NE/MT/02/04/01/fase3) and about the way of listening (NE/MT/02/04/02/fase3); a goal with the same wording, NE/MT/02/09/02/fase3, is listed under *Aanpak* (approach).

### Common mistakes

Keeping to a prediction after the text has continued differently. Writing so much while listening that the next part is missed. Using the same strategy for every text, whatever the listening purpose.
