## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reading and discussing texts for age and target group

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a book, a poem or an article written for children of their age and talks about it with others: what it was about, what they thought of it, what was hard to believe, and who else would like it. The child reads a passage aloud to show what they mean. A child of 10 talks about the events, the characters and what was funny or exciting; a child of 12 also talks about what the writer wanted, about subjects such as friendship or loss, and about what kind of reader the text suits.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. Two activities are named: reading and discussing. "Voor leeftijd en doelgroep" (for age and target group) is short: the wording does not say whose age or which target group, and the plain reading is texts meant for children of the pupils' own age and group. The goal names no kinds of text, no text length and no reading level, and it does not say with whom the texts are discussed. It stands under reading pleasure and reading motivation (NE/LE/01).

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often retell the text when asked to talk about it and say nothing about what they thought of it. Some say only "nice" or "boring" and stop there.
