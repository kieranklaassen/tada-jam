## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

During a spoken explanation of how a law is made, the child notices that they can no longer follow the steps and does something about it: looks at the diagram on the board, asks which step the speaker is at, or notes the question for the pause (the strategies in this note are the pack's examples). At a pause the child tells a partner the main points so far, to check whether it was understood. A child of 10 uses a strategy when reminded at the moment the listening becomes hard; a child of 12 notices themselves that they no longer follow, chooses a strategy and goes on listening.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no listening strategies and no number of them, and does not mention help. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/02/09, titled *Aanpak* (approach), between NE/MT/02/09/01/fase3 (orientation on the listening task) and NE/MT/02/09/03/fase3 (reflection on it), two goals that carry "(met hulp)" (with help); a goal with the same wording, NE/MT/02/04/03/fase3, is listed under *Luisterdoelen en -strategieën* (listening purposes and strategies).

### Common mistakes

Looking at the speaker without following, and not showing it. Asking for everything to be repeated instead of saying which part was unclear. Knowing the strategies by name and not using one when the listening becomes hard.
