## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

For a route description the child listens for each step and writes the steps in order; for a story read for enjoyment the child listens without notes. To find one fact, such as a departure time in a spoken announcement, the child waits for that part and does not try to remember the rest. To judge a speaker's opinion the child listens to the whole text and notes the arguments. A child of 10 chooses between listening for the main points and listening for one detail when the purpose has been made clear; a child of 12 chooses the way of listening themselves, says why, and changes it during the text when it does not serve the purpose.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement names no ways of listening and no purposes. NE/MT/02/04/01/fase3 is the goal about listening purposefully from a given listening purpose, and NE/MT/02/04/03/fase3 the goal about listening strategies. The open data lists it under sub-cluster NE/MT/02/04, titled *Luisterdoelen en -strategieën* (listening purposes and strategies). The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Listening to an instruction as to a story, and missing a step. Trying to note everything when the main points were needed. Keeping to the chosen way of listening when the text turns out to be different from what was expected.
