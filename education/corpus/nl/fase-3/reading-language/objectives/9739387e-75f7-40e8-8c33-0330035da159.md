## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) reflecting on the approach to and carrying out of the listening task

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a listening task the child looks at their notes and answers and says how they went about it: what they did before listening, what they noted and where they stopped following. The child says what worked ("reading the questions first") and what did not ("I wrote whole sentences and missed the next part"). A list of questions, or an adult who asks them, guides the looking back. A child of 10 says whether the task went well and names something they did; a child of 12 links the result to the approach and says what they would do differently in a task of the same kind.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help), which the content-line document prints at the end ("... van de luistertaak (met hulp)"); it does not say who helps or how. The reflection is on two things: the approach to the listening task and the carrying out of it. The statement names no points to reflect on and no form of reflection. NE/MT/02/09/01/fase3 is the goal about orientation on the same two things, and NE/MT/02/09/04/fase3 the goal about determining learning goals for a next listening task.

### Common mistakes

Judging the text ("it was boring", "he talked too fast") and not their own approach. Looking only at the number of right answers. Saying "I should listen better", without naming what to do differently.
