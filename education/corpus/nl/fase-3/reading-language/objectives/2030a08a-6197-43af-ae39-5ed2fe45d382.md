## Official wording

toelichten van eigen voorkeuren voor onderwerpen, genres en schrijvers

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/LE/07/03; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau 2030a08a-6197-43af-ae39-5ed2fe45d382.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

explaining one's own preferences for subjects, genres and writers

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays three books they liked next to each other and says what they share: the subject, the kind of book or the writer. The child explains the preference to someone else: historical stories, because they want to know how people lived then; everything by one writer, because the children in her books talk like real children. A child of 10 names favourite subjects and a series or a writer and gives a reason; a child of 12 explains a preference for a genre or a writer with reasons from the books themselves and says how the preference has changed.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is explaining (*toelichten*): giving reasons for a preference the child has. The goal does not say what the preference should be. Three things are named that a preference can be for: subjects, genres and writers. The goal names no books and no genres, sets no number of books read, and does not say to whom or in what form the child explains. Its cluster is about fiction. Developing reading preferences is in NE/LE/01/05/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Naming a title or a series and not saying what it is about those books that they like. Giving a reason that repeats the preference: "because those are good books". Giving a friend's preference as their own.
