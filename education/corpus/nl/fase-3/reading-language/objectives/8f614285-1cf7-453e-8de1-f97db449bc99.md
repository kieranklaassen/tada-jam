## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(with help) reflecting on the approach to and carrying out of a speaking task

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After a presentation the child watches a recording of it, or hears two points from classmates, and says what went as planned and what did not. The child looks back at the preparation too: when they started, how often they practised aloud and whether the key-word card worked. A list of questions, or an adult who asks them, guides the looking back. A child of 10 says what went well and what did not in the delivery; a child of 12 links how the talk went to how it was prepared and says what they would do differently.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The wording carries "(met hulp)" (with help), which the content-line document prints after the verb ("reflecteren (met hulp) op ..."); it does not say who helps or how. The reflection is on two things: the approach to a speaking task and the carrying out of it. The statement names no points to reflect on and no form of reflection. NE/MT/03/09/04/fase3 is the goal about determining learning goals for a next speaking task, and NE/MT/03/04/01/fase3 names dealing with comments or feedback.

### Common mistakes

Judging only the feeling ("I was nervous", "it went fine") without an example. Looking at the delivery and not at the preparation that led to it. Hearing classmates' points as a mark, and not using them to say what to change.
