## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

listening purposefully on the basis of a given listening purpose

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before a spoken text about volcanoes the child is given a purpose: "find out why some volcanoes erupt with ash and others with lava". The child listens for that, notes two key words when the part comes and afterwards answers the question to a partner. With another purpose, "decide whether you agree with the speaker", the child listens for the opinion and the reasons. A child of 10 listens for the part the purpose asks for and answers the question; a child of 12 keeps to the purpose through a text with side matters and says afterwards whether the purpose was reached.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The listening purpose is given: the statement does not ask the child to set a purpose, and does not say who gives it. It names no purposes and no kinds of text. The content-line document's text features for listening at this fase, which are no part of the goal and are quoted in the lane's frame, name the kinds of text ("informatieve, verhalende, instructieve en betogende teksten": informative, narrative, instructive and argumentative texts) and their length ("informatieve teksten: 10 tot 15 minuten", "verhalende teksten: tot 20 minuten": informative texts 10 to 15 minutes, narrative texts up to 20 minutes). NE/MT/02/04/02/fase3 is the goal about matching the way of listening to the purpose. The source prints the verb first and without *van* ("of") after it (*luisteren doelgericht*), unlike most neighbouring goals; the gloss reads it in the same form as theirs. The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Forgetting the listening purpose once the text becomes interesting. Trying to remember everything, and missing the part the purpose asked for. Stopping listening after the first part that seems to answer the purpose, when a later part corrects or completes it.
