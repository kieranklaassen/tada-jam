## Official wording

evalueren van een tekst met emotieve argumenten (bijv. spannend, meeslepend, grappig)

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_subcluster NE/LE/07/02; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau 7386514a-b5b7-40a0-b622-32f1df46d73e.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

evaluating a text with emotive arguments (e.g. exciting, gripping, funny)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

After finishing a story or a book, the child says what they think of it and gives the feeling it gave them as the reason: it was exciting, because until the last chapter it was not clear who could be trusted; it was funny, because of the grandmother's answers. The child writes a short opinion with such a reason and names the passage that caused it. A child of 10 gives a feeling word and points to a part of the text; a child of 12 says what in the text caused the feeling, and says where the text did not interest them.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is evaluating: giving a judgement of a text. The judgement is given with "emotieve argumenten" (emotive arguments), which the wording does not explain; the examples show that reasons from what the reader felt are meant. "Bijv." introduces three examples: exciting, gripping, funny. The goal does not name other kinds of argument, such as whether a story is realistic or well built. It says "een tekst" (a text) and names no kind; its cluster is about fiction. It does not say whether the evaluation is spoken or written. Exchanging reading experiences with fellow pupils is in NE/LE/07/03/01/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Saying only "nice" or "boring" and giving no reason. Retelling the story when asked what they thought of it. Giving a reason that is not about the text: "because my friend read it too".
