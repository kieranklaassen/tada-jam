## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes when nobody asks for it: a story that goes on over several days, a diary, a comic with text, the words of a song, a letter that asks for something, an article for the school newspaper. The child wants a reader for the text, shows it to someone and asks what they think. A child of 10 writes stories and notes about what interests them; a child of 12 also writes to say what they think about something, and chooses the form.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about motivation; it sets no kind of writing, no length and no correctness. The wording does not say whether "writing" means composing a text, forming letters by hand, or both; the other goals of its cluster (NE/SCH/02) are about composing texts. The goal itself does not settle this; the sentence the content-line document prints under the cluster heading, quoted in the lane's frame, speaks of writing coherent texts ("Samenhangende teksten schrijven ..."). It names nothing that should motivate the child.

### Common mistakes

This is not a skill with a typical error. Children most often leave out writing of their own accord: they write what is asked and make it as short as is allowed. Some say they have nothing to write about, and some stop when they expect every mistake to be marked.
