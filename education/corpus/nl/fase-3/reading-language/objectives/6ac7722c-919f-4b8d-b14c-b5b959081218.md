## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child listens to an explanation of how a project week is arranged: which group works where, what must be finished on which day, and what to do when material is missing. The child then starts the right task in the right place and explains the arrangement to a classmate who came in late. When one part was unclear, the child asks about that part only. A child of 10 follows an explanation with a condition ("if you finish early, then ...") and starts the right task; a child of 12 follows an explanation with an exception ("everyone except the group at the window") and keeps to it during the task.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The statement is about explanation of activities and tasks; NE/MT/02/01/02/fase3 is the separate goal about instructions for assignments that are carried out at another moment. It sets no number of steps and no length, and does not say who explains or whether the explanation is shown as well as spoken. The content-line document's text features for listening at this fase, which are no part of the goal and are quoted in the lane's frame, name the kinds of text ("informatieve, verhalende, instructieve en betogende teksten": informative, narrative, instructive and argumentative texts) and their length ("informatieve teksten: 10 tot 15 minuten", "verhalende teksten: tot 20 minuten": informative texts 10 to 15 minutes, narrative texts up to 20 minutes). The statement does not mention help.

### Common mistakes

Remembering the first and the last step and not the ones between. Missing a condition or an exception ("only if", "except") and doing the general thing. Stopping listening once the child thinks they know what to do, and missing what was said last.
