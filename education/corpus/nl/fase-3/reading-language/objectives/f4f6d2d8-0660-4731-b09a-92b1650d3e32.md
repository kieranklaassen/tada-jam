## Official wording

lezen van: - verhalende teksten en jeugdliteratuur (bijv. sciencefiction, oorlogsverhalen, fantastische vertellingen, jeugdromans en historische verhalen); - poëzie (bijv. vormvrije gedichten zonder rijm en met minder herkenbare opbouw, gevoels- en liefdesgedichten en -liederen, zelf voorgedragen raps)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reading: - narrative texts and literature for young readers (e.g. science fiction, war stories, fantasy tales, novels for young readers and historical stories); - poetry (e.g. free-form poems without rhyme and with a less recognisable structure, poems and songs of feeling and of love, self-performed raps)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a whole novel for young readers over a week or two, a war story or a historical story, and a science-fiction story. The child reads poems that do not rhyme, reads the words of a song as a poem, and performs a rap aloud. A child of 10 reads books with one clear line of events and poems with a form they recognise; a child of 12 reads novels in which the time or the narrator changes, and poems whose structure and meaning are less plain.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The verb is reading. Two groups are named: narrative texts and literature for young readers (*jeugdliteratuur*), and poetry. Everything in brackets follows "bijv." and is an example; none is required. "Minder herkenbare opbouw" (less recognisable structure) is a comparative, and the wording does not say less than what. "Zelf voorgedragen raps" (self-performed raps) stands among the examples of poetry to read; the wording does not say whether the child also writes the rap. The goal sets no text length, no reading level, no reading speed and no number of texts, and apart from that one example it does not say whether the child reads silently or aloud. The content-line document's text features for the fiction of this fase, which give subjects and structure and no text length, are no part of the goal and are quoted in the lane's frame.

### Common mistakes

Deciding that a text is not a poem because it does not rhyme, and reading it as plain prose without attention to line breaks and repeated words. Taking a historical story or a war story as a true account in every detail. No longer following a novel after a change of time or of narrator, and reading on without going back.
