## Official wording

handig en efficiënt optellen en aftrekken met hele getallen in contextsituaties en formele wiskundetaal waarbij een doelmatige oplossingsmanier wordt gekozen op basis van inzicht in de eigenschappen van bewerkingen en de structuur van getallen (zoals compenseren, analogie, omvormen, aanvullen, verschil bepalen, volgorde verwisselen en de inverse relatie tussen optellen en aftrekken)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

adding and subtracting with whole numbers handily (*handig*, with a shortcut that fits the numbers) and efficiently, in context situations and formal mathematical language, in which an effective way of solving is chosen on the basis of insight into the properties of operations and the structure of numbers (such as compensating, analogy, transforming (*omvormen*, changing it into an easier equal sum), completing (*aanvullen*, counting on to the larger number), finding the difference, changing the order and the inverse relation between adding and subtracting)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child works 2,998 + 1,465 as 3,000 + 1,465 − 2 = 4,463, and works 6,003 − 5,996 by counting on from 5,996, first 4 and then 3, so 7. The child changes 563 − 298 into 565 − 300 = 265. The child adds 275 + 480 + 25 by taking 275 and 25 together first, 300 + 480 = 780. A child of 10 uses a shortcut when it sees that a number is close to a round number; a child of 12 looks at the numbers first, chooses a way and says why the chosen one fits.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. "zoals" (such as) introduces the bracketed list as examples: compensating, analogy, *omvormen*, *aanvullen*, finding the difference, changing the order, and the inverse relation between adding and subtracting. The way of solving is chosen on insight into the properties of operations and the structure of numbers. The numbers are whole numbers, in context situations and in formal mathematical language. This goal states no number range. It does not say whether the working is done mentally or with notes on paper; rw/bew/3/17/fase3 allows notes on paper for its own content.

### Common mistakes

Compensating in the wrong direction, working 2,998 + 1,465 as 3,000 + 1,465 + 2. In a subtraction, changing the two numbers in opposite directions as one does in an addition, so that 563 − 298 becomes 561 − 300 = 261. Using the written algorithm for every sum without looking at the numbers first.
