## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

investigating the system of weight measures in the metric system, explaining the relations between the measures (kg, hg, dag, g, dg, cg, mg) and using the meaning of the prefixes milli- and kilo- in doing so

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sets the seven measures in a row from kg to mg and finds, by weighing and by reasoning, that each measure is ten times the next smaller one: 1 kg = 10 hg, 1 hg = 10 dag, 1 dag = 10 g, 1 g = 10 dg, 1 dg = 10 cg, 1 cg = 10 mg. The child explains that kilo- means a thousand, so 1 kg = 1000 g, and that milli- means a thousandth, so 1 g = 1000 mg. A child of 10 learns the row and the two prefixes; a child of 12 explains any step in the row, for example why 1 hg is 100 g.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name seven weight measures: kg, hg, dag, g, dg, cg and mg. The goal names two prefixes, milli- and kilo-. It does not name centi-, deci-, deca- and hecto-, although measures with those prefixes stand in the brackets; the goal for length, `rw/m/1/09/fase3`, names all six. The goal prints no relation as an equation: the steps of ten in the paragraph above follow from the metric system, not from the wording. The wording names no weight measures beyond the seven in the brackets; the *ton* is named in `rw/m/4/01/fase3`. Converting is the subject of `rw/m/4/06/fase3`. The goal names no formula and states no number range. The source prints a comma after "stelsel" and one after "milli-" that do not belong there; the gloss leaves them out.

### Common mistakes

The child confuses dag (decagram) and dg (decigram). The child takes milli- to mean a millionth, or the same as centi-. The child takes the step between two neighbouring measures to be 1000, as between kilogram and gram, the pair it knows from daily life.
