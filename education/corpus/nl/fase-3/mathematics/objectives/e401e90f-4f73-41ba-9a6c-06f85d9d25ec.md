## Official wording

hoeveelheden schatten en precies tellen door gebruik te maken van gegeven structuren en door zelf te structureren (bijvoorbeeld splitsen of samenvoegen in groepen)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

estimating (*schatten*: judging by eye, or working out roughly) and precisely counting quantities by making use of given structures and by structuring oneself (for example splitting or joining into groups)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child looks at a photo of a crowd with a grid of 20 squares laid over it, counts 12 people in one square and estimates about 240 people. To count a jar of coins precisely the child stacks them in piles of 10, counts 23 piles and 7 loose coins and says 237. A child of 10 uses a structure that is given, such as rows, boxes or a grid; a child of 12 chooses a structure, says how far the estimate can be off and compares it with a count.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names two actions, estimating and precise counting, and two ways: using structures that are given and structuring by oneself. The brackets give examples of structuring by oneself ("bijvoorbeeld"): splitting, or joining into groups. This goal states no number range and does not say how close an estimate has to be. The Dutch *schatten* can mean judging a quantity by eye or working it out roughly; the wording does not choose.

### Common mistakes

The child counts one by one and loses count where a structure would have helped. The child names a number at random as an estimate and does not start from a part that was counted. The child multiplies one counted square by the number of squares although the squares are filled very unevenly, and does not adjust.
