## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

reasoning about quantities and about the order of magnitude of quantities within different contexts

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child is asked whether a school with 8 classes has about 20, 200 or 2,000 pupils, takes about 25 pupils for one class and reasons to about 200. The child says whether a full football stadium holds hundreds, thousands or tens of thousands of people, and names a known quantity to compare with, such as the number of people who fit in the school hall. A child of 10 reasons from a known quantity to another, as from one class to the school; a child of 12 says whether a number in a news report has a believable size and gives the reason.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about reasoning, both about quantities and about their order of magnitude (tens, hundreds, thousands and so on), within different contexts; it names no contexts. This goal states no number range. The goal rw/gb/1/03/fase3 names its own range, "tot ten minste 1 miljard" (up to at least 1,000,000,000), for the structure of the counting sequence.

### Common mistakes

The child has no known quantity to compare large numbers with and treats a thousand, a hundred thousand and a million alike, as very many. The child gives an exact-looking number where only the order of magnitude can be known. The child does not compare an outcome with what is known, and accepts 2,000 pupils for a school with 8 classes.
