## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child starts a stopwatch when another child begins to run and stops it at the finish, reads the display and writes the time down. The child says what the places on the display stand for, for example reads 1:05.3 as 1 minute, 5 seconds and 3 tenths of a second. A child of 10 starts and stops the stopwatch and reads the display; a child of 12 does the same, so the two differ little here.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names one activity, measuring time, and one instrument, a stopwatch. It names no units, no accuracy and no kind of stopwatch (with hands or with digits); the reading in the paragraph above is the pack's example. Times to hundredths of a second are named in `rw/m/6/06/fase3`. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child presses too late or too early. The child reads the display as one decimal number, for example takes 1:05 for 1.05 minutes or for 105 seconds. The child forgets to set the stopwatch back to zero and measures on top of the last time.
