## Official wording

in contextsituaties en formele wiskundetaal handig en efficiënt vermenigvuldigen en delen met decimale getallen waarbij een doelmatige oplossingsmanier wordt gekozen op basis van inzicht in de eigenschappen van bewerkingen en in de structuur van getallen (zoals verwisselen, compenseren, analogie, omvormen, verdubbelen en halveren, volgorde verwisselen en de inverse relatie tussen vermenigvuldigen en delen). Hierbij zijn notities op papier toegestaan

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

in context situations and formal mathematical language, multiplying and dividing with decimal numbers handily (*handig*, with a shortcut that fits the numbers) and efficiently, in which an effective way of solving is chosen on the basis of insight into the properties of operations and into the structure of numbers (such as swapping, compensating, analogy, transforming (*omvormen*, changing it into an easier equal sum), doubling and halving, changing the order and the inverse relation between multiplying and dividing). Notes on paper are allowed here

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child works 8 × 2.5 by halving and doubling, 4 × 5 = 20, and works 6 × 1.99 as 6 × 2 − 6 × 0.01 = 11.94. The child swaps 0.25 × 36 to 36 × 0.25, takes a quarter of 36 and gets 9. The child works 4.8 ÷ 0.6 by thinking of 48 ÷ 6 = 8, and jots in-between results on paper. A child of 10 uses a shortcut when it sees that a number is close to a whole number; a child of 12 looks at the numbers first, chooses a way and says why the chosen one fits.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. "zoals" (such as) introduces the bracketed list as examples: swapping, compensating, analogy, *omvormen*, doubling and halving, changing the order, and the inverse relation between multiplying and dividing. The source lists both "verwisselen" (swapping) and "volgorde verwisselen" (changing the order). The last sentence allows notes on paper. The numbers are decimal numbers, in context situations and in formal mathematical language. This goal states no number range and no number of decimal places. The open data lists this goal under the cluster for multiplying and dividing with whole numbers (rw/bew/3); the content-line document prints it under multiplying and dividing with decimal numbers (rw/bew/4), and the pack follows the document. The code is kept as the data has it, so it begins with rw/bew/3.

### Common mistakes

Compensating with the wrong amount, working 6 × 1.99 as 12 − 0.01. In a division, making only one of the two numbers 10 times as large, so that 4.8 ÷ 0.6 becomes 48 ÷ 0.6. Halving both numbers of a multiplication instead of halving one and doubling the other.
