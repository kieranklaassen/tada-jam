## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes 7,500 apart in several ways: as 7 thousands and 5 hundreds, as 75 hundreds, as 3 × 2,500 and as half of 15,000. The child also says where 7,500 stands among other numbers: halfway between 7,000 and 8,000, at three quarters of the way from 0 to 10,000 and 2,500 below 10,000. A child of 10 does this with a number line; a child of 12 does it without the line.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The text does not define internal and external structures. One reading is that the internal structure is how a number is built (its digits and their place values, the parts and factors it can be split into) and that the external structure is where the number stands among other numbers (its neighbours, the round numbers near it, its place on a number line); the wording does not say so, and the example above follows that reading. The fase 2 goal of the same cluster, rw/gb/3/07/fase2, prints examples of internal and of external structures. This goal states no number range. The goal rw/gb/1/03/fase3 names its own range, "tot ten minste 1 miljard" (up to at least 1,000,000,000).

### Common mistakes

The child knows one way to split a number, by its digits, and does not see other splits such as 75 hundreds. The child cannot name the round numbers on either side of a number, or says which is nearer without checking. The child treats a number as a row of loose digits with no size.
