## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

discovering, applying and explaining the rules that hold for the order in which arithmetic operations must be carried out, and knowing the role of brackets in this (this concerns the precedence rules: 1. Adding and subtracting are carried out in the order in which they occur (from left to right). 2. Multiplying and dividing are carried out in the order in which they occur (from left to right). 3. Multiplying and dividing go before adding and subtracting

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child works out 3 + 4 × 5 and gets 23, then puts brackets in, (3 + 4) × 5, gets 35, and says why the two differ. The child works 20 − 8 + 5 from left to right and gets 17, and works 24 ÷ 4 × 2 from left to right and gets 12. A child of 10 finds the rules by working the same numbers in different orders and comparing the outcomes; a child of 12 applies them in longer calculations with brackets, such as 2 × (15 − 6) + 12 ÷ 3 = 22, and explains each step.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The bracket gives three precedence rules: adding and subtracting in the order in which they occur, from left to right; multiplying and dividing in the order in which they occur, from left to right; multiplying and dividing before adding and subtracting. The main clause adds knowing the role of brackets; the list holds no rule of its own for them. The list does not mention powers or roots. The content-line document prints an example with this goal, after the third rule: "3 − 2 + 1 = 2; 8 ÷ 2 × 4 = 16; 3 + 1 ÷ 2 × 4 = 5" (in Dutch notation 3 - 2 + 1 = 2; 8 : 2 x 4 = 16; 3 + 1 : 2 x 4 = 5); it is an example, not a limit. The goal's own wording states no number range and names no kind of number; the numbers calculated with in the example are whole numbers below 10, none of its three calculations has brackets, and in the third the in-between result 1 ÷ 2 is not a whole number. The source opens the bracket and does not close it.

### Common mistakes

Working strictly from left to right through 3 + 4 × 5 and answering 35. Following a memory aid that puts adding before subtracting, and so working 20 − 8 + 5 as 20 − 13 = 7. Ignoring brackets.
