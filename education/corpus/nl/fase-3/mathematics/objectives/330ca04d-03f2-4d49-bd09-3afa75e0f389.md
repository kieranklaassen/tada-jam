## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

(critically) reasoning about and reflecting on one's own spending in relation to a budget and thinking about spending (too much) money

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes down what it spent in a week, adds the amounts and compares the total with its budget: with a budget of 10 euros and spending of 3.50, 4.25 and 2.75 euros it finds a total of 10.50 euros, which is 0.50 euros more than the budget. The child says what it would leave out or put off, and looks back on a purchase: was it worth the money, and what could it not buy because of it. The child says what follows from spending too much, such as having nothing left for something it wants later. A child of 10 adds up what it spent and compares the total with its budget; a child of 12 also gives reasons for what it would leave out or put off.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names reasoning about and reflecting on one's own spending in relation to a budget, with "critically" in brackets, and thinking about spending money, with "too much" in brackets. The spending is the child's own (*eigen uitgaven*). The content-line document prints an example with this goal: the spending of *zakgeld* (pocket money) and *kleedgeld* (an allowance for buying one's own clothes); it is an example, not a limit. Neither the wording nor the example names an amount, a currency or a period; the amounts in the paragraph above are the pack's examples. This goal states no number range. Saving and *geld lenen* are named in `rw/m/7/04/fase3`.

### Common mistakes

The child looks at each purchase by itself ("it is only 2 euros") and does not add the purchases up against the budget. The child counts money it expects to get later as money it has now. The child forgets costs that come back, such as an amount that is paid every week.
