## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

using different strategies for calculating with percentages, such as applying the 1% rule and multiplying by corresponding fractions or decimal numbers

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child works out 35% of 240 in two ways: by the 1% rule, with 1% as 2.4 and 35 times 2.4 as 84, and by multiplying 240 by 0.35, which also gives 84. For 25% of 240 the child takes a quarter and finds 60 in one step. A child of 10 builds a percentage up step by step in a ratio table; a child of 12 chooses the strategy that fits the numbers and checks the result with another strategy.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal asks for the use of different strategies. After "zoals" it gives two examples: applying the 1% rule (finding 1% by dividing by 100 and then multiplying by the number of percent) and multiplying by the fraction or the decimal number that belongs to the percentage. This goal states no number range: it names no percentages and no amounts.

### Common mistakes

The child finds 1% and forgets to multiply by the number of percent. The child takes a wrong fraction or decimal number for a percentage, such as 1/5 or 0.5 for 5%. The child uses one fixed strategy for every calculation and works out 50% with the 1% rule in many steps.
