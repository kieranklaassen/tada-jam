## Official wording

optellen en aftrekken met decimale getallen met en zonder een gelijk aantal decimalen in contextsituaties en formele wiskundetaal volgens een of meer standaardprocedures, zoals rijgen, splitsen, kolomsgewijs rekenen en/of cijferen

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

adding and subtracting with decimal numbers with and without an equal number of decimal places, in context situations and formal mathematical language, according to one or more standard procedures, such as stringing (*rijgen*, the second number added or subtracted in steps), splitting (*splitsen*, working per place value), column-wise calculating (*kolomsgewijs rekenen*, whole place values, written in columns) and/or the written algorithm (*cijferen*, digit by digit in columns)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child adds 3.75 + 12.6 by writing the numbers one under the other with the decimal points in line and an extra zero, 12.60, and gets 16.35. The child subtracts 8.2 − 2.75 on an empty number line by going back 2, then 0.7, then 0.05, and arrives at 5.45. The child works out the change from a €20 note for a bill of €13.85 and gets €6.15. A child of 10 first writes extra zeros so that both numbers have the same number of decimal places and then adds or subtracts, as in 0.375 + 2.400 = 2.775; a child of 12 works the same sum with the decimal points in line and no extra zeros.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names decimal numbers with and without an equal number of decimal places, context situations and formal mathematical language, and "one or more" standard procedures. "zoals" (such as) introduces the procedures as examples: *rijgen*, *splitsen*, *kolomsgewijs rekenen* and/or *cijferen*; the goal does not ask for all four and does not prescribe one. This goal states no number range and does not say how many decimal places. The calculator is not named in this goal; it has its own goals, rw/bew/7/01/fase3 to rw/bew/7/08/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Lining up the last digits instead of the decimal points, so that in 3.75 + 12.6 the 6 stands under the 5. Treating the digits after the point as a whole number, so that 0.5 + 0.75 becomes 0.80. In 8.2 − 2.75, copying the 5 down as the last digit instead of subtracting it from an added zero.
