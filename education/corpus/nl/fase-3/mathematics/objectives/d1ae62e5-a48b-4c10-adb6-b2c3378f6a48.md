## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child has worked out 12 × 2.5 and tells another child how: 4 × 2.5 is 10, and 12 is three fours, so 30. The child has worked out 7.5 ÷ 0.25 and explains that there are 4 quarters in 1, so there are 7.5 × 4 = 30 quarters in 7.5. A child of 10 explains with coins or a measuring jug at hand; a child of 12 explains with the numbers alone and says why each step is allowed.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names three things to explain: strategies used, calculations made and reasoning followed. The wording itself names no operation and no kind of number; the cluster heading in the code scope is multiplying and dividing with decimal numbers (rw/bew/4). The open data lists this goal under the cluster for multiplying and dividing with whole numbers (rw/bew/3); the content-line document prints it under multiplying and dividing with decimal numbers (rw/bew/4), and the pack follows the document. The code is kept as the data has it, so it begins with rw/bew/3. It does not say to whom or in what form the explaining is done. This goal states no number range. The same wording stands at rw/bew/1/09/fase3, rw/bew/2/06/fase3 and rw/bew/3/11/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Explaining the place of the decimal point with "you count the digits" and nothing more. Leaving out what the numbers stand for when the calculation came from a situation. Giving only the outcome.
