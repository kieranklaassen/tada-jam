## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

dealing with the counting sequence (*telrij*) of the whole numbers, including counting on and counting back and counting in jumps

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child counts on aloud from 99,998 and passes 99,999, 100,000 and 100,001, and counts back from 1,000,002 past 1,000,000 to 999,999. The child counts in jumps of 25,000 from 950,000 and says 975,000, 1,000,000 and 1,025,000, and marks the same jumps on a number line. A child of 10 counts with a number line to point along; a child of 12 counts without the line.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names three things as included ("waaronder"): counting on, counting back and counting in jumps. This goal states no number range and names no size of jump. The goal rw/gb/1/03/fase3 names its own range, "tot ten minste 1 miljard" (up to at least 1,000,000,000), for investigating the structure of the counting sequence. The goal speaks of whole numbers and does not say whether negative numbers are meant; exploring negative numbers is in rw/gb/3/07/fase3.

### Common mistakes

When counting back across a round number the child changes the wrong digits and says 1,000,000 and then 999,000 instead of 999,999. In jumps the child loses the pattern where a jump crosses a thousand or a million. After 99,999 the child says one million and skips a hundred thousand.
