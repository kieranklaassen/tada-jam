## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child explains why 10 × 3,400 is 34,000: every digit moves one place to the left and becomes worth ten times as much. The child reads 45,000 as 45 thousands and as 450 hundreds, and works out that 1,000 bundles of ten 100-euro notes make a million euro. A child of 10 trades ten of one unit for one of the next with place-value material or play money; a child of 12 reasons without material and explains why 1,000,000 is 10 hundred thousands and also 1,000 thousands.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about reasoning, with the base-ten system as its basis; in that system each place is worth ten times the place to its right. This goal states no number range. The goal rw/gb/1/03/fase3 names its own range, "tot ten minste 1 miljard" (up to at least 1,000,000,000). The goal speaks of whole numbers; the structure of decimal numbers is in rw/gb/4/03/fase3.

### Common mistakes

The child says that multiplying by 10 means writing a zero behind the number and cannot say why. The child reads the digit and not its value, and says that the 4 in 45,000 is worth four. The child thinks that 45,000 holds only 5 thousands, because 5 is the digit in the thousands place.
