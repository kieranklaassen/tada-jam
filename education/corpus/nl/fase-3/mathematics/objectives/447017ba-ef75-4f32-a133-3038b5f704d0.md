## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child builds five towers of 4, 7, 5, 8 and 6 cubes, moves cubes from the tall towers to the short ones until all are equal, and finds five towers of 6. The child then adds 4 + 7 + 5 + 8 + 6 = 30, divides by 5 and gets the same 6. The child says in their own words that the average is what each tower would have if the cubes were shared out evenly. A child of 10 levels out the towers by hand; a child of 12 calculates the average of a list of numbers and explains that the average need not be one of the numbers in the list.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal has two parts: understanding and explaining what an average means, and calculating one. This goal states no number range, does not say how many numbers are averaged and does not say whether the outcome is a whole number.

### Common mistakes

Adding the numbers and forgetting to divide, or dividing by the wrong count. Thinking that the average must be one of the numbers in the list, or the one in the middle. Leaving a value of zero out of the count.
