## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

putting to use strip tables and ratio tables (*stroken- en verhoudingstabellen*) and scale lines (*schaallijnen*, scale bars, as on a map) as a means of solving ratio problems (*verhoudingsproblemen*; *verhouding* covers both ratio and proportion)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child solves "3 bottles cost 4.50 euro, what do 7 bottles cost?" in a ratio table with a row for bottles and a row for euros: from 3 to 1 by dividing by 3, which gives 1.50 euro, and from 1 to 7 by multiplying by 7, which gives 10.50 euro. The child draws a strip for a route of 60 kilometres, divides it into 4 equal parts and reads that 3 parts are 45 kilometres, and lays a ruler along the scale line of a map to read a distance. A child of 10 fills in a table or a strip that is given; a child of 12 draws the table, the strip or the scale line without being asked and chooses the steps.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names tools as a means of solving ratio problems: "stroken- en verhoudingstabellen" and scale lines. As printed, the hyphen makes the first words read as strip tables and ratio tables; they may be meant as strips (a bar divided into parts) and ratio tables, and the wording does not settle it. This goal states no number range and does not say what the ratio problems are about. Calculating with scale and scale lines is in rw/verh/2/08/fase3.

### Common mistakes

The child does an operation on one row of the table and a different one, or none, on the other row. The child adds the same number in both rows where both have to be multiplied. The child writes numbers in the wrong row, euros among the bottles.
