## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child plans an outing for 28 children with a bus that costs €140 and tickets of €6 each, and works out the cost per child in steps: 140 ÷ 28 = 5 and 5 + 6 = 11 euros. The child says which operation belongs to which step and why, and checks by another route: 28 × 6 + 140 = 308 and 308 ÷ 28 = 11. A child of 10 works step by step and writes each step as a separate calculation; a child of 12 compares two routes to the answer and judges whether the outcome fits the situation.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names critical thinking and reasoning, combinations of operations and problem situations, and nothing more. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: "4 + 5 × 6, is that 54 or 34, or can both be right?" (in Dutch notation 4 + 5 x 6); it is an example, not a limit. The goal's own wording names no operations, does not say what the problem situations are and states no number range; the example combines adding and multiplying, with the numbers 4, 5 and 6. The rules for the order of operations are in rw/bew/5/02/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Carrying out the operations in the order in which the numbers appear in the story. Stopping after the first step and giving an in-between result as the answer. Not checking whether the outcome can be right in the situation.
