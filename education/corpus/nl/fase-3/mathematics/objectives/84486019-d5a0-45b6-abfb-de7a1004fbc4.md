## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child has worked out 18 × 25 and tells another child how: halved 18 and doubled 25, so 9 × 50, which is 450. The child cuts a rectangle of 18 by 25 squares in two and lays the halves side by side, to show why halving one number and doubling the other keeps the amount the same. A child of 10 says what was done, step by step; a child of 12 also shows why the steps are allowed, with a drawing or with the properties of the operation.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names three things to explain: strategies used, calculations made and reasoning followed. The wording itself names no operation and no kind of number; the cluster heading in the code scope is multiplying and dividing with whole numbers (rw/bew/3). It does not say to whom or in what form the explaining is done. This goal states no number range. The same wording stands at rw/bew/1/09/fase3, rw/bew/2/06/fase3 and rw/bew/3/21/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Naming the strategy ("I did it the handy way") without showing the steps. Reciting the steps of the written algorithm without saying what the digits stand for. Giving the outcome again when asked for the way.
