## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

calculating with the fraction as operator (*breuk als operator*, a fraction applied to an amount), such as calculating a part of a quantity and calculating a quantity when the part is known

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child takes 3/4 of 24 marbles by making 4 equal groups of 6 and taking 3 of the groups, 18 marbles. The other way round, the child hears that 2/5 of a class is 10 children, works out that 1/5 is 5 children and that the whole class is 25 children. A child of 10 lays out the groups or draws a strip divided into equal parts; a child of 12 works with the numbers alone, such as 3/8 of 240 = 90.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. "zoals" (such as) introduces two examples: calculating a part of a quantity, and calculating the quantity when the part is known. The content-line document prints an example with each of the two: with the first, "3/4 part of 600 people is … people" (3/4 deel van 600 mensen is … mensen), and with the second, "400 is 4/5 part, how much is the total quantity then?" (400 is 4/5 deel, hoeveel is dan de totale hoeveelheid?); they are examples, not limits. The goal's own wording states no number range, names no denominators and does not say what kind of quantities are used; the examples use 3/4 of 600 people, and 400 as 4/5 of a total.

### Common mistakes

Dividing by the denominator and forgetting to multiply by the numerator, so that 3/4 of 24 becomes 6. When the part is known, taking the fraction of the part once more (2/5 of 10) instead of working back to the whole. Swapping the two steps: dividing by the numerator and multiplying by the denominator.
