## Official wording

handig en efficiënt vermenigvuldigen en delen met hele getallen in contextsituaties en formele wiskundetaal waarbij een doelmatige oplossingsmanier wordt gekozen op basis van inzicht in de eigenschappen van bewerkingen en in de structuur van getallen (bijvoorbeeld verwisselen bij vermenigvuldigen, compenseren, analogie, omvormen, verdubbelen en halveren bij vermenigvuldigen, en de inverse relatie tussen vermenigvuldigen en delen)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

multiplying and dividing with whole numbers handily (*handig*, with a shortcut that fits the numbers) and efficiently, in context situations and formal mathematical language, in which an effective way of solving is chosen on the basis of insight into the properties of operations and into the structure of numbers (for example swapping in multiplying, compensating, analogy, transforming (*omvormen*, changing it into an easier equal sum), doubling and halving in multiplying, and the inverse relation between multiplying and dividing)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child works 4 × 199 as 4 × 200 − 4 = 796, and works 28 × 5 as 5 × 28 = 140. The child works 16 × 25 by halving and doubling, first 8 × 50 and then 4 × 100 = 400, and works 240 ÷ 15 as 480 ÷ 30 = 16. The child finds 252 ÷ 12 by asking how many times 12 makes 252: 20 × 12 = 240 and 1 × 12 more, so 21. A child of 10 uses a shortcut when it sees that a number is close to a round number; a child of 12 looks at the numbers first, chooses a way and says why the chosen one fits.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about choosing an effective way of solving on insight into the properties of operations and into the structure of numbers. "bijvoorbeeld" (for example) introduces the bracketed list as examples: swapping in multiplying, compensating, analogy, *omvormen*, doubling and halving in multiplying, and the inverse relation between multiplying and dividing. The list ties swapping, and doubling and halving, to multiplying. The numbers are whole numbers, in context situations and in formal mathematical language. This goal states no number range and does not say whether the working is done mentally or with notes on paper; rw/bew/3/17/fase3 allows notes on paper for its own content.

### Common mistakes

Compensating with the wrong amount, working 4 × 199 as 800 − 1. In a division, doubling only one of the two numbers, so that 240 ÷ 15 becomes 240 ÷ 30. Swapping the two numbers of a division as one may in a multiplication, so that 240 ÷ 15 is read as 15 ÷ 240.
