## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

thinking critically and reasoning about combinations of quantities (*grootheden*, things that can be measured) in problem situations

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child gets a question in which two quantities work together, for example distance and time or price and weight, and first says which quantities there are and what "per" means in the situation. The child estimates whether an answer can be right and explains its reasoning, for example that twice the speed gives half the time for the same distance, so that a trip of 3 hours at 40 kilometres per hour takes 1.5 hours at 80 kilometres per hour. The child checks a claim, such as "the large pack is always cheaper", by working out the price per unit. A child of 10 reasons with doubling and halving in a situation it knows; a child of 12 chooses which quantities to combine in a new situation and judges whether an answer can be right.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about thinking critically and reasoning; it speaks of combinations of quantities in general and names none. The other goals of its cluster (rw/m/8) name speed and average speed, price or number per unit, and population density. The goal names no situations, no units and no formula; the example in the paragraph above is the pack's. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks that when one quantity doubles the other always doubles too, also where it halves, as with speed and time over the same distance. The child calculates with the numbers in the question without asking what each one measures, and adds or multiplies quantities that do not belong together. The child accepts an answer that cannot be right, such as a cyclist at 150 kilometres per hour, without checking it against what it knows.
