## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Before pressing the equals key for 48 × 52, the child says "about 50 × 50 = 2,500"; the calculator gives 2,496 and the child accepts it. Another time the calculator gives 249.6, the child says that this cannot be right and finds that 4.8 was typed instead of 48. A child of 10 estimates with rounded numbers and says whether the outcome can be right; a child of 12 also finds what went wrong when it cannot, such as a misplaced decimal point.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names two actions on outcomes of the calculator: checking them by estimation, and judging them critically. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: "Can this answer be right?" (Kan dit antwoord kloppen?); it is an example, not a limit. The goal does not say how close an estimate must be. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

Accepting whatever the calculator gives. Estimating only after seeing the outcome, so that the estimate follows the outcome. Comparing only the digits and not the place of the decimal point, and so missing an outcome that is 10 times too large or too small.
