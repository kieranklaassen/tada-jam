## Official wording

onderhouden van het memoriseren (uit het hoofd kennen) van alle vermenigvuldigingen uit de tafels tot en met 10

Source: SLO (Stichting Leerplanontwikkeling), SLO open data: curriculum-inhoudslijnen (inhoudslijnen po) (commit d2ec46f), inh_cluster rw/bew/3; SLO open data: curriculum-basis (doelen, doelniveaus, niveaus) (commit 5adf7a4), doelniveau ff2813e8-6df4-464d-89ec-0f81183298c0.

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

maintaining the memorising (knowing by heart) of all multiplications from the tables (*tafels*, multiplication tables) up to and including 10

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child answers 7 × 8 = 56, 6 × 9 = 54 and 4 × 7 = 28 at once, in mixed order, without building the answer up from another fact. The child picks out the facts that still take time, such as 7 × 8 = 56 and 6 × 7 = 42, and practises just those. A child of 10 knows these by heart; a child of 12 does the same, so the two differ little here.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about maintaining ("onderhouden") what was memorised earlier. The stated range is all multiplications from the tables up to and including 10 (*tot en met 10*, so the table of 10 is included); the wording does not say how far each table runs. The stated way of knowing is memorising, which the brackets explain as knowing by heart. Divisions are in rw/bew/3/03/fase3. Tables above 10 are not named.

### Common mistakes

Mixing up neighbouring facts, such as 7 × 8 = 54 and 6 × 9 = 56. Reciting a table from its start to reach one fact. Knowing 3 × 8 and hesitating at 8 × 3.
