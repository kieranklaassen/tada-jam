## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sorts a set of sum cards into two piles before working any of them out: "quicker myself" (25 × 4, 1,000 − 1, half of 86) and "calculator" (37.85 × 264, 9,417 ÷ 73). The child then tries both ways for a few cards and moves the cards that were judged wrongly. The child says what the sums in each pile have in common. A child of 10 judges by how large the numbers look; a child of 12 looks at the kind of numbers and at how exact the answer must be.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about learning to judge between using a calculator and calculating oneself ("zelf rekenen"), which it does not split into mental and written calculating. It gives no criteria for the judgement. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

Reaching for the calculator for known facts such as 25 × 4. Refusing the calculator for a long calculation where typing gives fewer errors. Judging by the size of the numbers alone, so that 1,000,000 − 1 goes on the calculator pile.
