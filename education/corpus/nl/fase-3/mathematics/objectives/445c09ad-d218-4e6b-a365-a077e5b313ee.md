## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes 35% as 35/100, simplifies it to 7/20 and writes it as 0.35. The child writes 8% as 8/100, 2/25 and 0.08, and writes 125% as 125/100, 1 1/4 and 1.25. A child of 10 shades a percentage on a hundred grid and reads the fraction and the decimal number from it; a child of 12 converts by writing the percentage as hundredths.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names one direction: from percentages to fractions and to decimal numbers. The reverse direction is not named in this goal; converting between fractions and decimal numbers in both directions is in rw/gb/5/07/fase3. This goal states no number range: it does not say which percentages are meant, whether percentages above 100% or percentages with decimals are among them, or whether the fraction is simplified.

### Common mistakes

The child writes a percentage below 10% with its digit in the tenths place, 8% as 0.8. The child writes 35% as 1/35. The child writes 125% as 0.125.
