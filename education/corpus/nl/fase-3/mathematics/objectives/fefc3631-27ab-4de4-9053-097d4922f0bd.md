## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child hears the claim that 0.35 is more than 0.4 because 35 is more than 4, and shows with hundredths, 35 against 40, that the claim is wrong. The child is asked whether a number lies between 0.7 and 0.8, names 0.75 and then several more, and explains why another one can always be found. A child of 10 argues with money, a measuring tape or a number line in hand; a child of 12 argues from place value and checks whether an outcome with decimals fits the situation, such as an outcome of 2.5 buses.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about critical thinking and reasoning with decimal numbers in problem situations; its own wording does not say what the problem situations are. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: "What problems would there be if we did not use decimal numbers?"; it is an example, not a limit. This goal states no number range and no number of decimal places; rw/gb/4/01/fase3 names four place values (wholes, tenths, hundredths and thousandths).

### Common mistakes

The child judges a decimal number by the count of its digits and calls the longer number the larger. The child thinks that there are no numbers between two neighbours such as 0.7 and 0.8. The child accepts an outcome with decimals that cannot exist in the situation, such as 2.5 buses.
