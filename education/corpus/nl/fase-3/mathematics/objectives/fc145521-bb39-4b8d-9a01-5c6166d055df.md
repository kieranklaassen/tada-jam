## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

thinking critically and reasoning about relations between fractions (as numbers) and decimal numbers in problem situations

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two recipes ask for 3/8 litre and 0.4 litre of milk, and the child works out that 3/8 is 0.375 and so the smaller amount. The child tests the claim that 0.3 is the same as 1/3 by writing 0.3 as 3/10 and comparing 3/10 with 1/3 as 9/30 and 10/30. A child of 10 reasons with a number line on which both forms are marked; a child of 12 reasons from the numbers and says which form, fraction or decimal number, makes the comparison easier.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about critical thinking and reasoning, in problem situations, on how fractions and decimal numbers relate; the brackets say that the fractions are taken as numbers. The text does not say what the problem situations are. This goal states no number range: it names no denominators and no number of decimal places. Converting between the two forms is in rw/gb/5/07/fase3.

### Common mistakes

The child treats 0.3 and 1/3 as the same number, or 0.25 and 1/25. The child thinks that a fraction and a decimal number are two different kinds of thing that cannot be equal or be compared. The child gives an answer and cannot say how it was checked.
