## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child has worked out 4,005 − 1,997 and tells another child how: from 1,997 to 2,000 is 3, from 2,000 to 4,005 is 2,005, together 2,008. The child draws the two jumps on an empty number line so that the other child can follow. A child of 10 retells what was done in the order it was done; a child of 12 also says why counting on was chosen here instead of subtracting in columns.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names three things to explain: strategies used, calculations made and reasoning followed. The wording itself names no operation and no kind of number; the cluster heading in the code scope is adding and subtracting with whole numbers (rw/bew/1). It does not say to whom or in what form the explaining is done. This goal states no number range. The same wording stands at rw/bew/2/06/fase3, rw/bew/3/11/fase3 and rw/bew/3/21/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Saying only the outcome when asked for the way. Leaving out a step that was done mentally, so that the listener cannot follow. Explaining what was done without the reason for it.
