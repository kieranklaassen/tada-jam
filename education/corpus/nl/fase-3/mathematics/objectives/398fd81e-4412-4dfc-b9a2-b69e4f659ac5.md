## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child works 25 × 36 in columns and as 100 × 9 = 900, and explains why the second way gives the same outcome: 36 is 4 × 9, and 25 × 4 is 100. The child looks at a worked multiplication in which 34 × 27 came to 306, estimates 30 × 30 = 900, says that 306 cannot be right and finds that the second partial product was not shifted. A child of 10 checks an outcome with an estimate and compares two ways; a child of 12 also explains why a procedure or a shortcut gives the right outcome.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names critical thinking and reasoning about standard procedures and handy strategies for multiplying and dividing with whole numbers. It names no procedure and no strategy; rw/bew/3/04/fase3 and rw/bew/3/05/fase3 name example procedures for their own content. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

Carrying a rule over from adding to multiplying, such as making one number 1 larger and the other 1 smaller, so that 19 × 21 is worked as 20 × 20. Not checking the size of an outcome with an estimate. Carrying out the steps of long division without being able to say what the in-between numbers stand for.
