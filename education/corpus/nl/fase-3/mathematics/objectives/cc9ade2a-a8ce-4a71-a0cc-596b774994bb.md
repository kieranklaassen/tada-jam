## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

thinking critically and reasoning about numerical information and calculations with percentages in daily life

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads "now 50% more" on a pack of 750 grams and works out that the pack held 500 grams before. The child compares two offers, 25% off 80 euro and 20 euro off 90 euro, finds 60 euro and 70 euro and says which costs less. A child of 10 tests a claim by working it out with an amount of its own choosing; a child of 12 asks what the percentage is taken of and says whether the claim holds.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about critical thinking and reasoning on two things met in daily life: numerical information and calculations with percentages. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: "If the price of a product is lowered by 10% and after that the new price is raised again by 10%, is the final price then lower than, higher than or the same as at first?"; it is an example, not a limit. The goal's own wording names no situations and no sources, and states no number range: it names no percentages and no amounts; the example uses 10% twice and gives no price.

### Common mistakes

The child takes a percentage in an advertisement or a report as true without asking of what it is a percentage. The child judges an offer by the size of the percentage alone and not by the amount to be paid. The child takes 50% of the new amount as the extra, 375 grams of the 750, where the extra is 250 grams.
