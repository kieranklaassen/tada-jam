benoemen en noteren van verhoudingen als 'zoveel op de zoveel' of deel van een totaal, als breuk en als percentage

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

naming and writing down ratios (*verhoudingen*, a word that covers both ratio and proportion) as 'so many in so many' (*zoveel op de zoveel*) or part of a total, as a fraction and as a percentage

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child counts 3 red beads among 12 beads and says "3 in 12" and then "1 in 4", says "a quarter of the beads", and writes 1/4 and 25%. The child reads "1 in 5 children wears glasses" and writes it as 1/5 and as 20%. A child of 10 counts the part and the total and names the ratio from them; a child of 12 rewrites a ratio from a text in another of these ways.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names two actions, naming and writing down, and three ways to give a ratio: as "zoveel op de zoveel" (so many in so many) or part of a total, as a fraction, and as a percentage. The first way is about a part and its total; the goal names no way that sets one part against another. This goal states no number range and names no ratios.

### Common mistakes

The child sets the part against the rest and says "3 in 9" for 3 red beads among 12. The child writes the two numbers of "1 in 4" as 1.4 or as 14%. The child thinks that 1/4 and 25% are different amounts.
