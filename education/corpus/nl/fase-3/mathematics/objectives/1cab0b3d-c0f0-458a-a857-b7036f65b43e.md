## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads that a country has 17 million inhabitants and that one of its towns has 170,000, and works out that 100 such towns would hold the whole population. The child is asked whether a million seconds is longer than a week, works out that a day has 86,400 seconds and finds that a million seconds is more than 11 days. A child of 10 tests a claim by rounding and comparing; a child of 12 also says which information is missing.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about critical thinking and reasoning with whole numbers; it does not say what the problem situations are. This goal states no number range. The goal rw/gb/1/03/fase3 names its own range, "tot ten minste 1 miljard" (up to at least 1,000,000,000), for the structure of the counting sequence.

### Common mistakes

The child accepts a large number in a text without asking whether it can be right. The child loses or adds a group of three zeros in a calculation with large numbers and does not notice that the answer is a thousand times too small or too large. The child gives an answer and no reasoning.
