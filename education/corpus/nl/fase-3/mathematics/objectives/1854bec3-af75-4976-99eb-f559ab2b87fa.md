## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

connecting with each other and comparing different wordings for ratios (*verhoudingen*, a word that covers both ratio and proportion)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child sorts cards with "1 in 5 children", "a fifth of the children", "20 percent of the children" and "2 out of every 10 children" and lays them together as the same ratio. The child sets "1 red bead to 4 blue beads" beside "1 in 5 beads is red", strings the beads and explains why the two say the same in different words. A child of 10 matches cards that say the same ratio in different wordings; a child of 12 rewrites a sentence from a news report in another wording and says what the new wording stresses.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names two actions on wordings for ratios: connecting them with each other and comparing them. It does not list the wordings; the ones in the examples above are the pack's. This goal states no number range. Naming and writing down ratios as part of a total, as a fraction and as a percentage is in rw/verh/1/01/fase3.

### Common mistakes

The child takes "1 to 4" and "1 in 4" for the same ratio; 1 red to 4 blue is 1 in 5. The child matches wordings by the numbers in them and not by their meaning, and lays "1 in 5" with "5 percent". The child thinks that different words must mean different amounts.
