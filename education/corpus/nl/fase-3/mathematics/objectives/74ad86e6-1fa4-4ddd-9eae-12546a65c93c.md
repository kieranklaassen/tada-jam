## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

maintaining the automating (calculating fluently) of adding and subtracting to at least 100 and of tens and hundreds by analogy with calculating under 100

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child works out 47 + 36 = 83 and 92 − 58 = 34 quickly and without writing. The child uses 47 + 36 = 83 to say that 470 + 360 = 830 and that 4,700 + 3,600 = 8,300. A child of 10 works these sums out fluently; a child of 12 does the same, so the two differ little here.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about maintaining ("onderhouden") what was automated earlier. The stated ranges are: adding and subtracting "tot ten minste 100" (to at least 100: the offer reaches 100 at the least, no upper limit is set, and the wording does not make clear whether 100 bounds the numbers or the outcome); and adding and subtracting tens and hundreds by analogy with calculating under 100, for which no upper limit is stated. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: "12 = 7 + 5; 1200 = 700 + 500; 67 – 30; 9000 + 30, 1200 – 800"; it is an example, not a limit. The example sets 12 = 7 + 5 beside 1200 = 700 + 500, and its largest number is 9000. The stated way of calculating is automating, which the brackets explain as calculating fluently; rw/bew/1/03/fase3 is about memorising. The goal names no written procedure and sets no measure for "fluently".

### Common mistakes

Losing a ten when a ten is crossed, so that 47 + 36 becomes 73. Misplacing zeros in the analogy, so that 470 + 360 becomes 8,300. Falling back on counting in ones for sums such as 92 − 58.
