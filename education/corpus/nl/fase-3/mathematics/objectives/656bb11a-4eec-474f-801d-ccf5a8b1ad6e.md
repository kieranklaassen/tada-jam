## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child lays down a strip that stands for 100% and a second strip one and a half times as long, and says that the second is 150% of the first. The child works out 150% of 80 as 80 plus half of 80, which is 120, and 200% of 80 as twice 80, which is 160. A child of 10 shows a percentage above 100% with strips or blocks; a child of 12 calculates with it and says whether a percentage above 100% can occur in the situation.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names percentages of more than 100% and has two parts: knowing and understanding what they mean, and calculating with them. It sets no upper limit to the percentages and states no range for the amounts. The text does not say in which situations these percentages occur; increase, decrease, profit and loss with percentages above 100% are in rw/verh/3/05/fase3.

### Common mistakes

The child thinks that more than 100% cannot exist, because 100% is everything. The child mixes "150% of" with "150% more than": 150% of 80 is 120, and 150% more than 80 is 200. The child takes 200% for 200 things.
