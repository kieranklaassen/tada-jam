## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child has worked out 6.4 − 2.85 and tells another child each step: wrote 6.40, took away 2, then 0.8, then 0.05, and got 3.55. Asked how they know it is right, the child adds 3.55 + 2.85 and gets 6.4 again. A child of 10 retells the steps in order; a child of 12 also says why each step is allowed and why this way was chosen.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names three things to explain: strategies used, calculations made and reasoning followed. The wording itself names no operation and no kind of number; the cluster heading in the code scope is adding and subtracting with decimal numbers (rw/bew/2). It does not say to whom or in what form the explaining is done. This goal states no number range. The same wording stands at rw/bew/1/09/fase3, rw/bew/3/11/fase3 and rw/bew/3/21/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Repeating the answer when asked how it was found. Listing the steps without saying why a step was taken. Describing a tidier method than the one actually used.
