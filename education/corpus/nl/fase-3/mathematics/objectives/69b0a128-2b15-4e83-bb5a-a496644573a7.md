## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child gets a question about time that has more than one step, or one in which the quick answer is wrong, and first says what is known and what is asked. For example, three lessons of 45 minutes with two breaks of 10 minutes between them start at 09:15, and the child reasons that the lessons take 135 minutes and the breaks 20 minutes, 155 minutes or 2 hours and 35 minutes in all, so that the last lesson ends at 11:50. The child checks whether an answer can be right and explains why a quick answer is wrong, such as counting three breaks for three lessons. A child of 10 solves such a question and tells how; a child of 12 also judges an answer given by another person and says where it went wrong.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about thinking critically and reasoning; its own wording speaks of time in general and of problem situations in general, and names no situation, no unit of time and no instrument. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: with time zones and time differences, and summer time and winter time (*zomertijd*, *wintertijd*, the clock time of the summer half and of the winter half of the year); it is an example, not a limit. The lessons and breaks in the paragraph above are the pack's example and are not about time zones or summer time. Other goals of its cluster (rw/m/6) name the content, such as converting units (`rw/m/6/05/fase3`) and time zones (`rw/m/6/10/fase3`). This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child miscounts by one: it counts three breaks between three lessons, or eight days from a Monday to the next Monday. The child calculates with the numbers given without asking whether a time is a moment or a duration. The child does not check the answer against what is possible, and accepts a school day that ends at 25:10.
