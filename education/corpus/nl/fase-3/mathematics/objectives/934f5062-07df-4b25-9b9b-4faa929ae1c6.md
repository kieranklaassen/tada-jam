## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

comparing and ordering whole numbers on the basis of the place value of the digits in numbers

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child writes 408,731 and 480,731 under each other, finds the first place from the left where the digits differ, the ten thousands with 0 against 8, and says that 480,731 is the larger. The child orders 99,875, 100,020 and 98,999 from small to large and explains that 100,020 is the largest because it alone has a digit in the hundred thousands place. A child of 10 compares numbers with a place-value chart; a child of 12 compares them without a chart and gives the reason in terms of place value.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal ties comparing and ordering to one basis: the place value of the digits. This goal states no number range and does not say how many numbers are ordered at one time. The goal rw/gb/1/03/fase3 names its own range, "tot ten minste 1 miljard" (up to at least 1,000,000,000). The goal does not mention the signs for greater than and less than.

### Common mistakes

The child compares the first digits without looking at how many digits each number has, and calls 99,875 larger than 100,020 because 9 is larger than 1. The child compares the digits from the right. The child takes two numbers that differ only in the place of a zero, such as 408,731 and 480,731, for the same number.
