## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

calculating discounts, including working out the new price, the original price and discount percentages

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads a tag with 20% off a coat of 60 euro and works out a discount of 12 euro and a new price of 48 euro. For trousers that cost 45 euro after 25% off, the child reasons that 45 euro is 75% of the old price, finds 1% as 0.60 euro and the original price as 60 euro. A child of 10 works a discount out row by row in a ratio table; a child of 12 first says what is asked, the new price, the original price or the percentage, and chooses the calculation from that.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names three things to work out as included ("waaronder") in calculating discounts: the new price, the original price and discount percentages. This goal states no number range: it names no prices and no percentages. The source prints "de oorspronkelijk prijs", a slip for "de oorspronkelijke prijs"; the gloss reads it as the original price. Strategies for calculating with percentages are in rw/verh/3/06/fase3.

### Common mistakes

The child gives the discount as the new price and says that the coat costs 12 euro. To find the original price the child adds the percentage of the reduced price: 25% of 45 euro is 11.25 euro, which gives 56.25 euro and not 60 euro. To find the discount percentage the child divides by the new price and not by the old one.
