## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

Two children each ate half a pizza and disagree about who ate more; the child points out that half of a large pizza is more than half of a small one. The child hears the claim that 1/3 of the class went by bike and 3/4 of the class went on foot, and says that it cannot be right, because together that is more than the whole class. A child of 10 reasons with drawings and pieces; a child of 12 reasons with the numbers and gives a counter-example.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names critical thinking and reasoning, fractions and problem situations, and nothing more. The content-line document prints an example with this goal: "Why can you not, in adding fractions, add the numerators together and add the denominators together? So why is 1/4 + 1/4 not 2/8?"; it is an example, not a limit. The goal's own wording does not say what the problem situations are, states no number range and names no operations; the example is a question about adding fractions, with 1/4 + 1/4. The operations with fractions of this band are listed in rw/bew/6/03/fase3.

### Common mistakes

Comparing two fractions without asking what whole each is a fraction of. Accepting an outcome that cannot be right, such as parts that add up to more than the whole. Giving an answer without a reason.
