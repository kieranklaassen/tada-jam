## Official wording

uitrekenen van bewerkingen met hele getallen en decimale getallen met behulp van de rekenmachine (optellen, aftrekken, vermenigvuldigen, delen)

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

working out operations with whole numbers and decimal numbers with the help of the calculator (adding, subtracting, multiplying, dividing)

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child types 348 × 27 and reads 9,396, types 12.75 + 8.6 and reads 21.35, and types 100 ÷ 8 and reads 12.5. The child looks at each number after typing it, to check the entry before pressing the equals key. A child of 10 types an operation and copies the outcome; a child of 12 does the same, so the two differ little here.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The brackets name four operations: adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing. The numbers are whole numbers and decimal numbers; fractions and percentages are not named in this goal. The stated way of calculating is with the calculator. This goal states no number range.

### Common mistakes

Not noticing a typing error, such as a digit left out or two digits swapped. Entering a division the wrong way round, 8 ÷ 100 for 100 ÷ 8. Misplacing the decimal point when copying a long outcome.
