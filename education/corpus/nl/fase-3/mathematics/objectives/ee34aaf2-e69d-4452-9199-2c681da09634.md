## Official wording

onderhouden van het memoriseren (uit het hoofd kennen) van de splitsingen van getallen tot en met 10 en van de optellingen en aftrekkingen onder de tien en over het eerste tiental

## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

maintaining the memorising (knowing by heart) of the splits (*splitsingen*, ways to split a number in two) of numbers up to and including 10 and of the additions and subtractions under ten and across the first ten

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child says at once that 10 splits into 6 and 4 and that 9 splits into 2 and 7, and answers 8 + 5 = 13 and 14 − 6 = 8 without counting. The child turns over a number card and names the number that completes it to 10. A child of 10 already answers these at once; a child of 12 does the same, so the two differ little here.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal is about maintaining ("onderhouden") what was memorised earlier. The stated ranges are: the splits of numbers up to and including 10 (*tot en met 10*, so 10 is included); additions and subtractions under ten; and additions and subtractions across the first ten (*over het eerste tiental*), which the wording does not bound further. The stated way of knowing is memorising, which the brackets explain as knowing by heart. The Dutch "onder de tien" leaves open whether an outcome of exactly 10 is included.

### Common mistakes

Still counting on by ones, on fingers, for 8 + 5. Answering the additions at once but hesitating at the matching subtractions, such as 14 − 6. Mixing up neighbouring facts, for example answering 7 + 6 = 12.
