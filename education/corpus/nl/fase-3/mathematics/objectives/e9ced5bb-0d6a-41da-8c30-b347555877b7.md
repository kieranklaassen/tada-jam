## English gloss

_The pack's gloss, not an official translation._

carrying out operations with data from tables and graphic representations

## Design notes

_The pack's inference, not the official text._

### In a child's hands

The child reads numbers from a table or a chart and calculates with them to answer a question that cannot be read off directly. For example, from a table with 120, 95, 140, 85 and 160 visitors on five days the child finds the total of 600, the difference of 75 between the busiest and the quietest day, and the average of 120 per day. The child says which numbers it took from the display and which operation it chose. A child of 10 works out an answer from the values it reads off; a child of 12 does the same, so the two differ little here.

### Limits

This statement describes what a school offers in fase 3 (*groep* 7 and 8, about ages 10 to 12), not what a child must be able to do, and nothing says in which year. The goal names one activity: carrying out operations with data from two kinds of source, tables and graphic representations. It does not say which operations, and it names no formula; the calculations in the paragraph above are the pack's examples. It names no kind of graphic representation and no number range.

### Common mistakes

The child reads a value wrongly from the scale of a chart, for example counts each line as 1 where it stands for 10, and then calculates correctly with the wrong numbers. The child takes numbers from the wrong row or column of a table. The child adds numbers that cannot be added, such as percentages of different groups or temperatures on different days.
