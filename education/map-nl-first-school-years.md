# The Netherlands (`nl`): the first school years, ages 5 to 8

The pack's own text, not official text; read the records it names.

## Ages and levels

Every mapping here is `convention` (`education/ages.ts`): no law ties an age to a *groep* (a school year). The bands are official: SLO, the curriculum institute, defines *fase* 1 as groep 1 to 3 and fase 2 as groep 4 to 6.

- **Age 5**: `fase-1`, sub-band groep 1 or groep 2; the sub-band notes that the language card's two blocks headed from groep 3 are for older children (`map-nl-ages-2-to-5.md`).
- **Age 6**: `fase-1`, groep 2 or groep 3.
- **Age 7**, on the border: `fase-1`, groep 3, and `fase-2`, groep 4.
- **Age 8**: `fase-2`, groep 4 or groep 5.

The fase 2 sub-band says this is not an age the pack set out to cover: the band is recorded because it also holds groep 6. Each age also returns the subject's `einde-po` lane, labelled `[end-of-primary goals]`: what a school works towards by the end of groep 8, not what a child of this age should master. No age here carries a gap line. Age is a hint and never gates content.

## What each subject holds

Every record of both bands is `curriculum-institute-guidance`, not law, in up to three parts:

- Goals per band from SLO's open data, each an offer goal (*aanbodsdoel*): what a school can offer in the band, not what a child must know. Written for the 2006 core goals; checked against SLO's content-line documents (*inhoudslijn*, a line of goals through the fases).
- Goals read from such a document where the open data lacks them; these have no code.
- At fase 1, bullets of a content card (*inhoudskaart*) for groep 1 and 2, which restates the goals; the social-emotional card restates none, and 25 language bullets stand under those two blocks (`map-nl-ages-2-to-5.md`).

A goal names no year within its fase unless a heading or a tag in its wording does. A goal marked *(met hulp)*, with help, is offered with help; leaving the help out is the design's own choice. An example the document prints with a mathematics goal is held as "accompanying official text": official, no part of the goal, no limit.

**Mathematics.** Fase 1: 195 records, 98 goals and 97 card bullets: counting, digits, adding and splitting by acting, informal measuring. Fase 2: 118 goals (Getalbegrip 18, Bewerkingen 27, Verhoudingen 6, Meten 44, Meetkunde 15, Verbanden 8): numbers up to at least 1000, written procedures, tables, standard measures.

**Reading and language.** Fase 1: 206 records, 103 goals, 5 writing goals read from the document and 98 card bullets: sounds and letters; decoding and first texts from groep 3. Fase 2: 110 records, 104 goals (Mondelinge taalvaardigheid 56, Lezen 32, Schrijven 13, Taalbeschouwing 3) and 6 such writing goals: automatic word reading, non-fiction and fiction, spelling, conversations.

**Science.** Fase 1: 68 records, 34 goals and 34 card bullets: living things, materials, the seasons. Fase 2: 56 goals (sky and weather 8, plants, animals and people 23, physics and technology 25): growth and reproduction, materials, light, magnetism, structures.

**Practical life and feelings.** Fase 1: 151 records, 32 goals, 32 bullets of the orientation card and 87 of the social-emotional card: feelings, working together, safety, health, traffic. Fase 2: 58 records, 57 goals (Jezelf en de ander 29, De samenleving 13, Omgaan met het milieu 6, Gezondheid en hygiëne 9) and 1 goal read from the document. SLO publishes no social-emotional card or line for this fase.

## Limits the frameworks state

Each is in `### Limits` of the record named; none sets what a child must be able to do.

- `edu.nl.fase-1.mathematics.objective.1afda844-5b7b-4a61-83ac-4104e46b5297`: "up to at least 20", no upper limit.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.mathematics.objective.a2a3466f-0c39-4336-afac-da2e34a7b582`: by acting on quantities; written sums and signs are not mentioned.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.mathematics.objective.326eb7a4-e04d-454b-b900-3db3b349d221`: whole hours only.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.mathematics.objective.46086393-c3f7-48cc-aa4d-e0aad1f2950e`: whole euros; no cents.
- `edu.nl.fase-2.mathematics.objective.c37fbf6d-f252-4e8f-b01f-e23af0bbbd4d`: whole numbers "up to at least 1000", no upper bound.
- `edu.nl.fase-2.mathematics.objective.241fe055-2076-4e5e-9dbd-f1c536e2ab9d`: the same range; the procedures named are examples.
- `edu.nl.fase-2.mathematics.objective.d5ea4967-4ae9-4d17-ba3d-84ad6babf60f`: the tables up to and including 10.
- `edu.nl.fase-2.mathematics.objective.32502029-d66c-4bfb-b196-b1eaa69bd3c2`: kilometre, metre, decimetre, centimetre and millimetre; whole numbers.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.reading-language.objective.8e208080-a96e-49f8-ae68-1d6f53c24bbe`: a year comes only from a heading: the content-line document heads the cluster from groep 3; the open data carries none.
- `edu.nl.fase-2.reading-language.objective.2c76ac54-9406-488c-a1ba-9be81476406e`: no text length or level; the fase's text features are quoted in the lane's frame, no part of a goal.
- `edu.nl.fase-2.reading-language.objective.f7ba3b35-e899-418d-818f-4e38e5310a47`: no category or rule named; the lists per pair of groepen are quoted in the frame, no part of the record.
- `edu.nl.fase-2.reading-language.objective.90a73eeb-e072-48d0-a199-b98bfe71c1a4`: *(met hulp)*, without saying who helps or how.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.practical-life-feelings.objective.b123aac0-dbf6-48b2-b23e-c4c75ce71a38`: no feelings named or counted.

## What the pack does not hold here

- Outside the four subjects, as the frames list: the arts, movement, digital literacy, English, the clusters the frames mark as civics, geography or history, and the examples per pair of groepen for the 2006 core goals.
- Confirmation of records marked `wording-differs`, where the open data and the content-line document print different words; the lane's review file under `education/reviews/nl/` records each reason. Fase 1 reading and language, 2: `NE/SCH/02/05/02/fase1`, `NE/SCH/02/05/03/fase1`. Fase 1 practical life and feelings, 1: `ojw/ja/2/03/fase1`. Fase 2 mathematics, 1: `rw/m/4/08/fase2`. Fase 2 reading and language, 7: `NE/LE/03/02/01/fase2`, `NE/LE/05/03/01/fase2`, `NE/MT/01/08/03/fase2`, `NE/MT/02/04/03/fase2`, `NE/MT/03/09/01/fase2`, `NE/MT/03/09/02/fase2`, `NE/SCH/02/06/02/fase2`.
