# The Netherlands (`nl`), ages 9 to 12

The pack's own text, not official text; read the records it names. Groep 3 to 5: `map-nl-first-school-years.md`.

## Ages and levels

Every mapping is `convention` (`education/ages.ts`): no law ties an age to a *groep* (school year). The bands are official: SLO, the curriculum institute, defines *fase* (band) 2 as groep 4 to 6 and fase 3 as groep 7 and 8.

- **Age 9**: `fase-2`, sub-band groep 5 or 6; nothing in the goals says which are for groep 6.
- **Age 10**: `fase-2`, groep 6, and `fase-3`, groep 7.
- **Age 11**: `fase-3`, groep 7 or 8.
- **Age 12**: `fase-3`, groep 8, and the gap line: a child who starts the school year at twelve is usually in secondary school, not in the pack.

Each age also returns the subject's `einde-po` lane, labelled `[end-of-primary goals]`: what a school works towards by the end of groep 8, not what a child of this age should master. Age is a hint and never gates content.

## Standing

Five kinds; the lookup prints `standing` and `regime`. None is a statement about one child.

1. **Fase goals** (`curriculum-institute-guidance`): SLO's offer goals (*aanbodsdoel*), written for the 2006 core goals. They say what a school can offer in a band, not what a child must know: guidance, not law.
2. **Core goals of 2026** (*kerndoel*; `legal-core-goal`, regime `2026`), Dutch and mathematics: law since 1 August 2026. A goal has goal sentences (*doelzin*) and items; several are addressed to the school.
3. **Core goals of 2006** (regime `2006`): for Dutch and mathematics struck on 1 August 2026 and usable until 1 August 2031; for science and practical life still law.
4. **Draft core goals for 2027** (`draft-not-yet-in-force`), science and practical life: unsigned, not law; the draft would withdraw the 2006 goals.
5. **Reference levels** (*referentieniveau*; `legal-reference-level`): for primary school the decree sets Dutch 1F and 2F (Dutch has no 1S) and arithmetic 1F and 1S. 1F is the level every pupil should reach at the end of primary school; 2F and 1S are the higher level many pupils reach.

## What each subject holds

**Mathematics.** Fase 2, 118 goals; fase 3, 187 (Getalbegrip 28, Bewerkingen 52, Verhoudingen 24, Meten 61, Meetkunde 12, Verbanden 10). Fase 2 offers fractions without notation, decimals for money and bar charts; fase 3 decimals beyond money, percentages, the metric system and line graphs. `einde-po`, 276: 90 nodes of core goals 10 to 18, 11 goals of 2006, 83 statements at 1F and 92 at 1S.

**Reading and language.** Fase 2, 110; fase 3, 107 (Mondelinge taalvaardigheid 52, Lezen 29, Schrijven 23, Taalbeschouwing 3): judging texts, writing four kinds of text, spelling. `einde-po`, 312: 114 nodes of core goals 1 to 9, 12 goals of 2006, 91 statements at 1F and 95 at 2F.

**Science.** Fase 2, 56; fase 3, 72 (sky and weather 9, plants, animals and humans 39, materials, physical phenomena and technology 24). `einde-po`, 77: 7 goals of 2006 and 70 draft nodes.

**Practical life and feelings.** Fase 2, 58; fase 3, 65 (Jezelf en de ander 31, De samenleving 18, environment 5, health 11): feelings, conflict, money, traffic; the frame quotes SLO's note on Relaties en seksualiteit. `einde-po`, 48: 5 goals of 2006 and 43 draft nodes.

## Limits the frameworks state

Each stands in the named record's `### Limits`.

- Fase goals name no school year; the ratio goal names a prepared model and no number range (`edu.nl.fase-2.mathematics.objective.c68e65fb-31aa-4aef-a8ec-b80019d39a63`).
- Fase 3: negative numbers are explored, without operations (`edu.nl.fase-3.mathematics.objective.3531e2dd-e00b-4437-9c3b-d4640819aed7`); no fraction divided by a fraction, and no denominators named (`edu.nl.fase-3.mathematics.objective.d9645309-3fbc-4ec7-a58e-3fdc37f06800`).
- Units: a fase 3 goal names six prefixes, milli- to kilo- (`edu.nl.fase-3.mathematics.objective.ee06068e-a1a4-4e18-b239-592f8546a675`); a 1F statement relates km, m, dm, cm, mm, l, dl, cl, ml, kg, g and mg only (`edu.nl.einde-po.mathematics.objective.referentieniveau-1f-rekenen-3-meten-en-meetkunde-b-met-elkaar-in-verband-brengen-functioneel-gebruiken-1`).
- A reference-level example is an example of the level, not a limit; the stated range is at most three digits divided by at most two (`edu.nl.einde-po.mathematics.objective.referentieniveau-1f-rekenen-1-getallen-c-gebruiken-paraat-hebben-9`).
- Sentence 10 A states no number range and no number of decimal places (`edu.nl.einde-po.mathematics.objective.2f322aaf-2123-443f-91d4-c0590de92077`); its item b states one: splits up to 20 (`edu.nl.einde-po.mathematics.objective.af862c32-d590-4ff0-a5e9-12630e7ee0cd`).
- *Paraat hebben* asks for knowing by heart, having ready (`edu.nl.einde-po.mathematics.objective.referentieniveau-1f-rekenen-2-verhoudingen-c-gebruiken-paraat-hebben-1`), *functioneel gebruiken* for use in practical situations (`edu.nl.einde-po.mathematics.objective.referentieniveau-1f-rekenen-2-verhoudingen-c-gebruiken-functioneel-gebruiken-1`), *weten waarom* for understanding why (`edu.nl.einde-po.mathematics.objective.referentieniveau-1f-rekenen-1-getallen-c-gebruiken-weten-waarom-1`).
- Spelling lists stand in the frame and are no part of the goal (`edu.nl.fase-3.reading-language.objective.169f63f4-b5b9-4d69-8410-a4bf92081d9f`); so do text features, which give no text length (`edu.nl.fase-3.reading-language.objective.f4f6d2d8-0660-4731-b09a-92b1650d3e32`).
- *(met hulp)*, with help, does not say whose (`edu.nl.fase-3.reading-language.objective.774cc805-3aae-4e63-a574-9a3451c1ba88`).
- Draft 28 D is addressed to the school, not the pupil (`edu.nl.einde-po.practical-life-feelings.objective.conceptkerndoelen-2027-onderdeel-e-mens-en-maatschappij-domein-mens-en-samenleving-kerndoel-28-28-d`).

## What the pack does not hold here

- Secondary education, and the levels above primary school: Dutch 3F and 4F, arithmetic 2F and 3F.
- Goals per school year; reference levels for science or practical life; an SLO social-emotional line for fase 2 or 3.
- As the frames list: English, Frisian, arts, movement, digital literacy, society, geography, history and special-education core goals. The lists without a level in sections 4.1 to 4.3 of the reference-level decree are quoted in the `einde-po` reading-language frame, not records.
- Twelve records are `unconfirmed` with the reason `wording-differs`, each a real difference between two official renditions: fase 2 mathematics 1 (`rw/m/4/08/fase2`), fase 2 reading and language 7 (`NE/MT/01/08/03/fase2`, `NE/MT/02/04/03/fase2`, `NE/MT/03/09/01/fase2`, `NE/MT/03/09/02/fase2`, `NE/LE/03/02/01/fase2`, `NE/LE/05/03/01/fase2`, `NE/SCH/02/06/02/fase2`), fase 3 reading and language 3 (`NE/MT/01/08/03/fase3`, `NE/SCH/02/05/01/fase3`, `NE/SCH/02/06/02/fase3`), fase 3 practical life 1 (`ojw/ds/1/06/fase3`). The review files under `education/reviews/nl/` record each reason.
- No other record here is unconfirmed; the lookup prints each record's check state.
