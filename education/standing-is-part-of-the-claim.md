---
title: A game's claim uses the words of each record's standing, so an adopted standard, a foundation, guidance, a replaced core goal and a draft are never all called the curriculum
applies_when:
  - writing a game's ART.md or PR text that names a school skill
  - describing a game as following the California standards or the Dutch core goals (kerndoelen)
  - planning a game with a learning goal from Dutch core goals, reference levels or SLO goals
  - reviewing a game that claims to follow a curriculum
tags: [learning-games, standing, core-goals, kerndoelen, drafts]
---

Every record has a `standing`, and a Dutch core goal also has a `regime`. The lookup prints both. The claim names the standing in plain words.

California:

- `state-board-adopted-standard`: a content standard adopted by the State Board of Education. Every record of the kindergarten and grade lanes, and part of the cross-grade lanes.
- `department-published-foundation`: a learning foundation published by a state department, for infants and toddlers and for preschool and transitional kindergarten. It is called a foundation, not a standard.
- `voluntary-guidance`: the Transformative SEL competencies. They are called voluntary guidance, never a requirement.

The Netherlands:

- `legal-core-goal`, regime `2026`: the core goals for Dutch and for arithmetic and mathematics, in force since 1 August 2026.
- `legal-core-goal`, regime `2006`, for Dutch and arithmetic: replaced on 1 August 2026. A school may still use them until 1 August 2031. The scope line of such a record reads "version in force until 31 July 2026". The claim says "the 2006 core goal, replaced in 2026 and usable until 1 August 2031".
- `legal-core-goal`, regime `2006`, in science and in practical life and feelings: still in force. The draft below would withdraw them.
- `draft-not-yet-in-force`, regime `2027-draft`: a consultation draft, unsigned, whose own last article sets 1 August 2027. The claim says "draft core goal, not in force".
- `legal-reference-level`: the reference levels set by decree for the end of primary school, 1F and 2F for language and 1F and 1S for arithmetic.
- `curriculum-institute-guidance`: SLO's goals per fase and its content cards, the cards for peuters included. It is guidance, not law: the fase goals say what a school can offer in a band, not what a child must know.
- `legal-aim-for-childcare`: an aim that childcare law sets for the care and the programme a child is given before school. It says what childcare provides, not what a child can do.

Do not:

- No guidance, foundation or draft is called a standard, a requirement or the law.
- No replaced goal is cited without saying that it is replaced, and where a 2026 record exists for the skill it is named as well.
- `status: draft` is on every objective record and is not its standing.

Reason: the standings differ in who is bound and for how long. A claim that leaves the standing out tells a parent or a school more than the source does.
