# California (`us-ca`), ages 2 to 5

The pack's own text, not official text; the records it names still have to be read.

## Ages and levels

Every mapping in this range is official (`education/ages.ts`).

- **Age 2**: `infant-toddler`. A record holds a foundation's statement and its indicator for 23 through 36 months.
- **Age 3**: `preschool-tk`, sub-band Early (3 to 4 ½ Years): the part most Summaries mark "At the earlier age (3 to 4½ years)".
- **Age 4**: `preschool-tk`, both statements: the earlier band and "At the later age (4 to 5½ years)" overlap for the whole year. Transitional kindergarten has no standards of its own and follows these foundations.
- **Age 5**: `preschool-tk` at the later age, and `kindergarten`, which admits a child whose fifth birthday is on or before 1 September. The subject's `cross-grade` lane is returned beside them, labelled `[cross-grade]`: its statements hold for every grade, not for this age.

No age here carries a gap line. Age is a hint and never gates content.

## What each subject holds

Infant-toddler records come from the Infant–Toddler Learning and Development Foundations (second edition, Department of Social Services), preschool records from the Preschool/Transitional Kindergarten Learning Foundations (Department of Education). Both are `department-published-foundation`: foundations, not standards. An infant-toddler record describes what children typically show, not a requirement on a child. A preschool record holds both age statements of one foundation. Kindergarten records are `state-board-adopted-standard` (mathematics, English language arts, science, health), as are the cross-grade lanes, except the Transformative SEL records, which are `voluntary-guidance`. For kindergarten, `map-us-ca-first-school-years.md` goes further.

**Mathematics.** Infant-toddler: 3 records, the Emergent Mathematical Thinking strand of Cognitive Development: number words, how things fit, sorting. Preschool: 24 records: Counting and Cardinality 6, Operations and Algebraic Thinking 7, Measurement and Data 5, Geometry and Spatial Thinking 6. They run from counting to sharing, patterns and shapes. Kindergarten: 24 records. Cross-grade: 8 Standards for Mathematical Practice.

**Reading and language.** Infant-toddler: 6 records, the Language Development domain: understanding, communicating and early literacy, spoken or signed, in any language the child is learning. Preschool: 23 records: Listening and Speaking 8, Foundational Literacy Skills 7, Reading 3, Writing 5. They run from vocabulary and stories to first sounds, letter names and the written name. Kindergarten: 41 records. Cross-grade: 32 anchor standards for kindergarten to grade 12.

**Science.** Infant-toddler: 1 record, Cause and Effect; the document has no science domain. Preschool: 29 records: Science and Engineering Practices 10, Physical Science 5, Life Science 7, Earth and Space Science 4, and Engineering, Technology, and Applications of Science 3: observing, predicting, testing, and describing materials, motion, living things and weather. Kindergarten: 10 performance expectations. Cross-grade: 6 engineering design expectations, three for kindergarten to grade 2.

**Practical life and feelings.** Infant-toddler: 21 records: Social and Emotional Development 10, Approaches to Learning 8, and Cognitive Development 3 (imitation, symbolic thinking, memory). Preschool: 44 records: Social and Emotional Development 16, Approaches to Learning 12, Health 16: feelings, relations with others, attention, planning, health and safety habits. Kindergarten: 64 health education standards; no standard is adopted for social-emotional learning. Cross-grade: 8 overarching health standards and 114 Transformative SEL records in three bands tied to no grades; Early Elementary (38) is the youngest.

## Limits the frameworks state

Each is in `### Limits` of the record named. Preschool values are given for the earlier age, then the later.

- `edu.us-ca.infant-toddler.mathematics.objective.cognitive-development-strand-2-0-emergent-mathematical-thinking-2-1`: no number range; mistakes in the count are allowed; numerals are not mentioned.
- `edu.us-ca.preschool-tk.mathematics.objective.mathematics-strand-1-0-counting-and-cardinality-1-1`: to ten or further, then to thirty; a few errors allowed.
- `edu.us-ca.preschool-tk.mathematics.objective.mathematics-strand-1-0-counting-and-cardinality-1-2`: five or more objects, then ten or more, both lower bounds; no adult support is mentioned.
- `edu.us-ca.preschool-tk.mathematics.objective.mathematics-strand-2-0-operations-and-algebraic-thinking-2-3`: very small amounts, then totals up to 10; objects in everyday situations, no written sums.
- `edu.us-ca.preschool-tk.mathematics.objective.mathematics-strand-3-0-measurement-and-data-3-3`: later age only; objects laid end to end; rulers and standard units are not mentioned; an adult's help sometimes.
- `edu.us-ca.kindergarten.mathematics.objective.k-cc-5`: up to 20 things in a row, grid or ring, up to 10 scattered.
- `edu.us-ca.infant-toddler.reading-language.objective.language-development-strand-3-0-early-literacy-3-1`: recognising letters and reading words are not mentioned.
- `edu.us-ca.preschool-tk.reading-language.objective.language-and-literacy-development-foundational-language-development-strand-2-0-foundational-literacy-skills-2-1`: in speech only, and with support from an adult, pictures or objects at both ages.
- `edu.us-ca.preschool-tk.reading-language.objective.language-and-literacy-development-foundational-language-development-strand-2-0-foundational-literacy-skills-2-4`: for English, Spanish and alphabets like them, about three to eight uppercase letters, then about 15 to 20 uppercase and about 12 to 16 lowercase.
- `edu.us-ca.preschool-tk.reading-language.objective.language-and-literacy-development-foundational-language-development-strand-2-0-foundational-literacy-skills-2-5`: only the idea that a letter has a sound, then, with adult support, the sounds of several letters.
- `edu.us-ca.preschool-tk.science.objective.science-strand-1-0-science-and-engineering-practices-1-5`: adult support is named for the check and for planning it, not for the prediction.
- `edu.us-ca.infant-toddler.practical-life-feelings.objective.social-and-emotional-development-strand-1-0-self-1-4`: managing with little or no help happens only sometimes.
- `edu.us-ca.preschool-tk.practical-life-feelings.objective.social-and-emotional-development-strand-1-0-self-1-4`: adult support at both ages.
- `edu.us-ca.preschool-tk.practical-life-feelings.objective.approaches-to-learning-strand-2-0-executive-functioning-2-1`: about one or two pieces of information, then about two or three, with adult support.

## What the pack does not hold here

- Any age under 2, and the infant-toddler indicators before 23 months.
- Outside the four subjects: perceptual and motor development (infant-toddler); physical development, history and social science, the arts and English language development (preschool); eight content areas in kindergarten, named in its frames.
- Five placeholder rows of kindergarten reading and language are skipped.
- Two of the 10 kindergarten science records, K-ESS2-1 and K-ESS2-2, are unconfirmed (`wording-differs`) for a real difference between two official renditions; the notes are in `education/reviews/us-ca/kindergarten/science.json`.
