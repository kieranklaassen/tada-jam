# The Netherlands (`nl`), ages 2 to 5

The pack's own text, not official text; read the records it names.

## Ages and levels

Every mapping here is `convention` (`education/ages.ts`): no law ties an age to a *groep* (a school year; a child is 4 to 5 in groep 1). The bands are official: SLO, the curriculum institute, defines *fase* 1 as groep 1, 2 and 3.

- **Ages 2 and 3**: `peuters` (children in childcare before school). The early-years programme decree starts at two and a half.
- **Age 4**: `peuters`, up to the fourth birthday, and `fase-1`, sub-band groep 1.
- **Age 5**: `fase-1`, sub-band groep 1 or groep 2.

From age 4 the sub-band notes that two blocks of the fase 1 language card are for groep 3 and up, and the lookup also returns the subject's `einde-po` lane, labelled `[end-of-primary goals]`: what a school works towards by the end of groep 8, not what a child of this age should master. No age here carries a gap line. Age is a hint and never gates content.

## What each subject holds

A peuter record is one bullet of an SLO content card (*inhoudskaart*), `curriculum-institute-guidance`: SLO calls its aims a free view of what a childcare worker might attend to. Six peuter records are `legal-aim-for-childcare`: law on what childcare provides, not on what a child can do. A fase 1 lane has two parts, both `curriculum-institute-guidance`, not law: the per-band goals of SLO's open data, offer goals (*aanbodsdoel*: what a school can offer in groep 1 to 3, not what a child must know) written for the 2006 core goals, and the fase 1 cards for groep 1 and 2, which restate them (the social-emotional card restates none). The `einde-po` lanes hold core goals (*kerndoel*) set by decree, in force or replaced, draft core goals, and reference levels; see `map-nl-ages-9-to-12.md`; groep 3: `map-nl-first-school-years.md`.

**Mathematics.** Peuters: 50 card records (Getalbegrip 13, Bewerkingen 3, Verhoudingen 2, Meten 19, Meetkunde 11, Verbanden 2): number words, counting small amounts, comparing sizes, shapes. Fase 1: 195 records, 98 goals (27, 7, 3, 39, 18 and 4, in that order) and 97 card bullets: the counting sequence, counting amounts, digits, adding and splitting by acting, informal measuring, whole hours.

**Reading and language.** Peuters: 54 card records (Mondelinge taalvaardigheid 29, Aanvankelijk lezen 15, Aanvankelijk schrijven 7, Taalbeschouwing 3): conversations, stories, vocabulary, sound games, scribbling. Fase 1: 206 records, 103 goals (Mondelinge taalvaardigheid 43, Lezen 39, Schrijven 18, Taalbeschouwing 3), 5 writing goals read from the content-line document, and 98 card bullets: listening, rhyme, letters and sounds, first written words; decoding from groep 3.

**Science.** Peuters: 20 card records (weather and sky 3, plants, animals and people 13, physics and technology 4): the senses, the body, growing plants, safe materials. Fase 1: 68 records, 34 goals (4, 20 and 10) and 34 card bullets: what living things need, materials, light, sound and force, the seasons.

**Practical life and feelings.** Peuters: 77 records: the 6 legal aims, 54 bullets of the social-emotional card (Zelfbeeld 8, Relaties 18, Zelfsturing 12, De ander 8, Kiezen 8) and 17 of the card Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld: basic emotions, sharing, taking turns, self-care. Fase 1: 151 records, 32 goals, 32 bullets of that card and 87 of the social-emotional card: feelings, working together, rules, health, traffic.

## Limits the frameworks state

Each is in `### Limits` of the record named; none sets what a child must be able to do.

- `edu.nl.peuters.mathematics.objective.inhoudskaart-rekenen-wiskunde-peuters-getallen-getalbegrip-hoeveelheden-1`: like every peuter card record, an offer to children of about 2 to 4; "small", no number range.
- `edu.nl.peuters.mathematics.objective.inhoudskaart-rekenen-wiskunde-peuters-getallen-getalbegrip-hoeveelheden-6`: groups of 2 or 3 only.
- `edu.nl.peuters.mathematics.objective.inhoudskaart-rekenen-wiskunde-peuters-getallen-bewerkingen-bewerkingen-1`: one object or person at a time; the example rhyme's ten is no range.
- `edu.nl.peuters.science.objective.inhoudskaart-orientatie-op-jezelf-en-de-wereld-peuters-planten-dieren-en-de-mens-groeien-bloeien-en-voortplanten-1`: experiencing; no words or explanation asked.
- `edu.nl.peuters.practical-life-feelings.objective.wet-kinderopvang-artikel-1-49-eerste-lid`: binds the provider, not the child; no ages or levels.
- `edu.nl.peuters.practical-life-feelings.objective.besluit-basisvoorwaarden-kwaliteit-voorschoolse-educatie-artikel-5`: four areas, among them language and arithmetic; no level; from two and a half.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.mathematics.objective.9dd503a4-bbdf-4dbb-9a53-ba92be1a6a76`: like every fase 1 record, what a school offers; nothing says in which year; "up to at least 20", no upper limit.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.mathematics.objective.a2a3466f-0c39-4336-afac-da2e34a7b582`: by acting on amounts; no written sums or signs.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.mathematics.objective.725c4b88-a86b-4980-a44b-0dfa14387370`: informal measures; no metres, centimetres or ruler.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.reading-language.objective.8e208080-a96e-49f8-ae68-1d6f53c24bbe`: a year comes only from a heading: the content-line document heads this cluster from groep 3; the open data carries none.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.reading-language.objective.bdc982ae-8fcc-411e-9df2-16fe32d645f9`: "with help", without saying who helps.
- `edu.nl.fase-1.reading-language.objective.cf93ce2e-771c-4c6c-aa80-b937053b4ed9`: discovering; no letters named, no number.
- `edu.nl.peuters.practical-life-feelings.objective.inhoudskaart-sociaal-emotionele-ontwikkeling-peuters-emotionele-competenties-zelfbeeld-zelf-beeld-ontdekken-van-behoeften-gevoelens-en-emoties-van-zichzelf-3`: four emotions: angry, happy, sad, afraid.

## What the pack does not hold here

- Any age under 2, and goals per school year: fase 1 goals are per band, the card for groep 1 and 2.
- Outside the four subjects: the arts, digital literacy and movement, English at fase 1, the society, geography and history parts of the orientation card and goals, and the examples per pair of groepen for the 2006 core goals.
- Text features and lists of terms and spelling categories for fase 1 language: quoted in the lane's frame, not records.
- Confirmation of three fase 1 records (`wording-differs`), where the open data and the content-line document print different words: `NE/SCH/02/05/02/fase1` and `NE/SCH/02/05/03/fase1` in reading and language, `ojw/ja/2/03/fase1` in practical life and feelings. The review files under `education/reviews/nl/fase-1/` record the reason; the other lanes here have none.
