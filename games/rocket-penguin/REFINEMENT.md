### Template notes

- `game.tsx` (here `rocket-penguin.tsx`): changed. The blank draw, act and load became the toy's. `resize` also has the view draw its figures and the page again. Going to rest makes the toy again from what was just saved, by the same function that makes it on load (`found` in `toy.ts`), so parked and loaded are one thing by construction. **For the template:** `resize` has no place marked for redrawing cached figures, and nothing suggests making the parked state through the loader.
- `config.ts`: changed, as it is meant to be. The backdrop is the paper; a tier also sets whether live lines take the grain and how many crumbs are drawn; `LADDER` is one place and `FIRST_VISIT` one row.
- `state.ts`: used as copied, wrapped by `save.ts` for the ring, the parts and the friend who is eating.
- `state.test.ts`: changed. **For the template:** it indexes `LADDER[1]` in four places, so a game whose designed order is one place fails it as copied.
- `scene.ts`: used as copied for the landing, the delivery, the arrival without a fish and the way back. A cue's sound is guarded with `finishing`, as its header says.
- `guidance.ts`, `input.ts`, `overlay.ts`, `stage.ts`, `rng.ts`: used as copied. **For the template:** `input.ts` works one finger and drops a second; a game that wants a second finger answered has to take it in its Mount, as here.
- `audio.ts`: used as copied. The game plays `voiceOf(notesOf(…))`; `tick` is no longer played, and stays because `audio.test.ts` uses it. **For the template:** put-away suspends the audio, so the tail of a voice that was sounding finishes when the child comes back. Here every voice goes out through a gain of the scene's own (`sceneGain.ts`), which is closed at put-away (`putAway.ts`) and when a touch ends a scene (`telling.ts`); the copied file has no place for that. The idle ladder is taken back to the bottom in the same call, since a parked game otherwise returns with the glow or the hand already showing.
- `symbols.ts` and its test: as copied, and nothing calls it. The game draws no numeral and no sign.
- `manifest.ts`: the icon identity is the `play` family on paper.
- `perf.ts`, `quality.ts`, `attention.ts`, `saveCadence.ts`: frozen, untouched, byte-equal to the template.
- `ART.md`, `REFINEMENT.md`: filled in under the template's headings.
- **For the template:** a canvas game has nothing to test its view with, since the test runner has no canvas. `recording.ts` here is a stand-in context that draws nothing, counts stamps, strokes and fills, and records each stamp with the transform it was laid under; the view takes what makes its canvases as a parameter. With it a test can hold the frame budget, the order things are drawn in, that a thing is touched where it is drawn, and that every value of the frame is read by the view. Any canvas game needs the same.
- **For the template:** nothing says how a canvas game counts draws. Here it is stamps plus live strokes and fills, written in the view and above.
- **For the template:** `testing.ts` here has `staged`, which puts the flying body anywhere with any load, so that each cell of a grid is one short test and not a search for a pull that reaches it.

### For the owner to decide

- **The size of the characters against one page.** The whole flight is on one fixed page so that a flight's line is seen whole beside the one before. The characters are 1.3 to 1.8 times what they were, not twice: the pilot is 132 tall on a stage 820 high. Twice the size needs either a camera that follows the flight, which loses the whole line, or a landscape with fewer things to strike.
- **The look.** Squared-paper pencil, filled out: keep it, or ask for the second reserved look.
- **The rocket.** It burns only while a finger is held on the page. It shows that it wants the finger (pass 7): a pilot flame that leans to the last touch, a flare under a rested finger, and a hold shown by the idle ladder's hand. After the first flight of a visit with the rocket on and no finger held, that hand shows its hold once in the air, on the flight's line (pass 8). Whether a child reads either cold has not been tried.
- **The umbrella on a seal's nose.** It stands on the nose, and the nose is on the seal, so the bowl lies over the top of the seal by up to 24 units, in front of it. Keep it, or have the seal lift its nose clear of its back for the hold.
- **The sounds.** 108 of them, none yet heard by anyone. The near seal's two claps are now heard; they are its habit beside a landing and are not for the child, but an owner who hears them as praise can have them silent again.
- **The pencil lines of the last two flights.** Ruled by the sheet's check to stay as the help a child goes and gets; they show where a flight went and give no number.

### How the game meets the quality bar

- **Alive at idle.** Each creature moves by a curve of its own (`motion.ts`, held apart by `motion.test.ts`); the clouds drift and the lights in the sky come and go; the rocket, when it is on, sputters a pilot flame.
- **Motion and sound on every touch.** A press on the penguin, on a part of the kit, on any of fifteen other things or on bare paper is answered in the call that brings the finger down, each with its own sound (`toy.test.ts`). A second finger on the penguin is answered too (`touch.test.ts`).
- **Weight, squash and follow-through.** Springs for the pouch, the fork, the sledge, the pilot's squash, each seal, the igloo, the crate, the balloon, the coils and the fish on its line; the scarf follows late; the chick gets onto the penguin and off it by a hop.
- **Kid-clear silhouettes and tappables.** One orange pencil for what the finger works; everything pressed is drawn where it answers, at 1180 by 820, 1024 by 768 and 820 by 1180, and nothing pressed is under the home control or in the grown-up corner (`view.test.ts`). Each target holds a square or a round of 48 pixels at the two small sizes, measured as an area (`touch.test.ts`), but for the igloo's dome, which holds it only together with the chick in its doorway.
- **Wordless clarity for the band.** No word, letter, numeral or sign is drawn, and no pencil mark reads as one (passes 3, 7 and 8). `symbols.ts` is never called. The cold playtest proxy left one unclear moment, the rocket (pass 6), which now shows that it wants the finger (passes 7 and 8).
- **Wordless idle guidance.** The idle ladder glows on the penguin and then shows a pencilled hand making a pull that eases back and throws nothing; with a rocket on, a hold in turn with it; and once, after the first flight of a visit with the rocket on and no finger held, that hold in the air on the flight's line. A character's own act never resets the ladder, and the grown-up corner does not either.
- **Sight and sound on one clock.** A sound is asked for in the step that shows its cause, a later sound is a cue on the game clock, and no voice holds a delay (`sync.test.ts`, `voices.test.ts`). A scene ended by a touch is silent from that frame, what was already sounding included (`telling.test.ts`).
- **Nothing passes through anything.** A model test of the penguin's round over every load and pulls all over the grid at the three sizes (`intersections.test.ts`); a test of every bolted part as it is drawn through whole flights (`clearance.test.ts`); the seated penguin against the near seal and its ball, and the ball against the umbrella (`nearSeal.test.ts`); the chick's feet on the penguin's outline (`perch.test.ts`). What is allowed, and its caps, are in `ART.md`. This is a 2D game, so these are model tests, and each fails the run.
- **Found as left.** The parts bolted on, the ring, the friend who is eating and the fish on the rod are saved at once when they change. The idle ladder and any sound in the air are taken back at put-away. Put away at any instant, in any of over a hundred states the tests reach, the game is the same as saved and loaded: the penguin in the pouch, no scene, no sound (`toy.test.ts`, `save.test.ts`, `putAway.test.ts`).
- **No engagement mechanics.** Nothing is counted, timed, kept as a best or praised; the fish in the kit never runs out; the near seal's clap is its habit beside any landing, a face-plant included.
- **Age is a hint.** A first visit is the same at every age and with none (`toy.test.ts`).
- **Paused while unattended.** The toy advances only by the attended clock's step, and the sound is closed at put-away.
- **Resize.** The Mount watches its own element, ignores a surface with no size, and fits the stage whole into any shape (`frameBudget.test.ts`, `stage.test.ts`).
- **Frame rate: 60 on a mid-range tablet.** Not measured by the builder. Draw count 39 at rest and 67 at the busiest the tests reach, under 80 (`frameBudget.test.ts`); a pixel ratio of at most 2; one full-surface stamp; no shadow, no post pass; four tiers; the grown-up overlay shows frame time, tier and draw count.
- **Assets.** All drawn by the game, and every sound synthesised from numbers. No file is loaded and nothing is fetched.
- **A distinct look.** Squared-paper pencil, the first of the two looks reserved for the game; the art guide is `ART.md`.

### Defaults taken for the owner

- The pilot wears goggles and an orange scarf; the bands are graphite, the pouch orange.
- A touch during a scene seats the penguin at once and is then an ordinary touch.
- The whole world is one page with no camera.
- A third part sends the part bolted on first back to the kit.
- A friend goes on eating until the next press on the pouch.
- The rocket burns under a finger held anywhere on the page, and sputters a pilot flame while none is held.
- A finger that moves no further than a tap allows pokes the penguin; it does not launch it. A second finger on the penguin pokes it too.
- The hold on a flight's line is shown once in a visit, after the first flight with the rocket on and no finger held, and not after later ones.
- The near seal's two claps are heard.
- The pull stops at the near seal and its ball; it does not push the seal aside.
- The near seal puts its ball on its belly while an umbrella stands on its nose.
- A seal sends a balloon on with four fifths of what it leaves any other load.
- The chick hops onto the back of a penguin that lies on its belly.

### What the next builder should know

- The flight model has one home, `flight.ts`, and the toy never moves the body by hand while it flies. A part of the kit is a force added in `step`.
- The landscape is one module, `world.ts`; the model strikes it and the view draws it from the same numbers. Changing a size there changes where the pulls of the grid lead, and `flight.test.ts` holds the spread.
- The toy (`toy.ts`) answers each happening of the flight in the step it falls in. A sound that comes a moment later is `soon`, a cue on the game clock, and a touch that ends a scene clears the cues and closes the scene's gain.
- The view reads a frame and nothing else. A new value in the frame must change what is drawn, or `view.test.ts` fails. The view keeps a few eased values between frames (the ball put aside, where the fish last hung), so a test that compares two drawings draws each frame twice.
- The basket cannot be landed on from above. Whatever reaches it comes from the side.
- A new figure is one more stamp. The budget has about a dozen to spare at the busiest.
- A part's outline is in `PART_SHAPES` in `art.ts`, beside its drawing, and the pilot's body and soles are `PILOT_OUTLINE` and `FOOT_OUTLINE` there. The view keeps a part's outline out of the ice and the seals and stands the chick on the pilot's, and `clearance.test.ts` and `perch.test.ts` measure them, so a figure that is redrawn needs its outline redrawn with it.
- `thingAt` in `world.ts` asks the things in the order of their depth, nearest first; a new thing goes in at its depth.
- The fish a friend eats is the same figure as the fish on the rod; a test that looks for the rod's fish finds it at the end of the rod's line.
