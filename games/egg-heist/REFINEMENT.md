### The learning claim

Egg Heist has no learning goal and is designed from no record.

### Defaults taken for the owner

- One tap on the barn door wakes the farmer; a second while he is awake tidies.
- A tap on the place whose own pairing is playing only ends that pairing; the thing stays in.
- Dragging leads the raccoon.
- The goose does not notice the raccoon after a load until the child has touched the screen, and after every scene, the boot hat included, she walks on from the far lane.
- `ctx.childAge` sets nothing: one yard at every age, and at no age.

### What the next builder should know

- A goose that walks away at the raccoon's own pace is never caught by a raccoon that aims at where her tail is now: aim nearer than the tail and pluck on the way in.
- A tap meant for bare floor lands on a thing within 48 of it. Tests that send the raccoon to "floor" must pick spots clear of the things, and a moved starting spot breaks them.
- Keep one timeline. A cell of `pairings.ts` lists who does what when and which sound it makes; the model takes its sounds from it and `frame.ts` its poses. A second list of times anywhere drifts.
- A thing lies in front of its groove and a bush covers its whole width: lay nothing within a bush's width, or it is half hidden.
- On a shared machine, stop the dev server by its own process id; a pattern match on the command line also matches the shell that runs it.
