### What the next builder should know

- A stencil look makes letters and signs by accident, and a redrawing makes new ones. Read every small figure at the stills' size, then again after each fix: here a splash became a letter, then an exclamation mark; a burst became a heart; a cup became a padlock, then a target.
- Time in beats, not seconds. With every event on a grid place and the jump one beat long, the dial needs no special case and a long frame plays as its short frames.
- Keep what is at rest in one layer and put right only the patch that changed, with a cap on how much is put right in a frame and the rest drawn live meanwhile. A full ring then costs what an empty one does.
- Hold the two surfaces to arithmetic before drawing anything on them: the room between them and the length of a beat on the shorter one decide how large everything can be. Do the same sum in pixels for a finger at the smallest surface before deciding how many places the shorter surface has: here a beat of the ceiling was 35 px on a standing tablet, and the answer was a place every second beat.
- A field the model sets is not drawn until a test changes it and sees the body change, in each state that sets it.
- When a sheet says things leave the frame, draw each one all the way out and pay for it elsewhere: things at rest on a layer of their own, the rest of a scene dropped as one layer, and a fall fast enough that few are in the air at once.
- Test a move in every step of it, not at its two ends: a throw that started and ended in the right places went through a drum pad on the way. And put the sound where the eye sees the hit: if the body is flat at the half, the throw is half a beat long.
- Count the frame at the standing size too. A frame that goes on below the stage keeps a falling thing in sight for longer, and what is in sight is drawn.
- A piece that is drawn off its foot, like a wall with its stub ahead, is centred by its drawn shape wherever a finger must find it, and touched by that shape.
- A count of a case is only true if the case was reached. A test named for twins in a pipe never had two lumps in one, because a ring full of springs jumps one twin over the mouth. Each budget case now asserts the state it is named for, and the honest case found the worst frame.
- The stage's plain fit puts the jam's home control over the top middle of the stage on a short wide surface. Place the stage by what must stay clear of the control, and hold the control's box at short wide sizes as well as tablets.
- A character's own act never resets the idle ladder; only the child's touch and what the child's touch started do.
- A pose function given an age of "never" must not multiply it: nothing times infinity is not a number. `cast.test.ts` holds every pose finite.
