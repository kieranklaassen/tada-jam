# Hillside Spring: thirty refinement passes

**Progress:** all 30 passes are done; the last, pass 30, gave the thirsty sprouts a two-beat ask facing the child. Next step: the owner's review of the draft PR, then the "Still weak" list.

After the style spike, Hillside Spring was refined in thirty logged passes, following the [refinement loop](../../docs/solutions/workflow-issues/refinement-loop-for-kid-3d-readability.md): screenshot or record a seeded garden, critique it honestly as a six-year-old would meet it, make one focused set of fixes, re-shoot, revert what hurts, and re-measure. The passes cover the look, clarity for the youngest age (including a cold playtest proxy), the guidance ladder, weight and squash, per-character animation, sound, lossless put-away and performance.

**How each pass was measured.**

- **Two seeded gardens.** The fresh garden is the first open for a six-year-old: the spring running down the middle, the starter bend turned the wrong way, four thirsty beds and the rack. The full garden is a save seeded into storage: three splits and the waterwheel, every bed watered and 80% grown, so all three visitors are out and the wheel turns.
- **Looking.** Screenshots at 1180×820 about 6.4 s after load, so the glow and a ghost-hand demonstration are in frame, plus zoomed crops and native-pixel sweeps at DPR 2. Motion and guidance were never judged from stills: they were recorded with Playwright's fake clock (`page.clock`, 33 ms steps, stitched at 30 fps) and reviewed frame by frame. The recording names in the table (cold1, arr21, w22 and so on) are those captures.
- **Perf column.** The jam's shared probe on the production build (`npm run build`, `vite preview`), headless Chromium, 1180×820, DPR 2, touch, CDP CPU throttle 6×. It reads `cpuP95Ms` from `window.__jamPerf` (the game's update plus the draw submission, timed around the frame work) while the probe taps a grid across the screen. Each cell gives `cpuP95Ms` for the fresh garden / the full garden, then draw calls fresh / full.
- **This VM has no GPU.** WebGL runs in software (SwiftShader in Chromium), so frames take hundreds of milliseconds, fps is meaningless, and the governor settles at its lowest tier. From pass 16 the draw calls are reported at the tier the probe measured (tier 2 unless stated). The CPU numbers are what carries over to an iPad.
- **Noise.** At software-GL frame rates a 10 s probe keeps only 30 to 60 frames, so a p95 is close to the second-worst frame, and identical builds swing by ±1.5 ms. The full-garden probe was doubled to 20 s from pass 16, and the host drifted about 2 ms slower over the day. Where a comparison mattered (passes 27 and 30) both builds were pinned to one tier and measured back to back in the same session.
- **A contaminated stretch.** A stuck capture (a recorder and its headless GPU process at about 70% CPU) ran from around pass 11 until it was found at pass 16 and inflated the readings of passes 12 to 15. Passes 11 to 15 were re-measured clean with the same probe; the table shows the clean numbers, with the first reading in brackets.
- **Tier numbering changed at pass 13.** Before it, tier 0 was the lowest; since, tier 0 is full quality and tier 3 minimal, like Pebble Table. `?tier=0` pins the top tier.
- **The VM was reset after pass 29.** The clone and the scratch harness were rebuilt by replaying the session's recorded edits and commands from its transcript; every commit came back with an identical tree. Pass 30 and the final measurement ran on the new VM, so the final table is its own same-session measurement, with pass 29 re-measured beside pass 30.

The final screenshots and videos are in the Project store under `media/jam-10-games/hillside-spring/`: `hero.png`, `iter-01.png`, `iter-10.png`, `iter-20.png` and `iter-30.png` (the full garden at the top tier after passes 1, 10, 20 and 30), `walkthrough.mp4`, and `cold-proxy-1.mp4` to `cold-proxy-3.mp4` (the recorded cold playtest runs of passes 17 and 18). The per-pass evidence frames named in the table were working files in the scratch harness.

## Still weak

- **Not measured on an iPad.** Every number here is from a GPU-less VM: CPU time is the proxy that carries over, and fill rate on a real iPad at DPR 2 is unmeasured. The governor exists for exactly that, but its settling on a real device has not been watched.
- **Per-frame allocation is not zero.** 6.5 KB a frame remains (pass 20): about half inside three.js (render-list sort, uniform uploads, frustum setup) and half numbers boxed across view calls. The heap is flat and minor collections take under a millisecond, but a long session on an older iPad is untested.
- **The mix was tuned by measurement, not by ear.** Loudness bands are consistent, but nobody has listened on an iPad speaker, where the low wheel thump and the brook may vanish.
- **The cold playtest is a proxy.** An agent playing as a newcomer found four unclear moments; a real six-year-old has not played it.
- **The harvest is shown late.** A harvest hint waits until every bed has bloomed, so a child who stops at three beds never sees a demonstration that a bloom can be picked.
- **The sparrow is the smallest thing on the hill.** It is drawn 2.4 times its modelled size and reads at play distance, but its head snaps and tail flicks are only a few pixels at 1180×820.
