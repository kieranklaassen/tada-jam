### How the game meets the quality bar

The builder's machine draws in software and measured no frame rate. Frame rates, on a graphics card and in the shared probe, are the lead's to measure and state, with the engine, the throttle, the pixel ratio, the build and whether a physical iPad was measured.

- **Alive at idle.** The branch at the window sways, leaf shadows slide over the patch of sun, dust drifts down the beam; the king rocks and breathes, the cook bounces and stirs, the dragon breathes and swings his tail tip, and each blinks at his own pace; in the evening the flags move and the moat ripples. - **Motion and sound on every touch.** A block comes up into the hand in the frame the finger lands, with its kind's own lift and sound; every landing has its kind's own knock, a step of a five-note scale higher for each level; each of the six castle things answers a tap in its own way, each of the folk a poke, the rug a pat, the basket a creak, and the bell rings. ### Considered and not built

- The dragon curling round the widest thing on the rug when nothing is to his taste: he stays where he is. Curled round a thing he would hide it from a finger.
- The dragon crossing a bridge or riding a slide: at his length he lies through whatever stands beside it.
- The cook's legs sticking out of a window: he is as large as the room, and lies beside it with his cap in.
- A block turned by a tap: a slope's way follows from where it stands.
- A basket that rocks when it is poked, as the cue that it tips: the idle hand and the glow show it instead, and only once the castle has been seen, so the first afternoon is not pointed at the way to clear the rug.
- Holding tidied blocks inside the frame: a tall block must be lifted over the others, and lowering it would take it through them.
- An allowance for the cube drawn taller under another: it was a fault, and is fixed.
- A body set inside the tower with only his head showing: the tower is a solid block, so he stands on its top under the lifted roof instead.
- The king clinging to a pole under its flag: he would hang in the flag's way down and up. He sits on the tip, and the sheet sentence is proposed to say so.
- A walk through the gate's hole for the king and the cook: they are too big for anything a child builds, which is the sheet's standing joke. They are set on top of the gate and then in their places, and the sheet sentences are proposed to say so.
- A sound for the board's dip: it dips silently and the boing follows a little over a tenth of a second later.
- A dragon carried only as high as the shape he is in at each moment needs: he is carried over everything either of his two shapes would touch, which is simpler to hold to and sometimes higher than it need be.

### What the next builder should know

- A carried thing held on the finger's own ray hides the place it will land on from a camera that looks down from the front: hang it above the point the finger touches on the surface it will land on.
- Sun from a window in the back wall throws every shadow toward the child, in front of the thing, where it shows. Blots laid under a thing's middle are hidden by the thing.
- A test that plays the toy by rays found that no block of the basket was ever offered. Write the play tests before the first still.
- Characters who travel in arcs pass through whatever stands near either end. Move them as a hand moves a toy (up, across, down), work out the height from boxes, and hold it with a test that plays every trip on many seeded piles: it found 97 failing cases that eleven stills had not shown.
- The first audit run found what the model tests could not: the boxes of the folk left out what each holds (the cook's pot in front of him, the king's crown and cushion). Put everything a character carries into his boxes before the trips are worked out from them.
- A block drawn taller, tilted or squashed by the view is larger than the box the model tests measure. Measure it as it is drawn, or hold the pose back while something is close: the third audit run found a cube stretched into the cube over it that sixty-four piles of box tests had passed.
- For a character's head under something that moves, a test that builds his drawn parts and checks every corner against the thing (`gate.test.ts`) found in seconds what the audit needs ten minutes for.
- One warm point light as the only key light in a blue scene leaves everything above it black. Put it above the highest thing that moves.
- A piece that takes two places and faces a way has two places it can take under one finger. Choose by what the child pointed at (here, the block under the finger is the slope's heel), and test every column: the third reading found the slope hanging by its toe in four of seven.
- Editing a source file while a stills run is in progress reloads the page under it. Edit tests and documents only until the run ends.
