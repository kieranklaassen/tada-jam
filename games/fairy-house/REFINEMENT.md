### What is built

The whole sheet: the toy of Run 1, and on it the evening and the morning.

- **The house.** Seven kinds on five supports, 35 cells, each lying, settling and sounding in its own way (`cells.ts`, `voices.ts`). A cell that rocks, rolls, swings, spins or tips does so in a number of turns, knocks at each of them and is still with its last sound (`rocks.test.ts`); each cell is drawn as the grid says (`lies.test.ts`). The resting rule is pure (`rest.ts`): a thing comes down on what is under its middle, and is shed only by the leaf, acorn cap or feather its very middle is over, from that thing's top, or where it would stand higher than the arch. Where it has no room to lie there (its edge is over one of those three, or a thing its middle is not over is in its way) it comes down on that thing if that thing carries, and otherwise slides to the nearest free place it can reach without passing another thing, straight down first and then along, with a knock where it touches and its own sound where it lands; it goes home only when there is no such place (`nearest.test.ts`). What sheds it decides how it leaves: flipped off a leaf, bounced off an acorn cap, slid off a feather (`tastes.test.ts`). The rule knows heights: what lies wholly above the height a thing is let go at is over it, so a thing let go under a roof, a table, a perch or a thatch goes under it when it fits there, and otherwise slides to the nearest free place at its own height; it is never set on top through the roof (`under.test.ts`). A thing of any kind set on the high end of a see-saw tips that end down and the other up, with a donk in the step it touches; whatever is set on a see-saw lies on its slope, a small thing where it was let go and a wide one along the slab, and lies level only where the slab is level (`seesaw.test.ts`).
- **The evening**, called by dragging the sun below the hill. It is worked out from the house as a plan with no time in it (`evening.ts`), saved whole in the call in which the sun is let go, and then shown as beats of a scene on the toy's own clock. The fat one tries every leaf from the left, the tiny one gets into every acorn cap from the right with the cradles last, the tidy one sweeps with the first feather from the right: eighteen acts as numbers (`acts.ts`), one for each cell of the second grid and one for each of the three with none of its things. A cue of an act is given only when what it sounds is seen: a crack for each twig that snaps, a whoosh only when a slab sails, a bonk only into something (`seen.test.ts`). A roof whose twigs snap comes straight down where it stands, and lies there or slides the shortest way to free ground; whoever stands on a thing that leaves drops off it in sight and lands on the ground in its own way (`snap.test.ts`). The three walk behind what stands in the hollow on their way, step to a thing on a side where the ground is free, and take a thing up and put it back in sight (`behind.test.ts`, `nojump.test.ts`).
- **The morning**, called by raising the sun, filled in from `asleep`.
- **Found as left.** Where a thing lies is one function of the saved list: the list holds for each thing where it was let go, or, where it came to rest somewhere else (it slid aside, came down on the thing beside it, or leans to its other side), a place that gives that same rest again, and the house is that list set down again in its order. A load, a cancelled drag, the start of an evening and a neighbour carried off all go through it, so nothing jumps: what has to lie otherwise when its support is taken hops over in sight, with its sound (`asLeft.test.ts`, with sixty games played by chance), and lifting a thing moves and sheds nothing but what rested on it (`nearest.test.ts`, in the first-visit house and three hundred houses made by chance). One routine lays everything where the saved state has it; the constructor, a put-away and the landing of a scene that a touch ends all call it, so a parked game and a loaded game are the same by construction (`scenes.test.ts` holds it for 34 states, a thing let go under a roof, a see-saw turning over with things on it and a thing sliding aside among them).
- **The idle ladder**, whole: a cue on four things, a ghost hand that carries; every third time by day it pulls the sun down, and at evening it raises it.

### How the game meets the quality bar

- **Alive at idle.** Three folk with breath, blinks and three idle delights each; a snail, a moth and an owl. At evening the sleepers breathe and fireflies drift. Found things have no face and no idle motion: a lamp's light is steady and a flag hangs.
- **Motion and sound on every touch.** Every found thing, each of the folk, the sun, the owl, the snail, the moth, bare ground, the bark, a big root, a bush, the far hill and the sky answer in the call in which the finger lands (`toy.test.ts`, `tastes.test.ts`), each with a voice of its own. At evening a touched thing hops and a touched sleeper answers as itself.
- **Weight, squash and follow-through.** Each kind falls by its own weight and springs back at its own rate; neighbours hop; cells rock, roll and swing to rest and knock as they do. Each of the three moves in parts on springs of its own stiffness, with its own walk, its own sleep and its own way of waking.
- **Kid-clear silhouettes and tappables.** Seven kinds in seven hues and shapes, against a wood pressed more lightly. A thing is touched in a box of 74 by 74 units round its middle, which is 48 px or more each way on every tablet surface with no grace added, and only where it is seen; the sun's touch shape is 48 px or more from a phone of 390 by 844 up, and the snail, the owl, the moth, the hill by day and the left root are 48 px or more each way on every tablet surface, 820 by 1180 and 768 by 1024 among them. What one of the three holds or wears (the dustpan, the nose, the toadstool hat, the brush) answers as its owner wherever it is drawn (`where.test.ts`).
- **Wordless clarity for the band.** No word, letter, numeral or sign; the wordless check passes. The one want of the day is in the three's hands (a pillow, a dustpan, a scrubbing brush) and the one want of the evening is the sun on the hill.
- **Wordless idle guidance.** A cue on four things, then a ghost hand that shows one carry with the cue on the one thing it carries, and every third time pulls the sun down with the cue on the sun alone; at evening the cue is on the sun and the hand raises it.
- **Frame rate.** Not measured: the builder's machine is not the target. The wood is one full-surface stamp; while the sun moves it is two parts that add up to one surface and three narrow bands, a tenth of a surface more. - **Assets.** All procedural; nothing is loaded.
- **Nothing passes through anything.** A canvas game's gate is its own model tests (`overlap.test.ts`): every cell alone, at both walls and on the root step; a wide thing on a narrow one; every slide; every kind of thing on each end of a see-saw and over its stone, and a thing on such a thing, measured against the slab as it leans and not against its box; a hundred and fifty houses with things let go at any height, under roofs and tables; four hundred houses built by chance, whole and with their twigs snapped; every sleeper; a twig tapped under a roof it shares, which stays down; a slab that sails, which stops at the first thing in its way; the disc of the sun at every height of its track, which the tidy one never covers; the tidy one asleep on the floor, clear of every bed and thing beside it wherever a stretch of floor holds it; a flag on a twig beside the root step, which flies away from the step; a bell and a flag on a mast that leans on a see-saw, which hang from its tip (`seesaw.test.ts`); a roof that reaches over a bed, which is drawn in front of the one asleep in it (`scenes.test.ts`). ### What the next builder should know

- Sight and sound share the toy's fixed step: a sound is queued in the step that shows its cause, no voice holds a delay, and `counts()` gives a test the visible knocks, steps and moments to hold the sounds to.
- The house is an ordered list of `{ id, x }` and is rebuilt by the resting rule, so the save, the load, a cancelled drag, a neighbour carried off and each stage of the evening are one function. `x` is where the thing was let go, or, where it came to rest somewhere else, a place that gives the same rest again: `settle` must give the same lie for that `x` with or without the thing it slid from or came down beside, so lifting a thing changes nothing it did not carry. `settle` also takes the height a thing is let go at: what lies wholly above it is over the thing, and a place found under something is taken only when a list can say it (`sayable` in `rest.ts`), so a new rule that puts a thing somewhere has to give a list that puts it there again.
- Everything on a tipped slab carries an `along` and a `tilt` and is placed square to the slab's face (`onFace`); the toy turns it with the slab while the slab swings. A new cell that can lie on a see-saw gets both, or the gate in `overlap.test.ts` finds it half on air. What hangs from a tip (a bell, a flag) has neither: it is placed at the tip where the mast leans (`sloped` in `rest.ts`) and hangs plumb.
- A landing voice may hold a note that sounds a motion (a rumble, a purr). Where the motion is not seen, the toy plays `touchOnly` of the voice; a new cell that rolls needs nothing more, as long as its rolling note is longer than `TOUCH_SECONDS`.
- Draw order at evening: things, then the three, then what one of them is in, wears or holds and what lies over a sleeper's bed (`overSleepers` in `toy.ts`). It is worked out from the saved state in `show`, so a loaded game has it too.
- A rock is a number of turns (`cells.ts`): its knocks, its length and its stop all come from that number, so a sound cannot outlast or fall short of its motion.
- A cue of an act has a `when`: it is given only if what it shows is in the plan (a twig to snap, a slab that sails, a cradle it sleeps in).
- The round a finger takes the sun by and the sun as it is drawn are two things: `SUN_AT.x` is the first, `sunX(y)` the second.
- A thing one of the three wears or holds goes to it and back over a short blend, and whoever stands on a thing that leaves is given a drop: a new act that moves a thing has to do both, or `nojump.test.ts` and `snap.test.ts` fail.
- The evening is saved whole when it starts and only then shown: what is shown is kept apart from what is saved until the beat that shows it, and one routine brings the shown state to the saved one.
- A still body is pinned to where it rests every step. Anything that should turn a resting thing slowly (a crosspiece levelling under a load) has to move that pin, not the body alone.
- Pre-drawn pencil sprites keep the frame cheap; the cost is at sizing, when the two wood sheets and 39 sprites are hatched once.
