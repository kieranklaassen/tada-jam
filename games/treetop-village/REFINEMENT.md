### Sentences proposed for the sheet

None is proposed after reading 4. The five proposed after reading 3 were checked and pasted by the lead in round 5:

- **Routes are remade.** As it stands: "A walker with somewhere to go takes the shortest way through the pieces that are in the tree, whoever they suit, and meets each piece as the grid says. Where it has to stay, it stays, on a landing, and looks across. When the child changes anything in the tree or sends a bucket, every walker that still has somewhere to go sets off again from where it stands." Replacement: "A walker with somewhere to go takes a way it can get all the way along, where the tree has one, however long: every piece on it is one it crosses or a bucket it waits for. Where the tree has none, it takes the shortest way whoever the pieces suit and goes as far as the first piece that stops it. It meets each piece as the grid says. Where it has to stay, it stays, on a landing, and looks across. When the child lets a piece go, onto the tree or off it, or sends a bucket, every walker that still has somewhere to go and now has another way sets off again from where it stands." Why: the sheet promises the way round in three places, and the shortest way whoever it suits never takes it. A piece in the hand has changed nothing yet, so that a touch taken away leaves the tree as a load finds it.
- **A try, its beats.** As it stands: "the walker turns, goes by the shortest way, meets each piece on it as the grid says, and stops where it gets across to or has to stay." Replacement: "the walker turns, goes by its way, meets each piece on it as the grid says, and stops where it gets across to or has to stay." Why: the same.

### How the game meets the quality bar

No frame rate has been measured by anyone. The lead measures it on a production build through the shared probe; nothing below is a frame rate.

- **Alive at idle.** Each walker breathes at its own pace and moves its one pinned part (tail, head, hat, the second twin); all pupils follow what happens; the six leaf cards sway, the bird bobs on its spring, the snail creeps, the clouds drift, the sun or the moon bobs. `motion.ts` holds the paces.
- **Motion and sound on every touch.** A piece jumps to the finger in the frame it lands; a piece in the tree knocks and jogs wherever it is drawn; a walker's flourish starts when the finger lands, and a second tap in the middle of it gets a small jump and its voice again; pegs knock, the oak rustles and drops an acorn, the sun and the moon hum. `voices.test.ts` holds that each of these sounds within a twentieth of a second. A touch on bare page makes the leaf cards quiver with a small sound of paper. A touch in the top right corner is the grown-up's and gets no answer from the game.
- **Weight, squash and follow-through.** A piece swings behind the hand, snaps on past its size and settles, and turns over and over on its way home; the basket hops when one lands; a plank bows under a walker, a bucket sags under the owl, a swing's seat turns about its pin; leaf cards shake after a snap.
- **Kid-clear silhouettes and tappables.** Four walker cards 70 to 120 high in the strongest colours; everything the child builds in one pale cream wood; pegs plain and pale. Basket slots are 94 wide and every tappable is 48 or more. Nothing tappable lies under the home control at the top centre or in the top right 72 by 72 (`tree.test.ts`).
- **Wordless clarity for age 4.** No word, letter, numeral or sign on the kid side; the letter is a leaf, each door is a colour and a shape. One activity is live. `node scripts/wordless-check.ts` passes.
- **Wordless idle guidance.** The glow on the basket from 3 s, the paper hand carrying a piece to a free place a few seconds later, a different piece and place each time; a tap on a walker when the tree is full; the glow and a tap on a bucket someone waits for; the sun drawn down, or the moon lifted.
- **The frame budget.** Canvas 2D, cached sprites, pixel ratio capped at 2 by the frozen quality tiers, no shadow maps, no post pass. `frameBudget.test.ts` counts 40 draws on a first visit and 73 in the worst state it can build, under eighty, and holds that only the page is stamped over more than a fifth of the surface.
- **Procedural or committed assets only.** Everything is drawn and synthesized in code. `node scripts/egress-check.ts` passes.
- **Every character moves like itself.** Fifty-six meetings written one by one in `scripts.ts`; `scripts.test.ts` holds that no two look or sound alike and that sound and picture share one timeline.
- **Nothing passes through anything.** A canvas game, so model tests hold it (`frame.test.ts`): a walker on a piece is on that piece between its ends, pieces sit on their pegs, what sinks into a bucket or a trough is cut at its wall, a rider is inside its cup, the piece in the hand is over everything.
- **Found as left.** Every scene saves its outcome when it starts; on load nothing walks and nothing replays (`scenes.test.ts`, `village.test.ts`, `save.test.ts`).

### Defaults taken for the owner

- The first visit by age: three pieces in the tree at 5 and younger and with no age, a bare tree from 6 (`save.ts`).
- The twins walk as one unit.
- The owl sinks to her eyes in a bucket far too small for her.
- Dusk is finished after six seconds; a longer walk home goes on as a try.
- The hand shows the pull tab one time in four by day and every other time by night.
- The moon is a sleeping face in a nightcap; the sun sleeps too. Neither asks for anything.
- Morning begins when the moon is let go at least half way up its slot, so a small finger need not reach the end.
- A walker takes a way it can get all the way along before a shorter one that stops it, so it tries a piece that stops it only when the tree gives it no way round.
- A new piece goes to the nearest walker with a way to it, busy or not; one that waits for a bucket is passed over for a piece beyond it, and where nobody has a way the nearest walker asks.
- Where two wait at one end of a bucket, a load seats the first in the order owl, twins, beetle.
- A bucket sent while a walker is still getting in goes empty, and the walker waits on its landing.

### What the next builder should know

- Write a walker's meeting once, as timed beats that set the pose, the piece and the notes together, and let the model, the frame and the voice all read that one script. The sync test then has something to walk.
- A card that holds someone needs two cards: what is behind the rider and what is in front. One card in front crosses the face.
- A night look made by a second printing of each card costs no draws, where a tint pass over the surface would cost a full stamp.
- A walker that waits for something needs a way out of the wait when its errand ends another way (evening, here).
