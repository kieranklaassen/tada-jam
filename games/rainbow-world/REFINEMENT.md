### What the next builder should know

- One clock: the world asks for each sound at the moment the picture shows it, and the Mount plays what it is handed. A sound with two beats is two sounds at two moments, never one voice with a delay.
- Translucent paper cannot hide what stands behind it. Keep feet apart in rows of depth, lay the nearer thing on a veil of its own outline, and take the thing whose shape is under the finger.
- A box that answers a touch has to come from the same numbers the view draws with. The lying basket was drawn in one place and touched in another until both were taken from one module (`basket.ts`), and a thing is touched by the outline it is drawn with (`shape.ts`).
- A basket of translucent paper shows a muddle of whatever is in it. It is the one opaque thing: draw only what looks out, whole.
- Every state of the hand needs an end that a put-away can reach. List the states (held, carried, let go and waiting) and test that each one ends.
- Stills are few. Spend one on every state a child rests in (here: the basket with things in it) before spending two on one scene.
