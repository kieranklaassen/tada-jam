---
title: A game for ages 2 to 4 is played with taps and forgiving drags on large separate targets, repeats one action a toddler already loves, and is funny through objects used wrongly
applies_when:
  - designing a game for children aged 2 to 4
  - planning a learning game for a given age when the band starts at 2 or 3
  - choosing gestures, target sizes or session length for the youngest children
tags: [ages-2-4, toddlers, gestures, repetition, humor, touch]

The jam's wordless-clarity convention has no research for two-year-olds. This rule fills that gap.

Do:

- Everything essential works with a tap. A drag survives a lifted finger and counts when partly done.
- Targets are about 2 cm across (about 100 logical pixels, above the jam's 48 px floor), well apart, and away from the bottom edge where wrists rest. Whatever looks touchable is touchable.
- The game is built on one action toddlers repeat unprompted: carrying and tipping out, lining up, spinning, dropping and flinging, covering, fencing in, joining, mixing.
- A whole cycle fits in one to three minutes.
- The same thing is offered again and again. On day 15 the child is faster, surer and trying one new thing.
- Funny at two: objects used wrongly, strange sayings, invented words, toilet humor. At three, add wrong names, tricks and "boo".
- Quantities stay at about five or fewer. Reading skills are sound play: rhyme, syllables, which animal made that sound.

Do not:

- No pinch, tilt, shake or double tap. A double tap makes a child this age think the game is broken.
- No instruction by voice alone, no letters, no numerals.
- No new content for its own sake.

Exception: none.

Evidence: Sesame Workshop's tablet guidelines, for gestures, wrists and targets [V]. Hoicka et al.'s survey of 671 children, for humor by age [V]. Three-year-olds learned words from the same three books read repeatedly and not from nine different ones [V, Horst et al. 2011; 16 children]. The repeated actions are Athey's schemas, from a summary that gives no ages [S]. The cycle length is inference [I]; the target size is from `lab/arcade/RESEARCH.md`.
